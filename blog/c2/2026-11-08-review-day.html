📝گرامر: Hedging and Distancing (it would appear that...)

Hedging means making a statement less direct or less certain, so you do not claim more than you can prove. Say *The data suggest that social media may contribute to anxiety*, not *Social media causes anxiety*.

Distancing means showing that a claim comes from someone else, not from you. *The CEO has allegedly resigned* keeps you at a safe distance from the claim.

Use seem / appear (+ to) and it would seem / appear that to soften a claim. *There seems to be a problem* is gentler than *There is a problem*.

Modals soften a claim too: may / might / could,
