📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
determined effort /duh-TUR-mind EF-urt/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
A strong, persistent attempt to achieve a goal.
تلاش مصمم، کوشش جدی
"Achieving financial independence requires determined effort and planning."
devaluation /dee-val-yoo-AY-shun/
اسم · noun
A reduction in the value of a currency.
کاهش ارزش پول
"The country experienced a sharp devaluation of its currency."
devalue /dee-VAL-yoo/
فعل · verb
To reduce the value or importance of something.
بی‌ارزش کردن، کاهش ارزش دادن
"Inflation can devalue savings over time."
development grant /duh-VEL-up-munt GRANT/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Money given to support growth or improvement projects.
کمک هزینه توسعه
"The startup received a development grant for its innovative project."
double-dip /DUB-ul-DIP/
فعل · verb
To receive two sources of income or benefits simultaneously.
از دو منبع درآمد داشتن
"Some retirees try to double-dip by working part-time."
ease debt /EEZ DET/
عبارت · phrase
To reduce the burden or amount of money owed.
کاهش بدهی، سبک کردن بدهی
"The government introduced policies to ease debt for students."
economic growth /ek-uh-NOM-ik GROHTH/
اسم · noun
An increase in the production of goods and services.
رشد اقتصادی
"Governments aim for sustainable economic growth."

پادکست: پول و خرید

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today Lena and I are talking about money, work, and the quiet choices that shape a whole community.

Lena

Hello everyone, I am Lena. Our theme today is money and shopping at an advanced level, and our story follows a small town that refused to give up when prices rose and work became hard to find.

Maya

That town is a perfect example of determined effort. The bakers, the teachers, and the shopkeepers all showed determined effort, week after week, even when nobody was watching.

Lena

I love that phrase, determined effort. It is not talent and it is not luck. Determined effort means you keep going in a focused way, and in our story, determined effort is what saved the local market.

Maya

Exactly. But first, the town faced a shock. There was a sharp devaluation of the national currency, and that devaluation made everything imported far more expensive overnight.

Lena

Let us slow down on devaluation. A devaluation happens when a currency loses value by official decision or by market pressure, and this devaluation meant a bag of imported flour suddenly cost almost twice as much.

Maya

And when devaluation hits, people worry that it will devalue their savings too. To devalue something is to reduce its value or its importance, and nobody wants inflation to devalue a lifetime of careful saving.

Lena

That is such an important distinction. Devaluation is the event in the currency, and to devalue is the wider verb. A crisis can devalue money, but it can also devalue skills if employers stop respecting experienced workers.

Maya

In the town, the mayor said something wise. She said, we must never devalue the people who kept this place alive, and we must never devalue honest work just because times are difficult.

Lena

She also applied for a development grant. A development grant is money given to support growth or improvement projects, and this development grant was meant to rebuild the market hall and train young shopkeepers.

Maya

The development grant did not arrive by magic. It took determined effort to write the application, determined effort to gather the figures, and more determined effort to answer every difficult question from the reviewers.

Lena

And here is a quick word from us about learning itself, because determined effort works for language too. It is completely free to install, so open the app every day, practise a little, and let your own determined effort turn small daily steps into real fluency over time.

Maya

Beautifully said. Now, back to the town. When the development grant finally arrived, there was a temptation for some officials to double-dip. To double-dip means to receive two sources of income or benefits at the same time in a way that is not fair.

Lena

Yes, a few people wanted to double-dip by taking a salary from the town and a second payment from the grant. The mayor stopped anyone who tried to double-dip, because a development grant belongs to the whole community, not to a few clever individuals.

Maya

That honesty mattered, because the grant had a clear purpose. Part of the money was used to ease debt for small businesses. To ease debt means to reduce the burden of money owed, and when you ease debt for a baker or a tailor, that person can breathe again and plan ahead.

Lena

I think ease debt is one of the most human phrases in this lesson. A town can ease debt by lowering interest, by extending deadlines, or by offering small loans, and each step to ease debt gives families more control over their lives.

Maya

And the long term goal behind all of this was economic growth. Economic growth means an increase in the production of goods and services, and healthy economic growth means more real work, more real products, and more real choices for ordinary people.

Lena

But the mayor kept warning that economic growth is not just a number on television. If economic growth only helps a few people who double-dip and take unfair benefits, then it is not the kind of economic growth a community should celebrate.

Maya

Let us tell the middle of the story. In the first winter after the devaluation, sales fell badly. Some visitors said the town would never recover, and a few newspapers seemed to devalue everything the town had built.

Lena

Yet the shopkeepers answered with determined effort again. They repaired the market hall together, they shared delivery vans to cut costs, and their determined effort slowly brought customers back from the bigger cities.

Maya

The development grant paid for new roofs and better lighting, and a second, smaller development grant later paid for a training kitchen where teenagers learned to cook, to budget, and to run a small cafe.

Lena

Those teenagers learned an early lesson about money too. Their teacher told them never to double-dip when they handled the cafe accounts, never to take money from both the till and the training fund, because learning not to double-dip is part of learning to be trusted.

Maya

Meanwhile, the local credit union created a plan to ease debt for young families. That plan to ease debt combined advice, smaller payments, and a rule that nobody would be charged unfair fees while they were trying honestly to pay.

Lena

And notice how these ideas connect. When you ease debt, people can spend and save with confidence. When confidence returns, economic growth becomes possible, and when economic growth is shared fairly, a town starts to feel hopeful again.

Maya

There was still a risk, of course. Another devaluation in a neighbouring country frightened investors, and that second devaluation reminded everyone how quickly a devaluation can change prices, plans, and even moods.

Lena

The mayor answered calmly. She said a devaluation is serious, but it does not devalue our knowledge, it does not devalue our friendships, and it must never devalue the next generation and their ideas.

Maya

I find that moving, because so many people only talk about money as numbers. She understood that to devalue a person is worse than any fall in a currency, and she refused to devalue the cleaners, the drivers, and the market porters who kept everything running.

Lena

Let us pause for our second reminder about your English, because stories like this are exactly how advanced vocabulary sticks. Maya

Now the final part of the story. Two years later, the market was full again. Visitors came for the bread, the cheese, and the handmade goods, and the town recorded steady economic growth for eight seasons in a row.

Lena

That economic growth was not an accident. It came from determined effort by hundreds of people, from a development grant that was spent honestly, and from leaders who refused to double-dip or to let others double-dip in secret.

Maya

It also came from the decision to ease debt early rather than late. Because the town acted quickly to ease debt, shops stayed open, and because shops stayed open, economic growth had something real to build on.

Lena

If a student asked me to summarise the lesson in one sentence, I would say this. Determined effort protects a community when devaluation threatens to devalue its savings, especially when a fair development grant helps people ease debt and build honest economic growth without anyone trying to double-dip.

Maya

That is a long sentence, but it uses every word beautifully. Let me try a shorter version for our listeners to repeat. With determined effort, we can survive devaluation, refuse to devalue honest work, use a development grant wisely, never double-dip, ease debt for our neighbours, and create economic growth that includes everyone.

Lena

I would sign my name under that. And for learners, notice the patterns. Devaluation is a noun for the fall in a currency. Devalue is the verb for reducing value or importance. A development grant is support money for improvement, and to double-dip is to take two benefits unfairly at once.

Maya

And ease debt is about lightening what people owe, while economic growth is about producing more goods and services in a healthy way. Determined effort, of course, is the engine behind all of it, because no grant and no policy works without determined effort from real people.

Lena

Before we close, think about your own life for a moment. Where do you need determined effort right now? Is there a way to ease debt, even a small one, this month? And do you ever feel that other people devalue your work, when in fact your work has lasting value?

Maya

Those are good questions to carry into the week. Remember, a single devaluation does not decide a whole future, a development grant is only a tool, and economic growth matters most when it lifts everyday lives. What decides the future is people, working together, with determined effort and fairness.

Lena

Thank you for listening to Muse English today. Keep practising, keep speaking out loud with the shadowing stories, and come back tomorrow for fresh words, a fresh story, and another chance to grow.

Maya

See you tomorrow, everyone. Keep going, your determined effort is already making a difference, and we are proud of every step you take with English.

📝گرامر: Cleft Sentences (It was... that, What I need is...)

A cleft sentence splits one idea into two parts to emphasise one of them. Instead of a plain statement, you put the important part in the spotlight, as in It was Tom who broke the window.

In an it-cleft, use It is / It was + the focus + that — or who for people. With determined effort, a speaker can show that it was the development grant that changed the town, not luck.

The verb after *It* stays singular, even with a plural focus: It was my parents who paid. We often use this pattern to correct someone: it is not devaluation I fear, it's the unfairness
