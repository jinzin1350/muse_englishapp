📚 Vocabulary

10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
sartorial /sar-TOR-ee-uhl/
adjective
relating to tailoring and elegant clothing style
"His sartorial choices always make him look sharp at formal events."
bespoke /bih-SPOHK/
adjective
made specially for one person, to their exact measurements
"She ordered a bespoke suit that fit her perfectly."
couture /koo-TOOR/
noun
very expensive high-fashion clothing made by hand
"The Paris atelier is famous for its couture gowns."
silhouette /sil-oo-ET/
noun
the overall outline or shape of a garment
"The dress has a sleek silhouette that flatters every figure."
dapper /DAP-er/
adjective
neat, smart, and stylishly dressed, usually of a man
"He arrived at the wedding looking dapper in a navy suit."
ensemble /ahn-SAHM-buhl/
noun
a complete set of clothes worn together as one outfit
"Her black ensemble looked elegant from head to toe."
embellishment /em-BEL-ish-ment/
noun
a decorative detail added to clothing, such as beads or embroidery
"The gown's gold embellishments caught the light beautifully."
drape /DRAYP/
verb
to hang or fall in soft, graceful folds
"The silk fabric drapes gracefully over the shoulders."
flamboyant /flam-BOY-ant/
adjective
very bright, bold, and showy
"His flamboyant jacket turned every head at the party."
understated /un-der-STAY-tid/
adjective
elegant in a simple, quiet way, without showing off
"Her understated dress was far more elegant than the flashy gowns."

📝 Podcast Transcript

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and with me as always is Lena. Today we are diving into the world of fashion and style, and the rich vocabulary that comes with it.

Lena

Hello, everyone! I am so glad we picked this topic, Maya, because style is everywhere. Every morning, we all stand in front of a mirror and make a dozen tiny decisions about how we want to present ourselves to the world.

Maya

Exactly. And those decisions say a lot. Think about it. When someone walks into a room in a perfectly cut jacket, you notice. You might not know why, but you feel it. That is what we call a sartorial statement. The word sartorial simply means relating to tailoring and elegant clothing.

Lena

I love that word, sartorial. It sounds as elegant as the clothes it describes. And a sartorial choice is never an accident. It shows care, attention, and taste.

Maya

Right. A sartorial decision could be as small as choosing a crisp white shirt over a wrinkled one, or as big as commissioning a whole new wardrobe. And honestly, most of us make sartorial decisions every day without even realizing it.

Lena

That is so true. And speaking of decisions, before we go deeper, let me take a quick moment to tell you about something that can help you with your English every single day. The Muse English app gives you daily words with photos and pronunciation, this very podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar lessons, and a leaderboard where you can earn points. It is free, it is fun, and it keeps you learning a little every day. Go and install it, and make English part of your morning routine.

Maya

Thank you, Lena. Now, let us talk about something that sits at the very top of the fashion world: clothes made just for you. Lena, have you ever dreamed of owning something bespoke?

Lena

Oh, absolutely. Bespoke means made specially for one person, to their exact measurements. A bespoke suit is not pulled off a rack. It is cut, fitted, and finished for one body and one body only.

Maya

And that is why a bespoke garment feels so different. When a tailor makes a bespoke jacket, they take dozens of measurements. They ask about your posture, your habits, even which hand you write with. The result is a bespoke piece that fits like it was born on you.

Lena

It is a luxury, of course, but it is also a philosophy. Bespoke fashion says that your body is not a standard size. It says that clothing should adapt to the person, not the other way around. I think everyone should experience one bespoke item in their life, even if it is just a shirt.

Maya

I agree. And if bespoke is the world of perfect fit, then couture is the world of pure art. Couture means very expensive high-fashion clothing made by hand, and the word alone makes people dream of Paris.

Lena

Paris is the heart of couture. In the great couture ateliers, seamstresses spend hundreds of hours on a single gown, sewing every bead and stitch by hand. Couture is not about practicality. It is about imagination.

Maya

That is what makes couture so fascinating. A couture collection might include a dress made of feathers, or a coat that looks like a sculpture. These pieces are rarely worn to the supermarket. They are made to push the boundaries of what clothing can be.

Lena

And yet, couture influences everything. The colors, the fabrics, and the shapes you see in couture shows eventually trickle down into the clothes we all buy. So even if we never wear couture ourselves, couture still shapes our wardrobes.

Maya

Let us move from the atelier to the mirror, and talk about shape. When designers sketch a new dress, the first thing they think about is its silhouette. The silhouette is the overall outline or shape of a garment.

Lena

The silhouette is so important, Maya. A long, straight silhouette feels modern and minimal. A full, flowing silhouette feels romantic. The same fabric can look completely different depending on its silhouette.

Maya

Exactly. Think of a classic evening gown. Its silhouette might be narrow at the waist and wide at the hem, like a bell. Or it might be a sleek silhouette, straight from shoulder to ankle. Designers play with silhouette the way architects play with buildings.

Lena

And silhouette is closely connected to our next word, drape. To drape means to hang or fall in soft, graceful folds. When you drape a piece of silk over your shoulders, it does not stand stiff. It flows.

Maya

Beautiful fabrics drape beautifully, and stiff fabrics do not. A good designer knows how a fabric will drape before they cut it. They know that velvet will drape in heavy, luxurious folds, while linen will drape in a lighter, more relaxed way.

Lena

I love watching a fabric drape. If you hold a piece of chiffon in the air and let it fall, it seems to float. The way a fabric drapes can make the difference between a dress that looks alive and a dress that looks like cardboard.

Maya

And when the drape is right, the silhouette follows. That is the magic: the drape of the fabric creates the silhouette of the garment. A designer who understands both can make a simple piece of cloth look extraordinary.

Lena

Now, let us talk about the gentlemen for a moment. Maya, what is the first word that comes to mind when you see a man in a perfectly fitted suit with a pocket square and polished shoes?

Maya

Dapper. Without a doubt. Dapper means neat, smart, and stylishly dressed, and it is usually used for men. A dapper man looks like he thought about every detail.

Lena

A dapper man does not need to wear anything expensive. He might wear a simple blazer and dark trousers, but everything is clean, pressed, and well matched. That is what makes him dapper.

Maya

I think dapper is a wonderful compliment. It says more than handsome. It says the man has taste and discipline. When my grandfather went out, even just to buy bread, he looked dapper, with his hat and his polished shoes.

Lena

That generation understood dapper dressing. But let us be honest, not every stylish man wants to be dapper. Some men want to be flamboyant. Flamboyant means very bright, bold, and showy.

Maya

Flamboyant is the opposite of quiet! A flamboyant jacket might be covered in sequins, or bright purple, or patterned with giant flowers. A flamboyant dresser wants to be noticed, and they usually are.

Lena

There is something joyful about flamboyant style. A flamboyant outfit says, I am here, and I am not afraid. It takes confidence to wear a flamboyant suit to a party where everyone else is in black.

Maya

It does. And I admire that confidence. A flamboyant look can be theatrical and fun. But flamboyant style is not for every occasion. You probably would not wear a flamboyant outfit to a job interview, unless the job is in show business.

Lena

True. Style is about context. And that brings us to putting it all together. An ensemble is a complete set of clothes worn together as one outfit, and building a great ensemble is a real skill.

Maya

Yes! An ensemble is not just one item. It is the jacket plus the shirt plus the trousers plus the shoes plus the accessories, all working together. A beautiful ensemble feels like a story.

Lena

I always plan my ensemble the night before an important day. I lay everything out and ask myself: does this ensemble work? Do the colors speak to each other? Is anything fighting for attention?

Maya

That is the secret of a great ensemble. Every piece should support the others. If you have a stunning necklace, the rest of the ensemble should stay calm. If your shoes are the stars, the rest of the ensemble should let them shine.

Lena

And sometimes, the detail that makes an ensemble unforgettable is an embellishment. An embellishment is a decorative detail added to clothing, like beads, embroidery, or lace.

Maya

Embellishment is where craftsmanship shines. Think of a jacket with delicate embellishment along the collar, or a dress where the embellishment forms a pattern of tiny flowers. That embellishment can take hours of handwork.

Lena

But embellishment is a balancing act. One beautiful embellishment can elevate a whole outfit. Too much embellishment, and the outfit starts to look busy. The best designers know exactly how much embellishment is enough.

Maya

Absolutely. And this balance brings us to our final word of the day, understated. Understated means elegant in a simple, quiet way, without showing off.

Lena

Understated elegance is my favorite kind of style. An understated dress might be a simple black column of silk, with no embellishment at all. And yet it looks more expensive than a dress covered in jewels.

Maya

That is the power of understated dressing. An understated outfit whispers instead of shouting. It says, I do not need to prove anything. An understated look is often the most confident look in the room.

Lena

And here is something interesting, Maya. An understated outfit still requires sartorial knowledge. Choosing the perfect simple dress, the right fabric, the right fit, that is a sartorial skill in itself.

Maya

So true. And before we wrap up, let me remind you about the Muse English app, because it is the easiest way to keep building your vocabulary. Every day you get new words with photos and pronunciation, this podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar lessons, and points on the leaderboard. It is completely free, and it turns ten minutes of your day into real progress. Download it today and keep learning with us.

Lena

Let us quickly revisit our ten words. We talked about sartorial choices, bespoke tailoring, and handmade couture. We explored silhouette and drape, the shape and the fall of fabric. We compared dapper and flamboyant style, built an ensemble, admired an embellishment, and finished with the quiet power of understated elegance.

Maya

Ten words, one rich world. Fashion is not just about clothes. It is about identity, culture, and creativity. And now you have the vocabulary to talk about it in English.

Lena

Try using these words this week. Describe what you are wearing. Notice the silhouette of a coat in a shop window. Ask yourself whether your own style is flamboyant, understated, or somewhere in between.

📝Grammar: The Subjunctive (I insist that he be)

The subjunctive expresses demands, suggestions, and necessities. It uses the base form of the verb, whatever the subject is. After verbs like **insist**, **demand**, **suggest**, and **recommend**, use **that** + subject + base verb. The verb never changes for the subject. We say **I insist that he be** on time, not "he is" on time. The subjunctive also appears after adjectives like **essential**, **vital**, and **crucial**. In formal style, **were** replaces **was** in unreal conditionals: **If I were** you, I would choose the simpler outfit.
