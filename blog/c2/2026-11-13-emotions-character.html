۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
blissfully happy /BLISS-fuh-lee HAP-ee/
گروه وصفی · adjective phrase
Extremely and perfectly happy.
غرق در خوشبختی، بسیار شاد
"The newlyweds looked blissfully happy together."
blissfully ignorant /BLISS-fuh-lee IG-nuh-runt/
گروه وصفی · adjective phrase
Happy because you don't know about something bad.
بی‌خبر و شاد، غافل از مشکلات
"He remained blissfully ignorant of the impending disaster."
blissfully unaware /BLISS-fuh-lee un-uh-WAIR/
گروه وصفی · adjective phrase
Happily not knowing about something negative.
بی‌خبر و بی‌خیال، غافل
"She was blissfully unaware of the gossip circulating about her."
bulk up /BULK UP/
فعل عبارتی · phrasal verb
To gain muscle mass and become bigger.
عضله آوردن، حجم گرفتن
"He's trying to bulk up for the bodybuilding competition."
chatty /CHAT-ee/
صفت · adjective
Fond of talking; talkative.
پرحرف، وراج
"She's a very chatty person and loves meeting new people."
chauvinistic /shoh-vuh-NISS-tik/
صفت · adjective
Believing one's own group or country is superior.
ناسیونالیست افراطی، مردسالار
"His chauvinistic remarks offended many colleagues."
clench your fist /KLENCH yor FIST/
گروه فعلی · verb phrase
To tightly close your hand into a ball.
مشت کردن
"He would clench his fist when he got angry."

پادکست: احساسات و شخصیت

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today Lena and I are talking about feelings, character, and the small moments that reveal who people really are.

Lena

Hello everyone, I am Lena. I love this topic because character is not what people claim to be. Character is what they do when they are tired, embarrassed, or blissfully happy and nobody is watching.

Maya

Blissfully happy is a wonderful place to start. When my sister got her dream job last spring, she looked blissfully happy for a whole week. She sang while she cooked, she danced in the kitchen, and she was so blissfully happy that even a rainy Monday could not spoil her mood.

Lena

That is the best kind, the kind that spreads. My neighbour was blissfully happy after his daughter was born. When you are blissfully happy like that, ordinary days feel golden.

Maya

But there is a shadow side, is there not? You can also be blissfully ignorant. My uncle is blissfully ignorant about technology. He thinks his phone listens to him, yet he is blissfully ignorant of every password he has ever set, and he stays calm because he simply does not know the risks.

Lena

Blissfully ignorant can be charming in small doses. A child who is blissfully ignorant of adult worries can play all afternoon without fear. But an adult who stays blissfully ignorant about money or health may face a shock later. I was blissfully ignorant about car repairs until the bill arrived, and then I wished I had asked questions much earlier.

Maya

That connects to our next phrase, blissfully unaware. At the surprise party we planned for Tom, he was blissfully unaware that twenty friends were hiding in his garden. He walked in, completely blissfully unaware, carrying shopping bags, while we held our breath behind the fence.

Lena

I have been blissfully unaware too. Last year I walked around all morning blissfully unaware that I had toothpaste on my chin. Nobody told me, so I stayed blissfully unaware and perfectly confident in every meeting.

Maya

Speaking of confidence, let us talk about our chatty friend, Daniel. Daniel is so chatty that a two minute queue becomes a full life story. He is chatty with taxi drivers, chatty with waiters, and chatty even when he is exhausted.

Lena

I admire people who are chatty in a generous way. A chatty nurse can calm a nervous patient. But a chatty colleague in a quiet office can also drive everyone mad, especially when you are trying to concentrate and they simply cannot stop.

Maya

That is true. Daniel knows he is chatty, and he laughs about it. He says, I was chatty as a child, I am chatty now, and I will probably be chatty at ninety, telling stories in a care home.

Lena

Maya

Thanks, Lena. Now let us turn to a very different word, chauvinistic. A chauvinistic person believes their own group or country is superior to all others. My old boss was chauvinistic about our company. He refused every idea from rival teams, and his chauvinistic attitude made talented people leave.

Lena

Chauvinistic behaviour is sadly common in sport and politics. A chauvinistic fan can love his team and still respect opponents, but the moment he says other fans are worthless, he has crossed a line. I once dated someone rather chauvinistic who thought his city was the only civilised place on earth, and every conversation became an argument.

Maya

How do you respond to someone chauvinistic without starting a fight?

Lena

I ask questions. I ask a chauvinistic speaker what he actually knows about the people he dismisses. Usually a chauvinistic opinion rests on very little experience. Calm curiosity can soften even a very chauvinistic view, although not always, and not quickly.

Maya

Let us change the energy completely. Our next phrase is bulk up. My cousin decided to bulk up for a bodybuilding competition. To bulk up safely, he ate more protein, lifted heavier weights, and slept properly, because you cannot bulk up on ambition alone.

Lena

Athletes often bulk up in winter and then train differently in summer. A rugby player may bulk up to become stronger in tackles. But doctors warn that trying to bulk up too fast can harm your joints. If you want to bulk up, do it slowly, with expert advice, not with internet fads.

Maya

And when emotions rise, the body speaks first. Think of the phrase clench your fist. When Sam is angry in a meeting, he will clench his fist under the table so nobody sees. I clench my fist, well, I mean, the instruction is clench your fist when you feel tension, then release it and breathe.

Lena

Actors use that trick too. Before a frightening scene, an actor will clench his fist, hold it, and let go. In our lesson phrase, clench your fist describes that tight ball your hand makes. My little brother will clench your fist, sorry, he will clench his own fist, whenever thunder scares him, and I hold his hand until he relaxes.

Maya

Let us be precise for our learners, because precision matters at this level. The exact phrase is clench your fist. If a coach shouts clench your fist, he wants power and focus before a lift. If a novel says she tried not to clench her fist, we still learn the pattern clench your fist as the dictionary form to remember.

Lena

Good point. And notice how these phrases paint pictures. A blissfully happy bride, a blissfully ignorant tourist who has lost his map but keeps smiling, a blissfully unaware singer performing while her microphone is off. Each image stays in your memory far longer than a dry definition.

Maya

Exactly. The tourist who is blissfully ignorant of the local customs may offend people without meaning to. The singer who is blissfully unaware of the silent microphone may wonder why nobody applauds. And the friend who is blissfully happy about a small picnic reminds us that joy does not need luxury.

Lena

Before we go further, here is a second reminder from me, Maya this time in spirit. If you enjoy learning like this, the Muse English app is waiting for you, free, on your phone. Every day you get new words with photos and clear pronunciation, this podcast to listen to, shadowing stories that train your mouth and your ear, short quizzes that check what you remember, grammar lessons that finally make sense, and a leaderboard where your points grow as you learn. Install Muse English, open today's lesson, and keep your streak alive. Small steps every day beat big promises once a year.

Maya

Beautifully said, even if I am the one saying your lines today. Let us return to character. Is being chatty always a gift? A chatty person can fill an awkward silence at a funeral or a wedding. A chatty grandmother can keep family history alive. Yet the same chatty energy can reveal secrets that were never meant to be shared.

Lena

Balance is everything. I try to be chatty when people need warmth and quiet when they need space. Daniel, our chatty friend, is learning that too. He is still chatty, gloriously chatty, but now he listens first, and that makes his company even better.

Maya

And what about trying to bulk up as a metaphor? Writers bulk up a thin story with detail. Students bulk up an essay with examples. Of course our phrase bulk up belongs to the body first, but hearing bulk up in the gym helps you remember it everywhere.

Lena

I like that. A young player who wants to bulk up needs patience. A boxer who needs to bulk up for a heavier class needs a careful plan. Nobody should bulk up because strangers on the internet mock their body. Bulk up for health and strength, or do not bulk up at all.

Maya

Let us test our listeners gently. Close your eyes and picture each phrase. A grandfather blissfully happy on a bench in the sun. A manager blissfully ignorant of the surprise inspection tomorrow. A cat blissfully unaware that the dog is watching it steal food. Can you see them?

Lena

I can. And I can see a chauvinistic commentator praising only his own country, a chatty hairdresser who knows every story in town, and a nervous student who will clench your fist, I mean clench her fist, before an exam. Wait, for our phrase list, remember it as clench your fist, the you form, the instruction form.

Maya

Yes, clench your fist is the form to learn, and in a story you will also meet clench his fist and clench her fist. The meaning never changes. When anger rises, people clench your fist in imagination, they tighten their hand into a ball, and the body tells the truth before the mouth does.

Lena

So let us gather it all together. Blissfully happy is pure, complete joy. Blissfully ignorant is happiness that depends on not knowing something bad. Blissfully unaware is similar, happily not noticing something negative around you. They sound alike, but a careful speaker chooses between blissfully ignorant for knowledge and blissfully unaware for awareness in the moment.

Maya

And a blissfully happy person is not necessarily blissfully ignorant or blissfully unaware. You can know every difficulty and still be blissfully happy because you have solved them. You can be fully awake to the world and still feel blissfully happy on a good morning.

Lena

Meanwhile, chatty describes a love of talking, chauvinistic describes a belief that your own group is superior, bulk up describes gaining muscle and size, and clench your fist describes closing your hand tightly when emotion is strong. Seven vivid phrases, one very human lesson.

Maya

Say them once with us, slowly, in your head or out loud. Blissfully happy. Blissfully ignorant. Blissfully unaware. Bulk up. Chatty. Chauvinistic. Clench your fist.

Lena

Again, because repetition is how memory grows. Blissfully happy. Blissfully ignorant. Blissfully unaware. Bulk up. Chatty. Chauvinistic. Clench your fist.

Maya

That is all for today on Muse English. Be kind, be curious, notice how people feel, and keep learning a little every day. We will be back tomorrow with fresh words and fresh stories.

Lena

Goodbye, and good luck. Remember, a chatty conversation, a blissfully happy moment, and a brave new word are all waiting for you today.

📝گرامر: Concession: Albeit, Much As, Notwithstanding

Concession means accepting one fact while saying something that contrasts with it. You already know although, despite and in spite of.

At this level, use more precise formal forms. Say *The plan worked, albeit slowly*, or *Much as I like him, I don't trust him*.

Use albeit before an adjective, adverb or phrase, never a full clause. A chatty guide can be excellent, albeit expensive.

Use much as before a clause to mean although very much. Much as I wanted to stay blissfully happy, I had to hear the news.

Use notwithstanding before or after a noun i
