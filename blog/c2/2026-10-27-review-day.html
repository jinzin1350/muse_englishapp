10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
absolute poverty /AB-suh-loot POV-er-tee/
phrase
Lacking basic necessities like food, water, and shelter.
"Absolute poverty remains a global challenge."
artificial intelligence /ar-tuh-FISH-ul in-TEL-uh-junss/
noun
Computer systems performing tasks normally requiring human intelligence.
"Artificial intelligence is transforming many industries rapidly."
analogue /AN-uh-log/
adjective
Relating to a signal or data that is continuous.
"Many older devices still use analogue technology."
breathalyser /BRETH-uh-ly-zer/
noun
A device used to measure alcohol in breath.
"Police used a breathalyser to check for drunk driving."
besiege /buh-SEEJ/
verb
To surround a place with armed forces; overwhelm.
"The city was besieged by enemy forces for months."
AI /AY-EYE/
noun
Artificial intelligence; computer systems performing human-like tasks.
"AI is transforming many industries rapidly."
Bluetooth™ /BLOO-tooth/
noun
Wireless technology for short-range device communication.
"My headphones connect to my phone via Bluetooth."
budget airline /BUJ-it AIR-line/
noun phrase
An airline offering cheap flights with fewer services.
"We flew with a budget airline to save money."
beep your horn /BEEP yor HORN/
phrase
To make a short, loud sound with a car horn.
"Please don't beep your horn unnecessarily in residential areas."
berth /BURTH/
noun
A sleeping place on a ship, train, or plane.
"I booked a comfortable berth for the overnight train journey."

📝 Podcast Transcript

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! Today is review day, so instead of new words, we are going back over the words from the last three days and putting them to work in real conversation.

Lena

That sounds perfect. Reviewing is how words really stick, and we covered some great ones. Shall we start with travel? I still remember your trip story from last week.

Maya

Oh yes. I flew with a budget airline to save money, and the flight itself was fine, but the real adventure started after landing. I took an overnight train, and I had booked a comfortable berth for the night. Sleeping in a berth on a moving train is strange at first, but I loved it.

Lena

A berth on a train sounds romantic, like something from an old film. Did you sleep well in your berth?

Maya

Surprisingly well. The gentle rocking helped. In the morning, though, the traffic in the city was terrible, and drivers kept shouting at each other. One driver kept telling everyone not to beep your horn, which was funny, because he was the one doing it.

Lena

That is so typical of city traffic. Everyone is in a hurry, everyone is impatient, and a short trip across town can take an hour. I usually walk when I can, just to avoid that kind of morning.

Maya

Before we move on, a quick word about the Muse English app, because it makes review days like this one so much easier. Every day you get new words with photos and clear pronunciation, plus this podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar lessons, and a leaderboard where you collect points as you learn. It is free to install, and it turns a few quiet minutes into real progress, so give it a try and keep your streak going.

Lena

That is a good reminder. So, back to your trip. What happened after the traffic?

Maya

Well, there was a breathalyser checkpoint near the station. The police used a breathalyser on a few drivers, and it reminded me how seriously they take road safety there. Nobody argued with the breathalyser result, and I was glad to see it.

Lena

Good. And what about the countryside part of your trip? You rented a car, right?

Maya

I rented a four by four, a 4x4, because the mountain roads were rough. A 4x4 can handle mud and steep hills, and the views were awe-inspiring. Truly awe-inspiring mountains, with snow on the tops even in late autumn.

Lena

An awe-inspiring view is worth a rough road. Was it an all-inclusive holiday, or did you plan everything yourself?

Maya

I planned it myself. An all-inclusive holiday is easy, but I like choosing my own places. Still, I can see why an all-inclusive holiday suits families who just want to relax and not think about bookings.

Lena

Now let us talk about technology, because our recent words were full of it. Artificial intelligence is transforming many industries rapidly, and honestly, sometimes it feels like artificial intelligence is everywhere, from our phones to our cars.

Maya

It really is. A I is transforming many industries rapidly too, and I use A I tools every day for writing and planning. But A I still surprises me. One day it writes a perfect email, the next day it invents a fact.

Lena

And the hardware side is just as interesting. My headphones connect to my phone via Bluetooth, and Bluetooth makes life so simple. No cables, no tangles. Bluetooth just works, until it does not, and then I miss the old days.

Maya

Do you mean analogue technology? Many older devices still use analogue technology, and analogue sound has a warmth that digital sometimes lacks. I still have an analogue radio in my kitchen, and I love its analogue dial.

Lena

I love that. But newer tech raises bigger questions. Biotechnology is moving fast, and biotechnology could cure diseases we once thought incurable. At the same time, cloning makes people nervous. Cloning an animal is one thing, but cloning raises deep questions about identity.

Maya

And then there is the idea of a designer baby. A designer baby sounds like science fiction, but a designer baby is a serious ethical debate now. Should parents choose traits for a designer baby? Most people say no, and I agree.

Lena

Even ordinary things are changing. My new washing machine is a smart appliance, and this appliance sends me a message when the wash is done. One appliance talking to another appliance feels normal now, which is strange when you think about it.

Maya

It is strange, and it makes me wonder where it stops. My fridge already suggests recipes based on what is inside it. A fridge that talks to an oven, an oven that talks to a phone, and soon the whole kitchen will be chatting without us. I like the convenience, but I also like switching things off and cooking in peace.

Lena

I agree. Technology should serve us, not manage us. A smart home is wonderful until it needs an update in the middle of dinner, and then you miss the simple switch on the wall.

Maya

It is strange. Now, a second quick note about the Muse English app, because review days work best with a little daily habit. It gives you daily words with photos and pronunciation, this podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar lessons that build step by step, and points and a leaderboard to keep you motivated. It is free, it fits in your pocket, so install it and keep learning with us.

Lena

Let us turn to the harder words from our list. Absolute poverty remains a global challenge, and absolute poverty means lacking food, water, and shelter. When we talk about absolute poverty, we should remember it is not a statistic. It is real people, and ending absolute poverty needs real action.

Maya

Exactly. And history gives us strong words for pressure. The city was besieged by enemy forces for months. When a city is besieged, ordinary life stops. Food runs out, and people wait. To besiege a place is to cut it off from the world, and being besieged is terrifying.

Lena

That contrast always strikes me. On one side of the world, affluent neighborhoods with every comfort. Affluent families hiring private tutors and taking three holidays a year. Affluent does not mean happy, though. It only means wealthy.

Maya

True. And kindness shows up in smaller places. Last weekend I visited an animal shelter, an animal rescue shelter, where volunteers care for abandoned dogs. The animal rescue shelter was full, but every dog had a name and a blanket, and every volunteer knew their stories.

Lena

I admire the people who work there. My friend is an animal rights activist, and this animal rights activist spends weekends raising money for shelters. An animal rights activist believes every creature deserves care, and she lives that belief every single day.

Maya

One more word from our list, from the world of stories. I watched a film where soldiers plan an ambush. An ambush in the forest, quiet, tense, and then sudden. The ambush scene was the best part of the film, even though an ambush is a frightening thing in real life.

Lena

Films make everything dramatic. Let us wrap up with a quick second pass over the review words, so they really settle. A budget airline can save you money, but a comfortable berth on a night train is worth paying for. Never beep your horn without a reason, and remember a breathalyser keeps roads safer.

Maya

And on the tech side, artificial intelligence and A I are changing work, Bluetooth connects our devices, and analogue technology still has its charm. A budget airline, a berth, and a breathalyser all came from our travel days, and they fit together nicely in one journey.

Lena

And the serious words matter too. Absolute poverty is a challenge we must not ignore, and a city besieged is a reminder of how fragile peace is. When forces besiege a town, the world should pay attention, and ordinary people pay the highest price.

Maya

Let me add one more round, because repetition is the whole point of review day. If you fly with a budget airline, you save money for the rest of the trip. If you book a berth, you sleep while you travel. If someone tells you to beep your horn, ask yourself whether the noise will actually help anyone.

Lena

And if police stop you, a breathalyser test is quick and fair. If you love music, Bluetooth headphones are wonderful, but an analogue record player has a sound that digital files cannot copy. And artificial intelligence, A I for short, will keep changing how we work, so learning the word now is a smart move.

Maya

One last thought on the big words. Absolute poverty should trouble all of us, and a besieged city should move us to care. We cannot fix everything, but we can learn the words to talk about it clearly, and that is a start.

Lena

So that is our review. Ten words, three days, one conversation. Keep repeating them, use them in your own sentences, and they will be yours. Try writing a short paragraph tonight using five of them, and say it out loud tomorrow morning. That small habit turns a word you recognize into a word you own, and it takes less than ten minutes. Reviewing is not glamorous, but it works, and tomorrow you will feel the difference.

Maya

See you tomorrow, and happy reviewing.

📝Grammar: Fronting and Word Order for Emphasis

**Fronting** means moving part of a sentence to the **beginning** to give it emphasis or a dramatic effect. For example, *A castle stood on the hill* becomes ***On the hill stood** a castle*. Sometimes fronting also changes the word order of the subject and verb. This change is called **inversion**, and it is common in storytelling, speeches, and formal writing. After a fronted place or direction, a simple verb like **be**, **stand**, **lie**, **come**, or **go** can come **before a noun subject**.
