فناوری — درس انگلیسی C2

📅 2026-11-18📊 سطح C2 (تسلط کامل)📚 ۷ لغت تازه🎧 پادکست روزانه

📖 Muse English چیست؟

Muse English یک اپلیکیشن رایگان آموزش زبان انگلیسی است، با درس روزانه برای هر شش سطح، از A1 (مبتدی) تا C2 (تسلط کامل). هر روز لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، یک پادکست واقعی، تمرین شدوئینگ، گرامر و آزمون دارید. دربارهٔ اپ

📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
envisage /en-VIZ-ij/
فعل · verb
To imagine something as a future possibility.
تصور کردن، پیش‌بینی کردن
"We envisage a future where technology enhances learning."
extraterrestrial being /ex-truh-tuh-RES-tree-ul BEE-ing/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
A hypothetical intelligent life form from another planet.
موجود فرازمینی
"Scientists search for signs of extraterrestrial beings."
flat-screen TV /FLAT-skreen tee-VEE/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
A television with a thin, flat display screen.
تلویزیون صفحه تخت
"We bought a new flat-screen TV for the living room."
foresee /for-SEE/
فعل · verb
To know or predict something before it happens.
پیش‌بینی کردن
"We couldn't foresee the sudden change in weather."
games console /GAYMZ KON-sohl/
اسم · noun
An electronic device for playing video games.
کنسول بازی
"My brother got a new games console for his birthday."
gene therapy /JEEN THER-uh-pee/
عبارت · phrase
Treating disease by replacing faulty genes.
ژن‌درمانی
"Gene therapy offers hope for many genetic conditions."
genetic modification /juh-NET-ik mod-uh-fuh-KAY-shun/
عبارت · phrase
Altering an organism's genetic material for specific traits.
اصلاح ژنتیکی
"Genetic modification raises ethical concerns for some."

پادکست: فناوری

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today Lena and I are looking ahead, imagining the technology that might change our lives in the next twenty years.

Lena

Hello everyone, I am Lena. It is easy to envisage a world that looks very different from ours, and yet some changes are already beginning in our homes and hospitals.

Maya

When I envisage the future, I picture a living room where a flat-screen TV is almost as thin as paper, and where a games console can create a whole world around you while you sit on your sofa.

Lena

I love that picture. When experts try to foresee what will happen next, they often start with the flat-screen TV, because screens keep getting lighter, brighter and cheaper every year.

Maya

That is true. My parents bought their first flat-screen TV twenty years ago, and nobody could foresee that the same screen would one day let us talk to family on another continent.

Lena

And nobody could foresee how a simple games console would become a place to meet friends, learn languages and even exercise. My nephew does his homework rewards on his games console every evening.

Maya

Technology is not only about entertainment, of course. In medicine, gene therapy is giving hope to families who once had no treatment at all, because gene therapy can replace a faulty gene with a healthy one.

Lena

Gene therapy still feels like science fiction to many people. A doctor explained to me that gene therapy is carefully tested for years before patients receive it, and that careful testing is why gene therapy is slowly becoming safer.

Maya

That careful approach matters even more with genetic modification. Genetic modification means altering the genetic material of a living thing, and people disagree about where genetic modification should stop.

Lena

Some farmers welcome genetic modification because it can help crops survive drought, while others worry that genetic modification could harm nature in ways we cannot foresee.

Maya

And that word foresee keeps coming back. We can foresee some results of genetic modification in a laboratory, but we cannot foresee every result in a forest or a river over fifty years.

Lena

Exactly. Good science tries to envisage every risk before it acts. When researchers envisage a new treatment, they imagine the best outcome, the worst outcome and everything in between.

Maya

Speaking of imagining, let us take a short break to talk about something real that can help you today. Lena

That is a lovely reminder, Maya. Now, back to the future. Do you think we will ever meet an extraterrestrial being? Scientists who search the sky say an extraterrestrial being is still only a hypothesis, but they keep listening.

Maya

I find that search deeply moving. If an extraterrestrial being exists, then an extraterrestrial being might be looking at its own sky and wondering about us. The idea of an extraterrestrial being changes how small and precious our own planet feels.

Lena

And if an extraterrestrial being ever sent a message, we would need technology to receive it. That technology might begin with something ordinary, like a better flat-screen TV for showing the data, or a powerful games console processor doing the calculations at home.

Maya

That is a fun thought, that a games console in a teenager's bedroom might one day help decode a message from an extraterrestrial being. Teenagers would certainly envisage themselves as heroes of that story.

Lena

They would, and they would probably envisage a future where travel between planets is normal. Adults are more cautious. Adults ask what we can foresee, and the honest answer is that we can foresee very little about first contact.

Maya

Let us bring it back to Earth for a moment. A friend of mine works in a hospital where gene therapy is being studied for children with rare diseases. She says gene therapy is not magic, it is patient, detailed work, repeated test by test.

Lena

I admire that patience. Genetic modification raises similar questions in food. When scientists use genetic modification on rice or wheat, they must envisage how that plant will behave in real fields, not only in clean laboratories.

Maya

And consumers deserve clear answers. If genetic modification can reduce the need for chemical sprays, many people will welcome it, but they still want honest labels and long studies that foresee problems early.

Lena

Maya

Welcome back. Let us talk about the home again, because the home is where most of us first meet new technology. A flat-screen TV used to be a luxury, but now a flat-screen TV hangs in kitchens, classrooms and even small cafes.

Lena

And beside that flat-screen TV there is often a games console waiting for the evening. A games console is no longer just for children, because a games console now streams films, teaches fitness and connects grandparents with grandchildren.

Maya

My grandmother learned video calls on a flat-screen TV, and my little cousin learned problem solving on a games console, so both generations gained something real from devices we once called toys or luxuries.

Lena

That is why I try to envisage technology with people at the centre. When we envisage a kinder future, we ask who benefits, who is left out, and who pays the cost.

Maya

And when we foresee a problem, we can act early. Engineers who foresee that old screens create waste are now designing a flat-screen TV that is easier to repair and recycle.

Lena

The same thinking applies in biology. If we foresee that gene therapy will be expensive, we should envisage fair ways to share gene therapy with poorer families, not only with rich hospitals.

Maya

Beautifully said. Genetic modification will also need fairness. If genetic modification helps rich farms first, smaller farms may fall behind, and that is a social result we can foresee today.

Lena

So the big lesson is not really about gadgets. Whether we talk about an extraterrestrial being far away, or gene therapy in a local hospital, or genetic modification in a field, the questions are human questions.

Maya

I agree. An extraterrestrial being remains a dream for now, but the search for an extraterrestrial being teaches us patience, curiosity and humility, and those qualities help us here on Earth too.

Maya

Let me share a story from a school I visited last spring. The teachers there envisage a library where every child can explore space on a large screen, ask questions about distant planets, and write stories about the kind of world they want to build when they grow up.

Lena

That school sounds wonderful. Good teachers foresee that children learn best when they are curious, so they give them time to wonder, to make mistakes safely, and to try again with better questions and kinder teamwork.

Maya

In one classroom, the children were designing a healthy city. They talked about clean buses, quiet parks, safe cycle paths and homes that stay cool in summer, and they explained their plans with such confidence that the whole room listened in silence.

Lena

Stories like that remind me that technology alone is never the answer. A powerful device can spread a lie in seconds or bring a great teacher into a remote village, and the difference always comes down to the choices that real people make every day.

Maya

That is why conversations like ours matter. When learners hear careful English about science, ethics and the future, they gain more than vocabulary. They gain the ability to join an international discussion, to disagree politely, and to explain their own values clearly to the wider world.

Lena

And they gain confidence step by step. Nobody becomes fluent in a week, but a learner who practises a little every day, listening, shadowing, answering quizzes and reviewing old words, will be amazed after a few months at how much natural English they truly understand.

Maya

Exactly, Lena. So whether the future brings us astonishing medicine, astonishing screens, or even astonishing visitors from the stars, I hope it also brings us wisdom, because wisdom is the one technology that every generation must build again for itself, slowly and together.

Lena

Before we finish, let us recap. Today we learned to envisage, which means to imagine a future possibility, and to foresee, which means to know or predict something before it happens.

Maya

We talked about a flat-screen TV, a thin television, and a games console, a device for playing video games, and we explored gene therapy, treating disease by replacing faulty genes, and genetic modification, altering genetic material for specific traits.

Lena

And of course our most mysterious phrase was extraterrestrial being, a hypothetical intelligent life form from another planet. Thank you for listening to Muse English today, keep practising, keep imagining, and we will see you tomorrow with fresh words and a fresh conversation.
