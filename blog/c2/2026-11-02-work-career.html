📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
(an) ideal fit for /un eye-DEE-ul FIT for/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Perfectly suitable for a particular role or situation.
کاملاً مناسب، ایده آل
"Her skills make her an ideal fit for this position."
administration /ud-min-uh-STRAY-shun/
اسم · noun
The management of a business or organization.
اداره، مدیریت
"She works in the university administration department."
adoption leave /uh-DOP-shun LEEV/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Time off work for adopting a child.
مرخصی فرزندخواندگی
"He took adoption leave to bond with his new son."
affinity /uh-FIN-uh-tee/
اسم · noun
A natural liking or understanding of something.
خویشاوندی، علاقه
"He has a strong affinity for languages."
annual bonus /AN-yoo-ul BOH-nus/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
An extra payment given to employees once a year.
پاداش سالانه
"Employees received a generous annual bonus after a successful year."
anti-social /an-tee-SOH-shul/
صفت · adjective
Not wanting to socialize; harmful to society.
ضد اجتماعی، گوشه‌گیر
"His anti-social behavior made him unpopular at work."
assemble /uh-SEM-bul/
فعل · verb
To put together parts to create a whole.
مونتاژ کردن، جمع کردن
"Workers assemble the car engines on the production line."

📝گرامر: The Subjunctive (I insist that he be on time)

The subjunctive is used for things that are demanded, suggested or necessary, not facts. It always uses the base form of the verb, for every person.

After verbs like insist, suggest and demand, use that + base form. The administration insisted that every report be ready on time.

After adjectives like essential, vital and important, the same pattern follows. Never add -s for he, she or it.

For negatives, use not + base form. For passives, use be + past participle.
