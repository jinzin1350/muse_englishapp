7 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
academic journal /ak-uh-DEM-ik JUR-nul/
noun phrase
A periodical containing scholarly articles on a specific subject.
"She published her research in a prestigious academic journal."
access online /AK-ses ON-line/
verb phrase
To get information or services using the internet.
"Students can access course materials online."
adjoining /uh-JOY-ning/
adjective
Next to or in contact with.
"The library is in the adjoining building."
aforementioned /uh-FOR-men-shund/
adjective
Referring to something previously mentioned.
"The aforementioned study highlights key findings."
articulate (adj.) /ar-TIK-yuh-lit/
adjective
Expressing ideas clearly and fluently.
"She is a highly articulate speaker, always clear and concise."
assess /uh-SES/
verb
To evaluate or estimate the nature, ability, or quality.
"Teachers assess students' progress through various assignments."
assignment /uh-SYNE-munt/
noun
A piece of work or task given to students.
"Students must submit their history assignment by Friday."

📝Grammar: Hedging and Distancing (it would appear that...)

**Hedging** means making a statement **less direct or less certain**, so you do not claim more than you can prove. For example, instead of saying social media causes anxiety, a careful writer says the data **suggest** that social media **may contribute** to anxiety. **Distancing** means showing that a claim comes from someone else, not from you. For example, we say the CEO has **allegedly** resigned when the news is not yet confirmed. Useful hedging tools include **seem** and **appear**, phrases like **it would appear that**, modals such as **may**, **might** and **could**, the verb **tend
