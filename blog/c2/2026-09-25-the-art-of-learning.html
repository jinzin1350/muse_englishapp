pedagogy /PED-uh-go-jee/

adjective

"The erudite professor quoted sources in four languages without notes."
autodidact /aw-tuh-DY-dakt/
noun

"As an autodidact, she taught herself calculus before starting college."
matriculate /muh-TRIK-yuh-layt/
verb

"He matriculated at Oxford at the age of eighteen."

📝 Grammar: 2A: Present perfect and past simple

Use the **present perfect (have/has + past participle)** for past experiences when you do not say exactly when they happened. The *erudite* traveler and the humble *autodidact* both collect such dateless experiences. Use it for recent past actions whose result still matters now, often with **just**, **already**, or **yet**. Picture a new student about to *matriculate*, bags packed and heart racing. **Ever** and **never** belong with the present perfect when you ask about or deny life experiences.
