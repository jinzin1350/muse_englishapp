📚 Vocabulary

10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
contactless /KON-takt-less/
adjective
Payment method not requiring physical contact with a reader.
"I paid for my coffee using a contactless card."
articulate (adj.) /ar-TIK-yuh-lit/
adjective
Expressing ideas clearly and fluently.
"She is a highly articulate speaker, always clear and concise."
academic journal /ak-uh-DEM-ik JUR-nul/
noun phrase
A periodical containing scholarly articles on a specific subject.
"She published her research in a prestigious academic journal."
cover (v.) /KUV-er/
verb
To provide enough money to pay for something.
"Does your insurance cover dental treatment?"
adjoining /uh-JOY-ning/
adjective
Next to or in contact with.
"The library is in the adjoining building."
aversion /uh-VUR-zhun/
noun
Strong dislike or feeling of repugnance towards something.
"She has a strong aversion to public speaking."
blissfully /BLIS-fuh-lee/
adverb
In a state of perfect happiness.
"She smiled blissfully, enjoying the quiet morning."
bite your nails /BITE yaw NAYLZ/
idiom
To chew on one's fingernails, usually from nervousness.
"She tends to bite her nails when she's stressed."
charge to (a credit card) /CHARJ too uh KREH-dit KARD/
phrasal verb
To pay for something using a credit card.
"You can charge the purchase to your credit card."
bliss /BLIS/
noun
Perfect happiness or joy.
"Lying on the beach was pure bliss."

Bliss is perfect happiness, Lena, the kind of deep joy that makes everything feel right. When I think of bliss, I think of a quiet morning, a warm cup of tea, and no rush at all. That is pure bliss.

Lena

And when you feel that way, you smile blissfully, without even trying. She smiled blissfully as she walked through the park, enjoying the quiet morning. That is a beautiful image, is it not? To smile blissfully is to show happiness on your face.

Maya

It is. But not every feeling is happy. Sometimes we feel an aversion, a strong dislike, toward something. She has a strong aversion to public speaking, for example. That aversion makes her nervous before every talk.

Lena

Yes, and when she feels nervous, she starts to bite your nails, or rather, she starts to bite her nails without noticing. Many people bite their nails when they are stressed. If you bite your nails during an exam, it is a sign that you need a deep breath.

Maya

Exactly. Now, here is a quick word about the app that helps you keep all these words in your head.

Lena

If you love this podcast, you will love the Muse English app even more. Maya

Thanks, Lena. Let us keep reviewing. Yesterday we talked about study life. An academic journal is a magazine full of serious research articles, and reading an academic journal can feel hard at first. But an academic journal is also where real discoveries are published, so it is worth the effort.

Lena

And to read an academic journal well, you need to be articulate. An articulate speaker explains ideas clearly and fluently. She is a highly articulate speaker, always clear and concise, and her classmates love listening to her. Being articulate is a skill you build by practising out loud.

Maya

That is true. And practising is easier when you can access online materials. Students can access course materials online from anywhere, and when you access online lessons every day, you never fall behind. The library is in the adjoining building, by the way, so if you need a quiet room, the adjoining room is perfect for study.

Lena

I remember the aforementioned study, the one we talked about yesterday. The aforementioned study highlights key findings about sleep and memory. When a writer says aforementioned, they mean something mentioned before, so the aforementioned idea is easy to follow if you read carefully.

Maya

Teachers assess students through assignments, and they assess progress fairly when they look at the whole term. An assignment is a piece of work given to students, and every assignment teaches you something new. Students must submit their history assignment by Friday, and a good assignment makes you think, not just memorise.

Maya

Let me tell you about Sara, a student who uses all these words in one week. Sara had an assignment about the adjoining library, and she wanted her presentation to sound articulate. She read an academic journal in the adjoining building, and she took notes in a quiet corner there.

Lena

Sara also had an aversion to speaking in front of a large class, and that aversion made her bite her nails before every presentation. She would bite her nails while she waited, and then she would breathe deeply and smile blissfully when the talk went well. To smile blissfully after hard work is the best reward.

Maya

Sara learned to access online articles quickly, and when she could access online sources, her research became much faster. The aforementioned study helped her a lot, and she quoted the aforementioned results in her talk. Her teacher will assess her talk on Monday, and when teachers assess fairly, students feel calm.

That is a lovely story, Maya. It shows how review words live in real life, not just in a list.

Let us pause for a moment, because there is something else I want to tell our listeners about learning with us.

The Muse English app is your daily English companion, and it is completely free. Lena

Wonderful. Now back to our review, and back to money, because money words can be stressful too. Imagine Sara again, this time at a cafe near the adjoining library. She pays with a contactless card, and the contactless reader beeps in one second. She smiles blissfully, because a contactless payment saves time when she is in a hurry.

Maya

Sara is articulate when she orders, and she explains her order clearly, like an articulate speaker in a cafe. She reads an academic journal at the table, and the academic journal article is about student budgets. Her budget must cover her books, and to cover her books she saves a little every week. A good plan will cover her travel too.

Lena

But Sara has an aversion to debt, a strong aversion that keeps her careful. That aversion is healthy, because debt can grow fast. She does not bite her nails about money any more. She used to bite her nails when bills arrived, but now she checks her balance calmly and she smiles blissfully at her progress.

Maya

And what is bliss for Sara? Bliss is a quiet evening with no bills to worry about. Pure bliss is a walk after a good study day. Let us keep going with the money words now. I paid for my coffee using a contactless card this morning. A contactless payment is quick because a contactless card does not need to touch the reader. Contactless payment is now everywhere, and I love how simple contactless paying feels.

Maya

It is simple, until you charge to a credit card without thinking. You can charge the purchase to your credit card, but if you charge to a credit card every day, the bill grows fast. It is better to charge to a credit card only when you have a plan to pay it back.

Lena

And does your insurance cover dental treatment? To cover something means to provide enough money to pay for it. Your savings should cover your rent, and a good budget will cover all your basic costs. If your insurance does not cover the treatment, you need another plan.

Maya

Money trouble can make anyone feel aloof, distant and quiet, and some people stay aloof from friends when money is tight. He remained aloof from the office gossip, but he was not unfriendly, just worried. He is not averse to taking risks, by the way, although he is averse to borrowing money from friends.

Lena

That is an important difference. Being averse to debt is wise. My cheque bounced once because I forgot to deposit money, and when a cheque bounces, the bank returns it unpaid. I felt completely broke that week. I cannot go out, I am completely broke, I told my friends, and they understood.

Maya

The government decided to cancel debt for developing nations in that story, and to cancel debt means to officially stop the requirement to repay it. When a country can cancel debt, it can spend on schools instead. Slowly, the country is coming out of recession, and to come out of recession means to recover from economic decline. Coming out of recession takes time, but it brings hope.

Lena

And hope brings us back to blissful feelings. They spent a blissful honeymoon in the Maldives, full of joy and calm. A blissful day is a day with no stress, and a blissful life is built from small happy moments. Lying on the beach was pure bliss, and that bliss stays in your memory for years.

Maya

Let us recap our ten review words one more time, slowly. Contactless, articulate, academic journal, cover, adjoining, aversion, blissfully, bite your nails, charge to a credit card, and bliss. Say them with us. Contactless payment, an articulate speaker, an academic journal, to cover a cost, an adjoining building, a strong aversion, to smile blissfully, to bite your nails, to charge to a credit card, and pure bliss.

Lena

And do not forget the other words from this week, because they belong in your review too. Aloof, averse, blissful, access online, aforementioned, assess, assignment, bounce, broke, cancel debt, and come out of recession. Every one of those words is now part of your English.

That is our review for today. Come back tomorrow for a brand new lesson. Thank you for listening to Muse English, and see you next time.

See you next time, and happy learning.

📝Grammar: Advanced Cohesion and Discourse Markers

**Cohesion** is what makes a text flow: the words that link sentences and show how ideas relate. **Discourse markers** like **moreover**, **consequently** and **admittedly** tell the reader what kind of idea comes next. At C2, choose the **exact** marker for the relationship, and punctuate it correctly.
