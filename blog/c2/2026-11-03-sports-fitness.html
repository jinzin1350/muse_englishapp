📝گرامر: The Unreal Past (It's time we left, I'd rather you didn't)

After certain expressions, we use a past tense to talk about the present or future. The past form shows that the situation is unreal, imagined, or merely wished for.

The core pattern is it's time + subject + past: *It's time we went home.* We have not gone yet, but we should. Adding high or about sharpens the criticism, as in *It's about time you got moving.*

With a different subject, would rather also takes the past: *I'd rather you didn't smoke here.* With the same subject, use the base form instead: *I'd rather stay at home.*
