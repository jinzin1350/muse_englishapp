۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
credit card fraud /KRED-it kard frawd/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Illegal use of someone else's credit card information.
کلاهبرداری کارت اعتباری
"He was a victim of credit card fraud last month."
debit card /DEB-it kard/
اسم · noun
A card used to pay directly from a bank account.
کارت بانکی، کارت بدهی
"I paid for groceries with my debit card."
debt burden /det BUR-dun/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
The total amount of debt owed by a person or country.
بار بدهی
"Many developing nations struggle with a heavy debt burden."
debt relief /det ri-LEEF/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
The partial or total cancellation of debts.
بخشودگی بدهی
"The organization advocates for debt relief for poor countries."
debtor country /DET-er KUN-tree/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
A country that owes money to other countries.
کشور بدهکار
"The debtor country sought an extension on its loan repayments."
debtor nation /DET-er NAY-shun/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
A nation that owes money to other nations.
ملت بدهکار
"The debtor nation faced economic challenges due to its loans."
deep poverty /deep POV-er-tee/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Extreme lack of money and basic necessities.
فقر شدید
"Millions of people worldwide live in deep poverty."

A debt burden is the total weight of what a person or a country owes. When interest rises, a heavy debt burden can crush a government budget.

Lena

Maya

Lena

Lena

Lena

📝گرامر: Ellipsis and Substitution

Natural English avoids repeating words. We either replace them with a short word, which is substitution, or leave them out, which is ellipsis.

Use one for a singular countable noun and ones for a plural noun. A shopper who fears credit card fraud might say: this debit card is blocked, can I use the other one?

Use do / does / did to replace a verb phrase. She checks her account more carefully than I do.

In formal English, do so replaces an action. A debtor country that wishes to request debt relief should do so in writing.

Use so for a positiv
