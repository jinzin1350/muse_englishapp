📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
desktop /DESK-top/
اسم · noun
A computer designed for use on a desk.
کامپیوتر رومیزی
"My new desktop has a very powerful processor."
destiny /DES-tuh-nee/
اسم · noun
The events that will happen to a person or thing.
سرنوشت، تقدیر
"She believes it was her destiny to become an artist."
digital technology /DIJ-uh-tul tek-NOL-uh-jee/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Electronic tools and systems that process data digitally.
فناوری دیجیتال
"Digital technology has transformed how we communicate globally."
disease-resistant /duh-ZEEZ ruh-ZIS-tunt/
صفت · adjective
Able to withstand or fight off diseases.
مقاوم به بیماری
"Scientists are developing disease-resistant crops to improve yields."
doomsday scenario /DOOMZ-day suh-NAIR-ee-oh/
اسم · noun
A prediction of a catastrophic future event or disaster.
سناریوی فاجعه‌بار، آخرالزمانی
"Experts warned of a doomsday scenario if climate change continues."
drastic measures /DRAS-tik MEZH-erz/
عبارت · phrase
Extreme and severe actions taken to solve a problem.
اقدامات شدید، تدابیر جدی
"Drastic measures are needed to combat climate change."
e-reader /EE-ree-der/
اسم · noun
An electronic device for reading digital books.
کتاب‌خوان الکترونیکی
"My e-reader holds hundreds of books."

Lena

Maya

I love paper books, but honestly, my e-reader gets more use these days. My e-reader holds hundreds of books, and it fits in my bag. When I travel, my e-reader is the first thing I pack.

Lena

I know what you mean. An e-reader is light, the screen is gentle on the eyes, and you can look up a word in seconds. But here is the interesting part. The e-reader did not kill the paper book. It just gave readers a new choice.

Maya

Exactly. And that is the pattern with digital technology. It rarely destroys the old thing completely. It changes it. The desktop did not destroy the office. The e-reader did not destroy the library. They changed how we live, quietly, day by day.

Lena

Which brings us back to destiny. If the tools around us change how we spend our days, then they also change who we become. That is why the choices we make about technology matter so much.

Maya

Now, let's move from the office to the farm, because digital technology is changing farming too, in ways most people never see.

Lena

Farming? That feels far away from my desktop. What does technology have to do with a field of wheat?

Maya

A lot, actually. Scientists are now developing disease-resistant crops. A disease-resistant plant is one that can withstand or fight off diseases, so farmers lose less of their harvest.

Lena

So a disease-resistant crop is like a plant with a strong immune system?

Maya

That is a good way to think about it. Researchers study the genes of a plant, and they look for the traits that make one variety disease-resistant while another one is not. Then they breed those strengths into the next generation of crops.

Lena

And where does digital technology come in?

Maya

Everywhere. Computers analyse the genetic data. Satellites watch the fields from above. Sensors in the soil send information to a farmer's desktop every hour. Without digital technology, none of this would be fast enough to help a real farm.

Lena

That is amazing. So a disease-resistant crop is not just a lucky accident. It is the result of data, science, and a lot of patient work.

Maya

Exactly. And think about what that means for the world. If more crops are disease-resistant, farmers in poor countries lose less food, prices stay steadier, and fewer people go hungry. One small change in a seed can change the destiny of a whole village.

Lena

Destiny again. I like how that word keeps coming back. We often think destiny is something that happens to us, but here, scientists are shaping it.

Maya

Yes, and that is the hopeful side of technology. But there is also a darker conversation we need to have, and it is about the climate.

Lena

I know where this is going. The doomsday scenario.

Maya

Yes, the doomsday scenario. That phrase means a prediction of a catastrophic future event, a disaster so big it changes everything. You hear it in the news all the time. A doomsday scenario for the climate, a doomsday scenario for the oceans, a doomsday scenario for food and water.

Lena

And honestly, Maya, when I hear about a doomsday scenario, my first reaction is to switch off. It feels too big and too frightening.

Maya

I understand that. But here is the thing. A doomsday scenario is a warning, not a promise. Scientists describe the worst case so that we can avoid it. The question is what we do with the warning.

Lena

Let me pause here with a reminder about the Muse English app. Every day the app gives you seven new words with photos and clear pronunciation, plus this podcast, a shadowing story to speak along with, a quiz to test yourself, and a grammar lesson to build your accuracy. Maya

Thank you, Lena. So what do we do? What would actually help?

Lena

Give me an example. What counts as drastic measures?

Maya

Experts say we need drastic measures. Drastic measures are extreme and severe actions taken to solve a problem. Not small changes, not gentle suggestions, but drastic measures.

Lena

That sounds almost impossible. Do drastic measures ever actually work?

Maya

They do, but only when people understand why they matter. Think back to the disease-resistant crops. Nobody called those drastic measures, but the science behind them took decades. Big change usually starts with small, patient work.

Lena

And digital technology helps there too, right? The same computers that sit on a desktop can model the climate, predict floods, and help cities prepare.

Maya

Exactly. The same digital technology that warns us about a doomsday scenario can also help us prevent one. The tools are neutral. The destiny is in how we use them.

Lena

So, Maya, we have talked about the desktop and the e-reader, about disease-resistant crops, about the doomsday scenario and drastic measures. What ties all of this together?

Maya

For me, it is that word we started with: destiny. We began by asking how much of our destiny is shaped by digital technology. And now I think the answer is clear. Technology shapes our destiny, but it does not decide it alone.

Lena

I agree. A desktop is just a machine until a person sits down and uses it to learn something new. An e-reader is just a device until a reader opens it and discovers a new idea.

Maya

And a disease-resistant crop is just a seed until a farmer plants it, waters it, and trusts the science behind it. The technology matters, but the human choice matters more.

Lena

That is a hopeful way to look at it. We are not passengers in a doomsday scenario. We are the people who decide whether that scenario ever happens.

Maya

Right. Drastic measures sound frightening, but every drastic measure starts with one person deciding to act. One city, one company, one family, one reader with an e-reader learning something new.

Lena

Let's bring it back to our own lives for a moment. Think about your own desktop. What do you use it for? Is it helping you become the person you want to be?

Maya

That is a good question to ask yourself. If your desktop is full of distractions, your days will drift. If your desktop is full of tools for learning, your days will build towards something.

Lena

And the same is true of an e-reader. My e-reader holds hundreds of books, but the books only matter if I open them. The device cannot read for me. My destiny is in my own hands, one page at a time.

Maya

So the lesson of today is simple. Digital technology is powerful, but it is not destiny by itself. Destiny is what happens when people take powerful tools and use them with purpose.

Lena

Beautifully said. Let's recap our seven words before we finish. A desktop is a computer designed for use on a desk. Destiny is the events that will happen to a person, the path of their life. Digital technology means the electronic tools and systems that process data digitally.

Maya

A disease-resistant plant can withstand or fight off diseases. A doomsday scenario is a prediction of a catastrophic future event. Drastic measures are extreme and severe actions taken to solve a problem. And an e-reader is an electronic device for reading digital books.

Lena

📝گرامر: Probability: Bound To, May Well, Likely To

English has many ways to say how likely something is. Beyond will, might and probably, you can say bound to, may well or likely to. For example, digital technology is likely to change how we read.

Think of a scale from almost certain to impossible. At the top, be bound to, be sure to and be certain to mean something will almost certainly happen.
