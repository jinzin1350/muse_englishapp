all the ingredients of /awl thee in-GREE-dee-unts uv/
اصطلاح · idiom
All the necessary qualities for something to happen.
تمام عناصر لازم
"The team has all the ingredients of a champion."
(an) ideal fit for /un eye-DEE-ul FIT for/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Perfectly suitable for a particular role or situation.
کاملاً مناسب، ایده آل
"Her skills make her an ideal fit for this position."
convenience food /kun-VEEN-yuns food/
عبارت · phrase
Pre-prepared food requiring little cooking.
غذای آماده
"Busy people often rely on convenience food for quick meals."
brisk /BRISK/
صفت · adjective
Quick and energetic.
سریع و پرانرژی، تند
"We took a brisk walk to warm up."
allergic /uh-LUR-jik/
صفت · adjective
Having an adverse physical reaction to certain substances.
حساسیت داشتن، آلرژیک
"She is allergic to peanuts."
anti-social /an-tee-SOH-shul/
صفت · adjective
Not wanting to socialize; harmful to society.
ضد اجتماعی، گوشه‌گیر
"His anti-social behavior made him unpopular at work."
allergy /AL-er-jee/
اسم · noun
A damaging immune response to a substance.
آلرژی، حساسیت
"His severe nut allergy requires an EpiPen."
administration /ud-min-uh-STRAY-shun/
اسم · noun
The management of a business or organization.
اداره، مدیریت
"She works in the university administration department."
diabetes /dy-uh-BEE-teez/
اسم · noun
A medical condition affecting blood sugar levels.
دیابت، بیماری قند
"Regular exercise helps manage diabetes effectively."
battery farming /BAT-uh-ree FAR-ming/
اسم · noun
Keeping farm animals in small cages for mass production.
مرغداری صنعتی (قفس)
"Many consumers oppose battery farming due to ethical concerns."

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! Hello everyone, and welcome to our review day episode. I am Maya, and I am here with Lena.

Lena

Hi everyone! Today is a special day, because we are not learning brand new words. We are looking back at the words from the last three days, and we are going to use them all again in real conversation.

Maya

Exactly. Over the last three days we talked about food and cooking, about work and careers, and about sports and fitness. Today we bring all of that together in one story, because real life mixes food, work, and health every single day.

Lena

Let us start with food, because food is where so many of our words live. Maya, do you cook at home, or do you mostly eat convenience food?

Maya

I try to cook, Lena, but honestly, on busy evenings I eat convenience food more than I want to admit. Convenience food is pre-prepared food that needs very little cooking, and when I am tired after work, convenience food feels like the easy answer.

Lena

I understand that completely. Convenience food saves time, but it is not always the healthiest choice. Some convenience food is full of salt and fat, and that is where a word like allergy comes in. If you have an allergy, you must read every label, because an allergy is a damaging immune response to a substance, and one wrong ingredient can trigger it.

Maya

That is so important. My cousin has an allergy to peanuts, and her allergy means she can never just grab a snack without checking. She once told me, my allergy decides what I can eat before I even look at the menu.

Lena

And that connects to another word: allergic. To be allergic means having an adverse physical reaction to certain substances. If you are allergic to peanuts, your body reacts badly, even to a tiny amount. So a person who is allergic to something has to be careful everywhere, at home, at restaurants, and at work.

Maya

At work! That brings us to our second theme. Imagine a company canteen, run by the administration of a big office. The administration is the management of a business or organization, and a good administration thinks about the people who eat there every day.

Lena

Yes, and a thoughtful administration asks the staff about their needs. Are you allergic to anything? Do you have an allergy we should know about? Those two questions can make lunch safe for everyone.

Maya

And the canteen itself needs someone who is an ideal fit for the job. The head cook should be an ideal fit for a busy kitchen, someone who understands both flavour and safety. When a cook is an ideal fit for the role, the whole team relaxes.

Lena

What makes someone an ideal fit for a job like that? I think the job needs all the ingredients of good cooking and good management. All the ingredients of success, really: skill, patience, and care for people.

Maya

I love that phrase. All the ingredients of a great canteen are not just pots and recipes. All the ingredients of a happy workplace include safe food, kind colleagues, and a little time to enjoy lunch together.

Lena

Maya

Thank you, Lena! Now, back to our canteen. The new cook, Sara, was perfect for the job, but one colleague, Tom, worried her. Tom always ate alone at his desk, and some people called him anti-social. To be anti-social means not wanting to socialize, or behaving in a way that is harmful to society, but Tom was not harmful at all.

Lena

He was just shy, right? Calling someone anti-social can be unfair. An anti-social label sticks fast, but an anti-social person in the strict sense avoids people on purpose, while Tom simply felt nervous in big groups.

Maya

Exactly. Sara noticed, and she decided not to treat Tom as anti-social at all. She invited him to help her assemble the new salad bar. To assemble means to put together parts to create a whole, and as they worked side by side, Tom started to talk.

Lena

That is a lovely picture. They assemble the salad bar together, they taste the dressings together, and slowly Tom stops eating alone. Food brings people together, and that is the opposite of anti-social behaviour.

Maya

Meanwhile, the administration had another idea: a lunchtime walking club. The administration announced it on Monday, and the administration promised a small annual bonus for the team that walked the most steps. An annual bonus is an extra payment given to employees once a year, and even a small annual bonus can motivate people.

Lena

A walking club is brilliant, because a brisk walk is one of the best habits there is. Brisk means quick and energetic, so a brisk walk wakes you up. A brisk walk at lunch also helps you burn calories, and when you burn calories in the middle of the day, you feel lighter all afternoon.

Maya

And a brisk walk is good for your cardiovascular health. Cardiovascular means relating to the heart and blood vessels, so anything that supports your cardiovascular system protects your future. Even a brisk walk of twenty minutes counts as real cardiovascular exercise.

Lena

Sara joined the walking club, and she started checking her BMI too. BMI means Body Mass Index, a measure of body fat, and Sara learned that BMI is only one number among many. Doctors use BMI to assess a healthy weight range, but BMI never tells the whole story.

Maya

True. What matters more is the daily pattern. Sara also has a friend with diabetes. Diabetes is a medical condition affecting blood sugar levels, and her friend manages diabetes with regular exercise and careful meals. Regular exercise helps manage diabetes effectively, and a short walk after lunch really helps her friend keep diabetes under control.

Lena

Food matters for diabetes as well. Cholesterol-rich foods can be a problem, because cholesterol-rich means containing a high amount of cholesterol, and too many cholesterol-rich meals can clog arteries over time. To clog means to block something, preventing movement or flow, and nobody wants clogged arteries.

Maya

So Sara changed the canteen menu. She cut back on cholesterol-rich dishes, she added fresh vegetables, and she checked every label for one additive after another. An additive is a substance added to food to improve it, but many processed foods contain artificial additives that nobody needs.

Lena

She also thought about animal welfare. Animal welfare means the physical and mental well-being of animals, and Sara refused to buy eggs from battery farming. Battery farming means keeping farm animals in small cages for mass production, and many consumers oppose battery farming due to ethical concerns.

Maya

Battery farming was a big topic in the canteen that week. Sara explained that battery farming keeps costs low, but battery farming also raises serious questions about animal welfare. In the end, the administration agreed to pay a little more for eggs from farms that respect animal welfare.

Lena

That is a wonderful result. And Sara has an affinity for this kind of cooking, by the way. Affinity means a natural liking or understanding of something, and Sara has a strong affinity for simple, honest food. Her affinity for fresh ingredients shows in every dish.

Maya

She also cares about people beyond the kitchen. When a colleague took adoption leave, Sara cooked meals for the new family. Adoption leave means time off work for adopting a child, and Sara said that adoption leave is a precious time that deserves support, not just paperwork.

Lena

Maya

Thank you! Now, let us pull the whole story together. The canteen, the walking club, and the new menu had all the ingredients of a real change. All the ingredients of a healthier office were there: a caring administration, a cook who was an ideal fit for her role, and colleagues who looked out for each other.

Lena

And Tom? Tom is not anti-social anymore. He walks with the club, he eats with the team, and he even leads the brisk walk on Fridays. A brisk walk changed his day, and a brisk lunch routine changed his friendships too.

Maya

And the lessons for us? If you are allergic to something, say so, and ask for what you need. If you have an allergy, remember that an allergy is manageable when people around you understand it.

Lena

If you live with diabetes, keep moving, keep checking, and remember that small steps help manage diabetes day by day. And if you eat convenience food sometimes, do not feel guilty. Just balance it, read the labels, and add a brisk walk when you can.

Maya

Beautiful. And we also met our other words again along the way: additive, animal welfare, adoption leave, affinity, annual bonus, assemble, BMI, burn calories, cardiovascular, cholesterol-rich, and clog. Review them, say them out loud, and use them this week.

📝گرامر: Nominalisation in Formal English

Nominalisation means expressing an action or quality as a noun instead of a verb or adjective. Prices rose sharply becomes a sharp rise in prices.

It is typical of academic, business and news writing. It packs more information into fewer words and sounds more objective.

To nominalise, turn the verb into a noun and the adverb into an adjective. The company expanded rapidly becomes the company's rapid expansion.
