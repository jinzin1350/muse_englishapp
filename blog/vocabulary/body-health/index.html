Body & Health Vocabulary

This page collects 87 English health words. Every word comes with a real photo, pronunciation audio, a clear meaning and an example sentence, so you can see and hear each word in context. Each card links to the daily lesson where the word was taught, so you can read the full conversation and practice shadowing.

Words are grouped by topic: Body Parts (14 words), Illnesses & Symptoms (33 words), At the Doctor & Hospital (11 words), Healthy Lifestyle & Habits (14 words), Mental Health & Wellbeing (5 words) and Medicine & Public Health (10 words). They cover all six CEFR levels, from beginner words like fever, hospital, medicine to advanced words like debilitating, ailment, convalescence.
