یک گفت‌وگوی واقعی گوش کنید

همین لغت‌ها و عبارت‌ها را در یک گفت‌وگوی طبیعی بشنوید.

Maya: You don't look well. What's wrong?
Lena: I have a terrible headache and I feel dizzy.
Maya: Oh no! Did you see a doctor?
Lena: Yes, this morning. She said I need to rest for two days.
Maya: Did she give you any medicine?
Lena: Yes, I need to take these pills twice a day.
Maya: Good. Drink lots of water and get some sleep.
Lena: Thanks! I hope I feel better soon.
