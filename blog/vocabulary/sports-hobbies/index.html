یک گفت‌وگوی واقعی گوش کنید

همین لغت‌ها و عبارت‌ها را در یک گفت‌وگوی طبیعی بشنوید.

Lena: Do you want to play tennis this Saturday?
Maya: Sure! I haven't played in weeks.
Lena: Me neither. Let's warm up first, okay?
Maya: Good idea. I go running every morning, so I'm ready!
Lena: Show-off! What's the score so far?
Maya: Zero zero, we haven't started yet!
Lena: Ha! Okay, let's play. Loser buys coffee!
Maya: You're on!
