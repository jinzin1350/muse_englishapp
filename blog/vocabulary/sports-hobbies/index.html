Sports Words

Common words you'll hear and use in real sports & hobbies situations.

ball
noun
The ball is round.
coach
noun
The coach teaches us every day.
goal
noun
He kicks the ball into the goal.
jump
verb
The boy can jump very high.
match
noun
We watched a football match.
play
verb
We play football after school.
player
noun
The player is very fast.
run
verb
I run in the park every morning.
score
verb
She can score a goal.
sport
noun
Football is my favorite sport.
swim
verb
I swim in the pool.
team
noun
Our team is very good.
win
verb
Our team wants to win today.
century
noun
The batsman scored a century in the match.
practice
verb
We practice free throws every day.
footballer
noun
He dreams of becoming a famous footballer.
go swimming
verb
We often go swimming on Saturdays.
ice hockey
noun
My brother loves to play ice hockey in winter.
agility
noun
Tennis players need agility as well as power.
foul
noun

penalty
noun

rivalry
noun

sprint

technique
noun

Fitness & Gym Words

Common words you'll hear and use in real sports & hobbies situations.

exercise
noun
I do exercise every morning.
balance
noun
Good balance helps in yoga.
stretch
verb
Stretch before you run.
do exercise
phrase
I try to do exercise every morning.
gym
noun
I go to the gym every morning.
cardio
noun
Swimming is great cardio for burning fat.
endurance
noun
Running builds endurance.
flex
verb
He flexed his arm to show the muscle.
interval
noun
Interval training burns calories fast.
routine
noun
She follows a strict gym routine.
stamina
noun
She has amazing stamina. She ran the whole game without getting tired.
strength
noun
He built strength with heavy squats.
workout
noun
My morning workout includes running and push-ups.
brisk
adjective
We took a brisk walk to warm up.
burn calories
phrase
Running is an excellent way to burn calories quickly.
regimen
noun
Her dawn training regimen includes ninety minutes of swimming before breakfast.
calisthenics
noun
Calisthenics like pull-ups and dips built his upper-body strength.
deload
verb
After three heavy weeks, he plans to deload with easy sessions.
ergogenic
adjective
Caffeine is one of the few supplements with proven ergogenic effects.
hypertrophy
noun
After months of heavy lifting, he noticed real hypertrophy in his shoulders.
macrocycle
noun
His coach mapped the whole season as one twelve-week macrocycle.
plyometrics
noun
Her coach added plyometrics like box jumps to sharpen her sprint starts.
taper
verb
She will taper her mileage in the final two weeks before the marathon.

Listen to a Real Conversation

Hear these words and phrases in a natural conversation.

Lena: Do you want to play tennis this Saturday?
Maya: Sure! I haven't played in weeks.
Lena: Me neither. Let's warm up first, okay?
Maya: Good idea. I go running every morning, so I'm ready!
Lena: Show-off! What's the score so far?
Maya: Zero zero, we haven't started yet!
Lena: Ha! Okay, let's play. Loser buys coffee!
Maya: You're on!
