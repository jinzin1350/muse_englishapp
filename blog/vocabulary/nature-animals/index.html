Animals Words

Common words you'll hear and use in real nature & animals situations.

bird
noun
The bird is in the tree.
wildlife
noun
We saw lots of wildlife on our trip to the national park.
migrate
verb
Many birds migrate south for the winter.
extinction
noun
Conservationists are racing to prevent the extinction of the vaquita.
invasive
adjective
Invasive species threaten native wildlife across the islands.
trophic cascade
noun
The wolves' return had triggered a trophic cascade that reshaped the valley's plant life.

Listen to a Real Conversation

Hear these words and phrases in a natural conversation.

Maya: It's such a beautiful day! Let's go for a walk in the park.
Lena: Great idea! Look at those flowers, they're blooming!
Maya: And look at that bird! It's building a nest.
Lena: I love spring. Everything feels alive.
Maya: Me too. But be careful, the path is slippery after the rain.
Lena: Thanks! Do you come here often?
Maya: Every weekend. It's my favorite place to relax.
Lena: I can see why!
