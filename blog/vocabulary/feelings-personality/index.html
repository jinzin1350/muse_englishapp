Listen to a Real Conversation

Hear these words and phrases in a natural conversation.

Lena: You look so happy today! What's going on?
Maya: I'm so excited! I got the job!
Lena: That's amazing! Congratulations!
Maya: Thanks! Honestly, I was a bit nervous during the interview.
Lena: Don't worry, you did great. When do you start?
Maya: Next Monday. I'm feeling a little tired but very happy.
Lena: You should celebrate tonight!
Maya: Good idea! Let's go out for dinner!
