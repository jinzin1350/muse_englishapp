یک گفت‌وگوی واقعی گوش کنید

همین لغت‌ها و عبارت‌ها را در یک گفت‌وگوی طبیعی بشنوید.

Maya: Hi! Welcome to our store. Are you looking for anything special today?
Lena: Hi! Yes, I'm looking for a birthday gift for my friend.
Maya: Great! How much would you like to spend?
Lena: Around fifty dollars. Do you have anything on sale?
Maya: Yes! This bag is on sale today. The price was eighty dollars, but now it's only forty-nine ninety-nine.
Lena: That's a good discount! Can I pay by credit card?
Maya: Of course. Would you like a receipt?
Lena: Yes, please. Thank you so much!
