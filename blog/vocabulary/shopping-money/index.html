Shopping & Money Vocabulary

This page collects 100 English shopping words. Every word comes with a real photo, pronunciation audio, a clear meaning and an example sentence, so you can see and hear each word in context. Each card links to the daily lesson where the word was taught, so you can read the full conversation and practice shadowing.

Words are grouped by topic: Shops & Stores (7 words), Shopping (33 words), Money & Banking (17 words), Personal Finance (23 words), Advertising & Marketing (1 word) and Economy & Globalisation (19 words). They cover all six CEFR levels, from beginner words like bakery, market, shop to advanced words like affluent, diversification, hedge.
