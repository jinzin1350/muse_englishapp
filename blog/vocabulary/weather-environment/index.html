Weather Words

Common words you'll hear and use in real weather & environment situations.

cloud
noun
The cloud is white.
cloudy
adjective
It's a cloudy day, so no sun.
rain
noun
I like the rain.
sky
noun
The sky is blue today.
sun
noun
The sun is hot today.
wind
The wind is strong today.
breeze
noun
A cool breeze blew through the trees.
calm
adjective
The sea was calm for our boat trip.
climate
noun
The climate in Canada is cold in winter.
monsoon
noun
Farmers wait for the monsoon before they plant rice.
thaw
verb
The lake will thaw when the warm spring winds arrive.
petrichor
noun
After weeks of heat, the petrichor rising from the first rain felt almost like a promise.

Seasons Words

Common words you'll hear and use in real weather & environment situations.

autumn
noun
The leaves turn red in autumn.
phenology
noun
Phenology records show that the cherry trees had been blooming weeks earlier than a century before.
vernal
adjective
The garden wakes each year with vernal energy: crocuses, daffodils, and the first bees.

Pollution & Recycling Words

Common words you'll hear and use in real weather & environment situations.

pollution
noun
Cars cause a lot of air pollution in big cities.
recycle
verb
We recycle all our plastic bottles and newspapers.
acid rain
noun
Acid rain damages trees and buildings.
air pollution
noun
Air pollution is a big problem in cities.
compost
noun
She turns her kitchen waste into compost for her vegetable garden.
contaminate
verb
The factory contaminated the river with chemicals.
smog
noun
If traffic doesn't drop, the smog will only get worse this winter.
emission
noun
The city has cut its carbon emissions by a third in five years.
bioaccumulation
noun
Through bioaccumulation, mercury concentrates in large predatory fish, making them unsafe to eat.
bioremediation
noun
The wetland project uses bioremediation: reeds and microbes absorb the toxins from the river water.

Climate Change & Energy Words

Common words you'll hear and use in real weather & environment situations.

climate change
noun phrase
Climate change is a serious global problem.
solar
adjective
They put solar panels on the roof of their house.
biodiversity
noun
The rainforest has amazing biodiversity, with thousands of species.
conserve
verb
We should conserve water during the dry season.
deforestation
noun
Deforestation is destroying the homes of many wild animals.
renewable
adjective
Wind and solar are renewable sources of energy.
sustainable
adjective
We need sustainable farming methods that protect the soil.
conservation
noun
Conservation programs have brought wolves back to the valley.
regenerative
adjective
Regenerative farming rebuilds the soil instead of wearing it out.
sequestration
noun
Forests play a vital role in carbon sequestration.
albedo
noun
As sea ice melts, the dark ocean absorbs more heat because its albedo is so much lower.
anthropocene
noun
Some scientists argue we have entered the Anthropocene, an era defined by human impact on the climate.
