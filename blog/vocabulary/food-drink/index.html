Food & Drink Vocabulary

This page collects 112 English food words. Every word comes with a real photo, pronunciation audio, a clear meaning and an example sentence, so you can see and hear each word in context. Each card links to the daily lesson where the word was taught, so you can read the full conversation and practice shadowing.

Words are grouped by topic: Fruit (3 words), Vegetables (3 words), Meals, Snacks & Desserts (18 words), Drinks (3 words), Groceries & Ingredients (7 words), Cooking (27 words), Kitchen (8 words), At the Restaurant (7 words), Taste & Texture (24 words), Diet & Nutrition (10 words) and More Food & Drink Vocabulary (2 words). They cover all six CEFR levels, from beginner words like bread, breakfast, cake to advanced words like amuse-bouche, degustation, sommelier.
