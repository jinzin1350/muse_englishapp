Listen to a Real Conversation

Hear these words and phrases in a natural conversation.

Lena: Good evening! Table for two, please.
Maya: Of course! Right this way. Can I get you something to drink?
Lena: Yes, I'll have a glass of water, please. Can I see the menu?
Maya: Here you go. Our special today is grilled salmon with vegetables.
Lena: That sounds delicious. Is it spicy?
Maya: No, it's very mild. Would you like to order now?
Lena: Yes, I'll have the salmon, please.
Maya: Great choice! Your food will be ready in fifteen minutes.
