یک گفت‌وگوی واقعی گوش کنید

Maya: Are you doing anything special this weekend?
Lena: Yes! We're having a big family dinner on Saturday.
Maya: That sounds lovely! Who's coming?
Lena: My parents, my brother and his wife, and my best friend.
Maya: Wow, a full house! Are you cooking?
Lena: My mom is cooking. I'm bringing dessert.
Maya: Say happy birthday to your dad for me!
Lena: I will! You should come next time.
