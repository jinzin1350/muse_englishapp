Family & Relationships Vocabulary

This page collects 48 English family words. Every word comes with a real photo, pronunciation audio, a clear meaning and an example sentence, so you can see and hear each word in context. Each card links to the daily lesson where the word was taught, so you can read the full conversation and practice shadowing.

Words are grouped by topic: Family Members (20 words), Friendship (4 words), Family Life & Parenting (15 words) and Love & Marriage (9 words). They cover all six CEFR levels, from beginner words like baby, brother, cousin to advanced words like alienate, camaraderie, patriarch.
