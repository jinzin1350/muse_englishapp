یک گفت‌وگوی واقعی گوش کنید

همین لغت‌ها و عبارت‌ها را در یک گفت‌وگوی طبیعی بشنوید.

Lena: How was your English exam yesterday?
Maya: It was hard, but I think I passed!
Lena: That's great! What was the most difficult part?
Maya: The listening section. The speaker was too fast.
Lena: I know! Can you explain the last question to me?
Maya: Sure! It was about the main idea of the story.
Lena: Thanks! Can I borrow your notes to study?
Maya: Of course! Let's study together tomorrow.
