Work & Jobs Vocabulary

This page collects 85 English work words. Every word comes with a real photo, pronunciation audio, a clear meaning and an example sentence, so you can see and hear each word in context. Each card links to the daily lesson where the word was taught, so you can read the full conversation and practice shadowing.

Words are grouped by topic: Jobs & Occupations (11 words), At the Office (13 words), Job Search & Interviews (12 words), Careers & Success (17 words), Business English (17 words) and Employment & Labour (15 words). They cover all six CEFR levels, from beginner words like doctor, job, nurse to advanced words like meritocracy, tenure, career pivot.
