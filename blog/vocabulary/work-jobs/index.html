یک گفت‌وگوی واقعی گوش کنید

همین لغت‌ها و عبارت‌ها را در یک گفت‌وگوی طبیعی بشنوید.

Lena: Good morning! Did you finish the report?
Maya: Almost! I need one more hour. What's the deadline?
Lena: The meeting is at two o'clock, so please send it before noon.
Maya: No problem. Can you send me last month's numbers first?
Lena: Sure, I'll email them right now.
Maya: Thanks! By the way, I need next Friday off.
Lena: That's fine. Just finish the report first!
Maya: Deal!
