Listen to a Real Conversation

Hear these words and phrases in a natural conversation.

Maya: Excuse me, where is the nearest subway station?
Lena: It's just around the corner. Are you going downtown?
Maya: Yes! How long does it take from here?
Lena: About twenty minutes. A ticket costs three dollars.
Maya: Great. What time does the next train leave?
Lena: In five minutes. Hurry, or you'll miss it!
Maya: Thank you so much for your help!
Lena: You're welcome! Have a great trip!
