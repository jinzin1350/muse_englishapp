Common words you'll hear and use in real technology situations.

noun
The new algorithm recommends videos based on what you watch.
automate
verb
The company automated its billing system last year.
autonomous
adjective
The autonomous car drove itself through the city.
robot
noun
A robot delivered the package to our office.
AI
noun
AI is transforming many industries rapidly.
automation
noun
Automation has eliminated thousands of repetitive jobs in warehouses.
deepfake
noun
Experts worry that deepfakes could undermine trust in video evidence.
accelerationism
noun
Silicon Valley accelerationism treats every regulation as an obstacle to inevitable progress.
agentic
adjective
The assistant's agentic capabilities allow it to book flights, file expenses, and chase invoices without being told each step.
artificial intelligence
noun
Artificial intelligence is transforming many industries rapidly.

noun

hallucination
noun

noun

quantization
noun
Through quantization, the lab squeezed a flagship model onto a laptop with barely any loss in quality.
singularity
noun
Some futurists predict the singularity will arrive within our lifetime; others call it science fiction.
technosolutionism
noun
Technosolutionism tempts politicians to fund apps instead of fixing the underlying problem.
tokenization
noun
Subword tokenization lets models handle rare words gracefully by breaking them into familiar pieces.
vibe coding
noun
Vibe coding has lowered the barrier to software, though purists still insist on understanding every line.
watermarking
noun
Watermarking generated text offers regulators a fragile but useful tool for spotting synthetic content.
zero-shot
adjective
Remarkably, the model answered zero-shot, solving tasks it had never seen during training.

Listen to a Real Conversation

Hear these words and phrases in a natural conversation.

Maya: Can you help me? My laptop won't connect to the Wi-Fi.
Lena: Sure! Is the Wi-Fi turned on?
Maya: I think so. Oh wait, my phone is out of battery too!
Lena: Ha! Let's fix one problem at a time. Can you send me a photo of the screen?
Maya: I can't, my phone is dead. Can I use your charger?
Lena: Of course. Here, plug it in over there.
Maya: Thanks! Now, about the Wi-Fi...
Lena: Try forgetting the network and connecting again.
