📝 Grammar: Must, mustn't, and should

Use **must** for a rule. You **must** wash your hands before you eat bread. Use **should** for good advice. You **should** eat an apple every day. Use **mustn't** when the answer is no. You **mustn't** run in the kitchen. **Must** is strong. **Should** is soft — it is only a good idea.
