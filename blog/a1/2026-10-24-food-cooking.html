noun
Small seeds eaten as food.
"I like to eat beans for breakfast."

noun
An alcoholic drink made from grain.
"He ordered a cold beer."
car park /KAR PARK/
noun
An area where people can leave their cars.
"I left my car in the car park."
carrot(s) /KA-ruht/
noun
An orange vegetable.
"I like to eat carrots."
chips /CHIPS/
noun
Thin pieces of fried potato. (US: fries)
"I like fish and chips for lunch."

📝Grammar: Question Words and Question Forms

**Question words** ask for information. **What** asks about things, **where** about places, and **when** about time. **Who** asks about people, **why** about reasons, and **how** about the way something happens. The word order is: **question word + be / do / can + subject + verb**. For example, **Where do** you live? With **be**, put it before the person: **Where are** you from? With **do** or **does**, use the base verb: **What do** you want? When **who** or **what** is the subject, do not use **do**, **does** or **did**: **Who lives** here? In everyday English, prepositions go at the en
