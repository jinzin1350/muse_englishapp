📝 Grammar: Can, can't, and could

Use **can** to talk about what you are able to do now. I **can** pay with cash. Use **can't** when you are not able to do it. I **can't** buy an expensive car. Use **could** to talk about the past. Yesterday I **could** buy cheap bread for one coin. **Can**, **can't**, and **could** never change. We say I **can**, you **can**, she **can** — no -s!
