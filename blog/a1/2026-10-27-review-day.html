brain /BRAYN/
noun
The organ inside your head that controls thought.
"Your brain helps you think and learn new things."
actually /AK-choo-uh-lee/
adverb
used to emphasize what is true or real
"I thought it was easy, but actually it was hard."
Africa /AF-ri-kuh/
proper noun
A large continent south of Europe.
"Africa is a big continent."
add to basket /ad tuh BAS-kit/
phrase
To put an item you want to buy into a virtual shopping cart.
"Click here to add to basket."
car park /KAR PARK/
noun
An area where people can leave their cars.
"I left my car in the car park."
carrot(s) /KA-ruht/
noun
An orange vegetable.
"I like to eat carrots."
chips /CHIPS/
noun
Thin pieces of fried potato. (US: fries)
"I like fish and chips for lunch."
beans /BEENZ/
noun
Small seeds eaten as food.
"I like to eat beans for breakfast."

noun

"My friend has asthma and uses an inhaler."
agree /uh-GREE/
verb
to have the same opinion
"Yes, I agree with you."

📝Grammar: Prepositions of Place (in, on, under, next to)

**Prepositions of place** tell us **where** something is. Use **in** for inside something, like a room, a city, or a country. Use **on** for a surface, like a table, a wall, or a floor. Use **under** for below something. Use **next to** for at the side of something. Remember the word **to**. Use **between** for the middle of two things. Use **at** for a point, like **at** the bus stop, **at** home, and **at** school.
