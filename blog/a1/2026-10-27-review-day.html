📝گرامر: Prepositions of Place (in, on, under, next to)

Prepositions of place tell us where something is.

Use in for inside something, like a room, a city, or a country.

Use on for a surface, like a table, a wall, or a floor.

Use under for below something.

Use next to for at the side of something. Remember the word to.

Use between for the middle of two things.

Use at for a point, like at the bus stop, at home, and at school.
