📝 Grammar: 1A: Present simple and continuous, action and non-action verbs

We use the **present simple** for things we do every day. I put sugar in my tea every morning. With he, she, or it, we add s to the verb. He puts salt in the soup. We use words like **always**, **usually**, and **every day** with the **present simple**. I always cut carrots for the soup. We use the **present continuous** for things happening right now. I am cooking in the kitchen right now. Be + verb-ing makes the **present continuous**. She is frying potatoes in the pan. Some verbs are not action verbs. We never use them in the **present continuous**. **like**, **love**, **need**, and *
