📝 Grammar: Talking about the future: if + will

We use **if** to talk about the future. **If** means maybe it happens. After **if**, we use the present simple. We do NOT use **will** after **if**. In the other part, we use **will**. **Will** shows what happens next. We also use **when** and **until** for the future. After **when** and **until**, we use the present simple too. The **teacher** says: **If** you study, you **will** learn. The **doctor** says: **If** you rest, you **will** feel better.
