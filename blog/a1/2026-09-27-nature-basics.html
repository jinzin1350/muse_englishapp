📝 Grammar: 3A: Choosing between comparatives and superlatives

**Comparatives** compare two things, like a **tree** and a **flower**. Add **-er** to short adjectives: **taller than**, **bigger than**. For long adjectives, use **more + adjective + than**. A **mountain** can be **more beautiful than** a **cloud**. Some words change. **Good** becomes **better**, and **bad** becomes **worse**. The **sun** is **better than** the **rain** for a picnic. To say two things are the same, use **(not) as + adjective + as**. The **grass** is **as** green **as** the **tree**. **Superlatives** compare three or more things.
