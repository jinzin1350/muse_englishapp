📝Grammar: Review: talking about the past (Simple Past)

Today we review, not learn. The **third conditional** is a B2 topic. We need **simple past** first. The **simple past** talks about yesterday. Use **was** and **were** for the past. I **was** happy. They **were** big. Use **saw** for see in the past. Use **found** for find in the past. Now use the day's words in the past. I **saw** the moon. The sky **was** blue.
