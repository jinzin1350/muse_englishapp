📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
leash /LEESH/
اسم · noun
a strong strap you hold when you walk a dog
قلادهٔ سگ (تسمهٔ نگه‌دارندهٔ سگ)
"She walks her dog on a leash in the park every morning."
kennel /KEN-ul/
اسم · noun
a small house where a dog sleeps
لانهٔ سگ
"Our dog sleeps in his kennel in the garden."
purr /PUR/
فعل · verb
the soft sound a cat makes when it is happy
خرخر کردن (گربه)
"The cat sat on my lap and began to purr."
stray /STRAY/
اسم · noun
an animal that is lost and has no home
حیوان ولگرد (بی‌خانمان)
"We gave food and water to a stray cat in our street."
groom /GROOM/
فعل · verb
to clean and brush an animal's hair
شانه و تمیز کردن موی حیوان
"She grooms her long-haired cat every evening."
scratching post /SKRA-ching pohst/
اسم · noun
a post that cats use to scratch their claws
ستون ناخن‌کشی گربه
"We bought a scratching post so our cat stops scratching the sofa."
fetch /FECH/
فعل · verb
to go and bring something back; a game dogs play
آوردن (بازی پرتاب و آوردن)
"My dog loves to play fetch with a ball in the garden."

📝گرامر: Past Simple and Irregular Verbs

We use the past simple to talk about actions that finished in the past. The time is clear or already understood.

Regular verbs add -ed to the base form: walk → walked, play → played, purr → purred.

Many common verbs are irregular and change completely: go → went, take → took, find → found. You must learn these forms by heart.

In negative sentences we use didn't + the base verb: I didn't walk the dog yesterday. In questions we use Did + the subject + the base verb: Did you groom the cat?
