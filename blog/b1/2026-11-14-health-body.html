پادکست: سلامت و بدن

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today we are talking about health, the sun, and learning to relax on a busy beach day.

Lena

Hello, everyone, I am Lena. It is a perfect topic, because last weekend my cousin came home from the beach with a terrible sunburn, and she could not relax at all.

Maya

Oh no, a bad sunburn can hurt a lot. Was it serious, or was she just a little bit tired and red?

Lena

At first we thought it was not serious. She was only a little bit red on her shoulders, and she said she just wanted to relax at home.

Maya

That sounds familiar. Many people get a little bit of sunburn on the first hot day, because they forget that the sun is strong even when the sky is a little bit cloudy.

Lena

Exactly. My cousin told me that one cloudy day cannot give you sunburn, but that idea is a load of rubbish. The sun can burn your skin through thin clouds, too.

Maya

A load of rubbish is a great phrase for today. It means something that is nonsense or not true, and people say a load of rubbish about health all the time.

Lena

They really do. Another load of rubbish is the idea that a serious sunburn will get better if you just put ice on it for an hour and forget it.

Lena

Before we continue with her story, I want to share something that helps me learn words like relax and sunburn every single day, in a simple and friendly way.

Maya

Yes, the Muse English app is free to install, and every day it gives you fresh words with clear photos and pronunciation, so you can hear each word and practise it. It also has this podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar lessons, and a leaderboard that keeps you coming back. If you want to relax and learn a little bit every day, open the app, try today's lesson, and build a learning habit with us.

Maya

Right, and if the pain feels serious, if you feel sick, or if your skin looks very bad, you should talk to a doctor and not just try to relax and wait.

Lena

I love that, because a little bit of practice every day is not a load of rubbish at all, it really works. Now, back to my cousin, because her evening got interesting.

Maya

Tell me everything. Did she finally relax, or did the sunburn get more serious in the evening?

Lena

She tried to relax on the sofa, but every time she moved, her shoulders hurt a little bit more. Then her brother arrived, and she had to shake hands with his friend very carefully.

Maya

Shake hands is another useful phrase today. When you meet someone, you shake hands to say hello, but with sunburn on your hands or shoulders, you shake hands gently.

Lena

Yes, she learned to shake hands gently that day. His friend works at a beach hotel, and he sees a serious case of sunburn almost every week in summer.

Maya

What does he tell guests when they arrive with sunburn and they cannot relax?

Lena

He tells them to drink a little bit of water, sit in the shade, and relax in a cool room. If the burn looks serious, he calls a doctor, because a serious burn needs real care.

Maya

That is good advice. At that hotel, does he also help guests who use a wheelchair?

Lena

He does. There is a kind man at the hotel who uses a wheelchair to move around, and he always greets new guests and likes to shake hands with everyone at the door.

Maya

I like stories about a wheelchair being just a normal part of daily life. A person in a wheelchair can work, travel, shake hands with friends, and enjoy the beach like anyone else.

Lena

Absolutely. This man told my cousin that she should relax her mind first. He said, when your body hurts a little bit, slow breathing can help you relax a little bit more.

Maya

That is wise. He uses a wheelchair, but he was the calmest person there, and he knew that a serious face does not help anyone relax.

Lena

And he joked that half the health advice on the internet is a load of rubbish, so we should listen to doctors, drink water, and rest when a problem feels serious.

Maya

Speaking of good advice, this is a good moment to remind everyone that learning English can also be calm and healthy, without stress and without pressure every day.

Lena

That is why I use the Muse English app when I want to relax and study. It is free to install, and it gives you daily words with photos, clear pronunciation, this podcast, shadowing practice, quizzes, grammar lessons, points, and a leaderboard. You can learn a little bit each morning, shake hands with new words, in a manner of speaking, and feel proud when your small steps grow into real English.

Maya

Beautifully said. Now, let us finish the beach story, because I want to know if your cousin could finally relax and sleep that night.

Lena

She could, after a cool shower and a little bit of cream. The next morning, the sunburn was still red, but it was not serious, and she could relax in the garden.

Maya

Did she shake hands with the hotel man again and thank him for his help with the wheelchair story and the good advice?

Lena

She did shake hands with him the next day, and she thanked the nurse too. The nurse had brought a wheelchair for an old guest who felt a little bit weak in the sun.

Maya

So a wheelchair helped the old guest relax, a little bit of water helped your cousin, and good information helped everyone ignore that load of rubbish online.

Lena

Exactly. And my cousin now says that a sunburn is never just a joke. A small sunburn may be fine, but a serious sunburn is a serious problem for your health.

Maya

Let us repeat today's ideas one more time, slowly. To relax means to rest and feel calm, and everyone needs to relax after work or after a long day in the sun.

Lena

A serious problem is bad or dangerous and needs attention, a sunburn is red and painful skin from too much sun, and a wheelchair is a chair with wheels that helps a person move around.

Maya

When you greet someone, you shake hands, a little bit means a small amount, and when something is nonsense or not true, you can say it is a load of rubbish.

Lena

Thank you for listening, everyone. Drink a little bit of water today, relax your shoulders, protect your skin from sunburn, and we will see you tomorrow on Muse English.

📝گرامر: Present Perfect Continuous

We use the present perfect continuous for an activity that started in the past and is still continuing. The form is have / has been + verb-ing, for example *I have been waiting for an hour*.

We often use it with for, since and how long. *She has been working here since May* tells us when the activity started.

We also use it for a recent activity with a result we can see now. If a friend cannot relax and looks tired, you can say *You have been working too hard*.
