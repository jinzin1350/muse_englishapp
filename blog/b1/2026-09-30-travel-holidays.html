📝Grammar: Present perfect: have/has + past participle

We use the **present perfect** to talk about past actions that are connected to the present. Form it with **have** or **has** + the **past participle** of the main verb: *I have visited*, *she has eaten*. Use **have** with *I, you, we, they* and **has** with *he, she, it*: *they have arrived*, *he has arrived*. We often use it for **life experiences** with *ever* and *never*: *Have you ever been to Japan? I have never tried sushi.* The exact time is not important — what matters is the experience itself.
