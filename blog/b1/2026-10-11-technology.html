📚 Vocabulary

10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
screen /SKREEN/
noun
the flat front part of a phone or computer where you see pictures and words
"I read the news on my phone screen every morning."
click /KLIK/
verb
to press a button on a mouse or a phone to choose something
"Click the green button to start the video."
upload /UP-lohd/
verb
to send a file from your device to the internet
"She uploads her photos to the internet every evening."
file /FYLE/
noun
a document, photo, or video saved on a computer
"I saved the report as a file on my laptop."
app /AP/
noun
a small program on a phone, like a map or a game
"This app helps me learn new English words every day."
data /DAY-tuh/
noun
information that computers store and use
"Our data shows that most people learn faster with pictures."
gadget /GA-jit/
noun
a small clever machine or tool
"He bought a new gadget that opens the door with his voice."
smart /SMAHRT/
adjective
for machines: able to work alone and connect to the internet
"My smart speaker plays music when I say a word."
camera /KAM-ruh/
noun
a tool for taking photos and videos
"The camera on this phone takes very clear photos."
speaker /SPEE-kur/
noun
a device that plays sound loudly
"We connected a speaker and danced in the living room."

📝Grammar: Third conditional

We use the third conditional to talk about the past. We imagine how the past could have been different. We build it with **if + past perfect** in one half, and **would have + past participle** in the other half. Example: *If I had charged my phone, I would have taken more photos.* I did not charge it, so I took no photos. We never say ~~if I would have~~. The word **would** goes in the second half only.
