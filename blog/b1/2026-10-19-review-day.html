10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
benefit /BEN-uh-fit/
noun
something good you get from a job, like free lunch or extra days off
"One benefit of the job is free lunch."
ice hockey /ISE HOK-ee/
noun
A sport played on ice with sticks and a puck.
"My brother loves to play ice hockey in winter."
fetch /FECH/
verb
to go and bring something back; a game dogs play
"My dog loves to play fetch with a ball in the garden."
report /ri-PORT/
noun
a written document about work that has been done
"She wrote a report about the meeting."
give up /GIV UP/
phrasal verb
Stop trying to do something.
"Don't give up on your dreams."
groom /GROOM/
verb
to clean and brush an animal's hair
"She grooms her long-haired cat every evening."
purr /PUR/
verb
the soft sound a cat makes when it is happy
"The cat sat on my lap and began to purr."
do exercise /doo EK-suh-size/
phrase
To do physical activity to stay healthy.
"I try to do exercise every morning."
go swimming /GOH SWIM-ing/
verb
Do an activity, often for fun or a task.
"We often go swimming on Saturdays."
training /TRAY-ning/
noun
learning the skills you need for a job
"New employees get two weeks of training."

📝Grammar: Stative Verbs

**Stative verbs** describe states, not actions. They talk about thoughts, feelings, senses and possession. We do not use them in the continuous. Say *I **know** the report* — never *I am knowing the report*. Common stative verbs: **know, want, need, like, love, hate, seem, have** (own), **belong, cost**. I **know** that good training is important.
