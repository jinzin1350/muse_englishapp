7 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
aircraft /AIR-kraaft/
noun
a vehicle that flies, like a plane
"Many aircraft fly over our house."
airport /AIR-port/
noun
a place where planes take off and land
"We arrived at the airport early."
at risk of /ut RISK uv/
phrase
In a situation where something bad might happen.
"People who smoke are at risk of lung disease."
available /uh-VAY-luh-bul/
adjective
Able to be used or found.
"Are there any rooms available for tonight?"
away [distance] /uh-WAY/
adverb
At a distance from a place.
"The shop is 5 miles away."
beach /beech/
noun
Sandy or stony land next to the sea.
"We walked on the beach yesterday."
bike /byk/
noun
A bicycle.
"Let's ride our bikes to the park."

📝 Podcast Transcript

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I'm Maya.

Lena

And I'm Lena! Today we're talking about travel and transport. Maya, do you love traveling?

Maya

I love it! Last month I took my first trip by aircraft in years. When the aircraft took off, my heart was racing!

Lena

Flying by aircraft is exciting. But the airport is the stressful part for me. I always arrive at the airport two hours early.

Maya

Smart. The airport can be chaotic. Did you know that an airport is at risk of long delays in winter? Any storm can slow everything down.

Lena

True! My flight home was delayed once because the aircraft was stuck at the airport for three hours. I felt completely at risk of missing my connection.

Maya

That's a scary feeling. Being at risk of missing a connection is every traveler's nightmare. Now, I have a question about your beach trip last summer. Did you rent a bike?

Lena

Yes! I rented a bike near the beach. Every morning I'd take my bike and ride along the beach road. The beach was only ten minutes away by bike.

Maya

That sounds perfect. A quiet bike ride by the beach, the ocean breeze. And the beach was just a short ride away. Did you feel safe there?

Lena

Very safe. The beach town was calm, and the bike paths were well marked. But one afternoon, a tourist on a bike was riding too fast and was at risk of hitting a pedestrian. His bike nearly went off the path!

Maya

Wow, that's dangerous. A fast bike near a busy beach can really put people at risk of an accident. You have to be careful.

Lena

Exactly. Quick break! If you love learning English this way, check out the Muse English app. It's free to install and available on your phone right now.

Maya

That's right! With the app you get daily words with photos and pronunciation, this very podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, and grammar lessons — plus a leaderboard to earn points!

Lena

So don't miss out. Download the Muse English app today and make English part of your daily routine.

Maya

Back to our trip! So after the beach, where did you go? How far away was the city?

Lena

The city was about an hour away. We took a bus, since our hotel was far away from the train station. But the hotel bikes were available for guests, which was nice.

Maya

Free bikes? That's a great perk. Were the bikes available every day?

Lena

Yes, there were always bikes available. I loved it. And the aircraft that brought us there had been wonderful too. The airline's aircraft was brand new and comfortable.

Maya

Modern aircraft are so quiet compared to the old ones. I remember the first aircraft I ever flew on — it was tiny!

Lena

My first aircraft memory is different. I flew to see my grandmother, and the airport seemed huge to me as a child. An airport feels like a small city, doesn't it?

Maya

It really does. The airport has shops, restaurants, even hotels. Some people spend the whole day at the airport between flights.

Lena

And if your luggage is late, you're at risk of having nothing to wear for days! That happened to my brother once. His bags stayed at the airport while he was far away in another country.

Maya

Oh no! Being far away from your luggage is terrible. Was the airline helpful?

Lena

Not really. They said no replacement bags were available until the next day. Nothing was available, so he had to buy new clothes.

Maya

That's frustrating. When nothing is available, you just have to wait. Travel teaches patience.

Lena

It really does. Anyway, back to the beach town. One day we decided to ride our bikes to a hidden beach. The hidden beach was twenty kilometers away!

Maya

Twenty kilometers away by bike? That's a long ride!

Lena

It was! But the road was flat, and the bike path followed the coast. Halfway there, we saw a sign: the old beach road was closed, and cyclists were at risk of falling rocks.

Maya

At risk of falling rocks? That's serious! What did you do?

Lena

We turned around. No view is worth being at risk of injury. So we rode our bikes back to the main beach and spent the afternoon there instead.

Maya

Good decision. The main beach is lovely anyway. Was the water warm?

Lena

Perfect. And in the evening, we sat on the beach and watched a huge aircraft fly low over the sea toward the airport.

Maya

I love watching aircraft from the beach. There's something magical about an aircraft crossing the sunset sky.

Lena

Agreed! You know, before we wrap up, let me mention the Muse English app again. The app is available in six levels, and everything is available offline too, so you can study on an aircraft with no internet.

Maya

That's brilliant. And daily lessons are available every morning: new words, this podcast, shadowing stories, and grammar quizzes. Thousands of learners are earning points every day!

Lena

So don't wait. Install the Muse English app today. It's free, it's fun, and it's available right now on your phone.

Maya

Back to travel tips! Lena, one more question. When you travel, do you prefer the bike or the bus?

Lena

Honestly, the bike. A bike gives you freedom. In a new city, a rented bike lets you explore places far away from the tourist areas.

Maya

That's true. Some of the best places are far away from the center. And a bike is cheap — usually much cheaper than a taxi.

Lena

Exactly. And riding a bike is good exercise. Last year I cycled around the whole beach area on a borrowed bike. The bike was old but reliable.

Maya

Borrowed bikes are the best adventure. So to summarize today's words: aircraft, airport, at risk of, available, away, beach, and bike. Seven travel words!

Lena

Let's review them once more. An aircraft is a vehicle that flies. The airport is where planes take off and land. If you are at risk of something, something bad might happen.

Maya

Available means able to be used or found. Away means at a distance — like the beach is five miles away. A beach is sandy land by the sea. And a bike is a bicycle!

Lena

Perfect summary! Travel safe, keep practicing, and we'll see you tomorrow.

Maya

Thanks for listening to Muse English. Goodbye!

📝Grammar: Be Able To (vs Can and Could)

**Be able to** means the same as *can*: *I **can** swim = I **am able to** swim.* We need it because *can* has only two forms — *can* and *could*. For every other tense or structure, we use **be able to**: *I**'ll be able to** ride a bike to the beach tomorrow.* *I **haven't been able to** visit the airport this year.* *I'd love **to be able to** travel far away more often.* After a modal verb, never use *can* — use **be able to**: *I **will be able to** drive you to the airport* (never "I will can drive").
