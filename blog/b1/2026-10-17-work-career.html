📚 Vocabulary

7 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
freelance /FREE-lans/
verb
to work for yourself, doing short jobs for different companies
"She works freelance as a designer for several clients."
benefit /BEN-uh-fit/
noun
something good you get from a job, like free lunch or extra days off
"One benefit of the job is free lunch."
paycheck /PAY-chek/
noun
the money you receive for your work
"He saves half of his paycheck every month."
training /TRAY-ning/
noun
learning the skills you need for a job
"New employees get two weeks of training."
employee /em-PLOY-ee/
noun
a person who works for a company
"The company has fifty employees."
report /ri-PORT/
noun
a written document about work that has been done
"She wrote a report about the meeting."
career /kuh-REER/
noun
the series of jobs you do in your life
"He started his career as a teacher."

📝 Podcast Transcript

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I'm Maya.

Lena

And I'm Lena. Today we are talking about work and careers. Almost everyone works, or will work one day, so these words are really useful.

Maya

Exactly. We picked seven useful words for today's lesson: freelance, benefit, paycheck, training, employee, report, and career.

Lena

Let's start with freelance. When you work freelance, you work for yourself. You do short jobs for different clients instead of working for one company.

Maya

Right. My friend Sara works freelance as a graphic designer. She has three or four clients at the same time.

Lena

That sounds nice. Freelance work gives you freedom. You choose your own hours and your own projects.

Maya

It does, but freelance life is not always easy. Some months are very busy, and some months are quiet. So a freelance worker has to plan carefully.

Lena

I agree. And a freelance worker also has to find new clients all the time. When you work for a company, the work comes to you. When you work freelance, you have to look for it.

Maya

That is the hardest part. But many people still choose freelance work because freedom is worth it. They can travel, they can rest, and nobody tells them what to wear.

Lena

Ha! That last one is a big benefit for me. Now, a quick break from our lesson.

Maya

If you like learning with us, you will love the Muse English app. Every day you get new words with photos and pronunciation, this podcast with a full transcript, shadowing stories you can practice with, quick quizzes, grammar lessons, and points on a leaderboard as you learn. It is free to install, so grab it today and make English a daily habit.

Lena

Thanks, Maya. Now back to our words. The next word is benefit. A benefit is something good you get from a job. Free lunch, health insurance, and extra days off are all benefits.

Maya

Benefits matter a lot when people choose a job. Sometimes a job with a smaller salary but better benefits is the smarter choice.

Lena

Exactly. My brother took a job because one benefit was working from home two days a week. That benefit saved him hours of travel every single day.

Maya

Nice. Every company offers different benefits, so it is smart to ask about benefits in a job interview. Write down the benefits before you say yes to any job.

Lena

Good advice. Let me tell you about my friend's company. They give every worker a free lunch, a gym membership, and a free language course. That is a package of benefits that keeps people happy.

Maya

Wow, that is generous. When the benefits are good, people stay longer and work harder.

Lena

Next word: paycheck. Your paycheck is the money you receive for your work. Most people get a paycheck every month.

Maya

And a paycheck can feel very different depending on the job. My first paycheck was small, but I was so proud of it.

Lena

I remember mine too. I spent the whole paycheck in one weekend, which was not smart at all.

Maya

We learn. The smart move is to save part of every paycheck. Even a small part of each paycheck grows over time, and your future self will thank you.

Lena

True. A paycheck is not just money, it is a reward for your hard work. When the paycheck arrives, it feels like a small celebration.

Lena

Our fourth word is training. Training is the learning you do to get the skills for a job. New employees usually get some training when they start.

Maya

Yes. The training at my first job took two weeks. Good training makes a new job much less stressful.

Lena

And training never really stops. Companies offer training all year, because new skills help every employee do better work.

Maya

I agree. The best training is the kind you use every single day.

Lena

Let me give you an example. My cousin started a job at a hotel. The training lasted one month. Every morning, the older workers showed him what to do. By the end of the month, he could do everything alone. That is what good training looks like.

Maya

That is a perfect example. Without that training, his first month would have been a disaster. Training is really a gift from the company to the new worker.

Lena

Exactly. And here is a tip for our listeners: when you start a new job, ask lots of questions during the training. The trainers are there to help you, and good questions show that you care.

Maya

Great advice. Now, before we continue, one more thing for our listeners.

Maya

Thousands of learners are already using the Muse English app to study between episodes. You get daily words with photos, pronunciation clips, our podcast with transcripts, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar lessons, and a leaderboard that keeps you motivated. Download it free and keep your English growing every single day.

Lena

Thanks. Let's keep going. The next word is employee. An employee is a person who works for a company. If you have a job, you are an employee.

Maya

A happy employee does better work. Smart companies take care of their employees with fair pay and good benefits.

Lena

Right. The company where my cousin works has two hundred employees. Every new employee gets a friendly welcome on the first day.

Maya

I like that. When an employee feels respected, the whole team works better.

Lena

Next is report. A report is a written document about work that has been done. Managers often ask for a weekly report.

Maya

Writing a good report is a useful skill. My manager read my report and said it was clear and short, which is exactly what a report should be.

Lena

A clear report saves everyone time. The report should show the facts, the results, and what to do next.

Maya

Exactly. Even a short report can change a big decision.

Lena

Our last word today is career. Your career is the series of jobs you do in your life. It is the big picture of your working life.

Maya

My uncle started his career as a taxi driver, and twenty years later he owns a small company. A career can change in amazing ways.

Lena

That is inspiring. A good career takes time to build. Every job teaches you something that helps your career grow.

Maya

And every career starts with a first step. Even a small job can be the start of a great career.

Lena

Let's review today's words one more time. Freelance means working for yourself with different clients. A benefit is something good you get from a job. Your paycheck is the money you earn. Training teaches you job skills. An employee works for a company. A report is a written document about work. And your career is your working life over time.

Maya

Seven great words. Try to use them today in real sentences. Thanks for listening to Muse English!

Lena

See you tomorrow for more words. Bye!

📝Grammar: Past Perfect and Narrative Tenses

We use the **past perfect** for something that happened **before** another past action. We form it with **had** + past participle: *She **had left**.* Words like **already**, **just**, **never** and **by the time** often go with the past perfect. "By the time he got his first **paycheck**, he **had finished** his **training** report." In a story we use three narrative tenses together.
