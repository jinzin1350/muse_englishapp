📚 Vocabulary

10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
embark /em-BARK/
verb
to get on a ship or airplane to start a journey
"We will embark on the cruise ship at noon tomorrow."
disembark /dis-em-BARK/
verb
to get off a ship or airplane at the end of a journey
"Passengers must disembark from the plane row by row."
jet lag /JET LAG/
noun
extreme tiredness you feel after a long flight across different time zones
"After my flight to Tokyo, I had terrible jet lag for three days."
turbulence /TUR-byuh-luhns/
noun
sudden rough shaking of an airplane in the air
"There was some turbulence during the flight, but we landed safely."
excursion /ex-KUR-zhuhn/
noun
a short enjoyable trip, often organized for a group of people
"Our hotel offers a day excursion to the old castle."
porter /POR-ter/
noun
a person whose job is to carry people's bags at a hotel, station, or airport
"The porter carried our heavy suitcases to the fourth floor."
stopover /STOP-oh-ver/
noun
a short break or stay in one place during a longer journey
"We had a four-hour stopover in Dubai before flying to London."
round-trip /ROWND-TRIP/
adjective
a ticket or journey that takes you somewhere and then brings you back
"A round-trip ticket is usually cheaper than two one-way tickets."
campsite /KAMP-syt/
noun
a place where people stay overnight in tents
"We cooked dinner over a fire at our campsite in the mountains."
road trip /ROHD TRIP/
noun
a long journey you take by car, often for fun
"Last summer we went on a road trip across Canada."

📝Grammar: Quantifiers

We use **a lot of** and **lots of** for large quantities. We can use them with countable and uncountable nouns. **Lots of** is a little more informal than **a lot of**. We use **many** with plural countable nouns, and **much** with uncountable nouns. These two are common in negative sentences and questions. **Plenty of** means "more than enough". For example, there was **plenty of** food at our campsite. For small quantities, we use **a little** with uncountable nouns. We use **a few** with plural countable nouns.
