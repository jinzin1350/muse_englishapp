📚 لغت‌ها

۱۰ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
nearest /NEER-ist/
صفت · adjective
Closest in distance.
نزدیک‌ترین
"Where is the nearest bus stop?"
on board /on BORD/
عبارت · phrase
On a ship, train, or aircraft.
سوار (کشتی، هواپیما)
"All passengers are now on board the plane."
height /HYT/
اسم · noun
How tall a person or thing is.
قد، ارتفاع
"What is your height?"
wheelchair /WEEL-chair/
اسم · noun
chair with wheels for disabled people
ویلچر
"He uses a wheelchair to move around."
serious [bad] /SEER-ee-us/
صفت · adjective
bad or dangerous; needing attention
جدی، وخیم
"He has a serious illness."
get used to sth get /get YOOST too/
عبارت · phrase
Become familiar with something and feel comfortable.
به چیزی عادت کردن
"I'm getting used to the cold weather."
relax /ree-LAKS/
فعل · verb
To rest and feel calm.

"I like to relax after work."
plane /PLAYN/
اسم · noun
an aircraft that flies in the sky.

"We traveled by plane to London."
grey /GRAY/
صفت · adjective
a color between black and white.

عبارت · phrase

📝گرامر: Used To

We use used to + base verb for past habits and past states that are not true now. Nina used to take the plane every month, but she does not travel so often now.

The positive form is subject + used to + base verb. The negative is didn't use to, and questions start with Did you use to...? After did and didn't, write use to with no d.

Used to is only for the past, and there is no present form. For a present habit, use the present simple with usually: *I usually relax after work.*

For past habits, you can also use would + base verb, for re
