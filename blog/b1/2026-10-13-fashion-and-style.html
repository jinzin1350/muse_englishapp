📚 Vocabulary

10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
outfit /OUT-fit/
noun
a set of clothes worn together
"She wore a smart outfit to the job interview."
stylish /STY-lish/
adjective
fashionable and attractive in appearance
"He always looks stylish in a black jacket."
fit /FIT/
verb
to be the right size for someone
"These jeans fit me perfectly."
trendy /TREN-dee/
adjective
popular and fashionable at the moment
"Oversized jackets are trendy this year."
tailor /TAY-lor/
noun
a person who makes or changes clothes to fit you
"A tailor shortened my trousers in two days."
wardrobe /WARD-robe/
noun
all the clothes that a person owns
"Her wardrobe is full of bright colours."
accessory /ak-SES-uh-ree/
noun
a small extra item, like a belt or scarf, that completes an outfit
"A silk scarf is a lovely accessory."
fabric /FAB-rik/
noun
cloth used for making clothes
"This shirt is made from soft cotton fabric."
vintage /VIN-tij/
adjective
old but valuable and fashionable, from a past time
"She found a beautiful vintage dress at the market."
pattern /PAT-ern/
noun
a repeated design on cloth
"I love the striped pattern on this shirt."

📝 Podcast Transcript

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I'm Maya.

Lena

And I'm Lena, and today we are talking about fashion and style.

Maya

Fashion is a great topic because everyone wears clothes, and everyone has an opinion about them.

Lena

That is so true. Some people love fashion, and some people just want to feel comfortable.

Maya

Let's start with a simple idea. An outfit is a full set of clothes that you wear together, like a shirt, trousers, and shoes.

Lena

Right. When I plan an outfit, I think about the weather first, and then I think about where I am going.

Maya

Exactly. A good outfit fits the day. For work, I like a clean and simple outfit. For a party, I like something more fun.

Lena

And the fit is so important. If your clothes fit well, you look good even in a simple outfit.

Maya

Yes. Fit means how clothes sit on your body. A jacket can be beautiful, but if the fit is wrong, it just feels uncomfortable.

Lena

I agree. I once bought a jacket that was too tight, and I never wore it again. The fit was all wrong.

Maya

That happens to everyone. And fit is not only about size. A good fit also means the shape suits you, so you can move and breathe easily.

Lena

True. When the fit is right, you forget about your clothes and enjoy your day.

Maya

Now, before we go on, let me tell you about something that can help your English every single day.

Lena

Oh, I love this part.

Maya

The Muse English app gives you new words with photos and pronunciation every day, plus this podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar lessons, and a leaderboard with points. It is free, and it makes learning English part of your day. You can practise a little in the morning and a little at night, and your English will grow week by week. Download the Muse English app and start your next lesson today.

Lena

That is such a good tip. Now, let's talk about what is trendy right now.

Maya

Trendy means popular and fashionable at the moment. This year, big jackets and bright colours are very trendy.

Lena

I have seen so many trendy shops with wide trousers and soft sweaters. Trendy styles change fast, though.

Maya

They really do. Last year, one colour was trendy, and this year it is something else. That is why I try not to follow every trend.

Lena

Smart. I like to mix one trendy piece with my normal clothes. That way, my outfit feels fresh, but it still feels like me.

Maya

That is a great idea. And speaking of fresh, let's talk about pattern. A pattern is a repeated design on cloth, like stripes or flowers.

Lena

I love a good pattern. A striped shirt is a classic pattern, and a floral dress is a happy pattern.

Maya

Patterns can change a whole outfit. A plain shirt with a strong pattern on the scarf can look very stylish.

Lena

Yes. And stylish means fashionable and attractive. You do not need expensive clothes to look stylish.

Maya

True. A stylish person often wears simple clothes with confidence. A stylish look is about how you put things together.

Lena

Let's talk about fabric next. Fabric is the cloth that clothes are made from, like cotton or wool.

Maya

Fabric matters so much. Soft cotton fabric feels cool in summer, and warm wool fabric feels cosy in winter.

Lena

I always check the fabric before I buy something. If the fabric feels rough, I will not enjoy wearing it.

Maya

Same here. Good fabric can make a cheap shirt feel wonderful, and bad fabric can ruin a lovely design.

Lena

Now, what about a tailor? A tailor is a person who makes clothes or changes them so they fit you.

Maya

A tailor is a real hero. If your trousers are too long, a tailor can shorten them. If your jacket is too wide, a tailor can fix it.

Lena

I took a dress to a tailor last month, and she made it perfect. The tailor changed the length and fixed the shoulders.

Maya

That is the magic of a tailor. A small change from a tailor can turn a good outfit into a great one.

Lena

Let's talk about the wardrobe. Your wardrobe is all the clothes that you own.

Maya

My wardrobe is full of black and blue. I want more colour in my wardrobe this year.

Lena

A good trick is to look at your wardrobe before you shop. If your wardrobe already has five black shirts, you do not need another one.

Maya

Great advice. And when you look at your wardrobe, think about accessories too. An accessory is a small extra item, like a belt, a scarf, or a bag, that completes an outfit.

Lena

Accessories are my favourite part. One accessory, like a bright scarf, can make a plain outfit look new.

Maya

Yes, an accessory is a small thing with a big effect. A belt, a hat, or a pair of earrings can each be the perfect accessory.

Lena

Before we finish, let me share a quick tip about the Muse English app. It is the free app that brings you daily words with photos and pronunciation, this podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar lessons, and a leaderboard with points. If you want steady progress in English, download the Muse English app today, open your level, and make it part of your morning.

Maya

Thanks, Lena. Now, one last word: vintage. Vintage means old but valuable and fashionable, from a past time.

Lena

I love vintage shops. A vintage jacket from the nineteen eighties can look so stylish today.

Maya

Vintage clothes have a story. A vintage dress with a lovely pattern and soft fabric feels special in a way that new clothes do not.

Lena

And a vintage piece can be a wonderful accessory to a modern outfit, like an old bag with a new dress.

Maya

So, to remember today: think about your outfit, check the fit, try one trendy piece, enjoy a fun pattern, feel the fabric, visit a tailor, look at your wardrobe, add one accessory, and maybe find something vintage.

Lena

And remember, a stylish look is not about money. It is about knowing what fits you and wearing it with confidence.

Maya

Thanks for listening. See you next time on Muse English.

Lena

Bye, everyone!

📝Grammar: Present Perfect vs Past Simple

We use the **present perfect** for past actions with a result now. We use the **past simple** for finished actions at a finished time. Form the **present perfect** with **have** or **has** + past participle. Form the **past simple** with the past form of the verb. Use the **present perfect** with **just**, **already**, **yet**, and **ever**. Use the **past simple** with **yesterday**, **last week**, and **in 2020**. Compare: **I have bought** a new outfit means it is new for you now. **I bought** an outfit yesterday tells you when it happened. Do not use the **present perfect** with a fini
