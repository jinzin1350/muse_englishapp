📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
large number/amount /LARJ NUM-bur/uh-MOWNT/
عبارت · phrase
A lot of something.
تعداد/مقدار زیاد
"A large number of people came to the sale."
make [cause sb to do sth] /MAYK/
فعل · verb
cause someone to do something.
باعث شدن (کسی کاری کند)
"The funny story made me laugh."
push /PUSH/
فعل · verb
To move something away from yourself.
هل دادن
"Push the door to close it."
review /ri-VYOO/
اسم · noun
An opinion about a book, film, or product.
نقد، بررسی
"I read a good review of that new movie."
save (up) /SAYV (up)/
فعل عبارتی · phrasal verb
To keep money for future use.
پس‌انداز کردن
"I am saving up for a new bike."
size /SYZ/
اسم · noun
How big or small something is.
اندازه
"What size is this shirt?"
throw sth away /THROH SUM-thing uh-WAY/
فعل عبارتی · phrasal verb
to get rid of something you don't want.
دور انداختن
"Don't throw away old clothes."

پادکست: پول و خرید

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! Today we are talking about smart shopping, and how to spend your money wisely.

Lena

I love this topic, Maya, because last weekend I went to a big sale, and I learned a lot about myself.

Maya

Tell me everything. Did you buy a large number of things?

Lena

Well, a large number of people were at the sale, and I almost bought a large amount of clothes I did not need.

Maya

That happens to me too. When a large number of shops have sales at the same time, it is hard to stay calm.

Lena

Yes, and the music in the shops can make you spend more money. A bright shop window can make you walk in, even when you do not need anything new.

Maya

And a friendly salesperson can make you try things on, and then you feel you must buy them.

Lena

Exactly. Last weekend, I wanted one jacket, but the sale signs made me look at ten jackets.

Maya

So what did you do? Did you read a review before you bought it?

Lena

I did. I read a review of the jacket online, and the review said the size was small, so I ordered a bigger size.

Maya

Smart move. I always read a review before I buy something expensive, because one honest review can save you a lot of money.

Maya

That is so true. Now, before we go on, let me tell you about something that can help you every single day.

Lena

Oh, I know what you mean. If you are learning English, you need the Muse English app. Every day it gives you new words with clear photos and pronunciation, so you can hear exactly how each word sounds.

Maya

Yes, and it also has this podcast, plus shadowing stories you can practise with, quick quizzes, grammar lessons, and a leaderboard where you earn points as you learn.

Lena

It is free to install, and it makes learning feel like a game. So open your app store today, search for Muse English, and start learning with us.

Lena

And I checked the size chart carefully, because the wrong size is the worst problem. If the size is wrong, you have to go back to the shop, and that wastes your whole afternoon.

Maya

Back to our shopping story. Lena, what happened when you got to the shop with your cart?

Lena

I had to push a very heavy cart through a large number of busy aisles, and I had to push it slowly because a large amount of people were in the way.

Maya

Pushing a heavy cart is hard work. Did you push the cart to the clothes section first?

Lena

Yes, and I tried the jacket on. The size was perfect this time, because I had read that review, remember?

Maya

Good. And did you buy anything else?

Lena

I wanted to buy a large amount of other things, but I stopped myself. I am saving up for a new bike, so I need to save up every month.

Maya

Saving up is a great habit. I am saving up for a holiday, so I save up a little money from every paycheck.

Lena

That is smart. When you save up for something big, small sales do not make you spend your savings.

Maya

True. A sale can make you buy a cheap thing, but it can also make you lose the money you wanted to save up.

Lena

Exactly. And at home, I have another problem. I have a large number of old clothes in my closet, and I never wear them.

Maya

Oh, I know that problem. A large amount of my closet is full of clothes I bought on sale and never wore.

Lena

So last Sunday, I decided to throw away the clothes I never wear. I did not throw away everything, just the things with holes and stains.

Maya

Did you really throw them away, or did you give them to a charity shop?

Lena

I gave the good ones to a charity shop, and I threw away only the broken ones. It felt great to throw away the old mess and make room for the new jacket.

Maya

That is a wonderful idea. When you throw away old things, your home feels clean, and you can see what you really own.

Lena

And now I will not buy a large number of new things again. I will read a review first, check the size, and think about what I really need.

Maya

I agree. So our advice is simple. Read a review, check the size, save up for what you love, and throw away what you do not use.

Lena

And remember, a large number of happy shoppers are not the ones who buy the most. They are the ones who buy what they really need.

Maya

Before we finish, here is a quick reminder about the Muse English app, because learning English should be part of your day, just like your morning coffee.

Lena

That is right. The app gives you fresh words every day with photos and pronunciation audio, so you can learn on the bus, at lunch, or before bed.

Maya

You also get this podcast, shadowing stories to speak along with, fun quizzes, clear grammar lessons, and points and a leaderboard to keep you going.

Lena

It is free, it is friendly, and it fits in your pocket. Search for Muse English in your app store and join thousands of learners today.

Maya

That is a perfect plan. Let me ask you one more thing. Do you think shops try to push us to buy more?

Lena

Yes, they do. They push big carts at us, they push special offers at us, and the ads push us to buy a large amount of things we do not need.

Maya

And those ads can make you feel unhappy with what you have. They make you want a new phone, a new coat, a new everything.

Lena

But a good review can stop that. When I read a review, I learn the truth about the size, the quality, and the price, and then the ads cannot make me buy it.

Maya

Now, let us recap today's words. We talked about a large number of shoppers, and a large amount of clothes on sale.

Lena

We talked about how music and ads make you buy things, and how a good review can help you choose well.

Maya

We talked about size, and why the right size matters so much.

Lena

We talked about how to save up for something big, and why you should throw away old things you never use.

Maya

And we talked about how you push a heavy cart through a busy shop.

Lena

So next time you go shopping, read a review, check the size, save up wisely, and do not let the ads make you buy a large number of things you do not need.

Maya

Thanks for listening to Muse English. See you tomorrow with seven new words.

📝گرامر: Quantifiers: All, Both, Either, Neither, None, Few

Quantifiers tell us how many or how much of something there is. All, both, either, neither, none, most, and few are common quantifiers.

Use both, either, and neither for two things. Both means the two together. Either means one or the other. Neither means not one and not the other.
