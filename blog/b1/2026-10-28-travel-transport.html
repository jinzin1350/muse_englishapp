📚 Vocabulary

7 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
brake n, v /BRAYK/
noun, verb
A device used to slow or stop a vehicle.
"Press the brake pedal to stop the car."
breeze /BREEZ/
noun
A gentle, light wind.
"A cool breeze blew through the trees."
bus stop /BUS STOP/
noun
A place where a bus stops for people.
"I wait for the bus at the bus stop."
calm (sea) /KAHM/
adjective
Without big waves or strong wind.
"The sea was calm for our boat trip."
cliff /KLIF/
noun
A high, steep rock face, usually by the sea.
"We walked along the top of the cliff."
coast /KOHST/
noun
The land next to the sea.
"We spent our holiday on the coast."
depart /duh-PART/
verb
To leave a place, especially to start a journey.
"The train will depart from platform 3."

📝 Podcast Transcript

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and this is my friend Lena.

Lena

Hello, everyone! Maya, you look excited today. What are you planning?

Maya

I am planning a trip to the coast, Lena. I want to travel by train along the coast, and I want to see the sea, the cliffs, and the small towns on the coast.

Lena

That sounds wonderful. When does your train depart?

Maya

My train will depart early on Saturday morning. It will depart from the main station at seven o'clock, and it will travel slowly along the coast all day.

Lena

A slow train along the coast is the best way to travel. You can watch the sea, feel the breeze, and enjoy the calm water from the window.

Maya

Exactly! I love the breeze on a train. A cool breeze through an open window makes the whole journey feel fresh and calm.

Lena

And where will you get off the train?

Maya

I will get off at a small town on the coast. There is a famous cliff near the town, and you can walk along the top of the cliff and look down at the calm sea.

Lena

A walk on a cliff sounds amazing, but be careful near the edge of the cliff. The cliff is high and steep, so stay on the path.

Maya

I will, I promise. And after the cliff walk, I will take a bus to the beach. The bus stop is right next to the station, so the bus stop is easy to find.

Lena

Good. At the bus stop, you can wait in the shade. Is the bus stop busy on Saturdays?

Maya

The bus stop can be busy, but the bus comes every twenty minutes. I will wait at the bus stop with a book and enjoy the breeze while I wait.

Lena

That is a perfect plan. Before we talk more about your trip, I want to tell you about something that helps me learn English every day.

Maya

Oh? What is it?

Lena

It is the Muse English app. It is free, and it gives you new words every day, each one with a photo and clear pronunciation, so you can learn words like coast and cliff and really remember them. It also has this podcast, fun shadowing stories, quick quizzes, grammar lessons, and a leaderboard where you can earn points as you learn. If you want to learn English step by step, install the Muse English app today and start learning with us.

Maya

I love that idea. Learning a little every day really works. Now, Lena, let me tell you more about my trip. The train will depart from platform three, and I need to be there early, because the train will depart exactly on time.

Lena

Good advice. When a train is ready to depart, it does not wait. What will you do first when you arrive on the coast?

Maya

First, I will walk to the cliff path. The cliff path is beautiful in the morning, when the sea is calm and the breeze is soft. A calm sea and a soft breeze make a perfect morning.

Lena

I agree. There is nothing better than a calm morning by the sea. And what about lunch?

Maya

I will eat lunch in a small cafe near the bus stop. After lunch, I will take the bus from the bus stop to the next town along the coast.

Lena

And will you use the brake on your bike? Wait, you are not taking a bike, are you?

Maya

No bike this time! But you reminded me of something funny. Last year, I rode a bike along the coast, and I had to press the brake very hard on a steep hill. The brake worked well, and I stopped safely. A good brake is important on a bike, and the driver of a bus uses the brake carefully too.

Lena

That is true. Every driver must check the brake before a long journey. A good brake keeps everyone safe on the road.

Maya

Yes, and the bus driver will use the brake gently when the bus arrives at the bus stop. I like a smooth stop at the bus stop, not a sudden one.

Lena

Me too. So, after the bus, what is next?

Maya

After the bus, I will walk along the coast to a quiet beach. The coast there is wild and beautiful, with high cliffs and a calm sea. I will sit on the sand, listen to the breeze, and watch the waves.

Lena

That sounds peaceful. The breeze on the coast is different from the breeze in the city. The breeze by the sea smells fresh and clean.

Maya

It does. And in the evening, the breeze gets cooler, and the sea stays calm. I will watch the sun go down over the calm water, and then I will walk back to the bus stop.

Lena

Will the bus still run in the evening?

Maya

Yes, the last bus leaves the bus stop at nine o'clock. I will be at the bus stop in good time, because I do not want to miss it.

Lena

Smart. And when will you depart for home?

Maya

My train home will depart on Sunday evening. It will depart from the same station, and it will travel back along the coast as the sun sets.

Lena

What a lovely weekend. A trip to the coast, a walk on a cliff, a calm sea, a soft breeze, and a train that will depart right on time.

Maya

Before we finish the trip talk, Lena, do you have one more tip for our listeners?

Lena

Yes! If you enjoyed learning words like depart and breeze with us today, open the Muse English app. Every day you get fresh words with photos and pronunciation, plus this podcast, shadowing stories you can practise aloud, short quizzes, clear grammar lessons, and points on the leaderboard as you improve. It is free, it is fun, and it helps you learn a little every day. Install Muse English and join us tomorrow for more words.

Maya

And do you know what else I will do? I will practise these words on my trip. Every time I see the coast, I will say coast. Every time I feel the breeze, I will say breeze. Every time I wait at the bus stop, I will say bus stop.

Lena

That is a great way to learn. And when you walk on the cliff, say cliff. When the sea is calm, say calm. When the bus driver uses the brake, think brake. And when your train is ready to depart, say depart.

Maya

Exactly! Learning words on a real trip makes them easy to remember. The coast, the cliff, the calm sea, the breeze, the bus stop, the brake, and the moment we depart, they all become part of the story.

Maya

Thanks, Lena. And thank you for listening. Remember our words today: brake, breeze, bus stop, calm, cliff, coast, and depart. Use them on your next trip, and we will see you tomorrow on Muse English. Goodbye!

Lena

Goodbye, everyone, and happy travels!

📝Grammar: Past Simple and Irregular Verbs

Use the **past simple** for finished actions and situations in the past. We say "We **visited** the coast yesterday." Regular verbs add **-ed**. For example, **depart** becomes **departed**: "Our train **departed** at seven o'clock." Irregular verbs have their own past form. **Go** becomes **went**: "We **went** to the cliff." In negatives, use **didn't** + the base verb. We say "We **didn't** wait at the bus stop," not "didn't waited." In questions, use **did** + subject + base verb. "**Did** you feel the breeze on the cliff?" **Was** and **were** are the past of **be**. The sea **was**
