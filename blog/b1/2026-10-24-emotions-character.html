📚 Vocabulary

7 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
able /AY-bul/
adjective
having the skill or power to do something
"I am able to speak three languages."
absolutely /ab-suh-LOOT-lee/
adverb
completely, totally, or definitely
""Are you sure?" "Absolutely!""
attitude /AT-ih-tood/
noun
A way of thinking or feeling about something.
"He has a positive attitude towards work."
bald /BAWLD/
adjective
Having little or no hair on the head.
"My uncle started to go bald in his thirties."
be into sth [like sth] /bee IN-too/
phrasal verb
Like or be interested in something.
"She is really into sports and plays every day."
can’t stand /KANT STAND/
phrase
Really dislike something or someone.
"I can't stand loud music."
cheek /CHEEK/
noun
The soft part of your face below your eye.
"She kissed her baby's soft cheek."

📝 Podcast Transcript

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and this is Lena. Today we are talking about feelings, attitudes, and the little things that make us who we are.

Lena

Hello, everyone! Maya, I have a question for you. Are you able to stay calm when something goes wrong?

Maya

That is a good question. I think I am able to stay calm most of the time, but not always. Yesterday, for example, I was absolutely sure I would miss my bus, and I felt so stressed.

Lena

Oh no! Did you miss it?

Maya

No, I was able to catch it at the last second. But my attitude in that moment was not great. I was angry at the traffic, angry at myself, and I could feel my cheek turn red.

Lena

Your cheek turned red? That happens to me too when I am embarrassed. My cheek goes red, and then I feel even more embarrassed.

Maya

Exactly. And that is when attitude matters. A positive attitude can change a bad morning into a normal morning. If your attitude is calm, small problems feel small.

Lena

I agree completely. Absolutely. A good attitude does not fix the bus, but it fixes how you feel about the bus.

Maya

Before we go on, let me tell you about something I love. The Muse English app gives you new words every day with photos and pronunciation, plus this podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar lessons, and a leaderboard where you can earn points. It is free, and it makes learning English part of your day. Try it today and see how fast your English grows.

Lena

Nice tip! Now, speaking of attitudes, tell me about your uncle. I heard he is bald and really proud of it.

Maya

Yes! He is bald, and he is absolutely proud of it. He says being bald saves him time every morning.

Lena

That is a wonderful attitude! Some people worry a lot about being bald, but he is bald and happy. He is able to laugh about it, and that makes everyone around him laugh too.

Maya

Yes! He is able to make a joke about anything. Last week someone asked him about his hair, and he touched his bald head and said, "I am absolutely free. No shampoo, no problem."

Lena

I love that. So, what are you into these days, Maya? What are you really into?

Maya

I am really into cooking now. I am into trying new recipes every weekend. Are you into cooking too?

Lena

Not really. I am into music and long walks. I am into anything that helps me relax. But cooking? I can't stand cooking when the kitchen is a mess.

Maya

You can't stand a messy kitchen? I can't stand that either. I absolutely can't stand cooking when there are dirty dishes everywhere.

Lena

Yes! I can't stand loud music in the kitchen too. Wait, no, I love music. Let me say it clearly: I can't stand loud arguments, and I can't stand rude people on the bus. But music is fine.

Lena

Tell me, are you able to learn new things easily, or does it take time?

Maya

I am able to learn fast when I am really into the topic. When I am into something, I want to know everything about it. But when I am not interested, I am not able to focus at all.

Lena

Same here. My attitude toward learning changed a lot this year. I used to think I was not able to speak English well, but now I know I am able to do it. I just need practice and a good attitude.

Maya

Absolutely. And you know what helped my attitude? My friend Sara. Sara is into running, and she is absolutely full of energy. She told me, "Maya, you are able to do hard things. You just need to believe it."

Lena

Sara sounds wonderful. Is Sara the one with the beautiful smile? The one whose cheek always turns pink when she laughs?

Maya

Yes, that is Sara! Her cheek turns pink, she laughs, and everyone laughs with her. She is able to make a whole room feel happy. That is the power of a good attitude.

Lena

And what about things you can't stand? We said we can't stand messy kitchens and rude people. Is there anything else you absolutely can't stand?

Maya

I can't stand waiting in long lines. I absolutely can't stand it. And I can't stand it when my phone battery dies at the worst moment. What about you?

Lena

I can't stand cold coffee. I absolutely can't stand cold coffee in the morning. And I can't stand boring meetings that go on for hours.

Maya

Fair enough. Now, let me ask you something personal. Do you think being bald changes how people see you? My uncle says people treat him differently now that he is bald.

Lena

I think some people notice, but a kind attitude matters much more than hair. If you are bald and kind, people remember your kindness, not your head. If you are bald and funny, people remember the jokes.

Maya

Let me pause our chat for a quick tip. The Muse English app gives you fresh words every day with photos and clear pronunciation, this podcast, fun shadowing stories, quick quizzes, helpful grammar lessons, and points on the leaderboard. It is free to install, and it turns a few minutes a day into real progress. Download it now and keep learning with us.

Lena

Good tip, Maya. Now, back to our chat.

Maya

Absolutely right. My uncle is able to walk into any room and feel confident. He is bald, he is proud, and his attitude is the best thing about him.

Lena

That is a great lesson. Being able to accept yourself is a real skill. Are you able to accept your own small problems, Maya?

Maya

I am learning. I am able to laugh at myself more now. Last week I burned the dinner, my cheek turned red, and instead of feeling bad, I laughed. My attitude saved the evening.

Lena

Wonderful. And are you still into cooking, even after burning dinner?

Maya

Absolutely! I am into cooking more than ever. I am into trying again and again. One bad dinner does not change what I am into.

Maya

So, to finish today: remember, a good attitude helps you stay calm, being able to laugh helps you stay strong, and knowing what you are into helps you enjoy life.

Lena

And remember what you can't stand, and what makes your cheek turn red, and that being bald or anything else never defines you. Your attitude does.

Maya

Absolutely. Thanks for listening to Muse English. We will see you tomorrow with new words and a new story. Bye for now!

Lena

Bye, everyone!

📝Grammar: Past Simple and Irregular Verbs

We use the **past simple** for finished actions and situations in the past. For example: I visited my grandmother yesterday. Regular verbs add **-ed**, like work to worked. Irregular verbs have their own past form, like go to went. In negatives and questions, use **did** + the base verb. For example: I didn't go. Did you go? The past form is the same for every person. I, you, he, she, we and they worked. There is no -s in the past. **Be** is different. Use **was** for I, he, she and it, and **were** for you, we and they. Do not use did: I wasn't tired.
