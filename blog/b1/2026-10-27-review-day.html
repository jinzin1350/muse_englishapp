📚 Vocabulary

10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
income tax /IN-kum taks/
noun
Money paid to the government from your earnings.
"Everyone has to pay income tax."
attachment /uh-TACH-ment/
noun
A file sent with an email message.
"I sent the report as an attachment."
answerphone /AHN-suh-fohn/
noun
A machine that records phone messages.
"Leave a message on my answerphone."
can’t stand /KANT STAND/
phrase
Really dislike something or someone.
"I can't stand loud music."
cheek /CHEEK/
noun
The soft part of your face below your eye.
"She kissed her baby's soft cheek."
item /EYE-tum/
noun
A single thing or object.
"I bought three items from the store."
credit card /KRED-it kard/
noun
A plastic card used to buy things now and pay later.
"I paid with my credit card."
able /AY-bul/
adjective
having the skill or power to do something
"I am able to speak three languages."
be into sth [like sth] /bee IN-too/
phrasal verb
Like or be interested in something.
"She is really into sports and plays every day."
bag /BAG/
noun
A container made of soft material, with a handle.
"I carry my lunch in a small bag."

📝Grammar: Reported Speech: Statements and Questions

We use **reported speech** to say what someone said without repeating their exact words. After a past reporting verb like **said**, **told** or **asked**, the tense usually moves **one step back** into the past. For example, present simple becomes past simple, and **will** becomes **would**. Use **say** + (that) + sentence, but **tell** + person + (that) + sentence. Never put a person after **say**, and never use **tell** without a person. Reported questions use **normal sentence word order**, with no **do / does / did** and no question mark. For yes/no questions, use **if** or **whether
