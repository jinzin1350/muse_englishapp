📚 Vocabulary

10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
tender /TEN-duhr/
adjective
soft and easy to chew
"The meat is tender and juicy."
vintage /VIN-tij/
adjective
old but valuable and fashionable, from a past time
"She found a beautiful vintage dress at the market."
trendy /TREN-dee/
adjective
popular and fashionable at the moment
"Oversized jackets are trendy this year."
data /DAY-tuh/
noun
information that computers store and use
"Our data shows that most people learn faster with pictures."
outfit /OUT-fit/
noun
a set of clothes worn together
"She wore a smart outfit to the job interview."
stopover /STOP-oh-ver/
noun
a short break or stay in one place during a longer journey
"We had a four-hour stopover in Dubai before flying to London."
speaker /SPEE-kur/
noun
a device that plays sound loudly
"We connected a speaker and danced in the living room."
tailor /TAY-lor/
noun
a person who makes or changes clothes to fit you
"A tailor shortened my trousers in two days."
accessory /ak-SES-uh-ree/
noun
a small extra item, like a belt or scarf, that completes an outfit
"A silk scarf is a lovely accessory."
screen /SKREEN/
noun
the flat front part of a phone or computer where you see pictures and words
"I read the news on my phone screen every morning."

📝Grammar: Present Perfect Continuous

We use the **present perfect continuous** for actions that started in the past and are still happening now. The form is **have/has been + verb-ing**. I **have been cooking** since noon — the meat is finally tender. Use **since** for a start point and **for** for a length of time. She **has been sewing** **since** eight this morning. The **tailor** **has been working** on my **outfit** **for** two hours. It also explains a present result. He **has been staring** at that **screen** **for** three hours — his eyes hurt.
