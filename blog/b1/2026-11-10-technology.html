📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
add to basket/bag /ad too BAS-kit bag/
عبارت · phrase
To select an item for purchase online.
اضافه کردن به سبد خرید
"I will add this book to my basket."
back up sth /BAK UP/
فعل عبارتی · phrasal verb
To make a copy of computer data.
پشتیبان گرفتن (از اطلاعات)
"Always back up your important files."
blog /BLOG/
اسم · noun
A website where someone writes regularly about topics.
وبلاگ
"I read her travel blog every week."
blogger /BLOG-uh/
اسم · noun
A person who writes a blog.
وبلاگ‌نویس
"She is a famous fashion blogger."
browse /BROWZ/
فعل · verb
look at things in a shop or on the internet
گشتن، مرور کردن
"I like to browse in bookstores."
browser /BROW-zuh/
اسم · noun
software to view websites
مرورگر
"Which internet browser do you use?"
call [phone] /KAWL/
فعل · verb
To contact someone using a phone.
تلفن زدن
"I will call you later tonight."

پادکست: فناوری

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today we are talking about technology in daily life, the small things we do on our phones and computers every single day.

Lena

Hi everyone, I am Lena. I love this topic, because yesterday I bought a gift online, and I learned how many useful English words I use without thinking about them.

Maya

Tell us what happened. Did you browse for a long time first?

Lena

I did. I love to browse in online shops in the evening. I browse slowly, I compare prices, and I read reviews before I decide anything. My friends say I browse too much, but browsing helps me save money.

Maya

I browse too, but I usually browse on my laptop, because the screen is bigger. Which browser do you use when you browse online?

Lena

I use the same browser every day. My browser is fast, and my browser remembers my favourite shops. A good browser makes shopping easy, and a slow browser can really annoy me.

Maya

That is true. So, you found a gift, and then what did you do?

Lena

I found a beautiful travel book, and I pressed add to basket. You know the button, add to basket, on every shopping page. I always check the add to basket button twice, because once I pressed add to basket two times and ordered two copies.

Maya

Oh no. So learners, remember this phrase. When you shop online, you add to basket the things you want to buy. You add to basket a book, you add to basket a gift, and later you pay for everything together.

Lena

Exactly. And yesterday, after I pressed add to basket, my phone asked me to call my bank. I had to call the bank to confirm the payment, so I decided to call them straight away.

Maya

Good idea. I hate that moment. I usually call my sister first and ask her advice, and then I call the bank. When I call people, I prefer a quiet room, because I cannot hear well in a noisy cafe.

Lena

I call the bank maybe once a month. This time, the woman was very kind, and after I finished the call, my payment worked. But the whole thing reminded me to back up my phone.

Maya

Yes, that is our next important phrase, back up. Please, everyone, back up your photos, back up your messages, and back up your important files every week.

Lena

I learned that lesson the hard way. Last year my phone broke, and I lost every photo, because I never used to back up anything. Now I back up my phone every Sunday, and my computer reminds me to back up my work files too.

Maya

You get new words with photos and pronunciation, a podcast like this one, shadowing stories, quizzes, and grammar lessons, and you earn points and climb the leaderboard. It is completely free to install, so why not try it today and make English a small daily habit?

Lena

I agree, a small habit every day really works. Now, Maya, you told me last week that you started writing online. Are you a blogger now?

Maya

I am a very new blogger. I write a small blog about learning languages. My blog is simple, but I write on my blog every Tuesday, and a few hundred people read the blog now.

Lena

That is wonderful. Every blogger starts small. A blogger writes, a blogger shares ideas, and a good blogger answers comments from readers. Do you enjoy being a blogger?

Maya

I do. As a blogger, I think carefully about my readers. One famous travel blogger told me to write like I talk, and that advice helped my blog a lot. Now people say my blog feels friendly.

Lena

I read a food blog every morning, and that blogger always posts beautiful photos. Her blog makes me hungry. I also follow a technology blog, because that blog explains new phones in simple English.

Maya

Blogs are a great way to practise reading. And in the app we talked about, you can save words from lessons like this one and review them later with smart quizzes, so words like blogger and browser stay in your memory. The shadowing stories help your speaking too, because you listen and repeat, sentence by sentence, until the English feels natural. Install Muse English free from the link in our bio, and join thousands of learners building a daily habit with us.

Lena

Yes, join us. Now back to shopping. After you add to basket and pay, do you always back up the email with your order?

Maya

I do now. I back up every important email, and when I call a company about an order, I call with that email open in front of me. It saves time when I call customer service.

Lena

Same here. And before I buy anything expensive, I browse three or four shops, I check each browser tab carefully, and only then do I add to basket. My browser often has ten tabs open when I browse for shoes.

Maya

Ten tabs in one browser is a lot, Lena. But I understand. To browse is to look slowly and enjoy it, and a browser is the app we use to look at websites. Those two words are a pair, like blog and blogger. A blog is the website, and a blogger is the person who writes it.

Lena

And remember, when something goes wrong, do not worry. You can call the shop, you can call your bank, or you can call a friend and ask for help. English speakers call people every day for small reasons.

Maya

And please back up your phone tonight. It takes two minutes to back up your photos, and one day you will be happy you did. Let us say our seven words one more time in real sentences, quickly.

Lena

I browse in my browser, I add to basket the book I love, and I call my friend to tell her about it.

Maya

I write my blog because I am a blogger, I back up my computer every week, and I call my readers my friends.

Lena

Perfect. That is all for today, everyone. Practise these words, browse a blog in English this week, and we will see you tomorrow on Muse English.

Maya

Bye for now, and happy learning!

📝گرامر: Verbs + Prepositions (depend on, belong to)

Many verbs are followed by a fixed preposition before their object. We say depends on, belongs to, and waiting for — the preposition is part of the verb, so learn the two words together.

Use on with depend on, rely on, insist on, and concentrate on. The price you pay when you add to basket in an online shop can depend on the season.

Use to with belong to, listen to, and talk to. Use for with wait for, look for, pay for, and apologise for — when you call the shop, you can pay for your order by card.
