📝 Grammar: 3B: Articles - a / an, the, no article

Use **a** or **an** with a singular countable noun when you say what something is. Say **an** before a vowel sound: **a charger**, **an old keyboard**. Use **a / an** the first time you mention something new, like **a notification** or **an update**. Later, when both people know which one you mean, use **the**: **the notification**, **the update**. Use **the** when there is only one of something, or when the meaning is clear. For example: **the battery** of your phone, **the software** on this device. Use **the** with superlatives and with places in town. For example: **the fastest download
