📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
make it /MAYK it/
عبارت · phrase
Succeed in doing something difficult; arrive.
موفق شدن، رسیدن
"I hope I can make it to the party on time."
team /TEEM/
اسم · noun
A group of people working or playing together.
تیم، گروه
"Our football team won the game."
tent /TENT/
اسم · noun
A portable shelter made of cloth.
چادر
"We slept in a tent when we went camping."
volleyball /VOL-ee-bawl/
اسم · noun
A game where two teams hit a ball over a net.
والیبال
"I like to play volleyball on the beach."
windsurfing /WIND-sur-fing/
اسم · noun
Sport of sailing on water with a board and sail.
موج سواری با بادبان
"I want to try windsurfing on my holiday."
after [follow sb/sth] /AF-ter/
حرف اضافه · preposition
following someone or something
دنبال، پی
"The dog ran after the ball."
after [time/event] /AF-ter/
حرف ربط · conjunction
when something has happened
بعد از اینکه
"I will call you after I finish work."

پادکست: ورزش و تناسب اندام

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today Lena and I are talking about a wonderful sports weekend by the sea.

Lena

Hello, everyone. I am Lena. Last summer, my friends and I joined a small team for a beach sports weekend, and I still smile when I think about it.

Maya

Tell us how it started. Did your team make it to the beach on time?

Lena

We almost did not make it. Our train was late, and after the train finally arrived, we ran after a bus that was already leaving the station.

Maya

Oh no. So after you missed that bus, what did you do?

Lena

We waited for the next one, and after we arrived at the beach, we put up our tent near the sand. Our tent was small, but it was warm and dry.

Maya

A tent by the sea sounds lovely. Was the tent comfortable at night?

Lena

It was. After dinner, we sat outside the tent and planned the next day. Everyone on the team wanted to play volleyball early in the morning.

Maya

Volleyball on the beach is my favourite sport. Did your team play well?

Lena

At first, no. The ball kept flying over the net, and I ran after the ball again and again. After an hour of practice, our team started to play much better.

Maya

That is how learning works. You practice, you make mistakes, and after some time, you make it. Did you really make it in the end?

Lena

Yes, we did make it. On the second day, our volleyball team won two games, and after the second game, we all jumped and shouted with joy.

Maya

I love that story. A team that stays together can make it, even when the beginning is hard. What else did you try that weekend?

Lena

After lunch, I tried windsurfing for the first time. Windsurfing looks easy, but standing on the board is really difficult.

Maya

I tried windsurfing once, and I fell into the water many times. After my lesson, my arms hurt, but I wanted to try windsurfing again the next day.

Lena

Same here. After I fell three times, the teacher helped me, and finally I could stand. I thought, if I keep trying, I can make it across the small bay.

Maya

And did you make it across the bay while windsurfing?

Lena

Almost. After twenty minutes of windsurfing, I reached the middle of the bay. The wind was strong, so I turned back, but I felt proud.

Maya

You should feel proud. Now, before we continue, let me tell our listeners something helpful. If you want to learn words like team, tent, volleyball, and windsurfing in a fun and easy way, try the Muse English app. It is free to install, and every day you get new words with clear photos and pronunciation. You also get this podcast, shadowing stories that help you speak, short quizzes, grammar lessons, and points on a leaderboard. Learning a little every day really works, so install Muse English today and start building your English step by step.

Lena

That is great advice, Maya. Daily practice helps you make it, just like in sport. After the windsurfing, our team went back to the tent to rest.

Maya

What did the team do after resting in the tent?

Lena

After a short rest, we played volleyball again. This time, another team joined us, and after the game, we became good friends with them.

Maya

Sport brings people together. Did anyone run after anything funny that day?

Lena

Yes. A friendly dog ran after our volleyball, and after the dog took the ball, two children ran after the dog. Everyone laughed.

Maya

That is a sweet picture. Where did you sleep that night, in the same tent?

Lena

Yes, we slept in the tent again. After the long day of volleyball and windsurfing, I slept very well in that little tent.

Maya

It sounds like the perfect weekend. A team, a tent, volleyball, and windsurfing. What did you learn from it?

Lena

I learned that you should never stop too early. After you fall, you stand up again. After you lose a game, you practice more. And if your team believes in you, you can make it.

Maya

Beautifully said. And now it is my turn to share something with our listeners. Friends, if this podcast helps you, imagine what you can do with the Muse English app every single day. In the app, you can hear each word, see a photo for each word, and practise speaking with shadowing stories, just like the story in today's lesson. There are fun quizzes, clear grammar lessons, and a leaderboard that keeps you motivated with points. It is completely free to install, and it only takes a few minutes a day. So join us in the Muse English app, learn with us daily, and watch your English grow stronger and stronger.

Lena

Yes, join us. And remember our story when you feel tired. After a hard day, rest in your tent, or rest at home, and start again tomorrow.

Maya

Let us go back to the beach for the last part. What happened on the final day?

Lena

On the final day, after breakfast, our team played one last volleyball game. After the game, we packed the tent and cleaned the beach.

Maya

Did everyone make it home safely after the weekend?

Lena

Yes, everyone did make it home, tired but happy. The dog even ran after us one last time when we left, as if it wanted to say goodbye.

Maya

What a lovely ending. So, listeners, remember today's words. A team works and plays together. A tent is a small shelter for camping. Volleyball is a game with a ball and a net. Windsurfing is a sport on water with a board and a sail. We use after when we follow someone, like running after a ball, and we also use after for time, like calling a friend after work. And when something is difficult but you succeed, you make it.

Lena

Practice these words today. Say them out loud, use them in your own sentences, and after you finish, try the quiz in the app.

Maya

Thank you for listening to Muse English. Keep practising, keep smiling, and we will see you tomorrow for a new lesson.

Lena

Goodbye, everyone, and good luck. You can make it!
