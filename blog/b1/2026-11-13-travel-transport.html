📝گرامر: Present Perfect for Experience (ever, never)

We use the present perfect to talk about experiences in our life up to now. We do not say exactly when they happened: *I've been to London. She's never flown on a plane.*

The form is have / has + past participle. Use have with I, you, we and they, and has with he, she and it.

Use ever in questions, to ask about any time in a life: Have you ever travelled by public transport? Use never with a positive verb to say not at any time: *I've never been on board a ship.*
