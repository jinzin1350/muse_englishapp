📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
recommend /rek-uh-MEND/
فعل · verb
To suggest something good to someone.
توصیه کردن، پیشنهاد کردن
"I recommend this hotel; it's very nice."
reliable /ri-LY-uh-bul/
صفت · adjective
Able to be trusted or depended on.
قابل اعتماد
"My car is old but very reliable."
rock /ROK/
اسم · noun
A type of music with a strong beat.
موسیقی راک
"My dad loves listening to rock music."
rough (sea) /RUF/
صفت · adjective
With big waves, not calm.
مواج، طوفانی
"The boat sailed on a rough sea."
round the corner /ROWND thuh KOR-ner/
عبارت · phrase
Very near, happening soon.
نزدیک، در شرف وقوع
"The shop is just round the corner."
sand /SAND/
اسم · noun
Tiny grains of rock found on beaches.
شن، ماسه
"We built a castle in the sand."
seaside resort /SEE-side ri-ZORT/
اسم · noun
A town by the sea for holidays.
تفریحگاه ساحلی
"We spent our summer holiday at a seaside resort."

پادکست: سفر و حمل‌ونقل

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today Lena and I are planning a dream holiday by the sea.

Lena

Hi everyone, I am Lena. Maya, I really need a break from work. Where do you recommend we go?

Maya

I recommend a small seaside resort on the south coast. My cousin went there last year, and she still talks about it every single week.

Lena

A seaside resort sounds perfect for us. But tell me, is it easy to get there?

Maya

Very easy. There is a reliable train from the city, and the trip takes only two hours. The train is clean, fast, and reliable, so we do not need to drive at all.

Lena

I like that idea. I always recommend the train for short trips because you can read, relax, and watch the sea from the window.

Maya

Exactly. And our hotel is just round the corner from the station, so we can walk there with our bags in five minutes.

Lena

Round the corner? That is great news. Last year my hotel was far away from everything, and the taxi cost me a lot of money.

Maya

Not this time. Everything in this seaside resort is round the corner. The beach, the cafés, and the market are all a short walk away.

Lena

That sounds wonderful. And how long shall we stay there?

Maya

Let us stay for five days. Five days is enough time to rest, swim in the sea, and visit the old town near the resort.

Lena

Five days sounds just right. So, what is the beach like?

Maya

The sand is soft and golden. In the morning, children build tall castles in the sand, and in the evening, people walk slowly on the sand and watch the sun go down.

Lena

I love walking on warm sand with no shoes on. But tell me honestly, is the sea always calm there?

Maya

Not always. In the afternoon, the sea can get a little rough. When the sea is rough, the waves are big and strong, and swimming is not a good idea.

Lena

A rough sea can be dangerous for swimmers. What do people do on a rough day, then?

Maya

On a rough day, they stay safe on the sand, play games, and drink coffee in the cafés. The local boat captains are reliable, and they never sail when the sea is too rough.

Lena

Maya

I use it every day too, and I recommend it to all of my students. Now, Lena, what shall we do in the evenings at the seaside resort?

Lena

I heard there is live music there. Is it true that a band plays rock music on the beach?

Maya

Yes, it is true. Every Friday, a local band plays rock songs near the harbour. People dance happily on the sand, and the rock music sounds amazing under the stars.

Lena

I am not a great dancer, but I really love rock music. My father played rock in the car when I was a child, so rock always makes me feel happy and free.

Maya

Then you will love these evenings. And if we get hungry, I recommend the little fish restaurant that is round the corner from our hotel.

Lena

You recommend it? Is the food there really that good?

Maya

It is wonderful. The fish is fresh every morning, and the owner is very friendly. I recommend their fish soup and their warm bread with tomatoes.

Lena

Perfect, I am hungry already. And what about a boat trip? I would love to see the coast from the water.

Maya

We can book a boat trip on Saturday morning, when the sea is calm and quiet. The company is reliable, the boats are new, and the captain always checks the weather first.

Lena

That is very important to me. A reliable captain makes everyone feel safe, even when the sea gets a bit rough later in the day.

Maya

Very true. And after the boat trip, we can buy gifts at the market. The market is round the corner from the harbour, and it sells bags, hats, and beautiful shells.

Lena

I can already see us there now. Sand on our feet, rock music in the evening, good food, and no stress at all.

Maya

And it is good value too. The hotel is small and family-run, the food is cheap, and the beach is free for everyone.

Lena

Perfect. A great holiday does not need to cost a lot of money.

Maya

Lena

That is a great tip, Maya. So, when do we leave for this seaside resort?

Maya

Summer is round the corner, so let us book the train and the hotel this week. I recommend booking early, because the best rooms sell very fast.

Lena

Agreed. A reliable plan, a beautiful seaside resort, soft sand, good food, and a little rock music in the evening. This holiday is going to be perfect.

Maya

It really is. Thank you for listening, everyone. Here is a question for your English journal: where do you recommend for a holiday, and why do you recommend it?

Lena

See you next time on Muse English, everyone. Bye for now!

Maya

Bye everyone, and happy learning!
