Passive Reporting Structures (It is said that..., He is thought to...)

Level: C1 · Free grammar guide

In news and formal writing, we often report what people in general say, think or believe without saying who: People say that he is rich. → It is said that he is rich. / He is said to be rich.

Common verbs: say, think, believe, know, report, expect, consider, claim, allege, estimate, suspect.

Two patterns

PatternExample
It + passive + that clauseIt is believed that the thieves left the country.
Subject + passive + to-infinitiveThe thieves are believed to have left the country.

With there: There are said to be over a million people in the square.

Choosing the infinitive

Time of the actionInfinitiveExample
same time / generalto + verbHe is said to be very rich.
in progress nowto be + -ingShe is reported to be recording a new album.
earlierto have + past participleShakespeare is thought to have written it in 1599.
earlier, in progressto have been + -ingHe is believed to have been living abroad.

The reporting verb can be past too: She was reported to have left the hospital.

Examples

  1. It is said that the castle is haunted. (It + that clause)
  2. The castle is said to be haunted. (subject + to-infinitive)
  3. It is expected that prices will rise. (expect)
  4. The new bridge is expected to open in 2028. (future)
  5. The singer is reported to be recording a new album. (in progress)
  6. The painting is estimated to be worth $5 million. (estimate)
  7. The suspect is alleged to have stolen the money. (earlier action)
  8. It is known that the drug has side effects. (know)
  9. He is considered to be one of the best players in the world. (consider)
  10. There are thought to be around 3,000 tigers left in the wild. (there + plural)

Common mistakes

❌ He is said that he is rich.

✅ It is said that he is rich. / He is said to be rich. (Don't mix the two patterns.)

❌ It says that the company will close.

✅ It is said that the company will close. (Use the passive: it is said.)

❌ He is believed to leave yesterday.

✅ He is believed to have left yesterday. (Earlier action: to have + past participle.)

❌ She is reported being in hospital.

✅ She is reported to be in hospital. (Use the to-infinitive.)

❌ There is said to be many problems.

✅ There are said to be many problems. (Plural noun: there are.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. It ___ that the company will close next year.

2. The president is said ___ very ill.
Same time: is said to be.
3. The thieves are believed ___ the country last night.
Earlier action: to have left.
4. The singer is reported ___ a new album at the moment.
In progress now: to be recording.
5. ___ that eating carrots improves your eyesight.
Passive reporting: It is often said that…
6. There ___ to be over a million people at the protest.
There + plural noun: There were said to be…
7. Shakespeare is thought ___ this play in 1599.
Earlier action: to have written.
8. It is expected ___ prices will rise again next month.
It is expected that + clause.
9. The missing climbers are thought ___ in a cave until the storm passes.
In progress now: to be sheltering.
10. He is known ___ a very generous man.
Same time: is known to be.
11. Choose the correct sentence.
Subject + passive + to-infinitive: The man is believed to be dangerous.
12. It ___ that the fire was started deliberately.
Passive reporting: It is suspected that…
13. The painting is estimated ___ around $5 million.
Same time: to be worth.
14. The suspect is alleged ___ the money from his employer over several years.
Earlier action: to have stolen.
15. She was reported ___ the hospital yesterday afternoon.
An action before the report: to have left.
16. The new bridge ___ to open in 2028.
Passive reporting: is expected to…
