Passive Reporting Structures (It is said that…, He is believed to…)

B2 · C1

In news, reports and academic writing, we often report what people in general say, think or believe, without saying who. We use passive structures: It is said that the house is haunted. / The house is said to be haunted.

These structures sound objective and formal. For the passive in general, see Passive Voice.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

It is said that…, It is believed that… B2

ActivePassive (it … that)
People say that he is rich.It is said that he is rich.
Experts believe that the drug is safe.It is believed that the drug is safe.
They reported that the fire was under control.It was reported that the fire was under control.

  • It is said that the castle is 800 years old. (people say)
  • It is estimated that 2 million people visited the festival. (estimate)
  • It was reported that two people were injured. (past report)
  • It is expected that prices will rise. (expectation)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ It says that he is rich. (meaning people say) → ✅ It is said that he is rich. (passive: it is said)
  • ❌ It is believed the drug safe. → ✅ It is believed that the drug is safe. (that + full clause)

He is said to be…, She is believed to live… B2

It … thatSubject + to
It is said that he is rich.He is said to be rich.
It is believed that she lives abroad.She is believed to live abroad.
It is expected that the strike will end soon.The strike is expected to end soon.

  • The singer is said to be very shy. (people say)
  • Two climbers are reported to be missing. (report)
  • The new phone is expected to cost $1,000. (expectation)
  • Mozart is considered to be a genius. (consider)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He is said being very rich. → ✅ He is said to be very rich. (is said to + base verb)
  • ❌ She is believed that she lives abroad. → ✅ She is believed to live abroad. / It is believed that she lives abroad. (don't mix the two patterns)

Practice: B2

1. ___ that the new law will reduce crime.
It + is + past participle + that.
2. It ___ that the company lost $10 million last year.
It has been reported that…
3. It ___ (believe) that the painting is a fake.
It is believed that…
4. The thief is believed ___ in the city centre.
Is believed to + infinitive.
5. This hotel ___ to be the best in town.
Is said to be.
6. The new bridge is expected ___ (open) in May.
Is expected to + base verb.

C1 · Advanced

Is thought to have done, is believed to be doing C1

MeaningFormExample
same time / in progressto be + -ingShe is said to be writing a new novel.
earlier (past)to have + past participleHe is believed to have left the country.
earlier, in progressto have been + -ingThey are thought to have been living in Paris.
earlier, passiveto have been + past participleThe painting is thought to have been stolen in 1990.

  • The robbers are believed to have escaped in a blue car. (earlier action)
  • The actor is reported to be recovering in hospital. (in progress now)
  • The temple is thought to have been built 2,000 years ago. (earlier, passive)
  • She is said to have been working on it for years. (earlier, in progress)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He is believed to left the country. → ✅ He is believed to have left the country. (to have + past participle)
  • ❌ The man is thought to have stole the car. → ✅ The man is thought to have stolen the car. (past participle: stolen)

There is said to be…, There are thought to be… C1

  • There are said to be wolves in these forests. (people say)
  • There is thought to be a link between diet and sleep. (formal)
  • There are believed to have been survivors. (past)
  • There is reported to be a delay on the line. (news)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ There is said to be many problems. → ✅ There are said to be many problems. (plural: there are)
  • ❌ It is said to be a tunnel under the castle. → ✅ There is said to be a tunnel under the castle. (existence: there)

Be supposed to, allegedly, reportedly C1

  • This beach is supposed to be the most beautiful in Greece. (people say (informal))
  • The actor is reportedly planning to retire. (according to reports)
  • He allegedly stole $2 million. (accused, not proven)
  • According to the forecast, it'll snow tomorrow. (source named)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ According to me, the film is great. → ✅ In my opinion, the film is great. (according to + another source, not me)
  • ❌ This museum is suppose to be interesting. → ✅ This museum is supposed to be interesting. (supposed, with -d)

Practice: C1

7. The suspect is believed ___ the country last night.
An earlier action: to have left.
8. The president is reported ___ a new plan at the moment.
In progress: to be preparing.
9. The ship is thought ___ (sink) in a storm in 1712.
Earlier action: to have sunk.

Singular, existence: There is said to be.

Plural: There are thought to be.

Past: to have been.
