زمان گذشتهٔ استمراریPast Continuous (I was doing)

گذشتهٔ استمراری (was / were + فعل ing‌دار) را برای کاری به کار می‌بریم که در لحظهٔ مشخصی در گذشته در جریان بود: At nine o'clock last night I was reading.

اغلب همراه گذشتهٔ ساده می‌آید: I was reading when the phone rang. فرق این دو صفحهٔ جداگانه دارد: گذشتهٔ ساده یا گذشتهٔ استمراری.

A2 · مقدماتی

ساختار و کاربرد: وسط انجام یک کار A2

was / were + فعل ing‌دار: کار در لحظه‌ای در گذشته در جریان بود. At 8 I was having dinner.

فاعلwas / wereفعل ing‌دارمثال
I / he / she / itwasفعل + ‑ingShe was cooking at 7 pm.
you / we / theywereفعل + ‑ingWe were waiting for the bus.

گذشتهٔ استمراری نشان می‌دهد کاری در زمانی در گذشته شروع شده بود و هنوز تمام نشده بود؛ ما وسط انجامش بودیم.

عبارت‌های زمانیِ رایج: at 6 o'clock yesterday, at this time last week, all morning, when you called.

قاعدهٔ املایی ‑ing همان قاعدهٔ حال استمراری است: make → making, sit → sitting, lie → lying. فعل‌های حالتی (know, want, like) معمولاً استمراری نمی‌شوند: I knew the answer (نه was knowing). صفحهٔ فعل‌های حالتی را ببینید.

  • At 7 o'clock this morning I was running in the park. ساعت ۷ در جریان بود
  • The children were playing in the garden all afternoon. در یک بازهٔ زمانی در جریان بود
  • Sorry I missed your call. I was driving. وسط کار بودم
  • What were you doing at midnight? سؤالی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ At 9 pm I was watch TV. → ✅ At 9 pm I was watching TV. was + فعل ing‌دار
  • ❌ They was waiting outside. → ✅ They were waiting outside. با they: were
  • ❌ I was knowing the answer. → ✅ I knew the answer. know فعل حالتی است.

منفی و سؤالی A2

منفی: wasn't / weren't + فعل ing‌دار. سؤالی: Was / Were + فاعل + فعل ing‌دار؟

ساختارمثال
منفیwasn't / weren't + فعل ing‌دارI wasn't listening.
سؤالیWas / Were + فاعل + فعل ing‌دار؟Were you sleeping?
جواب کوتاهYes, … was / were. No, … wasn't / weren't.Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
سؤال با کلمهٔ پرسشیکلمهٔ پرسشی + was / were + فاعل + فعل ing‌دار؟What were you doing?

در سؤالِ گذشتهٔ استمراری did نیاورید: Were you working? نه Did you working?

  • I wasn't feeling well, so I stayed at home. منفی
  • Was it raining when you left? ~ Yes, it was. سؤال + جواب کوتاه
  • Who were you talking to? سؤال با کلمهٔ پرسشی
  • We weren't expecting so many people. منفی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Did you sleeping? → ✅ Were you sleeping? در گذشتهٔ استمراری did نمی‌آید.
  • ❌ What you were doing? → ✅ What were you doing? were قبل از فاعل
  • ❌ I didn't listening. → ✅ I wasn't listening. منفی: wasn't + فعل ing‌دار

B1 · متوسط

دو کار که هم‌زمان در جریان بودند: while B1

دو کارِ طولانی که هم‌زمان در جریان بودند: While I was cooking, he was watching TV.

وقتی دو کارِ طولانی هم‌زمان در جریان بودند، می‌توانیم برای هر دو گذشتهٔ استمراری بیاوریم، اغلب با while («در حالی که»): While I was cooking, my husband was setting the table.

while می‌تواند اول جمله یا وسط آن بیاید: She was reading while the baby was sleeping. وقتی بخشِ while اول می‌آید، بعدش ویرگول بگذارید.

while (یا as) را با کارهای طولانی بیاورید. برای کارِ کوتاهی که وسط یک کار طولانی پیش می‌آید، when + گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید: I was cooking when the lights went out. صفحهٔ گذشتهٔ ساده یا گذشتهٔ استمراری را ببینید.

  • While we were having dinner, it was raining outside. دو کار در جریان
  • Some students were writing notes, and others were looking at their phones. توصیف یک صحنه
  • I was working while my flatmate was sleeping. while وسط جمله

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ While I was cooking, he watching TV. → ✅ While I was cooking, he was watching TV. گذشتهٔ استمراری was / were لازم دارد.
  • ❌ During I was studying, she was working. → ✅ While I was studying, she was working. while + جمله (during + اسم)

چیدن صحنهٔ یک داستان B1

گذشتهٔ استمراری را برای پس‌زمینهٔ داستان و گذشتهٔ ساده را برای اتفاق‌ها به کار ببرید.

اول داستان، گذشتهٔ استمراری موقعیت را توصیف می‌کند: چه چیزهایی دور و برِ اتفاق‌های اصلی در جریان بود. It was raining. People were hurrying home. A man was standing at the corner.

بعد گذشتهٔ ساده اتفاق‌ها را تعریف می‌کند: Suddenly he turned and walked towards me.

این تضاد قلبِ داستان‌گویی در انگلیسی است. برای همهٔ زمان‌های داستان‌گویی صفحهٔ زمان‌های روایی را ببینید.

  • The sun was shining and the birds were singing. پس‌زمینه
  • We were sitting in the café when a strange man came in. پس‌زمینه + اتفاق
  • Everyone was laughing and having a good time. وضعیت در آن زمان

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ When I arrived, everyone danced and sang. → ✅ When I arrived, everyone was dancing and singing. برای توصیف صحنه وقتی رسیدید: گذشتهٔ استمراری.
  • ❌ The music was playing, and suddenly the lights were going out. → ✅ The music was playing, and suddenly the lights went out. اتفاق ناگهانی: گذشتهٔ ساده.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Was always doing: annoying past habits B2

always / constantly + past continuous for repeated past behaviour, often annoying: He was always losing his keys.

Just like the present continuous with always (You're always complaining!), the past continuous with always, constantly, forever describes behaviour that happened again and again, usually with a feeling of annoyance or surprise.

My old flatmate was always borrowing my things without asking. = He did it very often, and it annoyed me.

Compare: He always borrowed my things is a neutral fact. He was always borrowing my things adds feeling: it happened too often. For the present version, see Present Continuous.

  • My brother was always taking my phone. (annoying)
  • She was constantly checking her messages during the meeting. (too often)
  • Our old car was forever breaking down. (it happened again and again)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He always was complaining. → ✅ He was always complaining. (always goes after was)
  • ❌ She was always borrow my pen. → ✅ She was always borrowing my pen. (was always + -ing)

C1 · پیشرفته

Temporary periods, repeated actions and duration C1

The past continuous can show a temporary routine (That summer I was working nights) or stress duration (I was cleaning all morning).

Temporary situations and routines. We use the past continuous for something that was true for a limited period: That year I was cycling to work every day. She was living with her parents while her flat was being repaired.

Duration. With all day, all morning, all week, the past continuous stresses that the activity filled the time, often to explain a result: I was gardening all afternoon, so I'm exhausted.

Not for a counted series. When we say how many times, we use the past simple: I phoned her five times. Not I was phoning her five times.

The past simple is also possible in many of these sentences (That year I cycled to work). The past continuous adds the idea that the situation was temporary or in progress.

  • When I was at university, I was working in a café at weekends. (temporary routine)
  • It was snowing all day, so the roads were closed. (duration + result)
  • In those days we were seeing each other every weekend. (temporary arrangement)
  • I called you three times last night! (a counted series: past simple)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I was calling you three times. → ✅ I called you three times. (Counted actions: past simple.)
  • ❌ I was knowing her for years. → ✅ I had known her for years. (know is stative; up to a past time: past perfect.)

I was wondering if…: polite requests and past plans C1

I was wondering if you could help is a polite request about now. She was leaving the next day is a plan seen from the past.

Politeness. With verbs like wonder, hope, think (of), the past continuous makes a request or suggestion softer and less direct. The meaning is now: I was wondering if you could help me. We were hoping you might join us. I was thinking we could go to the coast.

Plans in the past. The past continuous can describe an arrangement that was in the future at that time: I couldn't stay late because I was flying to Paris the next morning. See Future in the Past.

The degree of politeness, from direct to soft: I wonder if… → I wondered if… → I was wondering if… (softest).

  • I was wondering if I could leave early today. (polite request about now)
  • We were hoping you could come to dinner on Saturday. (polite invitation)
  • She was nervous because she was starting a new job the next day. (plan seen from the past)
  • I was thinking we could try that new restaurant. (soft suggestion)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I was wondering if you can help me. → ✅ I was wondering if you could help me. (Keep the past form: could.)
