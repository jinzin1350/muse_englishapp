Past Continuous and Past Simple

Level: A2 · Free grammar guide

We use the past continuous for an action that was in progress at a time in the past: At 8 o'clock last night, I was watching a film. Form it with was / were + verb-ing.

We often use it together with the past simple. The past continuous is the longer background action; the past simple is the shorter action that happened in the middle of it: I was cooking when the phone rang.

How to form the past continuous

FormExamples
Positivesubject + was / were + verb-ingI was working. They were playing.
Negativesubject + wasn't / weren't + verb-ingShe wasn't listening. We weren't sleeping.
QuestionWas / Were + subject + verb-ing?Were you driving? Was it raining?
Short answersYes, + subject + was/were. / No, + subject + wasn't/weren't.Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.

Use was with I, he, she, it and were with you, we, they.

When do we use the past continuous?

  • An action in progress at a time in the past: At 10 p.m., we were having dinner.
  • A longer action interrupted by a shorter one (past simple): I was walking home when it started to rain.
  • Two actions in progress at the same time: While I was studying, my sister was watching TV.
  • The background of a story: The sun was shining and the birds were singing.

When or while?

We often use when before the short action (past simple) and while before the long action (past continuous):

  1. I was having a shower when the doorbell rang. (when + short action)
  2. While I was having a shower, the doorbell rang. (while + long action)

Compare: When the phone rang, I answered it. (first the phone rang, then I answered: past simple + past simple) and When the phone rang, I was cooking. (I was already cooking).

Past continuous or past simple?

Past simplePast continuous
a complete, finished actionan action in progress, not finished at that moment
I wrote an email at 9. (I wrote it at 9)I was writing an email at 9. (I was in the middle of it)
It rained a lot yesterday.It was raining when I left the house.

State verbs like know, want, like, need and have (= own) don't usually go in the continuous: I knew the answer. (not I was knowing)

Examples

  1. At 7 o'clock this morning, I was having breakfast. (in progress at a time)
  2. They were playing football when it started to snow. (interrupted action)
  3. Were you sleeping when I called? (question)
  4. She wasn't wearing a coat, so she was cold. (negative)
  5. While we were waiting for the bus, we saw an accident. (while + long action)
  6. What were you doing at midnight? (wh- question)
  7. He was driving too fast when the police stopped him. (interrupted action)
  8. I was reading while my brother was cooking. (two actions at the same time)
  9. The children were laughing and the music was playing. (background)
  10. I cut my finger while I was cooking. (short action + long action)

Common mistakes

❌ I was work when you called.

✅ I was working when you called. (Use was/were + verb-ing.)

❌ They was playing tennis.

✅ They were playing tennis. (They takes were.)

❌ I was knowing the answer.

✅ I knew the answer. (Know is a state verb: use the past simple.)

❌ Were you study at 9 p.m.?

✅ Were you studying at 9 p.m.? (Use verb + -ing.)

❌ I was walking home when I was seeing my teacher.

✅ I was walking home when I saw my teacher. (The short action is past simple.)

❌ What did you doing at 8?

✅ What were you doing at 8? (Past continuous questions use was/were, not did.)

❌ She didn't sleeping when I got home.

✅ She wasn't sleeping when I got home. (Negative: wasn't/weren't + verb-ing.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. At 9 p.m. yesterday, I ___ TV.
An action in progress at a past time: I + was + verb-ing.
2. They ___ football when it started to rain.
They + were + verb-ing for the longer action.
3. I was cooking when the phone ___.
The short action that interrupted: past simple. Ring → rang.
4. ___ you sleeping when I called?
Past continuous question with you: Were + subject + verb-ing.
5. While she ___ for the bus, she met an old friend.
While + the long action: she + was waiting.
6. What ___ you doing at midnight last night?
Past continuous question with you: were.
7. He ___ the answer, so he put his hand up.
Know is a state verb: past simple, knew.
8. I ___ my finger while I was cutting vegetables.
The short, sudden action: past simple. Cut → cut.
9. The sun ___ and the birds were singing.
Background of a story: the sun (it) + was shining.
10. We ___ when you rang the doorbell.
Negative with we: weren't + verb-ing.
11. Choose the correct sentence.
Long action: was walking; short action: saw.
12. ___ the children playing outside when the storm started?
The children is plural: Were + subject + verb-ing.
13. When I arrived at the party, my friends ___. (They started before I arrived.)
They were already in the middle of dancing: were dancing.
14. While I was studying, my brother ___ video games.
Two actions at the same time: my brother (he) + was playing.
15. It ___ when we left the house, so we took umbrellas.
The rain was in progress when we left: was raining.
16. When the bell rang, the students ___ their books and left.
One action after another: past simple, closed … and left.
