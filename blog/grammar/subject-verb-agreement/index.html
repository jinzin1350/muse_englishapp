مطابقت فاعل و فعلSubject-Verb Agreement

فعل باید با فاعلش جور باشد: فاعل مفرد فعل مفرد می‌گیرد (She works) و فاعل جمع فعل جمع (They work).

با فاعل‌های کوتاه این کار آسان است، اما با کلمه‌هایی مثل everyone, each و one of و با فاعل‌های طولانی سخت‌تر می‌شود. برای اسم‌های گروهی مثل team و family صفحهٔ اسم‌های جمع را ببینید.

A1 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

he works, they work؛ there is, there are A1

در زمان حال، با he, she, it و اسم مفرد ‑s بیاورید (My brother works). با I, you, we, they و اسم جمع ‑s نمی‌آید (My brothers work). there is + مفرد و there are + جمع.

فاعلbeفعل‌های دیگر (حال)
Iamwork
he / she / it، اسم مفردisworks
you / we / they، اسم جمعarework

There is + اسم مفرد یا غیرقابل‌شمارش: There is a bank. There is some milk. There are + جمع: There are two banks.

گذشتهٔ be هم عوض می‌شود: I / he / she / it was؛ you / we / they were. و با he, she, it has: She has a car.

  • My sister lives in Toronto. اسم مفرد + ‑s
  • My parents live in Shiraz. اسم جمع
  • There is a park near my house. مفرد
  • There are three bedrooms in our flat. جمع

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ My brother work in a bank. → ✅ My brother works in a bank. اسم مفرد: ‑s.
  • ❌ The children is in the garden. → ✅ The children are in the garden. جمع: are.
  • ❌ There is two cafés on my street. → ✅ There are two cafés on my street. جمع: there are.

تمرین سطح A1

1. My father ___ in a hospital.
اسم مفرد: works.
2. There ___ two supermarkets near my flat.
جمع: there are.
3. These days my cat ___ (sleep) on my bed every night.
اسم مفرد: + ‑s.

B1 · متوسط

everyone is, each student has, one of my friends is B1

everyone, everybody, someone, nobody, everything فعل مفرد می‌گیرند (Everyone is here)، هرچند معنی‌شان جمع به نظر می‌رسد. each و every + اسم هم همین‌طور. one of + اسم جمع هم فعل مفرد می‌گیرد (One of my friends lives in Paris).

فاعلفعلمثال
everyone, everybody, someone, nobody, everything, nothingمفردEverybody knows him. Nothing is impossible.
each / every + اسم مفردمفردEvery student has a laptop.
each of + جمعمفردEach of the rooms has a TV.
one of + اسم جمعمفردOne of my sisters is a doctor.
both, many, few, severalجمعBoth my brothers are tall.

فعل با one جور می‌شود، نه با اسم جمعِ بعد از of: One of the students is late. (یکی از دانشجوها دیر کرده.)

بعد از everyone / someone اغلب برای اشاره به آن‌ها they / their می‌آوریم: Everyone has their own room.

  • Everyone is welcome. مفرد
  • Every child needs love. every + مفرد
  • One of my friends works at the airport. one of
  • Each of the hotels has a pool. each of

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Everybody are tired. → ✅ Everybody is tired. everybody: مفرد.
  • ❌ One of my cousins live in Dubai. → ✅ One of my cousins lives in Dubai. one of: مفرد.
  • ❌ Every students has a book. → ✅ Every student has a book. every + اسم مفرد.

تمرین سطح B1

4. Everybody ___ ready to start.
Everybody + فعل مفرد.
5. One of my brothers ___ in Germany.
One of + فعل مفرد.
6. Every room in this hotel ___ (have) a balcony. Ours looks out over the sea.
Every + اسم مفرد + فعل مفرد.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Long subjects: the list of names is, either… or B2

The verb agrees with the main noun of the subject, not the noun closest to it: The list of names is on the wall. With as well as / along with, it agrees with the first subject. With either… or / neither… nor, it agrees with the nearest subject.

PatternExample
noun + of + nounThe quality of the photos is excellent.
noun + relative clauseThe people who live next door are very kind.
as well as, along with, together withThe teacher, as well as the students, is here.
either… or / neither… norNeither my sister nor my parents are coming. Either Ali or Sara is coming.
the number of / a number ofThe number of students is rising. A number of students are absent.

Find the head noun and ignore of-phrases and relative clauses: The box of chocolates is on the table.

The number of = the total (singular verb). A number of = several (plural verb).

  • The price of these shoes is too high. (head noun: price)
  • The students in my class come from ten countries. (head noun: students)
  • A number of people have complained. (a number of: plural)
  • Neither the manager nor the staff were happy. (nearest subject)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The quality of the products are excellent. → ✅ The quality of the products is excellent. (head noun: quality)
  • ❌ The number of tourists are increasing. → ✅ The number of tourists is increasing. (the number of: singular)
  • ❌ The manager, as well as his assistants, are coming. → ✅ The manager, as well as his assistants, is coming. (as well as: first subject)

تمرین سطح B2

7. The price of houses in this city ___ very high.
Head noun: price (singular).
8. A number of students ___ asked for extra help.
A number of + plural verb.
9. The people who work in this office ___ (be) very friendly.
Head noun: people (plural).

C1 · پیشرفته

Amounts, what-clauses and none of them is or are C1

Amounts of money, time and distance usually take a singular verb (Ten dollars is enough). What-clauses usually take a singular verb (What we need is more time). With none of + plural, both singular and plural are used.

PatternExample
amounts as one unitTwo hours is too long to wait. Five kilometres is a long way.
what-clausesWhat I want is a holiday.
none of + pluralNone of my friends has / have a car. (singular = more formal)
most / half / all of + nounagrees with the noun: Most of the cake is gone. Most of the students are here.
more than one + singular nounsingular: More than one person has complained.

With percentages, fractions and most / some / all / half of, the verb agrees with the noun after of: Half of the money is gone. Half of the guests are here.

None of + plural: the singular verb is traditional and formal; the plural is very common and accepted. A what-clause takes a singular verb before a singular complement (What I need is a holiday); before a plural complement, a plural verb is also common (What we need are new ideas).

  • Fifty dollars is a lot for a T-shirt. (amount)
  • What surprised me was his attitude. (what-clause)
  • Most of the information is out of date. (agrees with information)
  • More than one student has failed. (more than one)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Fifty kilometres are a long way to drive. → ✅ Fifty kilometres is a long way to drive. (amount as one unit)
  • ❌ The majority of the work are done. → ✅ The majority of the work is done. (agrees with work)
  • ❌ More than one person have called. → ✅ More than one person has called. (more than one + singular verb)

تمرین سطح C1

10. Ten kilometres ___ a long way to walk.
Amount as one unit: singular.
11. What we really need ___ a bigger kitchen.
What-clause + singular complement (a bigger kitchen): singular verb.
12. Half of the students ___ (be) absent today because of the flu.
Half of + plural noun: plural verb.
