Conditionals in English

Conditionals are sentences with if (or a word like unless) that connect a condition with a result: If it rains, we'll stay at home.

The tenses you use depend on two things: is the situation real or imaginary, and is it about the past, the present or the future? This page shows all the types side by side. Each topic below has its own page with rules, examples and exercises at every level.

The conditionals at a glance

TypeFormUseExample
Zeroif + present, presentalways trueIf you heat ice, it melts.
Firstif + present, will + verba real possibility in the futureIf it rains, we'll stay in.
Secondif + past, would + verbimaginary now / unlikely futureIf I had a car, I'd drive.
Thirdif + past perfect, would have + past participleimaginary pastIf I'd known, I'd have come.
Mixedif + past perfect, would + verbimaginary past → result nowIf I'd taken the job, I'd be rich.

Which one do I need?

  • Is it a general truth or something that always happens? → zero: If you press this, it stops.
  • Is it a real possibility in the future? → first: If I see her, I'll tell her.
  • Is it imaginary now, or unlikely in the future? → second: If I were you, I'd wait.
  • Is it about a past that didn't happen? → third: If we'd left earlier, we'd have caught the train.
  • Does an imaginary past change now? → mixed: If I hadn't missed the bus, I'd be at work now.
  • Do you want to say if not, only if, as a precaution or it doesn't matter? → unless, as long as, in case, even if.
  • Do you want to say you'd like something to be different? → I wish…, If only…, It's time…, I'd rather…

Formal conditionals

Topics in this family

Zero and First Conditional

A2 B1 B2 C1

Real conditions: If you heat ice, it melts (always true). If it rains, we'll stay at home (possible future).

Second Conditional (If I had…, I would…)

A2 B1 C1

Imaginary or unlikely situations now or in the future: If I had more time, I would learn the piano.

Third Conditional (If I had known…, I would have…)

B1 B2 C1

Imaginary past: things that didn't happen and their results. If I had known, I would have helped.

Mixed Conditionals

B2 C1

Mix past and present: If I had taken that job, I would be rich now.

Unless, As Long As, Provided, In Case and Even If

B1 B2 C1

Other ways to make conditions: unless (= if not), as long as, provided, in case, even if.

Wish and If Only

B1 B2 C1

Wanting things to be different: I wish I had more time. I wish I hadn't said that. If only it would stop raining!

Unreal Past (It's time we went, I'd rather you didn't…)

B1 B2 C1

Past forms that don't mean past: It's time we went. I'd rather you stayed. He acts as if he owned the place.

Inverted Conditionals (Had I known…, Should you need…)

C1 C2

Formal conditionals without if: Had I known… Were she to leave… Should you need help…
