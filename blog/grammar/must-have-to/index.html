خانه / گرامر / must و have to

must و have to (اجبار)Must and Have To (Obligation)

must و have to را برای گفتن اینکه کاری لازم است («باید») به کار می‌بریم: I have to get up early tomorrow. You must wear a helmet.

منفی‌هایشان خیلی با هم فرق دارد: You mustn't smoke here (اجازه ندارید؛ «نباید») اما You don't have to come (لازم نیست). برای نصیحت صفحهٔ should و ought to را ببینید.

A2 · B1 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

have to: لازم است A2

have to / has to + شکل سادهٔ فعل یعنی لازم است: I have to work on Saturday. She has to wear a uniform.

I / you / we / theyhe / she / it
مثبتI have to go.She has to go.
منفیI don't have to go.She doesn't have to go.
سؤالیDo you have to go?Does she have to go?

have to مثل یک فعل معمولی رفتار می‌کند و برای سؤالی و منفی از do / does کمک می‌گیرد: Do you have to work tomorrow?

برای قانون‌ها، کارهای روزمره و چیزهایی که دیگران تعیین می‌کنند خیلی رایج است: In my job I have to answer a lot of emails.

تلفظ: در گفتار تند، have to شبیه /ˈhæftə/ و has to شبیه /ˈhæstə/ شنیده می‌شود.

  • I have to take the bus to work. لازم است
  • My brother has to wear glasses. با he / she / it: has to
  • Do we have to pay for parking? سؤالی
  • You don't have to bring anything. لازم نیست

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She have to work today. → ✅ She has to work today. با he / she / it: has to
  • ❌ Do you have go? → ✅ Do you have to go? have + to
  • ❌ I must to go now. → ✅ I have to go now. (or I must go now.) بعد از must حرف to نمی‌آید

must: اجبار قوی و توصیهٔ جدی A2

must + شکل سادهٔ فعل برای اجبار قوی، اغلب حسِ خودِ گوینده، و برای قانون‌ها: I must call my mum. You must wear a seat belt.

must برای همهٔ اشخاص یکی است و to ندارد: I must go, she must go. نه She musts go و نه I must to go.

must را برای این‌ها به کار می‌بریم:

  • چیزهایی که خودمان لازم می‌دانیم: I must remember to call Lisa.
  • قانون‌ها و تابلوها، اغلب در نوشته: Passengers must wear a seat belt.
  • توصیه‌های جدی: You must try this cake. It's amazing!

must فقط برای حال و آینده است. برای گذشته had to بیاورید: Yesterday I had to work late. نه I musted. بخش must یا have to؟ را ببینید.

  • I must finish this essay tonight. حسِ خودم
  • Visitors must sign in at reception. قانون نوشته‌شده
  • You must visit Isfahan. It's beautiful. توصیهٔ جدی
  • We must have dinner together soon. پیشنهاد دوستانه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She musts leave now. → ✅ She must leave now. must حرف ‑s نمی‌گیرد
  • ❌ You must to see this film. → ✅ You must see this film. must + شکل سادهٔ فعل (بدون to)
  • ❌ I must went to the bank yesterday. → ✅ I had to go to the bank yesterday. گذشته: had to

mustn't یا don't have to؟ A2

mustn't یعنی اجازه نیست / این کار را نکنید («نباید»). don't have to یعنی لازم نیست (اگر بخواهید می‌توانید).

mustn'tdon't have to
You mustn't smoke here. (ممنوع است.)You don't have to come. (لازم نیست.)
You mustn't tell anyone. (به هیچ‌کس نگو!)You don't have to tell me. (اگر بخواهی می‌توانی بگویی.)
Students mustn't use phones in the exam.Students don't have to wear a uniform.

mustn't (must not) یعنی موظفید کاری را نکنید: You mustn't park here.

don't / doesn't have to یعنی هیچ اجباری نیست: You don't have to pay. It's free. می‌توانید انجامش بدهید، اما لازم نیست.

این یکی از رایج‌ترین اشتباه‌هاست (چون در فارسی هر دو را «نباید» ترجمه می‌کنیم). از خودتان بپرسید: ممنوع است؟ ← mustn't. اختیاری است؟ ← don't have to.

  • You mustn't touch that wire. It's dangerous. ممنوع
  • You don't have to take your shoes off. It's fine. لازم نیست
  • We mustn't be late for the interview. مهم است که این کار نشود
  • She doesn't have to work on Fridays. اجباری نیست

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You don't have to smoke in the hospital. (meaning it's forbidden) → ✅ You mustn't smoke in the hospital. ممنوع: mustn't
  • ❌ It's free. You mustn't pay. → ✅ It's free. You don't have to pay. لازم نیست: don't have to

تمرین سطح A2

با she: has to.

سؤالی: Do you have to…?

با we: have to.
4. All students ___ bring their ID cards to the exam.
قانون: must + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
5. You ___ see this film. It's fantastic!
توصیهٔ جدی: must.

must + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
7. It's a secret. You ___ tell anyone.
گفتنش ممنوع است: mustn't.
8. Tomorrow is a holiday, so I ___ go to work.
لازم نیست: don't have to.

لازم نیست: don't have to.

B1 · متوسط

must یا have to؟ B1

must اغلب یعنی گوینده آن را لازم می‌داند. have to یعنی یک قانون یا موقعیت آن را لازم کرده. برای گذشته و آینده had to / will have to بیاورید.

musthave to
I must stop eating sweets. (خودم تصمیم می‌گیرم)I have to wear a uniform. (مدرسه تعیین می‌کند)
You must come and see us. (دلم می‌خواهد بیایی)You have to show your passport. (قانون است)

در خیلی از جمله‌ها هر دو ممکن‌اند، به‌خصوص در انگلیسی بریتانیایی. اما اغلب فرقی هست:

  • must یعنی حس یا تصمیمِ خودِ گوینده: I must call her. (فکر می‌کنم مهم است.)
  • have to یعنی قانون یا موقعیتی بیرونی: I have to call her. It's my job.

در انگلیسی آمریکایی برای اجبار have to (یا have got to) خیلی رایج‌تر از must است.

have got to همان معنی have to را دارد و در گفتار خودمانی رایج است: I've got to go. فقط در زمان حال به کار می‌رود.

  • I must go to bed earlier. I'm always tired. تصمیم خودم
  • I have to be at work by eight. My boss is strict. قانون بیرونی
  • This is a great book. You must read it. توصیهٔ گوینده
  • In Canada you have to be 16 to drive. قانون

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I must wear a uniform at work. (the company decides) → ✅ I have to wear a uniform at work. قانون بیرونی: have to (طبیعی‌تر)
  • ❌ I've got to went yesterday. → ✅ I had to go yesterday. گذشته: had to

had to, will have to, have had to B1

must شکل گذشته یا آینده ندارد. برای گذشته had to، برای آینده will have to و برای حال کامل have had to بیاورید.

زمانساختارمثال
گذشتهhad toI had to work late yesterday.
گذشتهٔ منفیdidn't have toWe didn't have to pay.
آیندهwill have toYou'll have to wait.
حال کاملhave / has had toI've had to cancel my trip.
بعد از فعل‌های وجهیmight / may have toWe might have to move.

سؤال در گذشته: Did you have to wait long?

برای آینده، وقتی همین حالا تصمیم گرفته می‌شود must و have to هر دو ممکن‌اند: I must / have to leave early tomorrow. وقتی اجبار در آینده به وجود می‌آید will have to بیاورید: If you fail, you'll have to take the exam again.

  • When I was a child, I had to walk to school. اجبار در گذشته
  • Did you have to show your passport? سؤال در گذشته
  • You'll have to book a table. It's always busy. آینده
  • She's had to work every weekend this month. حال کامل

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I musted work yesterday. → ✅ I had to work yesterday. گذشته: had to
  • ❌ Did you had to wait? → ✅ Did you have to wait? did + have to

need to, don't need to, needn't B1

need to یعنی لازم است. don't need to / needn't یعنی لازم نیست: You needn't come. You don't need to come.

need to مثل یک فعل معمولی رفتار می‌کند: I need to buy some milk. Do you need to leave?

برای لازم نبودن، don't need to یا needn't (بیشتر بریتانیایی و کمی رسمی) بیاورید: You don't need to shout. / You needn't shout. هر دو همان معنی don't have to را دارند.

بعد از needn't شکل سادهٔ فعل بدون to می‌آید: You needn't worry. نه You needn't to worry.

برای گذشته، didn't need to و needn't have معنی‌های متفاوتی دارند. صفحهٔ should have، could have و needn't have را ببینید.

  • I need to charge my phone. لازم است
  • You don't need to bring a towel. The hotel has them. لازم نیست
  • You needn't worry. Everything is fine. لازم نیست (بریتانیایی)
  • Do I need to book in advance? سؤالی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You needn't to come. → ✅ You needn't come. needn't + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ She doesn't needs to work. → ✅ She doesn't need to work. doesn't + need
  • ❌ Need I to bring anything? → ✅ Do I need to bring anything? سؤالی: Do I need to…?

C1 · پیشرفته

Formal obligation: be required to, be obliged to, be to C1

Formal ways to talk about obligation: be required to, be obliged to, be compelled to. Orders and rules: be to (You are to report at 9). Prohibition: be not permitted / be prohibited from.

In formal writing (rules, contracts, official letters) must and have to are often replaced by:

  • be required to + verb: All staff are required to complete the training.
  • be obliged to + verb: The company is obliged to inform its customers. (often legal)
  • be compelled / forced to + verb: something makes you do it against your wishes: He was compelled to resign.

Be to + verb gives an official order or instruction: You are to report to the manager at 9 am. Nobody is to leave the building.

For prohibition: be not permitted to, be prohibited from + -ing, be forbidden to / from: Smoking is not permitted. Visitors are prohibited from taking photos.

In everyday speech these sound very formal. Use them in writing, notices and official contexts.

  • Applicants are required to submit two references. (formal requirement)
  • The landlord is obliged to repair the heating. (legal duty)
  • Guests are not permitted to bring food into the rooms. (prohibition)
  • You are to stay here until the police arrive. (official order)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Passengers are required wear seat belts. → ✅ Passengers are required to wear seat belts. (required to + verb)
  • ❌ Visitors are prohibited to take photos. → ✅ Visitors are prohibited from taking photos. (prohibited from + -ing)

تمرین سطح C1

19. All employees ___ to wear protective clothing.
Be required to + verb.
20. Visitors are prohibited ___ the animals.
Prohibited from + -ing.
21. You ___ (be) to report to the main office at 8 am tomorrow.
Be to = an official instruction.
