although، despite و in spite ofAlthough, Despite and In Spite Of

این کلمه‌ها تضاد را نشان می‌دهند: چیزی با وجود چیز دیگری غیرمنتظره است (یعنی «با اینکه»، «با وجودِ»): Although it was raining, we went out. Despite the rain, we went out.

فرق اصلی در دستور است: although + جمله‌واره (فاعل + فعل)، despite / in spite of + اسم یا ‑ing. برای but و however صفحهٔ and، but، or، so، because را ببینید.

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

although و though + جمله‌واره A2

although (یا though) + فاعل + فعل یک تضاد غیرمنتظره را نشان می‌دهد (با اینکه): Although it was cold, we went swimming. although و but را با هم نیاورید.

الگومثال
although + جمله‌واره، جملهٔ اصلیAlthough I was tired, I finished my homework.
جملهٔ اصلی + although + جمله‌وارهI finished my homework although I was tired.
though (خودمانی‌تر)Though it was late, she called me.

یک کلمهٔ ربط بیاورید، نه دوتا: Although it was expensive, I bought it. یا It was expensive, but I bought it. (نه Although it was expensive, but I bought it.). فارسی می‌گوید «با اینکه گران بود، ولی خریدمش»، اما انگلیسی فقط یکی را می‌آورد.

وقتی جمله‌وارهٔ although اول می‌آید، بعدش ویرگول بگذارید.

  • Although he's rich, he lives in a small flat. although در اول
  • We enjoyed the trip although the weather was bad. although در وسط
  • Though it was raining, they played tennis. though
  • Although she's only six, she can read very well. تضاد غیرمنتظره

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Although it was late, but I wasn't tired. → ✅ Although it was late, I wasn't tired. although و but با هم نه.
  • ❌ Although the rain, we went out. → ✅ Although it was raining, we went out. although + جمله‌واره.

تمرین سطح A2

1. ___ it was very cold, the children played outside.
تضاد: Although.
2. کدام درست است؟
فقط یک کلمهٔ ربط.
3. ___ he's 80, my grandfather still goes running every day. (one word)
تضاد: Although.

B1 · متوسط

despite / in spite of + اسم یا ‑ing B1

despite و in spite of همان معنی although را دارند (با وجودِ)، اما بعدشان اسم، ضمیر یا ‑ing می‌آید: Despite the rain… In spite of being tired… با جمله‌واره despite the fact that بیاورید.

الگومثال
despite / in spite of + اسمDespite the rain, we went out.
despite / in spite of + ‑ingIn spite of being tired, she kept working.
despite / in spite of the fact that + جمله‌وارهDespite the fact that it was raining, we went out.
although + جمله‌وارهAlthough it was raining, we went out.

بگویید despite (بدون of) یا in spite of (با of). نه despite of.

despite + فاعل + فعل نمی‌شود: نه Despite it was raining. although یا despite the fact that بیاورید.

  • Despite the traffic, we arrived on time. despite + اسم
  • In spite of his injury, he finished the race. in spite of + اسم
  • She passed despite not studying much. despite + ‑ing
  • Despite the fact that he's 70, he's very fit. despite the fact that

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Despite of the rain, we played. → ✅ Despite the rain, we played. بعد از despite حرف of نمی‌آید.
  • ❌ Despite it was cold, we swam. → ✅ Although it was cold, we swam. despite + اسم، نه جمله‌واره.
  • ❌ In spite the noise, I slept well. → ✅ In spite of the noise, I slept well. in spite of.

تمرین سطح B1

با اسم: Despite.
5. She got the job ___ having no experience.
با ‑ing: in spite of.
6. In ___ of the noise, the baby slept all night. (one word)

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Even though, even if, and though at the end B2

Even though = a stronger although (a real fact). Even if = it doesn't matter whether it happens (a possible situation). Though can also go at the end of a sentence, meaning however.

PatternMeaningExample
even thoughstronger although; it's trueShe went to work even though she was ill.
even ifwhether or not; it may happenI'll go even if it rains.
…, though (end)however (informal)It was cold. I enjoyed it, though.

Compare: Even though I'm tired, I'll help you. (I am tired.) / Even if I'm tired, I'll help you. (Maybe I'll be tired, maybe not.)

Don't say even although or even despite.

  • He didn't stop even though he was exhausted. (real fact)
  • Even if you apologise, she won't forgive you. (possible situation)
  • The food was expensive. It was delicious, though. (though at the end)
  • Even though she lives near me, I rarely see her. (real fact)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Even although it was late, we stayed. → ✅ Even though it was late, we stayed. (even though (not even although))
  • ❌ Even if he's my brother, I don't trust him. (He is my brother.) → ✅ Even though he's my brother, I don't trust him. (a real fact: even though)

Whereas, while, on the other hand B2

To compare two different facts, use whereas or while (I like tea, whereas my wife likes coffee) or On the other hand at the start of a new sentence. On the contrary is different: it means that's not true; the opposite is true.

LinkerExample
whereas / while / whilst (BrE)My brother is tall, whereas I'm short.
on the other handThe flat is small. On the other hand, it's very cheap.
on the contrary (= the opposite is true)You're not boring. On the contrary, you're very interesting!

Whereas and while join two clauses in one sentence. On the other hand usually starts a new sentence.

Often on the one hand … on the other hand: On the one hand, it's cheap. On the other hand, it's far from the city.

  • Ali loves sport, whereas his sister hates it. (two different facts)
  • While some people enjoy the heat, others hate it. (while = whereas)
  • City life is exciting. On the other hand, it can be stressful. (new sentence)
  • I'm not angry. On the contrary, I'm happy for you. (the opposite is true)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The flat is small. On the contrary, it's cheap. → ✅ The flat is small. On the other hand, it's cheap. (comparing two facts: on the other hand)
  • ❌ My father is calm, whereas my mother is calm too. → ✅ My father is calm, whereas my mother is very nervous. (whereas = a difference)

تمرین سطح B2

Whether or not: even if.

A real fact: even though.

Two different facts: whereas.
11. The job is well paid. ___, the hours are very long.

C1 · پیشرفته

Albeit, much as, notwithstanding, tired as I was C1

Formal concession: albeit + adjective / adverb / noun phrase, much as + clause (= although I really), notwithstanding (= despite), adjective / adverb + as / though + clause (Tired as I was, …), however + adjective (However hard I try, …).

PatternExampleMeaning
albeit + adjective / phraseThe plan worked, albeit slowly.although it was
much as + clauseMuch as I like him, I can't agree.although I really
notwithstanding + nounNotwithstanding the cost, the project went ahead.despite
adjective + as / though + clauseTired as she was, she kept going.although she was very tired
however + adjective / adverbHowever hard I try, I can't do it.it doesn't matter how
nonetheless / neverthelessIt was risky. Nonetheless, we did it.however, in spite of this

Albeit is never followed by a full clause: a small, albeit important, change (not albeit it was important).

Notwithstanding can also come after the noun: The cost notwithstanding, the project went ahead.

  • It was a success, albeit a small one. (albeit + noun phrase)
  • Much as I'd love to come, I'm too busy. (much as)
  • Strange as it may seem, I've never seen the sea. (adjective + as)
  • However much you earn, you'll always want more. (however + much)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The plan worked, albeit it was slow. → ✅ The plan worked, albeit slowly. (albeit + no clause)
  • ❌ Much as I like him, but I can't agree. → ✅ Much as I like him, I can't agree. (much as: no but)

تمرین سطح C1

Albeit + adjective phrase.
14. ___ I admire her work, I don't agree with her methods.
Much as = although I really.
15. ___ hard she tried, she couldn't open the jar. (one word)
However + adverb.
