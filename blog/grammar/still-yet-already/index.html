Still, Yet, Already, Any More and No Longer

A2 · B1 · C1

Still = something continues. Yet = something we expect hasn't happened (in negatives and questions). Already = something happened sooner than expected.

For just, yet and already with the present perfect, see also Present Perfect.

A2 · Elementary

Still, yet, already A2

WordUsePositionExample
stillcontinuingmiddle (before the main verb, after be)She still lives with her parents. I'm still hungry.
alreadysooner than expectedmiddle or endI've already eaten. / I've eaten already.
yetnot until now (negatives, questions)endHave you finished yet? I haven't finished yet.

  • Is it still raining? (still)
  • I've already seen this film. (already)
  • Have you done your homework yet? (yet: question)
  • The bus hasn't come yet. (yet: negative)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I have finished my work yet. → ✅ I have already finished my work. (positive: already)
  • ❌ She lives still in Tehran. → ✅ She still lives in Tehran. (still before the main verb)
  • ❌ Have you yet eaten? → ✅ Have you eaten yet? (yet at the end)

Practice: A2

1. It's 10 am, and he's ___ in bed!
Continuing: still.
2. Have you finished your homework ___?
Question: yet at the end.
3. I don't need to buy milk. I've ___ bought some. (one word)
Sooner than expected: already.

B1 · Intermediate

Still not, not yet, already in questions B1

SentenceMeaning
She hasn't replied yet.neutral: I'm waiting
She still hasn't replied.surprise or impatience: it's late
Have you finished yet?neutral question
Have you finished already?surprise: that was fast!

  • I've been waiting for an hour, and the bus still hasn't come! (impatience)
  • The results haven't come out yet. (neutral)
  • Are you leaving already? It's only nine! (surprise)
  • She still doesn't understand. (still + negative)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He hasn't still called me. → ✅ He still hasn't called me. (still before the negative)
  • ❌ Have you finished already? (neutral question) → ✅ Have you finished yet? (neutral question: yet)

Not any more, not any longer, no longer B1

PatternExample
not… any more (BrE) / anymore (AmE)He doesn't play football any more.
not… any longerI can't wait any longer.
no longer (formal)The shop no longer sells newspapers.
still (the opposite)He still plays football.

  • I don't drink coffee any more. (any more)
  • We can't stay any longer. (any longer)
  • He no longer lives in Tehran. (no longer)
  • Do you still play the piano? ~ No, not any more. (still / any more)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I no longer don't work there. → ✅ I no longer work there. (no longer + positive verb)
  • ❌ She doesn't any more work here. → ✅ She doesn't work here any more. (any more at the end)

Practice: B1

4. I sent the email three weeks ago, and he ___ replied!
Impatience: still before the negative.
5. You've finished ___? That was quick!
Surprise: already.
6. It's 11 pm, and my son ___ hasn't come home. I'm worried. (one word)
Impatience or worry: still hasn't.
7. I used to smoke, but I don't ___.
Stopped: not… any more.
8. This model is ___ available.
Formal, after be: no longer.
9. I'm sorry, I can't wait any ___. I have to go. (one word)
Not… any longer.

C1 · Advanced

Formal yet and still: has yet to, as yet, still harder C1

PatternExampleMeaning
have / be yet to + verbWe have yet to receive payment.we haven't received it yet
as yet (formal)As yet, nobody has claimed the prize.until now
(and) yetThe plan is risky, (and) yet it could work.but
Still,…It was raining. Still, we had a great time.even so
still + comparativeThe second test was still harder.even harder
superlative + yetThis is her best work yet.so far

  • The cause of the fire has yet to be found. (has yet to)
  • As yet, there's no cure. (as yet)
  • It's a small car, yet it's very comfortable. (yet = but)
  • The weather was awful. Still, we enjoyed the trip. (still = even so)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The results have not yet to be announced. → ✅ The results have yet to be announced. (have yet to: positive form)
  • ❌ This is her better film yet. → ✅ This is her best film yet. (superlative + yet)

Practice: C1

10. The new bridge has ___ be opened.
Have yet to = haven't… yet.
11. It rained all day. ___, we had a great time.
Still,… = even so.
12. This is the band's best album ___. (one word)
Superlative + yet = so far.
