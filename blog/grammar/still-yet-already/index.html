still، yet، already، any more و no longerStill, Yet, Already, Any More and No Longer

still (هنوز) یعنی چیزی ادامه دارد. yet (هنوز / تا حالا) یعنی چیزی که انتظارش را داریم هنوز اتفاق نیفتاده (در جملهٔ منفی و سؤالی). already (قبلاً، همین حالا) یعنی چیزی زودتر از انتظار اتفاق افتاده.

برای just, yet و already با حال کامل، صفحهٔ حال کامل را هم ببینید.

A2 · B1 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

still, yet, already A2

still یعنی ادامه دارد (It's still raining، هنوز باران می‌آید). already یعنی زودتر از انتظار (I've already finished، تمامش کرده‌ام). yet یعنی تا الان، در جملهٔ منفی و سؤالی (Has she called yet? She hasn't called yet.)

کلمهکاربردجامثال
stillادامه داردوسط جمله (قبل از فعل اصلی، بعد از be)She still lives with her parents. I'm still hungry.
alreadyزودتر از انتظاروسط یا آخر جملهI've already eaten. / I've eaten already.
yetتا الان نه (منفی، سؤالی)آخر جملهHave you finished yet? I haven't finished yet.

yet معمولاً آخر جمله می‌آید. still و already معمولاً وسط جمله.

  • Is it still raining? still (هنوز)
  • I've already seen this film. already
  • Have you done your homework yet? yet در سؤال
  • The bus hasn't come yet. yet در جملهٔ منفی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I have finished my work yet. → ✅ I have already finished my work. جملهٔ مثبت: already.
  • ❌ She lives still in Tehran. → ✅ She still lives in Tehran. still قبل از فعل اصلی می‌آید.
  • ❌ Have you yet eaten? → ✅ Have you eaten yet? yet آخر جمله می‌آید.

تمرین سطح A2

1. It's 10 am, and he's ___ in bed!
ادامه دارد: still.
2. Have you finished your homework ___?
سؤال: yet در آخر.
3. I don't need to buy milk. I've ___ bought some. (one word)
زودتر از انتظار: already.

B1 · متوسط

still not، not yet، و already در سؤال B1

still در جملهٔ منفی تعجب یا بی‌حوصلگی را نشان می‌دهد: He still hasn't called! not… yet خنثی است: He hasn't called yet. already در سؤال تعجب را نشان می‌دهد که چیزی زود اتفاق افتاده: Have you finished already?

جملهمعنی
She hasn't replied yet.خنثی: منتظرم
She still hasn't replied.تعجب یا بی‌حوصلگی: دیر شده
Have you finished yet?سؤال خنثی
Have you finished already?تعجب: چه زود!

در جملهٔ منفی، still قبل از فعل کمکیِ منفی می‌آید: He still hasn't paid me. (نه He hasn't still paid me)

  • I've been waiting for an hour, and the bus still hasn't come! بی‌حوصلگی
  • The results haven't come out yet. خنثی
  • Are you leaving already? It's only nine! تعجب
  • She still doesn't understand. still + منفی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He hasn't still called me. → ✅ He still hasn't called me. still قبل از منفی می‌آید.
  • ❌ Have you finished already? (neutral question) → ✅ Have you finished yet? سؤال خنثی: yet.

not any more, not any longer, no longer (دیگر نه) B1

برای چیزی که تمام شده، not… any more / any longer (یا no longer که رسمی است) بیاورید: I don't live there any more. She no longer works here. (دیگر آنجا زندگی نمی‌کنم.) نقطهٔ مقابلش still است.

الگومثال
not… any more (بریتانیایی) / anymore (آمریکایی)He doesn't play football any more.
not… any longerI can't wait any longer.
no longer (رسمی)The shop no longer sells newspapers.
still (برعکسش)He still plays football.

any more و any longer آخر جمله می‌آیند. no longer وسط جمله (قبل از فعل اصلی، بعد از be): She is no longer the manager.

با no longer فعل مثبت بیاورید: I no longer smoke. (نه I don't no longer smoke)

  • I don't drink coffee any more. any more
  • We can't stay any longer. any longer
  • He no longer lives in Tehran. no longer
  • Do you still play the piano? ~ No, not any more. still / any more

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I no longer don't work there. → ✅ I no longer work there. no longer + فعل مثبت.
  • ❌ She doesn't any more work here. → ✅ She doesn't work here any more. any more آخر جمله می‌آید.

تمرین سطح B1

4. I sent the email three weeks ago, and he ___ replied!
بی‌حوصلگی: still قبل از منفی.
5. You've finished ___? That was quick!
تعجب: already.
6. It's 11 pm, and my son ___ hasn't come home. I'm worried. (one word)
بی‌حوصلگی یا نگرانی: still hasn't.
7. I used to smoke, but I don't ___.
تمام شده: not… any more.
8. This model is ___ available.
رسمی، بعد از be: no longer.
9. I'm sorry, I can't wait any ___. I have to go. (one word)
Not… any longer.

C1 · پیشرفته

Formal yet and still: has yet to, as yet, still harder C1

Formal uses: have yet to + verb = haven't … yet (The results have yet to be published); as yet = until now; (and) yet = but; Still,… = even so; still + comparative = even (still harder); superlative + yet = so far (her best work yet).

PatternExampleMeaning
have / be yet to + verbWe have yet to receive payment.we haven't received it yet
as yet (formal)As yet, nobody has claimed the prize.until now
(and) yetThe plan is risky, (and) yet it could work.but
Still,…It was raining. Still, we had a great time.even so
still + comparativeThe second test was still harder.even harder
superlative + yetThis is her best work yet.so far

Have yet to has a positive form but a negative meaning: I have yet to meet him. = I haven't met him yet.

  • The cause of the fire has yet to be found. (has yet to)
  • As yet, there's no cure. (as yet)
  • It's a small car, yet it's very comfortable. (yet = but)
  • The weather was awful. Still, we enjoyed the trip. (still = even so)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The results have not yet to be announced. → ✅ The results have yet to be announced. (have yet to: positive form)
  • ❌ This is her better film yet. → ✅ This is her best film yet. (superlative + yet)

تمرین سطح C1

10. The new bridge has ___ be opened.
Have yet to = haven't… yet.
11. It rained all day. ___, we had a great time.
Still,… = even so.
12. This is the band's best album ___. (one word)
Superlative + yet = so far.
