جمله‌های شکافته و do تأکیدیCleft Sentences and Emphatic Do

A cleft sentence splits one sentence into two parts to focus on the most important information: Ali broke the window. → It was Ali who broke the window. (not someone else) / What I need is a holiday.

We also use stressed do / does / did for emphasis: I do like it! See also So Do I, Neither Do I.

B2 · C1 · C2

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Emphatic do: I do like it! Do sit down. B2

Put do / does / did before the base verb in a positive sentence to add emphasis or contrast: I do like your dress! She did call you. I don't eat meat, but I do eat fish. Do + imperative is polite and warm: Do come in!

UseExample
strong feelingI do love this song!
contrast with a negative ideaI don't drive, but I do have a licence.
insisting it's trueI did lock the door, I promise!
polite imperativeDo sit down. Do help yourself.

After do / does / did, use the base verb: She does work hard (not does works). He did tell me (not did told).

  • I do hope you can come. (strong feeling)
  • He doesn't say much, but he does listen. (contrast)
  • I did send the email. Check your spam folder. (insisting)
  • Do have some more cake! (polite imperative)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She does works very hard. → ✅ She does work very hard. (does + base verb)
  • ❌ I did told you about it! → ✅ I did tell you about it! (did + base verb)

It was Ali who…: it-clefts B2

It is / It was + the focused part + who / that + the rest: It was Ali who broke the window (not someone else). It's the price that worries me (not the size).

Normal sentenceIt-cleft (focus)
Ali broke the window.It was Ali who broke the window.
I met her in Paris.It was in Paris that I met her.
The noise annoys me.It's the noise that annoys me.
We left on Friday.It was on Friday that we left.

Use who or that for people, that for things, places and times. Use It is for present and It was for past.

It-clefts are often used to correct someone: No, it wasn't me who called. It was my brother.

  • It was my mother who taught me to cook. (focus on a person)
  • It's the traffic that makes me late. (focus on a thing)
  • It was in 2019 that we moved to Canada. (focus on a time)
  • It wasn't me who told her! (correcting)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It was Ali which broke the window. → ✅ It was Ali who broke the window. (person: who / that)
  • ❌ Was in Paris that I met her. → ✅ It was in Paris that I met her. (it + be)

What I need is a holiday: what-clefts B2

What + clause + is / was + the focused part: What I need is a holiday. What I like about her is her honesty. The focus comes at the end.

Normal sentenceWhat-cleft
I need a holiday.What I need is a holiday.
I love his sense of humour.What I love is his sense of humour.
The weather surprised me.What surprised me was the weather.
You should apologise.What you should do is apologise.

The verb after what… is usually is (or was for the past), even when the focus is plural: What we need is more teachers.

You can reverse it: A holiday is what I need.

  • What I really want is a cup of tea. (focus on a thing)
  • What worries me is the cost. (focus on the subject)
  • What I liked most was the food. (past)
  • What you need to do is practise every day. (focus on an action)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The thing what I need is time. → ✅ What I need is time. / The thing that I need is time. (what-cleft)
  • ❌ What I need it is a holiday. → ✅ What I need is a holiday. (no extra it)

تمرین سطح B2

1. You never believe me, but I ___ tell you the truth.
Emphatic do + base verb.
2. I know you think he's lazy, but he ___ work very hard.
Present, he: does work.
3. Please ___ come in and sit down. You're very welcome. (one word: emphatic auxiliary)
Polite imperative: Do come in.
4. It was my brother ___ found the money.
Person: who (or that).
5. I don't mind the work. ___ the long hours that I don't like.
It-cleft: It's … that.
6. It was in Rome ___ they first met. (one word)
Place: It was in Rome that.
7. ___ I need right now is a long sleep.
What-cleft: What I need is.
8. What I love about this city ___ the food.
What … is.
9. ___ surprised me was how friendly everyone was. (one word)
What surprised me was…

C1 · پیشرفته

All I want is, the thing is, the reason why C1

Other cleft patterns: All I want is… (= the only thing), The thing (that) I…is, The reason (why)…is that, The place where…, The person who…, The first / last / only thing….

PatternExample
All + clause + is / wasAll I want is a quiet life. (= the only thing)
The thing (that) + clause + isThe thing I hate is waiting.
The reason (why) + clause + is thatThe reason I'm late is that the bus broke down.
The place where + clause + isThe place where we met is now a bank.
The person who + clause + isThe person who helped me was a stranger.
The only / first / last thing + clauseThe only thing I can do is wait.

After The reason (why)…, use is that (not is because in careful writing): The reason I called is that I need help.

After All and The only thing, a verb focus can be a base verb: All I did was ask a question.

  • All I did was ask a simple question! (all + did + base verb)
  • The thing I like most is the food. (the thing)
  • The reason why she left is that she was unhappy. (the reason why)
  • The only thing I can't eat is fish. (the only thing)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ All what I want is peace. → ✅ All I want is peace. / All that I want is peace. (all (that), not all what)
  • ❌ The reason I'm late is because the bus was slow. (careful writing) → ✅ The reason I'm late is that the bus was slow. (the reason … is that)

What she did was call the police; what happened was C1

To focus on an action, use What + subject + do / did + is / was + (to) + base verb: What she did was (to) call the police. To focus on a whole event: What happened was (that)…

Normal sentenceCleft
She called the police.What she did was (to) call the police.
You should rest.What you should do is (to) rest.
The car broke down.What happened was (that) the car broke down.
I'm going to sell the house.What I'm going to do is sell the house.

After What … did was, use the base verb (with or without to): What he did was leave / to leave. Not What he did was left.

  • What we did was take a taxi. (action in the past)
  • What you need to do is fill in this form. (action now)
  • What happened was that the bridge collapsed. (an event)
  • What I'm going to do is call him tomorrow. (future action)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ What she did was called her lawyer. → ✅ What she did was call her lawyer. (base verb)
  • ❌ What was happened was an accident. → ✅ What happened was an accident. (what happened)

تمرین سطح C1

10. ___ I want is a bit of peace and quiet.
All I want is…
11. The reason I'm calling ___ that I need your advice.
The reason … is that.
12. All I did ___ ask her a question, and she got angry! (one word)
All I did was + base verb.
13. What he did ___ to leave without saying goodbye.
What he did was + (to) verb.
14. What I'm going to do is ___ a new laptop.
Base verb after is.
15. What ___ was that the computer crashed and I lost everything. (one word)
What happened was…

C2 · تسلط کامل

It wasn't until, it's not that…it's that, that's what C2

Advanced patterns: It wasn't until + time + that (= only then), It's not that … , it's (just) that…, and reversed clefts: That's what I mean. This is where the problem starts. Here's why…

PatternExample
It wasn't until … thatIt wasn't until I moved abroad that I appreciated my family.
It's not that … , it's that …It's not that I don't like you; it's that I'm busy.
That's what / why / where / howThat's why I left. That's exactly what I meant.
This is where / howThis is where it gets interesting.
It is … who / that + passiveIt is the customers who are affected most.

It wasn't until can also start with Not until + inversion in very formal English: Not until I moved abroad did I appreciate my family. See Inversion After Negative Adverbials.

  • It wasn't until midnight that they finally arrived. (only then)
  • It's not that the job is hard; it's that the hours are long. (correcting the reason)
  • That's what I've been trying to tell you! (reversed cleft)
  • This is how you open the file. (reversed cleft)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It wasn't until I was 30 when I learned to drive. → ✅ It wasn't until I was 30 that I learned to drive. (it wasn't until … that)
  • ❌ It's not that I'm angry, but I'm tired. → ✅ It's not that I'm angry; it's that I'm tired. (it's not that … it's that)

تمرین سطح C2

16. It wasn't until I read the letter ___ I understood what had happened.
It wasn't until … that.
17. I'm not angry. It's ___ I'm disappointed.
It's (just) that…
18. You need more practice. That's exactly ___ I said! (one word)
Reversed cleft: That's what…
