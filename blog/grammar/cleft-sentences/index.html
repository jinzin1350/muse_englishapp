Cleft Sentences (It was... that, What I need is...)

Level: C1 · Free grammar guide

A cleft sentence splits one idea into two parts to emphasise one of them: Tom broke the window. → It was Tom who broke the window. (Tom, not someone else.)

There are two main types: it-clefts and wh-clefts.

It-clefts: It is / was + focus + that / who

Put the part you want to emphasise after It is / It was, then add that (or who for people):

OriginalEmphasis onCleft sentence
Tom broke the window.TomIt was Tom who broke the window.
Tom broke the window.the windowIt was the window that Tom broke.
We moved to Canada in 2015.the timeIt was in 2015 that we moved to Canada.
I realised only when I got home.the timeIt was only when I got home that I realised.

It + singular be stays the same with plural focus: It was my parents who paid. We often use it to correct: It's not the money I care about, it's the people.

Wh-clefts: What… is / was

Start with a what clause, then is / was, then the focus: I need a holiday. → What I need is a holiday.

  • For an action, use do: What I did was (to) call the manager.
  • For an event: What happened was (that) the car broke down.
  • Similar patterns: All I want is a cup of tea. The thing that surprised me was… The reason why I left was… The place where we met was…

The verb after the what clause is usually singular: What we need is more time. Don't add it: What I like is… (not What I like it is).

Examples

  1. It was my sister who told me the news. (it-cleft, person)
  2. It's the noise that I can't stand, not the heat. (it-cleft, thing)
  3. It was in Paris that they first met. (it-cleft, place)
  4. What I need is a long holiday. (wh-cleft)
  5. What annoys me is people who talk during films. (wh-cleft)
  6. What we did was book a taxi. (wh-cleft, action)
  7. What happened was that we got lost. (wh-cleft, event)
  8. All I want is some peace and quiet. (all-cleft)
  9. The reason why I'm late is that the train was cancelled. (the reason why)
  10. The thing that surprised me most was the price. (the thing that)

Common mistakes

❌ It was my brother which called.

✅ It was my brother who called. (For people, use who or that.)

❌ What I need it is a holiday.

✅ What I need is a holiday. (Don't add it after the what clause.)

❌ That I want is a new job.

✅ What I want is a new job. (Wh-cleft starts with what.)

❌ What I did was I called the police.

✅ What I did was (to) call the police. (After what I did was, use the (to-)infinitive.)

❌ It were the students who complained.

✅ It was the students who complained. (Use singular It is / It was.)

❌ The reason because I left was the hours.

✅ The reason why I left was the hours. (The reason why (or the reason that).)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. ___ I need is a long holiday.
Wh-cleft: What I need is…
2. It was my sister ___ told me the news.
It-cleft with a person: who (or that).
3. It was in 2015 ___ we moved to Canada.
It-cleft: It was + focus + that.
4. What I did ___ call the manager.
What I did was (to) + verb.
5. All I want ___ a cup of tea.
All I want is…
6. ___ annoys me is people who talk during films.
Wh-cleft: What annoys me is…
7. It's not the money I care about. ___ the people I work with.
Correcting with an it-cleft: It's the people…
8. The reason ___ I left was the long working hours.
The reason why…
9. What happened ___ that the car broke down.
What happened was (that)…
10. It was only when I got home ___ I realised I'd lost my phone.
It was only when… that…
11. Choose the correct sentence.
What + clause + is + focus (no it).
12. The place ___ we first met was a small café in Paris.
The place where…
13. 'Tom broke the window.' Which sentence emphasises Tom?
Focus on Tom: It was Tom who broke the window.
14. What we need ___ more time, not more money.
What we need is…
15. It ___ you who were right, not me.
Singular It was, whatever the focus.
16. The thing ___ surprised me most was how friendly everyone was.
The thing that…
