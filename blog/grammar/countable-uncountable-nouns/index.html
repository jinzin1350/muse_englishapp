اسم‌های قابل‌شمارش و غیرقابل‌شمارشCountable and Uncountable Nouns

اسم‌های قابل‌شمارش را می‌شود شمرد: one apple, two apples. اسم‌های غیرقابل‌شمارش را نمی‌شود: water, rice, advice. نمی‌توانید بگویید two rices یا an advice.

این تفاوت، کلمه‌هایی را که همراه اسم می‌آیند عوض می‌کند: a, some, much, many, a few, a little. صفحهٔ much, many, a few, a little را هم ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

قابل‌شمارش یا غیرقابل‌شمارش؟ A1

اسم‌های قابل‌شمارش شکل مفرد و جمع دارند (an apple, two apples). اسم‌های غیرقابل‌شمارش جمع ندارند و a / an نمی‌گیرند (water, rice, money)، و فعلشان مفرد است.

قابل‌شمارشغیرقابل‌شمارش
a / an + مفرد: a banana, an eggبدون a / an: bread, milk (نه a bread)
جمع: bananas, eggsجمع ندارد: rice (نه rices)
How many? many, a fewHow much? much, a little
some / any + جمع: some eggssome / any: some milk

اسم‌های غیرقابل‌شمارش اغلب این‌ها هستند: مایع‌ها (water, juice)، جنس‌ها و مواد (wood, paper, gold)، خوراکی‌هایی که دانه‌دانه شمرده نمی‌شوند (rice, sugar, cheese) و مفهوم‌ها (love, help, time).

اسم‌های غیرقابل‌شمارش فعل مفرد می‌گیرند: The milk is in the fridge. Money isn't everything.

  • I'd like an orange and some bread. قابل‌شمارش / غیرقابل‌شمارش
  • There is some cheese in the fridge. فعل مفرد
  • How many eggs do we need? قابل‌شمارش
  • How much sugar do you take? غیرقابل‌شمارش

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I need some moneys. → ✅ I need some money. money جمع ندارد.
  • ❌ The rice are ready. → ✅ The rice is ready. غیرقابل‌شمارش: فعل مفرد.
  • ❌ I eat a bread every morning. → ✅ I eat bread every morning. bread حرف a نمی‌گیرد. (در فارسی می‌گوییم «یک نان»، ولی در انگلیسی bread غیرقابل‌شمارش است.)

تمرین سطح A1

1. I'd like ___ milk in my tea, please.
غیرقابل‌شمارش: some milk.
2. کدام اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش است؟
Rice جمع ندارد.
3. The water ___ (be) very cold. Don't swim!
غیرقابل‌شمارش: فعل مفرد.

A2 · مقدماتی

اسم‌های غیرقابل‌شمارشِ دردسرساز: advice, information, news A2

بعضی اسم‌ها در انگلیسی غیرقابل‌شمارش‌اند، هرچند در فارسی و خیلی از زبان‌ها شمرده می‌شوند: advice, information, news, furniture, luggage, homework, equipment, traffic, weather, work, travel.

غیرقابل‌شمارش (بدون a و بدون جمع)جایگزینِ قابل‌شمارش
advicea piece of advice, a tip
informationa piece of information, a fact
newsa piece of news, a news story
furniturea chair, a table, a piece of furniture
luggage / baggagea bag, a suitcase
homeworkan exercise, a task
worka job
travela trip, a journey
weatherشکل قابل‌شمارش ندارد: What lovely weather!

این اسم‌ها فعل مفرد می‌گیرند و ‑s نمی‌گیرند: The information is on the website. Your advice was very helpful.

با آن‌ها از some, any, much, a lot of یا a piece of استفاده کنید: Can I give you some advice? I don't have much homework.

  • Can you give me some information about the course? some + غیرقابل‌شمارش
  • I have a lot of homework tonight. a lot of
  • Our luggage is in the car. فعل مفرد
  • I'm looking for a job. I need work. job (قابل‌شمارش) / work (غیرقابل‌شمارش)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She gave me a good advice. → ✅ She gave me some good advice. advice حرف a نمی‌گیرد. (فارسی‌زبان‌ها می‌گویند «یک نصیحت»، ولی در انگلیسی an advice غلط است.)
  • ❌ We bought new furnitures. → ✅ We bought new furniture. furniture حرف ‑s نمی‌گیرد.
  • ❌ The news are on at six. → ✅ The news is on at six. news فعل مفرد می‌گیرد.
  • ❌ I had a nice travel. → ✅ I had a nice trip. travel غیرقابل‌شمارش است؛ به جایش trip بیاورید.

تمرین سطح A2

4. Can I give you ___ advice?
غیرقابل‌شمارش: some advice.
5. I have ___ homework tonight.
غیرقابل‌شمارش: a lot of homework.
6. The information on this website ___ (be) very useful. I use it every day.
غیرقابل‌شمارش: فعل مفرد.

B1 · متوسط

a piece of, a slice of, a bottle of B1

برای شمردن اسم‌های غیرقابل‌شمارش از یک واحد استفاده کنید: a piece of cake, a slice of bread, a bottle of water, a cup of coffee, a bar of chocolate, a loaf of bread.

واحدمثال
a piece ofcake, paper, advice, information, furniture, news
a slice ofbread, pizza, cheese, ham
a bottle / glass / cup ofwater, juice, coffee, tea
a loaf ofbread
a bar ofchocolate, soap
a carton ofmilk, juice
a bag / packet / kilo ofrice, sugar, flour
a tube / jar / can oftoothpaste / honey, jam / cola

واحد قابل‌شمارش است، پس می‌تواند جمع شود: two slices of bread, three bottles of water. ولی اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش مفرد می‌ماند: two slices of bread (نه breads).

  • Could I have a glass of water, please? glass (لیوان)
  • I bought two loaves of bread. loaf ← loaves
  • She gave me a useful piece of advice. piece
  • We need a carton of milk. carton (پاکت)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Two breads, please. → ✅ Two loaves of bread, please. bread غیرقابل‌شمارش است؛ از loaf استفاده کنید.
  • ❌ three bottles of waters → ✅ three bottles of water اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش مفرد می‌ماند.

تمرین سطح B1

7. Would you like a ___ of pizza?
A slice of pizza (یک تکه پیتزا).
8. I bought two ___ of bread at the bakery.
Loaf در جمع می‌شود loaves.
9. Can you buy a ___ of milk on your way home? (one word)
A carton / bottle of milk (یک پاکت / بطری شیر).

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Nouns that can be both: paper or a paper, coffee or a coffee B2

Many nouns can be countable or uncountable with a different meaning: paper (material) / a paper (a newspaper); glass / a glass; time / a time; experience / an experience. With drinks, a coffee = a cup of coffee.

UncountableCountable
paper: the material: a sheet of papera paper: a newspaper or an essay
glass: the material: made of glassa glass: for drinking; glasses: for your eyes
hair: all the hair on your heada hair: one single hair
room: space: no room for a sofaa room: in a house
time: I don't have time.a time: an occasion: I've been there three times.
experience: knowledge from doing somethingan experience: something that happened to you
coffee, tea, juice: the drink in generala coffee: a cup or glass of it
chicken: the meata chicken: the animal
  • I need some paper to print this. (material)
  • I bought a paper at the station. (a newspaper)
  • Two coffees, please. (cups of coffee)
  • She has a lot of experience in marketing. (knowledge)
  • Travelling alone was an amazing experience. (an event)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I have a lot of experiences in sales. → ✅ I have a lot of experience in sales. (knowledge: uncountable)
  • ❌ She has long black hairs. → ✅ She has long black hair. (all the hair: uncountable)
  • ❌ Is there a room for one more person in the car? → ✅ Is there room for one more person in the car? (space: uncountable)

تمرین سطح B2

10. Is there ___ room in your car for my bag?
Space: uncountable.
11. I've been to London three ___.
A time = an occasion: three times.
12. She has ten years of ___ (experience) as a nurse.
Knowledge: uncountable, no -s.

C1 · پیشرفته

Formal uncountable nouns: research, evidence, feedback C1

In formal and academic English, research, evidence, feedback, knowledge, progress, accommodation, equipment are uncountable. Use a piece of, a study or another countable word for one. Some uncountable nouns become countable for kinds (French wines).

UncountableCountable way to say one
researcha study, a piece of research
evidencea piece of evidence, a clue
feedbacka comment, a piece of feedback
progressa step forward, an improvement
accommodation (BrE)a room, a flat (AmE often says accommodations)
equipment / softwarea tool, a device / a program, an app
knowledgea good knowledge of (with an adjective)

These nouns take a singular verb and have no plural: The evidence is clear. Recent research has shown…

Kinds: some uncountable nouns are countable when we mean types: This shop sells French wines and Italian cheeses. There are many different teas from China.

  • There is no evidence that the drug works. (singular verb)
  • She's doing research into childhood memory. (no a)
  • Thank you for your feedback. (uncountable)
  • We tasted three local cheeses. (kinds)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Recent researches show that sleep matters. → ✅ Recent research shows that sleep matters. (research: uncountable)
  • ❌ We found an evidence. → ✅ We found a piece of evidence. (evidence: no a)
  • ❌ You've made a good progress. → ✅ You've made good progress. (progress: no a)

تمرین سطح C1

13. Recent ___ shows that exercise improves mood.
Uncountable: research shows.
14. The police didn't find ___ evidence.
Uncountable: any evidence.
15. You've made very good ___ (progress) this term.
Uncountable: no -s.
