اسم‌های جمعPlural Nouns in English

بیشتر اسم‌های انگلیسی با ‑s جمع بسته می‌شوند: one book, two books. اما قاعده‌های املایی (box → boxes, city → cities) و چند جمعِ بی‌قاعدهٔ رایج (man → men, child → children) هم هست. (در فارسی بعد از عدد اسم مفرد می‌آید، «سه کتاب»، اما در انگلیسی اسم جمع می‌شود: three books.)

بعضی اسم‌ها همیشه جمع‌اند (jeans, scissors) و بعضی اصلاً جمع بسته نمی‌شوند. برای این‌ها صفحهٔ اسم‌های قابل‌شمارش و غیرقابل‌شمارش را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

جمع‌های باقاعده: ‑s, ‑es, ‑ies, ‑ves A1

به بیشتر اسم‌ها ‑s اضافه کنید (cars). بعد از ‑s, ‑sh, ‑ch, ‑x ‑es اضافه کنید (boxes). حرف بی‌صدا + y ← ‑ies (cities). خیلی از کلمه‌هایی که به ‑f / ‑fe ختم می‌شوند ← ‑ves (knives).

قاعدهمثال
بیشتر اسم‌ها: + ‑sbook → books, car → cars
‑s, ‑ss, ‑sh, ‑ch, ‑x: + ‑esbus → buses, class → classes, dish → dishes, watch → watches, box → boxes
حرف بی‌صدا + ‑y: y ← ‑iescity → cities, baby → babies
حرف صدادار + ‑y: + ‑sday → days, boy → boys, key → keys
بعضی کلمه‌های ‑oدار: + ‑estomato → tomatoes, potato → potatoes, hero → heroes
بقیهٔ کلمه‌های ‑oدار: + ‑sphoto → photos, piano → pianos, radio → radios
خیلی از کلمه‌های ‑f / ‑feدار: ← ‑vesknife → knives, leaf → leaves, wife → wives, half → halves

تلفظ: ‑s بعد از /p, t, k, f/ صدای /s/ می‌دهد (books)، بعد از بیشتر صداهای دیگر /z/ (cars, days)، و بعد از /s, z, ʃ, tʃ, dʒ/ صدای /ɪz/ (buses, watches, pages).

بعضی کلمه‌های ‑fدار فقط ‑s می‌گیرند: roofs, chefs, beliefs.

  • I have two brothers and three sisters. (+ s)
  • She washed the dishes. (+ es)
  • There are many big cities in Canada. (y → ies)
  • Put the knives in the drawer. (f → ves)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I bought three tomatos. → ✅ I bought three tomatoes. (tomato → tomatoes)
  • ❌ My two babys are asleep. → ✅ My two babies are asleep. حرف بی‌صدا + y ← ies
  • ❌ The leafs are falling. → ✅ The leaves are falling. (leaf → leaves)

جمع‌های بی‌قاعده: men, children, feet, people A1

بعضی اسم‌های رایج جمعِ بی‌قاعده دارند: man → men, woman → women, child → children, person → people, foot → feet, tooth → teeth, mouse → mice. بعضی هم تغییر نمی‌کنند: sheep, fish.

مفردجمع
man / womanmen / women
childchildren
personpeople
foot / tooth / goosefeet / teeth / geese
mousemice
sheep / fish / deersheep / fish / deer (بدون تغییر)

این اسم‌ها ‑s نمی‌گیرند، اما باز هم فعل جمع می‌گیرند: The children are playing.

people جمعِ معمولِ person است. (persons خیلی رسمی است، مثلاً در اطلاعیه‌های حقوقی.)

  • Three men and two women got on the bus. (men, women)
  • How many children do you have? (children)
  • Brush your teeth before bed. (teeth)
  • We saw a lot of sheep on the farm. بدون تغییر

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ There are many childs in the park. → ✅ There are many children in the park. (child → children)
  • ❌ My foots hurt. → ✅ My feet hurt. (foot → feet)
  • ❌ Ten persons came to the party. → ✅ Ten people came to the party. (person → people)

تمرین سطح A1

1. جمعِ city چیست؟
حرف بی‌صدا + y ← ‑ies.
2. کدام جمع درست است؟
photo + ‑s.
3. I have two ___ (box) of old photos.
‑x + ‑es.
4. Three ___ are waiting for you outside.
man ← men.
5. There were 20 ___ (person) at the meeting.
person ← people.
6. My little brother has lost two ___ (tooth).
tooth ← teeth.

A2 · مقدماتی

همیشه جمع: jeans, scissors, clothes, police A2

بعضی اسم‌ها همیشه جمع‌اند: trousers, jeans, glasses, scissors, clothes. برای شمردنشان از a pair of («یک جفت») استفاده کنید. police و people هم فعل جمع می‌گیرند.

همیشه جمعمثال
لباس‌هایی که دو پاچه دارندtrousers, jeans, shorts, pyjamas
چیزهایی که دو قسمت دارندglasses, scissors, headphones
بقیهclothes, stairs, goods, thanks, congratulations
فعل جمع، بدون ‑spolice, people, cattle

این اسم‌ها فعل جمع می‌گیرند: My jeans are too tight. Where are my glasses?

برای شمردن a pair of بیاورید: I bought a pair of jeans and two pairs of shoes. (نه a jeans)

police جمع است: The police are looking for him.

  • Your new trousers look great. فعل جمع
  • I need a pair of scissors. (a pair of)
  • Where are my glasses? فعل جمع
  • The police have arrested two men. police + فعل جمع

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ My jeans is dirty. → ✅ My jeans are dirty. jeans: فعل جمع
  • ❌ I bought a new trouser. → ✅ I bought a new pair of trousers. (a pair of)
  • ❌ The police is coming. → ✅ The police are coming. police: فعل جمع

تمرین سطح A2

7. My new jeans ___ too long.
jeans فعل جمع می‌گیرد.
8. I need ___ new glasses. My old ones are broken.
با a pair of شمرده می‌شود.
9. The police ___ (be) here now. They want to talk to you.
police فعل جمع می‌گیرد.

B1 · متوسط

اسم‌های جمعی: the team is یا the team are؟ B1

اسم‌های جمعی مثل family, team, government, staff, class در انگلیسی بریتانیایی می‌توانند فعل مفرد یا جمع بگیرند (The team is / are winning). انگلیسی آمریکایی معمولاً مفرد می‌آورد.

اسمیک واحد (مفرد)آدم‌های داخلش (جمع، بریتانیایی)
teamThe team is top of the league.The team are celebrating.
familyMy family is large.My family are all doctors.
staffThe staff is small.The staff are very helpful.

وقتی گروه را یک واحد در نظر می‌گیرید فعل مفرد بیاورید. در انگلیسی بریتانیایی وقتی به تک‌تکِ آدم‌ها فکر می‌کنید فعل جمع رایج است.

انگلیسی آمریکایی معمولاً فعل مفرد می‌آورد: The team is celebrating.

بعضی کلمه‌های جمعی همیشه جمع‌اند: people, police, cattle.

ضمیر با فعل هماهنگ می‌شود: The company has changed its logo. / The team are proud of their coach.

  • The government has announced new taxes. یک واحد
  • My family are coming for dinner. (BrE) آدم‌ها (بریتانیایی)
  • Our class is the biggest in the school. یک واحد
  • The staff are on strike. (BrE) آدم‌ها (بریتانیایی)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The police is investigating. → ✅ The police are investigating. police همیشه جمع است
  • ❌ The people is very friendly. → ✅ The people are very friendly. people: جمع
  • ❌ The team are proud of its coach. → ✅ The team are proud of their coach. فعل جمع ← their

تمرین سطح B1

10. The police ___ looking for two men.
police همیشه فعل جمع می‌گیرد.
11. کدام جمله هم در انگلیسی بریتانیایی و هم آمریکایی درست است؟
فعل مفرد + its: همه‌جا درست است.
12. The company has changed ___ logo. (one word)
فعل مفرد ← its.

C1 · پیشرفته

Criteria, crises, passers-by and the news is C1

Greek and Latin plurals (criterion → criteria, crisis → crises), compound plurals (passers-by), nouns that are the same in the singular and plural (series, species), and singular nouns ending in -s (news, physics).

TypeExamples
-is → -esanalysis → analyses, crisis → crises, thesis → theses, hypothesis → hypotheses
-on → -acriterion → criteria, phenomenon → phenomena
-um → -amedium → media, bacterium → bacteria
-us → -istimulus → stimuli, nucleus → nuclei (but bonuses, viruses)
same in bothseries, species, means, aircraft
compound nounspassers-by, mothers-in-law, runners-up

Data is plural in formal scientific writing (the data are), but most people use it as uncountable (the data is). Both are accepted today.

These nouns end in -s but take a singular verb: news, maths, physics, economics, athletics: The news is good.

In compound nouns, the main noun takes the -s: passers-by, mothers-in-law.

  • Several criteria were used to choose the winner. (criterion → criteria)
  • There have been two economic crises this decade. (crisis → crises)
  • Physics is my favourite subject. (-s, singular verb)
  • Both of his brothers-in-law are teachers. (compound plural)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The news are bad. → ✅ The news is bad. (news: singular)
  • ❌ This phenomena is rare. → ✅ This phenomenon is rare. (singular: phenomenon)
  • ❌ Two passer-bys helped me. → ✅ Two passers-by helped me. (compound: passers-by)

تمرین سطح C1

13. The news ___ very good today.
News takes a singular verb.
14. What is the plural of criterion?
Criterion → criteria.
15. There are many different ___ (analysis) of this poem.
Analysis → analyses.
