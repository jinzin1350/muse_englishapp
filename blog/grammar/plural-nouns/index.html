Plural Nouns: Regular and Irregular

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

Most nouns make the plural with -s: one book, two books. Some add -es or change their spelling, and some are irregular: one child, two children.

Spelling rules

RuleExamples
Most nouns: add -sbook → books, car → cars, day → days
Ending in -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z: add -esbus → buses, class → classes, dish → dishes, watch → watches, box → boxes, quiz → quizzes
Consonant + y: change y to -iescity → cities, baby → babies, party → parties
Vowel + y: add -sboy → boys, key → keys, toy → toys
Some nouns ending in -f / -fe: change to -vesknife → knives, wife → wives, leaf → leaves, shelf → shelves, half → halves
Some nouns ending in -o: add -espotato → potatoes, tomato → tomatoes, hero → heroes

There are exceptions: roof → roofs, chef → chefs, belief → beliefs (just -s), and photo → photos, piano → pianos, radio → radios (just -s).

Some nouns are the same in the singular and plural: one sheep, two sheep; one deer, two deer; one fish, two fish.

Nouns that are always plural

trousers, jeans, shorts, glasses (for your eyes), scissors, clothes are always plural: My jeans are new. To count them, use a pair of: a pair of jeans, two pairs of scissors.

How to pronounce the plural ending

The ending sounds like /s/ (books, cats), /z/ (days, dogs) or /ɪz/ with an extra syllable (buses, watches, boxes).

Examples

  1. I have two brothers and three sisters. (+ -s)
  2. There are lots of buses in the city centre. (-s → -es)
  3. We visited five cities in two weeks. (consonant + y → -ies)
  4. Be careful with those knives. (-fe → -ves)
  5. Many people work from home now. (irregular)
  6. My feet hurt after the long walk. (irregular)
  7. The children are playing in the garden. (irregular)
  8. Can you buy some tomatoes? (-o → -oes)
  9. There are three sheep in the field. (same in the plural)
  10. My jeans are too small. (always plural)

Common mistakes

❌ I have two childs.

✅ I have two children. (Child → children (irregular).)

❌ There are many peoples here.

✅ There are many people here. (The plural of person is people. (Peoples means nations.))

❌ I bought three tomatos.

✅ I bought three tomatoes. (Tomato → tomatoes.)

❌ The babys are sleeping.

✅ The babies are sleeping. (Consonant + y → -ies.)

❌ My tooths are white.

✅ My teeth are white. (Tooth → teeth (irregular).)

❌ My trousers is new.

✅ My trousers are new. (Trousers is always plural.)

❌ We need two knifes.

✅ We need two knives. (Knife → knives.)
