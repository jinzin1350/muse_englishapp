ساختار used to (عادت‌های گذشته)Used To (I used to play)

used to + شکل سادهٔ فعل را برای کارهایی به کار می‌بریم که در گذشته مرتب انجام می‌شدند یا درست بودند، اما حالا دیگر نه: I used to play the piano. (الان نمی‌زنم.) She used to live in Isfahan. (الان آنجا زندگی نمی‌کند.) در فارسی معمولاً با «قبلاً… می‌کردم» می‌گوییم.

این صفحه از ساختار پایه شروع می‌شود و به فرق used to و would می‌رسد، و اینکه will و would چطور رفتارِ همیشگیِ کسی را توصیف می‌کنند.

A2 · مقدماتی

used to: عادت‌ها و حالت‌های گذشته A2

used to + شکل سادهٔ فعل، برای چیزی که در گذشته درست بود یا مرتب اتفاق می‌افتاد اما حالا نه.

used to برای همهٔ اشخاص یکی است: I / you / he / she / we / they used to + base verb.

  • عادت‌های گذشته (کارهای تکراری): I used to walk to school every day.
  • حالت‌های گذشته (موقعیت‌ها): We used to have a dog. There used to be a cinema here.

معنی‌اش همیشه این است: در گذشته این‌طور بود، اما الان فرق کرده. I used to smoke یعنی قبلاً سیگار می‌کشیدم، اما الان نه.

used to فقط در گذشته وجود دارد. برای عادت‌های حال، حال ساده بیاورید: I usually walk to work. نه I use to walk to work.

  • I used to live in a small village. حالتِ گذشته
  • My father used to work in a bank. الان نه
  • We used to go camping every summer. عادتِ گذشته
  • There used to be a park here. Now it's a car park. there used to be

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I use to play football when I was young. → ✅ I used to play football when I was young. در جملهٔ مثبت used to بنویسید.
  • ❌ She used to lives in Paris. → ✅ She used to live in Paris. used to + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ I used to play tennis now. → ✅ I usually play tennis now. used to فقط برای گذشته است.

didn't use to و Did you use to…? A2

منفی: didn't use to. سؤالی: Did … use to…? (بعد از did حرف d نمی‌آید).

ساختارمثال
مثبتused to + شکل سادهٔ فعلI used to like fish.
منفیdidn't use to + شکل سادهٔ فعلI didn't use to like fish.
سؤالیDid + فاعل + use to + شکل سادهٔ فعل؟Did you use to like fish?
جواب کوتاهYes, … did. / No, … didn't.Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

بعد از did و didn't، مثل فعل‌های دیگر use to (بدون ‑d) بنویسید: didn't go, didn't use to go.

می‌توانید منفی را با never هم بسازید: I never used to eat vegetables. این در گفتار خیلی رایج است.

  • I didn't use to like coffee, but now I love it. منفی
  • Did you use to live in Tabriz? سؤالی
  • Where did you use to go on holiday? سؤال با کلمهٔ پرسشی
  • He never used to do any sport. (never used to)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I didn't used to watch TV. → ✅ I didn't use to watch TV. بعد از didn't: use to.
  • ❌ Did you used to play chess? → ✅ Did you use to play chess? بعد از did: use to.
  • ❌ Used you to live here? → ✅ Did you use to live here? سؤالی: did + فاعل + use to.

تمرین سطح A2

1. When I was a child, I ___ afraid of the dark.
used to + شکل سادهٔ فعل be.
2. جملهٔ My grandmother used to make bread یعنی چه؟
used to یعنی عادتی در گذشته که الان دیگر نیست.
3. There ___ (be) a cinema in our town, but it closed years ago. (Use used to.)
there used to be یعنی در گذشته وجود داشت.
4. I ___ enjoy reading, but now I read every day.
منفی: didn't use to.
5. ___ have a pet when you were a child?
سؤالی: Did you use to…?
6. اشتباه را پیدا کنید: روی بخش غلط بزنید.
My sister didn't used to eat meat, but now she does.
شکل درست: My sister didn't use to eat meat, but now she does.
بعد از didn't: use to.

B1 · متوسط

used to یا گذشتهٔ ساده؟ B1

used to را برای کارهای تکراری یا موقعیت‌های طولانی بیاورید. برای یک کارِ تک، تعداد دفعات مشخص یا مدت زمانِ دقیق گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید.

used to فقط کارهای تکراری یا موقعیت‌هایی را که مدت زیادی طول کشیدند توصیف می‌کند. برای بقیهٔ کارهای گذشته گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید:

used toگذشتهٔ ساده (نه used to)
I used to go to the gym. (مرتب)I went to the gym yesterday. (یک بار)
She used to live in Rome.She lived in Rome for six years. (مدت دقیق)
We used to visit them.We visited them three times. (تعداد دفعات)

برای کارهای تکراریِ گذشته گذشتهٔ ساده هم درست است: I went to the gym every day when I was a student. used to این معنی را اضافه می‌کند که الان دیگر این‌طور نیست.

used to را با for + مدت یا با how long نیاورید: How long did you live there? نه How long did you use to live there?

  • I used to play chess with my grandfather. تکراری، الان نه
  • I played chess with my grandfather last Sunday. یک بار
  • We lived in Kerman for ten years. مدت دقیق
  • She called me five times yesterday. تعداد دفعات

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I used to go to Spain last year. → ✅ I went to Spain last year. یک بار: گذشتهٔ ساده
  • ❌ He used to work there for twenty years. → ✅ He worked there for twenty years. مدت دقیق: گذشتهٔ ساده
  • ❌ I used to call her three times. → ✅ I called her three times. تعداد دفعات: گذشتهٔ ساده

used to do یا be used to doing؟ B1

I used to work at night یعنی در گذشته (الان نه). I'm used to working at night یعنی برایم عادی است، عجیب نیست.

این‌ها شبیه هم‌اند اما معنی‌شان فرق دارد:

ساختارمعنیمثال
used to + شکل سادهٔ فعلعادت یا حالتی در گذشته، الان نهI used to live alone. (اما الان نه)
be used to + اسم / فعل ing‌دارچیزی برایمان آشناست و عجیب یا سخت نیستI'm used to living alone. (برایم عادی است)
get used to + اسم / فعل ing‌داربه چیزی عادت کردنI'm getting used to living alone. (دارد راحت‌تر می‌شود)

بعد از be used to و get used to، کلمهٔ to حرف اضافه است، پس بعدش اسم یا فعل ing‌دار می‌آید: I'm used to the cold. I'm used to getting up early.

برای be used to و get used to در همهٔ زمان‌ها صفحهٔ be used to و get used to را ببینید.

  • I used to drive to work, but now I take the train. عادتِ گذشته
  • I'm used to driving on the right. برایم عادی است
  • She isn't used to the noise of the city yet. be used to + اسم
  • You'll soon get used to the food. get used to یعنی عادت کردن

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm used to get up early. → ✅ I'm used to getting up early. be used to + فعل ing‌دار
  • ❌ I used to getting up early when I was a student. → ✅ I used to get up early when I was a student. عادتِ گذشته: used to + شکل سادهٔ فعل

تمرین سطح B1

یک بار: گذشتهٔ ساده.

مدت دقیق (for fifty years): گذشتهٔ ساده.

همه درست‌اند؛ used to تأکید می‌کند که الان دیگر این‌طور نیست.

الان برایم عادی است: be used to + اسم.
11. جملهٔ He's used to working long hours یعنی چه؟
be used to + فعل ing‌دار یعنی آشناست و سخت نیست.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Would for past habits B2

would + base verb can describe repeated actions in the past, especially in stories: Every summer we would go to the lake. Not for states.

In stories and memories, we often use would for repeated past actions, usually after the scene has been set with used to or the past simple:

When I was a kid, we used to spend the summer at my grandparents' farm. Every morning, we would get up early and would help with the animals.

Used toWould
actions: We used to play outside. ✓actions: We would play outside. ✓
states: I used to have a bike. ✓states: I would have a bike. ✗
I used to live in Yazd. ✓I would live in Yazd. ✗

Would can't be used for states (be, have for possession, live, like, know, believe). Use used to or the past simple for states.

Don't start a story with would alone, because it can be confused with other meanings (I would go = conditional). First make the time clear: When we were children, … / Every summer, …

  • Every evening my father would read us a story. (repeated action)
  • On Fridays we would visit my aunt. (habit in a story)
  • I used to have a red bicycle. (state: not would)
  • She used to be very quiet. (state: not would)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ When I was young, I would have a cat. → ✅ When I was young, I used to have a cat. (have (possession) is a state: used to.)
  • ❌ We would live in a small flat in those days. → ✅ We used to live in a small flat in those days. (live is a state: used to.)

تمرین سطح B2

13. When I was a child, I ___ a lot of friends in my street.
Have (possession) is a state: used to, not would.
14. Every Sunday my grandmother ___ a big lunch for the whole family.
A repeated action in a memory: would + base verb.
15. Find the mistake: tap the wrong part.
In those days my parents would own a small shop.
Correct: In those days my parents used to own a small shop.
Own is a state: used to own (or owned).

C1 · پیشرفته

Will and would for typical behaviour C1

will / would can describe typical or characteristic behaviour: She'll sit there for hours. Stressed would can show criticism: He WOULD say that!

We can use will (present) and would (past) to describe what someone or something typically does:

  • My cat will sleep anywhere. (it's typical of her)
  • On a good day, the shop will sell 300 coffees.
  • My grandfather would sit in the garden for hours, just watching the birds.

When would (or will) is stressed in speech, it often shows that the speaker finds the behaviour annoying and predictable: He WOULD forget my birthday! (= that's typical of him, and I'm annoyed). She WILL leave her shoes in the hall.

Used to can also refer to things that no longer exist: This building used to be a cinema. Would is not used like this.

This will is not about the future: Boys will be boys means boys typically behave like this.

  • She'll talk to anyone who listens. (typical behaviour now)
  • In the evenings, my father would fall asleep in front of the TV. (typical past behaviour)
  • Of course he was late. He WOULD be! (stressed: criticism)
  • Oil will float on water. (a typical property)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ This shop would be a bakery when I was a child. → ✅ This shop used to be a bakery when I was a child. (A past state: used to, not would.)
  • ❌ She would to sit by the window for hours. → ✅ She would sit by the window for hours. (would + base verb)

تمرین سطح C1

16. My teenage son will spend all day in his room if you let him. The speaker is talking about:
Will here describes what he typically does.
17. A: Marco says the project was my fault. B: Well, he ___ say that, wouldn't he? (stressed)
Stressed would = typical, predictable behaviour (often criticism).
18. When my grandmother was alive, she ___ (sit) on the balcony for hours, watching people go by. (Use would.)
Would for typical past behaviour.
