Used To (I used to play)

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

We use used to + base verb for things that happened regularly or were true in the past, but are not true now: I used to play the piano. (I don't now.) She used to live in Isfahan. (She doesn't now.)

This page goes from the basic form to the difference between used to and would, and how will and would describe typical behaviour.

A2 · Elementary

Used to: past habits and states A2

  • Past habits (repeated actions): I used to walk to school every day.
  • Past states (situations): We used to have a dog. There used to be a cinema here.

  • I used to live in a small village. (a past state)
  • My father used to work in a bank. (not now)
  • We used to go camping every summer. (a past habit)
  • There used to be a park here. Now it's a car park. (there used to be)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I use to play football when I was young. → ✅ I used to play football when I was young. (Spell it used to in positive sentences.)
  • ❌ She used to lives in Paris. → ✅ She used to live in Paris. (used to + base verb)
  • ❌ I used to play tennis now. → ✅ I usually play tennis now. (used to is only for the past.)

Didn't use to and Did you use to…? A2

FormExample
Positiveused to + base verbI used to like fish.
Negativedidn't use to + base verbI didn't use to like fish.
QuestionDid + subject + use to + base verb?Did you use to like fish?
Short answersYes, … did. / No, … didn't.Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

  • I didn't use to like coffee, but now I love it. (negative)
  • Did you use to live in Tabriz? (question)
  • Where did you use to go on holiday? (wh-question)
  • He never used to do any sport. (never used to)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I didn't used to watch TV. → ✅ I didn't use to watch TV. (After didn't: use to.)
  • ❌ Did you used to play chess? → ✅ Did you use to play chess? (After did: use to.)
  • ❌ Used you to live here? → ✅ Did you use to live here? (Questions: did + subject + use to.)

Practice: A2

Used to + base verb be.
2. What does My grandmother used to make bread mean?
Used to = a past habit that isn't true now.
3. There ___ (be) a cinema in our town, but it closed years ago. (Use used to.)
There used to be = it existed in the past.
4. I ___ enjoy reading, but now I read every day.
Negative: didn't use to.
5. ___ have a pet when you were a child?
Question: Did you use to…?
6. Find the mistake: tap the wrong part.
My sister didn't used to eat meat, but now she does.
Correct: My sister didn't use to eat meat, but now she does.
After didn't: use to.

B1 · Intermediate

Used to or past simple? B1

Used toPast simple (not used to)
I used to go to the gym. (regularly)I went to the gym yesterday. (one time)
She used to live in Rome.She lived in Rome for six years. (exact time)
We used to visit them.We visited them three times. (number of times)

  • I used to play chess with my grandfather. (repeated, not now)
  • I played chess with my grandfather last Sunday. (one occasion)
  • We lived in Kerman for ten years. (exact period)
  • She called me five times yesterday. (a number of times)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I used to go to Spain last year. → ✅ I went to Spain last year. (one occasion: past simple)
  • ❌ He used to work there for twenty years. → ✅ He worked there for twenty years. (exact period: past simple)
  • ❌ I used to call her three times. → ✅ I called her three times. (number of times: past simple)

Used to do or be used to doing? B1

FormMeaningExample
used to + base verba past habit or state, not nowI used to live alone. (but now I don't)
be used to + noun / -ingsomething is familiar and not strange or difficultI'm used to living alone. (it's normal for me)
get used to + noun / -ingbecome familiar with somethingI'm getting used to living alone. (it's becoming easier)

  • I used to drive to work, but now I take the train. (past habit)
  • I'm used to driving on the right. (it's normal for me)
  • She isn't used to the noise of the city yet. (be used to + noun)
  • You'll soon get used to the food. (get used to = become familiar)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I'm used to get up early. → ✅ I'm used to getting up early. (be used to + -ing)
  • ❌ I used to getting up early when I was a student. → ✅ I used to get up early when I was a student. (past habit: used to + base verb)

Practice: B1

7. I ___ to the dentist last week.
One occasion: past simple.
8. My grandparents ___ in the countryside for fifty years.
An exact period (for fifty years): past simple.
9. When I was a teenager, I ___ (spend) hours on the phone. Now I hardly call anyone.
All are correct; used to stresses that it isn't true now.
10. I've lived in Canada for ten years, so I ___ the cold winters.
It's normal for me now: be used to + noun.
11. What does He's used to working long hours mean?
Be used to + -ing = it's familiar, not difficult.
12. When I was a student, I ___ (use / study) late at night. Now I go to bed at ten.
A past habit, not now: used to + base verb.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Would for past habits B2

Used toWould
actions: We used to play outside. ✓actions: We would play outside. ✓
states: I used to have a bike. ✓states: I would have a bike. ✗
I used to live in Yazd. ✓I would live in Yazd. ✗

  • Every evening my father would read us a story. (repeated action)
  • On Fridays we would visit my aunt. (habit in a story)
  • I used to have a red bicycle. (state: not would)
  • She used to be very quiet. (state: not would)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ When I was young, I would have a cat. → ✅ When I was young, I used to have a cat. (have (possession) is a state: used to.)
  • ❌ We would live in a small flat in those days. → ✅ We used to live in a small flat in those days. (live is a state: used to.)

Practice: B2

13. When I was a child, I ___ a lot of friends in my street.
Have (possession) is a state: used to, not would.
14. Every Sunday my grandmother ___ a big lunch for the whole family.
A repeated action in a memory: would + base verb.
15. Find the mistake: tap the wrong part.
In those days my parents would own a small shop.
Correct: In those days my parents used to own a small shop.
Own is a state: used to own (or owned).

C1 · Advanced

Will and would for typical behaviour C1

  • My cat will sleep anywhere. (it's typical of her)
  • On a good day, the shop will sell 300 coffees.
  • My grandfather would sit in the garden for hours, just watching the birds.

  • She'll talk to anyone who listens. (typical behaviour now)
  • In the evenings, my father would fall asleep in front of the TV. (typical past behaviour)
  • Of course he was late. He WOULD be! (stressed: criticism)
  • Oil will float on water. (a typical property)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ This shop would be a bakery when I was a child. → ✅ This shop used to be a bakery when I was a child. (A past state: used to, not would.)
  • ❌ She would to sit by the window for hours. → ✅ She would sit by the window for hours. (would + base verb)

Practice: C1

16. My teenage son will spend all day in his room if you let him. The speaker is talking about:
Will here describes what he typically does.
17. A: Marco says the project was my fault. B: Well, he ___ say that, wouldn't he? (stressed)
Stressed would = typical, predictable behaviour (often criticism).
18. When my grandmother was alive, she ___ (sit) on the balcony for hours, watching people go by. (Use would.)
Would for typical past behaviour.
