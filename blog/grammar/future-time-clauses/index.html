Future Time Clauses (when, as soon as, until)

Level: B1 · Free grammar guide

After when, as soon as, until, before, after and once, we use the present simple (not will) to talk about the future: I'll call you when I get home. (not when I will get home)

The rule

Time wordExample
whenI'll tell her when I see her.
as soon asAs soon as the film ends, we'll go.
until / tillWait here until I come back.
beforeFinish your homework before you go out.
afterAfter she graduates, she'll travel.
onceOnce you register, you'll get an email.

The other clause uses a future form or an imperative: I'll call you when I arrive. Text me when you arrive.

The present perfect to show completion

Use the present perfect to show that the first action will be finished before the second one happens: I'll go to bed when I've finished this chapter. You can leave as soon as you've done the test.

If and unless work the same way

If it rains, we'll stay at home. (not If it will rain) More practice: Zero and First Conditional.

When or if?

When = you're sure it will happen: I'll call you when I get home. If = it might happen: I'll call you if I have time.

Examples

  1. I'll send you the photos when I get home. (when + present simple)
  2. As soon as the rain stops, we'll go for a walk. (as soon as)
  3. We'll wait until you're ready. (until)
  4. Brush your teeth before you go to bed. (imperative + before)
  5. After I finish university, I'm going to travel. (after + going to)
  6. Once you have your visa, you can book the flight. (once)
  7. I'll call you as soon as I've spoken to the manager. (present perfect: completion)
  8. She won't leave until she gets an answer. (until)
  9. When I see him, I'll give him your message. (time clause first)
  10. Let me know if you need any help. (if + present simple)

Common mistakes

❌ I'll call you when I will arrive.

✅ I'll call you when I arrive. (After when, use the present simple.)

❌ As soon as she will finish, she'll come.

✅ As soon as she finishes, she'll come. (After as soon as, use the present simple.)

❌ Wait until I will come back.

✅ Wait until I come back. (After until, use the present simple.)

❌ When I'll see him, I'll tell him.

✅ When I see him, I'll tell him. (No will in the time clause.)

❌ Before you will leave, close the windows.

✅ Before you leave, close the windows. (After before, use the present simple.)

❌ I'll wait until you will be ready.

✅ I'll wait until you're ready. (After until, use the present simple.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. I'll call you when I ___ home.
After when (future meaning), use the present simple: get.
2. She ___ you as soon as she lands tomorrow.
The main clause is about the future: will phone.
3. Wait here until I ___ back.
After until, use the present simple: come.
4. As soon as the film ___, we'll go for dinner.
After as soon as, use the present simple: ends.
5. I won't buy a new phone until this one ___.
After until, use the present simple: breaks.
6. Before you ___ the house, turn off the lights.
After before, use the present simple: leave.
7. Choose the correct sentence.
Present simple after when, will in the main clause.
8. After she ___ university, she's going to work in Spain.
After after, use the present simple: finishes.
9. I'll go to bed when I ___ this chapter.
To show the reading will be complete first: present perfect, 've finished.
10. I'll text you as soon as I ___ the results.
After as soon as, use the present simple: get.
11. ___ you register, you'll receive an email.
Once = as soon as / after. It makes sense with the result.
12. Let's wait ___ the rain stops.
We wait up to the moment it stops: until.
13. We'll start the meeting when everyone ___.
After when, present simple. Everyone is singular: arrives.
14. I'll lend you the book after I ___ it.
The reading will be finished first: present perfect, 've read.
15. Which sentence is correct?
If works like a time word: present simple after if, will in the main clause.
