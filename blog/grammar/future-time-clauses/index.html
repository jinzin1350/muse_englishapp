جمله‌واره‌های زمانی آینده (when، as soon as)Future Time Clauses (When I get home, I'll…)

در جمله‌هایی که دربارهٔ آینده‌اند، کلمه‌هایی مثل when, as soon as, before, after, until یک جمله‌وارهٔ زمانی را شروع می‌کنند. در جمله‌وارهٔ زمانی زمان حال می‌آوریم، نه will: I'll call you when I get home. نه when I will get home. (فارسی هم می‌گوید «وقتی رسیدم زنگ می‌زنم»، نه «وقتی خواهم رسید».)

این همان قاعدهٔ if در شرطی نوع اول است. برای فرق if و when صفحهٔ شرطی نوع صفر و اول را ببینید.

A2 · مقدماتی

when, as soon as, before, after + حال A2

برای آینده، بعد از when, as soon as, before, after, until حال ساده بیاورید: I'll text you as soon as I arrive.

جمله‌وارهٔ زمانی (حال)جملهٔ اصلی (آینده)
When I finish work,I'll go to the gym.
As soon as the film ends,we'll leave.
Before you go out,turn off the lights.
After we eat,we're going to watch a film.

جملهٔ اصلی یک شکل آینده (will, going to) یا فعل امری دارد. جمله‌وارهٔ زمانی حال است: I'll be ready when you come.

وقتی جمله‌وارهٔ زمانی اول می‌آید، ویرگول بگذارید: When I get home, I'll call you. / I'll call you when I get home.

  • I'll call you when I get to the hotel. when + حال
  • As soon as the shop opens, I'm going to buy that dress. as soon as + حال
  • Please lock the door before you leave. before + حال
  • We'll have dinner after the kids go to bed. after + حال

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'll call you when I will arrive. → ✅ I'll call you when I arrive. بعد از when حرف will نمی‌آید.
  • ❌ As soon as she will finish, we'll go. → ✅ As soon as she finishes, we'll go. بعد از as soon as حال می‌آید.

تمرین سطح A2

1. I'll phone you when I ___ home.
When + حال ساده.
2. ___ the rain stops, we'll go for a walk.
As soon as + حال.
3. We'll start the meeting when everyone ___ (arrive).
When + حال ساده (یا حال کامل).

B1 · متوسط

When I've finished…: حال کامل در جمله‌وارهٔ زمانی B1

در جمله‌وارهٔ زمانی حال کامل بیاورید تا نشان دهید کار اول قبل از کار دوم کامل تمام می‌شود: I'll call you when I've finished work.

When I finish work, I'll call you و When I've finished work, I'll call you هر دو دربارهٔ آینده‌اند. حال کامل تأکید می‌کند که کار اول اول کاملاً تمام می‌شود.

حال کامل با when, after, as soon as, until, once رایج است: You can go out after you've done your homework.

وقتی دو کار هم‌زمان اتفاق می‌افتند حال کامل نیاورید: When I see him, I'll tell him. (اگر منظورتان همان لحظه است، نه When I've seen him)

  • I'll lend you the book when I've read it. اول خواندن، بعد قرض دادن
  • As soon as I've saved enough money, I'll buy a car. اول کامل تمام می‌شود
  • Don't go until you've signed the contract. اول کامل تمام می‌شود
  • After she's had lunch, she'll call you back. اول کامل تمام می‌شود

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'll give it back when I will have read it. → ✅ I'll give it back when I've read it. در جمله‌وارهٔ زمانی will نمی‌آید.

تمرین سطح B1

4. You can watch TV when you ___ your homework.
اول کامل تمام می‌شود: حال کامل.
5. کدام جمله تأکید می‌کند که کار اول کامل تمام می‌شود؟
When I've finished یعنی اول کاملاً تمام می‌شود.
6. As soon as I ___ (pack) my bags, we can leave.
اول کامل تمام می‌شود: have packed (یا pack).

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Until, once, by the time, the moment, next time B2

Other time words follow the same rule: I'll wait until you arrive. Once you know the rules, it's easy. By the time we get there, the film will have started.

ConjunctionMeaningExample
until / tillup to the time whenStay here until I get back.
onceafter, as soon asOnce you start, you can't stop.
by the timenot later thanBy the time you arrive, I'll have left.
the moment / the minuteimmediately whenI'll call you the moment I land.
next time / every timeon the next occasionNext time you come, bring your guitar.

By the time often goes with the future perfect in the main clause: By the time we get there, the shop will have closed. See Future Continuous and Future Perfect.

Don't use until with a negative meaning in the time clause: I'll wait until you come (not until you don't come).

  • I won't stop until I find it. (up to the time)
  • Once you've tried it, you'll love it. (after)
  • By the time the guests arrive, dinner will be ready. (not later than)
  • Tell me the moment you hear anything. (immediately)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'll wait until you will come back. → ✅ I'll wait until you come back. (present after until)
  • ❌ By the time we will get there, the film will have started. → ✅ By the time we get there, the film will have started. (present after by the time)

تمرین سطح B2

7. By the time we ___ to the station, the train will have left.
By the time + present simple.
8. I'll wait here ___ you finish. Take your time.
Up to the time when: until.
9. ___ you understand the basics, the rest is easy. (one word: as soon as)
Once = as soon as.

C1 · پیشرفته

When will is correct after when and if C1

Will is correct after when in questions and noun clauses (I don't know when he'll come), and after if for willingness (if you'll wait). Only time clauses take the present.

The no will rule is only for time clauses (when = at the time that). When when introduces a question or a noun clause (the object of a verb like know, ask, wonder), use the normal future: When will the results be ready? I don't know when they'll be ready.

Compare: Tell me when he arrives (= at the time he arrives; time clause) and Tell me when he'll arrive (= tell me the time of his arrival; noun clause).

After if, will is possible for willingness or insistence: If you'll follow me, I'll show you your room. See Zero and First Conditional.

  • I wonder when she'll call. (noun clause: will)
  • Do you know when the shop will open? (noun clause: will)
  • I'll call you when the shop opens. (time clause: present)
  • If you'll just wait a moment, I'll find the manager. (willingness)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I don't know when he arrives tomorrow. (asking about the time) → ✅ I don't know when he'll arrive tomorrow. (noun clause: will (the present is possible for timetables))

تمرین سطح C1

10. Do you know when the next bus ___?
A noun clause after know: will is fine (comes is also possible for a timetable).
11. Which sentence is correct?
A time clause: present simple.
12. I have no idea when they ___ (finish) the new road.
A noun clause: will finish.

همچنین ببینید:

  • if یا when؟
