حرف اضافهٔ مکانPrepositions of Place

حرف‌های اضافهٔ مکان می‌گویند چیزی کجاست: The keys are on the table. The cat is under the bed. I'm at the station.

سه حرف اضافهٔ اصلی in، on و at هستند و گیج‌کننده‌ترین‌ها هم همین‌ها هستند. این صفحه قدم‌به‌قدم توضیحشان می‌دهد.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

in, on, under, next to, between, behind A1

حرف‌های اضافهٔ پایهٔ مکان: in (داخلِ)، on (روی یک سطح)، under, next to, between, behind, in front of, opposite, near.

حرف اضافهمثال
in (داخلِ)The milk is in the fridge.
on (روی سطح)Your phone is on the table.
under (زیرِ)The cat is under the bed.
next to / beside (کنارِ)The bank is next to the café.
between (بینِ)I sat between Ali and Sara.
behind / in front of (پشتِ / جلوی)The car is behind the house. There's a tree in front of the house.
opposite (روبه‌روی)The school is opposite the park. (آن طرف خیابان)
near (نزدیکِ)We live near the beach.

next to و in front of همهٔ کلمه‌هایشان را لازم دارند: next to the door, in front of the school. near حرف to نمی‌خواهد: near the door.

  • Your keys are in your bag. in (داخلِ)
  • There's a picture on the wall. on (روی)
  • The shoes are under the bed. under (زیرِ)
  • I live next to a supermarket. next to (کنارِ)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The book is in the table. → ✅ The book is on the table. روی سطح: on.
  • ❌ She sat next me. → ✅ She sat next to me. next to.
  • ❌ The park is in front the school. → ✅ The park is in front of the school. in front of.

تمرین سطح A1

1. The cat is sleeping ___ the sofa.
روی سطح: on.
2. The bank is ___ the post office and the café.
بین دو چیز: between.
3. Your glasses are ___ the table, next to your phone. (one word)
روی سطح: on.

A2 · مقدماتی

in، on یا at؟ at the door, in the room, on the wall A2

at یعنی یک نقطه یا جایی که کاری آنجا انجام می‌شود (at the bus stop, at work, at a party). in یعنی داخلِ یک محدوده یا فضا (in the kitchen, in Toronto). on یعنی روی یک سطح یا یک خط (on the wall, on the second floor).

atinon
یک نقطه: at the door, at the traffic lightsداخلِ: in the box, in the carسطح: on the table, on the wall
مراسم یا فعالیت: at a party, at work, at schoolشهر یا کشور: in Tehran, in Canadaطبقه‌ها: on the third floor
نشانی: at 25 King Streetاتاق یا ساختمان (داخلش): in the kitchenخیابان: on Queen Street
at home, at the stationin bed, in hospitalon the left / right, on the map

برای ساختمان، وقتی منظورتان فعالیت یا محل قرار است at بیاورید: I'll meet you at the cinema. وقتی منظورتان داخل آن است in بیاورید: It's cold in the cinema.

با اسم خیابان on می‌آید (on Queen Street)، اما با نشانی کامل at (at 15 Queen Street).

  • I'll wait for you at the bus stop. یک نقطه
  • She lives in Vancouver. شهر
  • Our flat is on the fifth floor. طبقه
  • He's at work until six. فعالیت

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I live at Canada. → ✅ I live in Canada. کشور: in.
  • ❌ My office is in the second floor. → ✅ My office is on the second floor. طبقه: on. (فارسی می‌گوید «در طبقهٔ دوم»، ولی انگلیسی on.)
  • ❌ There were 100 people in the party. → ✅ There were 100 people at the party. مراسم: at.

تمرین سطح A2

4. My sister lives ___ Montreal.
شهر: in.
5. Let's meet ___ the main gate of the museum.
محل قرار: at.
6. Our classroom is ___ the third floor. (one word)
طبقه‌ها: on.

B1 · متوسط

on the bus, in the car؛ arrive in یا arrive at B1

برای وسیلهٔ نقلیهٔ عمومی و دوچرخه on بیاورید (on the bus, on a plane, on my bike) و برای ماشین و تاکسی in (in the car). به شهر یا کشور arrive in؛ به ساختمان یا یک مکان arrive at (arrive at the airport). هیچ‌وقت arrive to نگویید.

الگومثال
on + اتوبوس، قطار، هواپیما، کشتی، دوچرخهI read on the bus. She came on her bike.
in + ماشین، تاکسیWe sat in the car.
get on / off (سوار / پیاده شدن از اتوبوس یا قطار)Get off the bus at the next stop.
get in(to) / out of (سوار / پیاده شدن از ماشین یا تاکسی)Get into the car, quickly!
arrive in + شهر / کشورWe arrived in Paris at noon.
arrive at + ساختمان / مکانWe arrived at the hotel at 3.
go to / get to + یک مکانHow do I get to the station? (اما get home بدون to)

by + وسیلهٔ نقلیه، بدون the: by bus, by car, by plane (اما پیاده: on foot).

  • I met him on the train to Montreal. on + قطار
  • Get into the taxi. into + تاکسی
  • What time did you arrive at the airport? arrive at
  • We arrived in Canada in 2019. arrive in

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I arrived to the hotel late. → ✅ I arrived at the hotel late. arrive at. (فارسی می‌گوید «به هتل رسیدم»، ولی arrive to غلط است.)
  • ❌ She was sitting on the car. → ✅ She was sitting in the car. ماشین: in.
  • ❌ I go to work with bus. → ✅ I go to work by bus. by + وسیلهٔ نقلیه. (فارسی می‌گوید «با اتوبوس»، ولی انگلیسی by bus.)

تمرین سطح B1

7. We arrived ___ the airport two hours before the flight.
یک مکان: arrive at.
8. I always listen to music ___ the bus.
وسیلهٔ نقلیهٔ عمومی: on.
9. Get ___ the car. We're leaving! (one word)
ماشین: get in / into.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Over, above, across, through, along, past B2

Above / below = higher / lower. Over = above, often covering or moving to the other side. Across = from one side to the other of a flat surface. Through = inside, from one end to the other. Along = following a line. Past = going by.

PrepositionExample
above / belowThe plane flew above the clouds. The temperature is below zero.
over (covering or moving)She put a blanket over the baby. He jumped over the wall.
across (side to side)We walked across the bridge.
through (inside something)The train went through the tunnel. We walked through the forest.
along (a line)We walked along the river.
past (going by)Go past the bank and turn left.
into / out of / ontoShe jumped into the pool. Get out of the car.

For numbers and levels, use above / below: above average, 10 degrees below zero. Over often means more than: over 100 people.

  • A helicopter flew over the city. (over)
  • We swam across the lake. (across)
  • The road goes through the mountains. (through)
  • Walk along this street for 200 metres. (along)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We walked through the bridge. → ✅ We walked across the bridge. (side to side: across)
  • ❌ The cat jumped in the table. → ✅ The cat jumped onto the table. (movement to a surface: onto)
  • ❌ It's three degrees under zero. → ✅ It's three degrees below zero. (temperature: below)

تمرین سطح B2

10. Look both ways before you walk ___ the road to the other side.
Side to side: across.
11. The train goes ___ a long tunnel under the mountain.
Inside, end to end: through.
12. The temperature fell to ten degrees ___ zero last night. (one word)
Temperature: below zero.

C1 · پیشرفته

Under pressure, on the brink of, beyond repair C1

Prepositions of place are often used figuratively: under pressure / control / construction, on the verge / brink of, beyond repair / belief, within reach / walking distance, among (in a group).

ExpressionExampleMeaning
underunder pressure, under construction, under controlaffected by / in the process of
on the verge / brink ofThe company is on the brink of collapse.very close to
beyondThe car is beyond repair. It's beyond belief.too much for
withinThe hotel is within walking distance.inside the range of
among / betweenShe was among the winners. (a group) / a secret between you and me
at the heart ofthe problem at the heart of the debatecentral to

Between is for clearly separate things, often two; among is for a group or crowd: The house is hidden among the trees.

  • The new bridge is still under construction. (under)
  • The species is on the verge of extinction. (on the verge of)
  • My old phone is beyond repair. (beyond)
  • The beach is within walking distance. (within)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The situation is in control now. → ✅ The situation is under control now. (under control)
  • ❌ The car is over repair. → ✅ The car is beyond repair. (beyond repair)

تمرین سطح C1

13. Don't worry. Everything is ___ control.
Under control.
14. The old theatre is ___ repair. They'll have to knock it down.
Beyond repair.
15. Several people I know were ___ the guests at the wedding. (one word)
In a group: among.
