Prepositions of Place (in, on, under, next to)

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

Prepositions of place tell us where something is: The keys are on the table. The cat is under the bed.

The main prepositions of place

PrepositionMeaningExample
ininside somethingThe milk is in the fridge.
ontouching the top or a surfaceYour phone is on the table. There's a picture on the wall.
underbelow somethingThe dog is sleeping under the table.
next toat the side ofSit next to me.
betweenin the middle of two thingsThe bank is between the café and the hotel.
behindat the back ofThe garden is behind the house.
in front ofbefore, facing the frontThere's a bus stop in front of the school.
oppositeon the other side, facingThe pharmacy is opposite the bank.
nearnot far fromIs there a supermarket near here?
abovehigher than, not touchingThe light is above the table.

In, on or at?

  • in a room, a box, a building, a city, a country: in the kitchen, in Paris, in Canada
  • on a surface, a floor or a street: on the desk, on the floor, on the second floor, on Main Street
  • at a point or place where something happens: at the bus stop, at the door, at the station
  • Fixed phrases: at home, at work, at school and on the bus, on the train, on the plane

Use between for two things (between the bank and the café) and next to with to (next to the window, not next the window).

Examples

  1. My books are in my bag. (inside)
  2. There's a clock on the wall. (surface)
  3. Your shoes are under the bed. (below)
  4. Our house is next to a park. (at the side)
  5. The café is between the bank and the cinema. (two things)
  6. Don't stand behind me! (at the back)
  7. I'll meet you in front of the museum. (facing the front)
  8. The hotel is opposite the station. (facing, across the street)
  9. We live near the beach. (not far)
  10. Wait for me at the bus stop. (a point)

Common mistakes

❌ The book is next the lamp.

✅ The book is next to the lamp. (Next to always needs to.)

❌ I live at Toronto.

✅ I live in Toronto. (Use in for cities and countries.)

❌ I'm in home.

✅ I'm at home. (Fixed phrase: at home.)

❌ The shop is between the bank.

✅ The shop is between the bank and the café. (Between needs two things.)

❌ There's a picture in the wall.

✅ There's a picture on the wall. (On a surface.)

❌ The bus stop is in front the school.

✅ The bus stop is in front of the school. (In front of needs of.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. The milk is ___ the fridge. It's nice and cold.
Inside something: in the fridge.
2. There's a picture ___ the wall.
On a surface: on the wall.
3. The ball is ___ the table. (The table is above it.)
Below something: under.
4. The bank is ___ the post office and the supermarket.
In the middle of two things: between … and …
5. I live ___ Toronto.
Cities and countries: in.
6. Sit ___ me. There's a free chair here.
At the side of: next to (with to).
7. Are you ___ home? – No, I'm at work.
Fixed phrase: at home.
8. Don't stand ___ the TV! I can't see.
If I can't see the TV, you are in front of it.
9. Wait for me ___ the bus stop.
A point or place: at the bus stop.
10. The supermarket is ___ the bank. Just cross the street.
On the other side of the street, facing: opposite.
11. The children are ___ school until 3 o'clock.
Fixed phrase: at school.
12. The books are ___ the box. Open it and take one.
You open the box to take one, so they're inside: in.
13. Put the plates ___ the table, please. Dinner is ready.
On a surface: on the table.
14. Our garden is ___ the house, so you can't see it from the street.
At the back: behind the house.
15. The lamp is ___ the table. It hangs from the ceiling.
Higher than, not touching: above.
16. Is there a supermarket ___ here? – Yes, it's a five-minute walk.
Not far from: near here.
