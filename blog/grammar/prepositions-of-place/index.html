Prepositions of Place

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

Prepositions of place say where something is: The keys are on the table. The cat is under the bed. I'm at the station.

The three most important are in, on and at, and they're also the most confusing. This page goes step by step.

A1 · Beginner

In, on, under, next to, between, behind A1

PrepositionExample
in (inside)The milk is in the fridge.
on (on a surface)Your phone is on the table.
underThe cat is under the bed.
next to / besideThe bank is next to the café.
betweenI sat between Ali and Sara.
behind / in front ofThe car is behind the house. There's a tree in front of the house.
oppositeThe school is opposite the park. (on the other side of the road)
nearWe live near the beach.

  • Your keys are in your bag. (in)
  • There's a picture on the wall. (on)
  • The shoes are under the bed. (under)
  • I live next to a supermarket. (next to)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The book is in the table. → ✅ The book is on the table. (surface: on)
  • ❌ She sat next me. → ✅ She sat next to me. (next to)
  • ❌ The park is in front the school. → ✅ The park is in front of the school. (in front of)

Practice: A1

1. The cat is sleeping <span class="gap">___</span> the sofa.
Surface: on.
2. The bank is <span class="gap">___</span> the post office and the café.
Between two things.
3. Your glasses are <span class="gap">___</span> the table, next to your phone. (one word)
Surface: on.

A2 · Elementary

In, on or at? At the door, in the room, on the wall A2

atinon
a point: at the door, at the traffic lightsinside: in the box, in the cara surface: on the table, on the wall
an event or activity: at a party, at work, at schoola city or country: in Tehran, in Canadafloors: on the third floor
an address: at 25 King Streeta room or building (inside): in the kitchena street: on Queen Street
at home, at the stationin bed, in hospitalon the left / right, on the map

  • I'll wait for you at the bus stop. (a point)
  • She lives in Vancouver. (a city)
  • Our flat is on the fifth floor. (a floor)
  • He's at work until six. (an activity)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I live at Canada. → ✅ I live in Canada. (country: in)
  • ❌ My office is in the second floor. → ✅ My office is on the second floor. (floor: on)
  • ❌ There were 100 people in the party. → ✅ There were 100 people at the party. (event: at)

Practice: A2

A city: in.
5. Let's meet <span class="gap">___</span> the main gate of the museum.
A meeting point: at.

Floors: on.

B1 · Intermediate

On the bus, in the car, arrive in or at B1

PatternExample
on + bus, train, plane, ship, bikeI read on the bus. She came on her bike.
in + car, taxiWe sat in the car.
get on / off the bus or trainGet off the bus at the next stop.
get in(to) / out of the car or taxiGet into the car, quickly!
arrive in + city / countryWe arrived in Paris at noon.
arrive at + building / placeWe arrived at the hotel at 3.
go to / get to a placeHow do I get to the station? (but get home)

  • I met him on the train to Montreal. (on + train)
  • Get into the taxi. (into + taxi)
  • What time did you arrive at the airport? (arrive at)
  • We arrived in Canada in 2019. (arrive in)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I arrived to the hotel late. → ✅ I arrived at the hotel late. (arrive at)
  • ❌ She was sitting on the car. → ✅ She was sitting in the car. (car: in)
  • ❌ I go to work with bus. → ✅ I go to work by bus. (by + transport)

Practice: B1

7. We arrived <span class="gap">___</span> the airport two hours before the flight.
A place: arrive at.
8. I always listen to music <span class="gap">___</span> the bus.
Public transport: on.
9. Get <span class="gap">___</span> the car. We're leaving! (one word)
Car: get in / into.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Over, above, across, through, along, past B2

PrepositionExample
above / belowThe plane flew above the clouds. The temperature is below zero.
over (covering or moving)She put a blanket over the baby. He jumped over the wall.
across (side to side)We walked across the bridge.
through (inside something)The train went through the tunnel. We walked through the forest.
along (a line)We walked along the river.
past (going by)Go past the bank and turn left.
into / out of / ontoShe jumped into the pool. Get out of the car.

  • A helicopter flew over the city. (over)
  • We swam across the lake. (across)
  • The road goes through the mountains. (through)
  • Walk along this street for 200 metres. (along)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ We walked through the bridge. → ✅ We walked across the bridge. (side to side: across)
  • ❌ The cat jumped in the table. → ✅ The cat jumped onto the table. (movement to a surface: onto)
  • ❌ It's three degrees under zero. → ✅ It's three degrees below zero. (temperature: below)

Practice: B2

10. Look both ways before you walk <span class="gap">___</span> the road to the other side.
Side to side: across.
11. The train goes <span class="gap">___</span> a long tunnel under the mountain.
Inside, end to end: through.
12. The temperature fell to ten degrees <span class="gap">___</span> zero last night. (one word)
Temperature: below zero.

C1 · Advanced

Under pressure, on the brink of, beyond repair C1

ExpressionExampleMeaning
underunder pressure, under construction, under controlaffected by / in the process of
on the verge / brink ofThe company is on the brink of collapse.very close to
beyondThe car is beyond repair. It's beyond belief.too much for
withinThe hotel is within walking distance.inside the range of
among / betweenShe was among the winners. (a group) / a secret between you and me
at the heart ofthe problem at the heart of the debatecentral to

  • The new bridge is still under construction. (under)
  • The species is on the verge of extinction. (on the verge of)
  • My old phone is beyond repair. (beyond)
  • The beach is within walking distance. (within)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The situation is in control now. → ✅ The situation is under control now. (under control)
  • ❌ The car is over repair. → ✅ The car is beyond repair. (beyond repair)

Practice: C1

Under control.

15. Several people I know were <span class="gap">___</span> the guests at the wedding. (one word)
