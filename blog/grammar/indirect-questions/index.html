سؤال‌های غیرمستقیمIndirect Questions (Do you know where…?)

سؤال غیرمستقیم سؤالی است که داخل یک جملهٔ دیگر آمده: Where is the station? ← Do you know where the station is? سؤال‌های غیرمستقیم مؤدبانه‌ترند و در مکالمه خیلی رایج‌اند.

قاعده: بعد از کلمهٔ پرسشی (یا if / whether)، ترتیب جملهٔ خبری بیاورید و do / does / did نیاورید. برای سؤال‌های مستقیم صفحهٔ کلمه‌های پرسشی و ساختار سؤال را ببینید.

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

Do you know where…? I don't know what… A2

بعد از Do you know…? / I don't know… ترتیب جملهٔ خبری بیاورید: Do you know where she lives? (نه where does she live)

سؤال مستقیمسؤال غیرمستقیم
Where does she live?Do you know where she lives?
What time is it?Do you know what time it is?
Why did he leave?I don't know why he left.
How much does it cost?I'm not sure how much it costs.

کلمهٔ پرسشی می‌ماند، اما ترتیب کلمه‌ها فاعل + فعل می‌شود و do / does / did حذف می‌شوند: Where is the bank? ← Do you know where the bank is?

علامت سؤال را فقط وقتی بگذارید که کل جمله سؤال باشد: Do you know where it is? / I don't know where it is.

  • Do you know where the post office is? ترتیب جملهٔ خبری
  • I don't know what her name is. ترتیب جملهٔ خبری
  • I have no idea why he said that. بدون did
  • Can you remember when the film starts? بدون does

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Do you know where is the station? → ✅ Do you know where the station is? ترتیب جملهٔ خبری.
  • ❌ I don't know where does he work. → ✅ I don't know where he works. بدون does.
  • ❌ Do you know what time is it? → ✅ Do you know what time it is? ترتیب جملهٔ خبری.

تمرین سطح A2

1. Do you know where ___?
ترتیب جملهٔ خبری: the bathroom is.
2. I don't know what time ___.
ترتیب جملهٔ خبری، بدون does.
3. Where does Ali work? → Do you know where Ali ___ (work)?
ترتیب جملهٔ خبری: Ali works.

B1 · متوسط

if و whether: سؤال‌های بله/نهِ غیرمستقیم B1

برای سؤال‌های بله/نه if یا whether بیاورید (یعنی «که آیا»): Is she coming? ← Do you know if / whether she's coming?

وقتی سؤال مستقیم کلمهٔ پرسشی ندارد (سؤال بله/نه)، if یا whether بیاورید: Are you free? ← I wonder if you're free. Did he pass? ← Do you know whether he passed?

whether کمی رسمی‌تر است و با or not و بعد از حرف اضافه انتخاب معمول است: I'm not sure whether or not to go. It depends on whether it rains.

  • Do you know if the bank is open today? سؤال بله/نه
  • I wonder whether she got my message. whether
  • I'm not sure if I locked the door. not sure if
  • It depends on whether we have time. حرف اضافه + whether

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Do you know is the shop open? → ✅ Do you know if the shop is open? if + ترتیب جملهٔ خبری.
  • ❌ I wonder if does she like me. → ✅ I wonder if she likes me. بعد از if حرف does نمی‌آید.
  • ❌ It depends on if it rains. → ✅ It depends on whether it rains. حرف اضافه + whether.

Could you tell me…? سؤال‌های غیرمستقیمِ مؤدبانه B1

سؤال غیرمستقیم درخواستِ اطلاعات را مؤدبانه‌تر می‌کند: Could you tell me where the toilets are? Would you mind telling me how old you are?

مستقیم (رُک‌تر)غیرمستقیم (مؤدبانه‌تر)
Where is the station?Could you tell me where the station is?
What time does it close?Do you happen to know what time it closes?
How much is this?Can I ask how much this is?
Why was my card refused?I was wondering why my card was refused.

این عبارت‌ها را با غریبه‌ها، در محل کار، در مغازه و در موقعیت‌های رسمی به کار ببرید. سؤالِ داخل جمله همیشه ترتیب جملهٔ خبری دارد.

I was wondering… و I'd like to know… جملهٔ خبری‌اند، پس با نقطه تمام می‌شوند: I was wondering if you could help me.

  • Could you tell me where the lifts are? مؤدبانه
  • Do you happen to know if there's a pharmacy near here? خیلی مؤدبانه
  • Would you mind telling me what your name is? مؤدبانه (would you mind + ‑ing)
  • I'd like to know why the flight was cancelled. شکایت رسمی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Could you tell me where is the exit? → ✅ Could you tell me where the exit is? ترتیب جملهٔ خبری.
  • ❌ I was wondering if could you help me? → ✅ I was wondering if you could help me. ترتیب جملهٔ خبری؛ جمله خبری است.

تمرین سطح B1

4. Is the museum open on Mondays? → Do you know ___ open on Mondays?
If + ترتیب جملهٔ خبری.
5. It depends ___ we can get tickets.
بعد از حرف اضافه: whether.
6. Did she pass the exam? → I don't know ___ she passed the exam. (one word)
بله/نه: if یا whether.
7. Excuse me, could you tell me ___?
سؤالِ داخل جمله ترتیب جملهٔ خبری دارد.
8. کدام از همه مؤدبانه‌تر است؟
Do you happen to know…? خیلی مؤدبانه است.
9. Could you tell me how much this jacket ___ (cost)?
ترتیب جملهٔ خبری: this jacket costs.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

What to do, whether to go: question word + to-infinitive B2

After verbs like know, decide, wonder, ask, explain, a question word + to + verb can replace a full clause: I don't know what to say. She can't decide whether to go.

Question word + to-infinitive talks about what someone should or could do: I don't know what to do. = I don't know what I should do.

It works with what, where, when, how, who, which and whether (but not if or why): Can you show me how to use this? I'm not sure whether to call her.

The subject of the infinitive is the same as the subject of the sentence, or people in general: She told me where to park. (= where I should park)

  • I don't know what to wear to the wedding. (what I should wear)
  • Can you tell me how to get to the station? (how I can get)
  • We couldn't decide whether to stay or go. (whether + to)
  • She showed me where to sign. (where I should sign)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I don't know what should I do. → ✅ I don't know what to do. / I don't know what I should do. (question word + to)
  • ❌ I'm not sure if to accept. → ✅ I'm not sure whether to accept. (whether + to (not if))
  • ❌ I don't know why to go. → ✅ I don't know why I should go. (not why + to)

تمرین سطح B2

10. I've never used this machine. Can you show me ___ it?
How to + verb.
11. He can't decide ___ to take the job or not.
Whether to + verb (not if to).
12. I didn't know what ___ (say), so I said nothing.
What to + verb.

C1 · پیشرفته

What he said is true: question-word clauses as subjects and objects C1

A clause like what he said, where she lives, how it works can act as a noun: as a subject (What he said is true) or an object (I understand why you're upset).

Clauses beginning with what, where, how, why, whether, who can be used like a noun phrase. They keep statement order:

  • as subject: What you need is a holiday. How he did it is a mystery. Whether we win doesn't matter.
  • as object: I can't believe what she told me. I understand why you left.
  • after prepositions: It depends on how much it costs. We talked about where to live.

A clause as subject takes a singular verb: What they want is more money.

What clauses are also used for emphasis: What I love about Toronto is the diversity. See Cleft Sentences.

  • What happened next was amazing. (subject)
  • Why she left remains unclear. (subject (formal))
  • I can't remember where I parked. (object)
  • It depends on whether the weather improves. (after a preposition)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ What did he say is true. → ✅ What he said is true. (statement order)
  • ❌ Where does she live is a secret. → ✅ Where she lives is a secret. (statement order)
  • ❌ I can't believe what did she tell me. → ✅ I can't believe what she told me. (statement order, no did)

تمرین سطح C1

13. ___ is a mystery to everyone.
A clause as subject: statement order.
14. I don't agree with ___.
Object clause: statement order.
15. ___ (whether / we / go) depends on the weather.
A clause as subject: Whether we go…
