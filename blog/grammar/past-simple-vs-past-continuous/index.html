Past Simple vs Past Continuous

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

The past simple (I walked) tells us that an action happened and finished. The past continuous (I was walking) tells us that an action was in progress at a moment in the past.

We often use them together: I was walking home when it started to rain. This page shows how to choose between them, from the basic pattern to the differences in meaning.

A2 · Elementary

A longer action and a short action A2

  • I was having a shower when the phone rang.
  • We were driving to the airport when the car broke down.

Longer action (past continuous)Short action (past simple)
She was reading……when the lights went out.
They were playing football……when it started to rain.
I was walking to work……when I saw an old friend.

  • I was cooking dinner when my sister arrived. (cooking = longer, arrived = short)
  • He was cycling to school when he fell off his bike. (fell interrupted cycling)
  • When I saw them, they were sitting in the park. (the short action can come first)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I cooked dinner when my sister was arriving. → ✅ I was cooking dinner when my sister arrived. (Longer action: past continuous; short action: past simple.)
  • ❌ When the phone was ringing, I had a shower. → ✅ When the phone rang, I was having a shower. (The ring is the short action.)
  • ❌ I was knowing the answer. → ✅ I knew the answer. (know is a state verb.)

Practice: A2

1. I ___ TV when I heard a strange noise.
The longer action in progress: was watching.
2. We were having dinner when the lights ___ out.
The short action: past simple went.
3. She ___ (walk) to the bus stop when it started to snow.
Longer action in progress: was walking.
4. Find the mistake: tap the wrong part.
When I was seeing Sara, she was waiting for a taxi.
Correct: When I saw Sara, she was waiting for a taxi.
The short action is saw (past simple).

B1 · Intermediate

When and while B1

  • while + longer action: The phone rang while I was having a shower.
  • when + shorter action: I was having a shower when the phone rang.

  • While we were watching the film, someone knocked at the door. (while + longer action)
  • We were watching the film when someone knocked. (when + shorter action)
  • When the bell rang, everyone stood up. (two short actions: one after the other)
  • When I was at university, I worked in a café. (when + a period)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ When I was waiting for the bus, I was seeing an accident. → ✅ While I was waiting for the bus, I saw an accident. (The accident is a short action: saw.)
  • ❌ During I was waiting, I read the news. → ✅ While I was waiting, I read the news. (while + clause; during + noun)

How the tense changes the meaning B1

SentenceMeaning
When Ana got home, her husband cooked dinner.Ana got home → then he cooked.
When Ana got home, her husband was cooking dinner.He started before she got home and was still cooking.
When Ana got home, her husband had cooked dinner.Dinner was ready before she got home.

  • When the teacher came in, the students were talking. (already in progress)
  • When the teacher came in, the students stopped talking. (reaction: after she came in)
  • When I looked outside, it was snowing. (already in progress)
  • When we got to the station, the train was leaving. (in progress: we could still see it)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ When I woke up, the sun shone. → ✅ When I woke up, the sun was shining. (Already in progress when I woke up.)
  • ❌ When I saw the cake, I was eating it. → ✅ When I saw the cake, I ate it. (The reaction came after: past simple.)

Practice: B1

5. I hurt my back ___ I was carrying some heavy boxes.
While + the longer action in progress.
6. ___ the film ended, we went for a pizza.
Two short actions, one after the other: when.
7. Someone stole my bag while I ___ (look) at the menu and my friend was ordering.
Two actions in progress: was looking, like was ordering.
8. When we arrived, the party ___. We were just in time.
In progress at that moment: was starting. (Had finished would mean we were too late.)
9. When I called him, he was having dinner. What does this mean?
The past continuous = already in progress.
10. When I woke up, the sun ___ (shine) and the birds were singing.
Already in progress when I woke up: was shining.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Finished or unfinished? B2

Past simplePast continuous
I wrote a letter last night. (it's finished)I was writing a letter last night. (maybe not finished)
He drowned. (he died)He was drowning, but a lifeguard saved him. (it didn't happen)
They built a bridge. (it's there now)They were building a bridge when the money ran out. (not finished)

  • I was crossing the road when a car nearly hit me. (in progress, interrupted)
  • She was dying, but the doctors saved her. (didn't happen in the end)
  • We painted the kitchen on Saturday. (finished)
  • We were painting the kitchen when we ran out of paint. (not finished)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He drowned, but a lifeguard saved him. → ✅ He was drowning, but a lifeguard saved him. (He didn't die: the action was in progress.)
  • ❌ We were fixing the car yesterday, and now it works perfectly. → ✅ We fixed the car yesterday, and now it works perfectly. (The result is complete: past simple.)

Practice: B2

11. The building ___, but the firefighters saved it.
It wasn't destroyed: the action was in progress, not completed.
12. I was reading a novel last night. Did I finish it?
The past continuous doesn't say the action was completed.
13. They ___ (build) a new hospital, but the project stopped when the money ran out.
Not finished: were building.

C1 · Advanced

I was talking to… : introducing news and background C1

  • I was talking to Maryam the other day and she said she's moving to Vancouver.
  • I was reading an article about sleep, and it said we need eight hours.

  • I was chatting to my neighbour, and she told me about a great dentist. (background + news)
  • We were discussing your idea at the meeting, and everyone liked it. (background + result)
  • Sorry, what were you saying? (returning to the conversation)
  • As I was saying before the interruption, we need more staff. (continuing a topic)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I talking to Sara yesterday, and she said she's leaving. → ✅ I was talking to Sara yesterday, and she said she's leaving. (Past continuous: was + -ing.)
  • ❌ As I am saying, the deadline is Friday. → ✅ As I was saying, the deadline is Friday. (The fixed phrase is as I was saying.)

Practice: C1

14. ___ to my cousin last week, and she said she's getting married!
Past continuous to introduce news.
15. After an interruption, the speaker says: Now, as I ___, the results were very good.
As I was saying = continuing an interrupted point.
16. I ___ (read) an article about AI yesterday, and it said that most jobs will change.
Both are correct; was reading sets the background for the news.
