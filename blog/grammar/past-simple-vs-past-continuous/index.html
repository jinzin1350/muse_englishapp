گذشتهٔ ساده یا گذشتهٔ استمراریPast Simple vs Past Continuous

گذشتهٔ ساده (I walked) می‌گوید کاری انجام شد و تمام شد. گذشتهٔ استمراری (I was walking) می‌گوید کاری در لحظه‌ای در گذشته در جریان بود.

اغلب این دو را با هم به کار می‌بریم: I was walking home when it started to rain. این صفحه نشان می‌دهد چطور بین این دو انتخاب کنید، از الگوی پایه تا فرق‌های معنایی.

A2 · مقدماتی

یک کار طولانی و یک کار کوتاه A2

گذشتهٔ استمراری برای کارِ طولانی‌ای که در جریان بود؛ گذشتهٔ ساده برای کار کوتاهی که وسط آن اتفاق افتاد.

برای کارِ طولانی‌تر (پس‌زمینه) گذشتهٔ استمراری و برای کار کوتاه‌تری که وسطش پیش آمد گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید:

  • I was having a shower when the phone rang.
  • We were driving to the airport when the car broke down.

کارِ طولانی‌تر اول شروع شده بود. کار کوتاه وسط آن اتفاق افتاد.

کار طولانی (گذشتهٔ استمراری)کار کوتاه (گذشتهٔ ساده)
She was reading……when the lights went out.
They were playing football……when it started to rain.
I was walking to work……when I saw an old friend.

بعضی فعل‌ها معمولاً استمراری نمی‌شوند: know, want, like, believe, understand. بگویید I knew the answer، نه I was knowing the answer. صفحهٔ فعل‌های حالتی را ببینید.

  • I was cooking dinner when my sister arrived. cooking کار طولانی است، arrived کار کوتاه
  • He was cycling to school when he fell off his bike. fell وسط دوچرخه‌سواری پیش آمد
  • When I saw them, they were sitting in the park. کار کوتاه می‌تواند اول جمله بیاید

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I cooked dinner when my sister was arriving. → ✅ I was cooking dinner when my sister arrived. کار طولانی: گذشتهٔ استمراری؛ کار کوتاه: گذشتهٔ ساده.
  • ❌ When the phone was ringing, I had a shower. → ✅ When the phone rang, I was having a shower. زنگ خوردن کار کوتاه است.
  • ❌ I was knowing the answer. → ✅ I knew the answer. know فعل حالتی است.

تمرین سطح A2

1. I ___ TV when I heard a strange noise.
کار طولانی‌ای که در جریان بود: was watching.
2. We were having dinner when the lights ___ out.
کار کوتاه: گذشتهٔ ساده، went.
3. She ___ (walk) to the bus stop when it started to snow.
کار طولانی‌ای که در جریان بود: was walking.
4. اشتباه را پیدا کنید: روی بخش غلط بزنید.
When I was seeing Sara, she was waiting for a taxi.
شکل درست: When I saw Sara, she was waiting for a taxi.
کار کوتاه saw است (گذشتهٔ ساده).

B1 · متوسط

when و while B1

while معمولاً قبل از کار طولانی‌تر (گذشتهٔ استمراری) می‌آید. when معمولاً قبل از کار کوتاه‌تر (گذشتهٔ ساده).

هر دو کلمه می‌توانند دو کار را به هم وصل کنند، اما معمولاً جای متفاوتی دارند:

  • while + کار طولانی‌تر: The phone rang while I was having a shower.
  • when + کار کوتاه‌تر: I was having a shower when the phone rang.

when می‌تواند یک دورهٔ طولانی یا یک حالت را هم شروع کند: When I was a child, I lived in Mashhad. When we were living in Paris, we often went to museums.

اگر هر دو کار کوتاه‌اند، برای هر دو گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید. با when این یعنی یک کار بلافاصله بعد از دیگری اتفاق افتاد: When she saw the spider, she screamed.

می‌توانید با هر کدام از دو بخش شروع کنید. وقتی when یا while اول جمله است، بعد از بخش اول ویرگول بگذارید: While I was waiting, I read the news. / I read the news while I was waiting.

  • While we were watching the film, someone knocked at the door. while + کار طولانی‌تر
  • We were watching the film when someone knocked. when + کار کوتاه‌تر
  • When the bell rang, everyone stood up. دو کار کوتاه، یکی بعد از دیگری
  • When I was at university, I worked in a café. when + یک دوره

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ When I was waiting for the bus, I was seeing an accident. → ✅ While I was waiting for the bus, I saw an accident. تصادف کار کوتاه است: saw.
  • ❌ During I was waiting, I read the news. → ✅ While I was waiting, I read the news. while + جمله؛ during + اسم

زمان فعل چطور معنی را عوض می‌کند B1

When I arrived, they had lunch یعنی بعد از رسیدن من. When I arrived, they were having lunch یعنی از قبل در جریان بود.

با when، زمانِ فعلِ کار دوم ترتیب را نشان می‌دهد:

جملهمعنی
When Ana got home, her husband cooked dinner.آنا به خانه رسید ← بعد همسرش شام پخت.
When Ana got home, her husband was cooking dinner.همسرش قبل از رسیدن آنا شروع کرده بود و هنوز داشت شام می‌پخت.
When Ana got home, her husband had cooked dinner.شام قبل از رسیدن آنا آماده بود.

جملهٔ سوم گذشتهٔ کامل است. صفحهٔ گذشتهٔ کامل را ببینید.

از خودتان بپرسید: آیا آن کار در آن لحظه از قبل در جریان بود؟ اگر بله، گذشتهٔ استمراری بیاورید. اگر همان لحظه یا بعدش شروع شد، گذشتهٔ ساده.

  • When the teacher came in, the students were talking. از قبل در جریان بود
  • When the teacher came in, the students stopped talking. واکنش: بعد از آمدنش
  • When I looked outside, it was snowing. از قبل در جریان بود
  • When we got to the station, the train was leaving. در جریان بود: هنوز می‌دیدیمش

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ When I woke up, the sun shone. → ✅ When I woke up, the sun was shining. وقتی بیدار شدم از قبل در جریان بود.
  • ❌ When I saw the cake, I was eating it. → ✅ When I saw the cake, I ate it. واکنش بعدش آمد: گذشتهٔ ساده.

تمرین سطح B1

5. I hurt my back ___ I was carrying some heavy boxes.
while + کار طولانی‌ای که در جریان بود.
6. ___ the film ended, we went for a pizza.
دو کار کوتاه، یکی بعد از دیگری: when.
7. Someone stole my bag while I ___ (look) at the menu and my friend was ordering.
دو کار در جریان: was looking، مثل was ordering.
8. When we arrived, the party ___. We were just in time.
در آن لحظه در جریان بود: was starting. (had finished یعنی دیر رسیده بودیم.)
9. جملهٔ When I called him, he was having dinner. یعنی چه؟
گذشتهٔ استمراری یعنی از قبل در جریان بود.
10. When I woke up, the sun ___ (shine) and the birds were singing.
وقتی بیدار شدم از قبل در جریان بود: was shining.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Finished or unfinished? B2

The past simple often means the action was completed; the past continuous can mean it was not completed: I read a book vs I was reading a book.

The past simple usually presents an action as complete. The past continuous shows the action was in progress, so it may not have been finished:

Past simplePast continuous
I wrote a letter last night. (it's finished)I was writing a letter last night. (maybe not finished)
He drowned. (he died)He was drowning, but a lifeguard saved him. (it didn't happen)
They built a bridge. (it's there now)They were building a bridge when the money ran out. (not finished)

This is why the past continuous is useful when something stopped an action before it was completed.

Verbs that describe a change or result (die, drown, crash, fall, finish, build, fix) show this difference most clearly.

  • I was crossing the road when a car nearly hit me. (in progress, interrupted)
  • She was dying, but the doctors saved her. (didn't happen in the end)
  • We painted the kitchen on Saturday. (finished)
  • We were painting the kitchen when we ran out of paint. (not finished)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He drowned, but a lifeguard saved him. → ✅ He was drowning, but a lifeguard saved him. (He didn't die: the action was in progress.)
  • ❌ We were fixing the car yesterday, and now it works perfectly. → ✅ We fixed the car yesterday, and now it works perfectly. (The result is complete: past simple.)

تمرین سطح B2

11. The building ___, but the firefighters saved it.
It wasn't destroyed: the action was in progress, not completed.
12. I was reading a novel last night. Did I finish it?
The past continuous doesn't say the action was completed.
13. They ___ (build) a new hospital, but the project stopped when the money ran out.
Not finished: were building.

C1 · پیشرفته

I was talking to… : introducing news and background C1

We use the past continuous to introduce news or a report of what someone said: I was talking to Ali yesterday, and he told me…

In conversation, the past continuous often sets up a story or a piece of news. The background is in the past continuous; the news follows:

  • I was talking to Maryam the other day and she said she's moving to Vancouver.
  • I was reading an article about sleep, and it said we need eight hours.

With verbs of saying, the past continuous can also report something that was said recently, especially when we don't remember the exact words: Hassan was saying that the office might close. / What were you saying?

Compare: I talked to Maryam (a complete event, often as an answer to What did you do?) and I was talking to Maryam (background to what comes next).

The past continuous of say, tell, talk is common in spoken English when we come back to a topic: As I was saying, the deadline is Friday.

  • I was chatting to my neighbour, and she told me about a great dentist. (background + news)
  • We were discussing your idea at the meeting, and everyone liked it. (background + result)
  • Sorry, what were you saying? (returning to the conversation)
  • As I was saying before the interruption, we need more staff. (continuing a topic)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I talking to Sara yesterday, and she said she's leaving. → ✅ I was talking to Sara yesterday, and she said she's leaving. (Past continuous: was + -ing.)
  • ❌ As I am saying, the deadline is Friday. → ✅ As I was saying, the deadline is Friday. (The fixed phrase is as I was saying.)

تمرین سطح C1

14. ___ to my cousin last week, and she said she's getting married!
Past continuous to introduce news.
15. After an interruption, the speaker says: Now, as I ___, the results were very good.
As I was saying = continuing an interrupted point.
16. I ___ (read) an article about AI yesterday, and it said that most jobs will change.
Both are correct; was reading sets the background for the news.
