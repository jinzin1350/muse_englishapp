زمان حال استمراریPresent Continuous

حال استمراری را برای کارهایی به کار می‌بریم که همین حالا یا این روزها در جریان‌اند: I'm reading a book. She's studying for her exams this week.

ساختارش am / is / are + فعل ing‌دار است. در سطح‌های بالاتر برای برنامه‌های آینده، موقعیت‌هایی که دارند تغییر می‌کنند و عادت‌های آزاردهنده هم به کار می‌رود.

A1 · A2 · B1 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

ساختار و کاربرد: I'm working (همین حالا) A1

am / is / are + فعل ing‌دار، برای کاری که همین لحظه در جریان است.

ساختارمثال
مثبتفاعل + am / is / are + فعل ing‌دارI'm working. He's sleeping. They're playing.
منفیفاعل + am not / isn't / aren't + فعل ing‌دارI'm not working. She isn't sleeping. We aren't playing.
سؤالیAm / Is / Are + فاعل + فعل ing‌دار؟Are you working? Is he sleeping?
جواب کوتاهYes, + فاعل + am / is / are. / No, + فاعل + 'm not / isn't / aren't.Yes, I am. / No, she isn't.
سؤال با کلمهٔ پرسشیکلمهٔ پرسشی + am / is / are + فاعل + فعل ing‌دار؟What are you doing?
املا: فعل + ‑ingمثال
بیشتر فعل‌ها: ‑ing اضافه کنیدwork → working, read → reading, play → playing
فعل به ‑e ختم می‌شود: e را برداریدmake → making, write → writing, come → coming
یک حرف صدادار + یک حرف بی‌صدا در آخرِ هجای تکیه‌دار: حرف آخر را دوتا کنیدrun → running, sit → sitting, swim → swimming, stop → stopping
فعل به ‑ie ختم می‌شود: ie را به ‑ying تبدیل کنیدlie → lying, die → dying, tie → tying

برای کاری که همین لحظهٔ صحبت در جریان است. کلمه‌های رایج: now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!

در صحبت کردن معمولاً شکل کوتاه می‌آوریم: I'm, you're, he's, she's, it's, we're, they're. املای بریتانیایی و آمریکایی در بعضی فعل‌هایی که به ‑l ختم می‌شوند فرق دارد: travelling (بریتانیا) / traveling (آمریکا). هر دو درست‌اند.

  • I'm cooking dinner right now. همین حالا
  • She isn't working today. منفی
  • Are you listening to me? سؤالی
  • Look! It's snowing! Look! + استمراری
  • He's sitting next to the window. (sit → sitting)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I working now. → ✅ I'm working now. am / is / are را فراموش نکنید.
  • ❌ She is work at the moment. → ✅ She is working at the moment. فعل ing‌دار بیاورید.
  • ❌ He is runing. → ✅ He is running. حرف n دوتا می‌شود: run → running.
  • ❌ What you are doing? → ✅ What are you doing? ترتیب سؤال: are + فاعل + فعل ing‌دار.

تمرین سطح A1

1. Be quiet! The baby ___.
همین حالا در جریان است: حال استمراری، is sleeping.
2. They ___ TV right now.
منفی: aren't + فعل ing‌دار.
3. He's ___ in the sea.
حرف m دوتا می‌شود: swim → swimming.
4. Listen! Someone ___ at the door.
Listen! یعنی همین حالا: is knocking.

A2 · مقدماتی

این روزها و موقعیت‌های موقت A2

حال استمراری را برای کارهایی که این روزها در جریان‌اند (این هفته، این مدت) و موقعیت‌های موقت به کار ببرید.

لازم نیست کار دقیقاً همین ثانیه در جریان باشد؛ کافی است این روزها در جریان باشد: I'm reading a great book at the moment. (شاید همین الان نمی‌خوانمش، اما شروعش کرده‌ام و هنوز تمامش نکرده‌ام.)

برای موقعیت‌های موقت هم به کار می‌رود: My cousin is staying with us this month. Sam is working from home this week.

کلمه‌های رایج: at the moment, today, this week, this month, this year, currently.

  • I'm reading a great book at the moment. این روزها
  • My cousin is staying with us this month. موقت
  • We're learning about volcanoes in class this week. این هفته
  • Ben is learning Japanese this year. امسال
  • How is your new job going? موقعیتِ فعلی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I read a really good book at the moment. → ✅ I'm reading a really good book at the moment. کاری که این روزها در جریان است: حال استمراری.
  • ❌ My sister stays with us this month. → ✅ My sister is staying with us this month. موقعیت موقت: حال استمراری.

قرارهای آینده: I'm meeting Sam tonight A2

حال استمراری را برای برنامه‌های آینده‌ای به کار ببرید که از قبل هماهنگ شده‌اند و معمولاً زمانشان مشخص است.

حال استمراری را برای قرارهای آینده به کار ببرید: برنامه‌هایی که از قبل گذاشته‌اید، اغلب با زمان، مکان یا آدم‌های مشخص: I'm meeting Sam at 6 tonight. We're flying to Rome on Monday.

کلمه‌های رایج: tonight, tomorrow, on Friday, next week, at the weekend.

برای برنامه‌ها be going to هم درست است: I'm going to meet Sam at 6. برای جدول زمانی حال ساده بیاورید: The film starts at 8. صفحهٔ زمان آینده را ببینید.

  • I'm meeting Sam at 6 tonight. قرار
  • We're flying to Rome on Monday. بلیت رزرو شده
  • What are you doing this weekend? پرسیدن برنامه
  • She's starting her new job next week. برنامهٔ قطعی
  • They aren't coming to the party. منفی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I see the dentist tomorrow. (my appointment) → ✅ I'm seeing the dentist tomorrow. قرار شخصی: حال استمراری.
  • ❌ What do you do this weekend? → ✅ What are you doing this weekend? پرسیدن برنامه: حال استمراری.

تمرین سطح A2

5. I ___ a really interesting book at the moment.
این روزها: I'm reading.
6. My cousin ___ with us this month. She's looking for a flat.
موقعیت موقت: is staying.
7. We ___ a project about space in class this week.
this week (این روزها): are doing.
8. I ___ my grandparents on Sunday. We've booked a table at their favourite restaurant.
قرار هماهنگ‌شده: حال استمراری، I'm visiting.
9. What ___ this weekend? – Nothing special.
پرسیدن برنامه: What are you doing…?
10. کدام جمله دربارهٔ یک قرارِ هماهنگ‌شده است؟
برنامه‌ای با یک آدم مشخص و زمان مشخص: I'm having lunch with Maria tomorrow.

B1 · متوسط

موقعیت‌هایی که دارند تغییر می‌کنند: It's getting colder B1

حال استمراری را برای موقعیت‌هایی به کار ببرید که همین حالا دارند تغییر می‌کنند: get, become, increase, improve.

حال استمراری را برای تغییری که در حال اتفاق افتادن است به کار ببرید: The weather is getting colder. Prices are rising.

فعل‌هایی که اغلب این‌طور به کار می‌روند: get, become, change, improve, increase, rise, fall, grow, start, begin. ترکیب get + صفت تفضیلی خیلی رایج است: It's getting darker. Your English is getting better.

  • The weather is getting colder. get + صفت تفضیلی
  • Prices are rising again. (rise)
  • Your English is improving fast. (improve)
  • The city is growing every year. (grow)
  • I'm starting to enjoy my new job. (start to)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The population increases very fast at the moment. → ✅ The population is increasing very fast at the moment. تغییری که همین حالا در جریان است: حال استمراری.
  • ❌ It gets dark. Let's go home. → ✅ It's getting dark. Let's go home. تغییری که همین حالا در جریان است: حال استمراری.

always + استمراری: He's always complaining B1

always / constantly / forever + استمراری برای کاری که زیادی تکرار می‌شود و معمولاً آزاردهنده است.

always، constantly یا forever + حال استمراری یعنی کاری خیلی زیاد، معمولاً بیش از حد تکرار می‌شود. بیشتر برای گله و شکایت به کار می‌رود: He's always complaining. You're forever losing your keys!

مقایسه کنید: I always walk to work یعنی هر بار؛ یک عادت معمولی (بدون حس منفی).

ترتیب کلمه‌ها: be + always + فعل ing‌دار: She's always interrupting me. (نه always is interrupting)

  • He's always complaining about something. ناراحتی و گله
  • You're always losing your keys! بیش از حد
  • My neighbour is constantly playing loud music. (constantly)
  • She's forever borrowing my phone. (forever)
  • I always walk to work. عادت معمولی: حال ساده

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She always is interrupting me. → ✅ She's always interrupting me. ترتیب کلمه‌ها: be + always + فعل ing‌دار.
  • ❌ I'm always going to work by bike. (my normal habit) → ✅ I always go to work by bike. برای عادت معمولی حال ساده بیاورید.

تمرین سطح B1

تغییری در جریان: are rising.

تغییری که شروع شده: I'm starting to enjoy it.

be + always + فعل ing‌دار: he's always borrowing.

always + استمراری یعنی بیش از حد، و آزاردهنده.

C1 · پیشرفته

In progress at a regular time; always for typical behaviour C1

At 8 I'm usually driving = in the middle of it at that time; always + continuous can also describe typical (even positive) behaviour.

Use the present continuous for an action that is in progress at a regular time: At 7 a.m. I'm usually having breakfast, so call me later. Compare: I usually have breakfast at 7 = that's when I start it.

Always / constantly + continuous describes typical behaviour. It's often a complaint, but it can also be positive: My neighbour is always bringing us cakes. She's so kind.

  • At 7 a.m. I'm usually having breakfast. (in progress at that time)
  • Don't call between 6 and 7: we're usually eating then. (regular time)
  • On Saturday mornings I'm normally working in the garden. (routine in progress)
  • She's always helping other people. (positive behaviour)
  • Whenever I visit, he's watching TV. (whenever + continuous)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ At 8 I'm usually drive to work. → ✅ At 8 I'm usually driving to work. (be + verb-ing.)
  • ❌ She's always help people. → ✅ She's always helping people. (be + always + verb-ing.)

When not to use it for the future C1

Use the present continuous for arranged or decided plans, not for predictions or permanent future situations.

The present continuous for the future suggests something arranged or firmly decided: I'm not lending him my car again. So we don't use it for:

  • Predictions about things nobody can arrange, like the weather: It's going to rain tonight. / It'll rain tonight. (not It's raining tonight)
  • Permanent future situations: People will live longer in the future. (not are living longer in the future)

With go and come, many people prefer the present continuous to going to go / going to come: I'm going to town on Saturday. Are you coming home for lunch?

  • I'm going to Toronto next week. (rather than going to go)
  • Are you coming home for lunch? (come)
  • It's going to snow tonight. (prediction: not continuous)
  • Experts say the population will grow. (prediction)
  • I'm not lending him my car again. (firm decision)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It's raining tomorrow. → ✅ It's going to rain tomorrow. / It'll rain tomorrow. (The weather can't be arranged: use going to or will.)
  • ❌ In the future, people are living on other planets. → ✅ In the future, people will live on other planets. (Predictions about the future: will or going to.)

تمرین سطح C1

17. What does At 7 I'm usually having breakfast mean?
Continuous = the action is in progress at that time.
18. Which sentence describes kind, typical behaviour?
Always + continuous can describe typical behaviour, here positive.
19. Whenever I phone him, he ___.
In progress whenever I phone: he's watching TV.
20. Look at the forecast. It ___ tomorrow.
The weather can't be arranged: it's going to snow.
21. Which sentence is NOT correct?
A prediction about the future: use will or going to, not the present continuous.
22. Which sentence is a prediction, not an arrangement?
Nobody can arrange the sea level: it's a prediction (will).
