الگوهای فعل: ing یا toVerb Patterns: -ing or To?

When one verb follows another, English uses a fixed pattern: -ing (I enjoy reading), to + verb (I want to read), object + to (I want you to read) or base verb (Let me read).

This page gives the big picture. Each topic below has its own page with rules, examples and exercises at every level.

The main patterns at a glance

PatternCommon verbsExample
verb + -ingenjoy, finish, mind, avoid, keep, suggest, missI enjoy cooking.
verb + to + verbwant, need, decide, hope, plan, promise, refuseI decided to leave.
verb + object + to + verbwant, ask, tell, expect, advise, remindShe asked me to help.
verb + object + base verbmake, let, help, see, hearThey let us go.
verb + -ing or to (same meaning)like, love, hate, start, begin, continueIt started raining / to rain.
verb + -ing or to (different meaning)remember, forget, stop, try, regret, meanI stopped smoking. / I stopped to smoke.
preposition + -inggood at, interested in, before, after, withoutI'm good at swimming.

Which one do I need?

  • After a preposition (in, at, of, about, without), always -ing: Thanks for coming.
  • To say why you do something, use to: I went out to buy milk.
  • After would like / would love / would prefer, always to: I'd like to go.
  • Is something familiar to you? → be used to + -ing: I'm used to waking up early.
  • Not sure? Check the verb in a dictionary: the pattern is shown in the examples.

Gerund or Infinitive (-ing or to…?)

اسم مصدر یا مصدر با to

A1 A2 B1 B2 C1

Which verb form comes next? I enjoy swimming. I want to swim. I'm good at swimming. I saw him swim.

Verbs that Change Meaning with -ing or To

فعل‌هایی که با ing و to معنی‌شان فرق می‌کند

B1 B2 C1

Some verbs mean different things with -ing and to: I remember locking the door. Remember to lock the door!

Infinitive of Purpose (to, for, so that, in order to)

بیان هدف (to، in order to، so that)

A2 B1 B2 C1

Saying why you do something: I went to the shop to buy milk. I saved money so that I could travel.

Be Used To and Get Used To

be used to و get used to

B1 B2 C1

Talking about things that are familiar (or becoming familiar): I'm used to the cold. I'm getting used to driving on the left.
