ضمیرهای مفعولیObject Pronouns: Me, Him, Her, Us, Them

ضمیرهای مفعولی (me, you, him, her, it, us, them) بعد از فعل یا حرف اضافه می‌آیند (مثل «من را، به او، با آن‌ها»): Can you help me? I'm waiting for them.

برای I, he, she, they صفحهٔ ضمیرهای فاعلی و صفت‌های ملکی را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2

A1 · مبتدی

me, you, him, her, it, us, them بعد از فعل A1

بعد از فعل ضمیر مفعولی بیاورید: I love her. She called me. We saw them.

فاعلیمفعولی
Ime
youyou
hehim
sheher
itit
weus
theythem

فاعل کار را انجام می‌دهد؛ مفعول کار رویش انجام می‌شود: She helps me. I help her.

you و it تغییر نمی‌کنند.

  • Can you help me? (me)
  • I know him. He's my neighbour. (him)
  • Where are my keys? I can't find them. (them)
  • We love you! (you)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Can you help I? → ✅ Can you help me? بعد از فعل: me
  • ❌ I like she. → ✅ I like her. بعد از فعل: her
  • ❌ I bought a new phone, but I don't like. → ✅ I bought a new phone, but I don't like it. فعل مفعول لازم دارد: it

تمرین سطح A1

1. Do you know Reza? I see ___ every day.
مفعولِ see، یک مرد: him.
2. Where are my glasses? I can't find ___.
مفعولِ جمع: them.
3. Mina is very nice. I like ___ a lot. (one word)
مفعول، یک زن: her.

A2 · مقدماتی

ضمیر مفعولی بعد از حرف اضافه: with me, for them A2

بعد از حرف اضافه ضمیر مفعولی بیاورید: with me, for her, about him, to us, from them.

حرف اضافه + ضمیر مفعولیمثال
withDo you want to come with me?
forI bought this for you.
toCan I talk to him?
aboutWhat do you think about it?
atWhy are you looking at us?

بعد از حرف‌های اضافه (with, for, to, about, at, from, without, near, behind) ضمیر مفعولی می‌آید: Come with us! This is for you. Look at me.

بعضی فعل‌ها قبل از مفعول حرف اضافه لازم دارند: listen to me, wait for him, look at them, talk to her.

  • This present is for her. (for)
  • I often talk to them on the phone. (to)
  • Can you come with us? (with)
  • Don't worry about me. (about)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ This is for she. → ✅ This is for her. بعد از حرف اضافه: her
  • ❌ Come with we! → ✅ Come with us! بعد از حرف اضافه: us
  • ❌ Listen me! → ✅ Listen to me! listen to

تمرین سطح A2

4. These flowers are for ___. Happy birthday!
بعد از حرف اضافه: you.
5. Do you want to come to the cinema with ___?
بعد از حرف اضافه: us.
6. Wait ___ me! I'm coming. (one word)
wait for + مفعول.

B1 · متوسط

me too، it's me و my friend and I B1

در جواب کوتاه و بعد از be، در انگلیسی روزمره ضمیر مفعولی می‌آید: Me too! Who's there? ~ It's me. وقتی فاعل دو نفر است، شکل فاعلی بیاورید و خودتان را آخر بگذارید: My friend and I went to the cinema.

موقعیتانگلیسی روزمرهتوضیح
جواب کوتاهI'm hungry. ~ Me too. Who wants tea? ~ Me!I do / I am هم ممکن است
بعد از beWho's there? ~ It's me.It is I خیلی رسمی و قدیمی است
دو فاعلMy friend and I went out.Me and my friend went out خیلی خودمانی است
دو مفعولShe invited my friend and me.بعد از فعل شکل مفعولی

برای امتحان کردنِ ضمیر، نفر دیگر را حذف کنید: My friend and I went out ← I went out. She invited my friend and me ← She invited me.

Me and my friend went… در گفتار خودمانی رایج است، اما در نوشتن و امتحان غلط حساب می‌شود.

  • I love pizza. ~ Me too! جواب کوتاه
  • Who broke the window? ~ Not me! جواب کوتاه
  • Ali and I are best friends. دو فاعل
  • Our teacher gave Ali and me extra homework. دو مفعول

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Me and Sara are going to the cinema. → ✅ Sara and I are going to the cinema. فاعل: I، و خودتان را آخر بگذارید
  • ❌ She invited Ali and I. → ✅ She invited Ali and me. مفعول: me
  • ❌ I like this song. ~ I too. → ✅ I like this song. ~ Me too. جواب کوتاه: me too

تمرین سطح B1

7. In careful English: My brother and ___ are going to Spain next week.
فاعل: my brother and I (my brother and me در گفتار خودمانی رایج است، اما در انگلیسیِ دقیق نه).
8. The teacher asked Sara and ___ to stay after class.
مفعول: Sara and me.
9. I'm so tired. ~ ___ too! I didn't sleep well. (one word)
جواب کوتاه: Me too.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Than me or than I am? Between you and me B2

After than and as, use an object pronoun in everyday English (She's taller than me) or subject + verb in careful English (She's taller than I am). After prepositions like between and except, always use the object form: between you and me.

PatternEverydayCareful
than / as + pronounHe's older than me. She's as tall as him.He's older than I am. She's as tall as he is.
betweenbetween you and me(not between you and I)
except / butEveryone came except him.
likeShe looks like me.

Than I without a verb (He's older than I) is very formal and sounds old-fashioned. Use than me or than I am.

Between you and me = this is a secret. Between you and I is a common mistake.

  • My sister is younger than me. (everyday)
  • My sister is younger than I am. (careful)
  • Between you and me, I don't like the new boss. (between)
  • Everyone passed except her. (except)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Between you and I, he's leaving the company. → ✅ Between you and me, he's leaving the company. (after between: me)
  • ❌ She's taller than me am. → ✅ She's taller than I am. (than + subject + verb)
  • ❌ Nobody except he knew the answer. → ✅ Nobody except him knew the answer. (except + object pronoun)

تمرین سطح B2

10. ___, I think the plan is a bad idea.
After between: object pronouns.
11. My brother is much taller than ___.
Everyday English: than me.
12. She speaks English better than I ___. (one word)
Careful English: than I do.
