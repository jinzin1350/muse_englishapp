Object Pronouns (me, him, us...)

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

Object pronouns replace the object of a sentence, the person or thing that receives the action: I love you. Call me later. I know them.

We use them after verbs and after prepositions.

Subject and object pronouns

SubjectObjectExample
ImeCan you help me?
youyouI'll call you tomorrow.
hehimI like him.
sheherI saw her yesterday.
ititWhere's my phone? I can't find it.
weusCome with us!
theythemAsk them.

After prepositions

After prepositions, use object pronouns: with me, for him, to her, about us, from them. This present is for you. Are you talking to me?

Subject or object?

Use the subject pronoun before the verb (the person who does the action) and the object pronoun after the verb or a preposition: She called me. I called her.

After be, everyday English uses object pronouns: Who's there? – It's me. (It is I is very formal.)

With and: Tom and I went out (subject), but She invited Tom and me (object). To check, take away Tom and: She invited me.

Examples

  1. Can you help me, please? (after a verb)
  2. I don't know him. (he → him)
  3. We met her at the party. (she → her)
  4. Where are my keys? I can't find them. (plural things → them)
  5. This cake is for you. (after a preposition)
  6. Our grandparents often visit us. (we → us)
  7. I bought a new phone. I love it! (a thing → it)
  8. Please don't tell them. (they → them)
  9. Do you want to come with us? (after with)
  10. My parents invited Sam and me to dinner. (and + object pronoun)

Common mistakes

❌ Can you help I?

✅ Can you help me? (After a verb, use the object pronoun.)

❌ I saw she yesterday.

✅ I saw her yesterday. (After a verb, use the object pronoun.)

❌ This is for they.

✅ This is for them. (After a preposition, use the object pronoun.)

❌ Me and Tom went to the cinema.

✅ Tom and I went to the cinema. (Before the verb (subject), use I. You'll hear me and Tom in casual speech, but not in writing.)

❌ She invited Tom and I.

✅ She invited Tom and me. (After the verb (object), use me.)

❌ I like it the film.

✅ I like the film. / I like it. (Use the noun or the pronoun, not both.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. Can you help ___?
After a verb: object pronoun me.
2. Where's my phone? I can't find ___.
One thing: it.
3. I saw ___ at the supermarket yesterday. She said hello.
After a verb: she → her.
4. These flowers are for ___. Happy birthday, Mum!
After a preposition: you.
5. Do you want to come with ___?
After a preposition: we → us.
6. My grandparents live far away, so we don't see ___ often.
After a verb: they → them.
7. Who's at the door? – It's ___!
After it's in everyday English: me.
8. I like Tom. I'm going to call ___.
After a verb: he → him.
9. Choose the correct sentence.
Before the verb (subject): He and I.
10. She invited Tom and ___ to her wedding.
After the verb (object): Tom and me. Check: She invited me.
11. I bought some apples and ate ___ all.
Plural things after a verb: them.
12. Are you talking to ___? – Yes, I'm talking to you.
After a preposition: me.
13. Lisa is my best friend. I tell ___ everything.
After a verb: she → her.
14. Our teacher gave ___ a lot of homework.
After a verb: we → us.
15. I don't know these people. Do you know ___?
After a verb: they → them.
16. ___ called me last night.
Before the verb (subject): He.
