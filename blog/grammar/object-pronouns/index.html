Object Pronouns: Me, Him, Her, Us, Them

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2

Object pronouns (me, you, him, her, it, us, them) come after a verb or a preposition: Can you help me? I'm waiting for them.

For I, he, she, they, see Subject Pronouns and Possessive Adjectives.

A1 · Beginner

Me, you, him, her, it, us, them after a verb A1

SubjectObject
Ime
youyou
hehim
sheher
itit
weus
theythem

  • Can you help me? (me)
  • I know him. He's my neighbour. (him)
  • Where are my keys? I can't find them. (them)
  • We love you! (you)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Can you help I? → ✅ Can you help me? (after a verb: me)
  • ❌ I like she. → ✅ I like her. (after a verb: her)
  • ❌ I bought a new phone, but I don't like. → ✅ I bought a new phone, but I don't like it. (the verb needs an object: it)

Practice: A1

1. Do you know Reza? I see <span class="gap">___</span> every day.
Object of see, a man: him.
2. Where are my glasses? I can't find <span class="gap">___</span>.
Plural object: them.
3. Mina is very nice. I like <span class="gap">___</span> a lot. (one word)
Object, a woman: her.

A2 · Elementary

Object pronouns after prepositions: with me, for them A2

Preposition + object pronounExample
withDo you want to come with me?
forI bought this for you.
toCan I talk to him?
aboutWhat do you think about it?
atWhy are you looking at us?

  • This present is for her. (for)
  • I often talk to them on the phone. (to)
  • Can you come with us? (with)
  • Don't worry about me. (about)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ This is for she. → ✅ This is for her. (after a preposition: her)
  • ❌ Come with we! → ✅ Come with us! (after a preposition: us)
  • ❌ Listen me! → ✅ Listen to me! (listen to)

Practice: A2

4. These flowers are for <span class="gap">___</span>. Happy birthday!
After a preposition: you.
5. Do you want to come to the cinema with <span class="gap">___</span>?
After a preposition: us.
6. Wait <span class="gap">___</span> me! I'm coming. (one word)
Wait for + object.

B1 · Intermediate

Me too, it's me, my friend and I B1

SituationEveryday EnglishNote
short answersI'm hungry. ~ Me too. Who wants tea? ~ Me!I do / I am is also possible
after beWho's there? ~ It's me.It is I = very formal, old-fashioned
two subjectsMy friend and I went out.Me and my friend went out is very informal
two objectsShe invited my friend and me.object form after the verb

  • I love pizza. ~ Me too! (short answer)
  • Who broke the window? ~ Not me! (short answer)
  • Ali and I are best friends. (two subjects)
  • Our teacher gave Ali and me extra homework. (two objects)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Me and Sara are going to the cinema. → ✅ Sara and I are going to the cinema. (subject: I, and put yourself last)
  • ❌ She invited Ali and I. → ✅ She invited Ali and me. (object: me)
  • ❌ I like this song. ~ I too. → ✅ I like this song. ~ Me too. (short answer: me too)

Practice: B1

7. In careful English: My brother and <span class="gap">___</span> are going to Spain next week.
Subject: my brother and I (my brother and me is common in casual speech, but not in careful English).
8. The teacher asked Sara and <span class="gap">___</span> to stay after class.
Object: Sara and me.
9. I'm so tired. ~ <span class="gap">___</span> too! I didn't sleep well. (one word)
Short answer: Me too.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Than me or than I am? Between you and me B2

PatternEverydayCareful
than / as + pronounHe's older than me. She's as tall as him.He's older than I am. She's as tall as he is.
betweenbetween you and me(not between you and I)
except / butEveryone came except him.
likeShe looks like me.

  • My sister is younger than me. (everyday)
  • My sister is younger than I am. (careful)
  • Between you and me, I don't like the new boss. (between)
  • Everyone passed except her. (except)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Between you and I, he's leaving the company. → ✅ Between you and me, he's leaving the company. (after between: me)
  • ❌ She's taller than me am. → ✅ She's taller than I am. (than + subject + verb)
  • ❌ Nobody except he knew the answer. → ✅ Nobody except him knew the answer. (except + object pronoun)

Practice: B2

10. <span class="gap">___</span>, I think the plan is a bad idea.
After between: object pronouns.
11. My brother is much taller than <span class="gap">___</span>.
Everyday English: than me.
12. She speaks English better than I <span class="gap">___</span>. (one word)
Careful English: than I do.
