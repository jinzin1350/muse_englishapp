for، since و agoFor, Since and Ago

for و since هر دو به سؤال How long? («چه مدت؟») جواب می‌دهند، معمولاً با حال کامل: I've lived here for five years. I've lived here since 2020. ago می‌گوید چیزی کِی اتفاق افتاد: I moved here five years ago.

این صفحه از فرق پایه شروع می‌شود و به الگوهای کاربردی مثل It's two years since… و over the last few years می‌رسد.

A2 · مقدماتی

for + مدت، since + نقطهٔ شروع A2

for + مدت زمان (for three hours, for ten years)؛ since + زمانی که شروع شد (since 9 am, since Monday, since 2019).

for + مدتsince + نقطهٔ شروع
for ten minutessince 10 o'clock
for three dayssince Monday
for two weekssince 5 March
for six monthssince January
for a long timesince 2015
for agessince I was a child

با حال کامل، هر دو موقعیتی را توصیف می‌کنند که در گذشته شروع شده و هنوز ادامه دارد: She's worked here for two years / since 2023.

برای انتخاب بپرسید: آیا مقدار زمان است (چقدر وقت)؟ ← for. آیا یک نقطه روی تقویم یا ساعت است؟ ← since.

  • I've known Maryam for fifteen years. مدت
  • I've known Maryam since we were at school. نقطهٔ شروع
  • We've been here since 8 o'clock. نقطه‌ای روی ساعت
  • They've been married for a long time. مدت

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've lived here since three years. → ✅ I've lived here for three years. three years مدت است: for
  • ❌ She's been ill for Monday. → ✅ She's been ill since Monday. Monday نقطهٔ شروع است: since
  • ❌ I live here for five years. → ✅ I've lived here for five years. تا حالا: حال کامل.

ago، for یا since؟ A2

ago یعنی چه مدت قبل از حالا چیزی اتفاق افتاد (با گذشتهٔ ساده): I came two years ago. for / since یعنی موقعیتی چه مدت ادامه داشته (با حال کامل).

سؤالجوابزمان فعل
When did you come here?Two years ago.گذشتهٔ ساده
How long have you been here?For two years. / Since 2023.حال کامل

ago بعد از مقدار زمان می‌آید: three days ago, a long time ago, ten minutes ago. با گذشتهٔ ساده به کار می‌رود.

این سه جمله می‌توانند یک موقعیت را توصیف کنند: I met him five years ago. I've known him for five years. I've known him since 2020.

ago را با حال کامل نیاورید: I came here two years ago یا I've been here for two years. نه I've been here two years ago.

  • The film started twenty minutes ago. کِی: گذشتهٔ ساده
  • The film has been on for twenty minutes. چه مدت: حال کامل
  • We moved here in 2021. We've lived here since 2021. in + سال / since + سال

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've started this job three months ago. → ✅ I started this job three months ago. ago ← گذشتهٔ ساده
  • ❌ I came here before two years. → ✅ I came here two years ago. ago بعد از مقدار زمان

تمرین سطح A2

1. I've had this car ___ 2018.
2018 نقطهٔ شروع است: since.
2. We've been waiting ___ forty minutes!
forty minutes مدت است: for.
3. He's worked at the hospital ___ ten years.
مدت: for.
4. My sister got married six months ___.
کِی اتفاق افتاد: ago + گذشتهٔ ساده.
5. I ___ him since we were children.
since + موقعیتی تا حالا: حال کامل.
6. Dinner was ready an hour ___. It's cold now!
چه مدت قبل از حالا: ago.

B1 · متوسط

How long have you…? یا When did you…? B1

How long have you been here? (تا حالا) در برابر When did you arrive? (نقطه‌ای در گذشته). برای «چه مدت تا حالا» حال ساده نیاورید.

برای پرسیدن مدت یک موقعیت تا حالا، How long + حال کامل (ساده یا استمراری) بیاورید:

  • How long have you known each other?
  • How long have you been learning English?

برای پرسیدن نقطه‌ای در گذشته، When / What time + گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید: When did you meet?

یک اشتباه خیلی رایج استفاده از حال ساده است: How long are you here? یعنی «چقدر می‌مانی؟» (دربارهٔ آینده)، نه «چه مدت است اینجایی؟».

فعل‌های حالتی (know, have, be, like) با حال کاملِ ساده می‌آیند: How long have you had this phone? برای فعالیت‌ها اغلب شکل استمراری می‌آید: How long have you been waiting? صفحهٔ حال کامل استمراری را ببینید.

  • How long have you lived in Toronto? ~ For six years. مدت تا حالا
  • When did you move to Toronto? ~ In 2019. نقطه‌ای در گذشته
  • How long have they been married? فعل حالتی: ساده
  • How long are you staying in Paris? ~ Until Friday. دربارهٔ آینده

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ How long do you know him? → ✅ How long have you known him? تا حالا: حال کامل.
  • ❌ How long are you married? → ✅ How long have you been married? تا حالا: حال کامل.
  • ❌ When have you arrived? → ✅ When did you arrive? نقطه‌ای در گذشته: گذشتهٔ ساده.

It's two years since… و I haven't… for… B1

سه راه برای گفتن یک چیز: It's two years since I saw her. I haven't seen her for two years. The last time I saw her was two years ago.

این الگوها برای گفتن اینکه از اتفاقی چقدر گذشته به کار می‌آیند:

الگومثال
It's / It has been + مدت + since + گذشتهٔ سادهIt's two years since I saw her.
haven't + اسم مفعول + for + مدتI haven't seen her for two years.
The last time + گذشتهٔ ساده + was + ago / زمانThe last time I saw her was two years ago.
I last + گذشتهٔ ساده + agoI last saw her two years ago.

بعد از It's … since انتخاب معمول گذشتهٔ ساده است: It's ages since we went out.

در انگلیسی آمریکایی بعد از جملهٔ منفی in رایج است: I haven't seen her in two years. اینجا هم for درست است و هم in.

  • It's five years since they moved to Canada. it's + مدت + since
  • We haven't been to the cinema for months. منفی + for
  • The last time I ate meat was in 2020. the last time
  • I last saw him at your wedding. (I last…)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It's three years since I visit my aunt. → ✅ It's three years since I visited my aunt. بعد از it's … since: گذشتهٔ ساده.
  • ❌ I didn't see him for a long time. (and I still haven't) → ✅ I haven't seen him for a long time. تا حالا: حال کامل.

تمرین سطح B1

7. ___ have you had your dog?
How long + حال کامل.
8. How long ___ in this company?
مدت تا حالا: حال کامل.
9. How long ___ (you / know) your best friend?
فعل حالتی، تا حالا: have you known.
10. It's a long time since we ___.
It's … since + گذشتهٔ ساده.
11. جملهٔ I haven't been to the gym for a month. با کدام هم‌معنی است؟
It's a month since + گذشتهٔ ساده.
12. The last time I ___ (drive) a car was ten years ago.
The last time + گذشتهٔ ساده.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Since + a clause, ever since, and since meaning because B2

since + past simple clause for the starting point: since I left school. ever since = all the time from then until now. since can also mean because.

Since can be followed by a clause (subject + verb). The clause usually uses the past simple, because it refers to a moment in the past: I've lived here since I was ten. A lot has changed since we last met.

When the since clause describes a situation that continues now, the present perfect is possible: I've felt much better since I've been doing yoga (I'm still doing it).

Ever since emphasises all the time from then until now: We've been friends ever since we met at university. It can also go at the end: We met at university and we've been friends ever since.

Since can also mean because (a reason that both people know): Since it's raining, let's stay in.

Don't confuse since and for + a clause: I've worked here since I graduated. Not for I graduated.

  • She's been much happier since she changed jobs. (since + past simple)
  • Ever since I was a child, I've loved music. (ever since)
  • He hasn't called since he moved to Dubai. (starting point)
  • Since you're here, can you help me with this? (since = because)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've lived here since I am a child. → ✅ I've lived here since I was a child. (since + past simple for the starting point)
  • ❌ She has been sad for her cat died. → ✅ She has been sad since her cat died. (since + clause)

تمرین سطح B2

13. Things have changed a lot since I ___ this job.
The starting point: past simple.
14. We met in 2010 and we've been close friends ___.
At the end of the sentence: ever since.
15. How much weight have you lost since you ___ (start) running?
Since + past simple for the starting point.

C1 · پیشرفته

Over the last few years, in years, during, throughout C1

over / in the last + period with the present perfect for a period up to now; in years / for ages after negatives; during / throughout for a period.

To describe a period up to now, these phrases are very common with the present perfect:

  • Over the last / past ten years, the population has doubled.
  • In the last few months, we've had three new clients.
  • Recently / Lately / So far …

With a negative, we can use for or in (more common in American English): I haven't been to a concert for / in years. Not … for ages is informal.

During + noun = at some time or all the time in a period: I woke up twice during the night. Throughout = from the beginning to the end: It rained throughout the match. For says how long, not when: It rained for two hours.

Don't use during for how long: I stayed there for two weeks. Not during two weeks.

  • Over the past decade, smartphones have changed the way we live. (period up to now)
  • Our sales have risen by 15% in the last quarter. (in the last)
  • I haven't heard that song in years. (in years after a negative)
  • He was in hospital for a week, and during that time he read five books. (for = how long; during = in that period)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I stayed in Rome during three weeks. → ✅ I stayed in Rome for three weeks. (How long: for)
  • ❌ During two years I lived in Paris. → ✅ For two years I lived in Paris. (How long: for, not during.)

تمرین سطح C1

16. ___ the last five years, the company has opened twenty new shops.
Over the last… + present perfect.
17. We lived in Ahvaz ___ six years before moving to Tehran.
How long: for.
18. The baby cried twice ___ the night.
At some times in the period: during.

همچنین ببینید:
