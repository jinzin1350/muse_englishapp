For, Since and Ago

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

For and since both answer the question How long?, usually with the present perfect: I've lived here for five years. I've lived here since 2020. Ago tells us when something happened: I moved here five years ago.

This page goes from the basic difference to useful patterns like It's two years since… and over the last few years.

A2 · Elementary

For + a period, since + a starting point A2

for + a periodsince + a starting point
for ten minutessince 10 o'clock
for three dayssince Monday
for two weekssince 5 March
for six monthssince January
for a long timesince 2015
for agessince I was a child

  • I've known Maryam for fifteen years. (a period)
  • I've known Maryam since we were at school. (a starting point)
  • We've been here since 8 o'clock. (a point on the clock)
  • They've been married for a long time. (a period)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I've lived here since three years. → ✅ I've lived here for three years. (three years is a period: for)
  • ❌ She's been ill for Monday. → ✅ She's been ill since Monday. (Monday is a starting point: since)
  • ❌ I live here for five years. → ✅ I've lived here for five years. (Up to now: present perfect.)

Ago, for or since? A2

QuestionAnswerTense
When did you come here?Two years ago.past simple
How long have you been here?For two years. / Since 2023.present perfect

  • The film started twenty minutes ago. (when: past simple)
  • The film has been on for twenty minutes. (how long: present perfect)
  • We moved here in 2021. We've lived here since 2021. (in + year / since + year)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I've started this job three months ago. → ✅ I started this job three months ago. (ago → past simple)
  • ❌ I came here before two years. → ✅ I came here two years ago. (ago after the time)

Practice: A2

1. I've had this car ___ 2018.
2018 is a starting point: since.
2. We've been waiting ___ forty minutes!
Forty minutes is a period: for.
3. He's worked at the hospital ___ ten years.
A period: for.
4. My sister got married six months ___.
When it happened: ago + past simple.
5. I ___ him since we were children.
Since + a situation up to now: present perfect.
6. Dinner was ready an hour ___. It's cold now!
How long before now: ago.

B1 · Intermediate

How long have you…? or When did you…? B1

  • How long have you known each other?
  • How long have you been learning English?

  • How long have you lived in Toronto? ~ For six years. (length up to now)
  • When did you move to Toronto? ~ In 2019. (point in the past)
  • How long have they been married? (state verb: simple)
  • How long are you staying in Paris? ~ Until Friday. (about the future)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ How long do you know him? → ✅ How long have you known him? (Up to now: present perfect.)
  • ❌ How long are you married? → ✅ How long have you been married? (Up to now: present perfect.)
  • ❌ When have you arrived? → ✅ When did you arrive? (A point in the past: past simple.)

It's two years since… and I haven't… for… B1

PatternExample
It's / It has been + period + since + past simpleIt's two years since I saw her.
haven't + participle + for + periodI haven't seen her for two years.
The last time + past simple + was + ago / timeThe last time I saw her was two years ago.
I last + past simple + agoI last saw her two years ago.

  • It's five years since they moved to Canada. (it's + period + since)
  • We haven't been to the cinema for months. (negative + for)
  • The last time I ate meat was in 2020. (the last time)
  • I last saw him at your wedding. (I last…)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ It's three years since I visit my aunt. → ✅ It's three years since I visited my aunt. (After it's … since: past simple.)
  • ❌ I didn't see him for a long time. (and I still haven't) → ✅ I haven't seen him for a long time. (Up to now: present perfect.)

Practice: B1

7. ___ have you had your dog?
How long + present perfect.
8. How long ___ in this company?
Length up to now: present perfect.
9. How long ___ (you / know) your best friend?
State verb, up to now: have you known.
10. It's a long time since we ___.
It's … since + past simple.
11. I haven't been to the gym for a month. Which means the same?
It's a month since + past simple.
12. The last time I ___ (drive) a car was ten years ago.
The last time + past simple.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Since + a clause, ever since, and since meaning because B2

  • She's been much happier since she changed jobs. (since + past simple)
  • Ever since I was a child, I've loved music. (ever since)
  • He hasn't called since he moved to Dubai. (starting point)
  • Since you're here, can you help me with this? (since = because)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I've lived here since I am a child. → ✅ I've lived here since I was a child. (since + past simple for the starting point)
  • ❌ She has been sad for her cat died. → ✅ She has been sad since her cat died. (since + clause)

Practice: B2

13. Things have changed a lot since I ___ this job.
The starting point: past simple.
14. We met in 2010 and we've been close friends ___.
At the end of the sentence: ever since.
15. How much weight have you lost since you ___ (start) running?
Since + past simple for the starting point.

C1 · Advanced

Over the last few years, in years, during, throughout C1

  • Over the last / past ten years, the population has doubled.
  • In the last few months, we've had three new clients.
  • Recently / Lately / So far …

  • Over the past decade, smartphones have changed the way we live. (period up to now)
  • Our sales have risen by 15% in the last quarter. (in the last)
  • I haven't heard that song in years. (in years after a negative)
  • He was in hospital for a week, and during that time he read five books. (for = how long; during = in that period)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I stayed in Rome during three weeks. → ✅ I stayed in Rome for three weeks. (How long: for)
  • ❌ During two years I lived in Paris. → ✅ For two years I lived in Paris. (How long: for, not during.)

Practice: C1

16. ___ the last five years, the company has opened twenty new shops.
Over the last… + present perfect.
17. We lived in Ahvaz ___ six years before moving to Tehran.
How long: for.
18. The baby cried twice ___ the night.
At some times in the period: during.
