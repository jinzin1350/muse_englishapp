Be Going To (I'm going to…)

A1 · A2 · B1 · C1

We use be going to + base verb for plans we have already decided and for predictions based on what we can see now: I'm going to learn Spanish. Look out! You're going to fall!

To compare it with will, see Will vs Going To. For arrangements with other people, see Present Continuous.

A1 · Beginner

I'm going to…: plans and intentions A1

Subjectbegoing tobase verb
Iam ('m)going toI'm going to study tonight.
he / she / itis ('s)going toShe's going to visit Paris.
you / we / theyare ('re)going toWe're going to have a party.

  • I'm going to start a new job next month. (a plan)
  • My parents are going to visit us in July. (they've decided)
  • He's going to sell his car. (intention)
  • We're going to watch a film tonight. (a plan)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I going to see my friends. → ✅ I'm going to see my friends. (be + going to)
  • ❌ She's going to visits her mum. → ✅ She's going to visit her mum. (going to + base verb)
  • ❌ They are going see a film. → ✅ They are going to see a film. (going + to)

Not going to and Are you going to…? A1

FormExample
Negativesubject + be + not + going to + verbI'm not going to go out tonight.
Questionbe + subject + going to + verb?Are you going to call him?
Wh-questionquestion word + be + subject + going to + verb?What are you going to do?
Short answersYes, … am / is / are. No, … 'm not / isn't / aren't.Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

  • I'm not going to buy that dress. It's too expensive. (negative)
  • Are you going to watch the match? ~ Yes, I am. (question + short answer)
  • What are you going to wear to the wedding? (wh-question)
  • They aren't going to sell the house. (negative)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I don't going to come. → ✅ I'm not going to come. (negative: be + not)
  • ❌ What you are going to do? → ✅ What are you going to do? (be before the subject)

Practice: A1

1. I ___ going to visit my grandparents on Sunday.
I → am going to.
2. My sister ___ going to have a baby in May.
She → is going to.
3. We ___ (go) to the beach this weekend. We've already booked the hotel.
A plan: are going to go.
4. ___ you going to come to the party?
Question: Are you going to…?
5. Where ___ going to stay in London?
Where are you going to…?
6. She ___ (not / go) to the meeting. She's ill.
Negative: isn't going to.

A2 · Elementary

Predictions from evidence: It's going to rain A2

  • Look at the sky. It's going to snow. (evidence: the sky)
  • Watch out! That glass is going to fall! (it's about to happen)
  • We're stuck in traffic. We're going to be late. (evidence: the traffic)
  • The score is 4–0. They're going to win. (evidence: the score)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Look at those clouds! It rains. → ✅ Look at those clouds! It's going to rain. (prediction from evidence: going to)

Practice: A2

7. Look at that man on the ladder! He ___ fall!
Evidence now: is going to.
8. It's 8:55 and the meeting starts at 9. We ___ be late!
Evidence now: are going to.
9. Look at those dark clouds. It ___ (rain).
Evidence: is going to rain.

B1 · Intermediate

Going to or present continuous for plans? B1

be going to (intention)present continuous (arrangement)
I'm going to see a doctor. (I've decided to.)I'm seeing the doctor at 4. (I have an appointment.)
We're going to get married. (We've decided.)We're getting married on June 5th. (It's organised.)

  • I'm going to learn to play the guitar. (intention)
  • I'm meeting Sara for lunch on Friday. (arrangement)
  • They're flying to Rome tomorrow morning. (booked)
  • Someday I'm going to write a book. (intention, no arrangement)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ One day I'm writing a novel. → ✅ One day I'm going to write a novel. (an intention without arrangements: going to)

Was going to: plans that changed B1

  • I was going to cook, but we ordered a pizza. (plan changed)
  • They were going to get married in June, but they postponed it. (didn't happen)
  • Sorry, I was going to tell you, but I didn't have time. (excuse)
  • I had a feeling it was going to be a great day. (past prediction)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I was go to call you. → ✅ I was going to call you. (was going to + base verb)
  • ❌ We are going to visit you last week. → ✅ We were going to visit you last week. (past: were going to)

Practice: B1

10. I've booked a table. We ___ dinner at Luigi's at 8.
A fixed arrangement: present continuous.
11. I've decided. I ___ eating sugar.
An intention: going to.
12. My sister ___ (get) married next Saturday. All the guests have been invited.
A fixed arrangement: present continuous (going to is also possible).
13. I ___ going to email you, but my laptop broke.
A past plan: was going to.
14. What does We were going to buy that house mean?
Were going to = a plan that didn't happen.
15. She ___ (going to / go) to the party, but she felt ill.
A plan that didn't happen: was going to go.

C1 · Advanced

Going to: imminent events, if + going to, and in formal English C1

  • Oh no, I think I'm going to cry! (about to happen)
  • If you're going to be late, please call me. (if you intend to / if that's the situation)
  • If we're going to catch the train, we need to leave now. (if that's the plan)
  • The new bridge will open next year. (formal style: will)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ If you will be late, call me. → ✅ If you're going to be late, call me. (if + going to / present (not will) for this meaning)

Practice: C1

16. ___ to study abroad, you should start learning the language now.
If you're going to… = if that's your plan.
17. In a formal company report, which is most typical?
Formal style prefers will.
18. If you ___ (going to / borrow) my car, please fill up the tank.
If + going to = if you intend to.
