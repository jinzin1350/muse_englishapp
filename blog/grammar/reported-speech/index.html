نقل قول غیرمستقیمReported Speech (She said that…)

نقل قول غیرمستقیم (reported speech) یعنی به کسی بگوییم شخص دیگری چه گفته، بدون اینکه عین کلماتش را تکرار کنیم: “I'm tired.” → She said (that) she was tired.

تغییرهای اصلی در زمان فعل، ضمیرها و کلمه‌های زمان است. برای فعل‌های نقل قولِ دیگر (promise, suggest, warn) صفحهٔ فعل‌های نقل قول را ببینید.

A2 · B1 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

say یا tell؟ She said / She told me A2

say + (that) + حرف: She said (that) she was hungry. tell + شخص + (that): She told me (that) she was hungry.

saytell
She said (that) she was tired.She told me (that) she was tired.
He said hello.He told us a story.
She said to me that… (ممکن، اما کمتر رایج)She told that… ✗ (شخص لازم دارد)

بعد از tell باید شخص بیاید: tell me, tell him, tell the class. say شخص نمی‌خواهد: say something. اگر بعد از say شخص می‌خواهید، to بیاورید: He said to me…

that بعد از هر دو اختیاری است: She said (that) she was busy.

عبارت‌های ثابت با tell: tell the truth, tell a lie, tell a story, tell a joke, tell the time. با say: say hello, say goodbye, say sorry, say something / nothing.

  • Tom said he was hungry. say + حرف
  • Tom told me he was hungry. tell + شخص
  • My mum told us a funny story. tell + قصه
  • She said goodbye and left. say + goodbye

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He told that he was tired. → ✅ He said that he was tired. / He told me that he was tired. tell شخص لازم دارد.
  • ❌ She said me the answer. → ✅ She told me the answer. با شخص: tell. (فارسی می‌گوید «به من گفت»، اما said me غلط است.)
  • ❌ Can you say me the time? → ✅ Can you tell me the time? tell the time.

تمرین سطح A2

1. Anna ___ me she was moving to Canada.
Tell + شخص.
2. He ___ that he was too busy to come.
Say + (that): بعدش شخص نمی‌آید.
3. Don't ___ anyone my secret! (say / tell)
Tell + شخص.

B1 · متوسط

تغییر زمان: یک قدم به عقب B1

وقتی فعل نقل قول گذشته است (said, told)، زمان فعل معمولاً یک قدم به عقب می‌رود: “I'm tired” → She said she was tired. “I'll call” → He said he would call. (در فارسی زمان عوض نمی‌شود: «گفت خسته است».)

نقل قول مستقیمنقل قول غیرمستقیم
حال ساده: “I work here.”گذشتهٔ ساده: He said he worked there.
حال استمراری: “I'm leaving.”گذشتهٔ استمراری: She said she was leaving.
حال کامل: “I've finished.”گذشتهٔ کامل: He said he had finished.
گذشتهٔ ساده: “I lost it.”گذشتهٔ کامل: She said she had lost it. (یا lost)
will: “I'll help.”would: He said he would help.
can: “I can swim.”could: She said she could swim.
am going to: “I'm going to move.”was going to: He said he was going to move.

ضمیرها هم عوض می‌شوند تا با گویندهٔ جدید جور باشند: “I love my job.” → She said she loved her job.

وقتی زمان روشن است، گذشتهٔ ساده در نقل قول غیرمستقیم می‌تواند تغییر نکند: She said she lost / had lost her keys yesterday.

  • “I live in Toronto.” → He said he lived in Toronto. حال ← گذشته
  • “We're having dinner.” → They said they were having dinner. استمراری
  • “I've seen that film.” → She said she had seen that film. حال کامل ← گذشتهٔ کامل
  • “I'll be late.” → He told me he would be late. will ← would

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She said she is tired. (she isn't tired now) → ✅ She said she was tired. یک قدم به عقب: was (او الان خسته نیست).
  • ❌ He said he will come. (the time is past) → ✅ He said he would come. will ← would (آن زمان گذشته است).

وقتی زمان عوض نمی‌شود B1

وقتی فعل نقل قول حال است (She says she's tired) زمان را عوض نکنید؛ و وقتی موقعیت هنوز درست است عوض کردنش اختیاری است: He said he lives in Paris.

اگر فعل نقل قول حال باشد (says, tells, thinks)، زمان عوض نمی‌شود: My doctor says I need more rest.

اگر حرفی که زده شده هنوز درست است، می‌توانید زمان اصلی را نگه دارید: “I'm a vegetarian.” → She said she is a vegetarian. (هنوز گیاه‌خوار است.) اینجا هم is درست است هم was.

حقیقت‌های کلی معمولاً حال می‌مانند: The teacher said that water boils at 100°C.

اگر شک دارید، زمان را یک قدم به عقب ببرید. بعد از فعل نقل قولِ گذشته، اگر آن حرف در آن زمان درست بوده، این کار همیشه درست است.

  • The forecast says it's going to rain. فعل نقل قولِ حال
  • She said she has two children. هنوز درست است
  • He told me the Earth goes round the Sun. حقیقت کلی
  • Mina says she'll be late. فعل نقل قولِ حال

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He says he was tired now. → ✅ He says he's tired now. فعل نقل قولِ حال: زمان عوض نمی‌شود.

کلمه‌های زمان و مکان: tomorrow → the next day B1

وقتی زمان یا مکان عوض شده، کلمه‌هایی مثل now, today, tomorrow, here, this هم عوض می‌شوند: “I'll see you tomorrow.” → He said he'd see me the next day.

مستقیمغیرمستقیم
nowthen / at that time
today / tonightthat day / that night
yesterdaythe day before / the previous day
tomorrowthe next day / the following day
last weekthe week before / the previous week
next weekthe following week
agobefore / earlier
herethere
thisthat

این کلمه‌ها را فقط وقتی عوض کنید که موقع نقل کردن، زمان یا مکان فرق کرده باشد. اگر همان روز نقل می‌کنید، “I'll call you tomorrow” می‌تواند بماند: He said he'd call me tomorrow.

  • “I'm busy today.” → She said she was busy that day. today ← that day
  • “We arrived yesterday.” → They said they had arrived the day before. yesterday ← the day before
  • “I'll do it tomorrow.” → He said he'd do it the next day. tomorrow ← the next day
  • “I like it here.” → She said she liked it there. here ← there

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Last year she told me she was moving tomorrow. → ✅ Last year she told me she was moving the next day. بعداً نقل شده: the next day.

نقل سؤال: He asked me where I lived B1

در نقل سؤال، ترتیب جملهٔ خبری می‌آید و do / did نمی‌آید: “Where do you live?” → He asked me where I lived. برای سؤال‌های بله/نه if / whether بیاورید.

سؤال مستقیمسؤال نقل‌شده
“Where do you live?”He asked me where I lived.
“What are you doing?”She asked what I was doing.
“Are you married?”He asked if / whether I was married.
“Can you help me?”She asked if I could help her.

در نقل سؤال: ترتیبِ سؤالی نه، do / does / did نه، علامت سؤال نه: She asked where the station was. نه She asked where was the station?

زمان فعل مثل جمله‌های خبری یک قدم به عقب می‌رود.

  • “How old are you?” → She asked me how old I was. ترتیب جملهٔ خبری
  • “Did you enjoy the film?” → He asked if I had enjoyed the film. بله/نه: if
  • “Where is the bank?” → A tourist asked me where the bank was. ترتیب جملهٔ خبری
  • “Will you come?” → She asked whether I would come. whether

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He asked me where did I live. → ✅ He asked me where I lived. بدون did؛ ترتیب جملهٔ خبری.
  • ❌ She asked me was I hungry. → ✅ She asked me if I was hungry. بله/نه: if / whether.
  • ❌ They asked what time was it. → ✅ They asked what time it was. ترتیب جملهٔ خبری.

نقل دستور و درخواست: told me to, asked me not to B1

tell / ask + شخص + (not) to + فعل: “Sit down.” → She told me to sit down. “Please don't shout.” → He asked us not to shout.

مستقیمغیرمستقیم
“Close the door.”She told me to close the door.
“Don't touch it!”He told me not to touch it.
“Could you help me, please?”She asked me to help her.
“Please don't be late.”They asked us not to be late.

برای دستور و راهنمایی tell و برای درخواست ask بیاورید. شکل منفی not to + فعل است (نه to don't).

  • “Wait here.” → The guard told us to wait there. دستور
  • “Can you call me back?” → She asked me to call her back. درخواست
  • “Don't forget your passport.” → Mum told me not to forget my passport. دستور منفی
  • “Please turn down the music.” → My neighbour asked us to turn down the music. درخواست

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She told me don't worry. → ✅ She told me not to worry. not to + فعل.
  • ❌ He asked me that I help him. → ✅ He asked me to help him. ask + شخص + to.
  • ❌ The teacher told to us to be quiet. → ✅ The teacher told us to be quiet. tell + شخص (بدون to).

C1 · پیشرفته

Modals in reported speech C1

can → could, will → would, may → might, must → had to / must. Could, would, should, might, ought to don't change.

DirectReported
“I can help.”He said he could help.
“I will call.”She said she would call.
“It may rain.”He said it might rain.
“You must leave.”She said I had to / must leave.
“I should go.”He said he should go. (no change)
“I could come.”She said she could come. (no change)

Must for obligation can become had to or stay must: He said I had to / must sign the form. For deduction, must doesn't change: She said it must be a mistake.

Shall I / we questions become should: “Shall I open the window?” → He asked if he should open the window. (offer or advice)

Needn't can stay or become didn't need to / didn't have to.

  • “You may be right.” → She said I might be right. (may → might)
  • “We must hurry.” → He said we had to hurry. (must → had to)
  • “You should rest.” → The doctor said I should rest. (no change)
  • “Shall I carry it?” → He asked if he should carry it. (shall → should)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She said she can come. (the time is past) → ✅ She said she could come. (can → could)
  • ❌ He said I must have to go. → ✅ He said I had to go. / He said I must go. (must → had to (or must))

Choosing the tense: reporting news, facts and opinions C1

Backshift is not automatic. Choose the tense to show if something is still true, and use present reporting verbs for news, written sources and opinions: The report says…

Native speakers often don't backshift when the information is still true or relevant: She told me she has a new job. (She still has it.) Backshifting can suggest the information is no longer true or that you don't believe it: He said he was 25 (but I think he's older).

For written sources and news, we often use present reporting verbs: The article says that… The government claims that… Darwin argues that…

For past facts, the past simple often stays (no need for the past perfect) when the order is clear: She said she was born in 1990.

In academic writing, present reporting verbs are common for ideas in published work: Smith (2010) argues that… See Reporting Verbs.

  • She told me she is pregnant. (still true)
  • He said he was innocent, but nobody believed him. (doubt / past situation)
  • The report says that unemployment has fallen. (written source: present)
  • She said she was born in Tabriz. (past fact: no change needed)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She said she had been born in 1990. (correct but unnecessary) → ✅ She said she was born in 1990. (past facts: the past simple can stay)

تمرین سطح C1

Might doesn't change.
