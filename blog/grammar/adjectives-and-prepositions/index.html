صفت و حرف اضافهAdjectives and Prepositions

بعد از خیلی از صفت‌ها یک حرف اضافهٔ ثابت می‌آید: She's good at maths. I'm afraid of spiders. Are you interested in art? قاعدهٔ ساده‌ای ندارد، پس بهتر است صفت و حرف اضافه را با هم یاد بگیرید. (حرف اضافهٔ فارسی معمولاً کمکی نمی‌کند: «از سگ می‌ترسم» ← afraid of dogs.)

بعد از حرف اضافه اسم، ضمیر یا فعلِ ‑ing‌دار بیاورید: I'm good at cooking. برای فعل‌هایی که حرف اضافه دارند (wait for, depend on) صفحهٔ فعل‌ها و حرف اضافه را ببینید.

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

good at, afraid of, interested in A2

جفت‌های رایج: good / bad at، afraid / scared / proud / tired / full of، interested in، worried / excited about، keen on، famous for.

حرف اضافهصفت‌هامثال
atgood, bad, brilliant, terribleShe's good at tennis.
ofafraid, scared, frightened, proud, tired, fond, full, jealousI'm afraid of the dark.
ininterestedHe's interested in history.
aboutworried, excited, nervous, happy, sorryI'm worried about the test.
onkeenShe's keen on photography.
forfamous, lateParis is famous for its food.

حرف اضافه مالِ صفت است، پس آن‌ها را جفتی یاد بگیرید: good at، afraid of، interested in.

  • My son is very good at maths. good at (در چیزی خوب بودن)
  • Are you afraid of spiders? afraid of (ترسیدن از)
  • I'm not interested in football. interested in (علاقه داشتن به)
  • We're really excited about the trip. excited about (هیجان داشتن برای)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm interested on music. → ✅ I'm interested in music. interested in.
  • ❌ She's afraid from dogs. → ✅ She's afraid of dogs. afraid of. (فارسی می‌گوید «ترس از»، ولی انگلیسی of، نه from.)
  • ❌ He's very good in tennis. → ✅ He's very good at tennis. good at.

تمرین سطح A2

1. My brother is very good ___ drawing.
یک مهارت: good at.
2. I'm not interested ___ cars.
Interested in.
3. Are you afraid ___ the dark? (one word)
Afraid of.

B1 · متوسط

kind to, married to, similar to, good for B1

kind / nice / rude / polite to کسی؛ angry / annoyed with کسی about چیزی؛ similar to، different from، married to، responsible for، aware of، capable of، good / bad for.

الگومثال
kind / nice / rude / polite to کسیShe's always kind to animals.
angry / annoyed / upset with کسی (و about چیزی)I'm angry with Ali about the car.
similar toYour bag is similar to mine.
different from (در انگلیسی بریتانیایی to هم، در آمریکایی than)Life here is different from life in Iran.
married / engaged toShe's married to a doctor.
responsible forWho's responsible for this?
aware of / capable ofAre you aware of the problem?
good / bad forSugar is bad for your teeth.

married to کسی، نه married with: He's married to a teacher. (فارسی می‌گوید «با یک معلم ازدواج کرده»، ولی انگلیسی to.)

مقایسه کنید: good at (یک مهارت) و good for (سالم یا مفید): She's good at tennis. Tennis is good for you.

  • Be nice to your sister! nice to
  • My phone is similar to yours. similar to (شبیهِ)
  • He's responsible for the whole team. responsible for (مسئولِ)
  • Fruit is good for you. good for (مفید برای)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She's married with an engineer. → ✅ She's married to an engineer. married to.
  • ❌ The film is very different of the book. → ✅ The film is very different from the book. different from.
  • ❌ He was rude with the waiter. → ✅ He was rude to the waiter. rude to.

صفت + حرف اضافه + ‑ing B1

بعد از حرف اضافه اسم یا فعلِ ‑ing‌دار بیاورید، هیچ‌وقت شکل سادهٔ فعل: I'm good at cooking. She's tired of waiting. Are you interested in joining?

بعد از حرف اضافه اسم، ضمیر یا ‑ing می‌آید: I'm good at swimming (نه good at swim). He's interested in learning Chinese.

با afraid هر دو الگو ممکن است: I'm afraid of flying. I'm afraid to fly.

not را قبل از ‑ing بیاورید: I'm sorry for not calling you.

  • She's good at making friends. good at + ‑ing
  • I'm tired of hearing the same excuses. tired of + ‑ing (خسته شدن از)
  • Are you interested in joining our team? interested in + ‑ing
  • He's keen on playing chess. keen on + ‑ing

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm good at speak English. → ✅ I'm good at speaking English. حرف اضافه + ‑ing.
  • ❌ She's interested in learn Japanese. → ✅ She's interested in learning Japanese. حرف اضافه + ‑ing.

تمرین سطح B1

4. She's married ___ a famous actor.
Married to.
5. Exercise is good ___ your heart.
مفید برای سلامتی: good for.
6. Your idea is very similar ___ mine. (one word)
Similar to.
7. I'm not very good at ___ names.
Good at + ‑ing.
8. My grandmother is afraid of ___ (use) computers.
Afraid of + ‑ing.
9. I'm tired of ___ (wait) for the bus every morning.
Tired of + ‑ing.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Good at, good with, good for; sorry about, sorry for B2

Some adjectives take different prepositions with different meanings: good at (skill), good with (handling), good for (healthy), kind of you (an action); sorry about (a situation), feel sorry for (pity).

PatternMeaningExample
good atskilfulShe's good at chess.
good withskilful at handling people or thingsHe's good with children. She's good with her hands.
good forhealthy, usefulWalking is good for you.
good / kind / nice to someonetreating someone wellThey were very kind to me.
good / kind / nice of you (to do)describing someone's actionIt was kind of you to call.
sorry about somethingregret a situationI'm sorry about the noise.
feel sorry for someonepityI feel sorry for him.
responsible for a task / to a personin charge of / reporting toI'm responsible for sales. I'm responsible to the director.
concerned about / withworried / on the subject ofWe're concerned about safety. The book is concerned with politics.

Sorry for + -ing (your own action) is also common: I'm sorry for shouting.

  • She's brilliant at languages but not very good with money. (at / with)
  • It was really nice of you to remember my birthday. (nice of you)
  • I feel sorry for people who live alone. (pity)
  • This article is concerned with climate policy. (on the subject of)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It was very nice to you to call. → ✅ It was very nice of you to call. (describing an action: of)
  • ❌ I'm sorry of the delay. → ✅ I'm sorry about the delay. (sorry about)
  • ❌ I feel sorry about him. → ✅ I feel sorry for him. (pity: sorry for)

تمرین سطح B2

10. He's a great nurse. He's very good ___ elderly patients.
Handling people: good with.
11. It was very kind ___ you to drive me home.
Describing an action: kind of you.
12. I feel really sorry ___ him. He lost his job. (one word)
Pity: feel sorry for.

C1 · پیشرفته

Prone to, devoid of, consistent with: formal pairs C1

Formal and academic pairs: prone / susceptible / conducive / averse to, devoid / indicative / wary of, consistent / compatible / synonymous with, reliant / contingent on, exempt from, adept at.

PrepositionAdjectivesExample
toprone, susceptible, conducive, averse, akin, immuneChildren are more susceptible to infection.
ofdevoid, indicative, wary, mindfulThe film is devoid of humour.
withconsistent, compatible, synonymous, fraughtHer name is synonymous with quality.
on / uponreliant, dependent, contingentThe deal is contingent on approval.
from / at / inexempt from, adept at, inherent inBooks are exempt from VAT in the UK.

These are common in academic and business English.

In averse to, prone to and conducive to, to is a preposition, so it's followed by a noun or -ing: He isn't averse to taking risks.

  • The area is prone to flooding. (prone to)
  • The results are consistent with earlier research. (consistent with)
  • She's adept at handling difficult clients. (adept at)
  • The offer is contingent on a successful interview. (contingent on)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He isn't averse to take risks. → ✅ He isn't averse to taking risks. (to (preposition) + -ing)
  • ❌ The app isn't compatible to older phones. → ✅ The app isn't compatible with older phones. (compatible with)

تمرین سطح C1

13. This part of the coast is prone ___ flooding.
Prone to.
14. The new results are consistent ___ our earlier findings.
Consistent with.
15. The room was completely devoid ___ furniture. (one word)
Devoid of = without.
