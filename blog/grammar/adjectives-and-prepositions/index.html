Adjectives + Prepositions (good at, married to)

Level: A2 · Free grammar guide

Many adjectives are followed by a fixed preposition: I'm good at maths. She's interested in art. He's married to a doctor.

The preposition is often different from your language, so learn each adjective together with its preposition.

Common adjectives + prepositions

PrepositionAdjectivesExample
atgood at, bad at, surprised at (or by)She's good at tennis.
ininterested inI'm interested in history.
ofafraid of, scared of, tired of, proud of, full of, fond ofMy son is afraid of dogs.
aboutworried about, excited about, nervous about, happy aboutWe're excited about the trip.
tomarried to, similar to, kind to, nice toYour bag is similar to mine.
forfamous for, responsible for, late forParis is famous for its food.
onkeen onHe's keen on football.
fromdifferent fromOur house is different from yours.

You'll also hear different to (British English) and different than (American English). Different from is correct everywhere.

+ noun, pronoun or -ing

After the preposition, use a noun, a pronoun or a verb-ing: I'm good at languages. I'm proud of you. I'm tired of waiting. (not tired of wait)

Examples

  1. He's very good at drawing. (good at + -ing)
  2. I'm not interested in football. (interested in)
  3. Are you afraid of spiders? (afraid of)
  4. She's married to an Australian. (married to (not with))
  5. Canada is famous for its lakes and mountains. (famous for)
  6. I'm worried about my exam. (worried about)
  7. My parents are very proud of me. (proud of)
  8. Be kind to your little brother. (kind to)
  9. The fridge is full of food. (full of)
  10. I'm really tired of this rain. (tired of = bored with)

Common mistakes

❌ She's married with a doctor.

✅ She's married to a doctor. (Married to.)

❌ I'm interested on music.

✅ I'm interested in music. (Interested in.)

❌ He's afraid from dogs.

✅ He's afraid of dogs. (Afraid of.)

❌ I'm good in cooking.

✅ I'm good at cooking. (Good at.)

❌ I'm tired of wait.

✅ I'm tired of waiting. (After a preposition, use -ing.)

❌ Your idea is similar with mine.

✅ Your idea is similar to mine. (Similar to.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct preposition.

1. She's very good ___ playing tennis.
good at.
2. I'm interested ___ learning Japanese.
interested in.
3. My little brother is afraid ___ the dark.
afraid of.
4. Montreal is famous ___ its bagels.
famous for.
5. I'm really tired ___ waiting. Let's go.
tired of = bored with.
6. She's married ___ a doctor.
married to.
7. We're very excited ___ our holiday.
excited about.
8. Your phone is similar ___ mine.
similar to.
9. I'm worried ___ my exam tomorrow.
worried about.
10. My parents are very proud ___ me.
proud of.
11. This box is full ___ old photos.
full of.
12. He's really bad ___ remembering names.
bad at.
13. Why are you angry ___ me? I didn't do anything!
angry with a person (angry at is also used).
14. She's very kind ___ animals.
kind to.
15. Choose the correct sentence.
keen on.
16. Our new flat is very different ___ the old one.
different from.
