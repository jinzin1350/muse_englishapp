because، as، since و due to (بیان دلیل)Because, As, Since and Due To

because رایج‌ترین راه برای گفتن دلیل است: I stayed at home because I was ill. as و since همان معنی را دارند (چون، از آنجا که)، و وقتی دلیل از قبل معلوم است اغلب اول جمله می‌آیند: As it's late, let's go home.

بعد از due to, owing to و thanks to، مثل because of، اسم می‌آید. برای پایهٔ so و because صفحهٔ and، but، or، so، because را ببینید.

B1 · B2 · C1

B1 · متوسط

because, as و since B1

because، as و since همه دلیل + جمله‌واره می‌آورند. because روی دلیل تأکید بیشتری دارد و جواب معمول به Why? است. as و since وقتی دلیل روشن یا از قبل معلوم است اغلب اول جمله می‌آیند.

کلمهکاربرد معمولمثال
becauseدلیل همان اطلاعات تازه و مهم استI'm learning Spanish because I'm moving to Madrid.
asدلیلِ معلوم، اغلب اول جملهAs it was raining, we took a taxi.
sinceدلیلِ معلوم، اغلب اول جملهSince you're here, can you help me?

برای جواب دادن به سؤالِ Why? از because استفاده کنید: Why are you late? ~ Because I missed the bus. (نه As I missed the bus)

since معنی «از زمانِ» هم دارد (since 2010) و as معنی «در حالی که» یا «مثلِ»، پس معنی را در جمله چک کنید.

  • As we have time, let's have a coffee. دلیلِ معلوم
  • Since it's your birthday, I'll pay. دلیلِ معلوم
  • I couldn't sleep because the neighbours were noisy. دلیل به‌عنوان اطلاعات تازه
  • Why are you so happy? ~ Because I got the job! جواب به Why?

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Why did you leave early? ~ As I was tired. → ✅ Why did you leave early? ~ Because I was tired. جواب به Why?: because.
  • ❌ As it was late, so we went home. → ✅ As it was late, we went home. as و so با هم نه.

تمرین سطح B1

1. ___ it's a holiday tomorrow, we can stay up late.
دلیلِ معلوم: As.
2. Why are you crying? ~ ___ I've lost my phone.
جواب به Why?: Because.
3. ___ you're going to the shop, can you buy some bread? (one word)
دلیلِ معلوم: Since.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Now that, seeing as, in case B2

Now that = because of a new situation (Now that I've retired, I have more time). Seeing as / seeing that (informal) = because. Don't confuse reasons with in case (a precaution: Take an umbrella in case it rains).

LinkerExampleMeaning
now thatNow that the kids have left home, the house is quiet.because this is new
seeing as / seeing that (informal)Seeing as you're here, you can help.because, since
in caseTake some water in case you get thirsty.as a precaution

Now that often goes with the present perfect or a present tense: Now that I've passed my test, I can drive to work.

After in case, use a present tense for the future: in case it rains (not in case it will rain).

  • Now that I live near work, I walk every day. (new situation)
  • Seeing as it's Friday, let's order pizza. (informal reason)
  • Write down my number in case you need it. (precaution)
  • Now that you mention it, I do feel a bit cold. (now that)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Take a coat in case it will be cold. → ✅ Take a coat in case it's cold. (in case + present)
  • ❌ Now as I've finished, I can relax. → ✅ Now that I've finished, I can relax. (now that)

Due to, owing to, thanks to, because of B2

Because of, due to, owing to and thanks to + noun or -ing. Due to often follows be (The delay was due to the snow). Thanks to is usually for good results. Use due to the fact that + clause.

LinkerExampleNote
because ofWe were late because of the traffic.neutral, common
due toThe flight was cancelled due to bad weather. / The delay was due to a strike.more formal; often after be
owing toOwing to illness, the concert was cancelled.formal
thanks toThanks to your help, we finished on time.usually a good result
due to / owing to the fact that + clause…due to the fact that prices have risen.formal

Like because of, these are followed by a noun, not a clause: due to the rain (not due to it was raining).

  • The match was postponed due to heavy rain. (due to)
  • The accident was due to driver error. (be due to)
  • Owing to a technical problem, the website is down. (owing to)
  • Thanks to the new bridge, the journey is much shorter. (good result)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The train was late due to it was snowing. → ✅ The train was late due to the snow. (due to + noun)
  • ❌ Thanks to the storm, our house was damaged. → ✅ Because of the storm, our house was damaged. (bad result: because of)

تمرین سطح B2

4. ___ I've found a job, I can rent my own flat.
New situation: Now that.
5. Take your phone ___ you get lost.
Precaution: in case.
6. ___ that the exams are over, we can finally relax. (one word)
Now that = because of a new situation.
7. The concert was cancelled ___ the singer's illness.
+ noun: due to.
8. ___ my teacher, I passed the exam with a high mark.
Good result: Thanks to.
9. The delay was ___ to a problem with the engine. (one word)
Be due to + noun.

C1 · پیشرفته

Given that, in that, inasmuch as, for, in view of C1

Formal ways to give reasons: given (that), in view of, on account of, in that (= because, explaining in what way), inasmuch as, and literary for (= because, after the main clause).

LinkerExampleMeaning
given (that)Given that he's only 18, his success is amazing. / Given his age, …considering
in view of + nounIn view of the bad weather, the event has been cancelled.because of
on account of + nounHe retired early on account of ill health.because of
in thatThe plan is risky in that it depends on one supplier.because, in the way that
inasmuch as (very formal)He is guilty inasmuch as he knew about it.to the extent that, because
for (literary)She stopped, for she was exhausted.because

Literary for never starts a sentence and always follows a comma: We hurried home, for it was getting dark.

Given can be followed by a noun or that + clause: Given the cost, … / Given that it costs so much, …

  • Given that it's his first job, he's doing very well. (considering)
  • In view of recent events, security has been increased. (formal because of)
  • The results are surprising in that they contradict earlier research. (in what way)
  • He said nothing, for he knew it was hopeless. (literary for)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ For she was tired, she went to bed. → ✅ She went to bed, for she was tired. (literary for: never at the start)
  • ❌ In view the weather, we stayed in. → ✅ In view of the weather, we stayed in. (in view of)

تمرین سطح C1

10. ___ the circumstances, I think you made the right decision.
+ noun: Given.
11. The novel is unusual ___ it has no main character.
In what way: in that.
12. The match was postponed on ___ of the bad weather. (one word)
On account of + noun.
