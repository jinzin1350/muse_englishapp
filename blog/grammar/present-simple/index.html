Present Simple

A1 · A2 · B1 · C1

We use the present simple for habits, routines and things that are generally true: I drink coffee every morning. The sun rises in the east.

With he, she, it, add -s to the verb: She works in a bank. For negatives and questions, use do / does: I don't like fish. Does he speak French?

A1 · Beginner

Form: I work, she works A1

  • I live in a small flat. (I + base verb)
  • My brother works in a hospital. (he + -s)
  • He watches football every weekend. (-ch + -es)
  • My daughter studies in the evening. (y → ies)
  • She has two cats. (have → has)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ She work in a bank. → ✅ She works in a bank. (He / she / it: add -s.)
  • ❌ My mother haves two cars. → ✅ My mother has two cars. (Have → has.)
  • ❌ He watchs TV every night. → ✅ He watches TV every night. (Verbs ending in -ch add -es.)
  • ❌ I am work every day. → ✅ I work every day. (Don't add am / is / are.)

Negatives and questions: don't, doesn't, do, does A1

Wh- questionWhere do you work?Where does she work?

  • We don't watch TV in the morning. (negative)
  • She doesn't eat meat. (doesn't + base verb)
  • Do you like coffee? – Yes, I do. (question + short answer)
  • Does your sister speak English? (does + base verb)
  • What time does the shop open? (wh- question)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He don't like coffee. → ✅ He doesn't like coffee. (He / she / it: doesn't.)
  • ❌ She doesn't works on Sundays. → ✅ She doesn't work on Sundays. (After doesn't, use the base verb.)
  • ❌ Does he plays tennis? → ✅ Does he play tennis? (After does, use the base verb.)
  • ❌ Where you live? → ✅ Where do you live? (Questions need do / does.)

Uses: habits, facts and permanent situations A1

  • Habits and routines: I get up at 7 o'clock. We play football on Saturdays.
  • Facts and things that are always true: Water boils at 100°C. The sun rises in the east.
  • Permanent situations: My parents live in Toronto. She works for a big company.
  • Likes, dislikes and opinions: I love pizza. He doesn't like cold weather.

  • I get up at 7 every day. (routine)
  • Water boils at 100°C. (fact)
  • My parents live in Toronto. (permanent situation)
  • I love pizza, but I don't like pasta. (likes and dislikes)
  • How often do you go to the gym? – Twice a week. (frequency)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ She always is late. → ✅ She is always late. (Adverbs of frequency go after be.)
  • ❌ I go every day to work. → ✅ I go to work every day. (Time words like every day usually go at the end.)
  • ❌ Water is boiling at 100°C. → ✅ Water boils at 100°C. (For facts that are always true, use the present simple.)

A2 · Elementary

Timetables and schedules: The train leaves at 8 A2

  • The train leaves at 8:30 tomorrow. (transport timetable)
  • What time does the film start tonight? (schedule)
  • My English course starts on Monday. (fixed date)
  • The museum opens at 10 on Sundays. (opening times)
  • I'll text you when the plane lands. (time clause)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I meet my friends tonight. (my plan) → ✅ I'm meeting my friends tonight. (Personal arrangements: present continuous.)
  • ❌ I'll call you when I will get home. → ✅ I'll call you when I get home. (After when, use the present simple for the future.)

Practice: A2

12. Hurry up! Our train ___ in ten minutes.
A timetable: present simple, leaves.
13. What time ___ the concert start tomorrow?
A schedule: What time does the concert start?
14. I'll phone you as soon as I ___ at the hotel.
After as soon as, use the present simple for the future: arrive.

B1 · Intermediate

I promise, I agree, I apologise: saying is doing B1

  • I promise I'll call you tomorrow. (a promise)
  • I agree with you. (not I'm agree)
  • I apologise for the delay. (an apology)
  • I suggest we take a taxi. (a suggestion)
  • I would advise you to book early. (more formal)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I'm promising I'll be careful. → ✅ I promise I'll be careful. (Saying it is the action: present simple.)
  • ❌ I'm agree with you. → ✅ I agree with you. (Agree is a verb: don't add am.)
  • ❌ I am apologising for the mistake. → ✅ I apologise for the mistake. (Saying it is the action: present simple.)

Practice: B1

15. I ___ I won't tell anyone.
Saying it makes the promise: present simple, I promise.
16. I ___ with you. It's a great idea.
Agree is a verb, used in the present simple: I agree.
17. I ___ for being late. It won't happen again.
Saying it is the apology: present simple, I apologise.

C1 · Advanced

Headlines, summaries, instructions and I hear C1

  • Headlines use the present simple for recent events: Floods hit northern Italy. Minister resigns.
  • Summaries of books, films and plays: In the film, a young doctor moves to a small town and discovers a secret.
  • Instructions and demonstrations: First you heat the oil, then you add the onions.
  • Passing on news with I hear, I gather, I understand, they say, (someone) tells me: I hear you're moving to Vancouver. Anna tells me you've got a new job.

  • Scientists discover water on Mars. (headline)
  • In chapter 3, the author compares the two cities. (summary)
  • First you mix the flour and the eggs. (instructions)
  • I gather the meeting has been cancelled. (news I've heard)
  • My doctor tells me I need more exercise. (passing on news)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ In the film, the hero moves to London and was finding a job. → ✅ In the film, the hero moves to London and finds a job. (In a summary, keep the present simple.)
  • ❌ Anna is telling me you're engaged. → ✅ Anna tells me you're engaged. (To pass on news, use tells me.)

Practice: C1

18. Which headline is written in the usual headline style?
Headlines use the present simple for recent events.
19. What does I gather the meeting is cancelled mean?
I gather = I understand from what I've heard or read.
20. Choose the best sentence for a film summary.
Summaries use the present simple throughout.
