Present Simple

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

We use the present simple for habits, routines and things that are generally true: I drink coffee every morning. The sun rises in the east.

With he, she, it, add -s to the verb: She works in a bank. For negatives and questions, use do / does: I don't like fish. Does he speak French?

When do we use the present simple?

  • Habits and routines: I get up at 7 o'clock. We play football on Saturdays.
  • Facts and things that are always true: The sun rises in the east. Water boils at 100°C.
  • Permanent situations: My parents live in Toronto. She works for a big company.
  • Likes, dislikes and opinions: I love pizza. He doesn't like cold weather.
  • Timetables: The train leaves at 8:30.

Common time words: every day, every week, on Mondays, in the morning, always, usually, often, sometimes, never.

How to form the present simple

I / you / we / theyhe / she / it
PositiveI work.She works.
NegativeI don't (do not) work.She doesn't (does not) work.
QuestionDo you work?Does she work?
Short answersYes, I do. / No, I don't.Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Wh- questionWhere do you work?Where does she work?

Only add -s in positive sentences. After doesn't and does, use the base verb: She doesn't work. Does she work? (not doesn't works)

Be is different: I am, you are, she is. Negatives and questions with be don't use do: I'm not tired. Are you ready?

Spelling: he, she, it + -s

RuleExamples
Most verbs: add -swork → works, read → reads, live → lives
Verbs ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: add -eskiss → kisses, wash → washes, watch → watches, fix → fixes, go → goes, do → does
Consonant + y: change y to -iesstudy → studies, try → tries, fly → flies
Vowel + y: add -splay → plays, buy → buys, enjoy → enjoys
Irregularhave → has

How to pronounce -s and -es

SoundExamples
/s/works, likes, stops, eats
/z/plays, lives, goes, reads
/ɪz/ (an extra syllable)watches, washes, kisses, uses

Two common verbs sound different from how they look: says sounds like sez and does sounds like duz.

Always, usually, never: where do they go?

Words like always, usually, often, sometimes, never go before the main verb: I usually walk to work. She never eats meat. But they go after be: He's always late.

Present simple or present continuous?

The present simple is for habits and facts: I work from home on Fridays. The present continuous is for now or around now: I'm working from home today. More practice: Present Continuous.

Examples

  1. I live in a small flat. (I + base verb)
  2. My brother works in a hospital. (he + -s)
  3. We don't watch TV in the morning. (don't + base verb)
  4. She doesn't eat meat. (doesn't + base verb)
  5. Do you like coffee? (question with do)
  6. Does your sister speak English? (question with does)
  7. What time does the shop open? (wh- question)
  8. He watches football every weekend. (-ch → -es)
  9. My daughter studies in the evening. (consonant + y → -ies)
  10. The bus leaves at 7:45. (timetable)

Common mistakes

❌ She work in a bank.

✅ She works in a bank. (With he, she, it, add -s.)

❌ He don't like coffee.

✅ He doesn't like coffee. (With he, she, it, use doesn't.)

❌ She doesn't works on Sundays.

✅ She doesn't work on Sundays. (After doesn't, use the base verb.)

❌ Does he plays tennis?

✅ Does he play tennis? (After does, use the base verb.)

❌ Where you live?

✅ Where do you live? (Questions need do or does.)

❌ My mother haves two cars.

✅ My mother has two cars. (Have is irregular: she has.)

❌ I am work every day.

✅ I work every day. (Don't put am, is or are before a normal verb in the present simple.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. My brother ___ in a bank.
My brother = he, so add -s: works.
2. I ___ coffee every morning.
A routine with I: base verb, drink. No -s with I.
3. She ___ meat.
With she, use doesn't + base verb.
4. ___ you live near here?
Questions with you: Do + subject + base verb.
5. What time ___ the film start?
The film = it, so use does + base verb.
6. We ___ to work on Fridays.
With we, use don't + base verb.
7. My dad ___ the news every evening.
Verbs ending in -ch add -es: watches.
8. Lucy ___ hard for her exams.
Consonant + y: change y to -ies: studies.
9. He ___ two brothers.
Have is irregular: he has.
10. Choose the correct question.
Does + she + base verb: Does she like music?
11. They ___ in a big house.
With they, use the base verb: live.
12. The train ___ at 6:15 every morning.
Timetables use the present simple. The train = it: leaves.
13. Where ___ your parents live?
Your parents = they, so use do.
14. My cat ___ fish.
My cat = it: doesn't + base verb.
15. Which word has two syllables?
After -ch, -sh, -s and -x, the ending sounds like /ɪz/ and adds a syllable: watch-es.
16. Does your sister speak Spanish? – Yes, she ___.
Short answer with she: Yes, she does.
