The Unreal Past (It's time we left, I'd rather you didn't)

Level: C1 · Free grammar guide

After certain expressions, we use a past tense to talk about the present or future. The past tense shows that the situation is unreal, imagined or wanted: It's time we went home. (We haven't gone yet.)

To talk about an unreal past, use the past perfect: She looked as if she had seen a ghost.

Expressions with the unreal past

ExpressionExampleMeaning
It's (high / about) time + subject + pastIt's time we left.We should leave now (we're late).
would rather + subject + pastI'd rather you didn't smoke here.I prefer that you don't.
as if / as though + pastHe talks as if he knew everything.He doesn't.
suppose / supposing / imagine + pastSuppose you won a million dollars.An imagined situation.
wish / if only + pastIf only I had more time!I don't have it.

In formal English, were is used for all persons: He acts as if he were the boss. (was is common in speech).

Would rather: same subject or different subject?

Same subject: would rather + base form: I'd rather stay at home.

Different subject: would rather + subject + past: I'd rather you stayed at home. For the past, use the past perfect: I'd rather you hadn't told her.

You may hear the base form after a different subject in American English (I'd rather you call me). The past form is correct everywhere.

It's time: two patterns

It's time to go (general) and It's time we went (it's a bit late) both work. It's high / about time adds criticism: It's about time you got a job!

For wish and if only, see Wish and If Only.

Examples

  1. It's late. It's time we went home. (it's time)
  2. It's high time the government did something about pollution. (high time)
  3. I'd rather you didn't tell anyone. (would rather + past)
  4. I'd rather stay in tonight. (same subject: base form)
  5. Would you rather I called you tomorrow? (question)
  6. He spends money as if he were a millionaire. (as if)
  7. She looked as though she had seen a ghost. (unreal past: past perfect)
  8. Supposing it rained tomorrow, would we still go? (supposing)
  9. Imagine you lived in the 18th century. (imagine)
  10. If only I had more time to travel! (if only)

Common mistakes

❌ It's time we go home.

✅ It's time we went home. / It's time to go home. (It's time + subject + past.)

❌ I'd rather you don't smoke here.

✅ I'd rather you didn't smoke here. (Different subject: would rather + past.)

❌ I'd rather to stay at home.

✅ I'd rather stay at home. (Same subject: would rather + base form, no to.)

❌ It's about time you get a job.

✅ It's about time you got a job. (It's about time + past.)

❌ She looked as if she has seen a ghost.

✅ She looked as if she had seen a ghost. (Unreal past: past perfect.)

❌ I'd rather you didn't tell her yesterday.

✅ I'd rather you hadn't told her. (For the past: would rather + past perfect.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. It's late. It's time we ___ home.
It's time + subject + past: went.
2. I'd rather you ___ smoke in the house.
Different subject: would rather + past, didn't.
3. He talks as if he ___ everything, but he doesn't.
Unreal (he doesn't): as if + past, knew.
4. I'd rather ___ at home tonight. I'm tired.
Same subject: would rather + base form, stay.
5. Suppose you ___ a million dollars. What would you do?
Imagined situation: suppose + past, won.
6. It's high time the government ___ something about pollution.
It's high time + past: did.
7. She looked as though she ___ a ghost.
Unreal, earlier than looked: past perfect, had seen.
8. Would you rather I ___ you tomorrow?
Different subject: would rather + past, called.
9. Imagine you ___ in the 18th century. How would your life be different?
Imagined situation: imagine + past, lived.
10. It's about time you ___ a job!
It's about time + past: got.
11. I'd rather we ___ about this now. Can we talk tomorrow?
Different subject: would rather + past, didn't talk.
12. Choose the correct sentence.
It's time + subject + past: It's time we left.
13. He spends money as if he ___ a millionaire. (He isn't.)
Unreal: as if + were.
14. My parents would rather I ___ medicine, not art.
Different subject: would rather + past, studied.
15. Supposing it ___ tomorrow, would we still go?
Imagined situation with would: supposing + past, rained.
16. If only I ___ more time to travel!
Unreal present wish: if only + past, had.
