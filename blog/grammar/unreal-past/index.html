گذشتهٔ غیرواقعی (it's time، would rather)Unreal Past (It's time we went, I'd rather you didn't…)

B1 · B2 · C1

In some structures, a past form doesn't talk about past time. It shows that a situation is unreal, imagined or wanted: It's time we went. (We should go now.)

You already know this from Second Conditional and Wish and If Only. This page covers the other common structures: it's time, would rather, as if, suppose and what if.

B1 · متوسط

It's time…: it's time to go, it's time we went B1

  • It's time + to + verb: It's late. It's time to go. / It's time for + noun: It's time for bed.
  • It's time + subject + past simple: It's late. It's time we went home. The meaning is now, not the past.

  • It's time to start the meeting. (time + to)
  • It's time the kids went to bed. (time + subject + past)
  • It's high time you cleaned your room! (complaint)
  • It's about time the council did something about the roads. (should have happened already)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It's time we go. → ✅ It's time we went. (time + subject + past simple)
  • ❌ It's high time to you started. → ✅ It's high time you started. (time + subject + past simple)

تمرین سطح B1

1. It's nearly midnight. It's time we ___ home.
It's time + subject + past simple.
2. It's 7 am. It's time ___ up.
It's time + to + verb.
3. You're 30! It's about time you ___ (learn) to cook.
It's about time + subject + past simple.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Would rather: I'd rather stay, I'd rather you stayed B2

PatternExampleMeaning
would rather + base verbI'd rather stay at home tonight.my preference
would rather not + base verbI'd rather not talk about it.negative
would rather + person + past simpleI'd rather you stayed at home tonight.what I want you to do (now / future)
would rather + person + didn'tI'd rather you didn't smoke here.polite request

  • I'd rather have tea, please. (my preference)
  • I'd rather you didn't tell anyone about this. (polite request)
  • My parents would rather I studied medicine. (what they want me to do)
  • We'd rather walk than wait for a taxi. (rather … than)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'd rather to stay at home. → ✅ I'd rather stay at home. (would rather + base verb (no to))
  • ❌ I'd rather you don't call me at work. → ✅ I'd rather you didn't call me at work. (another person: past simple)
  • ❌ I'd rather you to come tomorrow. → ✅ I'd rather you came tomorrow. (person + past simple)

As if / as though: real or unreal? B2

  • You sound as if you've got a cold. (probably true)
  • He spends money as if he were a millionaire. (unreal: he isn't)
  • It feels as though we've known each other for years. (it seems true)
  • Don't talk to me as if I was stupid. (unreal)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It looks as it's going to rain. → ✅ It looks as if it's going to rain. (as if (not as))
  • ❌ She looks as she's tired. → ✅ She looks as if she's tired. (as if, not as)

تمرین سطح B2

4. I'd rather you ___ the window. It's cold in here.
Another person: would rather + past simple.
5. I'm tired. I'd rather ___ in tonight.
Own preference: base verb.
6. My mother would rather I ___ (live) closer to home.
Another person: past simple.
7. He's not my boss, but he gives orders as if he ___ my boss.
Unreal comparison: past (were).
8. Look at those dark clouds. It looks as if it ___ to rain.
Probably true: it's going to rain.
9. She treats her cat as though it ___ a baby.
Unreal: past (were, or was).

C1 · پیشرفته

Suppose, supposing, imagine, what if C1

PatternExampleMeaning
+ presentWhat if we miss the train?it could really happen
+ past simpleSuppose you won the lottery…imaginary / less likely
+ past perfectImagine you had been born in 1900.imaginary past

  • Suppose your boss found out. What would you say? (imaginary)
  • What if the shop is closed? (real possibility)
  • Supposing we went to Spain this summer? (suggestion)
  • Imagine you had missed that plane. You'd have been in the crash. (imaginary past)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Suppose you would win, what would you buy? → ✅ Suppose you won, what would you buy? (no would after suppose)
  • ❌ What if we had more time tomorrow, we will finish. → ✅ If we had more time tomorrow, we would finish. (Imaginary: would in the result.)

Unreal past about the past: past perfect C1

  • would rather + person + past perfect: I'd rather you hadn't invited her. (You did, and I'm not happy.)
  • as if + past perfect: She looked as if she had been crying.
  • suppose / imagine / what if + past perfect: What if I hadn't come?
  • wish / if only + past perfect: I wish I had known. See Wish and If Only.

  • I'd rather you hadn't mentioned my age. (you did mention it)
  • He talked as if nothing had happened. (something had happened)
  • What if we hadn't met that day? (imaginary past)
  • Suppose the police had stopped you. (imaginary past)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'd rather you didn't tell her yesterday. → ✅ I'd rather you hadn't told her yesterday. (past: past perfect)
  • ❌ He looked as if he saw a ghost. → ✅ He looked as if he had seen a ghost. (earlier unreal: past perfect)

تمرین سطح C1

10. ___ you could live anywhere. Where would you go?
Imagine + past for an imaginary situation.
11. What if the taxi ___ late? We'll miss the flight!
A real possibility: present.
12. Suppose you ___ (lose) your job tomorrow. What would you do?
Imaginary: past simple.
13. You told everyone my secret. I'd rather you ___ that.
Past action: would rather + past perfect.
14. He came in looking as though he ___ a marathon.
Unreal, earlier: past perfect.
15. What if the doctor ___ (not / arrive) in time? The baby might have died.
Imaginary past: past perfect.
