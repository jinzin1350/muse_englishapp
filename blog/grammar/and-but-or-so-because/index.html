حرف‌های ربط and، but، or، so و becauseAnd, But, Or, So and Because

Linking words (conjunctions) join ideas: I like tea and coffee. It's cheap but good. Tea or coffee? It was late, so I went home. I went home because it was late.

This page starts with the basics and goes up to formal linkers like however and therefore. For although and despite, see Although, Despite and In Spite Of.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2

A1 · مبتدی

And, but, or A1

And adds information. But shows a contrast (a different or surprising idea). Or gives a choice.

WordUseExample
andaddingI have a brother and a sister.
butcontrastThe hotel was nice but expensive.
orchoiceDo you want tea or coffee?

You can join words (bread and butter) or sentences (I called her, but she didn't answer).

Use or in negative sentences: I don't like tea or coffee. (not and coffee here)

We often put a comma before but when it joins two sentences: It was sunny, but it was cold.

  • I play football and tennis. (adding)
  • My flat is small but comfortable. (contrast)
  • Would you like rice or potatoes? (choice)
  • She doesn't eat meat or fish. (negative: or)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I like coffee but I like tea. → ✅ I like coffee and I like tea. (adding, not contrast: and)
  • ❌ Do you want water and juice? (one of them) → ✅ Do you want water or juice? (choice: or)

تمرین سطح A1

1. I have a cat ___ a dog.
Adding: and.
2. The film was long ___ very good.
Contrast: but.
3. Do you want to go out ___ stay at home? (one word)
Choice: or.

A2 · مقدماتی

So or because? Result and reason A2

Because gives the reason (why?). So gives the result (what happened next?): I was hungry, so I ate a sandwich. I ate a sandwich because I was hungry.

Reason → resultResult ← reason
It was raining, so we stayed at home.We stayed at home because it was raining.
I was tired, so I went to bed.I went to bed because I was tired.

Because can also start the sentence; then use a comma: Because it was raining, we stayed at home.

Don't use so and because together: Because it was late, I went home. (not Because it was late, so I went home.)

  • I'm learning English because I want a better job. (reason)
  • The shop was closed, so I went home. (result)
  • Because he was ill, he didn't come to work. (because at the start)
  • It's cold, so take a jacket. (result)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Because I was hungry, so I made a sandwich. → ✅ Because I was hungry, I made a sandwich. (not because + so together)
  • ❌ I didn't go out so I was ill. → ✅ I didn't go out because I was ill. (reason: because)

تمرین سطح A2

4. I was very tired, ___ I went to bed early.
Result: so.
5. She's happy ___ she passed her exam.
Reason: because.
6. The bus didn't come, ___ I took a taxi. (one word: a result)
Result: so.

B1 · متوسط

Because or because of? So, that's why B1

Because + a clause (subject + verb): because it was raining. Because of + a noun: because of the rain. That's why = so (in speaking).

PatternExample
because + subject + verbWe stayed in because it was raining.
because of + noun / pronounWe stayed in because of the rain.
so + subject + verbIt was raining, so we stayed in.
That's why + subject + verbIt was raining. That's why we stayed in.

After because of, use a noun, a pronoun or -ing: because of him, because of the traffic.

  • The flight was cancelled because of the snow. (because of + noun)
  • The flight was cancelled because it was snowing. (because + clause)
  • I failed the test because of you! (because of + pronoun)
  • I missed the bus. That's why I'm late. (that's why)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I was late because the traffic. → ✅ I was late because of the traffic. (+ noun: because of)
  • ❌ I was late because of the traffic was bad. → ✅ I was late because the traffic was bad. (+ clause: because)

Also, too, as well, either B1

Also goes before the main verb (after be): I also play tennis. Too and as well go at the end: I play tennis too / as well. In negatives, use either: I don't like it either.

WordPositionExample
alsobefore the main verb, after be and auxiliariesShe also speaks French. She's also a doctor.
tooend of the sentenceShe speaks French too.
as wellend of the sentenceShe speaks French as well.
eitherend of a negative sentenceShe doesn't speak German, and I don't either.

Also is more common in writing; too and as well are more common in speaking.

Don't use too in negatives: I don't like it either (not I don't like it too).

  • I also have a car. (also before the verb)
  • He's a singer, and he's also an actor. (also after be)
  • I love pizza. ~ Me too! (too at the end)
  • I can't swim. ~ I can't either. (negative: either)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I like also football. → ✅ I also like football. (also before the main verb)
  • ❌ I don't eat meat too. → ✅ I don't eat meat either. (negative: either)
  • ❌ Too I speak Spanish. → ✅ I speak Spanish too. (too at the end)

تمرین سطح B1

7. The match was cancelled ___ the rain.
+ noun: because of.
8. We couldn't sleep ___ our neighbours were having a party.
+ clause: because.
9. I didn't hear the phone ___ of the noise. (one word)
Because of + noun.
10. I speak English, and I ___ speak a little Spanish.
Before the main verb: also.
11. Mina doesn't like horror films, and I don't like them ___.
Negative: either.
12. I'm hungry. ~ Me ___! Let's eat. (one word)
Positive: Me too.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

However, therefore, in addition, as a result B2

In more formal writing, use linkers between sentences: However (contrast), Therefore / As a result (result), In addition / Moreover (adding). They usually start a new sentence, followed by a comma.

UseInformalFormal
contrastbutHowever, … / Nevertheless, …
resultsoTherefore, … / As a result, … / Consequently, …
addingand, alsoIn addition, … / Moreover, … / Furthermore, …

But and so join two parts of one sentence. However and therefore usually link two sentences: The plan was good. However, it was too expensive.

You can also put however in the middle, between commas: The plan, however, was too expensive. With a semicolon: The plan was good; however, it was expensive.

Don't use however like but with only a comma: not It was good, however it was expensive. Use a full stop or a semicolon.

  • The hotel was lovely. However, the food was terrible. (contrast)
  • Sales fell by 20%. As a result, 50 jobs were lost. (result)
  • The job is well paid. In addition, it offers flexible hours. (adding)
  • He didn't study. Therefore, he failed. (result)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The weather was bad. But however, we went out. → ✅ The weather was bad. However, we went out. (one linker, not two)
  • ❌ It's cheap. Moreover it's bad quality. (contrast) → ✅ It's cheap. However, it's bad quality. (contrast: however)

تمرین سطح B2

13. The flat is in a great location. ___, it's very small.
Contrast: However.
14. The company lost its biggest customer. ___, it had to close two factories.
Result: As a result.
15. The course is free. In ___, students get a certificate at the end. (one word)
Adding: In addition.
