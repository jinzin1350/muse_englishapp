خانه / گرامر / حرف‌های ربط and، but، or، so و because

حرف‌های ربط and، but، or، so و becauseAnd, But, Or, So and Because

کلمه‌های ربط ایده‌ها را به هم وصل می‌کنند: I like tea and coffee. It's cheap but good. Tea or coffee? It was late, so I went home. I went home because it was late.

این صفحه از پایه شروع می‌کند و تا کلمه‌های ربطِ رسمی مثل however و therefore پیش می‌رود. برای although و despite صفحهٔ although، despite و in spite of را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2

A1 · مبتدی

and, but, or A1

and (و) اطلاعات اضافه می‌کند. but (اما) تضاد را نشان می‌دهد، یعنی ایده‌ای متفاوت یا غیرمنتظره. or (یا) انتخاب می‌دهد.

کلمهکاربردمثال
andاضافه کردنI have a brother and a sister.
butتضادThe hotel was nice but expensive.
orانتخابDo you want tea or coffee?

می‌توانید کلمه‌ها را وصل کنید (bread and butter) یا جمله‌ها را (I called her, but she didn't answer).

در جملهٔ منفی or بیاورید: I don't like tea or coffee. (اینجا نه and coffee). فارسی می‌گوید «چای و قهوه دوست ندارم»، اما انگلیسی or.

وقتی but دو جمله را وصل می‌کند، معمولاً قبلش ویرگول می‌گذاریم: It was sunny, but it was cold.

  • I play football and tennis. اضافه کردن
  • My flat is small but comfortable. تضاد
  • Would you like rice or potatoes? انتخاب
  • She doesn't eat meat or fish. منفی: or

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I like coffee but I like tea. → ✅ I like coffee and I like tea. اضافه کردن است، نه تضاد: and.
  • ❌ Do you want water and juice? (one of them) → ✅ Do you want water or juice? انتخاب (یکی از آن دو): or.

تمرین سطح A1

1. I have a cat ___ a dog.
اضافه کردن: and.
2. The film was long ___ very good.
تضاد: but.
3. Do you want to go out ___ stay at home? (one word)
انتخاب: or.

A2 · مقدماتی

so یا because؟ نتیجه و دلیل A2

because (چون) دلیل را می‌گوید (چرا؟). so (پس، برای همین) نتیجه را می‌گوید (بعدش چه شد؟): I was hungry, so I ate a sandwich. I ate a sandwich because I was hungry.

اول دلیل، بعد نتیجه (so)اول نتیجه، بعد دلیل (because)
It was raining, so we stayed at home.We stayed at home because it was raining.
I was tired, so I went to bed.I went to bed because I was tired.

because می‌تواند اول جمله هم بیاید؛ آن‌وقت ویرگول بگذارید: Because it was raining, we stayed at home.

so و because را با هم نیاورید: Because it was late, I went home. (نه Because it was late, so I went home.). فارسی می‌گوید «چون دیر بود، برای همین رفتم خانه»، اما در انگلیسی فقط یکی می‌آید.

  • I'm learning English because I want a better job. دلیل
  • The shop was closed, so I went home. نتیجه
  • Because he was ill, he didn't come to work. because در اول جمله
  • It's cold, so take a jacket. نتیجه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Because I was hungry, so I made a sandwich. → ✅ Because I was hungry, I made a sandwich. because و so با هم نمی‌آیند.
  • ❌ I didn't go out so I was ill. → ✅ I didn't go out because I was ill. دلیل: because.

تمرین سطح A2

4. I was very tired, ___ I went to bed early.
نتیجه: so.
5. She's happy ___ she passed her exam.
دلیل: because.
6. The bus didn't come, ___ I took a taxi. (one word: a result)
نتیجه: so.

B1 · متوسط

because یا because of؟ so، that's why B1

because + جمله‌واره (فاعل + فعل): because it was raining. because of + اسم: because of the rain. That's why (برای همین) در صحبت همان so است.

الگومثال
because + فاعل + فعلWe stayed in because it was raining.
because of + اسم / ضمیرWe stayed in because of the rain.
so + فاعل + فعلIt was raining, so we stayed in.
That's why + فاعل + فعلIt was raining. That's why we stayed in.

بعد از because of اسم، ضمیر یا فعل ‑ing‌دار بیاورید: because of him, because of the traffic.

  • The flight was cancelled because of the snow. because of + اسم
  • The flight was cancelled because it was snowing. because + جمله‌واره
  • I failed the test because of you! because of + ضمیر
  • I missed the bus. That's why I'm late. that's why (برای همین)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I was late because the traffic. → ✅ I was late because of the traffic. با اسم: because of.
  • ❌ I was late because of the traffic was bad. → ✅ I was late because the traffic was bad. با جمله‌واره: because.

also, too, as well, either (هم) B1

also قبل از فعل اصلی می‌آید (و بعد از be): I also play tennis. too و as well آخر جمله می‌آیند: I play tennis too / as well. در جملهٔ منفی either بیاورید: I don't like it either.

کلمهجامثال
alsoقبل از فعل اصلی، بعد از be و فعل کمکیShe also speaks French. She's also a doctor.
tooآخر جملهShe speaks French too.
as wellآخر جملهShe speaks French as well.
eitherآخر جملهٔ منفیShe doesn't speak German, and I don't either.

also در نوشتن رایج‌تر است؛ too و as well در صحبت.

در جملهٔ منفی too نیاورید: I don't like it either (نه I don't like it too). در فارسی هر دو «هم» است، اما در منفی انگلیسی either می‌آید.

  • I also have a car. also قبل از فعل
  • He's a singer, and he's also an actor. also بعد از be
  • I love pizza. ~ Me too! too در آخر
  • I can't swim. ~ I can't either. منفی: either

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I like also football. → ✅ I also like football. also قبل از فعل اصلی می‌آید.
  • ❌ I don't eat meat too. → ✅ I don't eat meat either. منفی: either.
  • ❌ Too I speak Spanish. → ✅ I speak Spanish too. too آخر جمله می‌آید.

تمرین سطح B1

7. The match was cancelled ___ the rain.
با اسم: because of.
8. We couldn't sleep ___ our neighbours were having a party.
با جمله‌واره: because.
9. I didn't hear the phone ___ of the noise. (one word)
Because of + اسم.
10. I speak English, and I ___ speak a little Spanish.
قبل از فعل اصلی: also.
11. Mina doesn't like horror films, and I don't like them ___.
منفی: either.
12. I'm hungry. ~ Me ___! Let's eat. (one word)
مثبت: Me too.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

However, therefore, in addition, as a result B2

In more formal writing, use linkers between sentences: However (contrast), Therefore / As a result (result), In addition / Moreover (adding). They usually start a new sentence, followed by a comma.

UseInformalFormal
contrastbutHowever, … / Nevertheless, …
resultsoTherefore, … / As a result, … / Consequently, …
addingand, alsoIn addition, … / Moreover, … / Furthermore, …

But and so join two parts of one sentence. However and therefore usually link two sentences: The plan was good. However, it was too expensive.

You can also put however in the middle, between commas: The plan, however, was too expensive. With a semicolon: The plan was good; however, it was expensive.

Don't use however like but with only a comma: not It was good, however it was expensive. Use a full stop or a semicolon.

  • The hotel was lovely. However, the food was terrible. (contrast)
  • Sales fell by 20%. As a result, 50 jobs were lost. (result)
  • The job is well paid. In addition, it offers flexible hours. (adding)
  • He didn't study. Therefore, he failed. (result)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The weather was bad. But however, we went out. → ✅ The weather was bad. However, we went out. (one linker, not two)
  • ❌ It's cheap. Moreover it's bad quality. (contrast) → ✅ It's cheap. However, it's bad quality. (contrast: however)

تمرین سطح B2

13. The flat is in a great location. ___, it's very small.
Contrast: However.
14. The company lost its biggest customer. ___, it had to close two factories.
Result: As a result.
15. The course is free. In ___, students get a certificate at the end. (one word)
Adding: In addition.
