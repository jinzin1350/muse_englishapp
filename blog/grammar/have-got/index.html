خانه / گرامر / have got

have gotHave Got

A1 · A2 · B1

Have got means the same as have for possession, family, appearance and illness: I've got two sisters. She's got blue eyes. I've got a cold. It's very common in British English, especially in speech.

In American English, people usually say I have and Do you have…? Both are correct. For have to (obligation), see Must and Have To.
