خانه / گرامر / have got

have gotHave Got

have got برای مالکیت، خانواده، ظاهر و بیماری همان معنی have («داشتن») را دارد: I've got two sisters. She's got blue eyes. I've got a cold. در انگلیسی بریتانیایی، به‌خصوص در گفتار، خیلی رایج است.

در انگلیسی آمریکایی معمولاً I have و Do you have…? می‌گویند. هر دو درست‌اند. برای have to (اجبار) صفحهٔ must و have to را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1

A1 · مبتدی

I've got, she's got: شکل‌ها A1

I / you / we / they have got ('ve got). He / she / it has got ('s got). برای چیزهایی که دارید، خانواده، ظاهر و بیماری به کار ببرید.

شکل کاملشکل کوتاهمثال
I / you / we / they have got've gotWe've got a new car.
he / she / it has got's gotShe's got long hair.

He's got یعنی he has got (نه he is got). از جمله معلوم می‌شود: He's got a dog (has) / He's tired (is).

  • I've got a brother and a sister. خانواده
  • She's got green eyes. ظاهر
  • We've got a big garden. مالکیت
  • He's got a headache. بیماری

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She have got a cat. → ✅ She has got a cat. / She's got a cat. با she: has
  • ❌ I got two brothers. (BrE) → ✅ I've got two brothers. have got

I haven't got و have you got…? A1

منفی: haven't got / hasn't got. سؤالی: Have you got…? Has she got…? جواب کوتاه: Yes, I have. No, she hasn't. (نه Yes, I've got)

منفیسؤالیجواب کوتاه
I haven't got a car.Have you got a car?Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
She hasn't got a car.Has she got a car?Yes, she has. / No, she hasn't.

با have got از do استفاده نکنید: Have you got…? (نه Do you have got…?)

  • I haven't got any money. منفی
  • Have you got a pen? سؤالی
  • Has he got any brothers? ~ No, he hasn't. سؤال + جواب کوتاه
  • We haven't got time for lunch. منفی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Do you have got a car? → ✅ Have you got a car? / Do you have a car? با have got حرف do نمی‌آید
  • ❌ I don't have got a pen. → ✅ I haven't got a pen. / I don't have a pen. منفی
  • ❌ Have you got a car? ~ Yes, I've got. → ✅ Have you got a car? ~ Yes, I have. جواب کوتاه

تمرین سطح A1

1. My sister ___ three children.
She has got.
2. We ___ a new flat in the city centre.
We have got.
3. I ___ got a bad cold. (one word)
I have got.
4. ___ you got a pen I can borrow?
سؤالی: Have you got…?
5. She ___ any brothers or sisters. She's an only child.
منفی: hasn't got.
6. Has your flat got a balcony? ~ Yes, it ___. (one word)
جواب کوتاه: Yes, it has.

A2 · مقدماتی

have got یا have؟ have breakfast, had a car A2

have got و have برای مالکیت یک معنی دارند. اما برای کارها (have breakfast, have a shower, have a party) و در گذشته (I had a bike، نه I had got a bike) فقط have بیاورید.

have got = haveفقط have
I've got a car. / I have a car.I have breakfast at 7. (یک کار)
Have you got a pen? / Do you have a pen?Let's have a party.
She's got a cold. / She has a cold.I had a bike when I was a child. (گذشته)
I usually have a shower in the morning. (عادت)

با have (نه have got)، برای سؤالی و منفی do می‌آید: Do you have a car? I don't have time.

  • I have lunch at one o'clock. کار: have
  • We had a great time. گذشته: had
  • Do you have any brothers? have + do
  • She's got a beautiful voice. مالکیت: have got

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've got breakfast at 8. → ✅ I have breakfast at 8. کار: have
  • ❌ When I was young, I had got a dog. → ✅ When I was young, I had a dog. گذشته: had

تمرین سطح A2

7. I usually ___ a shower before breakfast.
کار / عادت: have.
8. When I was a child, I ___ a red bike.
گذشته: had.
9. ___ you have any pets? (one word)
have + do: Do you have…?

B1 · متوسط

have got to، got و gotten B1

have got to + فعل یعنی have to (اجبار، خودمانی): I've got to go. در انگلیسی آمریکایی اسم مفعولِ get اغلب gotten است (It's gotten cold)، اما have got (مالکیت) در هر دو یکی است.

الگومثالتوضیح
have got to + verbI've got to finish this today.یعنی have to (خودمانی)
has got to + verbShe's got to leave early.یعنی has to
gotta (خیلی خودمانی)I gotta go.فقط در گفتار
gotten (آمریکایی)It's gotten dark. / BrE It's got dark.اسم مفعولِ get

have got to برای حال و آینده است. برای گذشته had to بیاورید: I had to work late yesterday. (نه I had got to)

gotta یعنی (have) got to در گفتار خیلی خودمانی و ترانه‌ها. در نوشتن به کار نبرید.

  • I've got to call my mother tonight. اجبار
  • We've got to be at the airport by six. اجبار
  • Have you got to work tomorrow? سؤالی
  • It's gotten really cold. (AmE) gotten (آمریکایی)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I had got to leave early yesterday. → ✅ I had to leave early yesterday. گذشته: had to
  • ❌ She's got go now. → ✅ She's got to go now. got to + فعل

تمرین سطح B1

10. I'm sorry, I ___ go now. My bus is coming.
اجبار: I've got to.
11. Yesterday I ___ stay late at work.
گذشته: had to.
12. She's got ___ finish her essay by Friday. (one word)
have got to + فعل.
