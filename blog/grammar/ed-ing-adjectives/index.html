-ed and -ing Adjectives (bored or boring?)

Level: A2 · Free grammar guide

Many adjectives have two forms. The -ed adjective describes how a person feels. The -ing adjective describes the thing (or person) that causes the feeling.

The film was boring, so I was bored.

-ed or -ing?

MeaningExample
-edhow someone feelsI'm interested in history.
-ingwhat something or someone is likeHistory is interesting.

Be careful with people: He's bored = he feels bored. He's boring = he makes other people feel bored!

Prepositions after -ed adjectives

interested in, bored with, excited about, worried about, disappointed with. See Adjectives + Prepositions.

Examples

  1. The lesson was boring. Everyone was bored. (cause / feeling)
  2. I'm very tired. It was a tiring day. (feeling / cause)
  3. The kids are excited about the holiday. (feeling)
  4. This is an exciting match! (cause)
  5. I was surprised by the news. (feeling)
  6. The ending of the film was surprising. (cause)
  7. Maths is confusing for me. (cause)
  8. She felt embarrassed when she forgot his name. (feeling)
  9. A walk on the beach is very relaxing. (cause)
  10. We were disappointed with the hotel. (feeling)

Common mistakes

❌ I'm boring. There's nothing to do.

✅ I'm bored. There's nothing to do. (How you feel: -ed.)

❌ The film was very interested.

✅ The film was very interesting. (What the film is like: -ing.)

❌ I'm so exciting about my trip!

✅ I'm so excited about my trip! (How you feel: -ed.)

❌ The instructions were confused.

✅ The instructions were confusing. (What the instructions are like: -ing.)

❌ I feel very tiring today.

✅ I feel very tired today. (How you feel: -ed.)

❌ It was a very embarrassed moment.

✅ It was a very embarrassing moment. (What the moment was like: -ing.)

What the book is like: interesting.

What the instructions are like: confusing.

How you feel: interested in.

What the day was like: exhausting.

How she felt: embarrassed.
9. The kids are so ___ about the trip to the zoo!
How the kids feel: excited about.
10. That noise is really ___. Can you turn it off?
What the noise is like: annoying.
11. I was ___ with my exam results. I expected better.
How I felt: disappointed with.
12. A hot bath is very ___ after a long day.
What a hot bath is like: relaxing.

What the film was like: frightening.
14. My mum was very ___ because I didn't call her.
How my mum felt: worried.
15. It's ___ that he didn't come. He always comes to my parties.
What the situation is like: surprising.

How you feel: frightened.
