صفت‌های ed‌دار و ing‌دار-ed and -ing Adjectives

-ed adjectives describe how a person feels: I'm bored. -ing adjectives describe the thing or person that causes the feeling: The film is boring.

Most of these adjectives come from verbs: bore, interest, excite, tire, surprise, confuse.

A2 · B1 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

Bored or boring? Interested or interesting? A2

-ed = how you feel (I'm tired). -ing = what causes the feeling (My job is tiring).

-ed (feeling)-ing (cause)
I'm bored.This lesson is boring.
She's interested in art.Art is interesting.
We were excited.The match was exciting.
He's tired.The journey was tiring.
I was surprised.The news was surprising.

People can be both: He's bored (he feels bored) / He's boring (he makes other people feel bored!).

  • I'm interested in history. (feeling)
  • The museum was really interesting. (cause)
  • We were excited about the trip. (feeling)
  • That was an amazing concert. (cause)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm very interesting in football. → ✅ I'm very interested in football. (feeling: -ed)
  • ❌ The film was bored. → ✅ The film was boring. (cause: -ing)
  • ❌ I'm boring. Let's go out. (meaning I feel bored) → ✅ I'm bored. Let's go out. (feeling: -ed)

تمرین سطح A2

1. I'm ___. There's nothing to do.
Feeling: bored.
2. The football match was really ___.
Cause: exciting.
3. Are you ___ (interest) in science?
Feeling: interested.

B1 · متوسط

Confused or confusing? Embarrassed or embarrassing? B1

Other common pairs: confused / confusing, embarrassed / embarrassing, disappointed / disappointing, frightened / frightening, worried / worrying, annoyed / annoying, relaxed / relaxing, shocked / shocking.

-ed (feeling)-ing (cause)
confusedconfusing
embarrassedembarrassing
disappointeddisappointing
frightened / terrifiedfrightening / terrifying
worriedworrying
annoyedannoying
relaxedrelaxing
shockedshocking

Many -ed adjectives are followed by a preposition: disappointed with / by, worried about, annoyed with someone, frightened of. See Adjectives and Prepositions.

Some feeling adjectives don't have an -ing pair in this way: scared, upset.

  • I was embarrassed when I forgot her name. (feeling)
  • The instructions were very confusing. (cause)
  • It's worrying that he hasn't called. (cause)
  • A massage is very relaxing. (cause)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm so confusing. What does this mean? → ✅ I'm so confused. What does this mean? (feeling: -ed)
  • ❌ It was an embarrassed moment. → ✅ It was an embarrassing moment. (cause: -ing)
  • ❌ The results were disappointed. → ✅ The results were disappointing. (cause: -ing)

تمرین سطح B1

4. I fell over in front of everyone. It was so ___!
Cause: embarrassing.
5. My parents were very ___ about me because I didn't call.
Feeling: worried.
6. The ending of the film was very ___ (disappoint). We expected more.
Cause: disappointing.

C1 · پیشرفته

Overwhelmed, intrigued, unsettling: advanced pairs C1

Advanced pairs follow the same rule: overwhelmed / overwhelming, intrigued / intriguing, exhausted / exhausting, fascinated / fascinating, horrified / horrifying, unsettled / unsettling, inspired / inspiring, humiliated / humiliating.

-ed (feeling)-ing (cause)
exhausted (= very tired)exhausting
fascinatedfascinating
overwhelmedoverwhelming
intriguedintriguing
inspiredinspiring
horrifiedhorrifying
humiliatedhumiliating
unsettledunsettling

Strong adjectives like exhausted, fascinated and terrified are already extreme, so use absolutely or really with them, not very: absolutely exhausted.

The -ing adjective often follows find + object: I find his speeches inspiring. She found the experience humiliating.

  • I was overwhelmed by everyone's kindness. (feeling)
  • The amount of work is overwhelming. (cause)
  • She gave a really inspiring talk. (cause)
  • I'm intrigued. Tell me more! (feeling)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm absolutely exhausting after the marathon. → ✅ I'm absolutely exhausted after the marathon. (feeling: -ed)
  • ❌ I found the lecture fascinated. → ✅ I found the lecture fascinating. (find + object + -ing (cause))

تمرین سطح C1

7. Looking after three children all day is ___.
Cause: exhausting.
8. I was ___ by her story and wanted to know more.
Feeling: intrigued.
9. I find modern art absolutely ___ (fascinate).
Find + object + -ing (cause).
