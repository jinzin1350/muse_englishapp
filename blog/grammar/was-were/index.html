Was and Were (Past of Be)

A1 · A2 · B1

Was and were are the past forms of be (am / is / are): I was at home yesterday. They were late.

They are some of the most common words in English. This page starts with the basic forms and goes on to the mistakes learners often make with did, and to were in If I were you.

A1 · Beginner

I was, you were: the forms A1

PresentPastExample
I amI wasI was at work yesterday.
he / she / it ishe / she / it wasIt was cold last night.
you / we / they areyou / we / they wereWe were in Rome last summer.

  • I was very tired last night. (I → was)
  • My parents were in Spain last month. (plural noun → were)
  • The film was really good. (it → was)
  • You were right! (you → were, even for one person)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ They was at home. → ✅ They were at home. (they → were)
  • ❌ You was late. → ✅ You were late. (you → were)
  • ❌ I were hungry. → ✅ I was hungry. (I → was)

Wasn't, weren't and questions A1

I / he / she / ityou / we / they
NegativeI wasn't (was not) late.They weren't (were not) here.
QuestionWas she at home?Were you at school?
Short answersYes, she was. / No, she wasn't.Yes, we were. / No, we weren't.

  • The museum wasn't open on Monday. (negative)
  • Were you at the meeting? ~ No, I wasn't. (question + short answer)
  • How was your holiday? ~ It was great! (wh-question)
  • Where were you last night? (where + were + subject)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Did you were at home? → ✅ Were you at home? (No *did* with was / were.)
  • ❌ I didn't was hungry. → ✅ I wasn't hungry. (Negative: wasn't.)
  • ❌ Where you were yesterday? → ✅ Where were you yesterday? (Question: were before the subject.)

Practice: A1

1. Yesterday ___ my birthday.
Yesterday = past, it → was.
2. Tom and Anna ___ at the party last night.
Two people (they) → were.
3. I ___ (be) at the cinema last Saturday.
I → was.
4. ___ you at home yesterday evening?
You → Were you…?
5. We ___ late. We arrived at 9 o'clock exactly.
We → weren't.
6. Find the mistake: tap the wrong part.
Where you were on Saturday night?
Correct: Where were you on Saturday night?
Question: were before the subject.

A2 · Elementary

Was born, was / were + adjective, it was A2

  • I was born in 1998. (not *I born*)
  • Where were you born? (question)
  • The streets were empty. (were + adjective)
  • It was a long day. (it was)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I born in Shiraz. → ✅ I was born in Shiraz. (Use was born.)
  • ❌ Where did you born? → ✅ Where were you born? (Question: were you born.)
  • ❌ Was very cold yesterday. → ✅ It was very cold yesterday. (Weather needs it.)

Was / were or did? A2

With was / wereWith did
I was tired.I didn't sleep.
Were you at home?Did you go out?
She wasn't happy.She didn't like it.
Where were you?Where did you go?

  • I was hungry, so I made a sandwich. (was + adjective / past simple verb)
  • Were you at the gym? ~ No, I didn't go today. (were + place / did + verb)
  • The film wasn't bad, but I didn't love it. (both negatives)
  • Did they enjoy the trip? ~ Yes, it was fantastic. (did + verb / it was)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I was go to the park. → ✅ I went to the park. (Past of go: went (no was).)
  • ❌ Did you tired? → ✅ Were you tired? (Tired is an adjective: were.)
  • ❌ She wasn't like the food. → ✅ She didn't like the food. (Like is a verb: didn't.)

Practice: A2

7. My daughter ___ in 2019.
Use was born.
8. Where ___ (your parents / be) born?
Question: Where were your parents born?
9. Find the mistake: tap the wrong part.
Was very hot in Dubai last summer.
Correct: It was very hot in Dubai last summer.
The weather needs a subject: It was.
10. ___ you see the match last night?
See is a verb → Did you see…?
11. I ___ very busy yesterday.
Busy is an adjective → was.
12. Find the mistake: tap the wrong part.
I was buy a new phone last week.
Correct: I bought a new phone last week.
Past simple of buy: bought.
13. We ___ (not / be) at home, so we didn't hear the phone.
At home = place → weren't.

B1 · Intermediate

Were after if and wish: If I were you… B1

  • If I were you, I'd call her. (advice)
  • I wish it were the weekend. (unreal now)
  • If he were taller, he could play basketball. (imaginary situation)
  • She talks as if she were the boss. (formal style)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ If I am you, I would apologise. → ✅ If I were you, I would apologise. (Advice: If I were you.)
  • ❌ I wish I am taller. → ✅ I wish I were taller. (Unreal: were (or informal was).)

Practice: B1

14. ___, I'd take the train. It's much faster.
Advice: If I were you.
15. What does I wish it were Saturday mean?
Wish + were = an unreal present situation.
16. Find the mistake: tap the wrong part.
If I am you, I'd say sorry.
Correct: If I were you, I'd say sorry.
Advice: If I were you.
