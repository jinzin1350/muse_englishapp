Was and Were

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

Was and were are the past forms of be. Use was with I, he, she, it and were with you, we, they: I was tired. They were at home.

How to form was and were

I / he / she / ityou / we / they
PositiveI was at work.We were at work.
NegativeShe wasn't (was not) hungry.They weren't (were not) hungry.
QuestionWas he at school?Were you at school?
Short answersYes, he was. / No, he wasn't.Yes, we were. / No, we weren't.

Was and were don't use did in negatives and questions: I wasn't there. Were you there? (not Did you be there?)

When do we use was and were?

  • Past states and descriptions: The weather was beautiful.
  • Where someone was: We were at the cinema last night.
  • Age and feelings in the past: I was ten when we moved. She was happy.
  • Birth: I was born in 1995. (not I born in 1995)

There was and there were

Use there was with one thing or an uncountable noun, and there were with plural nouns: There was a big storm. There were lots of people.

Examples

  1. I was at home yesterday. (I + was)
  2. The film was really funny. (it + was)
  3. My parents were teachers. (they + were)
  4. We weren't hungry. (negative)
  5. Was it cold in Paris? (question)
  6. Where were you last night? (wh- question)
  7. She was born in Mexico. (was born)
  8. Were they at the party? – No, they weren't. (short answer)
  9. There was a big storm last week. (there was)
  10. There were lots of people on the beach. (there were)

Common mistakes

❌ I were tired.

✅ I was tired. (I → was.)

❌ They was happy.

✅ They were happy. (They → were.)

❌ Did you be at school yesterday?

✅ Were you at school yesterday? (Questions with be don't use did.)

❌ I born in 2000.

✅ I was born in 2000. (Say was born.)

❌ She didn't was at home.

✅ She wasn't at home. (Negative: wasn't. No did.)

❌ Was you at the party?

✅ Were you at the party? (You → were.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. I ___ at the gym yesterday.
Past, with I: was.
2. My friends ___ at the party last night.
My friends = they: were.
3. ___ you at home last Saturday?
Past question with you: Were you…?
4. It ___ cold yesterday. It was warm.
Negative with it: wasn't.
5. She ___ born in Brazil.
We say she was born.
6. We ___ late for the meeting.
Negative with we: weren't.
7. Was the film good? – Yes, it ___.
Short answer: Yes, it was.
8. Where ___ you last night?
You + past: were.
9. There ___ a lot of people at the concert.
Plural (people), past: there were.
10. There ___ a problem with my computer yesterday.
One thing, past: there was.
11. Choose the correct question.
Were + you…? No did.
12. Were your parents at the wedding? – No, they ___.
Short answer with they: weren't.
13. How old ___ you when you started school?
You + past: were.
14. My grandfather ___ a pilot.
My grandfather = he: was.
15. I ___ very happy with my exam results last year.
Negative with I: wasn't.
16. The shops ___ open on Sunday, so we couldn't buy anything.
The shops = they: weren't.
