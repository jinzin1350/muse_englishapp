Question Words and Question Forms

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

Question words ask for information: Where do you live? What time is it? Why are you late?

The word order is: question word + be / do / can… + subject + verb.

Question words

WordAsks aboutExample
whatthings and actionsWhat do you do?
whereplacesWhere do you live?
whentimeWhen is your birthday?
what timeclock timeWhat time does the film start?
whopeopleWho is your teacher?
whyreasons (answer: because…)Why are you sad?
howthe way something happens; how someone isHow do you get to work? How are you?
whicha choice from a small groupWhich colour do you want, red or blue?
whosewho something belongs toWhose bag is this?
how much / how manyamount / numberHow much is it? How many brothers have you got?
how old / how long / how oftenage, length of time, frequencyHow old are you? How long is the film?

Word order in questions

VerbQuestion formExample
be(question word) + am/is/are + subjectWhere are you from? Is she Italian?
present simple(question word) + do/does + subject + base verbWhat do you want? Does he work here?
past simple(question word) + did + subject + base verbWhere did you go?
can, will…(question word) + modal + subject + base verbCan you swim? When will they arrive?
present continuous(question word) + am/is/are + subject + -ingWhat are you doing?

Yes/no questions have no question word: Do you like jazz? Are they ready?

Questions about the subject

When who or what is the subject of the verb, we don't normally use do, does or did: Who lives here? What happened? Compare: Who did you see? (who is the object: you saw someone) and Who saw you? (who is the subject: someone saw you).

Prepositions at the end

In everyday English, prepositions usually go at the end of questions: Where are you from? Who are you talking to? What are you looking for?

Examples

  1. What is your name? (be)
  2. Where do your parents live? (do + base verb)
  3. When did you start learning English? (did + base verb)
  4. Who is that man? (people)
  5. Why are you laughing? (present continuous)
  6. How do you spell your surname? (the way)
  7. Which bus goes to the airport? (which is the subject: no does)
  8. Whose keys are these? (possession)
  9. How many languages can you speak? (number + can)
  10. Who called you last night? (who is the subject: no did)

Common mistakes

❌ Where you live?

✅ Where do you live? (Present simple questions need do or does.)

❌ What time the film starts?

✅ What time does the film start? (Does + subject + base verb.)

❌ Where did you went?

✅ Where did you go? (After did, use the base verb.)

❌ Who live in that house?

✅ Who lives in that house? (Who is the subject: use the normal verb form, lives.)

❌ How old you are?

✅ How old are you? (Be comes before the subject in questions.)

❌ Why you are late?

✅ Why are you late? (Be comes before the subject in questions.)

❌ Who's bag is this?

✅ Whose bag is this? (Whose = possession. Who's = who is.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. ___ do you live? – In Toronto.
A place: Where.
2. ___ are you sad? – Because I lost my phone.
A reason (because…): Why.
3. ___ is that woman? – She's my aunt.
A person: Who.
4. ___ is your birthday? – In May.
A time: When.
5. ___ bag is this? – It's Anna's.
Possession: Whose. (Who's = who is.)
6. Choose the correct question.
Question word + did + subject + base verb.
7. Where ___ you from?
Be question with you: are.
8. What time ___ the shop open?
The shop = it: does + base verb.
9. ___ do you go to the gym? – Three times a week.
Frequency: How often.
10. Who ___ in that house?
Who is the subject, so no do: Who lives…?
11. ___ colour do you prefer, red or green?
A choice from a small group: Which.
12. ___ brothers and sisters have you got?
A number of people (countable): How many.
13. Choose the correct question.
Be comes before the subject: How old are you?
14. ___ do you get to work? – By bus.
The way you travel: How.
15. ___ old is your grandmother?
Age: How old.
16. Who are you talking ___?
Talk to someone. The preposition goes at the end of the question.
