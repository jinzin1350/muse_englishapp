کلمه‌های پرسشی و ساختن سؤالQuestion Words and Question Forms

در سؤال‌های انگلیسی، فعل کمکی (be, do, have, can, will…) معمولاً قبل از فاعل می‌آید: Are you hungry? Do you like tea? Can she swim? (در فارسی ترتیب کلمه‌ها در سؤال عوض نمی‌شود، اما در انگلیسی عوض می‌شود.)

با کلمه‌های پرسشی (what, where, when…) کلمهٔ پرسشی اول می‌آید: Where do you live? این صفحه از سؤال‌های ساده شروع می‌کند و تا سؤال‌های منفی و تکراری پیش می‌رود. برای Do you know where…? صفحهٔ سؤال‌های غیرمستقیم را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

سؤال‌های بله/نه: Are you…? Do you…? Can you…? A1

be یا یک فعل کمکی (do, does, did, can, will, have) را قبل از فاعل بیاورید: Are you ready? Does she work here? Can you swim?

فعلجملهٔ خبریسؤال
beShe is Canadian.Is she Canadian?
حال سادهThey live here.Do they live here?
he / she / itHe works here.Does he work here?
گذشتهٔ سادهYou saw it.Did you see it?
فعل وجهیShe can drive.Can she drive?
حال کاملYou've been there.Have you been there?

با be و فعل‌های وجهی فقط جای کلمه‌ها را عوض کنید. با فعل‌های دیگر do / does / did اضافه کنید و شکل سادهٔ فعل را بیاورید: Does she like it? نه Does she likes it?

در جواب کوتاه، فعل کمکی تکرار می‌شود: Yes, she does. / No, I can't. / Yes, they are.

  • Are you a student? ~ Yes, I am. be
  • Do you speak English? ~ Yes, I do. do
  • Did they win the match? ~ No, they didn't. did
  • Have you finished? ~ Not yet. have

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You are hungry? (in writing) → ✅ Are you hungry? be قبل از فاعل می‌آید. (در نوشتن، سؤال را فقط با علامت سؤال نسازید.)
  • ❌ Does she likes coffee? → ✅ Does she like coffee? does + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
  • ❌ Do you can swim? → ✅ Can you swim? فعل وجهی do نمی‌خواهد.

کلمه‌های پرسشی: what, where, when, who, why, how A1

کلمهٔ پرسشی را اول بیاورید، بعد فعل کمکی و فاعل: Where do you live? What is your name? Why did she leave?

کلمهدربارهٔ چه می‌پرسدمثال
what (چه، چی)چیزها، کارهاWhat do you do?
where (کجا)جاهاWhere is the station?
when (کی، چه وقت)زمانWhen did you arrive?
who (چه کسی)آدم‌هاWho is that man?
why (چرا)دلیلWhy are you late?
how (چطور)روش، حالHow do you get to work?
which (کدام)انتخابWhich bus goes to the airport?
whose (مالِ چه کسی)صاحبWhose phone is this?

ترتیب: کلمهٔ پرسشی + فعل کمکی / be + فاعل + فعل اصلی: Where do you work? What are you doing?

  • What is your phone number? what
  • Where did you go on holiday? where + did
  • Why is the shop closed? why + be
  • Whose keys are these? whose

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Where you live? → ✅ Where do you live? do اضافه کنید.
  • ❌ What time the film starts? → ✅ What time does the film start? does + شکل سادهٔ فعل اضافه کنید.
  • ❌ Why you are sad? → ✅ Why are you sad? be قبل از فاعل می‌آید.

تمرین سطح A1

1. ___ you like pizza? ~ Yes, I love it.
حال ساده با you: Do you…?
2. ___ your sister a doctor?
سؤال با be: Is your sister…?
3. ___ (he / play) the guitar? ~ Yes, he does.
Does + فاعل + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
4. ___ is your birthday? ~ In May.
زمان: When.
5. ___ do you go to school? ~ By bus.
روش: How.
6. ___ do you live? ~ In Toronto. (one word)
جا: Where.

A2 · مقدماتی

how much, how many, how long, how often, how far A2

how + صفت / قید دربارهٔ مقدار، زمان و تکرار می‌پرسد: How much is it? How long does it take? How often do you exercise?

سؤالدربارهٔ چه می‌پرسدنمونهٔ جواب
How much…?قیمت، مقدارِ غیرقابل‌شمارشIt's $20. / A lot.
How many…?تعدادِ قابل‌شمارشThree.
How long…?مدت زمانTwo hours.
How often…?چند وقت یک بارTwice a week.
How far…?فاصلهAbout 5 km.
How old…?سنI'm 30.

How long does it take (to…)? می‌پرسد یک سفر یا کار چقدر وقت می‌گیرد: How long does it take to get to the airport? ~ It takes about 40 minutes.

how much با اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش می‌آید (money, time, water)؛ how many با اسم جمع (people, books).

  • How much does this jacket cost? قیمت
  • How many brothers and sisters do you have? تعداد
  • How long does it take to walk there? زمان
  • How often do you visit your parents? چند وقت یک بار

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ How many money do you have? → ✅ How much money do you have? غیرقابل‌شمارش: how much.
  • ❌ How long it takes to get there? → ✅ How long does it take to get there? does اضافه کنید.

Who saw you? یا Who did you see? سؤال دربارهٔ فاعل و مفعول A2

وقتی who / what خودش فاعل است، do / did نیاورید: Who called you? وقتی مفعول است، do / did بیاورید: Who did you call?

سؤال دربارهٔ فاعل (بدون do)سؤال دربارهٔ مفعول (با do)
Who called you? (کسی به شما زنگ زد.)Who did you call? (شما به کسی زنگ زدید.)
What happened?What did you do?
Who lives here?Who do you live with?

از خودتان بپرسید: آیا کلمهٔ پرسشی همان کسی یا چیزی است که کار را انجام می‌دهد؟ اگر بله، فاعل است: ترتیب عادی، بدون do: Who broke the window? ~ Tom did.

اگر کلمهٔ پرسشی کسی یا چیزی است که کار رویش انجام می‌شود، do / does / did بیاورید: Who did Tom see? ~ He saw Maria.

  • Who told you that? فاعل: کسی این را به شما گفت
  • Who did you tell? مفعول: شما به کسی گفتید
  • What happened at the meeting? فاعل: بدون do
  • Which team won the match? فاعل: بدون do

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ What did happen? → ✅ What happened? سؤال دربارهٔ فاعل: بدون did.
  • ❌ Who did break the window? (asking who did it) → ✅ Who broke the window? سؤال دربارهٔ فاعل (می‌پرسیم چه کسی این کار را کرد).
  • ❌ Who you saw at the party? → ✅ Who did you see at the party? سؤال دربارهٔ مفعول: did.

Who is she talking to? What is it like? حرف اضافه در آخر سؤال A2

در سؤال، حرف اضافه معمولاً آخر می‌آید: Who are you talking to? Where are you from? What is it like? یعنی «چطور است؟ توصیفش کن».

وقتی فعل حرف اضافه دارد (talk to, look at, come from, wait for, think about)، حرف اضافه آخر سؤال می‌آید: What are you looking at? Who is this present for? (در فارسی حرف اضافه اول می‌آید: «به چی نگاه می‌کنی؟»)

What is … like? توصیف می‌خواهد: What's your new school like? ~ It's big and modern. با Do you like…? (آیا از آن خوشت می‌آید؟) فرق دارد.

در انگلیسی خیلی رسمی، حرف اضافه می‌تواند اول بیاید: To whom did you speak? در انگلیسی معمولی Who did you speak to? خیلی رایج‌تر است.

  • Where are you from? حرف اضافه در آخر
  • What are you thinking about? think about
  • Who is the letter from? from
  • What's the weather like in Vancouver? توصیفش کن

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ From where are you? → ✅ Where are you from? حرف اضافه در آخر.
  • ❌ How is your new boss like? → ✅ What is your new boss like? What … like? (فارسی‌زبان‌ها از «چطور است؟» اشتباهی How is … like می‌سازند.)

تمرین سطح A2

7. ___ people came to the party? ~ About fifty.
قابل‌شمارش: How many.
8. ___ does it take to fly to Istanbul? ~ About four hours.
زمان: How long does it take…?
9. How ___ do you go to the gym? ~ Three times a week. (one word)
چند وقت یک بار: How often.
10. ___ the cake? ~ My sister made it.
سؤال دربارهٔ فاعل: Who made…?
11. ___ at the party? ~ I met Ali and his wife.
سؤال دربارهٔ مفعول: Who did you meet…?
12. What ___ (happen) to your car? It's damaged!
سؤال دربارهٔ فاعل: بدون did.
13. Who are you waiting ___?
Wait for ← Who are you waiting for?
14. ___ is your new flat like? ~ It's small but cosy.
توصیف: What is … like?

B1 · متوسط

what یا which؟ What kind of…? B1

which یعنی انتخاب از یک گروه کوچک و معلوم: Which colour do you want, red or blue? what یعنی انتخاب باز: What colour is your car?

وقتی گزینه‌ها محدود‌اند، معمولاً گفته شده‌اند یا معلوم‌اند، which بیاورید: Which of these bags is yours? Which way is the station, left or right?

برای سؤال باز با جواب‌های زیاد what بیاورید: What music do you like? What time is it?

which one(s)? و which of + جمع: Which one do you want? Which of your friends is coming? (نه what of)

what kind of / what sort of دربارهٔ نوع می‌پرسد: What kind of films do you like?

  • Which do you prefer, tea or coffee? دو گزینه
  • What do you want for your birthday? سؤال باز
  • Which of these photos do you like best? which of
  • What kind of job are you looking for? نوع

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ What of these is yours? → ✅ Which of these is yours? which of.
  • ❌ What one do you want? → ✅ Which one do you want? which one / which of.

تمرین سطح B1

16. ___ one do you want, the red one or the blue one?
انتخاب از دو گزینه: Which.
17. ___ of your parents do you look like?
Which of + گروه.
18. ___ kind of music do you listen to? (one word)
What kind of…?

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Negative questions: Don't you…? Isn't it…? B2

Negative questions (Don't you…? Isn't it…? Haven't you…?) show surprise, ask for agreement, or check something we expect to be true.

Negative questions are used:

  • for surprise: Don't you like chocolate? (I'm surprised.)
  • to ask for agreement: Isn't it a beautiful day? (I think it is.)
  • to check what we expect: Haven't we met before? (I think we have.)
  • in complaints: Can't you be quiet?

Answering: answer about the fact, not the question form: Don't you like coffee? ~ No, I don't. (= I don't like it) / Yes, I do. (= I like it)

In formal English, not can go after the subject: Do you not agree?

  • Don't you remember me? (surprise)
  • Isn't this view amazing? (asking for agreement)
  • Haven't you finished yet? (surprise / impatience)
  • Wouldn't it be better to wait? (suggestion)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Don't you like it? ~ No, I like it. → ✅ Don't you like it? ~ Yes, I do. / Yes, I like it. (answer about the fact)
  • ❌ Do you don't want it? → ✅ Don't you want it? (negative question: don't + you)

تمرین سطح B2

19. ___ tired? You've been working for ten hours!
Surprise: Aren't you…?
20. Don't you eat meat? ~ ___ I'm a vegetarian.
Answer about the fact: No, I don't.
21. ___ (it / not / be) a lovely evening?
Asking for agreement: Isn't it…?

C1 · پیشرفته

Echo questions and statement questions: You did what? C1

Echo questions repeat part of what someone said to show surprise or check: She's moving to Peru. ~ She's moving where? Statement questions keep normal word order with rising intonation: You're leaving?

An echo question repeats the sentence and replaces the surprising part with a question word, with strong stress: I paid $500 for it. ~ You paid how much? He married his boss. ~ He married who?

A statement question uses normal word order with rising intonation. It checks or expresses surprise about something we think we heard or understood: You've finished already? So you're not coming?

Short reply questions show interest: I've just got engaged. ~ Have you? Congratulations! See So Do I, Neither Do I.

These are spoken forms. In writing, use normal questions, except in dialogue.

  • I've sold the car. ~ You've sold what? (echo question)
  • They're getting married in Bali. ~ Where? (short echo)
  • You're not hungry? I made a huge dinner! (statement question)
  • He's 90. ~ Is he? He looks much younger. (reply question)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You paid how much? (in a formal essay) → ✅ How much did you pay? (echo questions are spoken forms)

تمرین سطح C1

22. I've eaten twelve pancakes. ~ You've eaten ___?
Echo question: replace the number with how many.
23. Which is a statement question?
Normal word order + rising intonation.
24. Ali has bought a horse. ~ He's bought a ___? (one word)
Echo question: what.
