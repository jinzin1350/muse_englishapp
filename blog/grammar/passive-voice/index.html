جملهٔ مجهولPassive Voice (It was made in China)

در جملهٔ مجهول، کسی یا چیزی که کار رویش انجام می‌شود فاعل جمله می‌شود: The bridge was built in 1932. (پل در ۱۹۳۲ ساخته شد.) لازم نیست بگوییم چه کسی آن را ساخت.

مجهول با be + اسم مفعول (قسمت سوم فعل) ساخته می‌شود، در هر زمانی: is made, was made, has been made, will be made. با جملهٔ معلوم مقایسه کنید: Engineers built the bridge in 1932.

A2 · مقدماتی

مجهولِ حال ساده: is made, are sold A2

am / is / are + اسم مفعول: Cheese is made from milk. These phones are made in Korea.

معلوممجهول
They make cars here.Cars are made here.
People speak English in Canada.English is spoken in Canada.
Someone cleans the office every day.The office is cleaned every day.

با فاعل مفرد is و با فاعل جمع are بیاورید. بعد اسم مفعول را اضافه کنید (made, spoken, cleaned).

منفی و سؤالی مثل be ساخته می‌شوند: Breakfast isn't included. Is breakfast included?

  • Rice is grown in many Asian countries. واقعیت کلی
  • The museum is visited by thousands of people every year. با by
  • These shoes are made of leather. made of (ساخته‌شده از)
  • Is this seat taken? سؤالی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ English is speak in Australia. → ✅ English is spoken in Australia. is + اسم مفعول.
  • ❌ The rooms is cleaned every day. → ✅ The rooms are cleaned every day. جمع: are.
  • ❌ Coffee grown in Brazil. → ✅ Coffee is grown in Brazil. مجهول be لازم دارد.

مجهولِ گذشتهٔ ساده: was built, were invited A2

was / were + اسم مفعول: The castle was built in 1450. We were invited to the wedding. برای گفتن اینکه چه کسی کار را کرده by اضافه کنید.

معلوممجهول
Picasso painted this picture.This picture was painted by Picasso.
They invited us to the party.We were invited to the party.
Someone stole my bike.My bike was stolen.

با I / he / she / it was و با you / we / they were بیاورید: The car was repaired. The cars were repaired.

by + شخص یا چیز را فقط وقتی بیاورید که گفتنِ انجام‌دهندهٔ کار مهم است: The telephone was invented by Bell.

  • My phone was stolen on the bus. نمی‌دانیم چه کسی
  • The Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo da Vinci. by + انجام‌دهنده
  • The match was cancelled because of the rain. دلیل
  • Were you born in Tehran? born مجهول است

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The bridge was build in 1990. → ✅ The bridge was built in 1990. was + اسم مفعول.
  • ❌ The letters was sent yesterday. → ✅ The letters were sent yesterday. جمع: were.
  • ❌ This book was written from Hafez. → ✅ This book was written by Hafez. انجام‌دهنده: by. (فارسی می‌گوید «توسط حافظ» یا «از حافظ»، ولی انگلیسی فقط by.)

تمرین سطح A2

1. Spanish ___ in Mexico.
مجهولِ حال ساده: is spoken.
2. These watches ___ in Switzerland.
فاعل جمع: are made.
3. Most of the world's cocoa ___ (produce) in West Africa.
Is + اسم مفعول.
4. The Eiffel Tower ___ in 1889.
مجهولِ گذشتهٔ ساده: was built.
5. Hamlet was written ___ Shakespeare.
انجام‌دهنده: by.
6. My car ___ (steal) last night.
Was + اسم مفعول.

B1 · متوسط

کِی مجهول به کار می‌بریم؟ و کِی by لازم است؟ B1

وقتی انجام‌دهندهٔ کار معلوم نیست، واضح است یا مهم نیست، یا وقتی می‌خواهیم روی خودِ کار یا نتیجه تمرکز کنیم، مجهول بیاورید. by را فقط وقتی بیاورید که انجام‌دهنده اطلاعات مفیدی اضافه کند.

مجهول را وقتی به کار می‌بریم که:

  • نمی‌دانیم چه کسی کار را کرده: My wallet has been stolen.
  • انجام‌دهنده واضح است یا مهم نیست: The rubbish is collected on Mondays. (معلوم است که شهرداری جمع می‌کند)
  • می‌خواهیم روی خودِ چیز تمرکز کنیم، نه روی آدم: This song was written in 1965.
  • در نوشتهٔ رسمی، علمی یا خبری: The samples were heated to 100°C.

وقتی by + انجام‌دهنده چیز تازه‌ای نمی‌گوید، حذفش کنید: The thief was arrested (نه by the police، که واضح است). وقتی مهم است نگهش دارید: The film was directed by a 19‑year-old.

در استفاده از مجهول زیاده‌روی نکنید. در صحبت روزمره، جملهٔ معلوم معمولاً طبیعی‌تر است: Someone stole my bike یا My bike was stolen هر دو خوب‌اند؛ My bike was stolen by someone طبیعی نیست.

  • Our flat was broken into last week. نمی‌دانیم چه کسی
  • The new hospital will be opened by the Prime Minister. انجام‌دهنده مهم است
  • Mistakes were made. تمرکز روی کار، نه آدم
  • The water is then filtered and bottled. یک فرایند (رسمی)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ My bag was stolen by someone. → ✅ My bag was stolen. by someone چیزی اضافه نمی‌کند.
  • ❌ The criminal was arrested by the police yesterday. (the police is obvious) → ✅ The criminal was arrested yesterday. انجام‌دهندهٔ واضح را حذف کنید (معلوم است که پلیس دستگیر می‌کند).

مجهولِ استمراری و کامل: is being done, has been done B1

استمراری: is / was being + اسم مفعول. کامل: has / had been + اسم مفعول: The road is being repaired. My bike has been stolen.

زمانشکل مجهولمثال
حال استمراریam / is / are being + اسم مفعولThe kitchen is being painted.
گذشتهٔ استمراریwas / were being + اسم مفعولWe were being followed.
حال کاملhas / have been + اسم مفعولThe flight has been cancelled.
گذشتهٔ کاملhad been + اسم مفعولThe house had been sold before we saw it.

زمان را همان‌طور انتخاب کنید که برای جملهٔ معلوم انتخاب می‌کردید، بعد با یک شکل از be + اسم مفعول مجهولش کنید.

being (استمراری) و been (کامل) را قاطی نکنید: is being built (الان در حال ساخت است) / has been built (ساخته شده و تمام شده).

  • A new school is being built near our house. الان در جریان است
  • I felt I was being watched. در گذشته در جریان بود
  • Your order has been sent. حال کامل
  • When we arrived, the food had been eaten. گذشتهٔ کامل

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The house is been painted. → ✅ The house is being painted. استمراری: being.
  • ❌ The parcel has being delivered. → ✅ The parcel has been delivered. کامل: been.

مجهولِ آینده و فعل‌های وجهی: will be done, can be done, must be done B1

بعد از will, can, must, should, might شکل be + اسم مفعول بیاورید: The results will be announced tomorrow. This can't be done.

معلوممجهول
They will announce the results.The results will be announced.
You can recycle plastic.Plastic can be recycled.
You must wear helmets.Helmets must be worn.
Someone should fix it.It should be fixed.

الگو همیشه فعل وجهی + be + اسم مفعول است: will be built, can be seen, must be paid, might be cancelled.

be going to هم همین‌طور است: The match is going to be shown on TV.

برای گذشته فعل وجهی + have been + اسم مفعول بیاورید: It should have been fixed last week. The letter might have been lost.

  • The winners will be announced on Friday. مجهولِ آینده
  • This medicine must be kept in the fridge. قانون
  • The meeting might be postponed. احتمال
  • The work should have been finished yesterday. مجهولِ وجهی در گذشته

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The money must pay by Friday. → ✅ The money must be paid by Friday. فعل وجهی + be + اسم مفعول.
  • ❌ It can be did easily. → ✅ It can be done easily. اسم مفعول: done.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Passive infinitives and -ing forms: to be done, being done B2

After verbs that take to, use to be + past participle: I want to be told. After verbs that take -ing, use being + past participle: I hate being interrupted.

PatternExample
verb + to be + ppShe expects to be promoted. The car needs to be washed.
verb / preposition + being + ppI don't like being told what to do. He's afraid of being laughed at.
perfect: to have been + ppHe's lucky to have been chosen.

Choose to be done or being done according to the verb pattern: want / hope / expect / need to be + pp; enjoy / hate / avoid / remember / after / without being + pp.

Need + -ing has the same meaning as need to be done (mainly British): The windows need cleaning. = need to be cleaned.

  • Everyone wants to be loved. (to be + pp)
  • I can't stand being kept waiting. (being + pp)
  • She left the room without being seen. (preposition + being + pp)
  • The project is expected to be finished by June. (to be + pp)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I don't like to being told what to do. → ✅ I don't like being told what to do. (like + being + pp (or to be told))
  • ❌ This shirt needs to iron. → ✅ This shirt needs to be ironed. / This shirt needs ironing. (passive meaning: to be ironed)

Get passive: get married, get paid, get hurt B2

In informal English, get + past participle can replace be in the passive, often for changes, accidents or things we don't control: He got injured. We get paid on Fridays.

Get + past participle is common in informal speech: My bike got stolen. She got promoted last month.

It's especially used for events that happen to someone (often unexpected or bad), and for changes: get hurt, get lost, get caught, get fired, get married, get dressed.

In questions and negatives, use do / did like a normal verb: Did you get invited? I didn't get paid.

Get married, get dressed, get lost are fixed and very common. In formal writing, use be: The employee was dismissed (not got fired).

  • I got stuck in traffic. (unexpected)
  • My brother got fired last week. (informal)
  • We get paid at the end of the month. (routine)
  • They got married in 2019. (fixed phrase)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He was got hurt. → ✅ He got hurt. (get + past participle (no be))
  • ❌ Did you got invited? → ✅ Did you get invited? (did + get)

Passives with two objects: I was given…, She was offered… B2

With verbs like give, offer, send, show, tell, teach, the person usually becomes the subject: I was given a present. (more natural than A present was given to me)

Some verbs have two objects: a person and a thing: They gave me a present. There are two possible passives:

  • I was given a present. (person as subject: more common)
  • A present was given to me. (thing as subject)

Common verbs: give, offer, send, show, tell, teach, promise, pay, lend, award, ask.

Some verbs can't take the person as subject: explain, describe, suggest, say: The problem was explained to me. Not I was explained the problem.

  • She was offered a job in London. (person as subject)
  • We were shown the new office. (person as subject)
  • The prize was awarded to a young scientist. (thing as subject)
  • The rules were explained to us. (explain: thing as subject only)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I was explained the instructions. → ✅ The instructions were explained to me. (explain can't have the person as subject)
  • ❌ She was given a present to. → ✅ She was given a present. (no extra to)

C1 · پیشرفته

Complex passives: elected president, looked after, it was decided C1

Passives with complements (He was elected president), prepositional verbs (She was looked after), and verbs that can't be passive (happen, arrive, have).

Verb + object + complement: the complement stays after the verb: They elected her leader. → She was elected leader. Also: was considered (a genius), was named (after), was made (captain).

Prepositional and phrasal verbs keep the preposition: They looked after the baby well. → The baby was well looked after. Nobody has dealt with the complaint. → The complaint hasn't been dealt with.

Intransitive verbs (no object) can't be passive: The accident happened at night (not was happened). Also arrive, die, exist, seem. Some state verbs aren't used in the passive: She has a car (not A car is had by her).

It + passive + that is common for decisions and agreements: It was decided that the meeting would be cancelled. For It is said that…, see Passive Reporting Structures.

  • He was named after his grandfather. (complement / preposition)
  • Our concerns were completely ignored. (focus on the result)
  • The issue is still being looked into. (phrasal verb kept)
  • It was agreed that prices would rise. (it + passive + that)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The accident was happened on Monday. → ✅ The accident happened on Monday. (happen can't be passive)
  • ❌ The children were looked well. → ✅ The children were well looked after. (keep the preposition: looked after)

تمرین سطح C1
