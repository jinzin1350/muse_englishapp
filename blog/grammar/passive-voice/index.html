Passive Voice (It was made in China)

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

In a passive sentence, the person or thing that the action happens to becomes the subject: The bridge was built in 1932. We don't need to say who built it.

The passive is made with be + past participle, in any tense: is made, was made, has been made, will be made. Compare the active: Engineers built the bridge in 1932.

A2 · Elementary

Present simple passive: is made, are sold A2

ActivePassive
They make cars here.Cars are made here.
People speak English in Canada.English is spoken in Canada.
Someone cleans the office every day.The office is cleaned every day.

  • Rice is grown in many Asian countries. (general fact)
  • The museum is visited by thousands of people every year. (with by)
  • These shoes are made of leather. (made of)
  • Is this seat taken? (question)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ English is speak in Australia. → ✅ English is spoken in Australia. (is + past participle)
  • ❌ The rooms is cleaned every day. → ✅ The rooms are cleaned every day. (plural: are)
  • ❌ Coffee grown in Brazil. → ✅ Coffee is grown in Brazil. (passive needs be)

Past simple passive: was built, were invited A2

ActivePassive
Picasso painted this picture.This picture was painted by Picasso.
They invited us to the party.We were invited to the party.
Someone stole my bike.My bike was stolen.

  • My phone was stolen on the bus. (we don't know who)
  • The Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo da Vinci. (by + agent)
  • The match was cancelled because of the rain. (reason)
  • Were you born in Tehran? (born is passive)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The bridge was build in 1990. → ✅ The bridge was built in 1990. (was + past participle)
  • ❌ The letters was sent yesterday. → ✅ The letters were sent yesterday. (plural: were)
  • ❌ This book was written from Hafez. → ✅ This book was written by Hafez. (agent: by)

B1 · Intermediate

When do we use the passive? And when do we need by? B1

  • we don't know who did it: My wallet has been stolen.
  • it's obvious or unimportant: The rubbish is collected on Mondays. (by the council, obviously)
  • we want to focus on the thing, not the person: This song was written in 1965.
  • in formal, scientific or news writing: The samples were heated to 100°C.

  • Our flat was broken into last week. (we don't know who)
  • The new hospital will be opened by the Prime Minister. (agent is important)
  • Mistakes were made. (focus on the action, not the person)
  • The water is then filtered and bottled. (process (formal))

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ My bag was stolen by someone. → ✅ My bag was stolen. (by someone adds nothing)
  • ❌ The criminal was arrested by the police yesterday. (the police is obvious) → ✅ The criminal was arrested yesterday. (leave out the obvious agent)

Continuous and perfect passives: is being done, has been done B1

TensePassive formExample
present continuousam / is / are being + ppThe kitchen is being painted.
past continuouswas / were being + ppWe were being followed.
present perfecthas / have been + ppThe flight has been cancelled.
past perfecthad been + ppThe house had been sold before we saw it.

  • A new school is being built near our house. (in progress now)
  • I felt I was being watched. (in progress in the past)
  • Your order has been sent. (present perfect)
  • When we arrived, the food had been eaten. (past perfect)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The house is been painted. → ✅ The house is being painted. (continuous: being)
  • ❌ The parcel has being delivered. → ✅ The parcel has been delivered. (perfect: been)

Future and modal passives: will be done, can be done, must be done B1

ActivePassive
They will announce the results.The results will be announced.
You can recycle plastic.Plastic can be recycled.
You must wear helmets.Helmets must be worn.
Someone should fix it.It should be fixed.

  • The winners will be announced on Friday. (future passive)
  • This medicine must be kept in the fridge. (rule)
  • The meeting might be postponed. (possibility)
  • The work should have been finished yesterday. (past modal passive)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The money must pay by Friday. → ✅ The money must be paid by Friday. (modal + be + past participle)
  • ❌ It can be did easily. → ✅ It can be done easily. (past participle: done)

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Passive infinitives and -ing forms: to be done, being done B2

PatternExample
verb + to be + ppShe expects to be promoted. The car needs to be washed.
verb / preposition + being + ppI don't like being told what to do. He's afraid of being laughed at.
perfect: to have been + ppHe's lucky to have been chosen.

  • Everyone wants to be loved. (to be + pp)
  • I can't stand being kept waiting. (being + pp)
  • She left the room without being seen. (preposition + being + pp)
  • The project is expected to be finished by June. (to be + pp)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I don't like to being told what to do. → ✅ I don't like being told what to do. (like + being + pp (or to be told))
  • ❌ This shirt needs to iron. → ✅ This shirt needs to be ironed. / This shirt needs ironing. (passive meaning: to be ironed)

Get passive: get married, get paid, get hurt B2

  • I got stuck in traffic. (unexpected)
  • My brother got fired last week. (informal)
  • We get paid at the end of the month. (routine)
  • They got married in 2019. (fixed phrase)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He was got hurt. → ✅ He got hurt. (get + past participle (no be))
  • ❌ Did you got invited? → ✅ Did you get invited? (did + get)

Passives with two objects: I was given…, She was offered… B2

  • I was given a present. (person as subject: more common)
  • A present was given to me. (thing as subject)

  • She was offered a job in London. (person as subject)
  • We were shown the new office. (person as subject)
  • The prize was awarded to a young scientist. (thing as subject)
  • The rules were explained to us. (explain: thing as subject only)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I was explained the instructions. → ✅ The instructions were explained to me. (explain can't have the person as subject)
  • ❌ She was given a present to. → ✅ She was given a present. (no extra to)

C1 · Advanced

Complex passives: elected president, looked after, it was decided C1

  • He was named after his grandfather. (complement / preposition)
  • Our concerns were completely ignored. (focus on the result)
  • The issue is still being looked into. (phrasal verb kept)
  • It was agreed that prices would rise. (it + passive + that)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The accident was happened on Monday. → ✅ The accident happened on Monday. (happen can't be passive)
  • ❌ The children were looked well. → ✅ The children were well looked after. (keep the preposition: looked after)
