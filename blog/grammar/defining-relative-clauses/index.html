جمله‌واره‌های موصولی محدودکنندهDefining Relative Clauses (the man who…, the book that…)

جمله‌وارهٔ موصولیِ تعریفی به ما می‌گوید دربارهٔ کدام آدم یا چیز حرف می‌زنیم (مثل «مردی که به تو زنگ زد»): The man who called you is my uncle. بدون این جمله‌واره، جمله کامل یا روشن نیست.

جمله‌واره‌های تعریفی ویرگول نمی‌گیرند. برای جمله‌واره‌هایی که اطلاعات اضافه می‌دهند (My uncle, who is a doctor, …) صفحهٔ جمله‌واره‌های موصولیِ غیرتعریفی را ببینید.

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

who, which و that A2

who برای آدم‌ها، which برای چیزها، that برای هر دو: the girl who / that won، the car which / that I bought. (هر سه معادل «که» در فارسی‌اند.)

ضمیر موصولیبرایمثال
whoآدم‌هاThe woman who works here is very kind.
whichچیزها، حیوان‌هاThe bus which goes to the airport is the 10.
thatآدم‌ها یا چیزها (خودمانی)The man that called you… / The bus that goes…

ضمیر موصولی جای یک کلمه را می‌گیرد. آن کلمه را تکرار نکنید: The man who he lives next door ✗ ← The man who lives next door ✓. (فارسی گاهی ضمیر را تکرار می‌کند: «مردی که او کنار ما زندگی می‌کند»؛ انگلیسی نه.)

جمله‌وارهٔ موصولیِ تعریفی ویرگول نمی‌گیرد: The book that you gave me is great.

  • I know a girl who speaks five languages. آدم‌ها: who
  • Where's the cake that was in the fridge? چیزها: that
  • The film which we saw last night was boring. چیزها: which
  • People who exercise live longer. آدم‌ها: who

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The man which lives next door is a doctor. → ✅ The man who lives next door is a doctor. آدم‌ها: who / that.
  • ❌ The phone who I bought is broken. → ✅ The phone that / which I bought is broken. چیزها: which / that.
  • ❌ The woman who she called me is my aunt. → ✅ The woman who called me is my aunt. فاعل را تکرار نکنید.

حذف who, which یا that: the people we met A2

وقتی ضمیر موصولی مفعولِ جمله‌واره است، می‌توانید حذفش کنید: the book (that) I'm reading, the people (who) we met.

به کلمهٔ بعد از ضمیر نگاه کنید. اگر فاعل است (I, we, she, the teacher…)، ضمیر مفعول است و می‌توانید حذفش کنید: The film (that) we saw was great.

اگر ضمیر خودش فاعل است (یعنی بلافاصله بعدش فعل آمده)، نمی‌توانید حذفش کنید: The man who called you… (نه The man called you… که معنی دیگری دارد).

  • The cake (that) you made was delicious. مفعول: می‌شود حذف کرد
  • The people (who) we met on holiday were lovely. مفعول: می‌شود حذف کرد
  • The man who lives next door is a pilot. فاعل: نمی‌شود حذف کرد
  • Is this the jacket (which) you wanted? مفعول: می‌شود حذف کرد

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The woman lives next door is a doctor. → ✅ The woman who lives next door is a doctor. فاعل: who را نگه دارید.
  • ❌ The book I'm reading it is great. → ✅ The book I'm reading is great. it اضافه نکنید.

تمرین سطح A2

1. The boy ___ won the race is my cousin.
آدم‌ها: who (یا that).
2. I lost the watch ___ my father gave me.
چیزها: that (یا which).
3. Do you know the woman ___ is talking to Ali? (one word)
آدم‌ها: who یا that.
4. در کدام جمله می‌توانید that را حذف کنید؟
That I bought: ضمیر مفعول است.
5. کدام جمله درست است؟
ضمیر را می‌شود حذف کرد (مفعول است)، و it نمی‌آید.
6. The people ___ work in this office are very friendly. (one word)
ضمیر فاعل است: نمی‌شود حذفش کرد.

B1 · متوسط

whose, where و when B1

whose یعنی «که … اش» (the man whose car was stolen، مردی که ماشینش دزدیده شد). where یعنی «که آنجا» (the town where I grew up). when برای زمان (the day when we met).

کلمهجای چه چیزی می‌آیدمثال
whosehis / her / their / itsThe girl whose bag was stolen called the police.
wherein / at which (مکان)This is the house where I was born.
whenزمانI remember the day when we first met.

بعد از whose یک اسم می‌آید: the man whose wife is a judge. برای آدم‌ها و گاهی چیزها به کار می‌رود: a company whose products are famous.

where را نمی‌شود حذف کرد، اما می‌توانید which / that … in بیاورید: the house where I live = the house (that) I live in.

when بعد از the day, the year, the time می‌تواند حذف شود: the day (when) we met.

  • That's the man whose dog bit me. whose + اسم
  • The café where we first met has closed. مکان
  • Summer is the time when most people travel. زمان
  • I have a friend whose father is a famous actor. whose

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The man who's car was stolen called the police. → ✅ The man whose car was stolen called the police. whose (نه who's که یعنی who is).
  • ❌ The hotel which we stayed was lovely. → ✅ The hotel where we stayed was lovely. / The hotel which we stayed at was lovely. مکان: where یا which … at.

حرف اضافه در جمله‌وارهٔ موصولی: the house I live in B1

در انگلیسی روزمره، حرف اضافه آخر می‌آید: the house I live in, the person I spoke to. در انگلیسی رسمی: the person to whom I spoke.

خیلی از فعل‌ها و صفت‌ها حرف اضافه دارند (live in, talk to, look for, interested in). در جمله‌وارهٔ موصولی، حرف اضافه معمولاً بعد از فعل می‌ماند: That's the man I was talking to. Is this the job you applied for?

ضمیر اغلب حذف می‌شود: the music (that) I listen to.

در انگلیسی رسمی، حرف اضافه می‌تواند اول بیاید و بعدش which (برای چیزها) یا whom (برای آدم‌ها): the house in which I live، the person to whom I spoke. هیچ‌وقت بعد از حرف اضافه that یا who نیاورید.

  • That's the girl I told you about. حرف اضافه در آخر
  • Is this the book you were looking for? حرف اضافه در آخر
  • The hotel we stayed at was by the sea. حرف اضافه در آخر
  • The committee to which we applied rejected the plan. رسمی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The person to who I spoke was rude. → ✅ The person (who) I spoke to was rude. / The person to whom I spoke was rude. حرف اضافه + whom (رسمی).
  • ❌ The house in that I live is old. → ✅ The house (that) I live in is old. / The house in which I live is old. حرف اضافه + that نه.

تمرین سطح B1

7. That's the woman ___ son is in my class.
مالکیت: whose + اسم.
8. This is the restaurant ___ we had our first date.
مکان: where.
9. Do you remember the year ___ we moved to Canada? (one word)
زمان: when (یا that).
10. This is the flat ___.
حرف اضافه در آخر.
11. Formal: The person ___ you should address your letter is Dr Smith.
رسمی: حرف اضافه + whom.
12. Who was the man you were talking ___? (one word)
Talk to: حرف اضافه در آخر.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Who or whom? B2

whom is the formal object form of who: the man whom I met. In everyday English, people use who or that, or leave it out. After a preposition, always whom.

Whom is used for the object of the relative clause in formal English: The candidate whom we interviewed was excellent. In normal speech: The candidate (who / that) we interviewed…

After a preposition, whom is the only choice: the people with whom I work. (Informal: the people (who) I work with)

Never use whom as the subject: the man who called (not the man whom called).

  • The doctor whom I saw was very kind. (formal object)
  • The doctor (who) I saw was very kind. (everyday)
  • The colleague with whom I share an office is on holiday. (after a preposition)
  • The woman who called you is here. (subject: who)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The man whom lives next door is noisy. → ✅ The man who lives next door is noisy. (subject: who)
  • ❌ The friend with who I travelled was ill. → ✅ The friend with whom I travelled was ill. (after a preposition: whom)

What or that? Everything that, all that, what you need B2

what = the thing(s) that. Don't use what after a noun or after everything, all, something, nothing: use that: Everything that he said was true.

What is not a normal relative pronoun. It means the thing that, so it has no noun before it: What you need is a holiday. = The thing that you need…

After a noun or after all, everything, something, anything, nothing, the only thing, use that (or leave it out): Is there anything (that) I can do? All (that) I want is some sleep.

  • What I love about Toronto is the people. (= the thing that)
  • Everything that you told me was true. (not everything what)
  • The only thing (that) I need is a coffee. (not the only thing what)
  • She didn't understand what I said. (= the thing that)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Everything what he said was a lie. → ✅ Everything (that) he said was a lie. (after everything: that)
  • ❌ The thing what I like most is the music. → ✅ The thing that I like most is the music. / What I like most is the music. (after a noun: that)

Reduced relative clauses: the woman talking to Tom, a car made in Japan B2

Shorten relative clauses with -ing (active: the man sitting there = who is sitting) or a past participle (passive: the car stolen yesterday = which was stolen).

Full clauseReduced clause
The woman who is talking to Tom is my aunt.The woman talking to Tom is my aunt.
Anyone who wants to come is welcome.Anyone wanting to come is welcome.
The car which was stolen yesterday was found.The car stolen yesterday was found.
Phones that are made in Korea…Phones made in Korea…

Use -ing for an active meaning (the noun does the action) and the past participle for a passive meaning (something is done to the noun).

Don't use -ing for a single completed action in the past: The man who broke the window ran away (not The man breaking the window ran away, which means he was in the middle of breaking it).

  • Who's the boy wearing the red hat? (active: who is wearing)
  • Most of the people invited to the party came. (passive: who were invited)
  • Students wishing to join should email the club. (active: who wish)
  • The new bridge, built in 2020, is very modern. (passive (non-defining))

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The man stood at the door is my father. → ✅ The man standing at the door is my father. (active: -ing)
  • ❌ A letter writing in English is easier to read. → ✅ A letter written in English is easier to read. (passive: past participle)

تمرین سطح B2

Formal object: whom (or who / that).

= the thing that: what.

After anything: that (or nothing).

C1 · پیشرفته

The first to arrive, nothing to do, the reason why, the way C1

Infinitive relative clauses: the first / last / only / next + to + verb (the first person to arrive), something / nothing + to + verb (nothing to do). Also the reason (why / that) and the way (that / in which).

After the first, the last, the only, the next and superlatives, a to-infinitive can replace a relative clause: She was the first woman to win the prize. (= who won)

After something, anything, nothing, a lot, somewhere and some nouns, to + verb shows purpose or possibility: There's nothing to eat. I need somewhere to stay. Is there anything to drink?

The reason (why / that) and the way (that / in which): That's the reason (why) I left. I like the way (that) she talks. Don't say the way how.

  • Neil Armstrong was the first person to walk on the Moon. (the first + to)
  • I've got a lot of work to do today. (noun + to)
  • Is there anywhere to park near here? (anywhere + to)
  • The reason (why) I called is to say thank you. (the reason why)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She was the first woman who to fly across the Atlantic. → ✅ She was the first woman to fly across the Atlantic. (the first + to)
  • ❌ I don't like the way how he speaks. → ✅ I don't like the way (that) he speaks. (not the way how)

تمرین سطح C1

22. He was the last person ___ the building.
The last + to + verb.
23. I'm bored. There's nothing ___.
Nothing + to + verb.
24. Is there anything ___ (eat) in the fridge?
Anything + to + verb.
