Can and Can't

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

We use can to talk about ability (I can swim), permission (You can sit here) and requests (Can you help me?).

The negative is can't (cannot).

How to form can and can't

FormExample
Positivesubject + can + base verbShe can speak three languages.
Negativesubject + can't / cannot + base verbI can't drive.
QuestionCan + subject + base verb?Can you cook?
Short answersYes, + subject + can. / No, + subject + can't.Yes, I can. / No, he can't.

Can is the same for every person: I can, he can, they can. No -s and no to: He can play (not He cans play or He can to play).

Cannot is usually written as one word.

What do we use can for?

  • Ability: My son can ride a bike. I can't swim very well.
  • Permission: Can I open the window? – Yes, of course you can.
  • Requests: Can you pass the salt, please?
  • Possibility: You can buy tickets online.

Could you…? is a more polite request: Could you help me, please?

How to pronounce can and can't

In positive sentences, can is usually short and weak (it sounds like kn): I can swim. Can't is strong and clear: I CAN'T swim. In American English can't sounds like kant; in British English it often sounds like kahnt.

Can in the past: could

For ability in the past, use could / couldn't: I could read when I was four. More practice: Could for Past Ability.

Examples

  1. I can play the guitar. (ability)
  2. She can't come to the party. (negative)
  3. Can you speak Spanish? – Yes, I can. (question + short answer)
  4. My little brother can't read yet. (ability)
  5. Can I use your phone? (permission)
  6. You can't park here. (not allowed)
  7. Can you open the door for me, please? (request)
  8. We can meet tomorrow if you like. (possibility)
  9. Penguins can't fly, but they can swim very well. (ability)
  10. What languages can you speak? (wh- question)

Common mistakes

❌ He cans swim.

✅ He can swim. (Can never takes -s.)

❌ I can to drive.

✅ I can drive. (No to after can.)

❌ She can speaks French.

✅ She can speak French. (Can + base verb.)

❌ Do you can help me?

✅ Can you help me? (Questions: can + subject. No do.)

❌ I don't can come.

✅ I can't come. (Negative: can't. No don't.)

❌ Can I to use your phone?

✅ Can I use your phone? (No to after can.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. My sister ___ speak four languages.
Can + base verb. No -s, no to.
2. I ___ swim. I never learned.
No ability: can't.
3. ___ you play the piano?
Ability question: Can + subject + base verb?
4. Can you ride a bike? – Yes, I ___.
Short answer: Yes, I can.
5. He can ___ very fast.
Can + base verb: run.
6. ___ I sit here? – Yes, of course.
Asking for permission: Can I…?
7. You ___ smoke in this building. It's not allowed.
Not allowed: can't.
8. Choose the correct sentence.
Can + subject + base verb. No do, no to.
9. Penguins ___ fly.
Penguins aren't able to fly: can't.
10. ___ you pass me the salt, please?
A request: Can you…?
11. Can she drive? – No, she ___.
Short answer: No, she can't.
12. We ___ meet tomorrow if you want.
Possibility: can + base verb.
13. What languages ___ you speak?
Wh- question with ability: question word + can + subject + base verb.
14. I'm sorry, I ___ come to your party.
Negative: can't + base verb.
15. Which word is the negative of *can*?
Cannot (one word), or the short form can't.
16. ___ I use your phone, please? Mine is dead.
Asking for permission: Can I…?
