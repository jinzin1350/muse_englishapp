Possessive 's

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

We use 's after a person or animal to show that something belongs to them: This is Tom's car. (= the car of Tom) My sister's name is Ana.

How to form the possessive

NounAddExample
singular noun'smy brother's room, the dog's bed
plural noun ending in -s' (only an apostrophe)my parents' house, the students' books
irregular plural (no -s)'sthe children's toys, women's clothes, people's opinions
name ending in -s's or 'James's car / James' car

When two people share something, add 's to the second name only: Tom and Anna's flat (one flat).

Things: usually of

For things, we usually use of: the end of the film, the top of the page. With places, organisations and time, 's is common: Canada's capital, the company's website, today's lesson, a week's holiday.

's can mean is, has or possession

  • She's happy. = she is
  • He's got a car. = he has
  • That's Sam's bike. = possession

Whose?

Ask with whose: Whose bag is this? – It's Lucy's. We can leave out the noun when it's clear: It's Sam's. We also use 's for shops and homes: at the doctor's, at my grandmother's.

Examples

  1. This is Maria's phone. (name + 's)
  2. My brother's wife is a nurse. (singular noun + 's)
  3. The children's bedroom is upstairs. (irregular plural + 's)
  4. My parents' house has a big garden. (plural -s + ')
  5. Today's lesson is about food. (time + 's)
  6. I'm staying at my aunt's this weekend. (= at my aunt's house)
  7. Tom and Lisa's wedding was beautiful. (two people, one wedding)
  8. Is this your sister's coat? (question)
  9. The students' results were excellent. (plural -s + ')
  10. Whose keys are these? – They're Dad's. (noun left out)

Common mistakes

❌ This is the car of my father.

✅ This is my father's car. (For people, use 's. The car of my father sounds unnatural.)

❌ My parent's house is big. (I have two parents.)

✅ My parents' house is big. (Plural noun ending in -s: add only an apostrophe.)

❌ The childrens' toys are everywhere.

✅ The children's toys are everywhere. (Children is already plural: add 's.)

❌ Toms bike is red.

✅ Tom's bike is red. (Don't forget the apostrophe.)

❌ It's Annas bag.

✅ It's Anna's bag. (Name + 's.)

❌ I like the film's of end.

✅ I like the end of the film. (For things, use of.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. This is ___ bike.
Name + 's: Tom's bike.
2. ___ phone is this? – It's Anna's.
Asking about possession: Whose. (Who's = who is.)
3. My ___ house is near the beach. (I have two parents.)
Plural ending in -s: add only ': parents'.
4. The ___ toys are all over the floor.
Irregular plural (no -s) + 's: children's.
5. She's ___ sister.
Name + 's: David's.
6. My ___ name is Leo. He's ten.
One brother: brother's.
7. What does 's mean in 'She's tired'?
She's tired = she is tired.
8. We're having dinner at my ___ tonight. She's a great cook.
= at my grandmother's house: grandmother's.
9. The ___ results were excellent. All 30 students passed.
Many students: plural + ': students'.
10. Tom and ___ flat is very small.
Two people, one flat: 's on the second name, Lisa's.
11. Choose the correct sentence.
For things, use of: the end of the film.
12. ___ lesson is about the weather.
Time + 's: Today's lesson.
13. These are the ___ coats. (the coats of the women)
Irregular plural + 's: women's.
14. The ___ bowl is empty. Can you fill it? (We have one dog.)
One dog: dog's.
15. What does 's mean in 'He's got a new car'?
He's got = he has got.
16. Is this ___ coat? It's not mine.
One sister + 's: your sister's coat.
