مالکیت با آپاستروف و sPossessive 's

Add 's to a person to show that something belongs to them: This is Sara's bag. For plural nouns ending in -s, add just an apostrophe: my parents' house.

For possessive words like my, his, mine and hers, see Possessive Pronouns.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

's and s': my sister's car, my parents' house A1

Add 's to a person or animal to show possession: Ali's phone, my sister's car. For regular plurals ending in -s, add only an apostrophe: my parents' house. Irregular plurals take 's: the children's toys.

NounPossessive
singular: my sistermy sister's car
regular plural: my parentsmy parents' house
irregular plural: the children, men, womenthe children's room, men's clothes
name: SaraSara's bag

The thing that is owned comes after the possessive: Ali's book (not the book of Ali).

Don't confuse possessive 's with 's = is or has: Ali's tired (= Ali is tired). Ali's car (possessive).

  • This is my brother's bedroom. (singular)
  • My parents' house is near the sea. (regular plural)
  • The children's toys are everywhere. (irregular plural)
  • Is this Maryam's coat? (name)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ This is the car of my father. → ✅ This is my father's car. (people: 's)
  • ❌ My parent's house is big. (two parents) → ✅ My parents' house is big. (plural: s')
  • ❌ The childrens' room is upstairs. → ✅ The children's room is upstairs. (irregular plural: 's)

تمرین سطح A1

1. This is ___ phone.
Name + 's.
2. My two sisters share a room. ___ room is very small.
Regular plural: apostrophe only.
3. The ___ (children) playground is next to the school. (one word)
Irregular plural: + 's.

A2 · مقدماتی

At the doctor's, Ali and Sara's house A2

Use 's for shops and homes without the noun: at the doctor's, at my aunt's. When two people share something, add 's to the second name: Ali and Sara's flat. For separate things, add it to both: Ali's and Sara's phones.

UseExample
shops and businessesI'm going to the hairdresser's / the dentist's / the chemist's.
someone's homeWe had dinner at my aunt's. (= at my aunt's house)
sharedAli and Sara's wedding (one wedding)
separateAli's and Sara's cars (two different cars)
names ending in -sJames's car or James' car (both correct)

In American English, people often say the dentist, the doctor without 's. Both are correct.

  • I've got an appointment at the dentist's. (place)
  • We stayed at my cousin's for a week. (home)
  • Reza and Mina's baby was born yesterday. (shared)
  • Chris's brother is a pilot. (name in -s)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Ali's and Sara's wedding was lovely. (one wedding) → ✅ Ali and Sara's wedding was lovely. (shared: 's on the second name)
  • ❌ We met at my grandma. (at her house) → ✅ We met at my grandma's. (home: 's)

تمرین سطح A2

4. We spent Christmas at ___.
Someone's home: at my aunt's.
5. Mina and Reza got married last year. ___ wedding was beautiful.
Shared (one wedding): 's on the second name.
6. I'm going to the ___ (dentist) tomorrow. I have toothache. (one word)
Place: the dentist's (AmE the dentist).

B1 · متوسط

's or of? My friend's name, the end of the film B1

Use 's mostly for people and animals (my friend's name). Use of for parts of things (the end of the street, the top of the page). Both are possible with places and organisations (Canada's capital / the capital of Canada).

'sof
people: my mother's jobparts of things: the top of the page, the back of the room
animals: the cat's tailthe middle of the night, the end of the film
places, organisations: Toronto's mayor, the company's profitsthe mayor of Toronto, the profits of the company
short, known ownerlong description: the son of the woman who lives next door

With things, of or a compound noun is usually more natural: the end of the street, the car door, the kitchen window. With top, bottom, back, front, middle, end, use of: the back of the room (not the room's back).

With a long description of the person, use of: the phone number of the man I met at the party.

  • What's your brother's name? (person)
  • I didn't understand the end of the film. (part of a thing)
  • Canada's capital is Ottawa. (place)
  • Write your name at the top of the page. (part)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I woke up in the night's middle. → ✅ I woke up in the middle of the night. (parts: of)
  • ❌ Let's sit at the room's back. → ✅ Let's sit at the back of the room. (parts: of)

تمرین سطح B1

7. Please write your name at ___.
Parts of things: the top of.
8. What's ___?
People: 's.
9. The phone rang in the middle ___ the night. (one word)
Parts: the middle of.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Today's news, a week's holiday, a friend of mine B2

Use 's / s' with time expressions: today's news, last week's meeting, a week's holiday, two hours' drive, in ten minutes' time. Use a friend of mine or a friend of my sister's to mean one of my friends.

PatternExamples
time: whentoday's newspaper, yesterday's meeting, next month's exam
time: how longa week's holiday, an hour's drive, two weeks' notice, in five minutes' time
of + possessive pronouna friend of mine, a cousin of hers
of + noun + 'sa colleague of my father's

With plural time words ending in -s, add only an apostrophe: two weeks' holiday, three days' walk.

A friend of mine = one of my friends (I have more than one). My friend often means a particular friend the listener knows about.

A compound adjective with a hyphen is another common pattern: a two-hour drive, a ten-minute walk. See Noun Modifiers and Compound Nouns.

  • Did you see yesterday's match? (when)
  • We had a week's holiday in Spain. (how long)
  • The hotel is ten minutes' walk from the beach. (plural: s')
  • A friend of mine works at Google. (one of my friends)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I read it in the today newspaper. → ✅ I read it in today's newspaper. (time: 's)
  • ❌ She's a friend of me. → ✅ She's a friend of mine. (of + possessive pronoun)
  • ❌ two weeks holiday → ✅ two weeks' holiday (plural time: s')

تمرین سطح B2

10. Have you read ___ newspaper?
Time: today's.
11. He's a colleague ___.
A colleague of mine.
12. The station is about ten ___ (minute) walk from here. (one word)
Plural time: minutes'.

C1 · پیشرفته

Group possessives, else's, its and one's C1

's can attach to a whole noun phrase: the King of Spain's visit, someone else's bag. In formal writing, prefer of with long phrases. Possessive pronouns have no apostrophe: its, hers, ours, theirs.

PatternExample
whole phrase + 'sthe Prime Minister of Canada's speech, my brother-in-law's car
elsesomeone else's coat, everybody else's ideas
its vs it'sThe company changed its name. / It's (= it is) late.
one'sIt's important to know one's rights. (formal)
long phrases in speechthe man next door's dog (informal) → the dog that belongs to the man next door (writing)

The 's goes at the end of the whole phrase, not on the main noun: the head of department's office (not the head's of department office).

In formal writing, long 's phrases can sound clumsy. Use of or rephrase: the speech of the Prime Minister of Canada or the Canadian Prime Minister's speech.

Possessive pronouns never have an apostrophe: its, hers, ours, yours, theirs.

  • That's someone else's problem. (else's)
  • My mother-in-law's cooking is amazing. (compound + 's)
  • The committee published its report. (its)
  • The Queen of Denmark's visit was a success. (whole phrase + 's)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ That's somebody's else bag. → ✅ That's somebody else's bag. ('s after else)
  • ❌ The dog wagged it's tail. → ✅ The dog wagged its tail. (possessive: its)
  • ❌ Their's is the white house. → ✅ Theirs is the white house. (possessive pronouns: no apostrophe)

تمرین سطح C1

13. This isn't my coat. It's ___.
Someone else's.
14. The bird hurt ___ wing.
Possessive: its (no apostrophe).
15. My ___ (brother-in-law) car is a Tesla. (one word)
Compound noun: 's at the end.
