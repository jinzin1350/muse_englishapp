مالکیت با آپاستروف و sPossessive 's

برای اینکه نشان دهید چیزی مالِ کسی است، به اسم آن شخص 's اضافه کنید: This is Sara's bag. (کیفِ سارا). برای اسم‌های جمعی که به ‑s ختم می‌شوند فقط آپاستروف بگذارید: my parents' house.

برای کلمه‌های ملکی مثل my, his, mine و hers صفحهٔ ضمیرهای ملکی را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

's و s': my sister's car, my parents' house A1

برای نشان دادن مالکیت به اسم آدم یا حیوان 's اضافه کنید: Ali's phone, my sister's car. برای جمع‌های باقاعده که به ‑s ختم می‌شوند فقط آپاستروف بگذارید: my parents' house. جمع‌های بی‌قاعده 's می‌گیرند: the children's toys.

اسمشکل ملکی
مفرد: my sistermy sister's car
جمع باقاعده: my parentsmy parents' house
جمع بی‌قاعده: the children, men, womenthe children's room, men's clothes
اسم شخص: SaraSara's bag

چیزی که مال کسی است بعد از شکل ملکی می‌آید: Ali's book (نه the book of Ali). ترتیب برعکس فارسی است: «کتابِ علی» ← Ali's book.

's ملکی را با 's به معنی is یا has قاطی نکنید: Ali's tired (یعنی Ali is tired). Ali's car (ملکی).

  • This is my brother's bedroom. مفرد
  • My parents' house is near the sea. جمع باقاعده
  • The children's toys are everywhere. جمع بی‌قاعده
  • Is this Maryam's coat? اسم شخص

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ This is the car of my father. → ✅ This is my father's car. برای آدم‌ها: 's.
  • ❌ My parent's house is big. (two parents) → ✅ My parents' house is big. جمع: s' (دو والد).
  • ❌ The childrens' room is upstairs. → ✅ The children's room is upstairs. جمع بی‌قاعده: 's.

تمرین سطح A1

1. This is ___ phone.
اسم شخص + 's.
2. My two sisters share a room. ___ room is very small.
جمع باقاعده: فقط آپاستروف.
3. The ___ (children) playground is next to the school. (one word)
جمع بی‌قاعده: + 's.

A2 · مقدماتی

at the doctor's، Ali and Sara's house A2

برای مغازه‌ها و خانهٔ کسی می‌توانید 's را بدون اسم بیاورید: at the doctor's, at my aunt's (مطبِ دکتر، خانهٔ خاله). وقتی دو نفر در چیزی شریک‌اند، 's را به اسم دوم اضافه کنید: Ali and Sara's flat. برای چیزهای جدا، به هر دو اسم: Ali's and Sara's phones.

کاربردمثال
مغازه‌ها و محل کارI'm going to the hairdresser's / the dentist's / the chemist's.
خانهٔ کسیWe had dinner at my aunt's. (یعنی خانهٔ خاله / عمه‌ام)
مشترکAli and Sara's wedding (یک عروسی)
جداAli's and Sara's cars (دو ماشین جدا)
اسم‌هایی که به ‑s ختم می‌شوندJames's car یا James' car (هر دو درست)

در انگلیسی آمریکایی اغلب the dentist, the doctor را بدون 's می‌گویند. هر دو درست است.

  • I've got an appointment at the dentist's. مکان
  • We stayed at my cousin's for a week. خانه
  • Reza and Mina's baby was born yesterday. مشترک
  • Chris's brother is a pilot. اسمِ ختم به ‑s

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Ali's and Sara's wedding was lovely. (one wedding) → ✅ Ali and Sara's wedding was lovely. مشترک: 's روی اسم دوم (یک عروسی).
  • ❌ We met at my grandma. (at her house) → ✅ We met at my grandma's. خانهٔ کسی: 's.

تمرین سطح A2

4. We spent Christmas at ___.
خانهٔ کسی: at my aunt's.
5. Mina and Reza got married last year. ___ wedding was beautiful.
مشترک (یک عروسی): 's روی اسم دوم.
6. I'm going to the ___ (dentist) tomorrow. I have toothache. (one word)
مکان: the dentist's (در انگلیسی آمریکایی the dentist).

B1 · متوسط

's یا of؟ my friend's name, the end of the film B1

's را بیشتر برای آدم‌ها و حیوان‌ها بیاورید (my friend's name). of را برای بخش‌های یک چیز (the end of the street, the top of the page). با مکان‌ها و سازمان‌ها هر دو ممکن است (Canada's capital / the capital of Canada).

'sof
آدم‌ها: my mother's jobبخش‌های یک چیز: the top of the page, the back of the room
حیوان‌ها: the cat's tailthe middle of the night, the end of the film
مکان‌ها، سازمان‌ها: Toronto's mayor, the company's profitsthe mayor of Toronto, the profits of the company
صاحبِ کوتاه و معلومتوصیف طولانی: the son of the woman who lives next door

برای چیزها معمولاً of یا یک اسم مرکب طبیعی‌تر است: the end of the street, the car door, the kitchen window. با top, bottom, back, front, middle, end حرف of بیاورید: the back of the room (نه the room's back).

وقتی توصیف آن شخص طولانی است، of بیاورید: the phone number of the man I met at the party.

  • What's your brother's name? آدم
  • I didn't understand the end of the film. بخشی از یک چیز
  • Canada's capital is Ottawa. مکان
  • Write your name at the top of the page. بخشی از یک چیز

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I woke up in the night's middle. → ✅ I woke up in the middle of the night. بخش‌ها: of.
  • ❌ Let's sit at the room's back. → ✅ Let's sit at the back of the room. بخش‌ها: of.

تمرین سطح B1

7. Please write your name at ___.
بخش‌های یک چیز: the top of.
8. What's ___?
آدم‌ها: 's.
9. The phone rang in the middle ___ the night. (one word)
بخش‌ها: the middle of.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Today's news, a week's holiday, a friend of mine B2

Use 's / s' with time expressions: today's news, last week's meeting, a week's holiday, two hours' drive, in ten minutes' time. Use a friend of mine or a friend of my sister's to mean one of my friends.

PatternExamples
time: whentoday's newspaper, yesterday's meeting, next month's exam
time: how longa week's holiday, an hour's drive, two weeks' notice, in five minutes' time
of + possessive pronouna friend of mine, a cousin of hers
of + noun + 'sa colleague of my father's

With plural time words ending in -s, add only an apostrophe: two weeks' holiday, three days' walk.

A friend of mine = one of my friends (I have more than one). My friend often means a particular friend the listener knows about.

A compound adjective with a hyphen is another common pattern: a two-hour drive, a ten-minute walk. See Noun Modifiers and Compound Nouns.

  • Did you see yesterday's match? (when)
  • We had a week's holiday in Spain. (how long)
  • The hotel is ten minutes' walk from the beach. (plural: s')
  • A friend of mine works at Google. (one of my friends)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I read it in the today newspaper. → ✅ I read it in today's newspaper. (time: 's)
  • ❌ She's a friend of me. → ✅ She's a friend of mine. (of + possessive pronoun)
  • ❌ two weeks holiday → ✅ two weeks' holiday (plural time: s')

تمرین سطح B2

10. Have you read ___ newspaper?
Time: today's.
11. He's a colleague ___.
A colleague of mine.
12. The station is about ten ___ (minute) walk from here. (one word)
Plural time: minutes'.

C1 · پیشرفته

Group possessives, else's, its and one's C1

's can attach to a whole noun phrase: the King of Spain's visit, someone else's bag. In formal writing, prefer of with long phrases. Possessive pronouns have no apostrophe: its, hers, ours, theirs.

PatternExample
whole phrase + 'sthe Prime Minister of Canada's speech, my brother-in-law's car
elsesomeone else's coat, everybody else's ideas
its vs it'sThe company changed its name. / It's (= it is) late.
one'sIt's important to know one's rights. (formal)
long phrases in speechthe man next door's dog (informal) → the dog that belongs to the man next door (writing)

The 's goes at the end of the whole phrase, not on the main noun: the head of department's office (not the head's of department office).

In formal writing, long 's phrases can sound clumsy. Use of or rephrase: the speech of the Prime Minister of Canada or the Canadian Prime Minister's speech.

Possessive pronouns never have an apostrophe: its, hers, ours, yours, theirs.

  • That's someone else's problem. (else's)
  • My mother-in-law's cooking is amazing. (compound + 's)
  • The committee published its report. (its)
  • The Queen of Denmark's visit was a success. (whole phrase + 's)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ That's somebody's else bag. → ✅ That's somebody else's bag. ('s after else)
  • ❌ The dog wagged it's tail. → ✅ The dog wagged its tail. (possessive: its)
  • ❌ Their's is the white house. → ✅ Theirs is the white house. (possessive pronouns: no apostrophe)

تمرین سطح C1

13. This isn't my coat. It's ___.
Someone else's.
14. The bird hurt ___ wing.
Possessive: its (no apostrophe).
15. My ___ (brother-in-law) car is a Tesla. (one word)
Compound noun: 's at the end.
