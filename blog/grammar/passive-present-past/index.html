The Passive: Present and Past Simple

Level: B1 · Free grammar guide

We use the passive when the action is more important than the person who does it, or when we don't know who did it: Paper is made from wood. My bike was stolen last night.

Form it with be + past participle: is/are made (present), was/were made (past).

How to form the passive

TenseActivePassive (be + past participle)
Present simpleThey make cars in this factory.Cars are made in this factory.
Present simpleSomeone cleans the office every day.The office is cleaned every day.
Past simpleSomeone stole my bike.My bike was stolen.
Past simpleThey built these houses in 1950.These houses were built in 1950.

Only be changes: is/are for the present, was/were for the past. The past participle stays the same: made, built, written, sent. For irregular past participles, see the irregular verbs list.

Negatives and questions

Present simple passivePast simple passive
NegativeEnglish isn't spoken here.The email wasn't sent.
QuestionIs breakfast included?When was the bridge built?
Short answersYes, it is. / No, it isn't.Yes, it was. / No, it wasn't.

Passive questions and negatives use be, not do or did: Was the house built in 1900? (not Did the house built…?)

When do we use the passive?

  • We don't know who did it: My phone was stolen.
  • It's obvious or not important who does it: The rubbish is collected on Mondays.
  • The thing is more interesting than the person: This song was written in 1971.
  • News, science and processes: Two people were injured in the accident. The water is heated to 100°C.

By + the person who does the action

Use by to say who did the action, but only when this information is important or interesting: The Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo da Vinci.

Don't add by people, by someone or by them: English is spoken here (not English is spoken by people here).

Active or passive?

Both are correct grammar. Choose the one that starts with the thing you're talking about:

  1. A dog bit my brother. (active: the sentence is about the dog)
  2. My brother was bitten by a dog. (passive: the sentence is about my brother)

Examples

  1. Coffee is grown in Brazil and Colombia. (present: a general fact)
  2. Dinner is served from 7 to 10 pm. (present: who serves it isn't important)
  3. My car was repaired yesterday. (past)
  4. These photos were taken in Iceland. (plural: were)
  5. Is this seat taken? (present question)
  6. The thief wasn't caught. (past negative)
  7. The Harry Potter books were written by J. K. Rowling. (by + the writer)
  8. Millions of emails are sent every day. (present: plural)
  9. When was your house built? (past question)
  10. The winners are announced at the end of the show. (present: a regular event)

Common mistakes

❌ The bridge was build in 1990.

✅ The bridge was built in 1990. (Use the past participle: build → built.)

❌ English speaks in Canada.

✅ English is spoken in Canada. (A language doesn't speak. Use be + past participle.)

❌ My wallet stolen yesterday.

✅ My wallet was stolen yesterday. (Don't forget be.)

❌ The letters was sent last week.

✅ The letters were sent last week. (Plural subject → were.)

❌ Did the house built in 1900?

✅ Was the house built in 1900? (Passive questions use be, not did.)

❌ The cake was made from my mum.

✅ The cake was made by my mum. (Use by for the person. (Made from is for materials: made from flour.))

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. Spanish ___ in Mexico.
A language doesn't speak; people speak it. Passive: is + past participle (spoken).
2. This bridge ___ in 1932.
In 1932 is the past: was + past participle. Build → built → built.
3. The rooms ___ every morning.
Rooms is plural and this is a habit: are + past participle.
4. My phone ___ last night.
Past simple passive: was + past participle. Steal → stole → stolen.
5. ___ breakfast included in the price?
Passive questions use be: Is breakfast included?
6. When ___ the Eiffel Tower built?
Past passive question: was + subject + past participle. The Eiffel Tower is singular.
7. These shoes ___ in Italy.
Plural subject in the past: were + past participle (made).
8. The package ___ because the address was wrong.
A package can't deliver itself. Passive negative: wasn't + past participle.
9. Romeo and Juliet ___ by Shakespeare.
Past passive with by for the writer: was written.
10. Our rubbish ___ on Tuesdays.
Rubbish is uncountable, so it's singular: is + past participle.
11. Choose the correct question.
Passive questions: Were + subject + past participle.
12. Thousands of photos ___ every day.
Plural, present: are + past participle. Photos don't upload themselves.
13. The thieves ___ by the police.
Plural, past: were + past participle. Catch → caught → caught.
14. Which sentence is the passive of: Someone painted the door?
Past active → past passive: was + past participle. We don't need by someone.
15. This song ___ in the 1980s.
In the 1980s is the past: was recorded.
16. The test results ___ on the classroom door every Friday.
Plural, a regular event: are + past participle.
