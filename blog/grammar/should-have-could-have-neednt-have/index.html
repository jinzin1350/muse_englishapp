Should Have, Could Have, Needn't Have

Level: B2 · Free grammar guide

We use modal + have + past participle to talk about the past in different ways: what was a good idea but didn't happen (should have), what was possible but didn't happen (could have), and what happened but wasn't necessary (needn't have).

Should have and shouldn't have: regrets and criticism

should have + past participle = it was the right thing to do, but it didn't happen: I should have studied harder. (I didn't.) shouldn't have + past participle = it happened, but it was a mistake: You shouldn't have said that.

Ought to have means the same as should have and is a little more formal: She ought to have told us.

Could have: missed possibilities

could have + past participle = it was possible, but it didn't happen: We could have gone to the beach, but we stayed at home. We also use it to criticise: You could have told me!

couldn't have + past participle = it was impossible: I couldn't have done it without you.

Needn't have or didn't need to?

FormMeaningExample
needn't have + past participleyou did it, but it wasn't necessaryI needn't have cooked so much. Nobody was hungry.
didn't need to + base verbit wasn't necessary (often you didn't do it)I didn't need to cook, so I relaxed.

Needn't have is mainly British. American English usually says didn't need to or didn't have to: I didn't have to cook so much.

Would have

would have + past participle describes what you were willing to do, or an imagined past: I would have helped you, but you didn't ask. See Third Conditional.

Examples

  1. I should have left earlier. Now I'm late. (regret)
  2. You shouldn't have eaten so much cake. (criticism)
  3. We could have won, but we made too many mistakes. (missed possibility)
  4. You could have called me! I was so worried. (criticism)
  5. I couldn't have passed without your help. (impossible)
  6. We needn't have hurried. The train was late anyway. (unnecessary action)
  7. She ought to have told her manager. (= should have)
  8. They should have booked a table. The restaurant was full. (regret)
  9. I didn't need to buy a ticket because my friend had a spare one. (not necessary, not done)
  10. I would have come to the party, but I was ill. (willing, but it didn't happen)

Common mistakes

❌ I should studied more.

✅ I should have studied more. (Should have + past participle.)

❌ You should have went to the doctor.

✅ You should have gone to the doctor. (Use the past participle: gone.)

❌ He could have be a great player.

✅ He could have been a great player. (Use the past participle: been.)

❌ I needn't have to buy it.

✅ I needn't have bought it. (Needn't have + past participle.)

❌ You shouldn't have tell her.

✅ You shouldn't have told her. (Use the past participle: told.)

❌ I should of called you.

✅ I should have called you. (Write have, not of.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. I'm so tired. I ___ to bed earlier last night.
A regret about the past: should have + past participle (gone).
2. You ___ that. Now she's upset.
It happened and it was a mistake: shouldn't have said.
3. We ___ the match, but we missed a penalty in the last minute.
It was possible but didn't happen: could have won.
4. You ___ me you were coming! I would have cooked something.
Criticism: could have told.
5. I ___ the exam without your help. Thank you!
It would have been impossible: couldn't have passed.
6. I cooked a huge meal, but only two people came. I ___ so much food.
I did it, but it wasn't necessary: needn't have made.
7. Choose the correct sentence.
Should have + past participle (called).
8. They ___ a table. The restaurant was full when we arrived.
It was a good idea, but it didn't happen: should have booked.
9. Which sentence means 'It was a mistake to do it'?
Shouldn't have + past participle = it happened, but it was wrong.
10. I ___ a taxi because my friend gave me a lift.
It wasn't necessary, and I didn't take one: didn't need to take.
11. He ___ a great musician, but he never practised.
A missed possibility: could have been.
12. You ___ the doctor sooner. Your cold might be better now.
Should have + past participle (seen).
13. We ___. The film started 20 minutes late anyway.
We hurried, but it wasn't necessary: needn't have hurried.
14. She ___ her manager about the problem. It was her responsibility.
Ought to have + past participle (told).
15. I ___ you, but you didn't ask.
I was willing, but it didn't happen: would have helped.
16. Which sentence means 'It was possible, but it didn't happen'?
Could have + past participle = a missed possibility.
