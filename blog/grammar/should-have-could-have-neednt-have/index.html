خانه / گرامر / should have، could have و needn't have

should have، could have و needn't haveShould Have, Could Have, Would Have, Needn't Have

با فعل وجهی + have + اسم مفعول دربارهٔ چیزهایی در گذشته حرف می‌زنیم که اتفاق نیفتادند یا از آن‌ها پشیمانیم: I should have left earlier. (باید زودتر راه می‌افتادم؛ نیفتادم و حالا دیرم شده.)

برای استنتاج دربارهٔ گذشته (must have, can't have, might have) صفحهٔ فعل‌های وجهیِ استنتاج را ببینید. برای جمله‌های if دربارهٔ گذشته صفحهٔ شرطی نوع سوم را ببینید.

B1 · B2 · C1

B1 · متوسط

should have / shouldn't have: پشیمانی و انتقاد B1

should have + اسم مفعول یعنی فکر خوبی بود، اما انجام نشد (باید … می‌کردم). shouldn't have یعنی انجام شد، اما اشتباه بود (نباید … می‌کردم).

ساختارمعنیمثال
should have + اسم مفعولانجام نشد (اما فکر خوبی بود)I should have studied harder.
shouldn't have + اسم مفعولانجام شد (اما فکر بدی بود)You shouldn't have said that.

این شکل‌ها را برای پشیمانی از کارهای خودمان و انتقاد از کار دیگران به کار می‌بریم: We should have booked a table. You shouldn't have shouted at him.

ought to have همان معنی should have را دارد اما رسمی‌تر است: You ought to have told me.

در صحبت، should have شبیه /ˈʃʊdəv/ (should've) شنیده می‌شود. هیچ‌وقت should of ننویسید.

  • I should have brought a jacket. I'm freezing. کاپشن نیاوردم
  • You shouldn't have eaten all that cake. خوردی
  • They should have checked the weather before they left. انتقاد
  • Should I have told her? سؤالی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I should have went to the doctor. → ✅ I should have gone to the doctor. should have + اسم مفعول.
  • ❌ You should of called me. → ✅ You should have called me. have، نه of.
  • ❌ I should studied more. → ✅ I should have studied more. should + have + اسم مفعول.

could have: فرصتی که از دست رفت B1

could have + اسم مفعول یعنی ممکن بود، اما اتفاق نیفتاد (می‌توانستم … بکنم): I could have gone to university, but I started working.

could have یک امکان یا فرصت در گذشته را توصیف می‌کند که اتفاق نیفتاد: We could have stayed longer, but we were tired.

برای انتقاد هم به کار می‌رود (انجامش ندادی، در حالی که باید می‌دادی): You could have told me you were coming! و برای چیزهایی که نزدیک بود اتفاق بیفتند: That was dangerous. You could have had an accident.

couldn't have یعنی ممکن نبود: I couldn't have come. I was in hospital.

  • She could have been a professional singer. ممکن بود، اتفاق نیفتاد
  • You could have asked for help! انتقاد
  • The accident could have been much worse. نزدیک بود اتفاق بیفتد
  • I couldn't have finished without you. ممکن نبود

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I could have go to the party, but I was too tired. → ✅ I could have gone to the party, but I was too tired. could have + اسم مفعول.
  • ❌ You could helped me. → ✅ You could have helped me. could + have + اسم مفعول.

تمرین سطح B1

1. I failed the exam. I ___ harder.
پشیمانی: should have + اسم مفعول.
2. You ___ that email to everyone. It was private!
انجام شد، اما اشتباه بود: shouldn't have sent.
3. We missed the bus. We ___ (should / leave) earlier.
پشیمانی: should have left.
4. I ___ a lot of money in that job, but I turned it down.
امکانی که اتفاق نیفتاد: could have earned.
5. جملهٔ You could have called me! چه چیزی را بیان می‌کند؟
Could have اینجا یعنی زنگ نزدی، در حالی که باید می‌زدی.
6. The fire was very serious. Someone ___ (could / die).
نزدیک بود اتفاق بیفتد: could have died.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Would have: what you were willing to do B2

would have + past participle = you were ready or willing to do something, but it didn't happen: I would have helped you, but you didn't ask.

Outside if sentences, would have still describes an imagined past: something that was going to happen or that you were willing to do, but didn't: I would have called, but my phone was dead.

Often the if part is hidden: I would have come (if you had invited me).

Wouldn't have + past participle = you were not willing, or it wasn't going to happen: I wouldn't have said yes anyway.

  • I would have bought it, but it was too expensive. (willing, didn't happen)
  • She would have loved this place. (imagined past)
  • We would have stayed, but the kids were tired. (willing, didn't happen)
  • He wouldn't have agreed, even if you'd asked. (not willing)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I would have came, but I was sick. → ✅ I would have come, but I was sick. (would have + past participle)

Needn't have or didn't need to? B2

needn't have + past participle = you did it, but it wasn't necessary. didn't need to = it wasn't necessary (you probably didn't do it).

FormDid it happen?Example
needn't have + past participleyes, but it was unnecessaryI needn't have cooked. They'd already eaten.
didn't need to + base verbusually noI didn't need to cook. We went to a restaurant.

Needn't have + past participle means you did something that wasn't necessary: You needn't have brought flowers. (You brought them, but it wasn't necessary.)

Didn't need to + base verb says it wasn't necessary, and usually you didn't do it: I didn't need to wear a coat. It was warm.

Needn't have is mainly British English. In American English, people usually say didn't need to (or didn't have to) for both meanings: I didn't need to cook, but I did.

  • You needn't have hurried. The meeting started late. (you hurried)
  • We didn't need to book. There were lots of free tables. (so we didn't book)
  • I needn't have worried. The exam was easy. (I worried)
  • She didn't need to wait long. The bus came quickly. (no need)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I needn't have worry about the test. → ✅ I needn't have worried about the test. (needn't have + past participle)
  • ❌ I didn't need to brought an umbrella. → ✅ I didn't need to bring an umbrella. (didn't need to + base verb)

تمرین سطح B2

7. I ___ you to the airport, but my car was at the garage.
Willing, but it didn't happen: would have driven.
8. What does My father would have loved this restaurant suggest?
Would have loved = an imagined past.
9. We ___ (would / come) to the party, but we didn't know about it.
Willing, but it didn't happen.
10. I took a taxi to the station, but the train was late. I ___ a taxi. (British English)
I took it, but it wasn't necessary: needn't have taken.
11. The museum was free, so we ___ tickets. We just walked in.
It wasn't necessary, so we didn't: didn't need to buy.
12. You ___ (need / not / bring) a present, but thank you!
You brought one, but it wasn't necessary.

C1 · پیشرفته

You shouldn't have!, should have for expectations, might have for annoyance C1

You shouldn't have! = a polite thank you for a gift. They should have arrived by now = expected. You might have warned me! = annoyance.

Some past modal forms have special meanings:

  • You shouldn't have! is a polite, warm reaction when someone gives you a present or does something kind. It doesn't mean they did something wrong: Oh, flowers! You shouldn't have!
  • Should have + past participle can describe an expectation about the past: The parcel should have arrived yesterday. (It was expected, but it hasn't arrived.) / They should have landed by now.
  • Might have / could have + past participle can express annoyance: You might have told me you weren't coming!

The meaning depends on the context and the tone of voice.

  • A cake for me? Oh, you shouldn't have! (polite thanks)
  • The train should have left ten minutes ago. Why are we still here? (expected)
  • You might have asked before you took my car! (annoyance)
  • By now they should have finished the building. (expected)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You shouldn't have bought me. (meaning thank you) → ✅ You shouldn't have! / You shouldn't have bought me anything! (the fixed phrase)

تمرین سطح C1

13. Your friend gives you an expensive present. What's a polite, warm reaction?
You shouldn't have! = thank you (you were too kind).
14. What does The report should have been ready yesterday mean?
Should have = an expectation that wasn't met.
15. You ___ (might / tell) me you were vegetarian! I made beef stew.
Annoyance: might have told.
