Adverbs of Manner

A2 · B1 · B2

Adverbs of manner describe how something happens: She sings beautifully. Drive carefully! Most are formed from an adjective + -ly: slow → slowly.

Adjectives describe nouns (a careful driver); adverbs describe verbs (He drives carefully).

A2 · Elementary

Adjective or adverb? Quick or quickly? A2

AdjectiveAdverbSpelling rule
slow, quiet, carefulslowly, quietly, carefully+ -ly
easy, happy, angryeasily, happily, angrily-y → -ily
terrible, possible, gentleterribly, possibly, gently-le → -ly
automatic, dramaticautomatically, dramatically-ic → -ically

  • He's a careful driver. He drives carefully. (adjective / adverb)
  • The children played happily in the garden. (-y → -ily)
  • Please speak slowly. (after the verb)
  • She closed the window quietly. (after the object)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ She sings beautiful. → ✅ She sings beautifully. (verb: adverb)
  • ❌ He drives very careful. → ✅ He drives very carefully. (verb: adverb)
  • ❌ I read quickly the email. → ✅ I read the email quickly. (adverb after the object)

Good or well? Hard, fast, late, early A2

AdjectiveAdverbExample
goodwellShe's a good cook. She cooks well.
hardhardIt's hard work. He works hard.
fastfasta fast car / He drives fast.
late / earlylate / earlya late train / I got up late.
straightstraightGo straight on.

  • You speak English very well. (good → well)
  • She works very hard. (hard)
  • Don't drive so fast! (fast)
  • I hardly know him. (hardly = almost not)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He plays the guitar very good. → ✅ He plays the guitar very well. (verb: well)
  • ❌ She works hardly. → ✅ She works hard. (hard (adverb) = with effort)
  • ❌ He drives fastly. → ✅ He drives fast. (fast (no -ly))

Practice: A2

1. Please drive ___. The roads are icy.
Verb: adverb carefully.
2. She's a ___ singer.
Noun: adjective beautiful.
3. The children are playing ___ (quiet) in their room.
Verb: adverb quietly.
4. You did ___ in the exam! Congratulations.
The adverb of good is well.
5. She studied ___ and passed all her exams.
With effort: hard.
6. I got up ___ (late) this morning, so I missed the bus.
Late is also the adverb.

B1 · Intermediate

Friendly, lively, lovely; look good, feel tired B1

PatternExample
-ly adjectivesShe's very friendly. It's a lovely day.
adverb meaningShe smiled in a friendly way. (not friendlily)
linking verb + adjectiveThe soup smells good. You sound happy. I feel tired.
action verb + adverbShe looked at me angrily. (an action)

  • Our neighbours are very friendly. (-ly adjective)
  • He spoke to me in a friendly way. (in a … way)
  • This cake tastes delicious. (linking verb + adjective)
  • You seem a bit sad today. (seem + adjective)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ She talked to me friendly. → ✅ She talked to me in a friendly way. (friendly is an adjective)
  • ❌ The flowers smell beautifully. → ✅ The flowers smell beautiful. (linking verb + adjective)
  • ❌ You look tiredly. → ✅ You look tired. (look (= seem) + adjective)

Practice: B1

7. The waiter spoke to us ___.
Friendly is an adjective: in a friendly way.
8. This soup tastes ___. Can I have some more?
Linking verb + adjective.
9. You look ___ (happy) today. Did you get good news?
Look (= seem) + adjective.

Without -lyWith -ly

  • The museum is free on Sundays. (free = no cost)
  • You can move freely around the city. (freely = without limits)
  • She's a highly paid lawyer. (highly = very)
  • I nearly missed the train. (nearly = almost)
  • She quietly closed the door. (before the verb)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I hard ever see him. → ✅ I hardly ever see him. (hardly = almost not)
  • ❌ This book is high recommended. → ✅ This book is highly recommended. (highly = very much)
  • ❌ Spanish is wide spoken in the USA. → ✅ Spanish is widely spoken in the USA. (widely = in many places)

Practice: B2

In many places: widely.
11. Children under five travel ___ on the bus.
Without paying: free.
