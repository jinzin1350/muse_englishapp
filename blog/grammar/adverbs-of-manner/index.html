قیدهای حالتAdverbs of Manner

قیدهای حالت می‌گویند کاری چطور انجام می‌شود: She sings beautifully. Drive carefully! بیشترشان از صفت + ‑ly ساخته می‌شوند: slow → slowly.

صفت اسم را توصیف می‌کند (a careful driver)؛ قید فعل را (He drives carefully). در فارسی گاهی هر دو یک کلمه‌اند («آرام»)، اما در انگلیسی فرق دارند.

A2 · B1 · B2

A2 · مقدماتی

صفت یا قید؟ quick یا quickly؟ A2

با اسم صفت بیاورید (a quick lunch) و با فعل قید (She ate quickly). بیشتر قیدها = صفت + ‑ly.

صفتقیدقاعدهٔ املایی
slow, quiet, carefulslowly, quietly, carefully+ -ly
easy, happy, angryeasily, happily, angrily-y → -ily
terrible, possible, gentleterribly, possibly, gently-le → -ly
automatic, dramaticautomatically, dramatically-ic → -ically

قیدهای حالت معمولاً بعد از فعل یا بعد از مفعول می‌آیند: She speaks quietly. He opened the door slowly. (نه He opened slowly the door)

  • He's a careful driver. He drives carefully. صفت / قید
  • The children played happily in the garden. ‑y ← ‑ily
  • Please speak slowly. بعد از فعل
  • She closed the window quietly. بعد از مفعول

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She sings beautiful. → ✅ She sings beautifully. فعل را قید توصیف می‌کند.
  • ❌ He drives very careful. → ✅ He drives very carefully. فعل را قید توصیف می‌کند.
  • ❌ I read quickly the email. → ✅ I read the email quickly. قید بعد از مفعول می‌آید.

good یا well؟ hard, fast, late, early A2

قیدِ good می‌شود well. بعضی قیدها همان شکل صفت را دارند: hard, fast, late, early, straight. hardly و lately معنی دیگری دارند.

صفتقیدمثال
goodwellShe's a good cook. She cooks well.
hardhardIt's hard work. He works hard.
fastfasta fast car / He drives fast.
late / earlylate / earlya late train / I got up late.
straightstraightGo straight on.

hardly یعنی تقریباً نه: I hardly slept. (تقریباً نخوابیدم). lately یعنی اخیراً: Have you seen him lately? این دو قیدِ hard و late نیستند.

well صفت هم هست، به معنی سالم، سرحال: I don't feel well.

  • You speak English very well. good ← well
  • She works very hard. hard (با تلاش)
  • Don't drive so fast! fast
  • I hardly know him. hardly یعنی تقریباً نه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He plays the guitar very good. → ✅ He plays the guitar very well. با فعل: well.
  • ❌ She works hardly. → ✅ She works hard. hard (قید) یعنی با تلاش.
  • ❌ He drives fastly. → ✅ He drives fast. fast حرف ‑ly نمی‌گیرد.

تمرین سطح A2

1. Please drive ___. The roads are icy.
با فعل: قید carefully.
2. She's a ___ singer.
با اسم: صفت beautiful.
3. The children are playing ___ (quiet) in their room.
با فعل: قید quietly.
4. You did ___ in the exam! Congratulations.
قیدِ good می‌شود well.
5. She studied ___ and passed all her exams.
با تلاش: hard.
6. I got up ___ (late) this morning, so I missed the bus.
قیدِ late هم late است.

B1 · متوسط

friendly, lively, lovely؛ look good, feel tired B1

بعضی کلمه‌هایی که به ‑ly ختم می‌شوند صفت‌اند: friendly, lovely, lonely, lively, silly, ugly. برای معنی قیدی از in a … way استفاده کنید: in a friendly way. بعد از فعل‌های ربطی (be, look, feel, sound, taste, smell, seem) صفت بیاورید: You look tired.

الگومثال
صفت‌هایی که به ‑ly ختم می‌شوندShe's very friendly. It's a lovely day.
معنی قیدیShe smiled in a friendly way. (نه friendlily)
فعل ربطی + صفتThe soup smells good. You sound happy. I feel tired.
فعلِ کاری + قیدShe looked at me angrily. (یک کار)

مقایسه کنید: She looks angry. (یعنی عصبانی به نظر می‌رسد: صفت) / She looked at me angrily. (یعنی چطور به من نگاه کرد: قید).

  • Our neighbours are very friendly. صفتِ ختم به ‑ly
  • He spoke to me in a friendly way. in a … way
  • This cake tastes delicious. فعل ربطی + صفت
  • You seem a bit sad today. seem + صفت

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She talked to me friendly. → ✅ She talked to me in a friendly way. friendly صفت است.
  • ❌ The flowers smell beautifully. → ✅ The flowers smell beautiful. فعل ربطی + صفت.
  • ❌ You look tiredly. → ✅ You look tired. look (به معنی به نظر رسیدن) + صفت.

تمرین سطح B1

7. The waiter spoke to us ___.
Friendly صفت است: in a friendly way.
8. This soup tastes ___. Can I have some more?
فعل ربطی + صفت.
9. You look ___ (happy) today. Did you get good news?
Look (به معنی به نظر رسیدن) + صفت.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Hard or hardly? Free or freely? Wide or widely? B2

Some pairs have different meanings: hard (with effort) / hardly (almost not); free (without paying) / freely (without limits); wide (fully) / widely (in many places); high / highly (very much); deep / deeply (very); near / nearly (almost).

Without -lyWith -ly
He works hard. (with effort)He hardly works. (almost not)
Children travel free. (no cost)You can speak freely. (without limits)
Open your mouth wide. (fully)English is widely spoken. (in many places)
The plane flew high.She's highly respected. (very much)
They dug deep.I'm deeply sorry. (very)
Come near.I nearly fell. (almost)

In writing, adverbs of manner can go before the main verb for emphasis: She quietly left the room. But never between a verb and its object (She left quietly the room ✗).

  • The museum is free on Sundays. (free = no cost)
  • You can move freely around the city. (freely = without limits)
  • She's a highly paid lawyer. (highly = very)
  • I nearly missed the train. (nearly = almost)
  • She quietly closed the door. (before the verb)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I hard ever see him. → ✅ I hardly ever see him. (hardly = almost not)
  • ❌ This book is high recommended. → ✅ This book is highly recommended. (highly = very much)
  • ❌ Spanish is wide spoken in the USA. → ✅ Spanish is widely spoken in the USA. (widely = in many places)

تمرین سطح B2

10. English is ___ used in international business.
In many places: widely.
11. Children under five travel ___ on the bus.
Without paying: free.
12. I ___ (near) fell off my bike this morning. (one word)
Almost: nearly.
