This, That, These, Those

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

We use this / these for things that are near us and that / those for things that are far from us.

This and that are singular; these and those are plural: This bag is mine. Those shoes are nice.

Near and far, singular and plural

Near (here)Far (there)
Singularthis: this bookthat: that book
Pluralthese: these booksthose: those books

This has a short sound (thiss); these has a long sound (theez).

With or without a noun

Before a noun: This phone is new. Alone: What's that? These are my keys. To avoid repeating a noun, use one: I don't like this jacket. I prefer that one.

Other uses

  • Introducing people: This is my friend, Leo.
  • On the phone: Hello, this is Anna.
  • Time: this week (now), that day (in the past), these days (nowadays)
  • Reacting to something someone just said: I failed my test. – Oh, that's a shame.

Examples

  1. This coffee is too hot. (near, singular)
  2. Who is that man over there? (far, singular)
  3. These shoes are very comfortable. (near, plural)
  4. Can you see those birds in the sky? (far, plural)
  5. This is my sister, Lena. (introducing someone)
  6. Hello, this is David. Can I speak to Maria? (on the phone)
  7. I'm very busy this week. (time: now)
  8. Do you remember that day at the beach? (time: in the past)
  9. These days, everyone has a smartphone. (nowadays)
  10. I lost my job. – Oh no, that's terrible. (reacting)

Common mistakes

❌ This books are mine.

✅ These books are mine. (Plural noun → these.)

❌ Those car is expensive.

✅ That car is expensive. (Singular noun → that.)

❌ These is my friend.

✅ This is my friend. (One person → this.)

❌ I love this shoes.

✅ I love these shoes. (Plural noun → these.)

❌ Look at that birds!

✅ Look at those birds! (Plural noun → those.)

❌ These day I'm very busy.

✅ These days I'm very busy. (These + plural noun: days.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. ___ book in my hand is very interesting.
Near (in my hand), singular: This.
2. Who is ___ woman over there?
Far (over there), singular: that.
3. Look at ___ mountains over there!
Far, plural: those.
4. Come and meet my kids! ___ are my children, Ben and Lily.
Plural, near: These are…
5. Hello, ___ is Anna. Can I speak to Tom, please?
On the phone: Hello, this is Anna.
6. I love ___ shoes I'm wearing. They're so comfortable.
Near (I'm wearing them), plural: these.
7. ___ is my phone.
One thing: This.
8. I'm very busy ___ week.
The present week: this week.
9. I failed my driving test. – Oh, ___ a shame.
Reacting to what someone said: that's a shame.
10. Choose the correct sentence.
Plural: these books are.
11. Do you remember ___ summer we spent in Greece? It was ten years ago.
A time in the past: that summer.
12. ___ days, lots of people work from home.
Nowadays = These days.
13. I don't like this jacket. I prefer ___ one.
Singular one: that one.
14. Are ___ your keys on the table over there?
Far, plural: those.
15. Who's ___ boy over there with the red hat?
Far, singular: that.
16. ___ pens in my bag don't work. Can I borrow yours?
Near (in my bag), plural: These.
