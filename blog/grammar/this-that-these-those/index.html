خانه / گرامر / this، that، these و those

this، that، these و those (اسم‌های اشاره)This, That, These and Those

this (مفرد) و these (جمع) را برای چیزهای نزدیک («این، این‌ها») و that (مفرد) و those (جمع) را برای چیزهای دور («آن، آن‌ها») به کار ببرید: This is my bag. That car over there is my dad's.

برای زمان هم به کار می‌روند (this week, in those days)، در گفت‌وگو (That's great!) و در نوشتن برای اشاره به حرفی که قبلاً زده شده (This means that…).

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

this, that, these, those: نزدیک و دور A1

this + مفرد و these + جمع یعنی نزدیک. that + مفرد و those + جمع یعنی دور.

نزدیک (اینجا)دور (آنجا)
مفردthis bookthat book
جمعthese booksthose books

آن‌ها را قبل از اسم (this phone) یا تنها (What's this?) بیاورید.

فعل هماهنگ می‌شود: This is… / These are… و That is… / Those are…

  • This coffee is really hot. نزدیک، مفرد
  • These shoes are too small. نزدیک، جمع
  • Can you see that bird? دور، مفرد
  • Those houses are very old. دور، جمع

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ This shoes are new. → ✅ These shoes are new. جمع: these
  • ❌ Those is my car. → ✅ That is my car. مفرد: that
  • ❌ These book is interesting. → ✅ This book is interesting. مفرد: this

تمرین سطح A1

1. ___ apples here are delicious. Try one!
نزدیک، جمع: these.
2. Look at ___ mountain over there!
دور، مفرد: that.
3. ___ are my keys, here in my hand. (one word)
نزدیک، جمع: these.

A2 · مقدماتی

This is…، that's great، this one A2

this را برای معرفی کردن آدم‌ها (This is my friend) و پشت تلفن (Hi, this is Sara) بیاورید. That's واکنش به حرفی است که کسی همین الان زده (That's great!). This one / that one جای اسم را می‌گیرند.

کاربردمثال
معرفی کردنThis is my sister, Mina.
پشت تلفنHello, this is Ali. Is that Reza? (آمریکایی: Is this Reza?)
واکنش نشان دادنI passed! ~ That's great! That's a good idea.
one / onesI like this one. Which shoes? ~ Those (ones).

that اغلب به چیزی اشاره می‌کند که همین الان گفته شد: I'm getting married. ~ Really? That's wonderful!

this one / that one از تکرار اسم جلوگیری می‌کنند: Which cake do you want? ~ That one, please. با جمع، خودِ these / those رایج‌تر از these ones است.

  • This is my colleague, David. معرفی کردن
  • Hi, this is Sara. Can I speak to Leila? پشت تلفن
  • I've found a new job. ~ That's fantastic! واکنش
  • Not that one, this one. (one)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ These is my parents. → ✅ These are my parents. جمع: are
  • ❌ Hello, I am Sara speaking. (on the phone) → ✅ Hello, this is Sara. پشت تلفن: this is

تمرین سطح A2

4. Mum, ___ is my friend Zahra.
معرفی یک نفر: this is.
5. My sister's having a baby! ~ ___ great!
واکنش به خبر: That's great!
6. Which cake would you like? ~ That ___, please. The chocolate one. (one word; don't repeat the noun)
that one جای اسم را می‌گیرد.

B1 · متوسط

this morning, that day, these days, in those days B1

this + زمان یعنی حالا یا زمانی نزدیک (this morning, this week). that + زمان یعنی زمانی در گذشته که از آن حرف زده‌ایم (that day, that night). these days یعنی این روزها؛ in those days یعنی آن روزها در گذشته.

عبارتمعنی
this morning / this week / this yearصبحِ امروز / همین هفته / امسال
that day / that night / that summerزمانی در گذشته که از آن حرف زده‌ایم
these daysاین روزها، این دوره
in those daysآن روزها، آن زمان در گذشته

وقتی داستان تعریف می‌کنید that بیاورید: We met in 2015. That summer was amazing.

در نقل قول غیرمستقیم، this اغلب به that تبدیل می‌شود: “I'm busy this week.” → She said she was busy that week. صفحهٔ نقل قول غیرمستقیم را ببینید.

  • I'm really busy this week. حال
  • I remember that day very well. گذشته
  • These days, everyone has a smartphone. این روزها
  • In those days, there was no internet. در گذشته

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I didn't sleep well this night. (meaning last night) → ✅ I didn't sleep well last night. دیشب: last night
  • ❌ In these days, people worked longer hours. (in the past) → ✅ In those days, people worked longer hours. گذشته: in those days

تمرین سطح B1

7. I went to the gym ___ morning, before work. Now I'm tired.
امروز: this morning.
8. My grandparents grew up in a village. In ___ days, there was no electricity there.
گذشته: in those days.
9. ___ days, most people work from home at least once a week. (one word)
این روزها: these days.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

This and that in writing: this means, those who B2

In writing, this often refers to an idea just mentioned (This means that…). That refers to what another person said. Those who = the people who; that of / those of replace a noun (higher than those of last year).

UseExample
referring back to an ideaPrices rose by 10%. This led to protests.
this + summary nounMany people lost their jobs. This problem…
the other person's wordsA: Let's leave early. B: That's a good idea.
those whoThose who arrived late missed the talk.
that of / those ofOur prices are lower than those of our competitors.

In formal writing, this + a summary noun is clearer than this alone: this situation, this change, this approach.

That of / those of replace a noun to avoid repeating it: The climate of Vancouver is milder than that of Toronto. (= the climate of) Use those of for plurals.

  • Sleep improves memory. This is why students should rest before exams. (referring back)
  • Those who want to join should sign up today. (those who)
  • The climate here is warmer than that of northern Europe. (that of)
  • You'll fail? Don't say that! (the other person's words)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Them who arrived late missed the start. → ✅ Those who arrived late missed the start. (those who)
  • ❌ Our results are better than these of last year. → ✅ Our results are better than those of last year. (those of)
  • ❌ The population of Toronto is bigger than Montreal. → ✅ The population of Toronto is bigger than that of Montreal. (compare the same things: that of)

تمرین سطح B2

10. ___ who finish early can leave.
Those who = the people who.
11. Unemployment fell last year. ___ was good news for the government.
Referring back to an idea: this.
12. The weather in Vancouver is milder than ___ of Toronto. (one word)
Singular: that of.

C1 · پیشرفته

Storytelling this, shared that, and not that hungry C1

In informal stories, this introduces something new (So this man comes up to me…). That refers to shared memories or shows dislike (Remember that café? I hate that noise.). That + adjective = very / so (I'm not that hungry).

UseExample
storytelling (informal)There was this strange guy on the bus… (like a, but more vivid)
shared knowledgeRemember that café in Paris?
dislike or distanceI don't trust that man.
this introducing newsListen to this: I've been promoted!
that + adjectiveI'm not that hungry. I didn't know it was that expensive.

That + adjective (informal) means so or very: The film wasn't that good. (= not very good)

It simply refers back to something already mentioned. This draws attention or points forward to new information: Here's the plan. This is what we'll do.

  • So I'm in a shop, and this woman starts shouting at me. (storytelling)
  • Remember that little restaurant near the beach? (shared memory)
  • The film wasn't that good. (that + adjective)
  • Listen to this: I've got the job! (introducing news)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It wasn't that much good. → ✅ It wasn't that good. (that + adjective: no much)

تمرین سطح C1

13. I'm not ___ hungry. A sandwich is enough.
That + adjective = very.
14. Remember ___ amazing holiday we had in Shiraz?
Shared memory: that.
15. Wait till you hear ___! I got the job! (one word)
Introducing news: this.
