This, That, These and Those

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

Use this (singular) and these (plural) for things near you, and that (singular) and those (plural) for things far from you: This is my bag. That car over there is my dad's.

They're also used for time (this week, in those days), in conversation (That's great!) and in writing to refer back to ideas (This means that…).

A1 · Beginner

This, that, these, those: near and far A1

Near (here)Far (there)
singularthis bookthat book
pluralthese booksthose books

  • This coffee is really hot. (near, singular)
  • These shoes are too small. (near, plural)
  • Can you see that bird? (far, singular)
  • Those houses are very old. (far, plural)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ This shoes are new. → ✅ These shoes are new. (plural: these)
  • ❌ Those is my car. → ✅ That is my car. (singular: that)
  • ❌ These book is interesting. → ✅ This book is interesting. (singular: this)

Practice: A1

1. ___ apples here are delicious. Try one!
Near, plural: these.
2. Look at ___ mountain over there!
Far, singular: that.
3. ___ are my keys, here in my hand. (one word)
Near, plural: these.

A2 · Elementary

This is…, that's great, this one A2

UseExample
introducingThis is my sister, Mina.
on the phoneHello, this is Ali. Is that Reza? (AmE: Is this Reza?)
reactingI passed! ~ That's great! That's a good idea.
one / onesI like this one. Which shoes? ~ Those (ones).

  • This is my colleague, David. (introducing)
  • Hi, this is Sara. Can I speak to Leila? (on the phone)
  • I've found a new job. ~ That's fantastic! (reacting)
  • Not that one, this one. (one)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ These is my parents. → ✅ These are my parents. (plural: are)
  • ❌ Hello, I am Sara speaking. (on the phone) → ✅ Hello, this is Sara. (on the phone: this is)

Practice: A2

4. Mum, ___ is my friend Zahra.
Introducing one person: this is.
5. My sister's having a baby! ~ ___ great!
Reacting to news: That's great!
6. Which cake would you like? ~ That ___, please. The chocolate one. (one word; don't repeat the noun)
That one replaces the noun.

B1 · Intermediate

This morning, that day, these days, in those days B1

ExpressionMeaning
this morning / this week / this yearthe morning of today / the current week / year
that day / that night / that summera time in the past that we've mentioned
these daysnowadays
in those daysat that time in the past

  • I'm really busy this week. (present)
  • I remember that day very well. (past)
  • These days, everyone has a smartphone. (nowadays)
  • In those days, there was no internet. (in the past)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I didn't sleep well this night. (meaning last night) → ✅ I didn't sleep well last night. (past night: last night)
  • ❌ In these days, people worked longer hours. (in the past) → ✅ In those days, people worked longer hours. (past: in those days)

Practice: B1

7. I went to the gym ___ morning, before work. Now I'm tired.
Today: this morning.
8. My grandparents grew up in a village. In ___ days, there was no electricity there.
Past: in those days.
9. ___ days, most people work from home at least once a week. (one word)
Nowadays: these days.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

This and that in writing: this means, those who B2

UseExample
referring back to an ideaPrices rose by 10%. This led to protests.
this + summary nounMany people lost their jobs. This problem…
the other person's wordsA: Let's leave early. B: That's a good idea.
those whoThose who arrived late missed the talk.
that of / those ofOur prices are lower than those of our competitors.

  • Sleep improves memory. This is why students should rest before exams. (referring back)
  • Those who want to join should sign up today. (those who)
  • The climate here is warmer than that of northern Europe. (that of)
  • You'll fail? Don't say that! (the other person's words)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Them who arrived late missed the start. → ✅ Those who arrived late missed the start. (those who)
  • ❌ Our results are better than these of last year. → ✅ Our results are better than those of last year. (those of)
  • ❌ The population of Toronto is bigger than Montreal. → ✅ The population of Toronto is bigger than that of Montreal. (compare the same things: that of)

Practice: B2

10. ___ who finish early can leave.
Those who = the people who.
11. Unemployment fell last year. ___ was good news for the government.
Referring back to an idea: this.
12. The weather in Vancouver is milder than ___ of Toronto. (one word)
Singular: that of.

C1 · Advanced

Storytelling this, shared that, and not that hungry C1

UseExample
storytelling (informal)There was this strange guy on the bus… (like a, but more vivid)
shared knowledgeRemember that café in Paris?
dislike or distanceI don't trust that man.
this introducing newsListen to this: I've been promoted!
that + adjectiveI'm not that hungry. I didn't know it was that expensive.

  • So I'm in a shop, and this woman starts shouting at me. (storytelling)
  • Remember that little restaurant near the beach? (shared memory)
  • The film wasn't that good. (that + adjective)
  • Listen to this: I've got the job! (introducing news)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ It wasn't that much good. → ✅ It wasn't that good. (that + adjective: no much)

Practice: C1

13. I'm not ___ hungry. A sandwich is enough.
That + adjective = very.
14. Remember ___ amazing holiday we had in Shiraz?
Shared memory: that.
15. Wait till you hear ___! I got the job! (one word)
Introducing news: this.
