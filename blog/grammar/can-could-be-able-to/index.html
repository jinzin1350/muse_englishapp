can، could و be able to (توانایی و امکان)Can, Could and Be Able To (Ability and Possibility)

can را برای توانایی و امکان در حال به کار می‌بریم: I can speak three languages. could گذشتهٔ آن است: I could swim when I was five. برای زمان‌های دیگر be able to می‌آوریم: I'll be able to help you tomorrow.

can و could برای درخواست و اجازه هم به کار می‌روند (Can I…? Could you…?). آن کاربردها صفحهٔ خودشان را دارند: درخواست، پیشنهاد و اجازه گرفتن.

A1 · A2 · B1 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

can و can't: توانایی A1

can / can't + شکل سادهٔ فعل برای توانایی: I can swim. She can't drive. Can you cook?

ساختارمثال
مثبتI / you / he / she / we / they can swim.
منفیShe can't (cannot) drive.
سؤالیCan you play the guitar?
جواب کوتاهYes, I can. / No, I can't.

can برای همهٔ اشخاص یکی است و ‑s نمی‌گیرد: He can sing. نه He cans sing. بعدش شکل سادهٔ فعل بدون to می‌آید: I can swim، نه I can to swim.

شکل کاملِ منفی یک کلمه نوشته می‌شود: cannot. در گفتار can معمولاً ضعیف /kən/ و can't قوی تلفظ می‌شود: /kɑːnt/ (بریتانیایی) یا /kænt/ (آمریکایی).

  • I can speak Farsi and English. توانایی
  • My grandma can't use a computer. منفی
  • Can you ride a bike? ~ Yes, I can. سؤال + جواب کوتاه
  • What languages can you speak? سؤال با کلمهٔ پرسشی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He cans swim. → ✅ He can swim. can حرف ‑s نمی‌گیرد
  • ❌ I can to dance. → ✅ I can dance. can + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ Do you can cook? → ✅ Can you cook? سؤالی: Can you…?

تمرین سطح A1

1. My little brother ___ read yet. He's only three.
توانایی، منفی: can't.
2. ___ you speak Spanish? ~ Yes, a little.
سؤال دربارهٔ توانایی: Can you…?
3. My sister ___ (can / play) the piano very well.
can + شکل سادهٔ فعل.

A2 · مقدماتی

could و couldn't: توانایی در گذشته A2

could / couldn't + شکل سادهٔ فعل برای توانایی کلی در گذشته: I could swim when I was five. I couldn't sleep last night.

could گذشتهٔ can برای توانایی کلی است: When I was young, I could run very fast.

couldn't هم برای توانایی کلی به کار می‌رود و هم برای یک موقعیت مشخص: I couldn't find my keys this morning. We couldn't hear the music.

سؤالی: Could you swim when you were a child?

با فعل‌های حسی (see, hear, smell, taste) و understand, remember اغلب برای یک موقعیت مشخص هم could می‌آوریم: I could see the sea from my window.

  • My grandmother could speak four languages. توانایی کلی
  • I couldn't open the door. It was locked. یک موقعیت مشخص (منفی)
  • Could you read before you started school? سؤالی
  • We could hear the waves all night. حس‌ها

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ When I was ten, I can swim. → ✅ When I was ten, I could swim. گذشته: could
  • ❌ I couldn't to sleep. → ✅ I couldn't sleep. could + شکل سادهٔ فعل

تمرین سطح A2

4. When I was a child, I ___ climb trees really well.
توانایی کلی در گذشته: could.
5. I ___ find a parking space, so I was late.
گذشته، منفی: couldn't.
6. It was so foggy that we ___ (not / could / see) the road.
couldn't + شکل سادهٔ فعل.

B1 · متوسط

be able to: زمان‌های دیگر و بعد از فعل‌های دیگر B1

هر جا can ممکن نیست، be able to بیاورید: I'll be able to help tomorrow. I've never been able to dance. I'd like to be able to ski.

can فقط دو شکل دارد: can و could. برای زمان‌ها و ساختارهای دیگر be able to بیاورید:

ساختارمثال
آیندهI'll be able to see you next week.
حال کاملShe hasn't been able to sleep well recently.
بعد از فعل‌های وجهیYou might be able to get a ticket.
مصدرI'd love to be able to play the guitar.
شکل ing‌دارI enjoy being able to work from home.

در حال، be able to ممکن است اما از can رسمی‌تر است: Are you able to come? = Can you come?

دو فعل وجهی را کنار هم نیاورید: I will be able to come. نه I will can come. You might be able to help. نه You might can help.

  • I'll be able to drive when I pass my test. آینده
  • I haven't been able to contact him. حال کامل
  • You should be able to finish by five. بعد از فعل وجهی
  • I'd like to be able to speak French. مصدر

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I will can help you tomorrow. → ✅ I will be able to help you tomorrow. آینده: will be able to
  • ❌ I haven't could finish it. → ✅ I haven't been able to finish it. حال کامل: been able to
  • ❌ She might can come. → ✅ She might be able to come. بعد از فعل وجهی: be able to

could یا was able to؟ managed to B1

برای یک کارِ موفق در گذشته was able to یا managed to بیاورید، نه could: The fire spread, but everyone was able to escape.

could برای توانایی کلی در گذشته است: I could swim when I was five.

برای یک موقعیت مشخص که کسی موفق شد (اغلب به‌سختی)، was / were able to یا managed to («توانستم، موفق شدم») بیاورید: The road was blocked, but we managed to get home. نه we could get home.

در منفی، couldn't برای هر دو درست است: We couldn't get home. = We weren't able to / didn't manage to get home.

استثنا: با فعل‌های حسی و فکری، could برای یک موقعیت مشخص هم درست است: I could see the fire from my window.

  • She could play chess when she was six. توانایی کلی
  • The exam was hard, but I managed to pass. یک موفقیت، به‌سختی
  • After two hours, firefighters were able to put out the fire. یک موفقیت
  • I couldn't get a ticket. They were sold out. منفی: couldn't درست است

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The test was difficult, but I could pass it. → ✅ The test was difficult, but I managed to pass it. یک موفقیت: managed to / was able to
  • ❌ We managed get there on time. → ✅ We managed to get there on time. managed + to

could و can برای امکان و پیشنهاد B1

could یعنی چیزی ممکن است (It could rain later) یا پیشنهاد است (We could go to the cinema). can یعنی چیزی به طور کلی ممکن است (You can see the sea from here).

could + شکل سادهٔ فعل نشان می‌دهد چیزی الان یا در آینده ممکن است، اما قطعی نیست: Take a coat. It could get cold later.

could برای پیشنهاد دادن هم به کار می‌رود: What shall we do tonight? ~ We could watch a film.

can نشان می‌دهد چیزی به طور کلی ممکن است: You can see the CN Tower from my window. It can take an hour to get there in traffic.

برای امکان در آینده could, might و may شبیه هم‌اند: It could / might / may rain. برای یک احتمالِ مشخص در آینده can نمی‌آید: نه It can rain tomorrow. صفحهٔ may و might را ببینید.

  • The flight could be delayed because of the snow. ممکن است
  • We could have a picnic if it's sunny. پیشنهاد
  • You can buy tickets online. به طور کلی ممکن است
  • Be careful. That dog could bite. خطرِ احتمالی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It can rain tomorrow, so take an umbrella. → ✅ It could rain tomorrow, so take an umbrella. احتمالی مشخص در آینده: could / might
  • ❌ We could to go to the beach. → ✅ We could go to the beach. could + شکل سادهٔ فعل

مصدر: to be able to.

یک کار موفق: were able to.

یک موفقیت: managed to.

C1 · پیشرفته

Can for typical behaviour, could for criticism C1

can = what is typical, sometimes true: She can be very rude. could = criticism or irritation: You could at least say thank you!

Can describes what is sometimes true or typical of a person, place or thing: Scotland can be very wet in summer. My brother can be really funny. (He isn't always funny.)

Could can express criticism or annoyance about what someone doesn't do: You could at least help me! They could have told us earlier. (They didn't.) Stress falls on could.

Could + well for a strong possibility: Prices could well rise again.

For past criticism with could have, see Should Have, Could Have, Needn't Have.

  • Teenagers can be difficult. (typical, sometimes true)
  • The motorway can get very busy on Fridays. (sometimes)
  • You could at least knock before you come in! (criticism)
  • The project could well take longer than planned. (strong possibility)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ My boss can to be very strict. → ✅ My boss can be very strict. (can + base verb)

Expressions with can and could C1

Common fixed phrases: I couldn't agree more. I can't help laughing. I can't stand it. I could do with a coffee. You can't be serious!

ExpressionMeaningExample
couldn't + comparativevery / completelyI couldn't be happier. (I'm extremely happy.)
couldn't agree moreI agree completelyI couldn't agree more.
can't help + -ingcan't stop myselfI can't help laughing at him.
can't stand / bear + -ing / nounreally dislikeI can't stand waiting.
could do with + nounwould like / needI could do with a holiday.
can't wait (to)very excitedI can't wait to see you!

Couldn't + comparative is positive in meaning: Things couldn't be better = things are as good as possible.

  • The timing couldn't have been worse. (the worst possible timing)
  • I can't help thinking about the accident. (can't stop myself)
  • My dad can't stand loud music. (really dislikes)
  • This room could do with a coat of paint. (needs)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I can't help to laugh. → ✅ I can't help laughing. (can't help + -ing)
  • ❌ I could do with to have a rest. → ✅ I could do with a rest. (could do with + noun)

تمرین سطح C1
