اسم مصدر یا مصدر با toGerund or Infinitive (-ing or to…?)

وقتی فعلی بعد از فعل دیگر می‌آید، فعل دوم اغلب یا شکل ‑ing دارد (gerund، اسم مصدر) یا to + شکل سادهٔ فعل (infinitive، مصدر): I enjoy cooking. I want to cook. فعل اول تعیین می‌کند کدام شکل لازم است.

قاعدهٔ ساده‌ای برای همهٔ فعل‌ها نیست، اما الگوهای مفیدی وجود دارد. این صفحه از پایه تا شکل‌های پیشرفته پیش می‌رود. بعضی فعل‌ها با ‑ing یا to معنی‌شان عوض می‌شود: صفحهٔ فعل‌هایی که معنی‌شان عوض می‌شود را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

like, love, hate + ‑ing A1

بعد از like, love, hate, enjoy فعل ‑ing‌دار بیاورید: I like swimming. She loves reading. They hate getting up early. (like / love / hate + to هم درست است.)

برای حرف زدن دربارهٔ کارهایی که دوست دارید یا ندارید، like / love / enjoy / hate / don't mind + ‑ing بیاورید: I love dancing. My dad hates waiting.

بعد از like, love و hate، to + فعل هم درست است، به‌خصوص در انگلیسی آمریکایی: I like to swim / I like swimming. اما بعد از enjoy و don't mind فقط ‑ing درست است: I enjoy swimming (نه enjoy to swim).

املا: swim → swimming, make → making, run → running, lie → lying. قاعده‌ها همان قاعده‌های حال استمراری است.

  • I like playing football. like + ‑ing
  • My sister loves watching films. love + ‑ing
  • We enjoy travelling. enjoy فقط با ‑ing
  • I don't mind cooking. don't mind فقط با ‑ing

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I enjoy to swim. → ✅ I enjoy swimming. enjoy + ‑ing.
  • ❌ She likes play tennis. → ✅ She likes playing tennis. / She likes to play tennis. like + ‑ing یا to.
  • ❌ I don't mind to wait. → ✅ I don't mind waiting. don't mind + ‑ing.

تمرین سطح A1

1. I enjoy ___ to music.
Enjoy فقط با ‑ing.
2. My brother hates ___ the dishes.
Hate + ‑ing (یا to do).
3. We love ___ (go) to the beach in summer.
Love + ‑ing (یا to go).

A2 · مقدماتی

want to, need to, decide to, hope to A2

بعد از want, need, would like, decide, hope, plan, try, learn, forget, promise فعلِ to + شکل ساده بیاورید: I want to travel. She decided to leave.

خیلی از فعل‌هایی که دربارهٔ خواسته، برنامه و تصمیم‌اند to + شکل سادهٔ فعل می‌گیرند: I want to learn Spanish. We hope to see you soon. They decided to move.

شکل منفی not to است: I decided not to go. (در انگلیسیِ دقیق نه decided to not go، هرچند در صحبت رایج است)

  • I need to buy some milk. need to
  • She would like to visit Japan. would like to
  • They plan to get married next year. plan to
  • He promised not to be late. not to

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I want go home. → ✅ I want to go home. want + to.
  • ❌ She decided moving to Spain. → ✅ She decided to move to Spain. decide + to.
  • ❌ I hope seeing you soon. → ✅ I hope to see you soon. hope + to.

enjoy, finish, mind, stop, avoid + ‑ing A2

بعد از enjoy, finish, mind, stop, avoid, keep, suggest, miss, practise فعل ‑ing‌دار بیاورید: Have you finished eating? I can't stop laughing.

بعضی فعل‌ها همیشه ‑ing می‌گیرند، هیچ‌وقت to: I finished working at six. He kept talking. Do you mind waiting?

keep (on) ‑ing یعنی ادامه دادن یا تکرار کردن: She keeps asking me the same question.

stop + ‑ing یعنی دیگر آن کار را نمی‌کنید: I stopped smoking last year. stop + to معنی دیگری دارد. صفحهٔ فعل‌هایی که معنی‌شان عوض می‌شود را ببینید.

  • Have you finished reading the book? finish + ‑ing
  • I miss living near the sea. miss + ‑ing
  • Try to avoid eating late at night. avoid + ‑ing
  • You need to practise speaking every day. practise + ‑ing

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Have you finished to eat? → ✅ Have you finished eating? finish + ‑ing.
  • ❌ I can't avoid to see him. → ✅ I can't avoid seeing him. avoid + ‑ing.
  • ❌ Would you mind to close the window? → ✅ Would you mind closing the window? mind + ‑ing.

تمرین سطح A2

Decide + to + فعل.

Hope + to + فعل.

Finish + ‑ing.

Keep + ‑ing.

Mind + ‑ing.

B1 · متوسط

want someone to do: فعل + مفعول + to B1

want / would like / ask / tell / expect / help + شخص + to + فعل: I want you to come. She asked me to help her. نه I want that you come.

برای گفتن اینکه می‌خواهید شخص دیگری کاری بکند، want + شخص + to + فعل بیاورید: I want you to listen. My parents want me to study medicine.

فعل‌های دیگر با همین الگو: would like, ask, tell, expect, help, teach, allow, advise, remind, encourage: She taught me to drive.

بعد از help شکل سادهٔ فعل بدون to هم می‌آید: Can you help me (to) carry this?

بعد از want جمله‌واره با that نیاورید: I want you to come (نه I want that you come). فارسی می‌گوید «می‌خواهم که بیایی»، اما انگلیسی این ساختار را ندارد. صفحهٔ فعل‌های نقل قول را ببینید.

  • I want you to be happy. want + شخص + to
  • My boss expects us to work hard. expect
  • She asked me to call her back. ask
  • Can you help me move this table? help + شکل سادهٔ فعل (یا to move)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I want that you come with me. → ✅ I want you to come with me. want + شخص + to.
  • ❌ My mum wants me study more. → ✅ My mum wants me to study more. + to.
  • ❌ He told me go home. → ✅ He told me to go home. tell + شخص + to.

بعد از حرف اضافه: ‑ing (interested in learning, before leaving) B1

بعد از حرف اضافه (in, at, of, for, about, before, after, without) فعل ‑ing‌دار بیاورید: I'm interested in learning Chinese. Think before speaking.

فعلی که بعد از حرف اضافه می‌آید همیشه شکل ‑ing دارد: She's good at drawing. Thank you for coming. After finishing work, I went home.

مراقب to باشید وقتی حرف اضافه است، نه بخشی از مصدر: I look forward to seeing you. I'm used to getting up early. (امتحانش کنید: اگر بشود بعدش اسم گذاشت، حرف اضافه است: I look forward to the party.)

  • I'm tired of waiting. of + ‑ing
  • She left without saying goodbye. without + ‑ing
  • Are you interested in joining our club? in + ‑ing
  • I'm looking forward to meeting you. to (حرف اضافه) + ‑ing

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm good at cook. → ✅ I'm good at cooking. حرف اضافه + ‑ing.
  • ❌ I look forward to hear from you. → ✅ I look forward to hearing from you. look forward to + ‑ing.
  • ❌ Before to leave, check the windows. → ✅ Before leaving, check the windows. before + ‑ing.

فعل‌های رایج با ‑ing و با to B1

الگوهای پرکاربرد را یاد بگیرید. + ‑ing: admit, avoid, consider, deny, imagine, keep, mind, miss, practise, risk, suggest. + to: afford, agree, arrange, decide, expect, fail, hope, manage, offer, plan, promise, refuse, seem.

فعل + ‑ingفعل + to + فعل
admit, avoid, consider, denyafford, agree, arrange, decide
enjoy, finish, imagine, involveexpect, fail, hope, learn
keep, mind, miss, practisemanage, offer, plan, pretend
risk, suggest, can't stand, give uppromise, refuse, seem, threaten

بعضی فعل‌ها هر دو را می‌گیرند، با تفاوت معنایی کم یا بدون تفاوت: begin, start, continue, like, love, hate, prefer: It started raining / to rain.

یک نکتهٔ کمکی: خیلی از فعل‌های to به آینده نگاه می‌کنند (hope, plan, promise, decide)؛ خیلی از فعل‌های ‑ing دربارهٔ چیزی‌اند که واقعی است یا در جریان است (enjoy, finish, avoid, admit). قاعدهٔ کاملی نیست، اما کمک می‌کند.

  • Have you considered moving abroad? consider + ‑ing
  • I can't afford to buy a car. afford + to
  • She pretended to be asleep. pretend + to
  • He admitted making a mistake. admit + ‑ing

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I can't afford buying a new phone. → ✅ I can't afford to buy a new phone. afford + to.
  • ❌ We considered to sell the house. → ✅ We considered selling the house. consider + ‑ing.
  • ❌ She managed finishing on time. → ✅ She managed to finish on time. manage + to.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

-ing as a subject; it's worth -ing; it's no use -ing B2

-ing can be the subject: Swimming is good for you. Useful phrases: it's worth -ing, it's no use -ing, there's no point (in) -ing, have difficulty / trouble (in) -ing.

An -ing form can be the subject of a sentence: Learning a language takes time. Smoking is bad for you. The verb is singular.

We can also say It is + adjective + to + verb: It's hard to learn a language. (more common in speech)

Fixed phrases with -ing:

  • it's worth / it isn't worth -ing: This museum is worth visiting.
  • it's no use / it's no good -ing: It's no use complaining.
  • there's no point (in) -ing: There's no point waiting. The bus has gone.
  • have difficulty / trouble / problems (in) -ing: I had trouble finding the hotel.
  • Running every morning keeps me healthy. (-ing as subject)
  • It's easy to get lost in this city. (it + adjective + to)
  • This book is worth reading. (worth + -ing)
  • There's no point arguing with him. (no point + -ing)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Swim is good exercise. → ✅ Swimming is good exercise. (-ing as subject)
  • ❌ It's worth to visit the old town. → ✅ It's worth visiting the old town. (worth + -ing)
  • ❌ I had difficulty to understand him. → ✅ I had difficulty understanding him. (difficulty + -ing)

See someone do or see someone doing? B2

After see, hear, watch, notice, feel: object + base verb = the whole action (I saw him cross the road). object + -ing = part of an action in progress (I saw him crossing the road).

PatternMeaningExample
see + object + base verbthe complete action, start to finishI saw the man get into the car.
see + object + -ingthe action in progress (maybe not finished)I saw the man getting into the car.

Use the base verb (no to) for a short or complete action: I heard someone shout. We watched the plane land.

Use -ing when you saw or heard the action in progress: I heard the children playing in the garden. (They were playing; I didn't hear the whole game.)

In the passive, see / hear + to: He was seen to leave the building. (formal)

  • I saw her drop her phone. (complete action)
  • I saw her talking to the manager. (in progress)
  • Did you hear the baby cry? (short action)
  • I could smell something burning. (in progress)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I saw him to cross the road. → ✅ I saw him cross the road. (see + object + base verb (no to))
  • ❌ I heard someone to knock. → ✅ I heard someone knock. / I heard someone knocking. (no to)

تمرین سطح B2

C1 · پیشرفته

Perfect and passive forms: having done, to have done, being done C1

having + past participle and to have + past participle show an earlier action: He denied having stolen it. She seems to have left. being + past participle and to be + past participle are passive.

FormExample
perfect -ing: having doneI regret having said that.
perfect infinitive: to have doneShe seems to have forgotten.
passive -ing: being doneI hate being interrupted.
passive infinitive: to be doneHe expects to be promoted.
perfect passive: having been done / to have been doneHe's proud of having been chosen.

The perfect forms show that the action happened before the main verb. They're often optional: He admitted taking / having taken the money. (same meaning)

After seem, appear, claim, pretend, be said, the perfect infinitive is needed for a past action: She seems to have lost weight. (not seems to lose, which means something different)

  • Having finished the report, she went home. (earlier action)
  • He claims to have met the President. (earlier action)
  • Nobody likes being ignored. (passive -ing)
  • The house appears to have been empty for years. (perfect infinitive)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She seems to forget my name yesterday. → ✅ She seems to have forgotten my name. (past action: to have + past participle)
  • ❌ He was proud of having chose. → ✅ He was proud of having been chosen. (perfect passive: having been + pp)

تمرین سطح C1

A past action after seem: to have lost.

Passive: being told.
27. He denied ___ (see) the email.
Deny + having seen (or seeing).
