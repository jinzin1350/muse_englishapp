Like, Love, Hate + -ing

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

To talk about activities we like or don't like, we use like, love, hate, enjoy, don't mind, can't stand + verb-ing: I love swimming. She hates getting up early.

From love to hate

VerbExample
loveI love dancing.
like / enjoyI like cooking. I enjoy reading.
don't mindI don't mind washing the dishes.
don't likeI don't like running.
hate / can't standI hate waiting. I can't stand shopping.

We can also use a noun: I love music. I don't like coffee.

After like, love and hate you can also use to + verb: I like to swim. After enjoy, don't mind and can't stand, use only -ing: I enjoy swimming (not enjoy to swim).

Spelling of -ing

  • Most verbs: + ing: read → reading, play → playing
  • Verb ending in -e: drop the e: dance → dancing, write → writing
  • Short verb ending in one vowel + one consonant: double the consonant: swim → swimming, run → running, shop → shopping

Questions and negatives

Use do / does: Do you like cooking? – Yes, I do. Does he like dancing? – No, he doesn't. What do you like doing at the weekend?

Do you like…? = in general. Would you like…? = an offer or invitation for now: Would you like a coffee? See I'd like and Would you like…?

Examples

  1. I love travelling with my friends. (love + -ing)
  2. My sister enjoys playing the piano. (enjoy + -ing)
  3. He doesn't like cooking. (negative)
  4. I don't mind walking. It's not far. (it's OK for me)
  5. We hate waiting for the bus in the rain. (hate + -ing)
  6. She can't stand watching horror films. (strong dislike)
  7. Do you like shopping? (question)
  8. What do you like doing in your free time? (wh- question)
  9. My kids love swimming. (double m)
  10. I like pizza, but I love pasta. (+ noun)

Common mistakes

❌ I enjoy to read.

✅ I enjoy reading. (After enjoy, use -ing.)

❌ She like dancing.

✅ She likes dancing. (He / she / it: likes.)

❌ I love swiming.

✅ I love swimming. (Swim → swimming (double m).)

❌ He doesn't likes cooking.

✅ He doesn't like cooking. (After doesn't, use the base form: like.)

❌ I don't mind to wait.

✅ I don't mind waiting. (After don't mind, use -ing.)

❌ Do you like a coffee? (offering now)

✅ Would you like a coffee? (For offers, use would you like.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. I love ___ in the sea.
love + -ing: swimming (double m).
2. She enjoys ___ books.
enjoy + -ing: reading.
3. My brother hates ___ up early.
hate + -ing: getting (double t).
4. Do you like ___? – Yes, I go to salsa classes.
Dance → dancing (drop the e).
5. I don't mind ___ the dishes.
don't mind + -ing: doing.
6. What ___ doing at the weekend?
Question with do: What do you like doing?
7. Tom ___ playing football. He plays every day.
He → likes.
8. I can't stand ___ in traffic.
can't stand + -ing: sitting (double t).
9. My parents ___ watching horror films. They think they're boring.
They → don't like.
10. She doesn't like ___. She always eats in restaurants.
like + -ing: cooking.
11. Choose the correct sentence.
We + enjoy + -ing: We enjoy walking.
12. I love ___ photos of nature.
Take → taking (drop the e).
13. Does your sister like ___? – No, she takes the bus everywhere.
like + -ing: driving (drop the e).
14. I really ___ shopping. I go every weekend and I have so much fun!
I have fun, so I love it.
15. They love ___ in the park.
Run → running (double n).
16. He ___ like getting up early.
Negative with he: doesn't like.
