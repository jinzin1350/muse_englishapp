فعل‌های حالتی (ایستا)Stative Verbs

فعل‌های حالتی (ایستا) حالت را توصیف می‌کنند، نه کار را: فکر، احساس، حس‌ها، مالکیت و وجود داشتن.

معمولاً این فعل‌ها را در زمان‌های استمراری به کار نمی‌بریم: I know the answer. (نه I'm knowing) She wants a new phone. (نه She's wanting)

A2 · مقدماتی

فعل‌هایی که استمراری نمی‌شوند A2

know, want, like, need, understand, believe, belong… را حتی برای همین حالا به شکل ساده بیاورید.

نوعفعل‌ها
فکر و دانستنknow, believe, understand, remember, forget, mean, realise, recognise
احساس و خواستنlike, love, hate, prefer, want, need
حس‌ها و ظاهرseem, sound, hear
مالکیتhave (به معنی داشتن)، own, belong
حالت‌های دیگرcost, contain, depend, matter

شکل ساده را حتی وقتی منظورتان همین حالاست بیاورید: I'm hungry. I want something to eat. (نه I'm wanting) Do you understand what I mean?

have به معنی «داشتن» حالت است: I have a car. اما have در عبارت‌هایی مثل have lunch, have a shower, have fun کار است، پس استمراری‌اش درست است: We're having lunch.

  • I don't understand this question. (understand)
  • This bag belongs to my sister. (belong)
  • Do you remember her name? (remember)
  • How much does this jacket cost? (cost)
  • We're having dinner at the moment. have به معنی خوردن: کار

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm knowing the answer. → ✅ I know the answer. know فعل حالتی است.
  • ❌ This book is belonging to me. → ✅ This book belongs to me. belong فعل حالتی است.
  • ❌ I'm having a car. → ✅ I have a car. have به معنی داشتن: حالت.

تمرین سطح A2

1. I ___ what you mean.
understand فعل حالتی است: I understand.
2. She ___ a new laptop for university.
need فعل حالتی است: she needs.
3. This phone ___ to my brother.
belong فعل حالتی است: it belongs.
4. جملهٔ درست را انتخاب کنید.
believe فعل حالتی است: I don't believe you.

B1 · متوسط

فعل‌های دومعنایی: think, have, see, taste B1

بعضی فعل‌ها در یک معنا حالت‌اند و در معنای دیگر کار؛ فقط معنای «کار» می‌تواند استمراری شود.

فعلحالت (ساده)کار (استمراری درست است)
thinkI think it's a good idea. (نظر)I'm thinking about my holiday. (فکر کردن به چیزی)
haveShe has two cats. (داشتن)She's having lunch / a shower / a good time.
seeI see what you mean. (فهمیدن)I'm seeing the doctor tomorrow. (دیدار و قرار)
taste / smellThis soup tastes great.He's tasting the soup. (دارد مزه‌اش را امتحان می‌کند)
lookYou look tired. (به نظر رسیدن)Why are you looking at me? (نگاه کردن)
weighThe box weighs 5 kilos.She's weighing the flour.

با feel (یعنی حالِ جسم یا روحیه) هر دو شکل درست‌اند: I feel tired. / I'm feeling tired. اما feel به معنی «فکر کردن» حالت است: I feel you're right.

  • I'm thinking of moving to Montreal. think یعنی فکر کردن به چیزی
  • What do you think of my new haircut? think یعنی نظر
  • We're having a great time. have یعنی تجربه کردن
  • This coffee tastes a bit strange. taste: حالت
  • Look! The cat is looking at the bird. look: کار

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm thinking this is a good idea. → ✅ I think this is a good idea. think به معنی نظر: حالت.
  • ❌ What do you think about? You look worried. → ✅ What are you thinking about? You look worried. think به معنی فکر کردن به چیزی: کار.
  • ❌ This soup is tasting delicious. → ✅ This soup tastes delicious. taste یعنی مزه‌ای که دارد: حالت.

تمرین سطح B1

5. Shh! I ___ about the problem. Give me a minute.
think به معنی فکر کردن به چیزی (کار): I'm thinking.
6. We ___ lunch right now. Can I call you later?
have lunch کار است: we're having.
7. This soup ___ delicious.
taste یعنی مزه‌ای که دارد (حالت): tastes.
8. I ___ the doctor at 3 o'clock tomorrow.
see یعنی دیدار (قرار): I'm seeing.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

He's being rude: be in the continuous, and states in perfect tenses B2

is being + adjective = behaviour right now; state verbs stay simple in perfect tenses (I've known, not I've been knowing).

Be is normally a state: He is rude = that's his character. But be + adjective in the continuous describes behaviour at the moment: He's being rude = he is behaving rudely now (maybe that's not normal for him).

We only do this with adjectives about behaviour: silly, rude, kind, careful, patient, difficult, quiet. Not with things you can't control: He's tall (not He's being tall).

The same rule applies to perfect continuous tenses: use the perfect simple with state verbs: I've known him for years. (not I've been knowing) They had owned the house for twenty years. (not had been owning)

  • You're being very quiet today. Is everything OK? (behaviour now)
  • Stop it! You're being silly. (behaviour now)
  • She's a very patient person. (character: simple)
  • I've known Sam since primary school. (state: perfect simple)
  • We had only had the car for a month when it broke down. (state: past perfect simple)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've been knowing her for years. → ✅ I've known her for years. (Know is a state: perfect simple.)
  • ❌ He's being tall for his age. → ✅ He's tall for his age. (Being + adjective is only for behaviour.)

تمرین سطح B2

9. He ___ very rude today. That's not like him.
Behaviour right now: is being + adjective.
10. I ___ her for ten years.
Know is a state: perfect simple, I've known.
11. Which sentence is correct?
Being + adjective is only for behaviour, not for height.

C1 · پیشرفته

State verbs in the continuous for emphasis or change C1

Informally, some state verbs take the continuous to stress a temporary feeling or a gradual change: We're loving it, I'm regretting it.

With some state verbs, the continuous is possible to stress that a situation is temporary or around now: The kids are loving their new school. You're looking great these days. This is common in informal English.

With verbs of mental states (regret, realise, understand, find), the continuous can show a feeling that is growing or new: I'm regretting my decision more every day. I'm slowly understanding how the system works. Consider (= think about carefully) is usually continuous: He's considering a move to Vancouver.

Some verbs are still very rarely continuous, even informally: believe, doubt, own, belong, consist of, know. In exams and formal writing, use the simple form with state verbs unless you have a clear reason not to.

  • We're loving our new flat! (informal, temporary)
  • You're looking great these days. (around now)
  • I'm regretting that I said yes. (growing feeling)
  • He's considering early retirement. (consider: usually continuous)
  • We're hoping to finish by Friday. (softer, more tentative)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm believing you now. → ✅ I believe you now. (Believe is very rarely continuous.)
  • ❌ This box is consisting of three parts. → ✅ This box consists of three parts. (Consist of is never continuous.)

تمرین سطح C1

12. Which sentence is informal but natural English?
I'm loving… is common in informal English for a temporary feeling.
13. What does I'm regretting my decision suggest?
The continuous shows a feeling that is growing.
14. Which verb is very rarely used in the continuous, even in informal English?
Own stays simple: She owns two cars.
