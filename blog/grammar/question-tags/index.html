Question Tags

Level: B1 · Free grammar guide

A question tag is a short question at the end of a sentence. We use it to check information or to ask someone to agree: It's cold today, isn't it? You don't eat meat, do you?

The basic rule

  • Positive sentence → negative tag: She's French, isn't she?
  • Negative sentence → positive tag: He can't swim, can he?
  • Use the same auxiliary verb as the sentence (be, have, can, will…). If there is no auxiliary, use do / does / did: You live here, don't you? She called you, didn't she?
  • The tag uses a pronoun, not a noun: Your brother works here, doesn't he?

Question tags in different tenses

SentenceTag
You're tired,aren't you?
He isn't coming,is he?
They live in Rome,don't they?
She works with you,doesn't she?
You saw the film,didn't you?
We've met before,haven't we?
You can drive,can't you?
It won't rain,will it?

Special cases

  • I am → aren't I: I'm late, aren't I?
  • Let's → shall we: Let's go, shall we?
  • Imperatives → will you / can you / would you: Close the door, will you?
  • Never, nobody, nothing, hardly make the sentence negative, so the tag is positive: You never call me, do you?
  • There is / there are → isn't there / aren't there: There's a problem, isn't there?
  • Everyone, someone, nobody → they: Everyone's here, aren't they?

Sometimes people use a positive tag after a positive sentence to show surprise or interest: So you're moving to Spain, are you? In the exercises below, choose the normal tag for checking information.

Intonation

If your voice goes down at the end, you're fairly sure and you want the other person to agree. If your voice goes up, it's a real question and you aren't sure of the answer.

Examples

  1. It's a beautiful day, isn't it? (be)
  2. You don't like coffee, do you? (negative → positive)
  3. She lives near here, doesn't she? (present simple → does)
  4. They didn't come, did they? (past simple → did)
  5. You've been to Paris, haven't you? (present perfect → have)
  6. He can speak French, can't he? (modal)
  7. I'm right, aren't I? (I am → aren't I)
  8. Let's have a break, shall we? (let's → shall we)
  9. Pass me that book, will you? (imperative)
  10. Nobody called, did they? (nobody → they + positive tag)

Common mistakes

❌ You're a student, aren't we?

✅ You're a student, aren't you? (Use the same subject (as a pronoun).)

❌ She lives here, isn't she?

✅ She lives here, doesn't she? (Present simple → do/does.)

❌ You don't like fish, don't you?

✅ You don't like fish, do you? (Negative sentence → positive tag.)

❌ I'm late, amn't I?

✅ I'm late, aren't I? (I am → aren't I.)

❌ Your brother is a doctor, isn't your brother?

✅ Your brother is a doctor, isn't he? (Use a pronoun in the tag.)

❌ You never call me, don't you?

✅ You never call me, do you? (Never is negative, so the tag is positive.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct question tag.

1. It's cold today, ___?
Positive it's (it is) → negative tag: isn't it.
2. They didn't call, ___?
Negative past simple → positive tag: did they.
3. She lives in Toronto, ___?
Present simple with she → doesn't she.
4. You don't eat meat, ___?
Negative sentence → positive tag: do you.
5. You've seen this film, ___?
Present perfect (have) → haven't you.
6. Let's go to the beach, ___?
Let's → shall we.
7. I'm late, ___?
I am → aren't I.
8. He can swim, ___?
Same auxiliary (can): can't he.
9. There's a bus stop near here, ___?
There is → isn't there.
10. Your sister works in a bank, ___?
Use a pronoun and does: doesn't she.
11. We met at the conference, ___?
Past simple → didn't we.
12. You never tell me anything, ___?
Never makes the sentence negative → positive tag: do you.
13. You aren't angry with me, ___?
Negative sentence → positive tag: are you.
14. Nobody was at home, ___?
Nobody → they, and the sentence is negative → were they.
15. Close the window, ___?
Imperative → will you.
16. He won't be late, ___?
Negative won't → positive tag: will he.
