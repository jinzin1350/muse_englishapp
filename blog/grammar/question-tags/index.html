سؤال‌های کوتاه آخر جمله (Question Tags)Question Tags (…, isn't it? …, don't you?)

سؤال ضمیمه (question tag) یک سؤال کوتاه در آخر جمله است، مثل «…، نه؟» یا «…، مگه نه؟» در فارسی: It's a nice day, isn't it? با آن اطلاعات را چک می‌کنیم یا از شنونده می‌خواهیم موافقت کند.

معمولاً جملهٔ مثبت سؤال ضمیمهٔ منفی می‌گیرد و جملهٔ منفی سؤال ضمیمهٔ مثبت. سؤال ضمیمه همان فعل کمکیِ جمله را تکرار می‌کند.

B1 · B2 · C1

B1 · متوسط

جملهٔ مثبت، سؤال ضمیمهٔ منفی (و برعکس) B1

مثبت + سؤال ضمیمهٔ منفی: You're Iranian, aren't you? منفی + سؤال ضمیمهٔ مثبت: She doesn't eat meat, does she? همان فعل کمکیِ جمله را بیاورید.

جملهسؤال ضمیمه
You're a student,aren't you?
She can swim,can't she?
They live here,don't they? (حال ساده ← do)
He went home,didn't he? (گذشتهٔ ساده ← did)
You haven't finished,have you?
It won't rain,will it?

فعل کمکیِ جمله را بیاورید (be, have, can, will…). اگر جمله فعل کمکی ندارد، do / does / did بیاورید.

در سؤال ضمیمه ضمیر می‌آید، نه اسم: Ali works here, doesn't he? (نه doesn't Ali)

  • It's hot today, isn't it? be
  • You like football, don't you? حال ساده ← don't
  • Your sister lives in Rome, doesn't she? he / she ← doesn't
  • They didn't call, did they? منفی ← مثبت

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You're tired, isn't it? → ✅ You're tired, aren't you? همان فعل و فاعل: aren't you. (فارسی‌زبان‌ها برای «نه؟» همیشه isn't it می‌گذارند، که غلط است.)
  • ❌ She went home, wasn't she? → ✅ She went home, didn't she? گذشتهٔ ساده ← did.
  • ❌ Tom is here, isn't Tom? → ✅ Tom is here, isn't he? سؤال ضمیمه ضمیر می‌گیرد.

لحن صدا: سؤال واقعی یا درخواست موافقت؟ B1

لحنِ رو به بالا (↗) یعنی سؤال واقعی است، مطمئن نیستم. لحنِ رو به پایین (↘) یعنی مطمئنم و می‌خواهم شما موافقت کنید.

معنی سؤال ضمیمه به لحن صدای شما بستگی دارد:

  • رو به بالا (↗): جواب را نمی‌دانید و واقعاً می‌پرسید: You're coming tonight, aren't you? ↗ (مطمئن نیستم.)
  • رو به پایین (↘): تقریباً مطمئنید و می‌خواهید شنونده موافقت کند: It's a lovely day, isn't it? ↘ (مطمئنم که همین‌طور است.)

سؤال ضمیمه با لحن رو به پایین در گپ‌های کوتاه و روزمره خیلی رایج است: دربارهٔ هوا، غذا یا تجربه‌ای که با هم داشته‌اید.

  • It's really cold, isn't it? ↘ درخواست موافقت
  • You've got the tickets, haven't you? ↗ چک کردن: مطمئن نیستم
  • That was a fantastic film, wasn't it? ↘ موافقت
  • The meeting's at three, isn't it? ↗ چک کردن

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It's a lovely day, is it? (asking for agreement) → ✅ It's a lovely day, isn't it? جملهٔ مثبت ← سؤال ضمیمهٔ منفی.

تمرین سطح B1

1. You're from Canada, ___?
جملهٔ مثبت با are ← aren't you?
2. She works in a bank, ___?
حال ساده ← doesn't she?
3. They haven't arrived yet, ___? (two words)
جملهٔ منفی ← سؤال ضمیمهٔ مثبت: have they?
4. مطمئن نیستید دوستتان ماشین دارد یا نه. جملهٔ You've got a car, haven't you? را چطور می‌گویید؟
سؤال واقعی: لحن رو به بالا.
5. لحنِ رو به پایین در سؤال ضمیمه معمولاً چه معنایی دارد؟
رو به پایین یعنی درخواست موافقت.
6. You and a friend are looking at a sunset. You say: It's beautiful, ___? (two words)
درخواست موافقت (لحن رو به پایین).

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Special tags: aren't I, let's… shall we, imperatives, there is B2

I am → aren't I? Let's → shall we? Imperatives → will you? / won't you? / can you? There is → isn't there? Everybody → they.

SentenceTag
I'm late,aren't I? (not amn't I)
Let's go,shall we?
Open the window,will you? / can you? / could you?
Don't be late,will you?
There's a problem,isn't there?
Everybody's here,aren't they?
Nothing happened,did it?

After everyone, everybody, someone, nobody, the tag uses they: Somebody called, didn't they? After everything, nothing, use it: Everything's ready, isn't it?

  • I'm right, aren't I? (I am → aren't I)
  • Let's have a break, shall we? (let's → shall we)
  • Pass me the salt, would you? (imperative → would you)
  • There's no milk, is there? (there is + negative word → is there)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm late, amn't I? → ✅ I'm late, aren't I? (I am → aren't I)
  • ❌ Let's go, don't we? → ✅ Let's go, shall we? (let's → shall we)
  • ❌ Nobody came, did he? → ✅ Nobody came, did they? (nobody → they)

Never, hardly, no one: negative words take a positive tag B2

Sentences with never, hardly, rarely, no, nobody, nothing are negative, so the tag is positive: You never call me, do you?

Words like never, seldom, rarely, hardly, scarcely, no, nobody, nothing, nowhere make the sentence negative, even without not. So use a positive tag: She hardly eats anything, does she?

Remember the pronoun rules: Nobody knows, do they? Nothing happened, did it?

  • You never listen to me, do you? (never → positive tag)
  • He hardly ever smiles, does he? (hardly → positive tag)
  • No one was hurt, were they? (no one → they)
  • There's nothing we can do, is there? (nothing → positive tag)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You never call me, don't you? → ✅ You never call me, do you? (never → positive tag)
  • ❌ Nobody saw it, didn't they? → ✅ Nobody saw it, did they? (nobody → positive tag)

تمرین سطح B2

7. I'm in the right room, ___?
I am → aren't I?
8. Let's watch a film, ___?
Let's → shall we?
9. There's a bank near here, ___? (two words)
There is → isn't there?
10. You've never been to Spain, ___?
Never = negative → positive tag.
11. She hardly ever goes out, ___?
Hardly = negative → positive tag.
12. Nobody called while I was out, ___? (two words)
Nobody → positive tag with they.

C1 · پیشرفته

Same-way tags and tags in requests C1

Positive + positive tags show surprise, interest or irony: So you're the new boss, are you? Negative sentence + positive tag in polite requests: You couldn't help me, could you?

Same-way tags (positive sentence + positive tag) don't really ask for information. They show surprise, interest, or sarcasm, and they often follow so or oh: Oh, you're a chef, are you? So you think it's funny, do you?

In polite requests, a negative statement with a positive tag sounds tentative and polite: You couldn't lend me £10, could you? You wouldn't have a pen, would you?

I don't suppose … , do you / could you? is another tentative pattern: I don't suppose you know where she is, do you?

Same-way tags with an angry tone can sound aggressive: Oh, you want a fight, do you? Use them carefully.

  • So you've finally arrived, have you? (surprise / irony)
  • You're moving to Japan, are you? How exciting! (interest)
  • You couldn't open the window, could you? (polite request)
  • I don't suppose you've seen my keys, have you? (tentative question)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You couldn't help me, couldn't you? → ✅ You couldn't help me, could you? (negative sentence → positive tag)

تمرین سطح C1

13. Which sentence shows surprise or interest?
Positive + positive tag = surprise or interest.
14. Which is the most polite request?
Negative sentence + positive tag sounds tentative and polite.
15. I don't suppose you know the answer, ___? (two words)
I don't suppose…, do you?
