Could for Past Ability

Level: A2 · Free grammar guide

Could is the past of can for ability. Use it for general abilities in the past: I could swim when I was five.

The negative is couldn't: I couldn't sleep last night.

How to form could and couldn't

FormExample
Positivesubject + could + base verbShe could play the violin at six.
Negativesubject + couldn't (could not) + base verbWe couldn't find the hotel.
QuestionCould + subject + base verb?Could you read when you were four?
Short answersYes, + subject + could. / No, + subject + couldn't.Yes, I could. / No, I couldn't.

Like can, could is the same for every person, with no -s and no to: He could run fast. (not He coulds or could to run)

Could or was able to?

For a general ability in the past, use could: My grandfather could speak five languages. For one specific success in a particular situation, use was / were able to or managed to, not could: The fire was big, but everyone was able to escape. (not could escape)

In the negative, couldn't is fine for both: I couldn't find my keys this morning.

With verbs of the senses (see, hear, smell, taste, feel) and with understand and remember, could is fine for a specific situation: I could see the mountains from my window.

Examples

  1. I could read before I started school. (general ability)
  2. She couldn't speak English when she arrived in Canada. (negative)
  3. Could you swim when you were a child? (question)
  4. We couldn't go to the beach because it was raining. (impossible in the past)
  5. My dad could run very fast when he was young. (general ability)
  6. I couldn't find a parking space. (negative, one situation)
  7. After two hours, we managed to fix the car. (one success)
  8. The test was hard, but I was able to answer all the questions. (one success)
  9. I could hear music from the next room. (senses)
  10. They couldn't understand the instructions. (negative)

Common mistakes

❌ I could to swim when I was six.

✅ I could swim when I was six. (No to after could.)

❌ She coulds play the violin.

✅ She could play the violin. (Could never takes -s.)

❌ I didn't could sleep.

✅ I couldn't sleep. (Negative: couldn't. No did.)

❌ Last night I could finish my essay on time.

✅ Last night I managed to finish my essay on time. (One specific success: managed to or was able to.)

❌ Could you to ride a bike?

✅ Could you ride a bike? (Could + base verb.)

❌ When I was five, I can swim.

✅ When I was five, I could swim. (Past ability: could.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. When I was four, I ___ read.
General ability in the past: could + base verb.
2. My grandmother ___ play the piano beautifully.
A general past ability: could.
3. I ___ sleep last night because of the noise.
Negative past ability: couldn't.
4. ___ you speak English when you were a child?
Past ability question: Could + subject + base verb?
5. The exam was difficult, but I ___ pass it.
One specific success: was able to, not could.
6. Could you ride a bike when you were five? – Yes, I ___.
Short answer: Yes, I could.
7. She ___ swim when she was three.
General past ability: could.
8. We ___ go to the park because it was raining.
Not possible in the past: couldn't. (Weren't able needs to.)
9. Choose the correct sentence.
Couldn't + base verb.
10. I tried for hours, and finally I ___ open the box.
One success after trying hard: managed to.
11. I ___ hear the birds from my bedroom window this morning.
With verbs of the senses, could is fine for one situation.
12. When he was young, he ___ run 10 kilometres easily.
General past ability: could.
13. Which sentence is about one specific success in the past?
Were able to describes one success in a particular situation.
14. ___ you understand the film? It was in Italian.
Past ability question: Could you understand…? (understand works with could)
15. They ___ understand the teacher because she spoke too fast.
Negative past ability: couldn't.
16. When I was a teenager, I ___ stay out late. My parents were very strict.
Not allowed / not possible in the past: couldn't.
