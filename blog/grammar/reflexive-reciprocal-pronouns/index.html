Reflexive Pronouns (myself) and Each Other

Level: B1 · Free grammar guide

We use reflexive pronouns (myself, yourself, himself…) when the subject and the object are the same person: I cut myself.

We use each other when two or more people do something to the other person: Tom and Anna love each other.

The reflexive pronouns

SubjectReflexiveExample
ImyselfI hurt myself.
you (one person)yourselfHelp yourself to a drink.
hehimselfHe looked at himself in the mirror.
sheherselfShe taught herself to code.
ititselfThe door closed by itself.
weourselvesWe enjoyed ourselves.
you (more than one)yourselvesBehave yourselves, children!
theythemselvesThey made the cake themselves.

Three uses

  • Same person as the subject: She burned herself on the cooker. Enjoy yourself!
  • Emphasis (that person and nobody else): I painted the house myself. The president himself opened the hospital.
  • by + reflexive = alone, without help: He lives by himself. I did it by myself.

Verbs that usually don't need a reflexive pronoun

In English, we don't usually use a reflexive pronoun with relax, feel, concentrate, meet, get up, sit down, and usually not with wash, dress, shave: I relaxed on the sofa (not relaxed myself). I feel tired. He got dressed quickly.

Use wash / dress myself only when it's a special effort: My son is three, but he can already dress himself.

Each other or themselves?

They looked at themselves = each person looked at himself or herself (in a mirror). They looked at each other = A looked at B and B looked at A. One another means the same as each other.

Examples

  1. Be careful! Don't cut yourself. (same person)
  2. We really enjoyed ourselves at the wedding. (enjoy yourself)
  3. She lives by herself in a small flat. (by herself = alone)
  4. I fixed my bike myself. (emphasis)
  5. The kids made breakfast themselves. (emphasis)
  6. Please help yourselves to some food. (plural you)
  7. The light turns off by itself. (it)
  8. My sister and I phone each other every day. (each other)
  9. Sam and Lisa met at university. They've known each other for ten years. (each other)
  10. I can't concentrate with this noise. (no reflexive)

Common mistakes

❌ He hurt him when he fell.

✅ He hurt himself when he fell. (Same person as the subject: himself.)

❌ They did it theirselves.

✅ They did it themselves. (The correct form is themselves.)

❌ I relaxed myself after work.

✅ I relaxed after work. (Relax doesn't need a reflexive pronoun.)

❌ We enjoyed very much.

✅ We enjoyed ourselves very much. (Enjoy needs an object: enjoy yourself, enjoy the party.)

❌ Tom and Anna love themselves. (Tom loves Anna and Anna loves Tom.)

✅ Tom and Anna love each other. (Doing it to the other person: each other.)

❌ I feel myself tired.

✅ I feel tired. (Feel doesn't need a reflexive pronoun.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. Be careful with that knife. Don't cut ___.
Same person as the subject: yourself.
2. We really enjoyed ___ at the party.
enjoy + reflexive: ourselves.
3. Tom and Lisa love ___. They're getting married next month.
Tom loves Lisa and Lisa loves Tom: each other.
4. She lives by ___ in a small flat.
by + reflexive = alone: by herself.
5. The children made the cake ___. Nobody helped them.
Emphasis, without help: themselves.
6. I don't need help. I can do it ___.
Emphasis: myself.
7. Everyone, please help ___ to some food and drinks.
Plural you: yourselves.
8. Nobody touched it. The door closed by ___.
by + reflexive: by itself.
9. My sister and I phone ___ every day.
I phone her and she phones me: each other.
10. Did you paint the house ___? – Yes, we did it all without help.
Plural you (we did it), emphasis: yourselves.
11. Choose the correct sentence.
Relax doesn't need a reflexive pronoun: I relaxed on the sofa.
12. He looked at ___ in the mirror.
Same person as the subject: himself.
13. After their big argument, Sam and Anna don't talk to ___ anymore.
Sam doesn't talk to Anna and Anna doesn't talk to Sam: each other.
14. I hurt ___ when I fell off my bike.
Same person as the subject: myself.
15. The kids are old enough to dress ___.
A special effort for children: dress themselves.
16. The president ___ opened the new hospital.
Emphasis: the president himself.
