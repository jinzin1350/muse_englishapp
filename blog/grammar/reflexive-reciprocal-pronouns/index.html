ضمیرهای انعکاسی و each otherReflexive Pronouns and Each Other

Use a reflexive pronoun when the subject and the object are the same person: I cut myself. She taught herself English.

Use each other when two or more people do something to one another: They love each other.

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

Myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, themselves A2

Use a reflexive pronoun when the subject and object are the same: I hurt myself. They enjoyed themselves.

SubjectReflexive pronoun
Imyself
you (one person)yourself
he / she / ithimself / herself / itself
weourselves
you (more than one)yourselves
theythemselves

Compare: She looked at her. (another woman) / She looked at herself. (in the mirror)

Spelling: singular -self, plural -selves. Say himself and themselves (not hisself, theirselves).

  • I cut myself while I was cooking. (myself)
  • Be careful! Don't hurt yourself. (yourself)
  • The cat is washing itself. (itself)
  • We introduced ourselves to the new neighbours. (ourselves)
  • She taught herself to play the guitar. (herself)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He hurt hisself. → ✅ He hurt himself. (himself)
  • ❌ They enjoyed theirselves. → ✅ They enjoyed themselves. (themselves)
  • ❌ We looked at ourself in the mirror. → ✅ We looked at ourselves in the mirror. (plural: -selves)

تمرین سطح A2

1. Ali fell off his bike and hurt ___.
He → himself.
2. Mina and Ali, help ___ to some cake!
More than one person: yourselves.
3. We really enjoyed ___ at the party. (one word)
We → ourselves.

B1 · متوسط

Enjoy yourself, help yourself, by myself; relax, feel, meet B1

Some verbs often take a reflexive pronoun: enjoy yourself, help yourself, behave yourself, introduce yourself. Many verbs usually don't in English: relax, feel, concentrate, meet, wash, get dressed. By myself = alone.

Often reflexiveUsually not reflexive
enjoy yourself (have a good time)relax (not relax yourself)
help yourself (take what you want)feel (not feel myself tired)
behave yourselfconcentrate
hurt / cut / burn yourselfwash, shave, get dressed
introduce yourselfmeet (We met in 2010.)
by myself = alone, without helpworry

Enjoy needs an object: Did you enjoy yourself? Did you enjoy the party? (not Did you enjoy?)

Use wash, shave and get dressed without myself unless you want to emphasise it: I got up, washed and got dressed.

  • Enjoy yourselves! (enjoy)
  • Please help yourself to coffee. (help yourself)
  • I feel much better today. (not reflexive)
  • She lives by herself. (alone)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Relax yourself and sit down. → ✅ Relax and sit down. (relax: not reflexive)
  • ❌ I feel myself tired. → ✅ I feel tired. (feel: not reflexive)
  • ❌ We met ourselves at university. → ✅ We met at university. (meet: not reflexive)
  • ❌ Did you enjoy? → ✅ Did you enjoy yourself? (enjoy needs an object)

Each other and one another B1

Use each other (or one another) when two or more people do something to the other(s): Ali and Sara love each other. Compare: They blamed themselves (each blamed himself or herself) / They blamed each other (A blamed B, and B blamed A).

ReflexiveReciprocal
They blamed themselves. (each one blamed himself or herself)They blamed each other. (A blamed B, B blamed A)
We hurt ourselves.We hurt each other.

Each other's = possessive: They borrowed each other's notes.

One another means the same and is a little more formal: The students helped one another.

Some verbs don't need each other: They met in 2010. They kissed. They got married.

  • My brother and I call each other every day. (each other)
  • They don't talk to each other any more. (each other)
  • We read each other's essays. (each other's)
  • The team members support one another. (one another)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ They love themselves very much. (meaning A loves B) → ✅ They love each other very much. (each other)
  • ❌ We send messages each other. → ✅ We send messages to each other. (to each other)
  • ❌ They know each others. → ✅ They know each other. (no -s)

تمرین سطح B1

4. Did you enjoy ___ at the concert?
Enjoy yourself = have a good time.
5. I can't ___. It's too noisy here.
Concentrate: not reflexive.
6. There's food in the kitchen. Help ___! (one word)
Help yourself = take what you want.
7. Sara and Mina are best friends. They tell ___ everything.
A tells B, B tells A: each other.
8. The two boys looked at ___ across the table and laughed.
A looked at B, B at A: each other.
9. My parents have known each ___ for 40 years. (one word)
Each other.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Emphatic myself: I did it myself B2

Use a reflexive pronoun for emphasis: I made the cake myself (= I made it, not someone else). The manager herself called me (= the manager, not her assistant).

PositionExampleMeaning
end of the clauseI painted the house myself.I did it, without help
after the nounThe president himself came to the opening.the president, not someone less important
by myselfI painted it by myself.alone, without help

An emphatic reflexive can be removed and the sentence is still correct: I made it (myself). This is the difference from normal reflexives (I hurt myself).

Compare: She herself told me. (she, not another person) / She told herself… (she said it to herself)

  • Did you make this dress yourself? (emphatic)
  • The hotel itself was nice, but the area was noisy. (the hotel, not the area)
  • I myself have never been to Japan. (emphasis on I)
  • We built the shed ourselves. (without help)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I fixed the car by me. → ✅ I fixed the car by myself. (by myself)
  • ❌ The director hisself answered the phone. → ✅ The director himself answered the phone. (himself)

تمرین سطح B2

10. Did you paint this picture ___? It's beautiful!
Emphatic: yourself (= you, without help).
11. The food was fine, but the restaurant ___ was dirty.
Emphatic: the restaurant itself.
12. Nobody helped me. I did it all ___. (one word)
Emphatic: myself.

C1 · پیشرفته

Me or myself? Reflexives after prepositions C1

Don't use myself instead of me / I to sound polite: Please contact me (not contact myself). After prepositions of place, use an object pronoun: She put the bag beside her. Use a reflexive when the object is the same person as the subject: He's talking to himself.

CorrectCommon mistake
Please contact me or my colleague.Please contact myself or my colleague.
My wife and I will attend.My wife and myself will attend.
She closed the door behind her.She closed the door behind herself. (unusual)
He was talking to himself.He was talking to him. (= to another man)
Look after yourself! Take care of yourself!

Myself as an object is correct when the subject is I (I hurt myself) or for emphasis (I did it myself). Using it as a “polite” subject or object is a common mistake in business English.

After prepositions of place, use object pronouns: She looked around her. He put his bag next to him. Use a reflexive when the meaning is “the same person”: She was angry with herself.

  • If you have questions, please ask me. (not myself)
  • My colleague and I will present the results. (not myself)
  • She looked around her nervously. (place: object pronoun)
  • He was very pleased with himself. (same person: reflexive)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Please send the report to myself. → ✅ Please send the report to me. (not myself)
  • ❌ Myself and Ali organised the event. → ✅ Ali and I organised the event. (subject: I)
  • ❌ She had no money with herself. → ✅ She had no money with her. (with + object pronoun)

تمرین سطح C1

13. If you have any questions, please contact ___.
Object: me (not myself).
14. She put her bag on the seat next to ___.
After a preposition of place: her.
15. Don't be so hard on ___. Everyone makes mistakes. (one word)
Same person: yourself.
