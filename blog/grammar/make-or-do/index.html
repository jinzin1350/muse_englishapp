make یا doMake or Do?

make معمولاً یعنی چیز تازه‌ای را ساختن یا درست کردن: make a cake, make a plan. do برای فعالیت‌ها، کار و وظیفه‌ها است: do your homework, do the dishes. (در فارسی هر دو اغلب «کردن» یا «درست کردن» ترجمه می‌شوند.)

خیلی از عبارت‌ها از این قاعده پیروی نمی‌کنند، پس آن‌ها را جفتی یاد بگیرید: make a mistake (نه do a mistake)، do someone a favour (نه make a favour).

A2 · B1 · B2

A2 · مقدماتی

make a cake, do your homework A2

make یعنی ساختن یا تولید کردن (make a cake, make tea, make a mess). do برای فعالیت‌ها، کارها و وظیفه‌هاست (do homework, do the dishes, do sport)، و برای کارهای کلی با something, anything, nothing, what.

make (ساختن، تولید کردن)do (فعالیت‌ها، کار)
make a cake / dinner / tea / coffeedo your homework / an exercise
make a mess / a noisedo the dishes / the shopping / the cleaning
make the beddo the housework / the laundry
make clothes / a film / a listdo sport / yoga / exercise
make a phone calldo something / nothing / anything

برای فعالیت‌های کلی do بیاورید: What are you doing? I'm not doing anything. What do you do? (یعنی شغلتان چیست؟)

  • I'll make some coffee. تولید کردن
  • Have you done your homework? وظیفه
  • Don't make a mess in the kitchen! تولید کردن
  • Who does the shopping in your family? کار خانه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I need to make my homework. → ✅ I need to do my homework. do homework.
  • ❌ Can you do a cake for the party? → ✅ Can you make a cake for the party? make یعنی درست کردن.
  • ❌ I always do my bed in the morning. → ✅ I always make my bed in the morning. make the bed (رختخواب را مرتب کردن).

تمرین سطح A2

1. Can you help me ___ dinner tonight?
درست کردن غذا: make dinner.
2. I ___ my homework every evening.
وظیفه: do homework.
3. Shh! Don't ___ a noise. The baby's asleep. (one word)
Make a noise (سروصدا کردن).

B1 · متوسط

make a mistake, do me a favour B1

جفت‌های ثابتِ رایج. با make: a mistake, a decision, money, progress, an effort, friends, a plan, an appointment, an excuse, a complaint, a suggestion. با do: a favour, business, your best, damage, good, harm, research, a course, an exam (بریتانیایی).

makedo
make a mistakedo someone a favour
make a decision / a choicedo your best
make money / a profitdo business
make progress / an effortdo damage / harm / good
make friendsdo research
make a plan / an appointmentdo a course / a degree
make an excuse / a complaintdo an exam / a test (بریتانیایی؛ take هم می‌گویند)

این‌ها هم‌آیی هستند، یعنی کلمه‌هایی که با هم می‌آیند: قاعدهٔ دقیقی ندارند، پس کل عبارت را یاد بگیرید.

  • Everyone makes mistakes. make a mistake (اشتباه کردن)
  • Could you do me a favour? do a favour (لطف کردن)
  • She's making good progress with her English. make progress (پیشرفت کردن)
  • The storm did a lot of damage. do damage (خسارت زدن)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I did a big mistake. → ✅ I made a big mistake. make a mistake.
  • ❌ Can you make me a favour? → ✅ Can you do me a favour? do a favour.
  • ❌ He does a lot of money. → ✅ He makes a lot of money. make money (پول درآوردن).

It made me laugh؛ she made me wait B1

make + شخص + شکل سادهٔ فعل یعنی کسی را وادار کردن یا باعث شدن کسی کاری بکند: The film made me laugh. My boss made me work late. make + شخص + صفت: It made me happy. مجهول: I was made to wait.

الگومثال
make + شخص + شکل سادهٔ فعلOnions make me cry. (نه make me to cry)
make + شخص + صفتThe news made her angry.
be made to + فعل (مجهول)We were made to wait for an hour.
make yourself + اسم مفعولI couldn't make myself understood.

در جملهٔ معلوم to نمی‌آید: She made me stay. در جملهٔ مجهول to برمی‌گردد: I was made to stay.

  • That joke made me laugh. make + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • The long wait made everyone tired. make + صفت
  • The teacher made us rewrite the essay. وادار کردن
  • He was made to apologise. مجهول: to

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ My mum made me to clean my room. → ✅ My mum made me clean my room. در جملهٔ معلوم to نمی‌آید.
  • ❌ The story made me sadly. → ✅ The story made me sad. make + صفت.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Make sense, make do with, could do with B2

Useful expressions: make sense (be logical), make sure (check), make up your mind (decide), make the most of (use well), make do with (manage with less), make it (arrive / succeed), could do with (want, need), do without (manage without), have (nothing) to do with.

ExpressionMeaningExample
make sensebe logicalThis doesn't make sense.
make up your minddecideHave you made up your mind?
make the most ofuse something wellMake the most of the sunshine!
make do withmanage with something not idealWe'll have to make do with a small flat.
make itarrive in time; succeedWe made it just in time. She made it as a singer.
could do withwant or needI could do with a coffee.
do withoutmanage withoutI can't do without my phone.
have (nothing) to do withbe (not) connected toIt has nothing to do with you.

Could do with is very common in British English for something you'd like or need: This room could do with a clean.

  • Your story doesn't make sense. (make sense)
  • I could do with a holiday. (could do with)
  • We missed the bus, but we still made it to the concert. (make it)
  • His job has something to do with computers. (to do with)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It doesn't do sense. → ✅ It doesn't make sense. (make sense)
  • ❌ I can't make without coffee in the morning. → ✅ I can't do without coffee in the morning. (do without)

تمرین سطح B2

10. I can't decide which dress to buy. I can't ___ up my mind.
Make up your mind.
11. I'm so tired. I ___ a long sleep.
Could do with = need.
12. The train leaves in five minutes. We'll never ___ it! (one word)
Make it = arrive in time.
