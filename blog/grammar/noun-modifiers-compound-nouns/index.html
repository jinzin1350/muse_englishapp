Noun Modifiers and Compound Nouns

Level: C1 · Free grammar guide

In English, a noun can describe another noun: a bus stop, a shoe shop, a coffee cup. The first noun works like an adjective, and the second noun is the main one: a shoe shop is a kind of shop.

Some combinations are written as one word (toothbrush), some as two (bus stop) and some with a hyphen (a ten-minute walk).

The first noun is usually singular

a shoe shop (not shoes shop), a toothbrush (not teethbrush), a taxi driver, a book club.

Some are plural by tradition: a sports car, a clothes shop, a savings account, the sales department, a goods train.

Numbers + noun: hyphen and no -s

When a number + noun comes before another noun, use a hyphen and a singular noun: a ten-minute walk, a five-year-old girl, a five-star hotel, a million-dollar budget, a two-hour meeting.

Compare: The walk takes ten minutes. She's five years old. (not before a noun, so plural)

Noun + noun, 's or of?

FormUseExample
noun + nouna type of thing, its purpose or materiala coffee cup (a cup for coffee), a glass door, a football match
… of …a container with its contents, or a parta cup of coffee (a cup with coffee in it), the end of the film
'spossession by a person or animalmy sister's car, the dog's bed

In speech, compound nouns usually have the stress on the first part: BUS stop, TOOTHbrush, FOOTball match.

Examples

  1. Where's the nearest bus stop? (two words)
  2. Put the plates in the dishwasher. (one word)
  3. It's a ten-minute walk from here. (number + noun)
  4. They have a five-year-old son. (age before a noun)
  5. We stayed in a five-star hotel. (number + noun)
  6. He drives a sports car. (plural first noun)
  7. I need to open a savings account. (plural first noun)
  8. Could I have a glass of water? (container + contents)
  9. The football match was cancelled. (type of match)
  10. Our team meeting is at 9. (noun + noun)

Common mistakes

❌ She works in a shoes shop.

✅ She works in a shoe shop. (The first noun is usually singular.)

❌ It's a ten-minutes walk.

✅ It's a ten-minute walk. (Number + noun before a noun: singular, with a hyphen.)

❌ My son is a five-years-old boy.

✅ My son is a five-year-old boy. (No -s in a compound before a noun.)

❌ I'd like a coffee cup, please. (You want to drink coffee.)

✅ I'd like a cup of coffee, please. (Cup of coffee = a cup with coffee in it.)

❌ He drives a sport car.

✅ He drives a sports car. (Fixed compound: sports car.)

❌ The stop of the bus is there.

✅ The bus stop is there. (Use the compound noun.)
