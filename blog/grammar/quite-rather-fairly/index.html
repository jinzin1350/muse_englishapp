Quite, Rather, Fairly and Pretty

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

Fairly, quite, pretty and rather go before adjectives and adverbs to mean “more than a little, but not very”: The test was fairly easy. It's pretty cold today.

Their exact meaning depends on the word after them, on your voice, and on British or American English.

A2 · Elementary

A bit, fairly, quite, very: how strong? A2

WordExampleMeaning
a bit / a littleIt's a bit cold.slightly (often negative)
fairlyThe exam was fairly easy.more than a little, not very
quite / prettyThe film was quite good. It's pretty cold.a bit more than fairly (pretty is informal)
very / reallyI'm very tired.strong
extremelyIt's extremely dangerous.very strong

  • I'm a bit hungry. (slightly)
  • My English is fairly good. (not very)
  • The restaurant was pretty busy. (informal)
  • She's really kind. (strong)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ It's very much cold. → ✅ It's very cold. (very + adjective)
  • ❌ I'm a few tired. → ✅ I'm a bit tired. (a bit + adjective)

Practice: A2

1. It's <span class="gap">___</span> expensive. Let's look for a cheaper one.
Slightly (negative): a bit.
2. Their flat is <span class="gap">___</span> big. It has four bedrooms.
Quite + adjective.
3. It's <span class="gap">___</span> big house. It has five bedrooms. (two words)
Quite a + adjective + noun.

B1 · Intermediate

Quite: fairly or completely? B1

UseExampleMeaning
quite + normal adjectiveThe film was quite good.fairly good
quite + extreme or absolute adjectiveYou're quite right. That's quite impossible.completely
quite a lot (of) / quite a fewThere were quite a few people.a fairly large number
not quiteI'm not quite ready.almost, but not completely
quite + verb (BrE)I quite like it.I like it, but not very much

  • The hotel was quite nice. (fairly)
  • Are you quite sure? (completely)
  • I've read quite a lot of his books. (a fairly large amount)
  • I'm not quite ready. Give me two minutes. (almost)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I quite am sure. → ✅ I'm quite sure. (quite before the adjective)
  • ❌ There were quite much people. → ✅ There were quite a lot of people. (quite a lot of)

Practice: B1

4. You're <span class="gap">___</span> right. I made a mistake.
Absolute adjective: quite right = completely right.
5. I haven't <span class="gap">___</span> finished. Give me five more minutes.
Not quite = almost.
6. There were quite <span class="gap">___</span> few people at the meeting. (one word)
Quite a few = a fairly large number.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Rather and pretty: rather expensive, pretty good B2

WordExampleMeaning
rather + negative wordIt's rather cold in here.more than I'd like
rather + positive wordThe food was rather good.surprisingly good
rather a / a rather + adjective + nounIt was rather a long day. = a rather long dayboth correct
rather + comparativeIt's rather better than I expected.
pretty (informal)I'm pretty sure. It's pretty good.quite, fairly

  • The instructions were rather confusing. (negative)
  • I rather liked the film. (surprisingly)
  • It was rather a strange evening. (rather a)
  • I'm pretty sure he'll come. (informal)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ It was a rather good. → ✅ It was rather good. (rather + adjective)
  • ❌ The test was rather more easy. → ✅ The test was rather easier. (rather + comparative)

Practice: B2

7. It's <span class="gap">___</span> cold in here. Can you close the window?
Rather + adjective.
8. I'm <span class="gap">___</span> sure he'll be late again. He always is.
Informal: pretty sure.
9. The exam was <span class="gap">___</span> easier than I expected. (one word)
Rather + comparative.

C1 · Advanced

Somewhat, relatively, reasonably: formal alternatives C1

WordExampleMeaning
somewhat (formal)The situation is somewhat complicated.a little
slightlyThe price is slightly higher.a little
reasonablyThe hotel was reasonably priced.fairly, acceptably
moderatelyThe plan was moderately successful.to an average degree
relativelyThe test was relatively easy.compared with others
rather / quite + verbI rather enjoyed it. I quite understand.fairly / completely

  • The new system is somewhat slower. (formal: a little)
  • Housing is relatively cheap in this region. (compared with others)
  • The restaurant is reasonably priced. (acceptably)
  • I quite understand your concern. (completely (polite))

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The results were somewhat of disappointing. → ✅ The results were somewhat disappointing. (somewhat + adjective)
  • ❌ It's relatively a cheap area. → ✅ It's a relatively cheap area. (a + relatively + adjective + noun)

Practice: C1

10. The results were <span class="gap">___</span> disappointing, but not a disaster.
Formal: somewhat + adjective.
11. Don't worry about the delay. I <span class="gap">___</span> understand.
Quite + verb = completely.
12. The hotel was <span class="gap">___</span> priced, so we stayed an extra night. (one word)
Reasonably priced = not too expensive.
