quite، rather، fairly و prettyQuite, Rather, Fairly and Pretty

fairly, quite, pretty و rather قبل از صفت و قید می‌آیند و یعنی «بیشتر از کمی، ولی نه خیلی» (نسبتاً): The test was fairly easy. It's pretty cold today.

معنی دقیقشان به کلمهٔ بعدشان، لحن صدای شما و انگلیسی بریتانیایی یا آمریکایی بستگی دارد.

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

a bit, fairly, quite, very: چقدر قوی؟ A2

از ضعیف به قوی: a bit / a little (کمی) < fairly (نسبتاً) < quite / pretty (نسبتاً زیاد) < very / really (خیلی) < extremely (فوق‌العاده).

کلمهمثالمعنی
a bit / a littleIt's a bit cold.کمی (اغلب منفی)
fairlyThe exam was fairly easy.بیشتر از کمی، نه خیلی
quite / prettyThe film was quite good. It's pretty cold.کمی بیشتر از fairly (pretty خودمانی است)
very / reallyI'm very tired.قوی
extremelyIt's extremely dangerous.خیلی قوی

a bit با صفت‌های منفی رایج است: It's a bit expensive. I'm a bit tired.

این کلمه‌ها قبل از صفت می‌آیند: It's quite big. با اسم، ترتیب معمول quite a + صفت + اسم است: It's quite a big house.

  • I'm a bit hungry. کمی
  • My English is fairly good. نه خیلی
  • The restaurant was pretty busy. خودمانی
  • She's really kind. قوی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It's very much cold. → ✅ It's very cold. very + صفت.
  • ❌ I'm a few tired. → ✅ I'm a bit tired. a bit + صفت.

تمرین سطح A2

1. It's ___ expensive. Let's look for a cheaper one.
کمی (منفی): a bit.
2. Their flat is ___ big. It has four bedrooms.
Quite + صفت.
3. It's ___ big house. It has five bedrooms. (two words)
Quite a + صفت + اسم.

B1 · متوسط

quite: نسبتاً یا کاملاً؟ B1

با بیشتر صفت‌ها quite یعنی نسبتاً (quite good). با صفت‌های مطلق (amazing, impossible, sure, right)، quite یعنی کاملاً (quite impossible, quite right). quite a lot / quite a few یعنی تعداد یا مقدارِ نسبتاً زیاد.

کاربردمثالمعنی
quite + صفت معمولیThe film was quite good.نسبتاً خوب
quite + صفت مطلقYou're quite right. That's quite impossible.کاملاً
quite a lot (of) / quite a fewThere were quite a few people.تعداد نسبتاً زیاد
not quiteI'm not quite ready.تقریباً، اما نه کامل
quite + فعل (بریتانیایی)I quite like it.دوستش دارم، اما نه خیلی

در انگلیسی آمریکایی quite اغلب یعنی very: The food was quite good (یعنی خیلی خوب). در انگلیسی بریتانیایی اغلب یعنی fairly.

not quite یعنی تقریباً، نه کامل: I haven't quite finished.

  • The hotel was quite nice. نسبتاً
  • Are you quite sure? کاملاً
  • I've read quite a lot of his books. مقدار نسبتاً زیاد
  • I'm not quite ready. Give me two minutes. تقریباً

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I quite am sure. → ✅ I'm quite sure. quite قبل از صفت.
  • ❌ There were quite much people. → ✅ There were quite a lot of people. quite a lot of.

تمرین سطح B1

4. You're ___ right. I made a mistake.
صفت مطلق: quite right یعنی کاملاً درست.
5. I haven't ___ finished. Give me five more minutes.
Not quite یعنی تقریباً.
6. There were quite ___ few people at the meeting. (one word)
Quite a few یعنی تعداد نسبتاً زیاد.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Rather and pretty: rather expensive, pretty good B2

Rather is like quite, but it often sounds negative or surprised: It's rather expensive. With positive words, it means surprisingly: The film was rather good. Pretty is informal: pretty good, pretty tired.

WordExampleMeaning
rather + negative wordIt's rather cold in here.more than I'd like
rather + positive wordThe food was rather good.surprisingly good
rather a / a rather + adjective + nounIt was rather a long day. = a rather long dayboth correct
rather + comparativeIt's rather better than I expected.
pretty (informal)I'm pretty sure. It's pretty good.quite, fairly

Rather is more common in British English and in writing. In everyday American English, pretty is more common.

Don't confuse rather (= fairly) with would rather (= prefer): I'd rather stay at home.

  • The instructions were rather confusing. (negative)
  • I rather liked the film. (surprisingly)
  • It was rather a strange evening. (rather a)
  • I'm pretty sure he'll come. (informal)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It was a rather good. → ✅ It was rather good. (rather + adjective)
  • ❌ The test was rather more easy. → ✅ The test was rather easier. (rather + comparative)

تمرین سطح B2

7. It's ___ cold in here. Can you close the window?
Rather + adjective.
8. I'm ___ sure he'll be late again. He always is.
Informal: pretty sure.
9. The exam was ___ easier than I expected. (one word)
Rather + comparative.

C1 · پیشرفته

Somewhat, relatively, reasonably: formal alternatives C1

In formal English, use somewhat, slightly, reasonably, moderately and relatively instead of a bit, quite or pretty: The results were somewhat disappointing. Prices are relatively low. Rather and quite can also go with verbs: I rather like it. I quite understand.

WordExampleMeaning
somewhat (formal)The situation is somewhat complicated.a little
slightlyThe price is slightly higher.a little
reasonablyThe hotel was reasonably priced.fairly, acceptably
moderatelyThe plan was moderately successful.to an average degree
relativelyThe test was relatively easy.compared with others
rather / quite + verbI rather enjoyed it. I quite understand.fairly / completely

I quite understand = I understand completely (polite). I quite like it (BrE) = I like it, but not very much.

Somewhat and relatively are common in academic writing: These results are relatively reliable.

  • The new system is somewhat slower. (formal: a little)
  • Housing is relatively cheap in this region. (compared with others)
  • The restaurant is reasonably priced. (acceptably)
  • I quite understand your concern. (completely (polite))

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The results were somewhat of disappointing. → ✅ The results were somewhat disappointing. (somewhat + adjective)
  • ❌ It's relatively a cheap area. → ✅ It's a relatively cheap area. (a + relatively + adjective + noun)

تمرین سطح C1

10. The results were ___ disappointing, but not a disaster.
Formal: somewhat + adjective.
11. Don't worry about the delay. I ___ understand.
Quite + verb = completely.
12. The hotel was ___ priced, so we stayed an extra night. (one word)
Reasonably priced = not too expensive.
