too و enoughToo and Enough

A2 · B1 · C1

Too means more than necessary or wanted: This coffee is too hot. Enough means as much as necessary: Is it warm enough for you? We have enough chairs.

Notice the word order: too goes before adjectives, and enough goes after adjectives but before nouns.

A2 · مقدماتی

Too hot, warm enough, enough money A2

PatternExample
too + adjectiveThis bag is too heavy.
adjective + enoughHe isn't old enough to drive.
enough + nounWe don't have enough money.
not + adjective + enoughThe room isn't big enough.

  • These shoes are too small. (too + adjective)
  • Is the water warm enough? (adjective + enough)
  • There isn't enough milk for everyone. (enough + noun)
  • You're driving too fast! (too + adverb)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She isn't enough old to vote. → ✅ She isn't old enough to vote. (adjective + enough)
  • ❌ We don't have time enough. → ✅ We don't have enough time. (enough + noun)
  • ❌ It's too much hot today. → ✅ It's too hot today. (too + adjective)

تمرین سطح A2

1. I can't drink this tea. It's ___.
A problem: too + adjective.
2. My son is 12. He isn't ___ to drive.
Adjective + enough.
3. We need six eggs. Have we got ___ eggs to make the cake? (one word: too or enough)
Enough + noun.

B1 · متوسط

Too much, too many, too difficult for me to do B1

PatternExample
too much + uncountableThere's too much sugar in this.
too many + pluralThere are too many people here.
verb + too muchYou work too much.
too + adjective + toThe box is too heavy to lift.
too + adjective + for + person + toThe text is too difficult for me to understand.
adjective + enough + toShe's old enough to travel alone.

  • I ate too much cake. (too much + uncountable)
  • There are too many cars in the city. (too many + plural)
  • The coffee is too hot to drink. (too + adjective + to)
  • Is he strong enough to carry it? (enough + to)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ There are too much people here. → ✅ There are too many people here. (plural: too many)
  • ❌ The soup was too hot to eat it. → ✅ The soup was too hot to eat. (no object after the verb)
  • ❌ It's too difficult for I to understand. → ✅ It's too difficult for me to understand. (for + object pronoun)

تمرین سطح B1

4. There's ___ traffic in the city centre. Let's take the metro.
Uncountable: too much.
5. This suitcase is too heavy ___.
Too + adjective + to (no object at the end).
6. The exercise was too difficult ___ me to do. (one word)
Too + adjective + for + person + to.

C1 · پیشرفته

Far too, more than enough, sufficiently, overly C1

PatternExample
far / much / way (informal) + tooIt's far too late. It's way too expensive.
a bit / a little + tooIt's a bit too short.
more than enoughWe have more than enough food.
barely / only just enoughWe had barely enough time.
sufficiently + adjective (formal)The results were not sufficiently clear.
overly / excessively + adjective (formal)The report was overly optimistic.
enough as a pronounI've had enough. Enough is enough!

  • The music is far too loud. (far too)
  • Don't cook any more. We have more than enough. (more than enough)
  • The plan was not sufficiently detailed. (formal)
  • She was overly critical of her students. (formal)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The hotel was very too expensive. → ✅ The hotel was far too expensive. (far / much + too)
  • ❌ We have enough more than food. → ✅ We have more than enough food. (more than enough)

تمرین سطح C1

7. It's ___ cold to go to the beach today.
Stronger too: far too.
8. Don't buy any more bread. We have ___.
Plenty: more than enough.
9. The evidence was not ___ strong to convict him. (one word, formal, meaning enough)
Formal: sufficiently + adjective.
