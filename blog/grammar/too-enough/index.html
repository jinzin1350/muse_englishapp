خانه / گرامر / too و enough

too و enoughToo and Enough

too یعنی بیشتر از حدِ لازم یا دلخواه (زیادی): This coffee is too hot. enough یعنی به اندازهٔ کافی: Is it warm enough for you? We have enough chairs.

به ترتیب کلمه‌ها دقت کنید: too قبل از صفت می‌آید، و enough بعد از صفت ولی قبل از اسم.

A2 · B1 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

too hot, warm enough, enough money A2

too + صفت یا قید (too expensive)، صفت یا قید + enough (big enough)، و enough + اسم (enough time).

الگومثال
too + صفتThis bag is too heavy.
صفت + enoughHe isn't old enough to drive.
enough + اسمWe don't have enough money.
not + صفت + enoughThe room isn't big enough.

too معنی منفی دارد (یعنی مشکلی هست): It's too cold to swim. با very مقایسه کنید: It's very cold, but we can still swim. («خیلی سرد» را همیشه too cold ترجمه نکنید.)

ترتیب کلمه‌ها: enough بعد از صفت می‌آید (good enough) ولی قبل از اسم (enough food).

  • These shoes are too small. too + صفت
  • Is the water warm enough? صفت + enough
  • There isn't enough milk for everyone. enough + اسم
  • You're driving too fast! too + قید

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She isn't enough old to vote. → ✅ She isn't old enough to vote. صفت + enough.
  • ❌ We don't have time enough. → ✅ We don't have enough time. enough + اسم.
  • ❌ It's too much hot today. → ✅ It's too hot today. too + صفت.

تمرین سطح A2

1. I can't drink this tea. It's ___.
مشکل: too + صفت.
2. My son is 12. He isn't ___ to drive.
صفت + enough.
3. We need six eggs. Have we got ___ eggs to make the cake? (one word: too or enough)
Enough + اسم.

B1 · متوسط

too much, too many، too difficult for me to do B1

too much را با اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش و too many را با اسم جمع بیاورید. الگوی too / enough + (for someone) + to + فعل: It's too cold (for us) to swim. She's old enough to vote.

الگومثال
too much + غیرقابل‌شمارشThere's too much sugar in this.
too many + جمعThere are too many people here.
فعل + too muchYou work too much.
too + صفت + toThe box is too heavy to lift.
too + صفت + for + شخص + toThe text is too difficult for me to understand.
صفت + enough + toShe's old enough to travel alone.

در جمله‌های too… to مفعول را آخر جمله تکرار نکنید: The box is too heavy to lift. (نه to lift it)

قبل از صفت too much نیاورید: too hot (نه too much hot).

  • I ate too much cake. too much + غیرقابل‌شمارش
  • There are too many cars in the city. too many + جمع
  • The coffee is too hot to drink. too + صفت + to
  • Is he strong enough to carry it? enough + to

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ There are too much people here. → ✅ There are too many people here. جمع: too many.
  • ❌ The soup was too hot to eat it. → ✅ The soup was too hot to eat. بعد از فعل، مفعول نیاورید.
  • ❌ It's too difficult for I to understand. → ✅ It's too difficult for me to understand. for + ضمیر مفعولی.

تمرین سطح B1

4. There's ___ traffic in the city centre. Let's take the metro.
غیرقابل‌شمارش: too much.
5. This suitcase is too heavy ___.
Too + صفت + to (بدون مفعول در آخر).
6. The exercise was too difficult ___ me to do. (one word)
Too + صفت + for + شخص + to.

C1 · پیشرفته

Far too, more than enough, sufficiently, overly C1

Make too stronger with far / much / way (far too expensive). More than enough = plenty. Formal English uses sufficiently (= enough) and overly / excessively (= too).

PatternExample
far / much / way (informal) + tooIt's far too late. It's way too expensive.
a bit / a little + tooIt's a bit too short.
more than enoughWe have more than enough food.
barely / only just enoughWe had barely enough time.
sufficiently + adjective (formal)The results were not sufficiently clear.
overly / excessively + adjective (formal)The report was overly optimistic.
enough as a pronounI've had enough. Enough is enough!

Don't use very with too: far too big (not very too big).

Sufficient is the formal adjective for enough: There isn't sufficient evidence. = There isn't enough evidence.

  • The music is far too loud. (far too)
  • Don't cook any more. We have more than enough. (more than enough)
  • The plan was not sufficiently detailed. (formal)
  • She was overly critical of her students. (formal)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The hotel was very too expensive. → ✅ The hotel was far too expensive. (far / much + too)
  • ❌ We have enough more than food. → ✅ We have more than enough food. (more than enough)

تمرین سطح C1

7. It's ___ cold to go to the beach today.
Stronger too: far too.
8. Don't buy any more bread. We have ___.
Plenty: more than enough.
9. The evidence was not ___ strong to convict him. (one word, formal, meaning enough)
Formal: sufficiently + adjective.
