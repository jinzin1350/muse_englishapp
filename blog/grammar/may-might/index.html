may و might (احتمال)May and Might (Possibility)

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

We use might and may to say that something is possible, but we're not sure: I might go to the gym later. It may rain tomorrow.

For being sure or almost sure (must, can't), and for possibility in the past (might have done), see Modals of Deduction. For May I…?, see Requests, Offers and Permission.

A2 · مقدماتی

Might and might not: perhaps A2

  • I might buy a new laptop next month. (perhaps)
  • We might not have enough time. (perhaps not)
  • Ask Sara. She might know the answer. (perhaps she knows)
  • The shop might be closed now. It's late. (perhaps, now)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I might to go to the beach. → ✅ I might go to the beach. (might + base verb)
  • ❌ He mights be late. → ✅ He might be late. (no -s)
  • ❌ It might rains. → ✅ It might rain. (might + base verb)

تمرین سطح A2

1. I'm not sure what to do tonight. I ___ watch a film.
Perhaps: might + base verb.
2. Don't wait for me. I ___ be late.
Might + base verb.
3. We ___ (might / not / go) on holiday this year. We don't have much money.
Perhaps not: might not go.

B1 · متوسط

May or might? B1

  • The meeting may finish late. (formal, perhaps)
  • Side effects may include headaches. (written instructions)
  • If you asked her nicely, she might help you. (unreal: might)
  • I may not be able to come. (perhaps not)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ If I won the lottery, I may stop working. → ✅ If I won the lottery, I might stop working. (unreal: might)
  • ❌ May it rain tomorrow? → ✅ Do you think it will rain tomorrow? (questions about possibility: Do you think…?)

Might be doing: now or in the future B1

  • Don't phone him now. He might be driving. (perhaps now)
  • The kids are quiet. They may be watching TV. (perhaps now)
  • I might be moving to Calgary next year. (possible plan)
  • We might be getting a new manager soon. (possible future change)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She might sleeping. → ✅ She might be sleeping. (might + be + -ing)
  • ❌ He might is working. → ✅ He might be working. (might + be)

تمرین سطح B1

4. This medicine ___ cause drowsiness. Do not drive after taking it.
Formal written warning: may.
5. If we left now, we ___ catch the earlier train.
Unreal (second conditional): might.
6. I ___ (may / not / be) home until 9, so don't wait for me.
Perhaps not: may not be.
7. Where's Sara? ~ I'm not sure. She ___ in the garden.
Perhaps now: might be working.
8. I ___ to Dubai for work next month. It isn't decided yet.
Possible arrangement: might be going.
9. Don't call her now. She ___ (might / have) dinner.
Perhaps now: might be having.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Might as well, may as well B2

  • The film has already started. We might as well go home. (no better option)
  • We've got an hour before the train. We may as well have a coffee. (nothing better to do)
  • Nobody listens to me. I might as well talk to the wall. (it would be the same)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We might as well to walk. → ✅ We might as well walk. (might as well + base verb)
  • ❌ We might as good stay here. → ✅ We might as well stay here. (as well, not as good)

تمرین سطح B2

10. The last bus has gone. We ___ walk home.
No better option: might as well.
11. What does I may as well finish the work tonight mean?
May as well = no better option.
12. Nobody else wants the last piece of pizza, so I might as ___ eat it. (one word)
Might as well.

C1 · پیشرفته

He may be rich, but…: may and might for concession C1

  • The hotel may be old, but it's very comfortable. (I accept it's old)
  • You might not like him, but he's a good manager. (concession)
  • He may have studied law, but he's never worked as a lawyer. (past)
  • I may be wrong, but I think she's lying. (polite disagreement)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The test may be difficult, so I passed it. → ✅ The test may have been difficult, but I passed it. (concession + but; past: may have been)

You might want to…, may well, might have told me C1

  • You might want to check the address before you go. (polite suggestion)
  • You might have warned me about the traffic! (annoyance)
  • The company may well close its London office. (quite likely)
  • She could well be right. (quite likely)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You might want check your answers. → ✅ You might want to check your answers. (want to + verb)
  • ❌ He may well to win. → ✅ He may well win. (may well + base verb)

تمرین سطح C1

13. The flat ___ be small, but it's in a great location.
Concession: may … but.
14. What does He may be clever, but he's lazy mean?
May … but = a concession.
15. She ___ (might / not / be) very friendly, but she's an excellent doctor.
Concession: might not be … but.
16. Before you submit the form, you ___ to read the instructions again.
Polite suggestion: might want to.
17. What does You might have told me! express?
Might have + past participle = annoyance.
18. Interest rates ___ (may / well) go up again this year.
Quite likely: may well.
