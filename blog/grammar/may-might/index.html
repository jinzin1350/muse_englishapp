خانه / گرامر / may و might

may و might (احتمال)May and Might (Possibility)

might و may را برای گفتن اینکه چیزی ممکن است اما مطمئن نیستیم («شاید») به کار می‌بریم: I might go to the gym later. It may rain tomorrow.

برای مطمئن بودن یا تقریباً مطمئن بودن (must, can't) و برای احتمال در گذشته (might have done) صفحهٔ فعل‌های وجهی برای حدس و استنتاج را ببینید. برای May I…? صفحهٔ درخواست، پیشنهاد و اجازه گرفتن را ببینید.

A2 · مقدماتی

might و might not: شاید A2

might / might not + شکل سادهٔ فعل یعنی «شاید» (الان یا در آینده): I might go to the party. It might not rain.

might + شکل سادهٔ فعل یعنی «شاید»، دربارهٔ حال یا آینده: Take an umbrella. It might rain. یعنی شاید باران بیاید.

منفی‌اش might not است (mightn't کم‌کاربرد و فقط بریتانیایی است): I might not come. یعنی شاید نیایم.

مثل بقیهٔ فعل‌های وجهی، might برای همهٔ اشخاص یکی است و to ندارد: She might call. نه She mights call و نه might to call.

مقایسه کنید: I'm going to visit Ali (برنامه) / I might visit Ali (شاید، مطمئن نیستم). برای سؤال معمولاً Do you think…? می‌گوییم: Do you think it'll rain? (نه Might it rain?)

  • I might buy a new laptop next month. شاید
  • We might not have enough time. شاید نه
  • Ask Sara. She might know the answer. شاید او بداند
  • The shop might be closed now. It's late. شاید، همین الان

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I might to go to the beach. → ✅ I might go to the beach. might + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ He mights be late. → ✅ He might be late. بدون ‑s
  • ❌ It might rains. → ✅ It might rain. might + شکل سادهٔ فعل

تمرین سطح A2

1. I'm not sure what to do tonight. I ___ watch a film.
شاید: might + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
2. Don't wait for me. I ___ be late.
might + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
3. We ___ (might / not / go) on holiday this year. We don't have much money.
شاید نه: might not go.

B1 · متوسط

may یا might؟ B1

may و might هر دو یعنی «شاید». may کمی رسمی‌تر است و ممکن است کمی مطمئن‌تر به نظر برسد. برای موقعیت‌های غیرواقعی might بیاورید.

در بیشتر جمله‌ها may و might هر دو با یک معنی ممکن‌اند: She may / might be at home. We may / might move to Vancouver.

may کمی رسمی‌تر است و در نوشته رایج است: Some medicines may cause sleepiness. خیلی از انگلیسی‌زبان‌ها آن را کمی محتمل‌تر از might حس می‌کنند.

در جمله‌های غیرواقعی (شرطی نوع دوم) might بیاورید، نه may: If I had more money, I might buy a house. نه I may buy.

may not یعنی «شاید نه»: He may not know. (در قانون‌های رسمی، may not می‌تواند یعنی «اجازه ندارید»: Visitors may not take photos.)

  • The meeting may finish late. رسمی، شاید
  • Side effects may include headaches. دستورالعمل نوشته‌شده
  • If you asked her nicely, she might help you. غیرواقعی: might
  • I may not be able to come. شاید نه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ If I won the lottery, I may stop working. → ✅ If I won the lottery, I might stop working. غیرواقعی: might
  • ❌ May it rain tomorrow? → ✅ Do you think it will rain tomorrow? سؤال دربارهٔ احتمال: Do you think…?

might be doing: الان یا در آینده B1

might / may be + فعل ing‌دار یعنی شاید کاری همین الان در جریان باشد، یا برنامه‌ریزی شده باشد: Don't call now. She might be sleeping. I might be working late tonight.

might / may + be + فعل ing‌دار نشان می‌دهد شاید کاری همین الان در جریان باشد: Where's Tom? ~ He might be having a shower.

می‌تواند دربارهٔ یک قرارِ احتمالی در آینده هم باشد: I may be going to Paris next week. (هنوز قطعی نیست.)

مقایسه کنید: She might work at home (شاید وضعیت کاری‌اش این است) / She might be working at home today (شاید همین الان دارد کار می‌کند).

برای احتمال در گذشته (might have done) صفحهٔ فعل‌های وجهی برای حدس و استنتاج را ببینید.

  • Don't phone him now. He might be driving. شاید همین الان
  • The kids are quiet. They may be watching TV. شاید همین الان
  • I might be moving to Calgary next year. برنامهٔ احتمالی
  • We might be getting a new manager soon. تغییر احتمالی در آینده

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She might sleeping. → ✅ She might be sleeping. might + be + فعل ing‌دار
  • ❌ He might is working. → ✅ He might be working. might + be

تمرین سطح B1

4. This medicine ___ cause drowsiness. Do not drive after taking it.
هشدارِ رسمیِ نوشته‌شده: may.
5. If we left now, we ___ catch the earlier train.
غیرواقعی (شرطی نوع دوم): might.
6. I ___ (may / not / be) home until 9, so don't wait for me.
شاید نه: may not be.
7. Where's Sara? ~ I'm not sure. She ___ in the garden.
شاید همین الان: might be working.
8. I ___ to Dubai for work next month. It isn't decided yet.
قرار احتمالی: might be going.
9. Don't call her now. She ___ (might / have) dinner.
شاید همین الان: might be having.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Might as well, may as well B2

might / may as well + base verb = there's no better option, so let's do it: The bus isn't coming. We might as well walk.

We say might as well or may as well when we do something because there's no good reason not to or nothing better to do: There's nothing on TV. I might as well go to bed.

It often sounds a little unenthusiastic. We might as well take a taxi = it's not ideal, but it's the best option.

Might as well can also compare two bad options: This phone is so slow, I might as well use a pigeon! (= it would be just as good)

  • The film has already started. We might as well go home. (no better option)
  • We've got an hour before the train. We may as well have a coffee. (nothing better to do)
  • Nobody listens to me. I might as well talk to the wall. (it would be the same)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We might as well to walk. → ✅ We might as well walk. (might as well + base verb)
  • ❌ We might as good stay here. → ✅ We might as well stay here. (as well, not as good)

تمرین سطح B2

10. The last bus has gone. We ___ walk home.
No better option: might as well.
11. What does I may as well finish the work tonight mean?
May as well = no better option.
12. Nobody else wants the last piece of pizza, so I might as ___ eat it. (one word)
Might as well.

C1 · پیشرفته

He may be rich, but…: may and might for concession C1

may / might … but = I accept this is true, but something else is more important: He may be rich, but he isn't happy.

We use may or might + but to admit that something is true before making a contrasting point. The may clause is not uncertain here; it's a concession: It may be expensive, but it's worth it. = I agree it's expensive, but…

The structure is common in arguments and discussions. Might is a little more tentative: You might think it's easy, but it isn't.

For the past: may / might have + past participle: She may have been tired, but that's no excuse.

  • The hotel may be old, but it's very comfortable. (I accept it's old)
  • You might not like him, but he's a good manager. (concession)
  • He may have studied law, but he's never worked as a lawyer. (past)
  • I may be wrong, but I think she's lying. (polite disagreement)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The test may be difficult, so I passed it. → ✅ The test may have been difficult, but I passed it. (concession + but; past: may have been)

You might want to…, may well, might have told me C1

might for polite suggestions (You might want to check that) and annoyance (You might have told me!). may / might well = probably.

You might (want to)… makes a suggestion very polite and indirect: You might want to save your work before you close the file. You might try calling them again.

You might have + past participle expresses annoyance about something someone didn't do: You might have told me you were bringing friends! (You didn't, and I'm annoyed.) Compare could have (same meaning).

May well / might well / could well + base verb = it's quite likely: Prices may well rise next year. It might well be the best film of the year.

  • You might want to check the address before you go. (polite suggestion)
  • You might have warned me about the traffic! (annoyance)
  • The company may well close its London office. (quite likely)
  • She could well be right. (quite likely)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You might want check your answers. → ✅ You might want to check your answers. (want to + verb)
  • ❌ He may well to win. → ✅ He may well win. (may well + base verb)

تمرین سطح C1
