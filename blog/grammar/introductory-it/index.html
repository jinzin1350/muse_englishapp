it مقدماتی (It is)Introductory It

جملهٔ انگلیسی فاعل لازم دارد، پس اغلب it را به‌عنوان فاعلِ «خالی» می‌آوریم: It's raining. It's five o'clock. (در فارسی می‌گوییم «باران می‌آید» یا «ساعت پنج است» بدون فاعلِ جدا.) it را برای معرفی یک ایدهٔ طولانی که بعداً می‌آید هم به کار می‌بریم: It's important to drink water. (یعنی آب خوردن مهم است.)

این صفحه از پایه تا الگوهای پیشرفته‌ای مثل I find it hard to… و It goes without saying that… پیش می‌رود. برای It is said that… صفحهٔ ساختارهای نقل قولِ مجهول را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1 · C2

A1 · مبتدی

It's raining, it's 5 o'clock, it's 10 km A1

برای هوا، ساعت، تاریخ، روزها و فاصله it را فاعل بیاورید: It's cold. It's half past six. It's Monday. It's 3 km to the station.

کاربردمثال
هواIt's raining. It's sunny today.
ساعتWhat time is it? ~ It's 7 o'clock.
روز و تاریخIt's Friday. It's 25 December.
فاصلهIt's 5 km from here to the beach.
موقعیت‌هاIt's very quiet here. It's my turn.

it را حذف نکنید: It's raining (نه Is raining).

  • It's very hot today. هوا
  • It's ten past nine. ساعت
  • It's my birthday tomorrow. تاریخ
  • How far is it to the airport? فاصله

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Is cold today. → ✅ It's cold today. هوا: it.
  • ❌ What time is? → ✅ What time is it? ساعت: it.

تمرین سطح A1

1. ___ raining again. Take an umbrella.
هوا: It's.
2. What time is ___? ~ It's half past eight.
ساعت: What time is it?
3. ___ very cold today. Wear a warm coat. (one word: = it is)
هوا: It's.

A2 · مقدماتی

it is یا there is؟ A2

there is / are می‌گوید چیزی وجود دارد یا جایی هست (اولین بار که اسمش می‌آید): There's a problem. (یک مشکلی هست.) it is چیزی را که از قبل معلوم است توصیف یا معرفی می‌کند: It's a big problem.

there is / areit is
There's a café near my house.It's very good. (همان کافه)
There's a man at the door.It's the postman.
There are lots of people here.It's very busy.

با there چیز تازه‌ای را معرفی کنید؛ بعد برای حرف زدن دربارهٔ آن it (یا they) بیاورید: There's a new film at the cinema. It's really funny.

  • There's a bank on the corner. It opens at 9. اول there، بعد it
  • There are two bedrooms. They're quite small. اول there are، بعد they
  • Who's at the door? ~ It's Ali. معرفی کردن
  • There's no milk in the fridge. وجود ندارد

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It's a supermarket near here. (first mention) → ✅ There's a supermarket near here. وجود دارد: there is (اولین بار که اسمش می‌آید).
  • ❌ There's very cold today. → ✅ It's very cold today. هوا: it.

تمرین سطح A2

4. ___ a new restaurant in our street. It's Italian.
وجود دارد (اولین بار): There's.
5. There's a letter for you. ___ from your bank.
همان نامه: It's.
6. Is ___ a pharmacy near here? (one word)
وجود دارد: Is there…?

B1 · متوسط

it's easy to learn, it's nice to meet you B1

It's + صفت + to + فعل: It's easy to use. It's nice to meet you. با for + شخص: It's hard for me to understand. همچنین It takes + زمان + to: It takes an hour to get there. (یک ساعت طول می‌کشد.)

الگومثال
It's + صفت + to + فعلIt's important to drink water.
It's + صفت + for + شخص + toIt's difficult for children to sit still.
It's + صفت + of + شخص + to (برای رفتار)It was kind of you to help.
It takes + زمان + toIt took me three hours to finish.
It costs + پول + toIt costs $10 to get in.

این الگو از شروع جمله با to طبیعی‌تر است: It's easy to cook pasta (معمولاً نه To cook pasta is easy).

  • It's dangerous to swim here. صفت + to
  • It's hard for me to wake up early. for + شخص
  • It was nice of you to call. of + شخص
  • It takes 20 minutes to walk to school. it takes

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Is easy to learn English. → ✅ It's easy to learn English. it + be.
  • ❌ It's difficult to me to understand. → ✅ It's difficult for me to understand. for + شخص.

تمرین سطح B1

7. ___ important to get enough sleep.
It's + صفت + to.
8. It's hard ___ me to say goodbye.
For + شخص.
9. It ___ me an hour to get to work every day. (one word)
It takes + زمان + to.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

It's a shame that, it seems that, it doesn't matter B2

It + be + adjective / noun + that-clause: It's a shame that you can't come. It's obvious that he's lying. With verbs: It seems / appears / turns out that… It doesn't matter whether / what…

PatternExample
It's + adjective + thatIt's strange that nobody called.
It's + noun + thatIt's a pity that you missed the party.
It seems / appears thatIt seems that we're lost.
It turns out thatIt turned out that she was right.
It doesn't matter + wh- / whetherIt doesn't matter what you wear.
It's (not) clear whether / whyIt's not clear why he left.

That can often be left out in speech: It's a shame you can't come.

  • It's a shame that the concert was cancelled. (noun + that)
  • It seems that the train is late again. (seems that)
  • It turned out that we were in the wrong room. (turned out that)
  • It doesn't matter if you're late. (doesn't matter)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Seems that we're lost. → ✅ It seems that we're lost. (it + seems)
  • ❌ It's a pity what you can't come. → ✅ It's a pity that you can't come. (that-clause)

تمرین سطح B2

10. ___ that the shop is closed today.
It seems that…
11. It's a pity ___ you can't stay longer.
It's a pity that…
12. It doesn't ___ what you think. I've already decided. (one word)
It doesn't matter what…

C1 · پیشرفته

I find it hard to, I'd appreciate it if, make it clear C1

It can also be a preparatory object: I find it hard to believe. I'd appreciate it if you could help. She made it clear that she was unhappy. I take it you're not coming.

PatternExample
find it + adjective + to / thatI find it difficult to concentrate.
make it + adjective + that / toHe made it clear that he disagreed.
I'd appreciate it ifI'd appreciate it if you didn't smoke here.
I hate / love it whenI hate it when people are late.
I take it (that)I take it you've heard the news.
owe it to someone toYou owe it to yourself to try.

Don't leave out it: I find it hard to sleep (not I find hard to sleep). I'd appreciate it if… (not I'd appreciate if…).

  • I find it easy to talk to her. (find it + adjective)
  • The teacher made it clear that phones weren't allowed. (make it clear)
  • I'd appreciate it if you could reply soon. (appreciate it if)
  • I love it when it snows. (love it when)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I find difficult to wake up early. → ✅ I find it difficult to wake up early. (find it + adjective)
  • ❌ I'd appreciate if you could call me. → ✅ I'd appreciate it if you could call me. (appreciate it if)

تمرین سطح C1

13. I find ___ hard to understand his accent.
Find it + adjective + to.
14. I'd appreciate ___ if you could send me the report today.
I'd appreciate it if…
15. She made ___ clear that she didn't want to come. (one word)
Make it clear that…

C2 · تسلط کامل

It's no use, it's worth, it goes without saying C2

Fixed patterns: It's no use / no good + -ing, It's worth + -ing, It goes without saying that…, It remains to be seen whether…, It stands to reason that…, It's not for me to…

PatternExampleMeaning
It's no use / no good + -ingIt's no use crying about it.it doesn't help
It's (not) worth + -ingIt's worth visiting the old town.it's a good idea
It goes without saying thatIt goes without saying that safety comes first.it's obvious
It remains to be seen whetherIt remains to be seen whether the plan will work.we don't know yet
It stands to reason thatIt stands to reason that prices will rise.it's logical
It's not for me toIt's not for me to decide.it's not my job

After no use / no good / worth, use -ing: It's no use complaining (not to complain).

  • It's no use arguing with him. He never listens. (no use + -ing)
  • It's really worth seeing the museum. (worth + -ing)
  • It goes without saying that you'll be paid. (obvious)
  • It remains to be seen whether he'll accept the offer. (not known yet)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It's no use cry about it. → ✅ It's no use crying about it. (no use + -ing)
  • ❌ It's worth to visit the castle. → ✅ It's worth visiting the castle. (worth + -ing)

تمرین سطح C2

16. It's no use ___ to him. He won't change his mind.
It's no use + -ing.
17. It ___ without saying that we'll help you.
It goes without saying that…
18. The museum is really interesting. It's worth ___ (visit).
It's worth + -ing.
