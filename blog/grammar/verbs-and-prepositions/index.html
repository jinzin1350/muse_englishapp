فعل و حرف اضافهVerbs and Prepositions

بعد از خیلی از فعل‌ها یک حرف اضافهٔ ثابت می‌آید: I'm waiting for the bus. It depends on the weather. Can you listen to me? فعل و حرف اضافه‌اش را با هم یاد بگیرید.

بعضی فعل‌ها اصلاً حرف اضافه نمی‌گیرند (discuss the problem، نه discuss about) و بعضی با حرف اضافهٔ دیگر معنی‌شان عوض می‌شود (look at, look for, look after). برای صفت‌ها صفحهٔ صفت‌ها و حرف اضافه را ببینید.

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

listen to, wait for, look at, depend on A2

جفت‌های رایج: listen to، wait for، look at، ask for، pay for، talk to / about، think about، belong to، depend on، agree with.

فعل + حرف اضافهمثال
listen toI listen to music every morning.
wait forWe're waiting for a taxi.
look atLook at this photo!
ask forHe asked for a glass of water.
pay forWho's going to pay for dinner?
talk to کسی about چیزیI talked to my boss about it.
think aboutWhat are you thinking about?
belong toDoes this bag belong to you?
depend onIt depends on the price.
agree withI agree with you.

در سؤال، حرف اضافه همراه فعل می‌ماند و اغلب آخر جمله می‌آید: Who are you waiting for? What are you looking at?

  • She's listening to a podcast. listen to (گوش دادن به)
  • I'm waiting for my friend. wait for (منتظرِ … بودن)
  • Look at the sky! look at (نگاه کردن به)
  • It depends on the weather. depend on (بستگی داشتن به)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm listening music. → ✅ I'm listening to music. listen to.
  • ❌ Wait me here. → ✅ Wait for me here. wait for. (فارسی می‌گوید «منتظرم باش»، ولی انگلیسی for لازم دارد.)
  • ❌ It depends of the price. → ✅ It depends on the price. depend on.

تمرین سطح A2

1. Can you wait ___ me? I'll be two minutes.
Wait for.
2. Who does this phone belong ___?
Belong to.
3. I always listen ___ the radio in the car. (one word)
Listen to.

B1 · متوسط

discuss, enter, marry, call: بدون حرف اضافه B1

بعضی فعل‌ها مفعول مستقیم می‌گیرند، بدون حرف اضافه: discuss a problem، enter a room، marry someone، call / phone someone، answer a question، reach a place، approach someone، lack something. (این‌ها همان‌هایی‌اند که فارسی‌زبان‌ها به خاطر «دربارهٔ»، «وارد … شدن»، «با … ازدواج کردن» و «به … زنگ زدن» برایشان حرف اضافه می‌گذارند.)

درستغلط
We discussed the plan.discussed about the plan
She entered the room.entered into the room
He married a doctor.married with a doctor
I'll call you tomorrow.call to you
Please answer the question.answer to the question
We reached the hotel at six.reached to the hotel
A man approached me.approached to me
The team lacks experience.lacks of experience

اما اسم‌های این فعل‌ها اغلب حرف اضافه دارند: a discussion about the plan, the answer to the question, a lack of experience.

explain چیزی را to کسی (نه explain me): Can you explain the rule to me?

  • Let's discuss your ideas tomorrow. discuss
  • She married her best friend. marry
  • Please call me when you arrive. call
  • We reached the top of the mountain. reach

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We need to discuss about the budget. → ✅ We need to discuss the budget. discuss حرف اضافه نمی‌گیرد.
  • ❌ Can you explain me this word? → ✅ Can you explain this word to me? explain چیزی to کسی.
  • ❌ I'll phone to you later. → ✅ I'll phone you later. phone حرف اضافه نمی‌گیرد.

thank someone for, remind someone of, spend money on B1

فعل + مفعول + حرف اضافه: thank / blame کسی for، accuse کسی of، remind کسی of / about، spend پول on، provide کسی with، protect / prevent کسی from، congratulate کسی on، borrow چیزی from.

الگومثال
thank کسی forShe thanked me for the present.
blame کسی forThey blamed him for the accident.
remind کسی of (شبیه بودن) / about (یادآوری کردن)You remind me of my sister. Remind me about the meeting.
spend پول / وقت onI spend too much on clothes.
provide کسی withThe hotel provided us with towels.
protect / prevent کسی fromSunscreen protects you from the sun.
congratulate کسی onWe congratulated her on her new job.
borrow چیزی fromCan I borrow a pen from you?
translate / divide چیزی intoTranslate this into English.

بعد از حرف اضافه اسم یا ‑ing بیاورید: Thank you for helping. They prevented us from leaving.

  • Thank you for your help. thank for
  • He spends hours on his phone. spend on
  • This song reminds me of my childhood. remind of (آدم را یادِ … انداختن)
  • We congratulated them on their wedding. congratulate on

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Thank you for help me. → ✅ Thank you for helping me. for + ‑ing.
  • ❌ Sunscreen protects you of the sun. → ✅ Sunscreen protects you from the sun. protect from.
  • ❌ I borrowed some money of my brother. → ✅ I borrowed some money from my brother. borrow from.

تمرین سطح B1

4. We discussed ___ for two hours.
Discuss + مفعول (بدون حرف اضافه).
5. کدام درست است؟
Explain چیزی را to کسی.
6. I phoned ___ last night, but you didn't answer. (one pronoun)
Phone + مفعول، بدون حرف اضافه.
7. Thank you ___ inviting me.
Thank کسی for.
8. How much do you spend ___ food every week?
Spend پول on.
9. The rain prevented us ___ going out. (one word)
Prevent کسی from + ‑ing.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Look at, look for, look after; think of or about B2

The preposition can change the meaning: look at / for / after, think of / about, hear about / of / from, care about / for, agree with / to / on, dream of / about.

PatternMeaningExample
look at / for / aftersee / try to find / take care ofLook at me. I'm looking for my keys. She looks after her mum.
think about / ofconsider / have an idea or opinionI'm thinking about moving. What do you think of my dress?
hear about / of / fromget news / know it exists / get a messageDid you hear about the fire? I've never heard of him. I heard from Sara yesterday.
care about / forthink it's important / look after or likeI care about the environment. She cares for her sick father.
agree with / to / onsame opinion / accept a request / decide togetherI agree with you. He agreed to the plan. We agreed on a price.
dream of / abouthope for / dream while asleepI dream of living by the sea. I dreamt about you last night.

Think of is also used for ideas: Can you think of a name for the dog?

  • I'm looking for a new flat. (try to find)
  • Who looks after your kids when you're at work? (take care of)
  • Have you heard from your brother? (get a message)
  • Finally, we agreed on a date for the wedding. (decide together)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She dreams to become a doctor. → ✅ She dreams of becoming a doctor. (dream of + -ing)
  • ❌ I agree to you. → ✅ I agree with you. (same opinion: agree with)
  • ❌ Can you look for my cat while I'm on holiday? → ✅ Can you look after my cat while I'm on holiday? (take care of: look after)

تمرین سطح B2

10. Can you look ___ the children for an hour? I need to go out.
Take care of: look after.
11. I've never heard ___ that singer. Is she famous?
Know it exists: hear of.
12. Can you think ___ a good name for our new cat? (one word)
An idea: think of.

C1 · پیشرفته

Comply with, refrain from, account for, attribute to C1

Formal verb + preposition pairs: comply with, adhere to, abide by, refrain from, account for, attribute something to, cope / contend with, object to + -ing, capitalise on, embark on, dispose of.

Verb + prepositionExample
comply with / adhere to / abide byAll staff must comply with the rules.
refrain from + -ingPlease refrain from smoking.
account forTourism accounts for 10% of the economy. How do you account for the delay?
attribute something toShe attributes her success to hard work.
cope / contend withWe had to contend with strong winds.
object to + -ingI object to being treated like a child.
capitalise onThe company capitalised on the trend.
embark onShe embarked on a new career.
dispose ofPlease dispose of batteries safely.

In object to, be committed to and look forward to, to is a preposition, so use -ing: I object to paying for this.

  • Visitors must adhere to the safety guidelines. (adhere to)
  • Young people account for half of our customers. (account for)
  • He attributes his long life to a good diet. (attribute to)
  • Guests are asked to refrain from using phones. (refrain from)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I object to pay for something I didn't order. → ✅ I object to paying for something I didn't order. (object to + -ing)
  • ❌ All products must comply to the new standards. → ✅ All products must comply with the new standards. (comply with)

تمرین سطح C1

13. All visitors must comply ___ the safety rules.
Comply with.
14. Please refrain ___ talking during the performance.
Refrain from + -ing.
15. Exports account ___ about 30% of the country's income. (one word)
Account for = make up.
