The Passive: All Tenses and Passive Infinitive

Level: B2 · Free grammar guide

The passive is be + past participle. To make any tense passive, put be in that tense: The house is being painted. The report has been finished. The results will be announced tomorrow.

For the basics, see The Passive: Present and Past Simple.

Passive forms in all tenses

TensePassive formExample
present simpleam / is / are + past participleCars are made here.
present continuousam / is / are being + past participleThe road is being repaired.
past simplewas / were + past participleThe bridge was built in 1900.
past continuouswas / were being + past participleI felt like I was being watched.
present perfecthave / has been + past participleMy wallet has been stolen.
past perfecthad been + past participleThe tickets had been sold before we arrived.
willwill be + past participleYou will be met at the airport.
be going toam / is / are going to be + past participleThe old cinema is going to be closed.
modal verbsmodal + be + past participleThis form must be signed. The work can be done online.
modal perfectmodal + have been + past participleThe letter should have been sent yesterday.

Passives of the perfect continuous tenses (has been being built) are possible but very rare. Use another structure instead.

Passive infinitive and passive -ing

  • to be + past participle: I want to be told the truth. The house needs to be cleaned.
  • being + past participle: She hates being interrupted. I don't like being photographed.

Need + -ing can also have a passive meaning: The car needs washing. (= needs to be washed)

Verbs with two objects

With give, send, offer, show, tell and teach, either object can become the subject. Starting with the person is more common: They gave me a prize. → I was given a prize. / A prize was given to me.

When to use the passive, and get + past participle

  • To focus on the action or the thing affected, not the person who did it: The suspect has been arrested.
  • In formal, scientific and news writing: The samples were analysed in a laboratory.

In informal English, get + past participle is common for things that happen to us, especially bad things: He got fired. Our car got stolen.

Examples

  1. The kitchen is being redecorated this week. (present continuous passive)
  2. Over 200 people have been invited to the wedding. (present perfect passive)
  3. The match will be shown live on TV. (will passive)
  4. When we arrived, the meal was being served. (past continuous passive)
  5. The documents had been signed before the meeting started. (past perfect passive)
  6. Your order is going to be delivered on Friday. (going to passive)
  7. Phones must be switched off during the exam. (modal passive)
  8. I hate being kept waiting. (passive -ing)
  9. She expects to be promoted next year. (passive infinitive)
  10. We were given a map at the entrance. (two objects: the person as subject)

Common mistakes

❌ The road is repairing.

✅ The road is being repaired. (Present continuous passive: is being + past participle.)

❌ My bike has stolen.

✅ My bike has been stolen. (Present perfect passive: has been + past participle.)

❌ The work will finished tomorrow.

✅ The work will be finished tomorrow. (Will be + past participle.)

❌ This form must signed.

✅ This form must be signed. (Modal + be + past participle.)

❌ I don't like being interrupt.

✅ I don't like being interrupted. (Being + past participle.)

❌ The letter should have sent yesterday.

✅ The letter should have been sent yesterday. (Modal perfect passive: should have been + past participle.)

❌ I was gave a present.

✅ I was given a present. (Use the past participle: given.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. The road ___ at the moment, so take another route.
Happening now, passive: is being + past participle.
2. The results ___ next Monday.
Future passive: will be + past participle.
3. My laptop ___. I left it on the train.
Present perfect passive: has been + past participle (stolen).
4. When we got to the hotel, our room ___.
In progress at a past moment, passive: was being + past participle.
5. This form must ___ in black ink.
Modal passive: must be + past participle.
6. All the tickets ___ before we arrived.
Before another past action, passive: had been + past participle. Tickets is plural.
7. She was ___ a prize for her research.
Passive with the person as subject: was given.
8. I hate ___ what to do.
Hate + passive -ing: being told.
9. The letter should ___ yesterday.
Past, modal, passive: should have been + past participle.
10. The meeting ___ until next week.
Present perfect passive: has been postponed.
11. I felt that I ___ by someone.
In progress in the past, passive: was being followed.
12. The old factory ___ into flats.
Going to passive: is going to be + past participle.
13. Choose the correct sentence.
Passive infinitive: to be + past participle.
14. He ___ fired last week. (informal)
Informal passive: got + past participle.
15. A new hospital ___ in our town next year.
Future passive: will be built.
16. Which sentence is passive?
Has been + past participle is the present perfect passive.
