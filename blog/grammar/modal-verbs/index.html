فعل‌های وجهی در انگلیسیModal Verbs in English

Modal verbs (can, could, may, might, must, shall, should, will, would) add meaning to the main verb: ability, permission, obligation, advice, possibility and more. You should see a doctor. It might rain. I can swim.

They all follow the same basic rules, but each one has several uses. This page gives the big picture. Each topic below has its own page with rules, examples and exercises at every level.

The basic rules

  • Modals are followed by the base verb, with no to: I can swim. Not I can to swim. (Exceptions: ought to, have to, be able to.)
  • They don't add -s: She can swim. Not She cans swim.
  • Questions and negatives don't use do: Can you swim? I can't swim. Not Do you can swim?
  • For the past, many modals use have + past participle: You should have told me. She must have left.

موضوع‌های این بخش

Can, Could and Be Able To (Ability and Possibility)

can، could و be able to (توانایی و امکان)

A1 A2 B1 C1

Ability and possibility: I can swim. I could read at four. I'll be able to help you tomorrow.

Must and Have To (Obligation)

must و have to (اجبار)

A2 B1 C1

Saying what is necessary: I have to work tomorrow. You must wear a seat belt. You don't have to come.

Should, Ought To and Had Better

should، ought to و had better (توصیه)

A2 B1 B2 C1

Giving advice and saying what is right or expected: You should see a doctor. You'd better hurry. It should arrive tomorrow.

May and Might (Possibility)

may و might (احتمال)

A2 B1 B2 C1

Saying something is possible but not certain: I might go out tonight. It may rain. She might be working.

Modals of Deduction and Probability

فعل‌های وجهی برای حدس و استنتاج

B1 B2 C1

How sure are you? It must be true. It might be true. It can't be true. She must have left.

Should Have, Could Have, Would Have, Needn't Have

should have، could have و needn't have

B1 B2 C1

Talking about the past that didn't happen: I should have studied. You could have called. You needn't have bought it.

Requests, Offers and Permission (Can you…? May I…? Shall I…?)

درخواست، پیشنهاد و اجازه گرفتن

A1 A2 B1 C1

Asking for things politely: Can you help me? Could I borrow your pen? Shall I open the window? Would you mind waiting?

Would Like, Would Rather and Prefer

would like، would rather و prefer

A1 A2 B1 B2 C1

Saying what you want and prefer, politely: I'd like a coffee. I'd rather stay in. I prefer tea to coffee.
