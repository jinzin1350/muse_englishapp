Present Perfect for Experience (ever, never)

Level: A2 · Free grammar guide

We use the present perfect to talk about experiences in our life up to now, when we don't say exactly when they happened: I've been to Japan. She's never eaten sushi. Have you ever ridden a horse?

Form: have / has + past participle.

How to form the present perfect

FormExample
Positivehave / has + past participleI've visited Paris. He's met a famous actor.
Negativehaven't / hasn't + past participle, or have/has + neverI haven't seen that film. She's never flown.
QuestionHave / Has + subject + (ever) + past participle?Have you ever tried Thai food?
Short answersYes, + subject + have/has. / No, + subject + haven't/hasn't.Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't.

Regular past participles end in -ed (visited, tried). Irregular verbs have their own forms (been, seen, eaten, flown). See the irregular verbs list.

Ever and never

  • ever = at any time in your life. We use it in questions: Have you ever been to Mexico?
  • never = not at any time. Use it with a positive verb: I've never been to Mexico. (not I haven't never been)

We also use ever after superlatives: It's the best meal I've ever had.

Been or gone?

been to = went and came back (an experience). gone to = went and is still there: I've been to London three times. Sam isn't here; he's gone to London.

Present perfect or past simple?

Use the present perfect for the experience, with no time: I've been to Rome. When you say when, use the past simple: I went there in 2019. Conversations often start with the present perfect and continue in the past simple: Have you ever been to Rome? – Yes, I have. I went last summer. More practice: Present Perfect vs Past Simple.

To say how often: I've seen this film three times. How many times have you been there?

Examples

  1. I've been to Canada twice. (experience + how many times)
  2. Have you ever eaten octopus? (ever in a question)
  3. She's never driven a car. (never + past participle)
  4. We haven't met your new boyfriend. (negative)
  5. He's read all the Harry Potter books. (read (sounds like red))
  6. Has your brother ever lived abroad? (has + he/she/it)
  7. I've seen this film three times. (how many times)
  8. They've never been on a plane. (never)
  9. It's the most beautiful place I've ever seen. (superlative + ever)
  10. Have you ever been to Italy? – Yes, I have. I went in 2022. (present perfect, then past simple)

Common mistakes

❌ Have you ever went to London?

✅ Have you ever been to London? (Use the past participle: been.)

❌ I have never saw snow.

✅ I have never seen snow. (Use the past participle: seen.)

❌ I haven't never been there.

✅ I've never been there. (Never already makes the sentence negative.)

❌ Have you ever try sushi?

✅ Have you ever tried sushi? (Have + past participle.)

❌ I've been to Paris last year.

✅ I went to Paris last year. (With a finished time, use the past simple.)

❌ He have been to Brazil.

✅ He has been to Brazil. (He, she, it → has.)

❌ Ever you have been to Japan?

✅ Have you ever been to Japan? (Question: Have + subject + ever + past participle.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. Have you ever ___ to Australia?
Have + past participle: been.
2. I've never ___ sushi.
Past participle of eat: eaten.
3. ___ she ever met a famous person?
She takes has: Has she ever met…?
4. We ___ been to that restaurant. Is it good?
We + have never + past participle.
5. My brother ___ in a band.
My brother (he) + has never + past participle (played).
6. Have you ever seen a ghost? – No, I ___.
Short answer to a have question: No, I haven't.
7. I've seen this film ___ times.
To say how often: three times.
8. It's the best pizza I've ___ eaten.
Superlative + present perfect + ever.
9. Have you ever been to Rome? – Yes, I went there ___.
With the past simple, say when: in 2019.
10. Choose the correct sentence.
Last year is a finished time: past simple, went.
11. Sam isn't here. He's ___ to the shops.
He's at the shops now: gone. (Been = he went and came back.)
12. How many times ___ you been to London?
How many times + have you + past participle?
13. She's never ___ a horse.
Past participle of ride: ridden.
14. Have they ever ___ abroad?
Have + past participle: lived.
15. ___ ever been to a concert?
Present perfect question: Have you ever + past participle?
16. Which sentence talks about an experience?
Life experience, no time given: present perfect, I've climbed.
