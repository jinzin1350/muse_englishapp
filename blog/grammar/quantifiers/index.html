کمیت‌نماها: all، most، each، every و noneQuantifiers: All, Most, Each, Every, None

کمیت‌نماها می‌گویند چند تا یا چه مقدار: all, most, some, none, each, every, both, either, neither. خیلی از آن‌ها با of یا بدون آن به کار می‌روند: most people / most of the people.

برای much, many, a few و a little صفحهٔ much، many، a few، a little را ببینید. برای both… and و either… or صفحهٔ both… and، either… or را ببینید.

A2 · مقدماتی

all, most, some, no: با of یا بدون آن A2

برای چیزها به طور کلی all / most / some / no + اسم بیاورید (Most people like music، بیشتر مردم). برای یک گروهِ مشخص all of / most of / some of / none of + the / my / these + اسم، یا یک ضمیر بیاورید (Most of my friends live here، بیشترِ دوستانم).

کلی (بدون of)مشخص (of + the / my / these…)
All children love games.All (of) the children in my class are friendly.
Most people work hard.Most of the people I know work hard.
Some shops close on Sundays.Some of the shops in my street are closed.
No students passed.None of the students passed.

با ضمیر همیشه of بیاورید: most of them, some of us, all of it, none of you.

all قبل از the / my / these می‌تواند of را حذف کند: all the students = all of the students. most و some نمی‌توانند: most of the students (نه most the students).

  • Most people in Canada speak English. کلی
  • Most of my friends are married. مشخص
  • All of us were tired. ضمیر
  • None of the shops were open. none of

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Most of people like chocolate. → ✅ Most people like chocolate. کلی: بدون of. (فارسی می‌گوید «بیشترِ مردم»، اما برای مردم به طور کلی of نمی‌آید.)
  • ❌ Most the students passed. → ✅ Most of the students passed. مشخص: most of the.
  • ❌ All of them is here. → ✅ All of them are here. فعل جمع.

تمرین سطح A2

1. ___ people like holidays.
به طور کلی: most + اسم.
2. ___ my friends live in Toronto.
مشخص (my friends): most of.
3. I invited ten people, but none ___ them came. (one word)
با ضمیر: none of them.

B1 · متوسط

each یا every؟ B1

each و every + اسم مفرد + فعل مفرد. every یعنی همهٔ یک گروه، با هم (Every student passed). each یعنی یکی‌یکی، جداجدا (Each child got a present). فقط each می‌تواند با of یا به‌تنهایی بیاید.

eachevery
یکی‌یکی: Each player has a number.همه با هم: Every player wants to win.
برای دو تا هم: She had a ring on each hand.برای دو تا نه (نه every hand)
each of the students, each of themevery one of the students (not every of)
به‌تنهایی: They cost $5 each.زمانِ تکراری: every day, every three hours

هر دو فعل مفرد می‌گیرند: Every room has a TV. Each of the rooms has a TV.

برای چیزهایی که مرتب تکرار می‌شوند every بیاورید: every morning, every two weeks (نه each two weeks).

  • Every child needs love. همهٔ بچه‌ها
  • The teacher spoke to each student individually. یکی‌یکی
  • Each of my parents has a car. each of
  • I go to the gym every other day. زمانِ تکراری

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Every of the students has a laptop. → ✅ Each of the students has a laptop. با of: each.
  • ❌ Every students are here. → ✅ Every student is here. اسم مفرد + فعل مفرد.
  • ❌ I visit my parents each two weeks. → ✅ I visit my parents every two weeks. زمانِ تکراری: every.

both, either, neither (of) B1

برای دو چیز: both یعنی هر دو (+ جمع)؛ either یعنی یکی از دو تا، هر کدام (+ مفرد)؛ neither یعنی هیچ‌کدام از دو تا (+ مفرد). با of: both of / either of / neither of + the / my / them.

کلمه+ اسم+ of
bothboth children (جمع)both of the children, both of them
eithereither day (مفرد)either of the days, either of them
neitherneither answer (مفرد)neither of the answers, neither of them

neither خودش منفی است، پس فعل مثبت بیاورید: Neither of them came. (نه didn't come). در جملهٔ منفی either بیاورید: I don't like either of them.

بعد از neither of / either of، فعل مفرد رسمی‌تر است و فعل جمع در صحبت رایج است: Neither of my parents speaks / speak English.

برای بیش از دو تا all, any و none بیاورید: None of my three brothers…

  • Both my brothers live in Toronto. both
  • You can park on either side of the street. either
  • Neither restaurant was open. neither
  • I don't like either of these shirts. منفی: either

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Both of them is teachers. → ✅ Both of them are teachers. both: فعل جمع.
  • ❌ Neither of them didn't come. → ✅ Neither of them came. neither: فعل مثبت.
  • ❌ I don't want neither of them. → ✅ I don't want either of them. فعل منفی: either.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

All or whole? All, everything, no and none B2

The whole + singular noun = all of one thing (the whole day, the whole cake). All goes with uncountable and plural nouns (all the money, all the people). Everything is more natural than all alone. None stands alone; no needs a noun.

PatternExample
the / my whole + singularI spent the whole day at home. She ate the whole pizza.
all (the) + uncountable / pluralall the money, all day, all my friends
everything aloneEverything is fine. (All is fine sounds old-fashioned)
all + relative clauseAll I want is some rest. (= the only thing)
no + noun / none aloneThere's no milk. ~ How much is there? None.
all after the subjectWe all agreed. They're all here.

All day / all night / all week (no the) = the whole day: I worked all day.

All goes before the main verb but after be and auxiliaries: We all love it. We're all tired. They've all gone.

  • I read the whole book in one night. (whole + singular)
  • All my friends came. (all + plural)
  • Everything went well. (everything)
  • All we need is a little more time. (all = the only thing)
  • How many tickets are left? ~ None. (none alone)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She spent whole day shopping. → ✅ She spent the whole day shopping. (the whole)
  • ❌ There are none tickets. → ✅ There are no tickets. (+ noun: no)

تمرین سطح B2

10. I was so hungry that I ate ___ pizza.
The whole + singular noun.
11. How much money is left? ~ ___. We spent it all.
Alone: none.
12. I didn't sleep at all last night. I was awake ___ night. (one word)
All night = the whole night.

C1 · پیشرفته

None of them is or are? All of whom, each and every C1

None of + plural takes a singular verb in formal style and a plural verb in everyday English. In relative clauses, use all / most / none / both of whom / which. Each and every is emphatic. Not all ≠ none.

PatternExample
none of + pluralNone of the candidates was / were suitable. (singular = formal)
quantifier + of whom / whichShe has three sons, all of whom are doctors. I bought two books, neither of which was cheap.
each and everyEach and every one of you has helped.
not all vs noneNot all students passed. (some failed) / None of the students passed.
every other / every fewevery other day (= every second day), every few weeks
all of + pronounall of us (not all us); us all after a verb: She thanked us all.

All… not can be ambiguous: All the students didn't pass may mean not all. Write Not all the students passed or None of the students passed to be clear.

After a comma, all of them / both of them + verb starts a new clause, so it needs and or a full stop. In one sentence, use of whom / of which.

  • None of the reports has been published yet. (formal: singular)
  • I have two brothers, both of whom live abroad. (both of whom)
  • Not all doctors agree with this. (some don't)
  • I see my parents every other weekend. (every second)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I have three sisters, all of them are married. → ✅ I have three sisters, all of whom are married. (in one sentence: of whom)
  • ❌ All us were tired. → ✅ All of us were tired. (all of + pronoun)

تمرین سطح C1

13. He has three daughters, ___ are lawyers.
In one sentence: all of whom.
14. Which sentence means that some students passed and some failed?
Not all = some did, some didn't.
15. I go to the gym every ___ day: Monday, Wednesday, Friday… (one word)
Every other day = every second day.
