Concession: Albeit, Much As, Notwithstanding

Level: C1 · Free grammar guide

Concession means accepting one fact while saying something that contrasts with it. At B1 you learned although, despite and in spite of.

At C1, you can use more precise and formal forms: The plan worked, albeit slowly. Much as I like him, I don't trust him. Notwithstanding the weather, the event went ahead.

The forms

FormFollowed byExample
albeitan adjective, adverb or phrase (not a full clause)It was a good, albeit short, holiday.
much asa clause (= although… very much)Much as I'd love to help, I don't have time.
notwithstandinga noun (before or after it); formalNotwithstanding the cost, the plan was approved. / These problems notwithstanding, …
however + adjective / adverb / mucha clause (= it doesn't matter how)However hard I try, I can't understand it.
adjective + as / though + clauseemphatic, formalStrange as it may seem, I've never seen the sea.
nevertheless / nonethelessa new sentence or clause (adverb)It was risky. Nevertheless, we tried.

Albeit can't start a clause with a subject and verb: albeit slowly / albeit with some problems (not albeit it was slow).

Review: although, despite, while

Although / though / even though + clause; despite / in spite of + noun or -ing; while / whilst + clause for contrast. See Although, Despite, In Spite Of.

Examples

  1. He accepted the offer, albeit reluctantly. (albeit + adverb)
  2. The results were positive, albeit with a few exceptions. (albeit + phrase)
  3. Much as I admire her work, I can't agree with her. (much as + clause)
  4. Notwithstanding the bad weather, the concert went ahead. (notwithstanding + noun)
  5. Budget limits notwithstanding, the team delivered on time. (noun + notwithstanding)
  6. However much you earn, you'll always want more. (however much)
  7. Tired though she was, she kept working. (adjective + though)
  8. Strange as it may seem, I've never been on a plane. (adjective + as)
  9. The test was hard. Nevertheless, everyone passed. (nevertheless)
  10. While she's very talented, she's also very modest. (while = although)

Common mistakes

❌ Albeit it was cold, we went out.

✅ Although it was cold, we went out. (Albeit doesn't take a subject + verb.)

❌ Much as I like him, but I don't trust him.

✅ Much as I like him, I don't trust him. (Don't add but after a concession clause.)

❌ However I try hard, I can't do it.

✅ However hard I try, I can't do it. (However + adjective / adverb comes first.)

❌ Notwithstanding it rained, we played.

✅ Notwithstanding the rain, we played. (Notwithstanding + noun.)

❌ Strange as may it seem, it's true.

✅ Strange as it may seem, it's true. (No inversion after as: subject + verb.)

❌ Despite it was risky, we tried.

✅ Despite the risk, we tried. / Although it was risky, we tried. (Despite + noun or -ing, not a clause.)
