Future in the Past (was going to, would…)

B2 · C1

Sometimes we look at the future from a point in the past: In 2010 I didn't know I would live in Canada one day. Each future form has a past version: will → would, am going to → was going to, am leaving → was leaving, am about to → was about to.

These forms are common in stories and in reported speech. For was going to on its own, see Be Going To.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

The future seen from the past: four forms B2

Future formFuture in the pastExample
willwouldI knew she would help us.
be going towas / were going toWe were going to call you.
present continuouswas / were + -ingHe was excited because he was flying to Japan the next day.
be about towas / were about toI was about to leave when you called.

  • I was sure you would pass. (prediction from the past)
  • We were going to buy a house, but prices went up. (plan)
  • She couldn't come because she was meeting a client. (arrangement)
  • The film was about to start when the power went off. (very soon)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I knew she will help us. → ✅ I knew she would help us. (future from the past: would)
  • ❌ Yesterday I'm going to call you, but I forgot. → ✅ Yesterday I was going to call you, but I forgot. (past plan: was going to)

Would as the future in the past (reported speech and thoughts) B2

  • He said he would be home by six. (reported will)
  • I thought the test would be easier. (past thought)
  • They were sure they wouldn't lose. (negative)
  • Did you know he would be there? (question)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He said he will come yesterday. → ✅ He said he would come yesterday. (reported: would)
  • ❌ I hoped you will win. → ✅ I hoped you would win. (past thought: would)

Practice: B2

1. I was nervous because I ___ my first presentation the next day.
An arrangement seen from the past: was giving.
2. I knew you ___ like this restaurant.
A prediction from the past: would.
3. We ___ (be / about / leave) when it started to snow.
Very soon, from the past: were about to.
4. She told me she ___ me later, but she never did.
Reported future (didn't happen): would.
5. We expected the train ___ late, but it was on time.
A past expectation: would be.
6. I was sure you ___ (not / forget) my birthday.
Past certainty about the future: wouldn't forget.

C1 · Advanced

Little did she know she would…: hints about the future in stories C1

  • He left home at 18. He would never return. (foreshadowing)
  • It was a small mistake, but it would cost the company millions. (later result)
  • That summer was to be the happiest of her life. (formal foreshadowing)
  • Little did they know that they would win the lottery a week later. (dramatic inversion)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Little they knew that the storm would destroy the town. → ✅ Little did they know that the storm would destroy the town. (inversion: little did they know)
  • ❌ She said goodbye. She will never see him again. (in a past story) → ✅ She said goodbye. She would never see him again. (past story: would)

Plans that didn't happen: was going to, was hoping to, had intended to C1

FormExample
was going toI was going to call you, but I forgot.
was / were supposed toWe were supposed to meet at 6.
had hoped / intended / planned toI had hoped to finish by Friday.
was hoping / thinking ofI was hoping to see you at the party.
was to have + past participleThe talks were to have taken place in May.

  • I had intended to write back, but I was too busy. (failed plan)
  • You were supposed to be here an hour ago! (expected, didn't happen)
  • We were hoping to buy a house this year. (hope (perhaps not now))
  • The match was to have been played yesterday. (formal failed plan)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I was going to called you. → ✅ I was going to call you. (going to + base verb)
  • ❌ I had intended finish it. → ✅ I had intended to finish it. (intended to + verb)

Practice: C1

7. They met at a party in 2005. Two years later, they ___ married.
Foreshadowing in a past story: would be.
8. ___ did he know that his life was about to change.
Little did he know…
9. It was a choice she ___ (would / regret) for years.
Foreshadowing: would regret.
10. I ___ to visit you, but my car broke down.
A plan that failed: had intended to.
11. You ___ supposed to lock the door last night! Now someone's stolen the bike.
Were supposed to = expected, but it didn't happen.
12. We ___ (hope) to go to Italy this summer, but now we can't afford it.
A hope that didn't work out: had hoped / were hoping.
