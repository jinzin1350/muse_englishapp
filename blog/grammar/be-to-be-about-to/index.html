خانه / گرامر / be about to، be due to و be to

be about to، be due to و be toBe About To, Be Due To and Be To

غیر از will و going to، انگلیسی عبارت‌های دیگری هم برای آینده دارد که معنی اضافه‌ای می‌دهند: be about to (خیلی زود، همین حالا)، be due to (طبق برنامه)، be to (برنامه‌ها و دستورهای رسمی).

خیلی از آن‌ها در خبرها و نوشته‌های رسمی رایج‌اند. برای شکل‌های اصلی آینده صفحهٔ will یا going to را ببینید.

B1 · B2 · C1

B1 · متوسط

be about to: خیلی زود B1

be about to + شکل سادهٔ فعل یعنی چیزی خیلی زود اتفاق می‌افتد (در شُرُف … است): Hurry up! The film is about to start. was about to یعنی همین الان می‌خواستم.

be about to + شکل سادهٔ فعل یعنی چیزی در زمان خیلی کوتاهی اتفاق می‌افتد: Turn off your phones. The plane is about to take off.

معمولاً عبارت زمانی مثل tomorrow اضافه نمی‌کنیم؛ معنی‌اش خودش «همین حالا» است.

was / were about to چیزی را توصیف می‌کند که در گذشته قرار بود فوراً اتفاق بیفتد، اغلب وقتی چیزی وسطش آمد: I was about to call you when you knocked on the door. (داشتم زنگ می‌زدم که…)

  • Quick, get inside! It's about to rain. خیلی زود
  • The shop is about to close. Please make your final purchases. خیلی زود
  • I was about to leave when the phone rang. برنامه‌ای که قطع شد
  • We were about to give up when we found the key. نزدیک بود اتفاق بیفتد

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The film is about starting. → ✅ The film is about to start. about to + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
  • ❌ The bus is about to leave tomorrow. → ✅ The bus leaves tomorrow. / The bus is about to leave. about to یعنی همین حالا (بدون عبارت زمانیِ آینده).

تمرین سطح B1

1. Sit down! The concert ___ about to begin.
Be about to + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
2. I was ___ to go to bed when my sister called.
Was about to یعنی همین الان می‌خواستم.
3. Hurry! The train is about ___ (leave).
About to + شکل سادهٔ فعل.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Be due to: scheduled events B2

be due to + verb = it's planned or expected at a particular time: The train is due to arrive at 6.15. be due + time: The baby is due in May.

Be due to + base verb describes something scheduled or expected: The new hospital is due to open next year. The meeting was due to start at nine.

Without a verb, be due + time: The rent is due on Friday. Our baby is due in March.

Was due to often suggests the plan didn't happen as expected: The flight was due to land at 6, but it was delayed.

Don't confuse it with due to + noun = because of: The match was cancelled due to the rain.

  • The President is due to arrive at noon. (scheduled)
  • Your essay is due next Monday. (deadline)
  • The film was due to start at 8, but it started late. (planned, didn't happen on time)
  • When is the baby due? (expected birth)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The train is due arriving at six. → ✅ The train is due to arrive at six. (due to + base verb)

تمرین سطح B2

4. The plane is ___ to land in ten minutes.
Scheduled: due to.
5. What does The report is due on Friday mean?
Due = expected / the deadline.
6. The new metro line ___ (be / due / open) in 2027.
Scheduled: is due to open.

C1 · پیشرفته

On the point of, on the verge of, be set to C1

be on the point / verge of + -ing = very close to doing something. be set to + verb (news) = expected to happen soon: Prices are set to rise.

PhraseMeaningExample
be on the point of + -ingvery close toI was on the point of leaving when she arrived.
be on the verge of + -ing / nounvery close to (often emotional or major)She was on the verge of tears.
be set to + verbexpected to (news style)Fuel prices are set to rise.
be poised to + verbready to (news style)The team is poised to win the title.

These phrases are more formal or dramatic than be about to. Be set to and be poised to are typical of news headlines and reports.

  • The company is on the point of closing down. (very close)
  • Scientists are on the verge of a major discovery. (very close (major))
  • Unemployment is set to fall this year. (news: expected)
  • The government is poised to announce new rules. (news: ready to)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I was on the point to leave. → ✅ I was on the point of leaving. (on the point of + -ing)
  • ❌ Prices are set rising. → ✅ Prices are set to rise. (set to + base verb)

Be to: official plans and orders C1

be to + base verb for official arrangements (The President is to visit Japan) and for orders (You are to stay here). If … is to = if … wants to / is going to.

Be to + base verb is a formal way to describe official plans, often in the news: The Prime Minister is to visit Brazil next month. Two hundred workers are to lose their jobs.

It can also give orders or instructions: You are to report to the manager at 9. Nobody is to leave the building. See Must and Have To.

If … is / are to = if … wants to / is going to succeed: If we are to finish on time, we need more staff.

  • The Queen is to open the new hospital on Monday. (official plan)
  • You are not to tell anyone about this. (order)
  • If the city is to grow, it needs better transport. (if it wants to)
  • The two leaders are to meet in Geneva. (news)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The President is to visiting Canada. → ✅ The President is to visit Canada. (be to + base verb)

Was to and was to have done: destiny and plans that failed C1

was to + verb = something that happened later, seen from the past (He was to become president). was to have + past participle = a plan that didn't happen.

In stories and history, was / were to + base verb describes what happened later (destiny): In 1990 she met the man who was to become her husband. (He became her husband later.)

Was / were to have + past participle describes a plan that didn't happen: The wedding was to have taken place in June, but it was cancelled.

Was to + base verb can also mean was planned to: We were to meet at noon, but she didn't come.

For more ways to describe the future from the past, see Future in the Past.

  • Little did he know that this was to be his last match. (destiny)
  • The hotel was to have opened in 2020, but the pandemic stopped it. (failed plan)
  • They were to marry the following spring. (planned)
  • It was to change her life forever. (destiny)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The meeting was to have take place on Monday. → ✅ The meeting was to have taken place on Monday. (was to have + past participle)
