بیان هدف (to، in order to، so that)Infinitive of Purpose (to, for, so that, in order to)

برای اینکه بگوییم کسی چرا کاری را انجام می‌دهد، معمولاً to + شکل سادهٔ فعل می‌آوریم (یعنی «برای اینکه»): I went to the bank to get some money. به این مصدرِ هدف می‌گویند.

for + اسم، so that + جمله‌واره و in order to / so as to (رسمی‌تر) هم به کار می‌روند. این صفحه نشان می‌دهد هر کدام کِی می‌آید.

A2 · مقدماتی

to + فعل: I went to the shop to buy bread A2

برای گفتن هدف to + شکل سادهٔ فعل بیاورید: I'm saving money to buy a car. She called to say sorry.

مصدر با to به سؤال چرا؟ جواب می‌دهد: Why did you go to the shop? ~ To buy some bread.

معمولاً بعد از کار اصلی می‌آید: I went to the library to study. We use a knife to cut bread.

برای گفتن هدف for + فعل نیاورید: I came here to learn English. نه for learn و نه for to learn. («برای یاد گرفتن» را کلمه‌به‌کلمه ترجمه نکنید.)

  • I'm learning English to get a better job. هدف
  • She went to the airport to meet her sister. هدف
  • We turned on the heating to keep warm. هدف
  • Why did you call? ~ To ask about the party. جواب کوتاه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I went to the shop for buy milk. → ✅ I went to the shop to buy milk. هدف: to + فعل.
  • ❌ He came to Canada for study. → ✅ He came to Canada to study. to + فعل.

تمرین سطح A2

1. I went to the post office ___ a parcel.
هدف: to + فعل.
2. Why are you going to the gym? ~ ___ fit.
هدف: To get fit.
3. She went to the market ___ (buy) some fruit.
هدف: to buy.

B1 · متوسط

to یا for؟ for + اسم، for + ‑ing B1

to + فعل برای یک کار: I went out to get some milk. for + اسم: I went out for some milk. for + ‑ing برای اینکه یک وسیله به چه دردی می‌خورد: a knife for cutting bread.

الگومثال
to + فعل (چرا کسی کاری می‌کند)I went to the shop to buy bread.
for + اسمI went to the shop for some bread. / Let's go out for dinner.
for + ‑ing (کاربرد یک وسیله)This tool is for opening bottles.

for + ‑ing کاربرد کلیِ یک چیز را توصیف می‌کند: A thermometer is used for measuring temperature. برای هدفِ یک آدم to بیاورید: I used the thermometer to measure the baby's temperature.

for + شخص + to + فعل: There's a bus for students to get to campus.

  • Let's go out for a walk. for + اسم
  • I went to the café for breakfast. for + اسم
  • These scissors are for cutting paper. کاربرد
  • I bought some scissors to cut the paper. هدفِ یک آدم

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I went to the shop for buying bread. → ✅ I went to the shop to buy bread. هدفِ یک آدم: to.
  • ❌ We went out for eat. → ✅ We went out to eat. / We went out for dinner. for + اسم، to + فعل.

so that + can / could / will / would B1

برای هدف so that + فاعل + can / could / will / would بیاورید (یعنی «تا اینکه»)، به‌خصوص وقتی فاعل عوض می‌شود: I'll write it down so that I don't forget. She spoke slowly so that we could understand.

so that یک جمله‌وارهٔ کامل را برای هدف شروع می‌کند. به‌خصوص وقتی فاعل دوم فرق دارد به درد می‌خورد: I gave him my number so that he could call me.

برای حال و آینده can / will و برای گذشته could / would بیاورید: I'm leaving early so that I can catch the train. I left early so that I could catch the train.

در صحبت that اغلب حذف می‌شود: Speak louder so everyone can hear.

برای هدفِ منفی، so that … won't / don't اغلب از to طبیعی‌تر است: I wrote it down so that I wouldn't forget. (در انگلیسیِ دقیق نه to not forget)

  • Turn the light on so that I can see. فاعل دیگر (I)
  • I set two alarms so that I wouldn't oversleep. هدف منفی، گذشته
  • She saved money so that her children could go to university. فاعل دیگر
  • Close the door so the cat doesn't get out. غیررسمی: so

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I gave her my number so that she call me. → ✅ I gave her my number so that she could call me. so that + فعل وجهی.
  • ❌ He spoke slowly so that we can understand. (past) → ✅ He spoke slowly so that we could understand. گذشته: could.

تمرین سطح B1

4. I went to the bakery ___ some bread.
For + اسم.
5. A corkscrew is a tool ___ wine bottles.
کاربرد یک وسیله: for + ‑ing.
6. Let's go out ___ a drink after work. (one word)
For + اسم.
7. I'm going to bed early ___ I can get up at five.
So that + جمله‌واره.
8. She wore a hat so that the sun ___ burn her face.
گذشته، هدف منفی: wouldn't.
9. I'll leave the key under the mat so that you ___ (can) get in.
هدف در آینده: so that you can.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

In order to, so as to (and the negative) B2

in order to / so as to + verb are more formal ways to give a purpose. For a negative purpose, use in order not to / so as not to (not not to alone).

In order to and so as to mean the same as to, but they're more formal and make the purpose clearer: We left early in order to avoid the traffic.

For a negative purpose, use in order not to or so as not to: We walked quietly so as not to wake the baby. Simple not to alone usually sounds wrong: not We walked quietly not to wake the baby.

In order to can go at the start of a sentence for emphasis: In order to apply, you must be over 18.

  • In order to get a visa, you need a letter from your employer. (formal)
  • I took notes so as to remember the details. (purpose)
  • He spoke quietly so as not to disturb anyone. (negative purpose)
  • She left early in order not to miss the train. (negative purpose)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I went out quietly not to wake anyone. → ✅ I went out quietly so as not to wake anyone. (negative purpose: so as not to / in order not to)
  • ❌ In order to pass, you need study hard. → ✅ In order to pass, you need to study hard. (need + to)

تمرین سطح B2

10. We turned off our phones ___ disturb the other guests.
Negative purpose: so as not to.
11. ___ to register, please complete the form below.
In order to at the start of a sentence.
12. She left early in order ___ (not / miss) the last train.
In order not to + verb.

C1 · پیشرفته

With a view to, with the aim of, for fear of, lest C1

Formal purpose expressions: with a view to / with the aim of + -ing, for fear of + -ing / for fear that, and very formal lest (= so that … not).

ExpressionMeaningExample
with a view to + -ingwith the intention ofThey bought land with a view to building a hotel.
with the aim of + -ingwith the purpose ofThe course was designed with the aim of improving skills.
for fear of + -ing / nounbecause you're afraid thatShe didn't speak for fear of making a mistake.
for fear that + clausein caseHe hid the letter for fear that someone would read it.
lest + (should) verbso that … not (very formal / literary)He wrote it down lest he (should) forget.

Note that to in with a view to is a preposition, so it's followed by -ing: with a view to buying (not to buy).

Lest is rare in modern speech; it appears in formal or literary writing. In everyday English, use so that … not or in case.

  • She's taking evening classes with a view to changing careers. (intention)
  • The law was passed with the aim of reducing pollution. (purpose)
  • I didn't mention it for fear of upsetting her. (afraid of the result)
  • They spoke in whispers lest they be overheard. (literary)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ They bought the house with a view to sell it. → ✅ They bought the house with a view to selling it. (with a view to + -ing)
  • ❌ He didn't move for fear to wake her. → ✅ He didn't move for fear of waking her. (for fear of + -ing)

تمرین سطح C1

With a view to + -ing.
14. She didn't ask any questions for fear ___ stupid.
For fear of + -ing.
15. The programme was set up with the ___ of helping young people find work. (one word)
