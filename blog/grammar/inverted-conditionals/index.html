Inverted Conditionals (Had I known, Should you need...)

Level: C1 · Free grammar guide

In formal English, we can leave out if and invert the subject and the auxiliary: If I had known → Had I known. If you need help → Should you need help.

This works with three words only: should, were and had.

Three types

With ifInvertedUse
If you need any help, call me.Should you need any help, call me.possible future (polite, formal)
If I were you, I'd accept.Were I you, I'd accept.unreal present
If the government raised taxes, …Were the government to raise taxes, …unlikely future
If I had known, I'd have come.Had I known, I'd have come.unreal past

Don't use Will, Would, Did or Was in this structure. Only Should, Were, Had.

Negatives

Put not after the subject, and don't use contractions: Had I not seen it… (not Hadn't I seen it). Should you not wish to continue…

Fixed phrases: Were it not for your help, … (= If it weren't for) and Had it not been for your help, … (= If it hadn't been for).

Examples

  1. Should you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask. (should)
  2. Should the problem continue, contact our support team. (should)
  3. Were I in your position, I would accept the offer. (were)
  4. Were I to win the lottery, I'd buy a house by the sea. (were + to)
  5. Had we left earlier, we wouldn't have missed the train. (had)
  6. Had she accepted the job, she would have moved to New York. (had)
  7. Had I not seen it, I wouldn't have believed it. (negative)
  8. Were it not for the rain, we could have a picnic. (were it not for)
  9. Had it not been for your help, we would have failed. (had it not been for)
  10. Had I known you were coming, I'd have cooked dinner. (mixed with would have)

Common mistakes

❌ Hadn't I known, I wouldn't have gone.

✅ Had I not known, I wouldn't have gone. (No contraction: Had I not…)

❌ Would you need help, call me.

✅ Should you need help, call me. (Use should, not would.)

❌ Was I you, I'd apologise.

✅ Were I you, I'd apologise. (Use were for all persons.)

❌ Had I know, I would have helped.

✅ Had I known, I would have helped. (Had + past participle.)

❌ If had I known, I would have come.

✅ Had I known, I would have come. (Leave out if when you invert.)

❌ Were it not of your help, we'd fail.

✅ Were it not for your help, we'd fail. (Fixed phrase: Were it not for.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. ___ I known about the traffic, I would have left earlier.
Unreal past: Had I known.
2. ___ you need any help, please call me.
Possible future, formal: Should you need.
3. ___ I in your position, I would accept the offer.
Unreal present: Were I (for all persons).
4. Had it not ___ for your help, we would have failed.
Fixed phrase: Had it not been for.
5. Were it not ___ the rain, we could have a picnic.
Fixed phrase: Were it not for.
6. Should you ___ to cancel, please let us know 24 hours in advance.
Should + base form: wish.
7. ___ we left earlier, we wouldn't have missed the train.
Unreal past: Had we left.
8. Choose the correct sentence.
Negative: Had I not seen (no contraction, not after the subject).
9. Were the government ___ taxes, people would protest.
Unlikely future: Were + subject + to raise.
10. ___ you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask.
Possible future, formal: Should you have.
11. If I had been more careful, I wouldn't have broken it. = ___ more careful, I wouldn't have broken it.
If I had been → Had I been.
12. If you should see Tom, tell him to call me. = ___ Tom, tell him to call me.
If you should see → Should you see.
13. If it weren't for my parents, I couldn't afford university. = ___ for my parents, I couldn't afford university.
If it weren't for → Were it not for.
14. ___ she accepted the job, she would have moved to New York.
Unreal past with would have: Had she accepted.
15. Were I ___ the lottery, I'd buy a house by the sea.
Were + subject + to + verb.
16. Should the problem ___, contact our support team.
Should + base form: continue.
