Relative Clauses in English

A relative clause gives more information about a noun, usually with who, which, that, whose, where or when: The woman who lives next door is a doctor.

There are two types: defining clauses tell us which person or thing, and non-defining clauses add extra information, with commas. Each type has its own page with rules, examples and exercises at every level.
