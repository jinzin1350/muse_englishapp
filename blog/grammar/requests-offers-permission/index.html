Requests, Offers and Permission (Can you…? May I…? Shall I…?)

A1 · A2 · B1 · C1

Modal verbs help us ask for things, offer help and ask for permission politely: Could you help me? May I come in? Shall I carry that for you?

In English, how polite you sound depends a lot on the words you choose. This page goes from simple requests to very polite and formal ones.

A1 · Beginner

Can you…? Could you…? Asking someone to do something A1

RequestHow polite?
Can you help me?friendly, informal
Could you help me, please?more polite
Will you help me?direct (careful: can sound impatient)
Would you help me, please?polite

  • Can you pass the salt? (friendly)
  • Could you open the window, please? (polite)
  • Could I have the bill, please? (asking for something)
  • Would you help me with this box? (polite)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Could you to help me? → ✅ Could you help me? (could + base verb)
  • ❌ Can you helping me? → ✅ Can you help me? (can + base verb)

Practice: A1

1. <span class="gap">___</span> you tell me the time, please?
A polite request: Could you…?
2. <span class="gap">___</span> I have a coffee, please?
Asking for something: Can I have…?
3. Could you <span class="gap">___</span> (repeat) that, please?
Could you + base verb.

A2 · Elementary

Can I…? Could I…? May I…? Asking for permission A2

AskingAnswering yesAnswering no
Can I use your phone?Yes, of course. / Sure. / Go ahead.Sorry, you can't.
Could I leave early today?Yes, that's fine.I'm afraid not.
May I sit here?Yes, of course. / Please do.I'm sorry, that seat is taken.

  • Can I borrow your charger? (informal)
  • Could I ask you a question? (polite)
  • May I see your passport, please? (formal)
  • You can use my computer if you like. (giving permission)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ May I to come in? → ✅ May I come in? (may + base verb)
  • ❌ Could I open the window? ~ Yes, you could. → ✅ Could I open the window? ~ Yes, of course. / Yes, you can. (Give permission with can, not could.)

Offers and suggestions: Shall I…? Shall we…? Would you like…? A2

FunctionExamples
offering to do somethingShall I open the window? Can I help you? I'll carry that.
offering somethingWould you like a drink?
making a suggestionShall we go out tonight? Let's take a taxi. Why don't we eat here?
invitingWould you like to come to dinner?

  • Shall I help you with that? (offer)
  • Shall we meet at six? (suggestion)
  • Would you like some tea? (offering something)
  • Let's order a pizza. (suggestion)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Shall I to help you? → ✅ Shall I help you? (shall + base verb)
  • ❌ Let's to go to the beach. → ✅ Let's go to the beach. (let's + base verb)
  • ❌ Do you like a coffee? (offering) → ✅ Would you like a coffee? (offering: would you like)

Practice: A2

4. <span class="gap">___</span> I use your bathroom? (formal)
Formal permission: May I…?
5. Could I sit here? ~ Yes, <span class="gap">___</span>.
Give permission: Yes, of course. (not you could)
6. <span class="gap">___</span> (can / I) borrow your bike this afternoon?
Informal permission: Can I…?
7. It's hot in here. <span class="gap">___</span> I open the window?
An offer: Shall I…?
8. <span class="gap">___</span> a cup of tea? ~ Yes, please.
Offering something: Would you like…?
9. Let's <span class="gap">___</span> (go) to the cinema tonight.
Let's + base verb.

B1 · Intermediate

Would you mind…? Do you mind if…? B1

PatternMeaningExample
Would you mind + -ing?please do thisWould you mind closing the door?
Do you mind if I + present?can I…?Do you mind if I sit here?
Would you mind if I + past?can I…? (more polite)Would you mind if I opened the window?

  • Would you mind waiting a moment? (please wait)
  • Do you mind if I open the window? ~ No, go ahead. (permission + yes)
  • Would you mind if I left a bit early? (more polite)
  • Would you mind not smoking here? (please don't)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Would you mind to close the door? → ✅ Would you mind closing the door? (mind + -ing)
  • ❌ Do you mind if I sit here? ~ Yes, sure! (meaning you can) → ✅ Do you mind if I sit here? ~ No, not at all. (No = I don't mind = you can.)

Be allowed to: permission and rules B1

  • Are we allowed to park here? (question)
  • Children aren't allowed to swim without an adult. (rule)
  • My parents allowed me to travel alone when I was 16. (allow someone to)
  • Will I be allowed to bring my dog? (future)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ We are not allow to eat here. → ✅ We are not allowed to eat here. (allowed, with -ed)
  • ❌ I'm allowed bring a guest. → ✅ I'm allowed to bring a guest. (allowed to + verb)

Practice: B1

10. Would you mind <span class="gap">___</span> the window? It's cold.
Would you mind + -ing.
11. Do you mind if I use your phone? Which answer means yes, you can?
No = I don't mind.
12. Would you mind if I <span class="gap">___</span> (bring) a friend to the party?
Would you mind if I + past simple.
13. You <span class="gap">___</span> allowed to use your phone in the exam.
Be not allowed to.
14. Yesterday the teacher let us go early. We <span class="gap">___</span> leave at 2 pm.
One particular occasion: were allowed to.
15. Are dogs <span class="gap">___</span> (allow) in this park?
Be allowed = have permission.

C1 · Advanced

Very polite requests: I was wondering if…, Would it be possible…? C1

PhraseExample
Do you think you could…?Do you think you could help me move on Saturday?
I was wondering if you could…I was wondering if you could check my CV.
Would it be possible (for me) to…?Would it be possible to change my appointment?
I'd be grateful / I'd appreciate it if you could…I'd appreciate it if you could reply by Friday.
Would you be so kind as to…? (very formal)Would you be so kind as to close the door?

  • I was wondering if you could give me a lift. (soft request)
  • Would it be possible to pay by card? (formal)
  • I'd be grateful if you could send me the details. (email)
  • Do you think you could lower the music a little? (polite)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I was wondering if could you help me. → ✅ I was wondering if you could help me. (statement word order after if)
  • ❌ I'd appreciate if you could reply. → ✅ I'd appreciate it if you could reply. (appreciate it if)

Practice: C1

A polite indirect request: I was wondering if you could…
17. Which is the most polite request in a formal email?
I would appreciate it if… is formal and polite.
18. Would it be <span class="gap">___</span> to change my seat? (one word: the adjective from possibility)
Would it be possible to…?
