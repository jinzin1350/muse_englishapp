درخواست، پیشنهاد و اجازه گرفتنRequests, Offers and Permission (Can you…? May I…? Shall I…?)

فعل‌های وجهی به ما کمک می‌کنند مؤدبانه چیزی بخواهیم، کمک پیشنهاد کنیم و اجازه بگیریم: Could you help me? May I come in? Shall I carry that for you?

در انگلیسی، اینکه چقدر مؤدب به نظر برسید خیلی به کلمه‌هایی بستگی دارد که انتخاب می‌کنید. این صفحه از درخواست‌های ساده شروع می‌شود و به درخواست‌های خیلی مؤدبانه و رسمی می‌رسد.

A1 · A2 · B1 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

Can you…? Could you…?: خواستن از کسی که کاری بکند A1

Can you…? و Could you…? + شکل سادهٔ فعل برای خواستن از کسی که کاری انجام بدهد. could مؤدبانه‌تر است: Could you close the door, please?

درخواستچقدر مؤدبانه؟
Can you help me?دوستانه، خودمانی
Could you help me, please?مؤدبانه‌تر
Will you help me?مستقیم (مراقب باشید: ممکن است بی‌حوصله به نظر برسد)
Would you help me, please?مؤدبانه

برای اینکه از کسی بخواهید کاری بکند، Can / Could / Would you + شکل سادهٔ فعل بیاورید: Could you speak more slowly, please?

برای خواستن چیزی، Can I have…? یا Could I have…? بیاورید: Can I have a glass of water, please?

برای مؤدبانه‌تر شدن please اضافه کنید. جواب‌های رایج: Sure. / Of course. / No problem. / Sorry, I can't.

  • Can you pass the salt? دوستانه
  • Could you open the window, please? مؤدبانه
  • Could I have the bill, please? خواستن چیزی
  • Would you help me with this box? مؤدبانه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Could you to help me? → ✅ Could you help me? could + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ Can you helping me? → ✅ Can you help me? can + شکل سادهٔ فعل

تمرین سطح A1

1. ___ you tell me the time, please?
درخواست مؤدبانه: Could you…?
2. ___ I have a coffee, please?
خواستن چیزی: Can I have…?
3. Could you ___ (repeat) that, please?
Could you + شکل سادهٔ فعل.

A2 · مقدماتی

Can I…? Could I…? May I…?: اجازه گرفتن A2

Can I…? (خودمانی)، Could I…? (مؤدبانه) و May I…? (رسمی) برای اجازه گرفتن: May I come in?

پرسیدنجواب مثبتجواب منفی
Can I use your phone?Yes, of course. / Sure. / Go ahead.Sorry, you can't.
Could I leave early today?Yes, that's fine.I'm afraid not.
May I sit here?Yes, of course. / Please do.I'm sorry, that seat is taken.

May I…? از همه رسمی‌تر است و در مغازه، اداره و با آدم‌هایی که خوب نمی‌شناسید رایج است.

برای اجازه دادن can یا may بیاورید (نه could): Yes, you can. / You may go now. (رسمی)

به Could I…? جواب ندهید Yes, you could. بگویید Yes, you can. یا Of course.

  • Can I borrow your charger? خودمانی
  • Could I ask you a question? مؤدبانه
  • May I see your passport, please? رسمی
  • You can use my computer if you like. اجازه دادن

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ May I to come in? → ✅ May I come in? may + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ Could I open the window? ~ Yes, you could. → ✅ Could I open the window? ~ Yes, of course. / Yes, you can. با can اجازه بدهید، نه could.

پیشنهاد کمک و پیشنهاد کار: Shall I…? Shall we…? Would you like…? A2

پیشنهاد کمک: Shall I…? / Can I…? / I'll… پیشنهاد کار: Shall we…? / Let's… / Why don't we…? دعوت: Would you like…?

کاربردمثال
پیشنهاد انجام کاریShall I open the window? Can I help you? I'll carry that.
تعارف کردن چیزیWould you like a drink?
پیشنهاد کاری با همShall we go out tonight? Let's take a taxi. Why don't we eat here?
دعوت کردنWould you like to come to dinner?

Shall I / shall we + شکل سادهٔ فعل برای پیشنهاد کمک و پیشنهاد کار به کار می‌رود، بیشتر در انگلیسی بریتانیایی: Shall I call a taxi? در انگلیسی آمریکایی بیشتر Should I…? یا Do you want me to…? می‌گویند.

I'll + شکل سادهٔ فعل، همان لحظهٔ صحبت پیشنهاد کمک می‌دهد: You look busy. I'll make dinner.

Let's + شکل سادهٔ فعل (بدون to): Let's go (نه Let's to go). منفی: Let's not argue.

  • Shall I help you with that? پیشنهاد کمک
  • Shall we meet at six? پیشنهاد کار
  • Would you like some tea? تعارف کردن
  • Let's order a pizza. پیشنهاد کار

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Shall I to help you? → ✅ Shall I help you? shall + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ Let's to go to the beach. → ✅ Let's go to the beach. let's + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ Do you like a coffee? (offering) → ✅ Would you like a coffee? برای تعارف: would you like

تمرین سطح A2

4. ___ I use your bathroom? (formal)
اجازهٔ رسمی: May I…?
5. Could I sit here? ~ Yes, ___.
اجازه دادن: Yes, of course. (نه you could)
6. ___ (can / I) borrow your bike this afternoon?
اجازهٔ خودمانی: Can I…?
7. It's hot in here. ___ I open the window?
پیشنهاد کمک: Shall I…?
8. ___ a cup of tea? ~ Yes, please.
تعارف کردن: Would you like…?
9. Let's ___ (go) to the cinema tonight.
Let's + شکل سادهٔ فعل.

B1 · متوسط

Would you mind…? Do you mind if…? B1

Would you mind + فعل ing‌دار یعنی درخواستی مؤدبانه. Do you mind if I + حال / Would you mind if I + گذشته یعنی اجازه گرفتن. برای «بله» جواب بدهید No, not at all!

الگومعنیمثال
Would you mind + فعل ing‌دار؟لطفاً این کار را بکنWould you mind closing the door?
Do you mind if I + حال؟اجازه هست…؟Do you mind if I sit here?
Would you mind if I + گذشته؟اجازه هست…؟ (مؤدبانه‌تر)Would you mind if I opened the window?

mind یعنی «ناراحت شدن، مخالف بودن». پس جوابِ مؤدبانه برای بله این است: No, not at all / No, go ahead / Of course not: Do you mind if I sit here? ~ No, not at all. (یعنی بله، بفرمایید.)

اگر واقعاً ناراحت می‌شوید، چیزی شبیه این بگویید: Actually, I'd rather you didn't. / Sorry, that seat is taken.

  • Would you mind waiting a moment? لطفاً صبر کن
  • Do you mind if I open the window? ~ No, go ahead. اجازه + جواب «بله»
  • Would you mind if I left a bit early? مؤدبانه‌تر
  • Would you mind not smoking here? لطفاً این کار را نکن

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Would you mind to close the door? → ✅ Would you mind closing the door? mind + فعل ing‌دار
  • ❌ Do you mind if I sit here? ~ Yes, sure! (meaning you can) → ✅ Do you mind if I sit here? ~ No, not at all. No یعنی ناراحت نمی‌شوم، یعنی می‌توانی.

be allowed to: اجازه و قانون B1

be allowed to + فعل یعنی اجازه داشتن: We are allowed to use phones at break. گذشته: was allowed to / could (کلی). قانون: not allowed to.

be allowed to اجازه‌ای را توصیف می‌کند که کسِ دیگری یا یک قانون داده: Students are allowed to use dictionaries in the exam. You're not allowed to smoke here.

در همهٔ زمان‌ها به کار می‌رود: Will we be allowed to take photos? I wasn't allowed to go out late when I was a teenager.

برای اجازهٔ کلی در گذشته could هم ممکن است: When I was a child, I could stay up late at weekends. برای یک بارِ مشخص was allowed to بیاورید: I was allowed to leave early yesterday. (برای یک کارِ مجاز در یک بار، نه I could leave early yesterday)

  • Are we allowed to park here? سؤالی
  • Children aren't allowed to swim without an adult. قانون
  • My parents allowed me to travel alone when I was 16. اجازه دادن به کسی
  • Will I be allowed to bring my dog? آینده

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We are not allow to eat here. → ✅ We are not allowed to eat here. allowed با ‑ed
  • ❌ I'm allowed bring a guest. → ✅ I'm allowed to bring a guest. allowed to + فعل

تمرین سطح B1

10. Would you mind ___ the window? It's cold.
Would you mind + فعل ing‌دار.
11. در جواب Do you mind if I use your phone? کدام جواب یعنی «بله، می‌توانی»؟
No یعنی ناراحت نمی‌شوم.
12. Would you mind if I ___ (bring) a friend to the party?
Would you mind if I + گذشتهٔ ساده.
13. You ___ allowed to use your phone in the exam.
be not allowed to.
14. Yesterday the teacher let us go early. We ___ leave at 2 pm.
یک بارِ مشخص: were allowed to.

C1 · پیشرفته

Very polite requests: I was wondering if…, Would it be possible…? C1

Indirect forms make requests softer and more formal: I was wondering if you could…, Would it be possible to…?, Do you think you could…?, I'd appreciate it if you could…

PhraseExample
Do you think you could…?Do you think you could help me move on Saturday?
I was wondering if you could…I was wondering if you could check my CV.
Would it be possible (for me) to…?Would it be possible to change my appointment?
I'd be grateful / I'd appreciate it if you could…I'd appreciate it if you could reply by Friday.
Would you be so kind as to…? (very formal)Would you be so kind as to close the door?

The longer and more indirect the request, the more polite it sounds. These forms are useful in emails, at work and when asking for a big favour.

Notice the word order: these are not direct questions inside: I was wondering if you could help (not if could you help).

Very formal phrases (Would you be so kind as to…) can sound sarcastic in casual situations. Choose the level of politeness that fits the situation.

  • I was wondering if you could give me a lift. (soft request)
  • Would it be possible to pay by card? (formal)
  • I'd be grateful if you could send me the details. (email)
  • Do you think you could lower the music a little? (polite)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I was wondering if could you help me. → ✅ I was wondering if you could help me. (statement word order after if)
  • ❌ I'd appreciate if you could reply. → ✅ I'd appreciate it if you could reply. (appreciate it if)

تمرین سطح C1

16. ___ if you could look at my report before Monday.
A polite indirect request: I was wondering if you could…

I would appreciate it if… is formal and polite.
18. Would it be ___ to change my seat? (one word: the adjective from possibility)
