گذشتهٔ کامل استمراریPast Perfect Continuous (had been doing)

B2 · C1

We use the past perfect continuous (had been + -ing) for an activity that was going on before a moment in the past, often up to that moment: When the bus finally came, I had been waiting for an hour.

It is the past version of the present perfect continuous: I've been waiting for an hour (up to now) → I'd been waiting for an hour (up to then).

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Form and use: up to a moment in the past B2

FormExample
Positive: had been + -ingShe had been working there for ten years.
Short formShe'd been working there for ten years.
NegativeI hadn't been sleeping well.
QuestionHow long had you been waiting?

  • We had been driving for six hours when we finally stopped. (duration up to a past moment)
  • How long had you been studying English before you moved to Canada? (question)
  • I hadn't been feeling well, so I went to the doctor. (negative)
  • It had been raining all night, and the roads were flooded. (up to the morning)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ When I arrived, she was waiting for two hours. → ✅ When I arrived, she had been waiting for two hours. (Duration up to a past moment: had been + -ing.)
  • ❌ He had been work there for years. → ✅ He had been working there for years. (had been + -ing)
  • ❌ How long you had been waiting? → ✅ How long had you been waiting? (Question: had + subject + been + -ing.)

Explaining what we saw: She'd been crying B2

  • The ground was wet. It had been raining.
  • She was out of breath because she had been running.
  • His hands were black. He'd been repairing the car.

  • The kitchen smelled wonderful. Mum had been baking. (evidence: the smell)
  • When I came in, the kids looked guilty. They'd been fighting. (evidence: their faces)
  • My legs hurt because I'd been walking all day. (the cause of the pain)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Her eyes were red because she has been crying. → ✅ Her eyes were red because she had been crying. (Past situation: had been crying.)
  • ❌ The ground was wet. It had rained been. → ✅ The ground was wet. It had been raining. (had been + -ing)

Had been doing or had done? B2

had been doing (activity)had done (completed / result)
She had been painting the room. (maybe not finished)She had painted the room. (finished)
I'd been writing emails all morning. (how long)I'd written twenty emails. (how many)
They'd been arguing. (the activity)They'd argued and stopped speaking. (a completed event)

  • I'd been cleaning for two hours, but the house still looked messy. (activity, not finished)
  • I'd cleaned the whole house, so I relaxed. (completed)
  • He'd been taking photos all day. (duration)
  • He'd taken over 500 photos. (how many)
  • We'd known each other since school. (state verb)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'd been knowing him for ten years. → ✅ I'd known him for ten years. (know is a state verb.)
  • ❌ She had been writing three letters before lunch. → ✅ She had written three letters before lunch. (how many: past perfect simple)

Was doing or had been doing? B2

Past continuousPast perfect continuous
When I arrived, she was cooking. (at that moment)When I arrived, she had been cooking for hours. (up to that moment)
It was raining when we left. (it was raining then)It had been raining, so the roads were wet. (before, maybe stopped)

  • When I called, he was having dinner. (at that moment)
  • When I called, he had been trying to call me too. (up to that moment)
  • The baby was sleeping, so we spoke quietly. (at that moment)
  • The baby had been sleeping for three hours when she woke up. (duration up to then)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ When I arrived, they were playing for two hours. → ✅ When I arrived, they had been playing for two hours. (How long up to a past moment: had been + -ing.)
  • ❌ When I phoned, he had been having a shower, so he didn't answer. → ✅ When I phoned, he was having a shower, so he didn't answer. (In progress at that moment: was having.)

تمرین سطح B2

1. When the plane finally took off, we ___ at the airport for five hours.
Duration up to a past moment: had been waiting.
2. How long ___ before she got her first job?
Question: had + subject + been + -ing.
3. By the time the doctor saw me, I ___ (sit) in the waiting room for over an hour.
Duration up to a past moment: had been sitting (had sat is also possible).
4. Tom's clothes were covered in paint. He ___ the fence.
Evidence in the past: had been painting.
5. When I got home, the TV was warm. What's the best explanation?
The past situation is explained by an earlier activity.
6. She was hot and tired because she ___ (play) tennis.
The activity that caused it: had been playing is the usual choice; had played is also possible.
7. By noon, I ___ five kilometres.
How many / how far = completed amount: had walked.
8. By the time we got married, we ___ each other for six years.
Know is a state verb: had known.
9. My hands were dirty because I ___ (work) in the garden.
The activity explaining the situation: had been working is usual.
10. When I looked out, it ___. I could see the rain falling.
In progress at that moment (I could see it): was raining.
11. When I looked out, the streets were wet, but the sun was shining. It ___.
The rain had stopped before that moment: had been raining.
12. When we arrived at the party, people ___ (dance) for hours and were exhausted.
Duration up to the moment we arrived: had been dancing.

C1 · پیشرفته

Repeated activities and plans up to a past time C1

  • She'd been phoning him all week, but he never answered.
  • We'd been seeing a lot of each other before he moved abroad.

  • I'd been going to the gym every day before I hurt my knee. (repeated up to a past time)
  • He'd been sending her flowers for months. (repeated)
  • We'd been hoping to see you at the wedding. (intention before a past moment)
  • The phone had rung once and then stopped. (a single action: simple)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The guest had been arriving at 8. → ✅ The guest had arrived at 8. (A single short action: had arrived.)
  • ❌ I'd meaning to tell you. → ✅ I'd been meaning to tell you. (had been + -ing)

In reported speech C1

Direct speechReported speech
I've been working all day.She said she had been working all day.
We were watching TV when it happened.They said they had been watching TV when it happened.
How long have you been learning English?He asked me how long I had been learning English.

  • He told me he had been trying to call me for hours. (from: I've been trying…)
  • She admitted she hadn't been paying attention. (from: I haven't been paying…)
  • They asked us how long we had been living there. (reported question)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She said she has been waiting since 6. (the waiting is over) → ✅ She said she had been waiting since 6. (The situation is finished: move back.)
  • ❌ He asked me how long had I been studying. → ✅ He asked me how long I had been studying. (Reported questions: normal word order.)

تمرین سطح C1

13. She was tired of the noise. Her neighbour ___ the door all evening.
Repeated action over a period: had been slamming.
14. I ___ to call you for weeks, but I never found the time.
An intention up to a past time: had been meaning.
15. By the time the police caught him, the thief ___ (steal) bikes from the station for months.
A repeated activity over months: had been stealing.
16. I've been looking for a job for months. → He said he ___ for a job for months.

17. She asked me ___.
