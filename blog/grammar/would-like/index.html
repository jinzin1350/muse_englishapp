خانه / گرامر / would like، would rather و prefer

would like، would rather و preferWould Like, Would Rather and Prefer

would like شکل مؤدبانهٔ want («می‌خواهم») است: I'd like a glass of water, please. would rather و prefer می‌گویند از دو چیز کدام را بیشتر می‌خواهید («ترجیح می‌دهم»): I'd rather walk. I prefer tea to coffee.

این عبارت‌ها همه‌جای انگلیسیِ روزمره هستند: سفارش غذا، برنامه‌ریزی و مؤدب بودن. برای would rather + یک نفر دیگر (I'd rather you didn't) صفحهٔ گذشتهٔ غیرواقعی را ببینید.

A1 · مبتدی

I'd like… و Would you like…? A1

would like + اسم یا to + فعل یعنی «می‌خواهم» به شکل مؤدبانه: I'd like a tea. I'd like to go home. با Would you like…? تعارف می‌کنیم و دعوت می‌کنیم.

ساختارمثال
I'd like + اسمI'd like a sandwich, please.
I'd like to + فعلI'd like to book a table.
Would you like + اسم؟Would you like some water?
Would you like to + فعل؟Would you like to come with us?
جواب کوتاهYes, please. / No, thank you. / Yes, I'd love to.

I'd یعنی I would. would like از want مؤدبانه‌تر است، به‌خصوص وقتی چیزی می‌خواهید: I'd like the chicken, please (بهتر از I want the chicken).

بعد از would like، to + فعل بیاورید: I'd like to see the menu. نه I'd like see و نه I'd like seeing.

  • I'd like a coffee, please. سفارش دادن
  • We'd like to check in. to + فعل
  • Would you like something to eat? ~ Yes, please. تعارف
  • Would you like to dance? ~ I'd love to! دعوت

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'd like see the doctor. → ✅ I'd like to see the doctor. would like + to + فعل
  • ❌ Would you like to a coffee? → ✅ Would you like a coffee? would like + اسم (بدون to)
  • ❌ I would like to coming. → ✅ I would like to come. to + شکل سادهٔ فعل

تمرین سطح A1

1. I'd ___ a cup of tea, please.
I'd like + اسم.
2. ___ you like to join us for dinner?
دعوت: Would you like to…?
3. I'd like ___ (book) a room for two nights.
I'd like to + فعل.

A2 · مقدماتی

like یا would like؟ A2

like یعنی به طور کلی دوست داشتن: I like swimming. would like یعنی الان یا در یک موقعیت خواستن: I'd like to swim now.

like (به طور کلی)would like (الان / یک خواستهٔ مشخص)
I like coffee. (به طور کلی دوست دارم.)I'd like a coffee. (الان یکی می‌خواهم.)
Do you like dancing? (سرگرمی‌ات است؟)Would you like to dance? (الان، با من؟)
She likes travelling.She'd like to travel to Peru.

بعد از like معمولاً فعل ing‌دار (یا to + فعل) می‌آید: I like reading / to read.

بعد از would like همیشه to + فعل می‌آید، هرگز فعل ing‌دار: I'd like to read that book.

در سؤال این فرق مهم است: Do you like tea? دربارهٔ سلیقه‌تان می‌پرسد. Would you like tea? تعارف است.

  • I like pizza. به طور کلی
  • I'd like a pizza tonight. خواستهٔ مشخص
  • Do you like Italian food? سلیقه
  • Would you like to try this? تعارف

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Do you like a drink? (an offer) → ✅ Would you like a drink? تعارف: would you like
  • ❌ I'd like going to the concert. → ✅ I'd like to go to the concert. would like + to + فعل

I'd rather…: آنچه ترجیح می‌دهید A2

would rather + شکل سادهٔ فعل یعنی ترجیح می‌دهم: I'd rather stay at home. would rather … than دو چیز را مقایسه می‌کند: I'd rather walk than take the bus.

I'd rather (I would rather) + شکل سادهٔ فعل (بدون to) می‌گوید در یک موقعیت مشخص چه چیزی را ترجیح می‌دهیم: Are you hungry? ~ No, I'd rather just have a drink.

برای مقایسه: would rather + فعل + than + فعل: I'd rather read than watch TV.

منفی: would rather not + شکل سادهٔ فعل: I'd rather not talk about it.

سؤالی: Would you rather have tea or coffee?

وقتی می‌خواهید شخص دیگری کاری بکند، الگو به گذشته تغییر می‌کند: I'd rather you stayed. صفحهٔ گذشتهٔ غیرواقعی را ببینید.

  • I'd rather go by train. ترجیح
  • She'd rather stay in than go to the party. rather … than
  • I'd rather not say. منفی
  • Would you rather live in the city or the countryside? سؤالی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'd rather to stay here. → ✅ I'd rather stay here. would rather + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ I'd rather walk than taking a taxi. → ✅ I'd rather walk than take a taxi. بعد از than شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ I'd not rather go. → ✅ I'd rather not go. would rather not

تمرین سطح A2

4. ___ you like horror films? ~ No, they scare me.
سؤال دربارهٔ سلیقهٔ کلی: Do you like…?
5. It's hot. I'd like ___ a swim.
would like to + فعل.
6. My son ___ (like) playing video games. He plays every day.
به طور کلی: likes.
7. I'm tired. I'd rather ___ home.
would rather + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
8. Would you rather ___ pizza or pasta tonight?
would rather + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
9. I'd rather ___ (not / drive). It's snowing.
would rather not + شکل سادهٔ فعل.

B1 · متوسط

prefer و would prefer B1

prefer (به طور کلی): I prefer tea to coffee. I prefer walking to driving. would prefer (الان): I'd prefer to walk (rather than drive).

الگومثال
prefer + اسم + to + اسمI prefer tea to coffee.
prefer + فعل ing‌دار + to + فعل ing‌دارI prefer walking to driving.
prefer to + فعل + rather than + فعلI prefer to walk rather than drive.
would prefer + اسمI'd prefer water, please.
would prefer to + فعل (+ rather than)I'd prefer to stay in tonight (rather than go out).

prefer برای ترجیح‌های کلی است. would prefer برای یک موقعیت مشخص است، مثل would rather: I'd prefer to eat now. = I'd rather eat now.

بعد از prefer … to، کلمهٔ to حرف اضافه است (to driving)، نه than: I prefer cats to dogs. نه I prefer cats than dogs.

  • My father prefers tea to coffee. کلی
  • I prefer working at home to working in an office. (-ing to -ing)
  • I'd prefer to sit by the window, please. موقعیت مشخص
  • She'd prefer to take a taxi rather than wait for the bus. (rather than)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I prefer coffee than tea. → ✅ I prefer coffee to tea. prefer … to
  • ❌ I'd prefer stay at home. → ✅ I'd prefer to stay at home. would prefer + to + فعل
  • ❌ I prefer swimming to run. → ✅ I prefer swimming to running. بعد از to فعل ing‌دار

تمرین سطح B1

10. I prefer summer ___ winter.
prefer … to.
11. I'd prefer ___ now, if that's OK.
would prefer to + فعل.
12. She prefers cycling ___ (to / drive) to work.
prefer + فعل ing‌دار + to + فعل ing‌دار.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

I'd like you to…, I'd prefer you to…, I'd prefer it if… B2

To talk about what you want someone else to do: would like / would prefer + person + to + verb, or would prefer it if + past: I'd like you to call me.

PatternExample
would like + person + to + verbI'd like you to meet my parents.
would prefer + person + to + verbI'd prefer you to stay here.
would prefer it if + person + past simpleI'd prefer it if you didn't smoke.
would rather + person + past simpleI'd rather you didn't smoke.

Don't use a that clause after would like: I'd like you to come. Not I'd like that you come.

Would prefer it if and would rather + past simple are very polite ways to ask someone not to do something.

  • I'd like you to finish this by Friday. (what I want you to do)
  • My parents would like me to study medicine. (what they want)
  • I'd prefer you not to mention it. (negative)
  • I'd prefer it if you called before visiting. (polite request)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'd like that you help me. → ✅ I'd like you to help me. (would like + person + to)
  • ❌ I'd prefer it if you don't tell anyone. → ✅ I'd prefer it if you didn't tell anyone. (prefer it if + past simple)

تمرین سطح B2

13. I'd like ___ my new colleague, Leila.
Would like + person + to + verb.
14. I'd prefer it if you ___ me before you came.
Would prefer it if + past simple.
15. My boss would like me ___ (lead) the new project.
Would like + person + to + verb.

C1 · پیشرفته

I would have liked to…, I'd rather have… C1

For past wishes that didn't happen: would have liked to + verb (or would like to have done), would rather have + past participle, would have preferred to.

To say you wanted something in the past but it didn't happen, use:

  • would have liked to + base verb: I would have liked to see the museum, but there wasn't time.
  • would like to have + past participle (same meaning, a little more formal): I would like to have met her.
  • would have preferred to + base verb: She would have preferred to stay in a hotel.
  • would rather have + past participle: I'd rather have gone by train. (We didn't.)

Don't put have in both places. I would have liked to see it is better than I would have liked to have seen it (which is common in speech but criticised in careful writing).

  • I would have liked to say goodbye. (I didn't get the chance)
  • We'd have preferred to sit outside, but it was raining. (we didn't)
  • I'd rather have stayed at home. The party was boring. (I didn't)
  • She would like to have studied art. (formal)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I would have like to come. → ✅ I would have liked to come. (would have liked)
  • ❌ I'd rather had stayed. → ✅ I'd rather have stayed. (would rather + have + past participle)

تمرین سطح C1

16. The tour was too short. I ___ more of the city.
A past wish that didn't happen: would have liked to see.
17. We went to a noisy club. I'd rather ___ to a quiet restaurant.
Would rather have + past participle.
18. She ___ (would / prefer / take) the train, but all the tickets were sold.
A past preference that didn't happen.
