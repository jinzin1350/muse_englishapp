Imperatives (Open the door, Don't be late)

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

We use the imperative to tell people what to do: instructions, directions, warnings, advice and invitations. Sit down. Turn left. Don't touch that!

The form is very easy: use the base form of the verb, with no subject.

Form

FormExample
PositiveverbOpen the window. Be quiet.
NegativeDon't + verbDon't open the window. Don't be late.
Suggestion (we)Let's + verbLet's go to the beach.
Negative suggestionLet's not + verbLet's not wait for the bus.

The imperative is the same for one person and for many people: Come in, Tom. Come in, everyone.

With be: Be careful! Don't be late! (not Don't late)

When we use it

  • Instructions: Mix the flour and the eggs.
  • Directions: Go straight on and take the second left.
  • Warnings: Be careful! Don't touch the cooker.
  • Advice: Drink lots of water.
  • Invitations and offers: Come in and sit down. Have a biscuit.
  • Signs: Push. Keep out. Do not enter.

Being polite

An imperative can sound strong. Add please to be polite: Please sit down. Close the door, please. For requests to people you don't know, Can you…? or Could you…? is often better: Could you close the door, please?

Always and never go before the imperative: Always lock the door. Never tell anyone your password.

Examples

  1. Sit down, please. (polite instruction)
  2. Don't forget your keys. (negative)
  3. Turn right at the traffic lights. (directions)
  4. Be careful. The floor is wet. (warning with be)
  5. Don't be sad. Everything will be OK. (negative with be)
  6. Let's have a pizza tonight. (suggestion)
  7. Let's not go out. It's raining. (negative suggestion)
  8. Have a seat and make yourself at home. (invitation)
  9. Never leave your bag on the train. (never + imperative)
  10. Listen and repeat. (classroom instruction)

Common mistakes

❌ Not touch that!

✅ Don't touch that! (Negative imperative: Don't + verb.)

❌ Don't to go.

✅ Don't go. (Use the base form, without to.)

❌ Opens the door, please.

✅ Open the door, please. (Use the base form, without -s.)

❌ Don't late!

✅ Don't be late! (Late is an adjective, so you need be.)

❌ Let's to go.

✅ Let's go. (Let's + base form, without to.)

❌ No smoke here.

✅ Don't smoke here. (Negative imperative: Don't + verb.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. ___ the door, please. It's cold.
Imperative: base form, Close.
2. ___ touch that! It's hot.
Negative imperative: Don't + verb.
3. ___ late for class tomorrow.
Late is an adjective: Don't be late.
4. It's a beautiful day. ___ go to the park!
Suggestion: Let's + verb (with an apostrophe).
5. ___ the lights when you leave.
Imperative: base form, Turn off.
6. Turn left at the bank and ___ straight on.
Two imperatives joined with and: turn… and go.
7. ___ your hands before dinner.
Imperative: base form, Wash.
8. Please ___ your phone in class.
Negative imperative: please don't use.
9. ___ forget your umbrella. It's going to rain.
Negative imperative: Don't forget.
10. Choose the correct sentence.
Let's + base form: Let's have.
11. ___ careful! The floor is wet.
Imperative of be: Be careful.
12. Have a seat and ___ yourself at home.
Two imperatives joined with and: have… and make.
13. I'm tired. – OK, ___ stop and have a rest.
Suggestion for us: let's stop.
14. Never ___ your password to anyone.
Never + imperative: never tell.
15. How do I get to the station? – ___ the second street on the right.
Directions use the imperative: Take.
16. Don't ___ the dog. It bites!
Don't + base form: don't touch.
