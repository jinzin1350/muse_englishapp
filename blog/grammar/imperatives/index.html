جمله‌های امریImperatives

جملهٔ امری همان شکل سادهٔ فعل است، بدون فاعل: Open the window. Sit down. Don't touch that! آن را برای دستورالعمل، دستور، نصیحت، هشدار و دعوت به کار می‌بریم.

برای مؤدبانه‌تر کردن جملهٔ امری، please اضافه کنید یا سؤالی مثل Could you…? بپرسید. صفحهٔ درخواست، پیشنهاد و اجازه گرفتن را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2

A1 · مبتدی

Open the door. Don't run. A1

مثبت: شکل سادهٔ فعل (Sit down. Be quiet.). منفی: Don't + شکل سادهٔ فعل (Don't worry. Don't be late.). برای دستورالعمل، دستور، نصیحت و هشدار.

مثبتمنفی
Open your books.Don't open your books.
Be careful!Don't be late!
Turn left at the lights.Don't turn right.
Take an umbrella.Don't forget your umbrella.

جملهٔ امری برای یک نفر و چند نفر یکی است: Sit down, Ali. Sit down, everyone.

با be، منفی می‌شود Don't be: Don't be silly. (نه Be not silly)

  • Close the door, please. دستورالعمل
  • Don't worry. Everything will be OK. نصیحت
  • Be careful! The floor is wet. هشدار
  • Don't touch the oven. It's hot. هشدار

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Closes the door, please. → ✅ Close the door, please. شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ Not run in the corridor! → ✅ Don't run in the corridor! don't + فعل
  • ❌ Don't to be late. → ✅ Don't be late. don't + شکل سادهٔ فعل

تمرین سطح A1

1. ___ your hands before dinner.
جملهٔ امری: شکل سادهٔ فعل.
2. ___ late for school!
منفی با be: Don't be.
3. ___ worry! I'll help you. (one word)
منفی: Don't + فعل.

A2 · مقدماتی

Let's go, let's not, shall we? A2

Let's + شکل سادهٔ فعل پیشنهادی است که من و تو را با هم شامل می‌شود («بیا…»): Let's go to the beach. منفی: Let's not + فعل (Let's not argue). برای نرم‌تر شدن shall we? اضافه کنید.

الگومثال
Let's + فعلLet's have a coffee.
Let's not + فعلLet's not wait any longer.
Let's …, shall we?Let's start, shall we?
Let me + فعل (پیشنهاد کمک)Let me help you with that.

Let's یعنی let us. شکل کاملِ let us رسمی است: Let us pray.

Let me + فعل یعنی پیشنهاد کمک، یا راهی مؤدبانه برای گفتن کاری که می‌خواهید بکنید: Let me explain.

  • Let's order a pizza tonight. پیشنهاد
  • Let's not tell anyone yet. منفی
  • Let's go for a walk, shall we? (shall we?)
  • Let me carry that bag for you. پیشنهاد کمک

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Let's to go to the cinema. → ✅ Let's go to the cinema. let's + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ Let's don't argue. (BrE, formal) → ✅ Let's not argue. (let's not)
  • ❌ Let's going home. → ✅ Let's go home. شکل سادهٔ فعل

please, make sure, don't forget to A2

جملهٔ امری را با please، just و عبارت‌هایی مثل Make sure you…، Don't forget to…، Remember to…، Feel free to…، Have a… دوستانه‌تر کنید.

عبارتمثال
pleasePlease sit down. / Sit down, please.
just (نرم‌تر)Just wait a minute.
Make sure (that) + جملهMake sure you lock the door.
Don't forget / Remember to + فعلDon't forget to call me.
Feel free to + فعلFeel free to ask questions.
Have a + اسم (آرزوی خوب)Have a good weekend!

جملهٔ امری اگر به غریبه یا رئیس‌تان بگویید ممکن است بی‌ادبانه به نظر برسد. please اضافه کنید یا سؤال بپرسید: Could you close the door, please?

  • Please don't smoke here. (please)
  • Make sure you drink enough water. (make sure)
  • Don't forget to bring your passport. (don't forget to)
  • Have a nice day! آرزوی خوب

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Don't forget calling me. → ✅ Don't forget to call me. forget to + فعل
  • ❌ Make sure to you lock the door. → ✅ Make sure you lock the door. / Make sure to lock the door. make sure + جمله یا to + فعل

تمرین سطح A2

4. I'm hungry. ___ something to eat.
Let's + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
5. It's late. ___ watch another film.
پیشنهاد منفی: Let's not.
6. Let's take a taxi, ___ we? (one word)
پسوند سؤالی: Let's…, shall we?
7. ___ you turn off the lights when you leave.
Make sure (that) + جمله.
8. Don't forget ___ your homework.
Don't forget to + فعل.
9. ___ a great holiday! See you in two weeks. (one word)
آرزوی خوب: Have a…

B1 · متوسط

You sit here. Somebody call a doctor! B1

گاهی جملهٔ امری برای تأکید یا تضاد فاعل دارد: You sit here, and I'll sit there. با someone / everybody: Somebody call an ambulance! Everyone listen. و Don't you dare…! (هشداری قوی).

الگومثالکاربرد
You + فعل امریYou wait here. I'll get the car.تضاد، تأکید
somebody / everyone + فعل امریSomebody help me!چه کسی باید کاری بکند
Don't you + فعلDon't you worry about that.دلگرمی یا هشدار
Don't you dare + فعلDon't you dare touch my phone!هشدار قوی
فعل امری + will you / can you?Close the door, will you?درخواست نرم‌تر

فاعل در جملهٔ امری ممکن است بی‌حوصله یا تند به نظر برسد، پس با احتیاط به کارش ببرید.

  • You stay here. I'll be back in a minute. تضاد
  • Everybody sit down, please. (everybody)
  • Somebody open the window. It's too hot! (somebody)
  • Pass me the salt, will you? (question tag)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Don't you to worry. → ✅ Don't you worry. don't you + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ Somebody calls a doctor! → ✅ Somebody call a doctor! شکل سادهٔ فعل

تمرین سطح B1

10. There's been an accident! Somebody ___ an ambulance!
جملهٔ امری با فاعل: شکل سادهٔ فعل.
11. ___ dare tell her my secret!
هشدار قوی: Don't you dare.
12. Pass me that book, ___ you? (one word)
پسوند سؤالی: will you?

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Study hard and you'll pass; hurry up, or we'll be late B2

Imperative + and = if you do this, the result will happen: Study hard and you'll pass. Imperative + or / or else = if you don't, something bad will happen: Hurry up, or we'll miss the train.

PatternMeaningExample
imperative + and + willif you…, … willPress this button and the door will open.
imperative + or (else) + willif you don't…, … willLeave now, or you'll be late.
negative imperative + orif you…, … willDon't move, or I'll shoot! (film)
Always / Never + imperativegeneral adviceNever give up. Always check your work.

These are common in advice, warnings and adverts: Buy two and get one free!

  • Practise every day and you'll improve quickly. (and = if you)
  • Wear a coat, or you'll catch a cold. (or = if you don't)
  • Never tell anyone your password. (never)
  • Call before 6 pm and we'll deliver today. (advert)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Hurry up, and we'll miss the bus. → ✅ Hurry up, or we'll miss the bus. (bad result: or)
  • ❌ Study hard or you'll pass. → ✅ Study hard and you'll pass. (good result: and)

تمرین سطح B2

13. Work hard, ___ you'll get a promotion.
Good result: and.
14. Turn the music down, ___ the neighbours will complain.
Bad result: or.
15. Don't be late, ___ else you'll miss the start of the film. (one word)
Or else = if you don't.
