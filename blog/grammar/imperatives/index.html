جمله‌های امریImperatives

The imperative is the base form of the verb with no subject: Open the window. Sit down. Don't touch that! We use it for instructions, orders, advice, warnings and invitations.

To make an imperative more polite, add please or use a question like Could you…? See Requests, Offers and Permission.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2

A1 · مبتدی

Open the door. Don't run. A1

Positive: base verb (Sit down. Be quiet.). Negative: Don't + base verb (Don't worry. Don't be late.). Use it for instructions, orders, advice and warnings.

PositiveNegative
Open your books.Don't open your books.
Be careful!Don't be late!
Turn left at the lights.Don't turn right.
Take an umbrella.Don't forget your umbrella.

The imperative is the same for one person or many: Sit down, Ali. Sit down, everyone.

With be, the negative is Don't be: Don't be silly. (not Be not silly)

  • Close the door, please. (instruction)
  • Don't worry. Everything will be OK. (advice)
  • Be careful! The floor is wet. (warning)
  • Don't touch the oven. It's hot. (warning)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Closes the door, please. → ✅ Close the door, please. (base verb)
  • ❌ Not run in the corridor! → ✅ Don't run in the corridor! (don't + verb)
  • ❌ Don't to be late. → ✅ Don't be late. (don't + base verb)

تمرین سطح A1

1. ___ your hands before dinner.
Imperative: base verb.
2. ___ late for school!
Negative with be: Don't be.
3. ___ worry! I'll help you. (one word)
Negative: Don't + verb.

A2 · مقدماتی

Let's go, let's not, shall we? A2

Let's + base verb makes a suggestion that includes you and me: Let's go to the beach. Negative: Let's not + verb (Let's not argue). Add shall we? to make it softer.

PatternExample
Let's + verbLet's have a coffee.
Let's not + verbLet's not wait any longer.
Let's …, shall we?Let's start, shall we?
Let me + verb (offer)Let me help you with that.

Let's = let us. The full form let us is formal: Let us pray.

Let me + verb is an offer or a polite way to say what you're going to do: Let me explain.

  • Let's order a pizza tonight. (suggestion)
  • Let's not tell anyone yet. (negative)
  • Let's go for a walk, shall we? (shall we?)
  • Let me carry that bag for you. (offer)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Let's to go to the cinema. → ✅ Let's go to the cinema. (let's + base verb)
  • ❌ Let's don't argue. (BrE, formal) → ✅ Let's not argue. (let's not)
  • ❌ Let's going home. → ✅ Let's go home. (base verb)

Please, make sure, don't forget to A2

Make imperatives friendlier with please, just, and phrases like Make sure you…, Don't forget to…, Remember to…, Feel free to…, Have a…

PhraseExample
pleasePlease sit down. / Sit down, please.
just (softer)Just wait a minute.
Make sure (that) + clauseMake sure you lock the door.
Don't forget / Remember to + verbDon't forget to call me.
Feel free to + verbFeel free to ask questions.
Have a + noun (wishes)Have a good weekend!

Imperatives can sound rude if you say them to strangers or your boss. Use please, or a question: Could you close the door, please?

  • Please don't smoke here. (please)
  • Make sure you drink enough water. (make sure)
  • Don't forget to bring your passport. (don't forget to)
  • Have a nice day! (wish)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Don't forget calling me. → ✅ Don't forget to call me. (forget to + verb)
  • ❌ Make sure to you lock the door. → ✅ Make sure you lock the door. / Make sure to lock the door. (make sure + clause or to + verb)

تمرین سطح A2

4. I'm hungry. ___ something to eat.
Let's + base verb.
5. It's late. ___ watch another film.
Negative suggestion: Let's not.
6. Let's take a taxi, ___ we? (one word)
Tag: Let's…, shall we?
7. ___ you turn off the lights when you leave.
Make sure (that) + clause.
8. Don't forget ___ your homework.
Don't forget to + verb.
9. ___ a great holiday! See you in two weeks. (one word)
Wish: Have a…

B1 · متوسط

You sit here. Somebody call a doctor! B1

Sometimes imperatives have a subject for emphasis or contrast: You sit here, and I'll sit there. With someone / everybody: Somebody call an ambulance! Everyone listen. And Don't you dare…! (a strong warning).

PatternExampleUse
You + imperativeYou wait here. I'll get the car.contrast, emphasis
somebody / everyone + imperativeSomebody help me!who should act
Don't you + verbDon't you worry about that.reassurance or warning
Don't you dare + verbDon't you dare touch my phone!strong warning
imperative + will you / can you?Close the door, will you?softer request

A subject with an imperative can sound impatient or strong, so use it with care.

  • You stay here. I'll be back in a minute. (contrast)
  • Everybody sit down, please. (everybody)
  • Somebody open the window. It's too hot! (somebody)
  • Pass me the salt, will you? (question tag)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Don't you to worry. → ✅ Don't you worry. (don't you + base verb)
  • ❌ Somebody calls a doctor! → ✅ Somebody call a doctor! (base verb)

تمرین سطح B1

10. There's been an accident! Somebody ___ an ambulance!
Imperative with a subject: base verb.
11. ___ dare tell her my secret!
Strong warning: Don't you dare.
12. Pass me that book, ___ you? (one word)
Tag: will you?

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Study hard and you'll pass; hurry up, or we'll be late B2

Imperative + and = if you do this, the result will happen: Study hard and you'll pass. Imperative + or / or else = if you don't, something bad will happen: Hurry up, or we'll miss the train.

PatternMeaningExample
imperative + and + willif you…, … willPress this button and the door will open.
imperative + or (else) + willif you don't…, … willLeave now, or you'll be late.
negative imperative + orif you…, … willDon't move, or I'll shoot! (film)
Always / Never + imperativegeneral adviceNever give up. Always check your work.

These are common in advice, warnings and adverts: Buy two and get one free!

  • Practise every day and you'll improve quickly. (and = if you)
  • Wear a coat, or you'll catch a cold. (or = if you don't)
  • Never tell anyone your password. (never)
  • Call before 6 pm and we'll deliver today. (advert)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Hurry up, and we'll miss the bus. → ✅ Hurry up, or we'll miss the bus. (bad result: or)
  • ❌ Study hard or you'll pass. → ✅ Study hard and you'll pass. (good result: and)

تمرین سطح B2

13. Work hard, ___ you'll get a promotion.
Good result: and.
14. Turn the music down, ___ the neighbours will complain.
Bad result: or.
15. Don't be late, ___ else you'll miss the start of the film. (one word)
Or else = if you don't.
