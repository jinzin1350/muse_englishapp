Reporting Verbs (She promised to…, He denied…)

B2 · C1

Instead of say and tell, we can use reporting verbs that show the speaker's purpose: promise, refuse, warn, admit, suggest. “I'll definitely call you.” → She promised to call me.

Each verb has its own pattern: to + verb, object + to, -ing, preposition + -ing, or a that clause. This page groups them. For the basics, see Reported Speech.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Verb + to: agree, offer, promise, refuse, threaten B2

Direct speechReported
“OK, I'll help you.”He agreed to help me.
“Shall I carry that?”She offered to carry it.
“I'll definitely be on time.”He promised to be on time.
“No, I won't do it.”She refused to do it.
“Pay or I'll call the police!”He threatened to call the police.

  • My brother offered to drive us to the airport. (offer)
  • The manager refused to give me a refund. (refuse)
  • She promised not to be late again. (negative)
  • They threatened to leave if nothing changed. (threaten)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He offered me to help. → ✅ He offered to help me. (offer + to (no object before to))
  • ❌ She refused answering the question. → ✅ She refused to answer the question. (refuse + to)

Verb + object + to: advise, remind, warn, encourage, invite B2

Direct speechReported
“You should see a doctor.”She advised me to see a doctor.
“Don't forget your keys!”He reminded me to take my keys.
“Don't swim here. It's dangerous.”They warned us not to swim there.
“Would you like to come to dinner?”She invited me to come to dinner.
“Go on, apply for the job!”He encouraged me to apply for the job.

  • My teacher encouraged me to enter the competition. (encourage)
  • The police warned people not to travel. (warn + not to)
  • She persuaded her father to buy a new car. (persuade)
  • They invited us to stay for the weekend. (invite)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ She advised to me see a doctor. → ✅ She advised me to see a doctor. (advise + person + to)
  • ❌ He reminded me that I call him. → ✅ He reminded me to call him. (remind + person + to)

Verb + -ing and verb + preposition + -ing: deny, admit, apologise for, accuse of B2

Direct speechReported
“I didn't take the money.”He denied taking the money.
“Yes, I broke the window.”She admitted breaking the window.
“I'm sorry I was late.”He apologised for being late.
“You stole my phone!”She accused him of stealing her phone.
“I'll pay. I insist!”He insisted on paying.
“Well done! You won!”They congratulated me on winning.

  • The minister denied lying to Parliament. (deny + -ing)
  • I apologise for not calling you back. (apologise for + -ing)
  • She thanked me for helping her. (thank for + -ing)
  • They blamed the driver for causing the accident. (blame for + -ing)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He denied to steal the car. → ✅ He denied stealing the car. (deny + -ing)
  • ❌ She apologised to be late. → ✅ She apologised for being late. (apologise for + -ing)
  • ❌ They accused him for lying. → ✅ They accused him of lying. (accuse of)

Verb + that: explain, admit, claim, complain, agree, insist B2

  • She explained (to me) that the office was closed. (explain + to + person)
  • He complained that the food was cold. (complain)
  • The company announced that it would cut 200 jobs. (announce)
  • My friend pointed out that I had made a mistake. (point out)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He explained me that the bus was late. → ✅ He explained to me that the bus was late. (explain + to + person)
  • ❌ She complained the food was cold to the manager that. → ✅ She complained to the manager that the food was cold. (complain to + person + that)

C1 · Advanced

Suggest and recommend: the right patterns C1

PatternExample
suggest + -ingShe suggested going to the beach.
suggest + (that) + person + base verbShe suggested (that) we go to the beach.
suggest + (that) + person + should + verbShe suggested that we should go to the beach. (BrE)
suggest + (that) + person + past simpleShe suggested we went to the beach. (BrE, informal)
suggest + nounShe suggested a walk.

  • My friend suggested trying the new café. (suggest + -ing)
  • The doctor suggested that I get more sleep. (that + base verb)
  • I recommend that everyone should read this book. (should)
  • Can you suggest a good hotel? (+ noun)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He suggested me to buy a new car. → ✅ He suggested (that) I buy a new car. / He suggested buying a new car. (not suggest + person + to)
  • ❌ She suggested to go out. → ✅ She suggested going out. (suggest + -ing)

Reporting with nouns and adjectives: the claim that, her refusal to C1

VerbNounExample
claim (that)claim (that)The claim that the drug is safe is false.
refuse torefusal toHer refusal to answer surprised us.
insist oninsistence onHis insistence on paying was annoying.
suggest (that)suggestion (that)I liked your suggestion that we leave early.
promise topromise toHe broke his promise to help.

  • The news that the president had resigned shocked everyone. (noun + that)
  • Her refusal to cooperate made things difficult. (noun + to)
  • There was no mention of the problem in the report. (noun + of)
  • He was adamant that he hadn't seen anything. (adjective + that)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ His insistence to pay was annoying. → ✅ His insistence on paying was annoying. (insistence on + -ing)
  • ❌ Her refusal of answering surprised us. → ✅ Her refusal to answer surprised us. (refusal + to)
