Reporting Verbs (suggest, advise, deny, refuse)

Level: B2 · Free grammar guide

Instead of say and tell, we often use a reporting verb that shows the speaker's purpose: 'I'll help you,' he said. → He offered to help me.

Each reporting verb has its own pattern: to-infinitive, object + to-infinitive, -ing, preposition + -ing, or a that clause.

Reporting verb patterns

PatternVerbsExample
verb + to-infinitiveagree, offer, promise, refuse, threaten, decide, claimShe refused to answer.
verb + object + to-infinitiveadvise, ask, tell, warn, remind, encourage, persuade, invite, orderHe advised me to rest.
verb + -ingdeny, admit, suggest, recommendHe denied taking the money.
verb + preposition + -ingapologise for, insist on, admit toShe apologised for being late.
verb + object + preposition + -ingaccuse sb of, blame sb for, congratulate sb on, thank sb for, warn sb againstThey accused him of lying.
verb + that clauseexplain, admit, deny, insist, promise, claim, suggest, agreeHe admitted that he was wrong.

Negative infinitive: She warned me not to touch it. (not to not touch / don't touch)

Suggest

Suggest never takes object + to-infinitive. Use -ing or a that clause: Tom suggested going to the beach. Tom suggested that we go / should go to the beach. (not suggested us to go)

See Reported Speech for tense changes after reporting verbs.

Examples

  1. 'I'll carry your bag.' → He offered to carry my bag. (offer to)
  2. 'I won't do it.' → She refused to do it. (refuse to)
  3. 'You should take a break.' → My boss advised me to take a break. (advise sb to)
  4. 'Don't forget your passport.' → Mum reminded me to take my passport. (remind sb to)
  5. 'I didn't break it.' → He denied breaking it. (deny -ing)
  6. 'Let's order pizza.' → She suggested ordering pizza. (suggest -ing)
  7. 'You stole my idea!' → She accused him of stealing her idea. (accuse sb of)
  8. 'Well done on your new job!' → They congratulated me on getting the job. (congratulate sb on)
  9. 'You must try the cake.' → She insisted on us trying the cake. (insist on)
  10. 'It's my fault.' → He admitted that it was his fault. (admit that)

Common mistakes

❌ She suggested me to take a taxi.

✅ She suggested that I take a taxi. / She suggested taking a taxi. (Suggest + -ing or that clause, never object + to.)

❌ He denied to take the money.

✅ He denied taking the money. (Deny + -ing.)

❌ She apologised to be late.

✅ She apologised for being late. (Apologise for + -ing.)

❌ They accused him for stealing.

✅ They accused him of stealing. (Accuse sb of.)

❌ He advised me don't go.

✅ He advised me not to go. (Negative: not to + verb.)

❌ She offered me to help.

✅ She offered to help me. (Offer + to-infinitive (no object before to).)

3. 'I didn't take the money,' he said. → He denied ___ the money.
deny + -ing.
4. 'Why don't we go to the beach?' Tom said. → Tom suggested ___ to the beach.
suggest + -ing (never suggest us to go).

advise + object + to-infinitive.

apologise for + -ing.
7. 'You broke the window!' the neighbour said to me. → The neighbour accused me ___ the window.
accuse sb of + -ing.
8. 'Congratulations on passing your exam!' they said. → They congratulated me ___ my exam.
congratulate sb on + -ing.
9. 'Remember to buy some bread,' Mum said to me. → Mum reminded me ___ some bread.
remind + object + to-infinitive.
10. 'I won't sign the contract,' he said. → He refused ___ the contract.
refuse + to-infinitive.
11. 'Yes, I cheated in the test,' the student said. → The student admitted ___ in the test.

12. 'Come to my party on Saturday!' Lisa said to us. → Lisa invited us ___ her party.
invite + object + to-infinitive.

insist on (sb) + -ing.
14. Choose the correct sentence.
