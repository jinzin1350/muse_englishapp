حال کامل یا گذشتهٔ سادهPresent Perfect vs Past Simple

هر دو زمان دربارهٔ گذشته‌اند. گذشتهٔ ساده (I lost) دربارهٔ زمانی تمام‌شده است. حال کامل (I've lost) گذشته را به حالا وصل می‌کند.

مهم‌ترین سؤال این است: آیا زمانی تمام‌شده را می‌گویید یا در ذهن دارید؟ اگر بله، گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید. این صفحه از این قاعدهٔ پایه شروع می‌شود و به موقعیت‌هایی می‌رسد که هر دو ممکن‌اند.

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

زمانِ تمام‌شده یا تا حالا؟ A2

گذشتهٔ ساده با زمانِ تمام‌شده (yesterday, last week, in 2019, ago). حال کامل وقتی زمانِ تمام‌شده‌ای در کار نیست و گذشته به حالا وصل است.

گذشتهٔ ساده (زمانِ تمام‌شده)حال کامل (تا حالا / بدون گفتن زمان)
I saw Sara yesterday.I've never seen a ghost.
We went to Rome in 2019.We've been to Rome twice.
She left two hours ago.She's just left.
When did you arrive?Have you ever been to Rome?

کلمه‌هایی که زمانِ تمام‌شده را نشان می‌دهند با گذشتهٔ ساده می‌آیند: yesterday, last night / week / year, ago, in 2015, on Monday, when I was a child, What time…? When…?

کلمه‌هایی که معنی تا حالا دارند اغلب با حال کامل می‌آیند: ever, never, already, yet, so far, since, for (تا حالا).

حال کامل را با زمانِ تمام‌شده نمی‌شود به کار برد: I saw him yesterday. نه I've seen him yesterday.

  • I lost my phone last week. زمانِ تمام‌شده
  • I've lost my phone. Can you call it? نتیجه در حال، بدون زمان
  • When did you buy that dress? When ← گذشتهٔ ساده
  • Have you bought the tickets? بدون زمان، نتیجه در حال

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've seen that film last night. → ✅ I saw that film last night. last night تمام شده: گذشتهٔ ساده
  • ❌ When have you got home? → ✅ When did you get home? When ← گذشتهٔ ساده
  • ❌ I have visited my aunt on Sunday. → ✅ I visited my aunt on Sunday. on Sunday تمام شده: گذشتهٔ ساده

Have you ever…? و Yes, I went there in… A2

با حال کامل دربارهٔ یک تجربه بپرسید یا آن را مطرح کنید، بعد برای جزئیات (کِی، کجا، چطور) گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید.

گفت‌وگو دربارهٔ تجربه‌ها اغلب با حال کامل شروع می‌شود و با گذشتهٔ ساده ادامه پیدا می‌کند:

A: Have you ever been to Isfahan?

B: Yes, I have. I went there last spring.

A: Really? What did you think of it?

B: I loved it. We stayed near the bridge.

سؤال اول دربارهٔ زندگی تا حالاست (بدون زمان). سؤال‌های بعدی دربارهٔ یک سفرِ مشخص است، پس با گذشتهٔ ساده می‌آیند.

Have you been…? دربارهٔ خودِ تجربه می‌پرسد. When did you go? دربارهٔ جزئیات.

  • I've met a famous actor. I met him at the airport in Dubai. تجربه + جزئیات
  • Have you tried this café? ~ Yes, I had lunch there on Monday. سؤال + جزئیات
  • She's won a lot of prizes. She won her first one when she was ten. کلی + مشخص

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Have you ever been to Japan? ~ Yes, I've been there in 2018. → ✅ Have you ever been to Japan? ~ Yes, I went there in 2018. جزئیات با زمان: گذشتهٔ ساده.
  • ❌ I've seen the match. It has been fantastic. → ✅ I've seen the match. It was fantastic. جزئیات دربارهٔ آن مسابقه: گذشتهٔ ساده.

تمرین سطح A2

1. I ___ my grandparents last weekend.
last weekend زمانِ تمام‌شده است.
2. ___ in a hot-air balloon?
تجربهٔ زندگی: Have you ever flown…?
3. We ___ (move) to this flat in 2020.
in 2020 زمانِ تمام‌شده است.
4. Have you ever eaten Korean food? ~ Yes, I ___ it in Seoul last year.
جزئیات با زمان: گذشتهٔ ساده.

When ← گذشتهٔ ساده.

جزئیاتِ یک بارِ مشخص: گذشتهٔ ساده.

B1 · متوسط

هنوز درست است یا تمام شده؟ B1

I've lived here for ten years یعنی هنوز اینجا زندگی می‌کنم. I lived there for ten years یعنی الان نه. برای کسانی که درگذشته‌اند: گذشتهٔ ساده.

با for و دربارهٔ موقعیت‌ها، زمان فعل نشان می‌دهد موقعیت هنوز ادامه دارد یا نه:

حال کامل (هنوز درست است)گذشتهٔ ساده (تمام شده)
He's worked at the bank for five years. (هنوز آنجا کار می‌کند)He worked at the bank for five years. (الان نه)
We've been married since 2010.We were married for ten years. (الان نه)

وقتی دربارهٔ کسانی حرف می‌زنیم که دیگر زنده نیستند، یا دوره‌هایی که تمام شده‌اند، گذشتهٔ ساده می‌آوریم: Hafez wrote beautiful poetry. برای آدم‌های زنده که کارشان ادامه دارد، حال کامل رایج است: This author has written twelve novels (و شاید بیشتر هم بنویسد).

به حالا فکر کنید: آیا موقعیت هنوز ادامه دارد؟ آیا آن آدم هنوز می‌تواند آن کار را بکند؟ اگر نه، گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید.

  • My grandfather lived in Tabriz all his life. درگذشته است
  • My parents have lived in Tabriz all their lives. هنوز آنجا زندگی می‌کنند
  • Picasso painted thousands of works. دیگر زنده نیست
  • She's already written three books, and she's only 25. می‌تواند بیشتر بنویسد

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've worked there for three years, but then I left. → ✅ I worked there for three years, but then I left. تمام شده: گذشتهٔ ساده.
  • ❌ Einstein has won the Nobel Prize. → ✅ Einstein won the Nobel Prize. دیگر زنده نیست: گذشتهٔ ساده.

today, this morning, this year: تمام شده یا نه؟ B1

this morning, today, this week اگر آن بازه هنوز تمام نشده با حال کامل می‌آیند و اگر تمام شده با گذشتهٔ ساده.

بعضی عبارت‌های زمانی می‌توانند بازه‌ای تمام‌شده یا تمام‌نشده باشند. بستگی دارد کِی دارید حرف می‌زنید:

ساعت ۱۰ صبح حرف می‌زنیدساعت ۳ بعدازظهر حرف می‌زنید
I've had two coffees this morning. (هنوز صبح است)I had two coffees this morning. (صبح تمام شده)
Have you seen Reza this morning?Did you see Reza this morning?

با today, this week, this month, this year تا وقتی آن بازه ادامه دارد، حال کامل انتخاب معمول است: It's been a busy week. آخرِ بازه، خیلی‌ها گذشتهٔ ساده می‌آورند: (جمعه عصر) It was a busy week.

اگر زمان مشخصی داخل همان بازهٔ تمام‌نشده را بگویید، گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید: I've spoken to her today. I spoke to her at 9.

  • (at 11 am) I've sent ten emails this morning. صبح تمام نشده
  • (at 6 pm) I sent ten emails this morning. صبح تمام شده
  • I've seen her today. We had lunch together at one. today + زمان مشخص
  • We've had a lot of rain this year. سال تمام نشده

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ (at 9 pm) Have you seen the doctor this morning? → ✅ (at 9 pm) Did you see the doctor this morning? ساعت ۹ شب، صبح تمام شده است.
  • ❌ I've phoned him at 10 o'clock today. → ✅ I phoned him at 10 o'clock today. زمان مشخص: گذشتهٔ ساده.

تمرین سطح B1

تمام شده: گذشتهٔ ساده.

حال کامل + for یعنی هنوز درست است.

دیگر زنده نیست: گذشتهٔ ساده.

ساعت ۴ بعدازظهر صبح تمام شده: گذشتهٔ ساده.

هنوز صبح است: حال کامل.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

American and British English B2

In American English, the past simple is common with just, already, yet and ever: I just ate. Did you finish yet? British English prefers the present perfect.

Both varieties use the present perfect, but American English often uses the past simple where British English uses the present perfect, especially with just, already, yet, ever and for recent news:

British English (usual)American English (also common)
I've just eaten.I just ate.
Have you finished yet?Did you finish yet?
I've already seen it.I already saw it.
Have you ever been to Mexico?Did you ever go to Mexico? / Have you ever been…?
I've lost my keys.I lost my keys.

Both forms are correct. In exams and international English, the present perfect is the safer choice with these words. With a finished time (yesterday, ago), both varieties use the past simple.

American English also uses gotten as a past participle of get in some meanings: It's gotten colder. British English: It's got colder.

  • (BrE) Have you had lunch yet? / (AmE) Did you have lunch yet? (both correct)
  • (BrE) She's just left. / (AmE) She just left. (both correct)
  • (both) She left ten minutes ago. (finished time: past simple everywhere)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ (in any variety) I've just finished it ten minutes ago. → ✅ I finished it ten minutes ago. (With ago, only the past simple.)
  • ❌ (in any variety) Did you see him yet yesterday? → ✅ Did you see him yesterday? (yet doesn't go with a finished time.)

تمرین سطح B2

13. Which sentence is correct in both British and American English?
The present perfect with already is correct everywhere.
14. An American friend says I just got here. What is the British English equivalent?
BrE: present perfect I've just got here.
15. (British English) ___ (you / see) the new Batman film yet?
British English with yet: present perfect.

C1 · پیشرفته

When both are possible: the meaning changes C1

Sometimes both tenses are correct, with a different focus: I've always wanted (still) vs I always wanted (not now); Have you heard? vs Did you hear?

In some contexts, both tenses are grammatical. The choice shows how the speaker sees the event:

  • I've always wanted to visit Japan. (I still want to.) / I always wanted to visit Japan. (Maybe I've visited now, or I've given up.)
  • Have you heard? They're getting married! (news, now) / Did you hear the news this morning? (a specific occasion)
  • I've recently started running. / I recently started running. (Both are fine with recently.)
  • Who's eaten my sandwich? (I've just noticed; the result) / Who ate my sandwich? (also natural, especially in American English)

Speakers also switch tense to show distance: I haven't seen him since the party (up to now) / I didn't see him at the party (one occasion).

With already, just, yet, ever and recently, both tenses are acceptable in modern English, though the present perfect is the standard in British English. With finished time expressions, only the past simple is correct.

  • My father has always loved gardening. (still true)
  • My father always loved gardening. (he has probably died, or it's no longer true)
  • Have you seen my glasses? I can't find them. (present result)
  • Did you see my glasses when you were in the kitchen? (a specific occasion)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I always wanted to try sushi, and I still want to. → ✅ I've always wanted to try sushi, and I still want to. (Still true: present perfect.)
  • ❌ Have you heard the president's speech last night? → ✅ Did you hear the president's speech last night? (last night: past simple)

تمرین سطح C1

16. I always wanted to be a pilot. What does this suggest?
Past simple: the situation is seen as finished.
17. Which sentence suggests that the speaker still loves the city?
Present perfect + always = still true.
18. I ___ (always / want) to learn the piano, and now I'm finally taking lessons.
Both are possible: have always wanted connects the wish to now; always wanted sees it as past.
