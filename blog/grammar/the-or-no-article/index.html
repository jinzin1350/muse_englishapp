the یا بدون حرف تعریفThe or No Article?

In English, many nouns have no article at all. This is called the zero article: I love music. Dogs are friendly. She's at work. This page shows when to use the and when to use nothing.

For a / an and the basic uses of the, see A, An and The.

A2 · مقدماتی

General or specific? Music or the music A2

No article for things in general (plural and uncountable nouns): I love music. Cats are clever. Use the for specific things: The music at the party was great.

General (no article)Specific (the)
I like music.I like the music in this film.
Dogs are friendly.The dogs next door bark all night.
Life is short.The life of Mozart was short.
She hates coffee.The coffee here is terrible.

With plural and uncountable nouns, no article means all of them, in general: Children need sleep. Water is important.

The makes it specific: a particular group or thing that we can identify: the children in my class, the water in this bottle.

Many languages use the for general ideas. In English, general = no article: Love is blind. Nature is beautiful.

  • Vegetables are good for you. (general)
  • The vegetables from my garden are delicious. (specific)
  • I'm not interested in politics. (general, uncountable)
  • The people at the meeting were very friendly. (specific group)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The life is beautiful. → ✅ Life is beautiful. (general: no article)
  • ❌ I love the nature. → ✅ I love nature. (general: no article)
  • ❌ Money can't buy the happiness. → ✅ Money can't buy happiness. (abstract and general: no article)

At home, go to work, in bed, by bus, have lunch A2

No article in fixed phrases with home, work, school, bed, hospital, prison, church, university, with meals (have breakfast) and with by + transport (by bus).

WordPhrases with no article
homego home, be at home (not go to home)
workgo to work, be at work
school / university / collegego to school, start school, be at university
bedgo to bed, be in bed
hospital / prison / churchbe in hospital (BrE), go to church
mealshave breakfast / lunch / dinner
transportby bus / car / train / plane, on foot

These phrases are about the purpose of the place: go to school = to study; in hospital = as a patient. Use the when you mean the building: My mother went to the school to talk to the teacher. (She isn't a student.)

With an adjective or a specific meal, use an article: We had a great lunch. The dinner at the wedding was amazing.

American English usually says in the hospital. Both are correct.

  • I usually get home at six. (no to with home)
  • She's at work until five. (at work)
  • We had breakfast in the garden. (meal)
  • He goes to work by bike. (by + transport)
  • Dad went to the hospital to visit Grandma. (the building (a visitor))

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I go to the bed at eleven. → ✅ I go to bed at eleven. (to bed: no article)
  • ❌ We came to home late. → ✅ We came home late. (home: no to, no article)
  • ❌ She goes to work by the bus. → ✅ She goes to work by bus. (by + transport: no article)

تمرین سطح A2

1. ___ is the most important thing in life.
General, abstract: love (no article).
2. Which sentence is correct?
General: nature (no article).
3. ___ students in my class are all from different countries. (one word)
Specific group: the students in my class.
4. It's late. I'm going to ___.
Go to bed.
5. I go to work ___.
By + transport: no article.
6. Mum went to ___ school yesterday for a meeting with my teacher. (one word)
The building: the school (or my school). She isn't a student.

B1 · متوسط

Countries, cities, rivers and mountains B1

No article for most countries, cities, streets, lakes, single mountains and continents (Canada, Lake Ontario, Mount Everest). Use the for rivers, seas, oceans, mountain ranges, island groups, deserts and plural names (the Nile, the Alps, the USA).

No articleThe
countries: Canada, Iran, Japanthe UK, the USA, the Netherlands, the Philippines
cities and towns: Toronto, Shirazrivers: the Nile, the Thames
continents: Asia, Europeseas and oceans: the Caspian Sea, the Pacific
streets and squares: Oxford Street, Times Squaredeserts: the Sahara
single mountains: Mount Everest, Damavandmountain ranges: the Alps, the Rockies
lakes: Lake Ontarioisland groups: the Bahamas

Use the with country names that include Republic, Kingdom, States or Union, or are plural: the Czech Republic, the United Kingdom, the United States, the Netherlands.

Use the with north, south, east, west as regions: the north of Iran, the Middle East. But compass words that are part of a name have no article: North America, South Korea.

  • I live in Canada, near Lake Ontario. (no article)
  • The Danube flows into the Black Sea. (river, sea)
  • They went skiing in the Alps. (mountain range)
  • My cousin lives in the United States. (States)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I visited the Canada last year. → ✅ I visited Canada last year. (countries: no article)
  • ❌ We swam in Caspian Sea. → ✅ We swam in the Caspian Sea. (seas: the)
  • ❌ She climbed the Mount Kilimanjaro. → ✅ She climbed Mount Kilimanjaro. (single mountain: no article)

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Names of buildings, organisations and newspapers B2

Use the with hotels, museums, theatres, newspapers and names with of (the Hilton, the Louvre, the Guardian, the University of Toronto). No article with place name + airport / station / university / park (Pearson Airport, Oxford University, Central Park) or company names.

TheNo article
hotels, theatres, museums: the Hilton, the Louvreplace + noun: Pearson Airport, Victoria Station
names with of: the University of Toronto, the Bank of CanadaOxford University, Central Park
newspapers: the Globe and Mail, the Timesnames with 's: McDonald's, Harrods, St Paul's Cathedral
organisations: the United Nations, the BBCcompanies: Google, Samsung

Organisations known by their initials often take the when we say the letters: the BBC, the UN, the EU. Names said as words usually don't: NATO, UNESCO.

Names with 's (often the founder's name) have no article: McDonald's, St Paul's Cathedral, Harrods.

  • We stayed at the Marriott. (hotel)
  • I flew into Pearson Airport. (place + airport)
  • She studies at the University of Tehran. (name with of)
  • My dad reads the Guardian every morning. (newspaper)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We visited St Paul's Cathedral and the Harrods. → ✅ We visited St Paul's Cathedral and Harrods. (names with 's: no article)
  • ❌ We visited British Museum. → ✅ We visited the British Museum. (museums: the)
  • ❌ He works for the Google. → ✅ He works for Google. (companies: no article)

تمرین سطح B2

Museums: the.
11. Which is correct?
Names with of: the.

Newspapers: the.

C1 · پیشرفته

Fixed pairs, roles, headlines and decades C1

No article in fixed pairs (arm in arm, from start to finish), for unique roles after verbs like elect, appoint, become (She became CEO), and in headlines. Use the for decades (the 1990s), and say at night (in general) but in the night (one night).

PatternExample
fixed pairshand in hand, face to face, day after day, from top to bottom, husband and wife
unique rolesShe was elected president. He became head of the department.
headlines, notes, signsStorm hits coast. Exit. Turn left at end of road.
decades and centuriesin the 1990s, in the 21st century
time expressionsat night (in general) / in the night (one night); next week / the next week

Roles: if only one person can have the role, the article is often dropped after be, become, elect, appoint, make, name: They appointed her director. With a role that many people have, keep a: She became a director (one of several).

Next / last: next week = the week after this one; the next week = the week after another point in time: I arrived on Monday, and the next week was very busy.

  • They walked along the beach hand in hand. (fixed pair)
  • He was appointed captain of the team. (unique role)
  • Fashion changed a lot in the 1960s. (decade)
  • I woke up twice in the night. (one night)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We met in 1990s. → ✅ We met in the 1990s. (decades: the)
  • ❌ I often work in night. → ✅ I often work at night. (in general: at night)

تمرین سطح C1

13. My grandparents got married in ___.
Decades: the 1970s.
14. Which sentence is correct?
Unique role: no article.
15. I didn't sleep well. The baby woke up three times in ___ night. (one word)
One night: in the night.
