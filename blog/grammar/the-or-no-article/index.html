the یا بدون حرف تعریفThe or No Article?

در انگلیسی خیلی از اسم‌ها اصلاً حرف تعریف ندارند. به این حرف تعریفِ صفر می‌گویند: I love music. Dogs are friendly. She's at work. این صفحه نشان می‌دهد کِی the بیاورید و کِی هیچ چیز.

برای a / an و کاربردهای پایهٔ the صفحهٔ a، an و the را ببینید.

A2 · مقدماتی

کلی یا مشخص؟ music یا the music A2

برای چیزها به طور کلی (اسم جمع و غیرقابل‌شمارش) حرف تعریف نیاورید: I love music. Cats are clever. برای چیزهای مشخص the بیاورید: The music at the party was great.

کلی (بدون حرف تعریف)مشخص (the)
I like music.I like the music in this film.
Dogs are friendly.The dogs next door bark all night.
Life is short.The life of Mozart was short.
She hates coffee.The coffee here is terrible.

با اسم جمع و غیرقابل‌شمارش، نبودنِ حرف تعریف یعنی همه‌شان، به طور کلی: Children need sleep. Water is important.

the آن را مشخص می‌کند: گروه یا چیزی خاص که می‌شود تشخیصش داد: the children in my class, the water in this bottle.

خیلی از زبان‌ها برای مفهوم‌های کلی the می‌آورند. در انگلیسی، کلی یعنی بدون حرف تعریف: Love is blind. Nature is beautiful. (فارسی‌زبان‌ها گاهی «زندگی» یا «طبیعت» را با the ترجمه می‌کنند؛ لازم نیست.)

  • Vegetables are good for you. کلی
  • The vegetables from my garden are delicious. مشخص
  • I'm not interested in politics. کلی، غیرقابل‌شمارش
  • The people at the meeting were very friendly. گروهِ مشخص

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The life is beautiful. → ✅ Life is beautiful. کلی: بدون حرف تعریف.
  • ❌ I love the nature. → ✅ I love nature. کلی: بدون حرف تعریف.
  • ❌ Money can't buy the happiness. → ✅ Money can't buy happiness. مفهومِ کلی و انتزاعی: بدون حرف تعریف.

at home, go to work, in bed, by bus, have lunch A2

در عبارت‌های ثابت با home, work, school, bed, hospital, prison, church, university، با وعده‌های غذا (have breakfast) و با by + وسیلهٔ نقلیه (by bus) حرف تعریف نمی‌آید.

کلمهعبارت‌های بدون حرف تعریف
homego home, be at home (نه go to home)
workgo to work, be at work
school / university / collegego to school, start school, be at university
bedgo to bed, be in bed
hospital / prison / churchbe in hospital (بریتانیایی)، go to church
وعده‌های غذاhave breakfast / lunch / dinner
وسیلهٔ نقلیهby bus / car / train / plane, on foot

این عبارت‌ها دربارهٔ کاربرد آن مکان‌اند: go to school یعنی برای درس خواندن؛ in hospital یعنی به‌عنوان بیمار. وقتی منظورتان خودِ ساختمان است the بیاورید: My mother went to the school to talk to the teacher. (او دانش‌آموز نیست.)

با صفت یا یک وعدهٔ غذای مشخص حرف تعریف بیاورید: We had a great lunch. The dinner at the wedding was amazing.

انگلیسی آمریکایی معمولاً می‌گوید in the hospital. هر دو درست است.

  • I usually get home at six. با home حرف to نمی‌آید
  • She's at work until five. at work
  • We had breakfast in the garden. وعدهٔ غذا
  • He goes to work by bike. by + وسیلهٔ نقلیه
  • Dad went to the hospital to visit Grandma. خودِ ساختمان (به‌عنوان ملاقات‌کننده)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I go to the bed at eleven. → ✅ I go to bed at eleven. to bed: بدون حرف تعریف.
  • ❌ We came to home late. → ✅ We came home late. home: بدون to و بدون حرف تعریف.
  • ❌ She goes to work by the bus. → ✅ She goes to work by bus. by + وسیلهٔ نقلیه: بدون حرف تعریف.

تمرین سطح A2

1. ___ is the most important thing in life.
کلی، انتزاعی: love (بدون حرف تعریف).
2. کدام جمله درست است؟
کلی: nature (بدون حرف تعریف).
3. ___ students in my class are all from different countries. (one word)
گروهِ مشخص: the students in my class.
4. It's late. I'm going to ___.
Go to bed.
5. I go to work ___.
By + وسیلهٔ نقلیه: بدون حرف تعریف.
6. Mum went to ___ school yesterday for a meeting with my teacher. (one word)
خودِ ساختمان: the school (یا my school). او دانش‌آموز نیست.

B1 · متوسط

کشورها، شهرها، رودها و کوه‌ها B1

برای بیشتر کشورها، شهرها، خیابان‌ها، دریاچه‌ها، تک‌کوه‌ها و قاره‌ها حرف تعریف نیاورید (Canada, Lake Ontario, Mount Everest). برای رودها، دریاها، اقیانوس‌ها، رشته‌کوه‌ها، مجمع‌الجزایر، بیابان‌ها و اسم‌های جمع the بیاورید (the Nile, the Alps, the USA).

بدون حرف تعریفthe
کشورها: Canada, Iran, Japanthe UK, the USA, the Netherlands, the Philippines
شهرها: Toronto, Shirazرودها: the Nile, the Thames
قاره‌ها: Asia, Europeدریاها و اقیانوس‌ها: the Caspian Sea, the Pacific
خیابان‌ها و میدان‌ها: Oxford Street, Times Squareبیابان‌ها: the Sahara
تک‌کوه‌ها: Mount Everest, Damavandرشته‌کوه‌ها: the Alps, the Rockies
دریاچه‌ها: Lake Ontarioمجمع‌الجزایر: the Bahamas

با اسم کشورهایی که Republic, Kingdom, States یا Union دارند یا جمع‌اند the بیاورید: the Czech Republic, the United Kingdom, the United States, the Netherlands.

با north, south, east, west به معنی منطقه the بیاورید: the north of Iran, the Middle East. اما وقتی جهت بخشی از اسم است حرف تعریف نمی‌آید: North America, South Korea.

  • I live in Canada, near Lake Ontario. بدون حرف تعریف
  • The Danube flows into the Black Sea. رود، دریا
  • They went skiing in the Alps. رشته‌کوه
  • My cousin lives in the United States. States

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I visited the Canada last year. → ✅ I visited Canada last year. کشورها: بدون حرف تعریف.
  • ❌ We swam in Caspian Sea. → ✅ We swam in the Caspian Sea. دریاها: the.
  • ❌ She climbed the Mount Kilimanjaro. → ✅ She climbed Mount Kilimanjaro. تک‌کوه: بدون حرف تعریف.

تمرین سطح B1

7. My uncle lives in ___.
اسمِ جمع: the Netherlands.
8. We took a boat trip on ___.
رودها: the Nile.
9. Have you ever been to ___ USA? (one word)
The USA (States).

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Names of buildings, organisations and newspapers B2

Use the with hotels, museums, theatres, newspapers and names with of (the Hilton, the Louvre, the Guardian, the University of Toronto). No article with place name + airport / station / university / park (Pearson Airport, Oxford University, Central Park) or company names.

TheNo article
hotels, theatres, museums: the Hilton, the Louvreplace + noun: Pearson Airport, Victoria Station
names with of: the University of Toronto, the Bank of CanadaOxford University, Central Park
newspapers: the Globe and Mail, the Timesnames with 's: McDonald's, Harrods, St Paul's Cathedral
organisations: the United Nations, the BBCcompanies: Google, Samsung

Organisations known by their initials often take the when we say the letters: the BBC, the UN, the EU. Names said as words usually don't: NATO, UNESCO.

Names with 's (often the founder's name) have no article: McDonald's, St Paul's Cathedral, Harrods.

  • We stayed at the Marriott. (hotel)
  • I flew into Pearson Airport. (place + airport)
  • She studies at the University of Tehran. (name with of)
  • My dad reads the Guardian every morning. (newspaper)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We visited St Paul's Cathedral and the Harrods. → ✅ We visited St Paul's Cathedral and Harrods. (names with 's: no article)
  • ❌ We visited British Museum. → ✅ We visited the British Museum. (museums: the)
  • ❌ He works for the Google. → ✅ He works for Google. (companies: no article)

تمرین سطح B2

10. We spent the whole day in ___.
Museums: the.
11. Which is correct?
Names with of: the.
12. I read about it in ___ Guardian this morning. (one word)
Newspapers: the.

C1 · پیشرفته

Fixed pairs, roles, headlines and decades C1

No article in fixed pairs (arm in arm, from start to finish), for unique roles after verbs like elect, appoint, become (She became CEO), and in headlines. Use the for decades (the 1990s), and say at night (in general) but in the night (one night).

PatternExample
fixed pairshand in hand, face to face, day after day, from top to bottom, husband and wife
unique rolesShe was elected president. He became head of the department.
headlines, notes, signsStorm hits coast. Exit. Turn left at end of road.
decades and centuriesin the 1990s, in the 21st century
time expressionsat night (in general) / in the night (one night); next week / the next week

Roles: if only one person can have the role, the article is often dropped after be, become, elect, appoint, make, name: They appointed her director. With a role that many people have, keep a: She became a director (one of several).

Next / last: next week = the week after this one; the next week = the week after another point in time: I arrived on Monday, and the next week was very busy.

  • They walked along the beach hand in hand. (fixed pair)
  • He was appointed captain of the team. (unique role)
  • Fashion changed a lot in the 1960s. (decade)
  • I woke up twice in the night. (one night)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We met in 1990s. → ✅ We met in the 1990s. (decades: the)
  • ❌ I often work in night. → ✅ I often work at night. (in general: at night)

تمرین سطح C1

13. My grandparents got married in ___.
Decades: the 1970s.
14. Which sentence is correct?
Unique role: no article.
15. I didn't sleep well. The baby woke up three times in ___ night. (one word)
One night: in the night.
