The or No Article?

Level: A2 · Free grammar guide

Use no article to talk about things in general: Dogs are friendly. Coffee is expensive.

Use the for specific things that the listener knows: The dogs next door are friendly. The coffee in this café is expensive.

No article: things in general

With plural and uncountable nouns used in general, don't use the: I love music. Children need sleep. Life is short. (not The life is short)

No article: common expressions

GroupExamples
Mealshave breakfast / lunch / dinner

Transport with byby bus, by car, by train

The: specific and unique things

  • Something specific or already mentioned: Where's the milk I bought?
  • Something everyone knows in the situation: Can you pass the salt? Close the door.
  • Unique things: the sun, the moon, the world, the internet

Examples

  1. I don't like cats. (cats in general)
  2. The cats in my street are very noisy. (specific cats)
  3. Water is important for your health. (general, uncountable)
  4. We have lunch at 1 o'clock. (meal)
  5. She plays basketball at school. (sport)
  6. I go to work by train. (by + transport)
  7. The moon is very bright tonight. (unique)
  8. The Amazon is in South America. (river)
  9. They live in Japan. (country)
  10. We visited the Netherlands last year. (plural country name)

Common mistakes

❌ The life is beautiful.

✅ Life is beautiful. (In general: no article.)

❌ I love the dogs. (all dogs)

✅ I love dogs. (In general: no article.)

❌ We have the dinner at 8.

✅ We have dinner at 8. (Meals: no article.)

❌ She plays the tennis.

✅ She plays tennis. (Sports: no article.)

❌ I live in the Canada.

✅ I live in Canada. (Most countries: no article.)

❌ Sun is very hot today.

✅ The sun is very hot today. (Unique things: the.)

❌ I go to school by the bus.

✅ I go to school by bus. (By + transport: no article.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. I love ___. I think all dogs are wonderful.
Dogs in general: no article, dogs.
2. ___ is the closest star to Earth.
Unique things: the sun.
3. We have ___ at 7 o'clock every evening.
Meals: no article, dinner.
4. She speaks ___ and French.
Languages: no article, Spanish.
5. I usually go to work ___.
By + transport: no article, by bus.
6. ___ is the longest river in Africa.
Rivers: the Nile.
7. Have you ever been to ___?
States in the name: the United States.
8. My brother plays ___ every Saturday.
Sports: no article, football.
9. Can you pass me ___, please? It's next to you.
A specific thing we both can see: the salt.
10. They live in ___.
Most countries: no article, Canada.
11. I'm tired. I'm going to ___.
Fixed expression: go to bed (to sleep).
12. ___ is very important for your health.
In general, uncountable: no article, Sleep.
13. The children are at ___ now. They finish at 3.
Places with a usual purpose: at school.
14. Where's ___ I bought yesterday?
Specific milk (I bought it yesterday): the milk.
15. Mount Everest is in ___.
Mountain ranges: the Himalayas.
16. ___ is short. Enjoy it!
In general: no article, Life.
