صفت‌های درجه‌پذیر و مطلقGradable and Extreme Adjectives

صفت‌های درجه‌پذیر می‌توانند کم یا زیاد باشند: cold, big, good. با آن‌ها very, a bit و extremely بیاورید: very cold. صفت‌های مطلق خودشان معنی «خیلی» را دارند: freezing (یخ‌زده)، huge (غول‌آسا)، amazing (فوق‌العاده). با آن‌ها absolutely یا really بیاورید: absolutely freezing.

آوردنِ کلمهٔ درست قبل از صفت یکی از آسان‌ترین راه‌ها برای طبیعی‌تر حرف زدن است.

B1 · B2 · C1

B1 · متوسط

very cold, absolutely freezing B1

با صفت‌های درجه‌پذیر very / extremely / a bit بیاورید (very big). با صفت‌های مطلق absolutely یا really بیاورید (absolutely huge). really با هر دو می‌آید.

درجه‌پذیر (+ very)مطلق (+ absolutely)
coldfreezing
hotboiling
bighuge, enormous
smalltiny
goodamazing, fantastic, excellent
badterrible, awful
tiredexhausted
hungrystarving
angryfurious
interestingfascinating
scaredterrified

نگویید very freezing یا absolutely cold. really با هر دو درست است: really cold, really freezing. (در فارسی «خیلی» با همه چیز می‌آید، اما در انگلیسی very با صفت مطلق نمی‌آید.)

  • It's very cold today. درجه‌پذیر
  • It's absolutely freezing! مطلق
  • The hotel was really amazing. really با هر دو
  • I'm extremely tired. درجه‌پذیر

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The film was very fantastic. → ✅ The film was absolutely fantastic. صفت مطلق: absolutely.
  • ❌ I'm absolutely hungry. → ✅ I'm absolutely starving. absolutely + صفت مطلق.
  • ❌ Their house is very enormous. → ✅ Their house is absolutely enormous. صفت مطلق: absolutely.

تمرین سطح B1

1. I haven't eaten all day. I'm ___.
صفت مطلق + absolutely.
2. Their new house is ___. It has eight bedrooms!
صفت مطلق + absolutely.
3. The view from the top was ___ amazing. (one word)
صفت مطلق: absolutely amazing.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Absolute adjectives: completely different, almost empty B2

Absolute adjectives describe all-or-nothing qualities: impossible, perfect, unique, dead, empty, full, finished, different, wrong, right. Use completely, totally, absolutely, entirely with them, and almost / nearly for “not quite”: almost empty.

Absolute adjectiveNatural combinations
impossibleabsolutely / completely impossible
differentcompletely / totally different
wrong / rightcompletely wrong, absolutely right
empty / fullcompletely empty, almost full
unique / perfectabsolutely perfect (very unique is common but avoided in careful English)
finished / readyalmost / nearly finished

Almost, nearly and practically go well with absolute adjectives: The bottle is almost empty.

Some adjectives can be gradable or absolute with different meanings: The party was really dead (= boring, informal) / The battery is completely dead.

  • Her new job is completely different from her old one. (completely)
  • You're absolutely right. (absolutely)
  • The cinema was almost empty. (almost)
  • It's totally impossible to finish today. (totally)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ This painting is very unique. → ✅ This painting is unique. (unique: no very in careful English)
  • ❌ The task was very impossible. → ✅ The task was completely impossible. (impossible: completely / absolutely)

تمرین سطح B2

4. The two brothers are ___ identical. Nobody can tell them apart.
Absolute adjective: completely identical.
5. The petrol tank is ___ empty. We need to stop soon.
Not quite: almost empty.
6. You're ___ right. I should apologise. (one word)
Absolute adjective: absolutely right.

C1 · پیشرفته

Highly unlikely, deeply concerned: adverb + adjective pairs C1

Advanced English uses natural adverb + adjective pairs instead of very: highly unlikely, deeply concerned, bitterly disappointed, fully aware, perfectly normal, seriously injured.

AdverbNatural adjectives
highlyunlikely, likely, successful, recommended, qualified, effective
deeplyconcerned, moved, shocked, offended, grateful
bitterlydisappointed, cold, opposed
fullyaware, booked, insured, qualified
perfectlyclear, normal, capable, safe
utterly / totallyridiculous, exhausted, convinced
seriously / badlyill, injured, damaged

These pairs are collocations: they aren't grammar rules, but native speakers use them far more often. Highly unlikely is natural; highly cheap is not.

Don't use highly with simple physical words: very tall (not highly tall).

  • It's highly unlikely that it will snow in June. (highly)
  • I was deeply moved by her speech. (deeply)
  • The fans were bitterly disappointed. (bitterly)
  • I'm fully aware of the risks. (fully)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The hotel comes strongly recommended. → ✅ The hotel comes highly recommended. (highly recommended)
  • ❌ He was heavily injured in the accident. → ✅ He was seriously injured in the accident. (seriously / badly injured)

تمرین سطح C1

7. It's ___ unlikely that he'll get the job. He has no experience.
Collocation: highly unlikely.
8. We were ___ disappointed when the concert was cancelled.
Collocation: bitterly disappointed.
9. I'm ___ aware of the risks, but I still want to try. (one word)
Collocation: fully aware.
