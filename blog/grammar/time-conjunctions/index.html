حرف‌های ربط زمانی و ترتیب رویدادهاTime Conjunctions and Sequencing Words

حرف‌های ربط زمانی دو کار را به هم وصل می‌کنند و نشان می‌دهند کِی اتفاق می‌افتند: I had dinner when I got home. While I was cooking, the phone rang. Wash your hands before you eat.

این صفحه دربارهٔ کلمه‌های ربط زمانی به طور کلی و در گذشته است. برای قاعدهٔ when I get home (حال برای آینده) صفحهٔ جمله‌واره‌های زمانی آینده را ببینید.

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

when, while, before, after A2

when یعنی در آن زمان (وقتی که). while یعنی در طول یک کارِ طولانی‌تر (اغلب با گذشتهٔ استمراری). before و after ترتیب را نشان می‌دهند. جمله‌وارهٔ زمانی می‌تواند اول (با ویرگول) یا دوم بیاید.

حرف ربطمثال
whenWhen I got home, I made dinner.
whileThe phone rang while I was having a shower.
beforeBrush your teeth before you go to bed.
afterAfter we finished lunch, we went for a walk.

وقتی جمله‌وارهٔ زمانی اول می‌آید، بعدش ویرگول بگذارید: When the film ended, we went home. وقتی دوم می‌آید ویرگول لازم نیست: We went home when the film ended.

بعد از before و after فعل ‑ing‌دار هم می‌آید: Before leaving, turn off the lights. After finishing work, I went to the gym.

  • When I saw her, I smiled. when
  • I fell asleep while I was watching TV. while + کار طولانی‌تر
  • Check the price before you buy it. before
  • After the meeting finished, we had coffee. after

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ While I watched TV, the phone was ringing. → ✅ While I was watching TV, the phone rang. کار طولانی‌تر: گذشتهٔ استمراری.
  • ❌ After finish work, I go to the gym. → ✅ After finishing work, I go to the gym. after + ‑ing.

تمرین سطح A2

1. I hurt my leg ___ I was playing football.
در طول یک کار طولانی‌تر: while.
2. Always wash your hands ___ you eat.
ترتیب: before.
3. ___ I arrived at the party, everyone was dancing. (one word: at that time)
در آن زمان: When.

B1 · متوسط

as soon as, until, since, once, by the time B1

as soon as یعنی بلافاصله بعد از. until یعنی تا آن زمان. since یعنی از آن زمان تا الان (با حال کامل). once یعنی بعد از اینکه (وقتی چیزی تمام شد). by the time یعنی تا وقتی که، قبل از آن لحظه یا همان لحظه.

حرف ربطمثال
as soon asI called you as soon as I heard the news.
until / tillWe waited until the rain stopped.
sinceI've lived here since I was ten.
onceOnce you've paid, you'll get a receipt.
by the timeBy the time we arrived, the film had started.

بعد از since یک زمانِ گذشته بیاورید (since 2019، since I left school)؛ جملهٔ اصلی معمولاً حال کامل است: I've known him since we were children.

  • She left as soon as the lesson ended. بلافاصله بعد از
  • I stayed in bed until 10. تا آن زمان
  • We've been friends since we met at university. از آن موقع تا الان
  • By the time I got to the station, the train had left. by the time + گذشتهٔ کامل

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I know him since I was a child. → ✅ I've known him since I was a child. since: حال کامل. (فارسی می‌گوید «از بچگی می‌شناسمش»، اما انگلیسی حال کامل لازم دارد.)
  • ❌ Wait here until I will come back. → ✅ Wait here until I come back. until + حال.

first, then, after that, finally, in the end, at last B1

برای تعریف کردن ماجرا یا دادن دستورالعمل به ترتیب: first (اول)، then / next / after that (بعد)، finally (در آخر). in the end یعنی بعد از مدتی طولانی یا تغییرهای زیاد. at last یعنی بالاخره، بعد از انتظاری طولانی (با حس آسودگی). eventually یعنی سرانجام، بعد از مدتی طولانی.

کلمهکاربردمثال
first / firstlyقدم اولFirst, heat the oil.
then / next / after thatقدم بعدیThen add the onions. After that, add the rice.
finallyقدم آخرFinally, add salt.
in the endنتیجهٔ نهایی بعد از مشکل‌ها یا تغییرهاWe looked at ten flats. In the end, we chose the first one.
at lastبعد از انتظاری طولانی (اغلب با آسودگی)At last! You're here!
eventuallyبعد از مدتی طولانیThe bus eventually arrived.

in the end (سرانجام) را با at the end of (بخش آخرِ چیزی) قاطی نکنید: At the end of the film, everyone cried.

  • First, open the app. Then, create an account. دستورالعمل
  • After that, we had lunch in a café. قدم بعدی
  • In the end, we decided to stay at home. نتیجهٔ نهایی
  • At last, the exams are over! آسودگی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ In the end of the lesson, we had a quiz. → ✅ At the end of the lesson, we had a quiz. بخش آخرِ چیزی: at the end of.
  • ❌ After, we went to the cinema. → ✅ After that, we went to the cinema. after that (به‌عنوان کلمهٔ ربط).

تمرین سطح B1

4. I've worked here ___ I finished university.
از آن موقع تا الان: since.
5. We stayed on the beach ___ the sun went down.
تا آن زمان: until.
6. I'll call you as ___ as I land. (one word)
As soon as یعنی بلافاصله بعد از.
7. ___, mix the flour and the sugar. Then add the eggs.
قدم اول: First.
8. We waited for three hours, and ___ the plane took off.
بعد از مدتی طولانی: eventually.
9. We'll have a party at the ___ of the course. (one word)
بخش آخر: at the end of.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

The moment, every time, as, whenever B2

The moment / the minute = as soon as. Every time / whenever = each time. As = at the same time as (two things changing or happening together). Hardly … when = something happened immediately after.

ConjunctionExample
the moment / the minuteThe moment I saw him, I knew something was wrong.
every time / wheneverEvery time I see that photo, I smile.
as (same time)As I was leaving, the phone rang. As you get older, you worry less.
just asThe bus left just as I arrived.
hardly / barely … whenI had hardly sat down when the doorbell rang.

As is common with changes that happen together: As the population grows, cities get bigger.

  • The minute she walked in, everyone stopped talking. (as soon as)
  • Whenever I go to Paris, I visit the Louvre. (each time)
  • The rain started just as we left the house. (exactly then)
  • As prices rise, people spend less. (two changes together)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Every times I call him, he's busy. → ✅ Every time I call him, he's busy. (every time (singular))
  • ❌ I had hardly arrived than it started raining. → ✅ I had hardly arrived when it started raining. (hardly … when)

تمرین سطح B2

10. ___ I hear this song, I think of my grandmother.
Each time: Every time.
11. The train left just ___ we got to the platform.
Exactly then: just as.
12. The ___ I saw the house, I knew I wanted to buy it. (one word)
The moment = as soon as.

C1 · پیشرفته

No sooner … than, upon arrival, prior to, following C1

Formal time expressions: no sooner … than and hardly / scarcely … when (often with inversion), upon / on + -ing or noun, prior to, following, having + past participle.

ExpressionExample
no sooner … thanWe had no sooner arrived than it started to rain. / No sooner had we arrived than it started to rain.
scarcely / hardly … whenScarcely had she left when the phone rang.
upon / on + -ing or nounUpon arriving, guests are given a key. On arrival, …
prior to (= before)Prior to the meeting, please read the report.
following (= after)Following the announcement, shares fell.
having + past participleHaving finished the report, she went home.

When no sooner, hardly or scarcely start the sentence, use inversion (had + subject). See Inversion After Negative Adverbials.

Remember: no sooner goes with than; hardly / scarcely go with when.

  • No sooner had I sat down than the phone rang. (no sooner … than)
  • Upon receiving the results, the team celebrated. (upon + -ing)
  • Please arrive 30 minutes prior to departure. (prior to)
  • Following the storm, many roads were closed. (following)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ No sooner had I arrived when they left. → ✅ No sooner had I arrived than they left. (no sooner … than)
  • ❌ No sooner I had arrived than they left. → ✅ No sooner had I arrived than they left. (inversion: had I)

تمرین سطح C1

13. No sooner had the match started ___ it began to snow.
No sooner … than.
14. ___ the meeting, all participants must sign in.
Formal before: Prior to.
15. Scarcely had we left the house ___ it started to pour with rain. (one word)
Scarcely … when.
