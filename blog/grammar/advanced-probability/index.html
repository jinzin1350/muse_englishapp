Probability: Bound To, May Well, Likely To

Level: C1 · Free grammar guide

Beyond will, might and probably, English has many ways to say how likely something is: He's bound to call. It may well rain. Prices are likely to rise.

These expressions help you sound more precise and natural, especially in writing and discussion.

From certain to impossible

ProbabilityExpressionsExample
almost certainbe bound to, be sure to, be certain toShe's bound to pass. She's so well prepared.
probablebe likely to, may / might / could well, there's a good chance (that), in all likelihood, the chances are (that)It may well rain later.
possiblemay / might / could, there's a chance (that)There's a chance we'll be late.
improbablebe unlikely to, it's doubtful whether, there's little chance (that)He's unlikely to change his mind.
impossiblethere's no chance (that), can't possiblyThere's no chance we'll finish today.

Grammar notes

  • bound / sure / certain / likely / unlikely + to + verb: He's likely to be late. Or It's likely that he'll be late. (not It's bound that)
  • may / might / could + well + verb: She may well be right. (= it's quite likely)
  • chance + that clause: There's a good chance that it'll rain. The chances are (that)… (plural verb)
  • probably / definitely: after will, before won't: He'll probably forget. She probably won't come.

Bound to often sounds like a confident prediction from experience: It's bound to rain. It always rains on my birthday.

Examples

  1. Don't worry, he's bound to call you back. (almost certain)
  2. With this traffic, we're sure to be late. (almost certain)
  3. Prices are likely to rise again next year. (probable)
  4. She may well be right, but we need more evidence. (quite likely)
  5. There's a good chance that it'll snow this weekend. (probable)
  6. In all likelihood, the project will be finished by May. (formal, probable)
  7. The chances are that he's forgotten. (probable)
  8. The new rule is unlikely to make much difference. (improbable)
  9. It's highly unlikely that they'll win. (very improbable)
  10. There's no chance that he'll change his mind. (impossible)

Common mistakes

❌ He's bound pass the exam.

✅ He's bound to pass the exam. (Bound to + verb.)

❌ It's bound that he will win.

✅ He's bound to win. (Bound / sure don't take It… that.)

❌ She may good be right.

✅ She may well be right. (May / might / could well.)

❌ He'll forget probably.

✅ He'll probably forget. (Probably goes after will.)

❌ The chances is that he'll be late.

✅ The chances are that he'll be late. (The chances are (plural).)

❌ It's very impossible.

✅ It's highly unlikely. / It's impossible. (Impossible is already extreme; use highly with unlikely.)
