ضمیرهای ملکی (mine، yours)Possessive Pronouns: Mine, Yours, His, Hers

ضمیرهای ملکی (mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs) جای صفت ملکی + اسم را می‌گیرند (مثل «مال من، مال تو»): This is my bag → This bag is mine.

برای my, your, his و her صفحهٔ ضمیرهای فاعلی و صفت‌های ملکی و برای Ali's و my parents' صفحهٔ مالکیت با آپاستروف و s را ببینید.

A2 · مقدماتی

mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs A2

ضمیر ملکی را بدون اسم بیاورید: This phone is mine. Is this yours? صفت ملکی را قبل از اسم بیاورید: This is my phone.

صفت ملکی + اسمضمیر ملکی
my bagmine
your bagyours
his baghis
her baghers
our bagours
their bagtheirs

ضمیرهای ملکی تنها می‌آیند و هیچ وقت قبل از اسم نمی‌آیند: This is mine. (نه This is mine bag)

آپاستروف ندارند: yours, hers, ours, theirs (نه your's, her's).

  • That's not my coffee. Mine is the big one. (mine)
  • Is this pen yours? (yours)
  • The red car is hers. (hers)
  • Their house is bigger than ours. (ours)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ This is mine book. → ✅ This is my book. قبل از اسم: my
  • ❌ Is this your's? → ✅ Is this yours? بدون آپاستروف
  • ❌ These seats are our. → ✅ These seats are ours. تنها: ours

تمرین سطح A2

1. Is this your umbrella? ~ No, it isn't ___.
تنها: mine.
2. These aren't our tickets. ___ are in my bag.
تنها: ours.
3. This is Mina's coat. It's ___. (one word, = Mina's)
تنها، یک زن: hers.

B1 · متوسط

Whose is it? Whose bag is this? B1

برای پرسیدن صاحبِ چیزی whose («مال کی») بیاورید: Whose bag is this? یا Whose is this bag? با ضمیر ملکی یا 's جواب بدهید: It's mine. It's Ali's.

سؤالجواب
Whose phone is this?It's mine. / It's Sara's.
Whose are these shoes?They're his. / They're my brother's.

whose می‌تواند قبل از اسم (Whose car is this?) یا تنها (Whose is this car?) بیاید.

whose (مالکیت) را با who's (یعنی who is / who has) قاطی نکنید.

  • Whose coat is this? whose + اسم
  • Whose are these keys? ~ They're mine. whose تنها
  • Whose turn is it? ~ It's yours. نوبت
  • Who's coming tonight? who is

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Who's bag is this? → ✅ Whose bag is this? مالکیت: whose
  • ❌ Whose is this bag? ~ It's of Ali. → ✅ Whose is this bag? ~ It's Ali's. 's
  • ❌ Who's are these? → ✅ Whose are these? مالکیت: whose

تمرین سطح B1

4. ___ phone is ringing? ~ It's mine.
مالکیت: whose.
5. Whose are these glasses? ~ They're ___.
صاحب + 's.
6. ___ turn is it to wash the dishes? (one word)
مالکیت: whose turn.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

My own, of my own, on my own B2

Own emphasises possession: my own room, a room of my own. On my own = alone or without help (I live on my own). A friend of mine = one of my friends.

PatternExample
possessive + own + nounI have my own car. She runs her own business.
a / some / no + noun + of my ownI'd like a room of my own. He has no children of his own.
on my own = aloneShe lives on her own. = She lives by herself.
on my own = without helpI fixed it on my own.
noun + of + possessive pronouna friend of mine, a cousin of hers

Own always follows a possessive adjective (my, your, his…) or 's: my own room, Ali's own idea (not an own room).

  • I'd love to have my own flat. (my own)
  • They want a house of their own. (of their own)
  • He travelled around Asia on his own. (alone)
  • A friend of mine is a pilot. (a friend of mine)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I want an own room. → ✅ I want my own room. (own after a possessive)
  • ❌ She lives on herself. → ✅ She lives on her own. (on her own (or by herself))
  • ❌ He's a cousin of me. → ✅ He's a cousin of mine. (of + possessive pronoun)

تمرین سطح B2

7. My sister has ___ company. She started it in 2020.
Possessive + own.
8. I don't need help. I can do it on ___ own.
On my own = without help.
9. Leila is a colleague ___ mine. We work together. (one word)
A colleague of mine.

C1 · پیشرفته

Formal possessives: one's own, whose for things, of which C1

In formal English, use one's own for people in general. Whose can refer to things (a company whose profits rose), and of which is a formal alternative. Possessive pronouns avoid repeating a noun in comparisons: Her results were better than his.

PatternExample
one's ownIt's important to have one's own opinions.
whose for thingsIt's a city whose history goes back 3,000 years.
of which (formal)a house, the roof of which needs repair
comparisonsOur prices are lower than theirs.
each other'sThey borrow each other's clothes.

Whose for things is correct and common: a word whose meaning I don't know. The… of which is more formal.

In comparisons, use possessive pronouns to avoid repeating the noun: Your English is better than mine (= my English).

  • One must make one's own decisions. (one's own)
  • She works for a company whose products are sold worldwide. (whose for things)
  • The building, the walls of which were cracked, was closed. (of which)
  • My car is older than yours. (comparison)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It's a country which history is very long. → ✅ It's a country whose history is very long. (possession: whose)
  • ❌ My score was higher than your. → ✅ My score was higher than yours. (comparison: yours)

تمرین سطح C1

10. It's a book ___ ending surprised everyone.
Possession (things too): whose.
11. Their house is much bigger than ___.
Comparison: ours (= our house).
12. It is important to respect other people's opinions as well as ___ own. (one word, formal)
Formal: one's own.
