Possessive Pronouns: Mine, Yours, His, Hers

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

Possessive pronouns (mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs) replace a possessive adjective + noun: This is my bag → This bag is mine.

For my, your, his and her, see Subject Pronouns and Possessive Adjectives. For Ali's and my parents', see Possessive 's.

A2 · Elementary

Mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs A2

Possessive adjective + nounPossessive pronoun
my bagmine
your bagyours
his baghis
her baghers
our bagours
their bagtheirs

  • That's not my coffee. Mine is the big one. (mine)
  • Is this pen yours? (yours)
  • The red car is hers. (hers)
  • Their house is bigger than ours. (ours)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ This is mine book. → ✅ This is my book. (before a noun: my)
  • ❌ Is this your's? → ✅ Is this yours? (no apostrophe)
  • ❌ These seats are our. → ✅ These seats are ours. (alone: ours)

Practice: A2

1. Is this your umbrella? ~ No, it isn't <span class="gap">___</span>.
Alone: mine.
2. These aren't our tickets. <span class="gap">___</span> are in my bag.
Alone: ours.
3. This is Mina's coat. It's <span class="gap">___</span>. (one word, = Mina's)
Alone, a woman: hers.

B1 · Intermediate

Whose is it? Whose bag is this? B1

QuestionAnswer
Whose phone is this?It's mine. / It's Sara's.
Whose are these shoes?They're his. / They're my brother's.

  • Whose coat is this? (whose + noun)
  • Whose are these keys? ~ They're mine. (whose alone)
  • Whose turn is it? ~ It's yours. (turn)
  • Who's coming tonight? (who is)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Who's bag is this? → ✅ Whose bag is this? (possession: whose)
  • ❌ Whose is this bag? ~ It's of Ali. → ✅ Whose is this bag? ~ It's Ali's. ('s)
  • ❌ Who's are these? → ✅ Whose are these? (possession: whose)

Practice: B1

4. <span class="gap">___</span> phone is ringing? ~ It's mine.
Possession: whose.
5. Whose are these glasses? ~ They're <span class="gap">___</span>.
Owner + 's.
6. <span class="gap">___</span> turn is it to wash the dishes? (one word)
Possession: whose turn.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

My own, of my own, on my own B2

PatternExample
possessive + own + nounI have my own car. She runs her own business.
a / some / no + noun + of my ownI'd like a room of my own. He has no children of his own.
on my own = aloneShe lives on her own. = She lives by herself.
on my own = without helpI fixed it on my own.
noun + of + possessive pronouna friend of mine, a cousin of hers

  • I'd love to have my own flat. (my own)
  • They want a house of their own. (of their own)
  • He travelled around Asia on his own. (alone)
  • A friend of mine is a pilot. (a friend of mine)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I want an own room. → ✅ I want my own room. (own after a possessive)
  • ❌ She lives on herself. → ✅ She lives on her own. (on her own (or by herself))
  • ❌ He's a cousin of me. → ✅ He's a cousin of mine. (of + possessive pronoun)

Practice: B2

7. My sister has <span class="gap">___</span> company. She started it in 2020.
Possessive + own.
8. I don't need help. I can do it on <span class="gap">___</span> own.
On my own = without help.
9. Leila is a colleague <span class="gap">___</span> mine. We work together. (one word)
A colleague of mine.

C1 · Advanced

Formal possessives: one's own, whose for things, of which C1

PatternExample
one's ownIt's important to have one's own opinions.
whose for thingsIt's a city whose history goes back 3,000 years.
of which (formal)a house, the roof of which needs repair
comparisonsOur prices are lower than theirs.
each other'sThey borrow each other's clothes.

  • One must make one's own decisions. (one's own)
  • She works for a company whose products are sold worldwide. (whose for things)
  • The building, the walls of which were cracked, was closed. (of which)
  • My car is older than yours. (comparison)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ It's a country which history is very long. → ✅ It's a country whose history is very long. (possession: whose)
  • ❌ My score was higher than your. → ✅ My score was higher than yours. (comparison: yours)

Practice: C1

10. It's a book <span class="gap">___</span> ending surprised everyone.
Possession (things too): whose.
11. Their house is much bigger than <span class="gap">___</span>.
Comparison: ours (= our house).
12. It is important to respect other people's opinions as well as <span class="gap">___</span> own. (one word, formal)
Formal: one's own.
