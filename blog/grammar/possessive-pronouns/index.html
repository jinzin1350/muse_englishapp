Possessive Pronouns (mine, yours, hers...)

Level: A2 · Free grammar guide

Possessive pronouns (mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs) replace a possessive adjective + noun: This is my bag. → This bag is mine.

We use them so we don't repeat the noun: Your car is red, and mine is blue.

Possessive adjectives and pronouns

Possessive adjective (+ noun)Possessive pronoun (no noun)
It's my phone.It's mine.
It's your phone.It's yours.
It's his phone.It's his.
It's her phone.It's hers.
It's our phone.It's ours.
It's their phone.It's theirs.

Possessive pronouns never have an apostrophe: yours, hers, ours, theirs (not your's, her's). And don't use the before them: It's mine (not the mine).

Its is almost never used as a possessive pronoun. Say its + noun: The dog wants its dinner.

Whose…?

Use whose to ask who something belongs to. Answer with a possessive pronoun or 's: Whose keys are these? – They're mine. / They're Tom's.

A friend of mine

We say a friend of mine, a cousin of hers, a colleague of ours = one of my friends, one of her cousins. (not a friend of me)

Examples

  1. That isn't my coffee. Mine is the one with milk. (mine = my coffee)
  2. Is this umbrella yours? (yours = your umbrella)
  3. Sam forgot his book, so I gave him mine. (mine = my book)
  4. Lisa says the blue bike is hers. (hers = her bike)
  5. Their garden is big, but ours is bigger. (ours = our garden)
  6. We've got our tickets. Have they got theirs? (theirs = their tickets)
  7. Whose jacket is this? – It's his. (whose)
  8. She's a friend of mine. (a friend of mine)
  9. Your phone is newer than mine. (comparing)
  10. These aren't my keys. Mine are in my bag. (adjective vs pronoun)

Common mistakes

❌ This bag is my.

✅ This bag is mine. (With no noun after it, use the pronoun: mine.)

❌ Is this yours bag?

✅ Is this your bag? (Before a noun, use the adjective: your.)

❌ The red car is their's.

✅ The red car is theirs. (No apostrophe in possessive pronouns.)

❌ He's a friend of me.

✅ He's a friend of mine. (Fixed phrase: a friend of mine.)

❌ It's the mine.

✅ It's mine. (No the before possessive pronouns.)

❌ This is mines.

✅ This is mine. (Mine has no -s.)

No noun after it: mine.
3. That's not their car. ___ is the red one.
No noun after it, no apostrophe: Theirs.
4. Whose coat is this? Is it ___, Anna?
No noun after it: yours (no apostrophe).
5. We've got our tickets. Have you got ___?
= your tickets: yours.
6. Sam lent me a book. It's ___, not mine.
= Sam's book: his.
7. Lisa says the umbrella is ___.
No noun after it: hers (no apostrophe).

= their house: theirs.
9. He's a friend of ___.
Fixed phrase: a friend of mine.
10. This isn't your room. It's ___. (It belongs to my brother and me.)
= our room: ours.
11. Choose the correct sentence.
No noun after them: mine and yours.
12. Can I borrow your phone? ___ isn't working.
= my phone: Mine.
13. Is this ___ bag or hers?
Before a noun (bag), use the adjective: your.

= our garden: ours.
15. The black car is ___. (It belongs to my parents.)
= their car: theirs.
