زمان حال کامل (ماضی نقلی)Present Perfect (I have done)

حال کامل (have / has + اسم مفعول) گذشته را به حالا وصل می‌کند: I've lost my keys (الان پیدایشان نمی‌کنم). She's been to Japan three times (در طول زندگی‌اش تا حالا).

مهم این نیست که کِی اتفاق افتاده، مهم این است که حالا اهمیت دارد. اگر زمانش را بگویید (yesterday, in 2020)، گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید. صفحهٔ حال کامل یا گذشتهٔ ساده را ببینید.

A2 · B1 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

ساختار: have / has + اسم مفعول A2

have / has + اسم مفعول (قسمت سوم فعل): I've finished. She hasn't called. Have you eaten?

I / you / we / theyhe / she / it
مثبتI have (I've) finished.She has (she's) finished.
منفیI haven't finished.She hasn't finished.
سؤالیHave you finished?Has she finished?
جواب کوتاهYes, I have. / No, I haven't.Yes, she has. / No, she hasn't.

اسم مفعولِ فعل‌های باقاعده مثل گذشتهٔ ساده به ‑ed ختم می‌شود: work → worked, play → played. فعل‌های بی‌قاعده شکل خودشان را دارند:

فعلگذشتهٔ سادهاسم مفعول
bewas / werebeen
gowentgone / been
dodiddone
seesawseen
eatateeaten
writewrotewritten
taketooktaken
havehadhad

He's می‌تواند he is باشد یا he has. اگر کلمهٔ بعدی اسم مفعول باشد، has است: He's gone یعنی he has gone. He's going یعنی he is going.

  • We've bought a new car. (have + bought)
  • She hasn't done her homework. منفی
  • Have you seen my phone? سؤالی
  • My parents have never eaten sushi. اسم مفعولِ بی‌قاعده

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She have finished. → ✅ She has finished. با she: has
  • ❌ I have went to Spain. → ✅ I have been to Spain. اسم مفعول بیاورید (been / gone)، نه گذشتهٔ ساده.
  • ❌ Have you ate breakfast? → ✅ Have you eaten breakfast? (eat → eaten)

تجربه‌ها: ever, never, before A2

حال کامل را برای تجربه‌های زندگی تا امروز به کار ببرید، بدون گفتن زمانش: Have you ever been to Canada? I've never tried sushi.

وقتی از تجربه‌هایمان در زندگی حرف می‌زنیم (در هر زمانی تا حالا)، حال کامل می‌آوریم و نمی‌گوییم کِی:

  • I've been to Italy three times.
  • Have you ever ridden a horse? (ever یعنی «تا حالا، هیچ وقت در زندگی‌تان»)
  • She's never seen snow. (never یعنی «هیچ وقت»)
  • I've met him before.

ever در سؤال می‌آید؛ never خودش جمله را منفی می‌کند (not اضافه نکنید): I've never been there. نه I haven't never been there.

اگر بعد جزئیات بدهید (کِی، کجا، چطور)، به گذشتهٔ ساده بروید: I've been to Rome. I went there in 2019 with my family.

been to یعنی رفته و برگشته (یک تجربه). I've been to Paris یعنی زمانی در زندگی‌ام به پاریس سفر کرده‌ام.

  • Have you ever eaten Persian food? ever: هیچ وقت تا حالا
  • I've never flown in a helicopter. (never)
  • We've seen this film before. (before)
  • How many countries have you visited? چندتا: تا حالا

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I have never not been to Asia. → ✅ I have never been to Asia. never خودش جمله را منفی می‌کند
  • ❌ Have you ever went to Rome? → ✅ Have you ever been to Rome? اسم مفعول بیاورید: been.
  • ❌ I have been to London in 2019. → ✅ I went to London in 2019. با زمانِ گذشتهٔ مشخص: گذشتهٔ ساده

just, already و yet A2

just یعنی کمی پیش؛ already یعنی زودتر از آنچه انتظار داشتیم؛ yet یعنی تا حالا (در سؤال و منفی، آخر جمله).

کلمهمعنیجایگاهمثال
justکمی پیش، همین الانقبل از اسم مفعولI've just had lunch.
alreadyقبل از حالا، شاید زودتر از انتظارقبل از اسم مفعول (یا آخر جمله)She's already left.
yetتا حالا (انتظار داریم اتفاق بیفتد)آخر جمله؛ در سؤال و منفیHave you finished yet? I haven't finished yet.

yet در سؤال می‌پرسد آیا کاری که انتظارش را داریم انجام شده یا نه. در جملهٔ منفی یعنی هنوز انجام نشده، اما انتظار داریم بشود: The bus hasn't come yet.

در انگلیسی آمریکایی با این کلمه‌ها گذشتهٔ ساده هم رایج است: I just ate. Did you finish yet? در انگلیسی بریتانیایی حال کامل انتخاب معمول است. هر دو درست‌اند.

  • Your taxi's just arrived. همین چند لحظه پیش
  • I've already paid the bill. زودتر از آنچه فکر می‌کردید
  • Has the film started yet? سؤال با yet
  • We haven't decided yet. منفی با yet

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've finished yet. → ✅ I've already finished. yet برای سؤال و منفی است
  • ❌ Have you yet finished? → ✅ Have you finished yet? yet آخر جمله می‌آید
  • ❌ I've eaten just. → ✅ I've just eaten. just قبل از اسم مفعول می‌آید

تمرین سطح A2

1. My brother ___ his leg. He's in hospital.
با he: has + اسم مفعول broken.
2. ___ you finished your homework?
با you: Have you…?
3. I ___ (write) three emails this morning. It's still morning.
هنوز صبح است: have written (در انگلیسی آمریکایی wrote هم ممکن است).
4. ___ been to a football match?
سؤال دربارهٔ تجربه: Have you ever been…?
5. I ___ a famous person.
have + never + اسم مفعول.
6. My grandmother is 85, and she ___ (never / use) a computer in her life.
تجربه تا امروز: has never used.
7. Don't cook for me. I've ___ eaten.
already یعنی قبل از حالا.
8. Have you booked the hotel ___?
سؤال دربارهٔ کاری که انتظارش را داریم: yet آخر جمله.
9. Sorry, Mr Karimi isn't here. He ___ (just / go) out.
همین چند لحظه پیش: has just gone (در انگلیسی آمریکایی: just went).

B1 · متوسط

been یا gone؟ B1

She's gone to Paris یعنی الان آنجاست. She's been to Paris یعنی رفته و برگشته.

جملهمعنی
Sara has gone to the bank.الان در بانک است (یا در راه بانک).
Sara has been to the bank.به بانک رفته و الان برگشته.
Sara has been to Japan.زمانی در زندگی‌اش به ژاپن سفر کرده.

وقتی آن آدم هنوز آنجاست gone بیاورید. وقتی برگشته و برای تجربه‌ها been بیاورید.

Where have you been? یعنی جایی بودی و حالا برگشته‌ای. Where has he gone? یعنی الان اینجا نیست.

  • My parents have gone to Mashhad for a week. الان آنجا هستند
  • Have you ever been to Mashhad? تجربه
  • Where have you been? I've been waiting for an hour! الان برگشته‌ای
  • Ali isn't here. He's gone home. الان خانه است

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've gone to Rome twice. → ✅ I've been to Rome twice. تجربه: been.
  • ❌ Where's Reza? ~ He's been to the gym. (he's still there) → ✅ Where's Reza? ~ He's gone to the gym. هنوز آنجاست: gone.

کاری در گذشته که نتیجه‌اش الان پیداست، و خبر B1

حال کامل نشان می‌دهد کاری در گذشته الان نتیجه‌ای دارد (I've lost my keys یعنی کلیدها را ندارم) و خبر تازه می‌دهد: The prime minister has resigned.

اغلب وقتی حال کامل می‌آوریم که کاری در گذشته به خاطر نتیجه‌اش در حال مهم است:

  • I've lost my keys. (الان نمی‌توانم وارد شوم.)
  • Somebody has broken the window. (الان شکسته است.)
  • She's had a haircut. (موهایش الان فرق کرده.)

برای دادن خبر هم به کار می‌رود: The police have arrested a man in connection with the robbery. وقتی جزئیات اضافه می‌کنیم (کِی، چطور)، معمولاً به گذشتهٔ ساده می‌رویم: He was arrested at his home last night.

مقایسه کنید: I've lost my keys (هنوز پیدایشان نکرده‌ام) و I lost my keys yesterday, but I found them later (ماجرای تمام‌شده). صفحهٔ حال کامل یا گذشتهٔ ساده را ببینید.

  • The lift has broken down again. We'll have to use the stairs. نتیجه در حال
  • I've cut my finger. It's bleeding. نتیجه در حال
  • Scientists have discovered a new species of frog. خبر
  • Prices have gone up. Everything is more expensive. تغییری که نتیجه‌اش الان پیداست

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Prices have went up. → ✅ Prices have gone up. اسم مفعول: gone.
  • ❌ The president has resigned yesterday. → ✅ The president resigned yesterday. با yesterday: گذشتهٔ ساده.

today, this week, recently, so far B1

حال کامل را با بازه‌های زمانیِ تمام‌نشده به کار ببرید: today, this week, this year, recently, lately, so far, up to now.

وقتی بازهٔ زمانی حالا را هم شامل می‌شود، حال کامل بیاورید:

  • I've drunk three coffees today. (امروز هنوز تمام نشده.)
  • We haven't had any rain this month.
  • She's been very busy recently / lately.
  • So far, 200 people have signed the letter.

مقایسه کنید: I've seen her this morning (هنوز صبح است) و I saw her this morning (الان بعدازظهر است و صبح تمام شده).

recently با هر دو زمان می‌آید: I've recently started yoga / I recently started yoga. lately معمولاً با حال کامل می‌آید.

  • I haven't seen Ali this week. این هفته تمام نشده
  • It's rained a lot this year. امسال تمام نشده
  • Have you been to the cinema lately? این اواخر
  • Up to now, we haven't had any problems. تا حالا

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've seen her yesterday. → ✅ I saw her yesterday. yesterday تمام شده: گذشتهٔ ساده.
  • ❌ Lately I watched a lot of films. → ✅ Lately I've watched a lot of films. با lately: حال کامل

It's the first time… و the best… I've ever… B1

بعد از It's the first / second time و بعد از صفت عالی + ever حال کامل بیاورید: It's the first time I've driven. It's the best film I've ever seen.

وقتی دربارهٔ حالا حرف می‌زنیم، بعد از این الگوها حال کامل می‌آوریم:

  • It's the first time I've eaten sushi. نه It's the first time I eat sushi.
  • This is the third time he's been late this week.
  • She's the nicest person I've ever met.
  • That was the most boring film I've ever seen.

برای موقعیتی در گذشته گذشتهٔ کامل بیاورید: It was the first time I'd ever flown. صفحهٔ گذشتهٔ کامل را ببینید.

ever با صفت عالی اختیاری است، اما خیلی رایج است: the best meal I've (ever) had.

  • This is the first time I've visited Canada. بار اول + حال کامل
  • It's the second time you've asked me that today. بار دوم
  • This is the best coffee I've ever tasted. صفت عالی + ever
  • He's the funniest person I've ever known. صفت عالی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It's the first time I see snow. → ✅ It's the first time I've seen snow. بار اول + حال کامل
  • ❌ This is the best book I ever read. → ✅ This is the best book I've ever read. صفت عالی + حال کامل (انگلیسی بریتانیایی)

تمرین سطح B1

الان برگشته‌ای: have you been.

هنوز در جلسه است: gone.

نتیجه در حال (موهایت عالی شده): Have you had…? (در انگلیسی آمریکایی اغلب Did you have…?).
16. I ___ much work this week. I've been ill.
این هفته تمام نشده: حال کامل.

so far today یعنی تا این لحظه.

با lately: حال کامل.

It's the first time + حال کامل.

the third time + حال کامل.

C1 · پیشرفته

The present perfect in news and reports C1

News and reports often introduce a topic with the present perfect and then give the details in the past simple: A storm has hit the coast. It arrived at 3 am and destroyed…

In news reports, the first sentence often uses the present perfect to present the event as new and relevant now. The following sentences, which give details of time, place and manner, use the past simple:

Firefighters have rescued a family of five from a burning building in Tabriz. The fire started in the kitchen just after midnight, and the family escaped through a window.

In academic and formal writing, the present perfect introduces recent research or a general state of knowledge, and the past simple reports specific studies: Several studies have shown a link between sleep and memory. In 2019, a team at Oxford found that…

Headlines often drop have / has and use the past participle or the present simple: Fire destroys factory. Missing child found safe.

Once you mention a specific past time, place or event, the past simple usually takes over. Going back to the present perfect is possible to sum up: So far, the fire has caused damage worth millions.

  • The central bank has raised interest rates. The decision was announced this morning. (news, then details)
  • Researchers have developed a new test for the disease. They tested it on 500 patients. (report, then details)
  • Rescue teams have found two more survivors. (news with a result now)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ A plane has crashed in the mountains. It has crashed at 6 am. → ✅ A plane has crashed in the mountains. It crashed at 6 am. (With the time detail: past simple.)
  • ❌ Smith has published a famous study in 1998. → ✅ Smith published a famous study in 1998. (A specific date: past simple.)

When I've finished…: the present perfect for the future C1

In time clauses about the future, the present perfect shows that one action will be complete before another: I'll call you when I've finished.

After when, after, as soon as, until, once, before + a future meaning, we don't use will. We use the present simple, or the present perfect to stress that the first action will be finished first:

  • I'll call you when I've finished the report. (first finish, then call)
  • You can't leave until you've answered all the questions.
  • Once you've tried it, you'll love it.

Often both tenses are possible with little difference: I'll call you when I get home / when I've got home. The present perfect is clearer when the first action takes time and must be completed.

Don't use the present perfect when the two actions happen at the same time: When I see her, I'll tell her. Not When I've seen her, I'll tell her (you tell her while you see her).

See Future Time Clauses for when, if, as soon as with the future.

  • As soon as I've saved enough money, I'm going to buy a car. (first save, then buy)
  • Don't send the email until you've checked it. (complete the check first)
  • After we've eaten, we'll go for a walk. (first eat)
  • When I arrive, I'll call you. (same time: present simple)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'll call you when I will finish. → ✅ I'll call you when I've finished. (No will in the time clause.)
  • ❌ When I've seen him tomorrow, I'll give him the book. → ✅ When I see him tomorrow, I'll give him the book. (Same time: present simple.)

تمرین سطح C1

22. Police ___ a man after a robbery in the city centre. He was arrested at his home on Tuesday.
The news is introduced with the present perfect; the details follow in the past simple.
23. In an essay: A number of researchers ___ this question in recent years.
A general state of recent research: present perfect.
24. A strong earthquake has shaken parts of Japan. It ___ (strike) at 4.15 local time.
A time detail: past simple struck.
25. You can watch TV after you ___ your homework.
Time clause, first action complete: have done.
26. I'll lend you the book when I ___ it.
The reading must be complete first: have read.
27. Once you ___ (fill) in the form, give it to the receptionist.
Once + present perfect (or present simple): no will.
