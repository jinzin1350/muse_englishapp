حرف تعریف a، an و theA, An and The (Articles)

a / an (حرف تعریف نامعین) دربارهٔ یک چیزِ نامشخص است: I need a pen. (یک خودکار، هر خودکاری). the (حرف تعریف معین) دربارهٔ چیز مشخصی است که شنونده می‌داند کدام است: Can you pass me the pen? (همان خودکاری که روی میز است)

حرف تعریف برای خیلی از زبان‌آموزها سخت است، چون بعضی زبان‌ها آن را ندارند؛ فارسی هم حرف تعریف معینی مثل the ندارد. این صفحه قدم‌به‌قدم جلو می‌رود. برای وقتی که هیچ حرف تعریفی لازم نیست، صفحهٔ the یا بدون حرف تعریف را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

a یا an؟ A1

a را قبل از صدای بی‌صدا (a car, a university) و an را قبل از صدای صدادار (an apple, an hour) بیاورید. فقط با اسم مفردِ قابل‌شمارش.

a + صدای بی‌صداan + صدای صدادار
a book, a cat, a dogan apple, an egg, an orange
a university (/juː/)an umbrella (/ʌ/)
a European (/jʊə/)an hour (حرف h خوانده نمی‌شود)
a one-way ticket (/w/)an MBA (/em/)

انتخاب به صدا بستگی دارد، نه به حرف: an hour (h خوانده نمی‌شود)، a uniform (با صدای /juː/ شروع می‌شود).

a / an را فقط با اسم مفردِ قابل‌شمارش بیاورید: a chair. نه a chairs و نه a water.

  • I've got a brother and an older sister. (a / an)
  • She's waiting for an hour. h خوانده نمی‌شود
  • He goes to a university in Toronto. صدای /juː/
  • Would you like an ice cream? صدای صدادار

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I have a apple. → ✅ I have an apple. صدای صدادار: an
  • ❌ She's an university student. → ✅ She's a university student. صدای /juː/: a
  • ❌ I need a informations. → ✅ I need some information. غیرقابل‌شمارش: بدون a

a یا the؟ بار اولِ گفتن و چیزی که هر دو می‌دانیم A1

اولین بار که از چیزی حرف می‌زنید a / an بیاورید. وقتی معلوم است کدام یکی است the بیاورید: I saw a cat. The cat was black. برای چیزهایی که فقط یکی از آن‌ها هست هم the می‌آید: the sun, the moon.

a / an یعنی یکی از چندتا؛ شنونده هنوز نمی‌داند کدام: There's a man at the door.

the یعنی شنونده می‌داند کدام، چون:

  • قبلاً از آن حرف زده‌ایم: A man called. The man said he was your uncle.
  • از موقعیت معلوم است: Close the door, please. (درِ همین اتاق)
  • فقط یکی هست: the sun, the sky, the internet, the world.
  • I bought a jacket and a shirt. The jacket was cheap. بار اول a، بعد the
  • Can you turn off the light? از موقعیت معلوم است
  • The moon is very bright tonight. فقط یکی هست
  • There's a good film on TV tonight. بار اول

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I saw a film yesterday. A film was great. → ✅ I saw a film yesterday. The film was great. بار دوم: the
  • ❌ Look at a sun! → ✅ Look at the sun! فقط یکی هست: the

تمرین سطح A1

1. There's ___ egg in the fridge if you're hungry.
صدای صدادار: an egg.
2. My cousin studies at ___ university in Montreal.
university با صدای /juː/ شروع می‌شود: a.
3. We waited for ___ hour. (one word)
h خوانده نمی‌شود: an hour.
4. We stayed in a hotel near the beach. ___ hotel had a great pool.
بار دوم: the hotel.
5. Someone's knocking. Can you open ___ door, please?
از موقعیت معلوم است: the door (درِ همین اتاق).
6. ___ sun rises in the east. (one word)
فقط یکی هست: the sun.

A2 · مقدماتی

a / an برای شغل، توصیف، قیمت و دفعات A2

a / an را برای شغل (She's a nurse)، توصیف (It's a beautiful day)، what a…! و به معنی «در هر» (twice a week, $5 a kilo) به کار ببرید.

a / an به کار می‌رود:

  • برای شغل و نقش: My father is an engineer. (نه My father is engineer)
  • در توصیف با be: It's a great idea. She's a very kind person.
  • در جمله‌های تعجبی با اسم مفردِ قابل‌شمارش: What a beautiful view!
  • به معنی per (در هر): three times a day, 60 km an hour, $3 a kilo.

با اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش یا جمع، بعد از what حرف تعریف نمی‌آید: What lovely weather! What nice people!

  • My sister is a teacher. شغل
  • That's a good question. توصیف
  • What a lovely garden! جملهٔ تعجبی
  • I go to the gym three times a week. در هر

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She is doctor. → ✅ She is a doctor. شغل: a / an
  • ❌ What a beautiful weather! → ✅ What beautiful weather! غیرقابل‌شمارش: what (بدون a)
  • ❌ I drink coffee two times in day. → ✅ I drink coffee twice a day. در هر: a

تمرین سطح A2

7. My brother is ___ architect. He designs schools.
شغل + صدای صدادار: an architect.
8. ___ amazing view!
اسم مفردِ قابل‌شمارش: What an amazing view!
9. I see my grandparents twice ___ month. (one word)
در هر: a month (یا per month).

B1 · متوسط

the cinema, the piano, the same, the best B1

the را با جاهای رایج شهر (the bank, the cinema)، سازها (play the guitar)، صفت عالی (the best)، و the same, the first, the only بیاورید.

کاربردمثال
جاهای رایجgo to the bank / the post office / the cinema / the doctor's
سازهای موسیقیplay the piano / the violin
صفت عالیthe best, the tallest, the most interesting
ترتیب و only / samethe first, the last, the next, the only, the same
محیط دور و بر ماthe sea, the countryside, the weather, the news

اینجا the به معنی یک ساختمانِ مشخص نیست: I'm going to the bank (هر شعبه‌ای).

ورزش‌ها و بازی‌ها حرف تعریف ندارند: play football, play chess (نه play the football).

  • I'm going to the dentist tomorrow. جاهای رایج
  • She plays the violin beautifully. ساز
  • It was the best day of my life. صفت عالی
  • We were in the same class at school. (the same)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He plays the football every weekend. → ✅ He plays football every weekend. ورزش: بدون حرف تعریف
  • ❌ She's best student in the class. → ✅ She's the best student in the class. صفت عالی: the
  • ❌ We go to same school. → ✅ We go to the same school. (the same)

تمرین سطح B1

10. My son is learning to play ___ guitar.
سازها: the guitar.
11. Everest is ___ highest mountain in the world.
صفت عالی: the highest.
12. Let's go to ___ cinema tonight. (one word)
جاهای رایج: the cinema.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

General statements: the tiger, a doctor, tigers; the rich B2

To talk about a type in general: plural with no article (Tigers are…), the + singular (formal: The tiger is…), or a + singular (A tiger can…). the + adjective = a group of people (the rich, the elderly).

PatternExampleNote
plural, no articleTigers are endangered.the most common
the + singularThe tiger is endangered.formal, scientific
a + singularA tiger can run very fast.any one tiger
the + adjectiveThe rich pay higher taxes.a group of people (plural verb)

The + singular is also used for inventions and musical instruments in general: The smartphone changed our lives.

The + adjective describes a group of people: the young, the old, the elderly, the unemployed, the homeless, the blind. It takes a plural verb and has no -s: The unemployed need help.

  • Elephants are very intelligent. (plural, general)
  • The computer has changed the world. (invention)
  • A doctor must be patient. (any doctor)
  • The elderly often feel lonely. (the + adjective)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The elephants are very intelligent animals. (in general) → ✅ Elephants are very intelligent animals. (general plural: no article)
  • ❌ The poors need more help. → ✅ The poor need more help. (the + adjective: no -s)

تمرین سطح B2

13. ___ are very loyal animals.
General plural: no article.
14. Who invented ___?
Inventions: the telephone.
15. The government should do more to help ___ homeless. (one word)
The + adjective = a group of people.

C1 · پیشرفته

Advanced article use: a coffee, a good knowledge, the French, a Mr Smith C1

Articles can change uncountable nouns into countable ones (a coffee, a knowledge of), refer to nationalities (the French), and show you don't know someone (a Mr Smith called).

Uncountable nouns with a / an: with an adjective or a specific type or amount: She has a good knowledge of history. I need a good night's sleep. Two coffees, please.

Nationalities: the + nationality adjective for the whole people: the French, the Chinese, the British. For a person: a Frenchman / a French woman.

Names: a + name = someone with that name you don't know: A Mr Ahmadi called while you were out. The + name for emphasis: Is that the Taylor Swift? (the famous one)

Illnesses: a cold, a headache, a sore throat (countable); flu or the flu (both correct); cancer, diabetes (no article).

  • He has a deep understanding of the problem. (uncountable + adjective)
  • The Japanese are famous for their politeness. (nationality)
  • A Ms Lopez is waiting for you in reception. (unknown person)
  • I think I've got a cold. (illness)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She has good knowledge of Spanish. → ✅ She has a good knowledge of Spanish. (a + adjective + knowledge)
  • ❌ Japanese are very polite. → ✅ The Japanese are very polite. (nationality: the)

تمرین سطح C1

16. She speaks with ___ excellent command of the language.
Uncountable noun with an adjective: an excellent command.
17. ___ are known for their love of cheese and wine.
Nationality: the French.
18. ___ Mr Karimi phoned this morning. Do you know him? (one word)
A + name = someone you don't know.
