A, An and The (Articles)

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A / an (the indefinite article) talk about one thing that isn't specific: I need a pen. The (the definite article) talks about something specific that the listener knows: Can you pass me the pen? (the one on the table)

Articles are difficult for many learners because some languages don't have them. This page goes step by step. For when to use no article, see The or No Article.

A1 · Beginner

A or an? A1

a + consonant soundan + vowel sound
a book, a cat, a dogan apple, an egg, an orange
a university (/juː/)an umbrella (/ʌ/)
a European (/jʊə/)an hour (h is silent)
a one-way ticket (/w/)an MBA (/em/)

  • I've got a brother and an older sister. (a / an)
  • She's waiting for an hour. (silent h)
  • He goes to a university in Toronto. (/juː/ sound)
  • Would you like an ice cream? (vowel sound)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I have a apple. → ✅ I have an apple. (vowel sound: an)
  • ❌ She's an university student. → ✅ She's a university student. (/juː/: a)
  • ❌ I need a informations. → ✅ I need some information. (uncountable: no a)

A or the? First mention and things we both know A1

  • we mentioned it before: A man called. The man said he was your uncle.
  • it's clear from the situation: Close the door, please. (the door of this room)
  • there's only one: the sun, the sky, the internet, the world.
  • I bought a jacket and a shirt. The jacket was cheap. (first mention, then the)
  • Can you turn off the light? (clear from the situation)
  • The moon is very bright tonight. (only one)
  • There's a good film on TV tonight. (first mention)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I saw a film yesterday. A film was great. → ✅ I saw a film yesterday. The film was great. (second mention: the)
  • ❌ Look at a sun! → ✅ Look at the sun! (only one: the)

Practice: A1

1. There's ___ egg in the fridge if you're hungry.
Vowel sound: an egg.
2. My cousin studies at ___ university in Montreal.
University starts with /juː/: a.
3. We waited for ___ hour. (one word)
Silent h: an hour.
4. We stayed in a hotel near the beach. ___ hotel had a great pool.
Second mention: the hotel.
5. Someone's knocking. Can you open ___ door, please?
Clear from the situation: the door (of this room).
6. ___ sun rises in the east. (one word)
Only one: the sun.

A2 · Elementary

A / an for jobs, descriptions, prices and frequency A2

  • for jobs and roles: My father is an engineer. (not My father is engineer)
  • in descriptions with be: It's a great idea. She's a very kind person.
  • in exclamations with a singular countable noun: What a beautiful view!
  • to mean per (each): three times a day, 60 km an hour, $3 a kilo.

  • My sister is a teacher. (job)
  • That's a good question. (description)
  • What a lovely garden! (exclamation)
  • I go to the gym three times a week. (per)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ She is doctor. → ✅ She is a doctor. (jobs: a / an)
  • ❌ What a beautiful weather! → ✅ What beautiful weather! (uncountable: what (no a))
  • ❌ I drink coffee two times in day. → ✅ I drink coffee twice a day. (per: a)

Practice: A2

7. My brother is ___ architect. He designs schools.
Job + vowel sound: an architect.
8. ___ amazing view!
Singular countable: What an amazing view!
9. I see my grandparents twice ___ month. (one word)
Per: a month (or per month).

B1 · Intermediate

The cinema, the piano, the same, the best B1

UseExamples
common placesgo to the bank / the post office / the cinema / the doctor's
musical instrumentsplay the piano / the violin
superlativesthe best, the tallest, the most interesting
order and only / samethe first, the last, the next, the only, the same
the environment around usthe sea, the countryside, the weather, the news

  • I'm going to the dentist tomorrow. (common places)
  • She plays the violin beautifully. (instrument)
  • It was the best day of my life. (superlative)
  • We were in the same class at school. (the same)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He plays the football every weekend. → ✅ He plays football every weekend. (sports: no article)
  • ❌ She's best student in the class. → ✅ She's the best student in the class. (superlative: the)
  • ❌ We go to same school. → ✅ We go to the same school. (the same)

Practice: B1

10. My son is learning to play ___ guitar.
Instruments: the guitar.
11. Everest is ___ highest mountain in the world.
Superlative: the highest.
12. Let's go to ___ cinema tonight. (one word)
Common places: the cinema.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

General statements: the tiger, a doctor, tigers; the rich B2

PatternExampleNote
plural, no articleTigers are endangered.the most common
the + singularThe tiger is endangered.formal, scientific
a + singularA tiger can run very fast.any one tiger
the + adjectiveThe rich pay higher taxes.a group of people (plural verb)

  • Elephants are very intelligent. (plural, general)
  • The computer has changed the world. (invention)
  • A doctor must be patient. (any doctor)
  • The elderly often feel lonely. (the + adjective)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The elephants are very intelligent animals. (in general) → ✅ Elephants are very intelligent animals. (general plural: no article)
  • ❌ The poors need more help. → ✅ The poor need more help. (the + adjective: no -s)

Practice: B2

13. ___ are very loyal animals.
General plural: no article.
14. Who invented ___?
Inventions: the telephone.
15. The government should do more to help ___ homeless. (one word)
The + adjective = a group of people.

C1 · Advanced

Advanced article use: a coffee, a good knowledge, the French, a Mr Smith C1

  • He has a deep understanding of the problem. (uncountable + adjective)
  • The Japanese are famous for their politeness. (nationality)
  • A Ms Lopez is waiting for you in reception. (unknown person)
  • I think I've got a cold. (illness)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ She has good knowledge of Spanish. → ✅ She has a good knowledge of Spanish. (a + adjective + knowledge)
  • ❌ Japanese are very polite. → ✅ The Japanese are very polite. (nationality: the)

Practice: C1

16. She speaks with ___ excellent command of the language.
Uncountable noun with an adjective: an excellent command.
17. ___ are known for their love of cheese and wine.
Nationality: the French.
18. ___ Mr Karimi phoned this morning. Do you know him? (one word)
A + name = someone you don't know.
