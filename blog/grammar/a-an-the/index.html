Articles: A, An and The

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

Use a or an with one thing when it isn't specific, or when you mention it for the first time: I have a dog. Use the when the listener knows which thing you mean: The dog is in the garden.

Use an before a vowel sound: an apple, an hour.

A or an?

It depends on the sound at the start of the next word, not the letter.

UseBeforeExamples
aa consonant sounda book, a car, a university (sounds like you), a one-way ticket (sounds like won)
ana vowel soundan apple, an egg, an idea, an hour (the h is silent), an umbrella

A and an only go with one countable thing: a chair, an orange. Not a water or a chairs.

When do we use the?

  • When we talk about something again: I bought a shirt and a jacket. The jacket was expensive.
  • When there is only one: the sun, the moon, the internet, the world
  • When it's clear which one we mean: Can you close the door? (the door of this room)
  • With superlatives: the best, the biggest
  • With some country names: the USA, the UK, the Netherlands

Before a vowel sound, the sounds like thee: the apple. Before a consonant sound, it sounds like thuh: the book.

No article

  • Plural and uncountable nouns when we talk in general: I like dogs. Water is important. Music makes me happy.
  • Most countries, cities and streets: Canada, Toronto, Oxford Street
  • Meals: We have breakfast at 8.
  • Some places when we go there for their usual purpose: go to school, go to work, go to bed, be at home
  • Languages and sports: I speak English. She plays tennis.

Jobs need a / an: She's a doctor. He's an engineer. (not She's doctor)

Examples

  1. I have a brother and a sister. (first mention)
  2. She's an English teacher. (a job; vowel sound)
  3. Can you open the window, please? (we know which window)
  4. The sun is very hot today. (there is only one)
  5. I don't like coffee. (uncountable, in general)
  6. We bought a car. The car is red. (first mention → second mention)
  7. It takes an hour to get there. (silent h)
  8. He goes to work by bus. (no article)
  9. Dogs are friendly animals. (plural, in general)
  10. This is the best pizza in town. (superlative)

Common mistakes

❌ She is doctor.

✅ She is a doctor. (Jobs need a or an.)

❌ I have a apple.

✅ I have an apple. (Use an before a vowel sound.)

❌ He goes to an university.

✅ He goes to a university. (University starts with a y sound (you), so use a.)

❌ The life is beautiful.

✅ Life is beautiful. (No article with uncountable nouns in general.)

❌ I go to the bed at 11.

✅ I go to bed at 11. (Go to bed = go to sleep: no article.)

❌ Sun is hot.

✅ The sun is hot. (There is only one sun, so use the.)

❌ I need an advice.

✅ I need some advice. (Advice is uncountable, so no a or an.)

Choose the correct answer.

1. Choose the correct sentence.
Jobs need a/an, and engineer starts with a vowel sound: an engineer.
2. It takes ___ hour to drive to the airport.
The h in hour is silent, so it starts with a vowel sound: an hour.
3. She studies at ___ in Spain.
University starts with a y sound (you), so use a. A/an only goes with one thing.
4. Look at ___ moon! It's so bright tonight.
There is only one moon, so use the.
5. I bought a shirt and a jacket. ___ jacket was expensive.
Second mention: we already know which jacket, so use The.
6. I'm tired. I'm going to ___ bed.
Go to bed (= go to sleep) has no article.
7. He goes to ___ work by bus.
Go to work has no article.
8. She speaks ___ English and French.
Languages have no article.
9. We visited ___ USA last summer.
Some country names take the: the USA, the UK.
10. Can you close ___ door, please? It's cold in here.
We both know which door (the door of this room), so use the.
11. I eat ___ every day.
Apple starts with a vowel sound: an apple. A/an only goes with one thing.
12. Choose the correct sentence.
Life in general is uncountable: no article.
13. This is ___ best day of my life!
Superlatives use the: the best.
14. My grandfather is ___.
The h in honest is silent: an honest man.
15. There's ___ cat in our garden. I don't know whose it is.
First mention, not a specific cat we know: a cat.
16. Choose the correct sentence.
Advice is uncountable: no a/an and no plural. Use some advice.
