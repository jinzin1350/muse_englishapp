خانه / گرامر / زمان گذشتهٔ ساده

زمان گذشتهٔ سادهPast Simple (I worked, I went)

گذشتهٔ ساده را برای کارها و موقعیت‌هایی به کار می‌بریم که در گذشته تمام شده‌اند: I watched a film last night. We went to Spain in 2022.

فعل‌های باقاعده ‑ed می‌گیرند (work → worked). خیلی از فعل‌های پرکاربرد بی‌قاعده‌اند (go → went). برای گذشتهٔ فعل be، صفحهٔ was و were را ببینید.

A1 · مبتدی

فعل‌های باقاعده: ‑ed A1

به فعل‌های باقاعده ‑ed اضافه کنید: work → worked, live → lived, study → studied, stop → stopped. شکل فعل برای همهٔ اشخاص یکی است.

آخر فعلقاعدهمثال
بیشتر فعل‌ها+ -edwork → worked, want → wanted, play → played
-e+ -dlive → lived, like → liked, dance → danced
حرف بی‌صدا + ‑yy → iedstudy → studied, try → tried, carry → carried
حرف صدادار + ‑y+ -edplay → played, enjoy → enjoyed
یک حرف صدادار + یک حرف بی‌صدا (هجای تکیه‌دار)حرف آخر دوتا + ‑edstop → stopped, plan → planned, drop → dropped

شکل فعل برای I, you, he, she, it, we, they یکی است: I worked, she worked, they worked.

تلفظ. بعد از صدای t یا d، پسوند ‑ed یک هجای اضافه است و «اِد» (/ɪd/) خوانده می‌شود: wanted, needed, started. بعد از صداهای دیگر فقط /t/ یا /d/ است: worked (/t/), played (/d/). نگویید work-ed.

  • I cleaned my room yesterday. (clean + ed)
  • She studied English at university. (y → ied)
  • The bus stopped outside the school. حرف p دوتا می‌شود
  • We lived in Toronto for three years. (live + d)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She studyed hard. → ✅ She studied hard. (consonant + y → ied)
  • ❌ The car stoped. → ✅ The car stopped. حرف p دوتا می‌شود: stop → stopped
  • ❌ He workeds in a bank. → ✅ He worked in a bank. در گذشته ‑s نمی‌گیرد.

فعل‌های بی‌قاعده A1

خیلی از فعل‌های پرکاربرد شکل گذشتهٔ مخصوص خودشان را دارند: go → went, have → had, see → saw, buy → bought. این‌ها را مثل لغت حفظ کنید.

فعل‌های بی‌قاعده ‑ed نمی‌گیرند و باید شکل گذشته‌شان را یاد بگیرید. چند تا از پرکاربردترین‌ها:

مثل فعل‌های باقاعده، شکل گذشته برای همهٔ اشخاص یکی است: I went, she went, they went.

بعضی فعل‌ها اصلاً تغییر نمی‌کنند: put → put, cut → cut, cost → cost, hit → hit, let → let. فعل read در گذشته همان‌طور نوشته می‌شود اما تلفظش فرق می‌کند: read (/riːd/) → read (/red/).

  • We went to the beach on Saturday. (go → went)
  • I had a sandwich for lunch. (have → had)
  • She bought a new laptop last week. (buy → bought)
  • The meal cost $40. (cost → cost)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We goed to the park. → ✅ We went to the park. (go → went)
  • ❌ I buyed a coat. → ✅ I bought a coat. (buy → bought)
  • ❌ She taked a photo. → ✅ She took a photo. (take → took)

didn't و did…? (جملهٔ منفی و سؤالی) A1

منفی: didn't + شکل سادهٔ فعل. سؤالی: Did + فاعل + شکل سادهٔ فعل؟ I didn't go. Did you go?

ساختارمثال
منفیفاعل + didn't + شکل سادهٔ فعلI didn't see him.
سؤالیDid + فاعل + شکل سادهٔ فعل؟Did you see him?
جواب کوتاهYes, … did. / No, … didn't.Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
سؤال با کلمهٔ پرسشیکلمهٔ پرسشی + did + فاعل + شکل سادهٔ فعلWhere did you go?

بعد از didn't و did شکل سادهٔ فعل می‌آید (بدون ‑ed و بدون شکل گذشتهٔ بی‌قاعده): She didn't go. نه She didn't went.

وقتی who یا what خودش فاعل است، did نمی‌آوریم: Who called you? (کسی به شما زنگ زد.) مقایسه کنید: Who did you call? (شما به کسی زنگ زدید.)

  • I didn't sleep well last night. didn't + شکل ساده
  • Did you enjoy the party? ~ Yes, I did. سؤال + جواب کوتاه
  • What did she say? سؤال با کلمهٔ پرسشی
  • We didn't have time for lunch. have همان have می‌ماند

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I didn't went to school. → ✅ I didn't go to school. بعد از didn't شکل سادهٔ فعل می‌آید.
  • ❌ Did you saw the news? → ✅ Did you see the news? بعد از did شکل سادهٔ فعل می‌آید.
  • ❌ Where you went? → ✅ Where did you go? سؤال با کلمهٔ پرسشی: where + did + فاعل + شکل سادهٔ فعل.

تمرین سطح A1

تکیه روی vi- است، پس t دوتا نمی‌شود: visit + ‑ed = visited.

حرف صدادار + y: فقط ‑ed اضافه کنید.

حرف بی‌صدا + y، پس ied: cry → cried.
4. Last night we ___ a great film.
See → saw.

Get → got.
6. My dad ___ (make) pancakes for breakfast last Sunday.
Make → made.

سؤال در گذشته با یک فعل اصلی (call): Did.

بعد از didn't شکل سادهٔ فعل می‌آید.

شکل درست: We didn't go out last weekend.

A2 · مقدماتی

کلمه‌های زمان: yesterday, last week, ago, in 2010 A2

گذشتهٔ ساده را با زمانِ تمام‌شده به کار ببرید: yesterday, last Monday, two years ago, in 2010, when I was young.

کلمهٔ زمانمثال
yesterdayyesterday, yesterday morning, the day before yesterday
lastlast night, last week, last Monday, last year (بدون the)
agoten minutes ago, three days ago, a long time ago (بعد از مقدار زمان)
in / on / atin 2010, in May, on Friday, at 8 o'clock
when …when I was a child, when we lived in Paris

ago بعد از مقدار زمان می‌آید: I met her two years ago. نه before two years و نه ago two years.

بگویید last night، نه yesterday night. بگویید last week، نه the last week (the last week یعنی هفت روزِ گذشته تا همین حالا).

برای پرسیدن دربارهٔ زمانِ تمام‌شده از When…? + گذشتهٔ ساده استفاده کنید: When did you arrive? نه When have you arrived? صفحهٔ حال کامل یا گذشتهٔ ساده را ببینید.

  • I saw her three days ago. ago بعد از مقدار زمان
  • We moved here in 2019. in + سال
  • Did you go out last night? نه yesterday night
  • When I was a child, I lived near the sea. when + گذشته

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I met him before two weeks. → ✅ I met him two weeks ago. ago بعد از مقدار زمان می‌آید
  • ❌ I called you yesterday night. → ✅ I called you last night. last night
  • ❌ She left the last Monday. → ✅ She left last Monday. بدون the

تعریف کردن ماجرا: اتفاق‌ها به ترتیب A2

برای چند اتفاقِ تمام‌شده که پشت سر هم افتاده‌اند گذشتهٔ ساده به کار ببرید: I got up, had a shower and left.

وقتی تعریف می‌کنیم چه اتفاقی افتاد، یعنی کارها یکی پس از دیگری، برای هر کار گذشتهٔ ساده می‌آوریم: She opened the door, looked around and walked in.

کلمه‌های مفید برای ترتیب: first, then, after that, next, later, finally, in the end.

گذشتهٔ ساده برای عادت‌های گذشته و دوره‌های تمام‌شده هم به کار می‌رود: I walked to school every day. She worked there for ten years (الان آنجا کار نمی‌کند).

وسط ماجرا بی‌دلیل زمان فعل را عوض نکنید: I went in and sat down. نه I went in and sit down.

  • We arrived at the hotel, left our bags and went to the beach. اتفاق‌ها به ترتیب
  • First I made a coffee, then I checked my emails. (first, then)
  • He lived in Japan for five years. دورهٔ تمام‌شده
  • When I was a student, I cycled to class every day. عادت گذشته

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I went home and cook dinner. → ✅ I went home and cooked dinner. گذشتهٔ ساده را نگه دارید.
  • ❌ Yesterday I get up at 6. → ✅ Yesterday I got up at 6. با yesterday گذشتهٔ ساده می‌آید.

B1 · متوسط

فعل‌های بی‌قاعده‌ای که راحت قاطی می‌شوند B1

شکل گذشتهٔ بعضی فعل‌ها شبیه هم است یا شبیه هم تلفظ می‌شود: fell / felt, thought / taught, bought / brought, left / lived, won / earned.

فعلگذشتهقاطی نکنید با
fallfellfeel → felt
thinkthoughtteach → taught
buyboughtbring → brought
leaveleftlive → lived
loselost(loose صفت است)
catchcaught(caught با thought هم‌قافیه است)
winwon(won با sun هم‌قافیه است)

این جفت‌ها در نوشتن و صحبت کردن خیلی اشتباه درست می‌کنند: I fell off my bike (از fall) با I felt ill (از feel) فرق دارد.

bought (از buy) و brought (از bring) تقریباً یک‌جور تلفظ می‌شوند. brought مثل bring یک r دارد: I brought a cake to the party (کیک را با خودم بردم). I bought a cake (پولش را دادم و خریدم).

  • She fell down the stairs. (fall → fell)
  • I felt tired after the trip. (feel → felt)
  • My mother taught me to cook. (teach → taught)
  • He brought his guitar to the party. (bring → brought)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I feeled sick. → ✅ I felt sick. (feel → felt)
  • ❌ She teached us maths. → ✅ She taught us maths. (teach → taught)
  • ❌ We losed the game. → ✅ We lost the game. (lose → lost)

تمرین سطح B1

گذشتهٔ fall می‌شود fell. (felt گذشتهٔ feel است.)
17. Mr Ahmadi ___ us English when I was at school.
Teach → taught.
18. Yesterday I ___ (bring) some sandwiches for the trip.
Bring → brought.

C1 · پیشرفته

Past forms that don't mean past C1

The past simple can make requests softer (I wondered if…) and describe unreal situations (It's time we left).

Sometimes the past simple refers to the present or future, not the past. There are two main uses:

  • Distance and politeness. The past makes a request or question less direct: I wondered if you could help me. Did you want to see me? I hoped you might have a minute.
  • Unreal situations. After if, wish, if only, it's time, would rather, suppose / imagine: If I had more time, I'd learn the piano. It's time we went home. I'd rather you didn't tell anyone.

In both uses the meaning is now or in the future. It's time we went means we should go now.

The past continuous is even softer for requests: I was wondering if you could help. See Past Continuous. For the full picture of unreal past forms, see Unreal Past.

  • I wondered if you were free on Friday. (polite: about now)
  • Did you want a coffee? (softer than Do you want…?)
  • It's high time we booked our tickets. (we should book now)
  • I'd rather you came a bit earlier. (preference about the future)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It's time we go home. → ✅ It's time we went home. (it's time + past simple)
  • ❌ I'd rather you don't smoke here. → ✅ I'd rather you didn't smoke here. (would rather + past simple)

تمرین سطح C1

19. It's late. It's time we ___ home.
It's time + subject + past simple; the meaning is now.
20. In which sentence does the past simple refer to the present?
A polite request about now: I wondered if you could…
21. I'd rather you ___ (not / tell) my parents about this.
Would rather + another subject + past simple (not tell is also used in American English).
