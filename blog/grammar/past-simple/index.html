Past Simple and Irregular Verbs

Level: A2 · Free grammar guide

We use the past simple for finished actions and situations in the past: I visited my grandmother yesterday. Regular verbs add -ed (work → worked). Irregular verbs have their own past form (go → went).

In negatives and questions, we use did + the base verb: I didn't go. Did you go?

When do we use the past simple?

  • A finished action at a finished time: She called me last night.
  • Actions one after another: He got up, had a shower and left for work.
  • A situation or habit that was true in the past but is finished now: We lived in Toronto for five years. (We don't live there now.)
  • Stories: A young girl found an old key in the garden.

Common time words: yesterday, last night, last week, last year, two days ago, in 2019, when I was a child.

How to form the past simple

FormExample
Positivesubject + past formI worked late. She went home.
Negativesubject + didn't (did not) + base verbI didn't work late. She didn't go home.
QuestionDid + subject + base verb?Did you work late? Did she go home?
Short answersYes, + subject + did. / No, + subject + didn't.Yes, I did. / No, she didn't.
Wh- questionquestion word + did + subject + base verb?Where did she go?

The past form is the same for every person: I, you, he, she, it, we, they worked. There is no -s in the past.

Be is different. Use was (I, he, she, it) and were (you, we, they), and don't use did: I wasn't tired. Were you at home?

Spelling: regular verbs with -ed

RuleExamples
Most verbs: add -edwork → worked, watch → watched, clean → cleaned
Verb ends in -e: add -dlive → lived, like → liked, dance → danced
Consonant + y: change y to -iedstudy → studied, try → tried, carry → carried
Vowel + y: add -edplay → played, enjoy → enjoyed, stay → stayed
One vowel + one consonant at the end of a stressed syllable: double the consonantstop → stopped, plan → planned, prefer → preferred

We don't double the consonant when the last syllable is not stressed: visit → visited, open → opened, listen → listened.

British and American spelling are different for some verbs ending in -l: travel → travelled (UK) / traveled (US), cancel → cancelled (UK) / canceled (US). Both are correct.

How to pronounce -ed

SoundWhenExamples
/t/after the sounds /p/, /k/, /s/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/ and /f/stopped, worked, kissed, washed, watched, laughed
/d/after vowel sounds and the sounds /b/, /g/, /v/, /z/, /l/, /m/, /n/played, lived, opened, called, robbed
/ɪd/after /t/ and /d/wanted, needed, visited, decided

-ed only adds an extra syllable after a t or d sound. Worked and played have one syllable; wan-ted has two.

Past simple or present perfect?

Use the past simple when you say or know when it happened: I lost my keys yesterday. Use the present perfect when the time isn't important and the result matters now: I've lost my keys. (I can't find them now.) More practice: Present Perfect vs Past Simple.

Examples

  1. I watched a film last night. (regular verb: + -ed)
  2. We went to the beach on Saturday. (irregular: go → went)
  3. She didn't call me yesterday. (negative: didn't + base verb)
  4. Did you finish your homework? (question: did + base verb)
  5. They moved to Canada in 2018. (finished time: in 2018)
  6. He was very tired after work. (be: was)
  7. Were the shops open on Sunday? (be question: no did)
  8. I bought a new phone two weeks ago. (irregular: buy → bought)
  9. When did the film start? (wh- question)
  10. My grandfather worked in a bank for 30 years. (finished situation: he doesn't work there now)

Common mistakes

❌ Did you went to the party?

✅ Did you go to the party? (After did, use the base verb.)

❌ I didn't saw him.

✅ I didn't see him. (After didn't, use the base verb.)

❌ She goed to school.

✅ She went to school. (Go is irregular: go → went.)

❌ I was go to the park yesterday.

✅ I went to the park yesterday. (Don't put was before a normal verb.)

❌ Did you be at home?

✅ Were you at home? (With be, use was/were, not did.)

❌ He studyed all night.

✅ He studied all night. (Consonant + y → -ied.)

❌ I have seen him yesterday.

✅ I saw him yesterday. (With a finished time like yesterday, use the past simple.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. We ___ dinner at a Thai restaurant last night.
Last night is a finished time, so use the past simple. Have is irregular: have → had.
2. She ___ her keys this morning, so she couldn't open the door.
The action is finished, so use the past simple. Lose is irregular: lose → lost.
3. I ___ the email because I was busy.
Negative: didn't + base verb. Don't add -ed after didn't.
4. ___ you see the match last Sunday?
Questions: Did + subject + base verb. Last Sunday is a finished time.
5. They ___ to Spain two years ago.
Two years ago is a finished time. Go is irregular: go → went. (Gone needs have: have gone.)
6. My parents ___ at home when I arrived.
Parents is plural, so use were.
7. He ___ to the party because he was ill.
Negative: didn't + base verb (come, not came).
8. Where ___ you go on holiday last summer?
Wh- questions with a normal verb: question word + did + subject + base verb.
9. The children ___ in the garden all afternoon.
Vowel + y: just add -ed: play → played.
10. I ___ for my exam all weekend.
Consonant + y: change y to -ied: study → studied.
11. The bus ___ suddenly, and everyone fell forward.
Stop ends in one vowel + one consonant, so double the p: stopped.
12. Choose the correct sentence.
After didn't, use the base verb: didn't know.
13. How much ___ your new laptop cost when you bought it?
When you bought it is in the past, so use did + base verb. Cost is irregular: cost → cost → cost.
14. ___ it cold in Montreal last week?
With be, don't use did. It takes was.
15. Last year, I ___ my first marathon.
Last year is a finished time, so use the past simple: run → ran.
16. Which verb has an extra syllable when you say the -ed ending?
Want ends in a t sound, so -ed sounds like /ɪd/ and adds a syllable: want-ed. The other three have one syllable.
