Past Simple (I worked, I went)

A1 · A2 · B1 · C1

We use the past simple for actions and situations that finished in the past: I watched a film last night. We went to Spain in 2022.

Regular verbs add -ed (work → worked). Many common verbs are irregular (go → went). For the past of be, see Was and Were.

A1 · Beginner

Regular verbs: -ed A1

Verb ends inRuleExamples
most verbs+ -edwork → worked, want → wanted, play → played
-e+ -dlive → lived, like → liked, dance → danced
consonant + -yy → iedstudy → studied, try → tried, carry → carried
vowel + -y+ -edplay → played, enjoy → enjoyed
one vowel + one consonant (stressed)double + -edstop → stopped, plan → planned, drop → dropped

  • I cleaned my room yesterday. (clean + ed)
  • She studied English at university. (y → ied)
  • The bus stopped outside the school. (double p)
  • We lived in Toronto for three years. (live + d)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ She studyed hard. → ✅ She studied hard. (consonant + y → ied)
  • ❌ The car stoped. → ✅ The car stopped. (stop → stopped (double p))
  • ❌ He workeds in a bank. → ✅ He worked in a bank. (No -s in the past.)

Didn't and did… ? (negatives and questions) A1

FormExample
Negativesubject + didn't + base verbI didn't see him.
QuestionDid + subject + base verb?Did you see him?
Short answersYes, … did. / No, … didn't.Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Wh-questionquestion word + did + subject + base verbWhere did you go?

  • I didn't sleep well last night. (didn't + base)
  • Did you enjoy the party? ~ Yes, I did. (question + short answer)
  • What did she say? (wh-question)
  • We didn't have time for lunch. (have stays have)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I didn't went to school. → ✅ I didn't go to school. (After didn't: base verb.)
  • ❌ Did you saw the news? → ✅ Did you see the news? (After did: base verb.)
  • ❌ Where you went? → ✅ Where did you go? (Wh-question: where + did + subject + base verb.)

Practice: A1

1. Yesterday I ___ my grandmother.
Visit + -ed = visited (stress is on vi-, so no double t).
2. We ___ (play) tennis last Sunday.
Vowel + y: just add -ed.
3. The baby ___ (cry) three times last night.
Consonant + y → ied.
4. Last night we ___ a great film.
See → saw.
5. I ___ (get) up at 7 o'clock this morning.
Get → got.
6. My dad ___ (make) pancakes for breakfast last Sunday.
Make → made.
7. ___ you call your mum yesterday?
Past question with a verb (call): Did.
8. She didn't ___ the email.
After didn't: base verb.
9. Find the mistake: tap the wrong part.
We didn't went out last weekend.
Correct: We didn't go out last weekend.
After didn't: base verb go.

Time words: yesterday, last week, ago, in 2010 A2

Time wordExamples

  • I saw her three days ago. (ago after the period)
  • We moved here in 2019. (in + year)
  • Did you go out last night? (not yesterday night)
  • When I was a child, I lived near the sea. (when + past)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I met him before two weeks. → ✅ I met him two weeks ago. (ago after the period)
  • ❌ I called you yesterday night. → ✅ I called you last night. (last night)
  • ❌ She left the last Monday. → ✅ She left last Monday. (no the)

Telling a story: events in order A2

  • We arrived at the hotel, left our bags and went to the beach. (events in order)
  • First I made a coffee, then I checked my emails. (first, then)
  • He lived in Japan for five years. (finished period)
  • When I was a student, I cycled to class every day. (past habit)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I went home and cook dinner. → ✅ I went home and cooked dinner. (Keep the past simple.)
  • ❌ Yesterday I get up at 6. → ✅ Yesterday I got up at 6. (Yesterday → past simple.)

Practice: A2

10. I started this job three months ___.
Ago comes after the period.
11. We went to a concert ___.
Say last night.
12. Find the mistake: tap the wrong part.
I have bought this jacket two weeks ago.
Correct: I bought this jacket two weeks ago.
Ago = finished time: past simple bought.
13. I got up, ___ breakfast and went to work.
Events in order: past simple.
14. My grandfather ___ in a factory for forty years. He retired in 2010.
The period is finished (he retired): past simple.
15. She closed the book and ___ (turn) off the light.
Next event in the story: past simple.

C1 · Advanced

Past forms that don't mean past C1

  • Distance and politeness. The past makes a request or question less direct: I wondered if you could help me. Did you want to see me? I hoped you might have a minute.
  • Unreal situations. After if, wish, if only, it's time, would rather, suppose / imagine: If I had more time, I'd learn the piano. It's time we went home. I'd rather you didn't tell anyone.

  • I wondered if you were free on Friday. (polite: about now)
  • Did you want a coffee? (softer than Do you want…?)
  • It's high time we booked our tickets. (we should book now)
  • I'd rather you came a bit earlier. (preference about the future)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ It's time we go home. → ✅ It's time we went home. (it's time + past simple)
  • ❌ I'd rather you don't smoke here. → ✅ I'd rather you didn't smoke here. (would rather + past simple)

Practice: C1

19. It's late. It's time we ___ home.
It's time + subject + past simple; the meaning is now.
20. In which sentence does the past simple refer to the present?
A polite request about now: I wondered if you could…
21. I'd rather you ___ (not / tell) my parents about this.
Would rather + another subject + past simple (not tell is also used in American English).
