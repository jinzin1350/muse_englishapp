unless، as long as، provided، in case و even ifUnless, As Long As, Provided, In Case and Even If

if تنها کلمه برای شرط نیست. انگلیسی از unless, as long as, provided (that), in case و even if برای معنی‌های مختلف استفاده می‌کند: یک استثنا، یک شرط سفت‌وسخت، یک احتیاط، یا «فرقی نمی‌کند».

بعد از همهٔ این‌ها، مثل بعد از if، برای آینده زمان حال می‌آوریم: I'll come unless it rains. برای الگوهای پایه صفحهٔ شرطی نوع صفر و اول را ببینید.

B1 · متوسط

unless یعنی if … not (مگر اینکه) B1

unless یعنی if … not (مگر اینکه). بعد از آن فعل مثبت بیاورید: I'll go unless it rains (یعنی اگر باران نیاید).

unless یعنی «مگر اینکه» یا «اگر … نه»: You can't come in unless you have a ticket. = You can't come in if you don't have a ticket.

فعلِ بعد از unless از نظر شکل مثبت است، اما معنی منفی دارد. not اضافه نکنید: unless it rains (نه unless it doesn't rain).

مثل if، بعد از unless برای آینده زمان حال می‌آید: Unless you hurry, you'll miss the bus.

  • I'll be there at eight unless the train is late. یعنی اگر قطار دیر نکند
  • Unless you book now, there won't be any seats. یعنی اگر رزرو نکنید
  • Don't call me unless it's an emergency. فقط اگر اورژانسی باشد
  • He won't help you unless you ask him. یعنی اگر از او نخواهید

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'll come unless it doesn't rain. → ✅ I'll come unless it rains. unless + فعل مثبت.
  • ❌ Unless you will study, you'll fail. → ✅ Unless you study, you'll fail. بعد از unless حرف will نمی‌آید.

as long as, provided (that), providing B1

as long as / provided (that) / providing یعنی فقط به شرطی که: You can borrow my car as long as you're careful.

این عبارت‌ها یعنی فقط اگر یا به شرطی که. یک شرط سفت‌وسخت می‌گذارند: You can go out as long as you're back by ten. یعنی می‌توانی بیرون بروی، اما فقط به شرطی که تا ساعت ده برگردی.

provided (that) و providing (that) همان معنی را دارند، اما رسمی‌ترند. on condition that خیلی رسمی است: The loan was approved on condition that it was repaid within a year.

برای آینده زمان حال بیاورید: As long as you work hard, you'll pass. نه as long as you will work.

  • You can stay as long as you don't make a mess. فقط اگر
  • Provided that the weather is good, the match will go ahead. رسمی
  • I'll lend you the money providing you pay me back next week. فقط اگر
  • You can use the photos on condition that you credit the photographer. خیلی رسمی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You can come as long as you will be quiet. → ✅ You can come as long as you're quiet. بعد از as long as حال می‌آید.
  • ❌ Provided you don't hurry, you'll miss the train. → ✅ Unless you hurry, you'll miss the train. provided یعنی «فقط اگر»، نه «اگر نه»؛ اینجا unless لازم است.

in case: برای احتیاط B1

in case یعنی چون ممکن است چیزی اتفاق بیفتد (محض احتیاط): Take an umbrella in case it rains. با if فرق دارد.

کاری را in case چیزی انجام می‌دهیم یعنی محض احتیاط، چون ممکن است اتفاق بیفتد: I'll take an umbrella in case it rains. (چتر را همین حالا برمی‌دارم، چون شاید باران بیاید.)

مقایسه کنید: I'll take an umbrella if it rains یعنی فقط اگر موقع بیرون رفتن باران بیاید چتر برمی‌دارم.

بعد از in case برای آینده زمان حال بیاورید: in case it rains (نه in case it will rain). برای گذشته گذشتهٔ ساده بیاورید: I took my coat in case it got cold.

in case of + اسم روی تابلوها و در انگلیسی رسمی به کار می‌رود: In case of fire, use the stairs. (یعنی اگر آتش‌سوزی شد)

  • Write down the address in case you forget it. احتیاط
  • I'll give you my number in case you need help. چون ممکن است لازم شود
  • We left early in case there was traffic. گذشته
  • In case of emergency, break the glass. رسمی: اگر اورژانسی پیش آمد

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Take a jacket in case it will be cold. → ✅ Take a jacket in case it's cold. بعد از in case حرف will نمی‌آید.
  • ❌ I'll call you in case I'm late. → ✅ I'll call you if I'm late. فقط وقتی دیرم شد زنگ می‌زنم: if.

تمرین سطح B1

Unless یعنی if … not: اگر ورزش نکنی، روی فرم نمی‌آیی.

Unless یعنی if … not.

Unless یعنی اگر الان راه نیفتیم.

فقط اگر تمامش کرده باشی: as long as.

On condition that از همه رسمی‌تر است.

بعد از as long as حال ساده می‌آید.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Even if, even though and whether or not B2

even if = it doesn't matter if: I'll go even if it rains. even though = although (a fact). whether or not = in both cases.

Even if says that the result will be the same whatever happens: I'll finish this report even if I have to work all night. (Maybe I'll have to, maybe not. It doesn't matter.)

Even though is different: it introduces a fact, like although: Even though it was raining, we went for a walk. (It really was raining.) See Although, Despite and In Spite Of.

Whether or not (or whether … or not) = in both cases: I'm going, whether or not you come. / …whether you come or not.

  • Even if you apologise, she won't forgive you. (it makes no difference)
  • Even though he's rich, he lives in a tiny flat. (a fact: he is rich)
  • We'll play the match whether or not it rains. (rain or no rain)
  • I wouldn't tell you even if I knew. (imaginary: even if + past)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Even it rains, we'll go. → ✅ Even if it rains, we'll go. (even if, not even alone)
  • ❌ Even if I was tired, I finished the work. (I really was tired) → ✅ Even though I was tired, I finished the work. (A fact: even though.)

تمرین سطح B2

10. I'm going to the concert ___ I have to go alone.
It doesn't matter if I have to go alone: even if.
11. ___ she had a cold, she went to work. (She really had a cold.)
A fact: even though.
12. We're leaving at nine, whether you like it ___ not.
Whether … or not.

C1 · پیشرفته

When unless doesn't work, and only if C1

Unless = except if. Don't use it for feelings or results of not happening (I'd be upset if you didn't come). Only if makes the condition the one and only condition.

Unless means except if. It doesn't work when the sentence is about the effect of something not happening, especially feelings: I'll be very upset if you don't come. Not unless you come. She'd be surprised if he didn't call. Not unless he called.

Unless is also unusual in questions: What will you do if she doesn't agree? Not unless she agrees.

Unless is often added as an afterthought: Let's take the bus, unless you'd rather walk.

Only if = in this one case and no other: I'll lend you money only if you promise to pay it back. In formal English it can start the sentence with inversion: Only if you promise will I lend you the money.

  • I'll be really disappointed if you don't pass. (feeling: if … not)
  • What happens if the payment doesn't go through? (question: if … not)
  • We could eat out, unless you're too tired. (afterthought)
  • You'll get a refund only if you keep the receipt. (the one condition)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'll be sad unless you come to my party. → ✅ I'll be sad if you don't come to my party. (feelings: if … not)
  • ❌ What will you do unless you get the visa? → ✅ What will you do if you don't get the visa? (questions: if … not)

تمرین سطح C1

13. I'd be amazed ___ the exam. He's studied so hard.
A feeling about something not happening: if … not.
14. Which sentence is NOT natural?
For feelings, use if … not, not unless.
15. The machine will start ___ if both buttons are pressed together. (one word)
Only if = in this one case.
