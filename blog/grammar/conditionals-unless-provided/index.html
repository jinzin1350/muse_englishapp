Unless, Provided, As Long As

Level: B2 · Free grammar guide

Besides if, English has other words to introduce conditions. Unless means if … not: I won't go unless you come too.

Provided (that) and as long as mean only if: You can borrow my car as long as you drive carefully.

Unless

Unless = if not. The verb after unless is positive: We'll be late unless we leave now. = We'll be late if we don't leave now.

Don't add a negative after unless when you mean if not: unless you hurry (not unless you don't hurry).

Provided, providing, as long as, on condition that

These all mean only if (a strong condition): I'll lend you the money provided (that) you pay me back next week. You can stay out late as long as you text me. They agreed on condition that we kept it secret. Providing (that) is also possible.

Like if, these words use the present simple for the future: as long as you are home by ten (not will be).

In case

In case = because something might happen (a precaution). It isn't the same as if: Take an umbrella in case it rains. (Take it now, because it might rain.) Take an umbrella if it rains. (Take it only when it's raining.)

Even if, suppose and what if

  • Even if = it doesn't matter whether: I'll go even if it rains.
  • Suppose / supposing and what if introduce imaginary situations: Suppose you won the lottery, what would you do? What if we miss the train?

Examples

  1. I won't come unless you invite me. (unless = if not)
  2. Unless you hurry, you'll miss the bus. (unless at the start)
  3. You can use my laptop as long as you're careful with it. (only if)
  4. We'll go camping provided the weather is good. (only if)
  5. She agreed to sing on condition that nobody filmed her. (formal condition)
  6. Take some cash in case the shop doesn't accept cards. (precaution)
  7. Supposing you lost your job, what would you do? (imaginary situation)
  8. I'll finish this tonight even if I have to stay up late. (it doesn't matter)
  9. What if nobody comes to the party? (worry about a possibility)
  10. You won't get better unless you rest. (unless = if not)

Common mistakes

❌ I'll go to the party unless you don't come.

✅ I'll go to the party unless you come. (Unless already means if not. (Or: I'll go if you don't come.))

❌ You can come as long as you will be quiet.

✅ You can come as long as you're quiet. (Present simple after as long as.)

❌ Take a coat if it gets cold later.

✅ Take a coat in case it gets cold later. (For a precaution (take it now), use in case.)

❌ Unless it will rain, we'll play tennis.

✅ Unless it rains, we'll play tennis. (Present simple after unless.)

❌ Provided you will pay, you can have it.

✅ Provided you pay, you can have it. (Present simple after provided.)

❌ I'll lend it to you on condition you will return it.

✅ I'll lend it to you on condition that you return it. (On condition that + present simple.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. I don't want to go alone. I won't go to the party ___ you come with me.
I won't go if you don't come = unless you come.
2. Take an umbrella ___ it rains later.
A precaution because it might rain: in case.
3. You can borrow my bike ___ you bring it back by six.
Only if you bring it back: as long as.
4. ___ you hurry, you'll miss the train.
If you don't hurry = Unless you hurry.
5. We'll have the barbecue outside provided the weather ___ good.
Present simple after provided: is.
6. She agreed to help ___ that we paid for her travel.
On condition that = only if.
7. Choose the sentence that means 'If you don't apologise, I won't forgive you.'
If you don't apologise = unless you apologise.
8. Unless you ___, you won't pass.
After unless, a positive present verb: study.
9. I'm going to the beach ___ it rains. I don't care!
It doesn't matter whether it rains: even if.
10. ___ you won a million dollars, what would you do?
An imaginary situation: Suppose.
11. I'll write down the address ___ I forget it.
A precaution because I might forget: in case.
12. You can watch TV as long as you ___ your homework first.
Present simple after as long as: finish.
13. You won't get a table ___ you book in advance.
If you don't book = unless you book.
14. Providing you ___ the rules, you can join the club.
Present simple after providing: follow.
15. ___ we miss the last bus? How will we get home?
Asking about a worrying possibility: What if…?
16. Which sentence is correct?
Present simple after unless, and no extra negative.
