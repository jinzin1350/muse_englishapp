Future Continuous and Future Perfect (will be doing, will have done)

B1 · B2 · C1

The future continuous (will be doing) describes an action in progress at a time in the future: This time tomorrow I'll be lying on a beach.

The future perfect (will have done) describes an action that will be complete before a time in the future: By June I'll have finished my course. For assumptions like He'll be sleeping now, see Modals of Deduction.

B1 · Intermediate

Future continuous: will be doing B1

FormExample
PositiveI'll be working at 10 tomorrow.
NegativeShe won't be using the car tonight.
QuestionWill you be waiting for me?

  • This time next week we'll be skiing in the Alps. (in progress then)
  • Don't phone between 2 and 4. I'll be sleeping. (in progress)
  • When you arrive, I'll be waiting at the gate. (in progress when you arrive)
  • Will you be working late tonight? (question)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ At 10 tomorrow I will working. → ✅ At 10 tomorrow I will be working. (will + be + -ing)
  • ❌ This time tomorrow I'll be fly to Rome. → ✅ This time tomorrow I'll be flying to Rome. (be + -ing)

Practice: B1

1. This time tomorrow I ___ on the beach.
In progress at a future time: will be lying.
2. Don't come at 7. We ___ dinner then.
In progress: will be having.
3. At 3 pm tomorrow I ___ (sit) in an exam.
In progress at a future time: will be sitting.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Will you be using…? Polite questions and things that will happen anyway B2

  • Will you be going to the shops later? I need some milk. (polite question)
  • Will you be staying for dinner? (polite question about plans)
  • I'll be seeing Mina at work tomorrow, so I'll give her the book. (happens anyway)
  • The train will be stopping at all stations. (announcement)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Will you use the printer this afternoon? (just asking about plans politely) → ✅ Will you be using the printer this afternoon? (polite question: will you be + -ing)

Future perfect: will have done by… B2

FormExample
PositiveI'll have finished by six.
NegativeThey won't have arrived by then.
QuestionWill you have finished by Friday?

  • By next summer, I'll have saved enough for a car. (complete before then)
  • The film will have started by the time we arrive. (complete before we arrive)
  • Will you have finished the report by Monday? (question)
  • Don't worry, they won't have forgotten your birthday. (negative)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ By 2030 I will finish university. → ✅ By 2030 I will have finished university. (complete before a future time: will have + past participle)
  • ❌ By the time you will come, I'll have left. → ✅ By the time you come, I'll have left. (present after by the time)

Practice: B2

4. ___ the meeting room this afternoon? If not, we'd like to use it.
Polite question about plans: Will you be using…?
5. What does I'll be driving past your house, so I can pick you up suggest?
The future continuous = it will happen anyway.
6. ___ (you / stay) at the hotel again next week?
Polite question about plans: Will you be staying…?
7. By the end of the year, I ___ ten books.
Complete before a future time: will have read.
8. Hurry up! By the time we get there, the concert ___.
By the time + future perfect.
9. By Friday, the builders ___ (finish) the kitchen.
Complete by Friday: will have finished.

C1 · Advanced

Future perfect continuous: will have been doing C1

  • Next month I'll have been learning English for a year. (duration up to then)
  • By the time we get there, we'll have been driving for ten hours. (duration)
  • In September, she'll have been teaching at this school for 20 years. (duration)
  • By 2030, I'll have known him for half my life. (state verb: simple)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Next year I will be working here for five years. (meaning the length of time up to then) → ✅ Next year I will have been working here for five years. (duration up to a future time: will have been + -ing)
  • ❌ By June we'll have been knowing each other for a year. → ✅ By June we'll have known each other for a year. (state verb: simple)

Practice: C1

10. By 10 pm, I ___ for twelve hours without a break.
Duration up to a future time: will have been working.
11. In July, they ___ married for 40 years.
Be is a state: will have been, not continuous.
12. By the time the plane lands, we ___ (travel) for 18 hours.
Duration: will have been travelling.
