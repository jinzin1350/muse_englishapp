آیندهٔ استمراری و آیندهٔ کاملFuture Continuous and Future Perfect (will be doing, will have done)

آیندهٔ استمراری (will be doing) کاری را توصیف می‌کند که در زمانی در آینده در جریان است: This time tomorrow I'll be lying on a beach. (فردا همین موقع دارم کنار ساحل دراز می‌کشم.)

آیندهٔ کامل (will have done) کاری را توصیف می‌کند که قبل از زمانی در آینده تمام شده است: By June I'll have finished my course. برای حدس‌هایی مثل He'll be sleeping now صفحهٔ فعل‌های وجهیِ استنتاج را ببینید.

B1 · B2 · C1

B1 · متوسط

آیندهٔ استمراری: will be doing B1

will be + ‑ing برای کاری که در زمان مشخصی در آینده در جریان است: At 8 tonight I'll be watching the match.

شکلمثال
مثبتI'll be working at 10 tomorrow.
منفیShe won't be using the car tonight.
سؤالیWill you be waiting for me?

آیندهٔ استمراری شما را وسط یک کار در آینده قرار می‌دهد: Don't call at 9. I'll be having dinner. (قبل از ۹ شروع می‌کنم و بعد از ۹ تمام می‌کنم.)

عبارت‌های زمانیِ معمول: this time tomorrow / next week, at 6 o'clock tomorrow, all day tomorrow, when you arrive.

با گذشتهٔ استمراری مقایسه کنید که همین کار را برای گذشته می‌کند: At 8 last night I was watching TV. صفحهٔ گذشتهٔ استمراری را ببینید.

  • This time next week we'll be skiing in the Alps. آن موقع در جریان است
  • Don't phone between 2 and 4. I'll be sleeping. در جریان
  • When you arrive, I'll be waiting at the gate. وقتی برسی در جریان است
  • Will you be working late tonight? سؤالی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ At 10 tomorrow I will working. → ✅ At 10 tomorrow I will be working. will + be + ‑ing.
  • ❌ This time tomorrow I'll be fly to Rome. → ✅ This time tomorrow I'll be flying to Rome. be + ‑ing.

تمرین سطح B1

1. This time tomorrow I ___ on the beach.
در جریان در زمانی در آینده: will be lying.
2. Don't come at 7. We ___ dinner then.
در جریان: will be having.
3. At 3 pm tomorrow I ___ (sit) in an exam.
در جریان در زمانی در آینده: will be sitting.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Will you be using…? Polite questions and things that will happen anyway B2

Will you be + -ing? asks politely about someone's plans: Will you be using the car tonight? It also describes things that will happen as a matter of course: I'll be seeing Ali tomorrow.

Will you be + -ing? is a polite way to ask about someone's plans, often before asking for something: Will you be using your laptop this afternoon? If not, could I borrow it?

It sounds less direct than Will you use…?, which can sound like a request or an order.

The future continuous also describes things that will happen anyway, as part of a routine or arrangement: I'll be passing the post office, so I can post your letter. (I'm going that way anyway.)

  • Will you be going to the shops later? I need some milk. (polite question)
  • Will you be staying for dinner? (polite question about plans)
  • I'll be seeing Mina at work tomorrow, so I'll give her the book. (happens anyway)
  • The train will be stopping at all stations. (announcement)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Will you use the printer this afternoon? (just asking about plans politely) → ✅ Will you be using the printer this afternoon? (polite question: will you be + -ing)

Future perfect: will have done by… B2

will have + past participle for something that will be complete before a time in the future: By 2030 I'll have paid off my loan.

FormExample
PositiveI'll have finished by six.
NegativeThey won't have arrived by then.
QuestionWill you have finished by Friday?

The future perfect looks back from a point in the future: By the end of the month, I'll have worked here for two years.

It often goes with by (+ time) or by the time (+ present): By the time you get home, I'll have cooked dinner.

By means not later than. By Friday = on or before Friday. It's different from until: I'll be here until Friday (all the time up to Friday).

  • By next summer, I'll have saved enough for a car. (complete before then)
  • The film will have started by the time we arrive. (complete before we arrive)
  • Will you have finished the report by Monday? (question)
  • Don't worry, they won't have forgotten your birthday. (negative)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ By 2030 I will finish university. → ✅ By 2030 I will have finished university. (complete before a future time: will have + past participle)
  • ❌ By the time you will come, I'll have left. → ✅ By the time you come, I'll have left. (present after by the time)

تمرین سطح B2

4. ___ the meeting room this afternoon? If not, we'd like to use it.
Polite question about plans: Will you be using…?

The future continuous = it will happen anyway.
6. ___ (you / stay) at the hotel again next week?
Polite question about plans: Will you be staying…?

Complete before a future time: will have read.

C1 · پیشرفته

Future perfect continuous: will have been doing C1

will have been + -ing for the duration of an activity up to a point in the future: By June, I'll have been working here for ten years.

The future perfect continuous measures how long an activity will have continued by a future time: In March, we'll have been living in Toronto for five years.

It's often used with for + a period and by / in + a future time.

Like other continuous forms, it's not used with state verbs: By May, I'll have known her for ten years. (not will have been knowing)

Compare: By 6 pm I'll have written 3 chapters (how much: future perfect simple) / By 6 pm I'll have been writing for 8 hours (how long: continuous).

  • Next month I'll have been learning English for a year. (duration up to then)
  • By the time we get there, we'll have been driving for ten hours. (duration)
  • In September, she'll have been teaching at this school for 20 years. (duration)
  • By 2030, I'll have known him for half my life. (state verb: simple)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Next year I will be working here for five years. (meaning the length of time up to then) → ✅ Next year I will have been working here for five years. (duration up to a future time: will have been + -ing)
  • ❌ By June we'll have been knowing each other for a year. → ✅ By June we'll have known each other for a year. (state verb: simple)

تمرین سطح C1

10. By 10 pm, I ___ for twelve hours without a break.
Duration up to a future time: will have been working.
11. In July, they ___ married for 40 years.
Be is a state: will have been, not continuous.
12. By the time the plane lands, we ___ (travel) for 18 hours.
Duration: will have been travelling.

همچنین ببینید:

  • That'll be the postman: will and should for assumptions
