ترتیب کلمه‌ها در جملهWord Order in English

ترتیب کلمه‌ها در انگلیسی نسبتاً ثابت است: فاعل + فعل + مفعول: Sara loves coffee. (نه Sara coffee loves). در فارسی فعل آخر می‌آید («سارا قهوه دوست دارد»)، اما در انگلیسی فعل بعد از فاعل می‌آید. اطلاعات مکان و زمان معمولاً آخر جمله و به همین ترتیب می‌آیند.

این صفحه ترتیب پایه، جای قیدها و نحوهٔ آوردن دو مفعول را نشان می‌دهد.

A1 · A2 · B1 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

فاعل + فعل + مفعول A1

ترتیب عادی فاعل + فعل + مفعول است (I like tea). بین فعل و مفعولش چیزی نگذارید: I like tea very much (نه I like very much tea).

فاعلفعلمفعولبقیه
Iliketeavery much.
My brotherplaysfootballon Saturdays.
ShespeaksEnglishvery well.

در انگلیسی، در جملهٔ خبری فاعل قبل از فعل می‌آید، حتی برای هوا: It is raining. (فارسی می‌گوید «باران می‌آید» بدون فاعل جدا، اما انگلیسی It لازم دارد.)

فعل و مفعولش را کنار هم نگه دارید: She speaks English well. (نه She speaks well English)

  • We watched a film last night. فاعل + فعل + مفعول
  • I like this song a lot. فعل و مفعول کنار هم
  • My parents speak Persian at home. فاعل + فعل + مفعول
  • He drinks coffee every morning. فاعل + فعل + مفعول

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I like very much chocolate. → ✅ I like chocolate very much. فعل و مفعول کنار هم.
  • ❌ She speaks very well English. → ✅ She speaks English very well. فعل و مفعول کنار هم.
  • ❌ Coffee I drink every day. → ✅ I drink coffee every day. فاعل اول می‌آید.

تمرین سطح A1

1. کدام جمله درست است؟
فعل و مفعول کنار هم: love pizza.
2. کدام جمله درست است؟
فعل و مفعول کنار هم: speaks Spanish.
3. My sister ___ (tennis / plays) every weekend. (two words)
فعل + مفعول: plays tennis.

A2 · مقدماتی

مکان قبل از زمان: at the station yesterday A2

آخر جمله، مکان را قبل از زمان بیاورید: I met her at the station yesterday. اگر حالت (چطور) هم باشد، ترتیب معمول حالت ← مکان ← زمان است: She worked hard at the office all day.

فاعل + فعل (+ مفعول) + حالتمکان (کجا)زمان (کی)
I met Aliat the stationyesterday.
We're goingto Spainnext week.
She worked hardat the officeall day.

عبارت‌های زمان می‌توانند اول جمله هم بیایند: Yesterday I met Ali at the station.

کلمه‌هایی مثل today و tomorrow آخر یا اول جمله می‌آیند، نه وسط: I'm working today. (نه I'm today working)

  • I bought this jacket in Toronto last year. مکان، زمان
  • She goes to the gym every morning. مکان، زمان
  • Last night we ate at a Persian restaurant. زمان در اول جمله
  • He drove carefully through the city. حالت، مکان

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I was yesterday at home. → ✅ I was at home yesterday. آخر جمله مکان قبل از زمان می‌آید.
  • ❌ We arrived last night in Paris. → ✅ We arrived in Paris last night. مکان قبل از زمان.
  • ❌ I'm tomorrow working. → ✅ I'm working tomorrow. زمان در آخر جمله.

تمرین سطح A2

4. کدام جمله درست است؟
فعل + مفعول، بعد مکان، بعد زمان.
5. کدام جمله درست است؟
فعل + مفعول، بعد مکان، بعد زمان.
6. We met ___ (last year / in Rome). (four words)
مکان قبل از زمان.

B1 · متوسط

قیدها کجا می‌آیند؟ also, probably, already, only B1

خیلی از قیدهای کوتاه وسط جمله می‌آیند: قبل از فعل اصلی، اما بعد از be و اولین فعل کمکی: I also like tea. She's probably at home. We've already eaten.

قیدجامثال
also, just, already, still, probably, definitely, even, only, almostقبل از فعل اصلیI also speak French.
بعد از beShe's probably tired.
بعد از اولین فعل کمکیThey've just left. I can definitely help.
probably, definitely + منفیقبل از won't / don'tHe probably won't come. (not won't probably)

در جملهٔ منفی، probably و definitely قبل از فعل کمکیِ منفی می‌آیند: I probably won't be late. بیشتر قیدهای دیگر بعد از آن می‌آیند: I don't often go.

only قبل از کلمه‌ای می‌آید که توصیفش می‌کند، و جایش می‌تواند معنی را عوض کند: Only I saw her (فقط من، نه کس دیگر) / I only saw her (فقط دیدمش، با او حرف نزدم).

  • I also have a sister. قبل از فعل اصلی
  • He's always late. بعد از be
  • We've already finished. بعد از فعل کمکی
  • She probably won't come. probably + منفی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I like also tea. → ✅ I also like tea. قبل از فعل اصلی.
  • ❌ He won't probably come. → ✅ He probably won't come. probably قبل از won't.
  • ❌ They have finished already their homework. → ✅ They have already finished their homework. نه بین فعل و مفعولش.

دو مفعول: give me the book, give the book to me B1

فعل‌هایی مثل give, send, show, lend, tell می‌توانند دو مفعول داشته باشند: give someone something (She gave me a present) یا give something to someone (She gave a present to me). با buy, make, get, cook از for استفاده کنید.

الگومثال
فعل + شخص + چیزI sent my mum a card.
فعل + چیز + to + شخصI sent a card to my mum.
فعل + چیز + for + شخص (buy, make, get, cook)He bought flowers for his wife.
وقتی «چیز» ضمیر استGive it to me. (Give me it خودمانی است)

وقتی «چیز» ضمیر است (it, them)، از to / for استفاده کنید: Can you send it to me?

بعضی فعل‌ها فقط to + شخص می‌گیرند: explain it to me, describe it to her, say something to him (نه explain me it).

  • My father gave me some advice. شخص + چیز
  • Can you lend your car to my brother? چیز + to + شخص
  • I made a cake for my sister. for
  • Please explain the rules to me. explain + to

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Can you explain me the problem? → ✅ Can you explain the problem to me? explain + to. (فارسی می‌گوید «برایم توضیح بده»، ولی explain me غلط است.)
  • ❌ She bought a present to her mother. → ✅ She bought a present for her mother. buy + for.
  • ❌ Give me it, please. (careful English) → ✅ Give it to me, please. وقتی «چیز» ضمیر است: to (در انگلیسیِ دقیق).

تمرین سطح B1

قبل از فعل اصلی: also speaks.
8. I ___ be late tonight. Don't wait for me.
بعد از فعل کمکی: will probably.

بعد از فعل کمکی: have already eaten.
10. Can you ___ the photos?
فعل + شخص + چیز.
11. He bought some flowers ___ his wife.
Buy + چیز + for + شخص.
12. Could you explain the problem ___ me again? (one word)
Explain + چیز + to + شخص.

C1 · پیشرفته

Fronting for emphasis and here comes the bus C1

For emphasis or contrast, English sometimes puts an object or phrase at the start (That film I'll never forget). After here / there and place phrases at the start, the verb can come before a noun subject (Here comes the bus. On the hill stood an old castle.).

PatternExample
object fronting (contrast)Some subjects I love; maths I hate.
here / there + verb + noun subjectHere comes the bus! There goes my money. (but: Here it comes.)
place + verb + subject (literary)On the hill stood an old castle.
adjective + as + subject + verbStrange as it may seem, I enjoyed it.

With pronouns, don't invert after here / there: Here it comes (not Here comes it). There she is!

Fronting is common in speech for contrast and in literary writing for effect. For Never have I…, see Inversion After Negative Adverbials.

  • That film I'll never forget. (object fronting)
  • Here comes the train. (here + verb + noun)
  • In the corner sat an old man. (place + verb + subject)
  • Tired as she was, she kept working. (adjective + as)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Here comes it! → ✅ Here it comes! (pronoun: no inversion)
  • ❌ There is she! → ✅ There she is! (pronoun: no inversion)
  • ❌ Tired as was she, she kept going. → ✅ Tired as she was, she kept going. (adjective + as + subject + verb)

تمرین سطح C1

13. Look! Here ___!
Here + verb + noun subject.
14. Where's Mina? ~ Oh, there ___!
Pronoun: no inversion.
15. Strange ___ it may seem, I actually enjoyed the exam. (one word)
Adjective + as + subject + verb.
