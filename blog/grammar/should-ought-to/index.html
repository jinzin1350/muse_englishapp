should، ought to و had better (توصیه)Should, Ought To and Had Better

should را برای نصیحت و گفتن اینکه به نظرمان چه کاری درست است («بهتر است، باید») به کار می‌بریم: You should drink more water. ought to تقریباً همان معنی را دارد و had better قوی‌تر است و اغلب هشدار است.

should از must ضعیف‌تر است: نصیحت است، نه قانون. برای اجبار صفحهٔ must و have to و برای حسرت‌های گذشته (I should have…) صفحهٔ should have، could have و needn't have را ببینید.

A2 · مقدماتی

should و shouldn't برای نصیحت A2

should / shouldn't + شکل سادهٔ فعل برای نصیحت و نظر: You should go to bed earlier. You shouldn't eat so much sugar.

ساختارمثال
مثبتYou should see a doctor.
منفیYou shouldn't (should not) worry.
سؤالیShould I call her?
نظرI think you should apologise. I don't think you should go.

should برای همهٔ اشخاص یکی است و بعدش شکل سادهٔ فعل می‌آید: She should rest. نه She shoulds rest و نه She should to rest.

برای نظرِ نرم‌تر اغلب I think … should یا I don't think … should می‌آوریم: I don't think you should buy it. (طبیعی‌تر از I think you shouldn't buy it)

  • You should try the fish. It's delicious. نصیحت
  • You shouldn't drive when you're tired. نصیحتِ منفی
  • What should I wear to the wedding? نصیحت خواستن
  • I think we should leave now. نظر

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You should to see a doctor. → ✅ You should see a doctor. should + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ She shoulds study more. → ✅ She should study more. بدون ‑s
  • ❌ Do I should call him? → ✅ Should I call him? سؤالی: Should I…?

تمرین سطح A2

1. You look tired. You ___ go to bed.
نصیحت: should + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
2. ___ I bring a present to the party?
نصیحت خواستن: Should I…?
3. You ___ (should / not / eat) so much chocolate. It's bad for your teeth.
نصیحتِ منفی: shouldn't + شکل سادهٔ فعل.

B1 · متوسط

ought to B1

ought to + شکل سادهٔ فعل یعنی should. کمی رسمی‌تر است و در سؤالی و منفی کمتر به کار می‌رود: You ought to call your mother.

ought to برای نصیحت و کار درست همان معنی should را دارد: You ought to tell the truth. = You should tell the truth.

برخلاف should، to دارد: ought to go (نه ought go). برای همهٔ اشخاص یکی است: She ought to go.

منفیِ ought not to / oughtn't to وجود دارد اما رسمی به نظر می‌رسد. سؤال با ought (Ought I to…?) کم پیش می‌آید. بیشتر مردم برای هر دو should می‌آورند: You shouldn't go. Should I go?

ought to اغلب وظیفه یا کار درستِ اخلاقی را نشان می‌دهد: We ought to help them. در انگلیسی آمریکایی should خیلی رایج‌تر است.

  • You ought to apologise to her. = should
  • We ought to be more careful with money. کار درست
  • You ought not to park there. منفیِ رسمی
  • Ought I to tell him? → Should I tell him? در سؤال should طبیعی‌تر است

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You ought apologise. → ✅ You ought to apologise. ought + to
  • ❌ She oughts to know. → ✅ She ought to know. بدون ‑s

had better: نصیحت جدی و هشدار B1

had better ('d better) + شکل سادهٔ فعل یعنی نصیحت جدی برای یک موقعیت مشخص، اغلب همراه هشدار («بهتر است… وگرنه»): You'd better hurry or you'll miss the train.

had better از should قوی‌تر است و نشان می‌دهد اگر به نصیحت عمل نکنید، اتفاق بدی می‌افتد: You'd better take an umbrella. It's going to rain.

دربارهٔ یک موقعیت مشخص الان یا به‌زودی است، نه نصیحت کلی: You'd better go now (نه You'd better eat vegetables به عنوان نصیحت کلی).

ساختار: had better (معمولاً 'd better) + شکل سادهٔ فعل. منفی: had better not: You'd better not be late. با اینکه had دارد، دربارهٔ حال یا آینده است.

  • We'd better leave now. The traffic is terrible. هشدار
  • You'd better not tell Mum about this. منفی
  • I'd better call my wife. She's waiting for me. تصمیمِ همین حالای خودم
  • You'd better be careful. That dog bites. هشدار

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ You'd better to go now. → ✅ You'd better go now. had better + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ You hadn't better be late. → ✅ You'd better not be late. منفی: had better not
  • ❌ You would better hurry. → ✅ You'd better hurry. 'd یعنی had

should برای انتظار: It should arrive tomorrow B1

should معنی «احتمالاً…» یا «قرار است…» هم دارد: The package should arrive tomorrow. They should be here by now.

should می‌تواند بگوید چیزی محتمل یا مورد انتظار است، چون عادی یا برنامه‌ریزی‌شده است: The meeting should finish at five. (برنامه این است.) This test should be easy for you. (انتظارش را دارم.)

should be + فعل ing‌دار و should have + اسم مفعول برای انتظارِ الان یا گذشته: They should be arriving soon. The parcel should have arrived yesterday. (اما نرسید)

با by now، should اغلب نشان می‌دهد آن چیزِ مورد انتظار اتفاق نیفتاده: It's 9 o'clock. Jo should be here by now. (پس کجاست؟)

should (انتظار دارم، اما مطمئن نیستم) را با must (از روی نشانه مطمئنم) مقایسه کنید: She should be home soon در برابر Her car is outside. She must be home. صفحهٔ فعل‌های وجهی برای حدس و استنتاج را ببینید.

  • The train should be here in five minutes. احتمالاً
  • You've studied a lot. You should pass easily. انتظارش را دارم
  • It's 7 pm. The shops should be open until 8. معمولاً
  • The repair should have taken an hour, but it took all day. انتظار داشتیم، اما نشد

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The bus should to arrive soon. → ✅ The bus should arrive soon. should + شکل سادهٔ فعل

تمرین سطح B1

4. You ___ to visit your grandparents more often.
ought to + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
5. کدام جمله همان معنی You should save more money را دارد؟
ought to یعنی should.
6. We ___ (ought) to leave now if we want to get there on time.
ought to + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
7. It's late. We ___ go home.
'd better + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
8. You'd ___ touch that. It's hot!
منفی: 'd better not.
9. The shop closes in ten minutes. We ___ (better / hurry).
'd better + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
10. My flight lands at 6 pm, so I ___ be home by 8. That's the plan.
طبق برنامه انتظارش می‌رود: should.
11. It's 10 o'clock. Ali ___ be here by now. I wonder where he is.
انتظار داشتیم اما نشده: should … by now.
12. The weather ___ (should / be) good this weekend. The forecast says sunny.
انتظار: should be.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Be supposed to B2

be supposed to + verb = what is expected, arranged or the rule (often not what really happens): We're supposed to wear a tie. You were supposed to call me!

Be supposed to says what should happen because of a rule, an arrangement or what people expect: You're supposed to check in an hour before the flight.

It often suggests that the thing doesn't happen, or didn't happen: I'm supposed to be on a diet, but I love cake! You were supposed to be here at six! (You weren't.)

It can also mean people say: This film is supposed to be very good.

Not supposed to = not allowed: You're not supposed to park here.

Don't forget the -d: supposed to (not suppose to). In speech the d is almost silent, but it is always written.

  • We're supposed to finish this by Friday. (an arrangement)
  • You weren't supposed to tell anyone! (you did tell)
  • This restaurant is supposed to be the best in town. (people say)
  • What are we supposed to do now? (what is expected)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ We're suppose to arrive at 9. → ✅ We're supposed to arrive at 9. (supposed, with -d)
  • ❌ You supposed to call me. → ✅ You were supposed to call me. (be + supposed to)

تمرین سطح B2

13. You ___ supposed to use your phone in the cinema.
Be not supposed to = not allowed.
14. The train was supposed to leave at 8, but it ___.
Was supposed to often means it didn't happen.
15. That new pizza place ___ (be) supposed to be amazing. Shall we try it?
Is supposed to = people say.

C1 · پیشرفته

Should in that-clauses and for surprise C1

After verbs like suggest, insist, recommend and adjectives like important, essential, British English often uses should: I suggest that you should apply. Should also shows surprise: It's strange that she should say that.

After verbs and adjectives of suggestion, demand and importance, we can use should + base verb in the that clause (especially in British English): They recommended that we should book early. It's essential that everyone should attend.

The subjunctive (base verb with no should) is also correct and is more common in American English and formal writing: They recommended that we book early. See Subjunctive.

Should after expressions of feeling (strange, odd, surprising, a pity) adds surprise or emotion: It's odd that you should mention her. I was just thinking about her!

Both should and the subjunctive are correct after suggest / insist / important: I insisted that he should come / he come. The ordinary present (he comes) is also common in informal British English.

  • The doctor suggested that I should rest for a week. (suggestion)
  • It's important that every child should have access to education. (importance)
  • It's a pity that you should feel that way. (emotion)
  • I find it surprising that he should say such a thing. (surprise)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I suggest that you should to apply. → ✅ I suggest that you should apply. (should + base verb)
  • ❌ It's essential that he should arrives on time. → ✅ It's essential that he should arrive on time. (should + base verb)

تمرین سطح C1

16. The committee insisted that the report ___ rewritten.
Should + base verb (be rewritten).
17. Which sentence expresses surprise?
It's strange that … should = surprise.
18. It's vital that the patient ___ (should / take) the medicine every day.
Should + base verb.
