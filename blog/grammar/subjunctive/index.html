The Subjunctive (I insist that he be on time)

Level: C2 · Free grammar guide

The subjunctive is a verb form used for things that are demanded, suggested or necessary, not facts. It uses the base form of the verb for every person: The doctor insisted that she stay in bed. (not stays)

It's formal and more common in American English. British English often uses should instead: …that she should stay in bed.

After verbs and adjectives

AfterWordsExample
verbs + thatinsist, suggest, recommend, demand, propose, request, ask, require, urgeThey demanded that the report be published.
adjectives + thatessential, vital, important, necessary, crucial, imperativeIt is essential that every student be on time.
nouns + thatthe suggestion, the demand, the requirement, the recommendationThe requirement that every driver have insurance…
  • No -s for he / she / it: I suggest that she consult a lawyer.
  • Be for every person: that he be, that they be.
  • Negative: not + base form: They recommended that he not accept the job.
  • Passive: be + past participle: She asked that her name not be mentioned.

In British English, you'll also hear a normal present tense: I insist that he pays. It's correct in informal British English, but it isn't the subjunctive.

Were for unreal situations

Were is used for all persons in unreal conditions and wishes: If I were you… I wish she were here. He acts as if he were the boss. See The Unreal Past.

Fixed phrases

Some old subjunctive forms survive in fixed expressions: Be that as it may (= even so), come what may (= whatever happens), if need be (= if necessary), so be it, suffice it to say, long live the King, God save the King.

Examples

  1. The doctor insisted that she stay in bed for a week. (insist that)
  2. I suggest that he apply for the job. (suggest that)
  3. It is vital that the patient not eat before surgery. (negative)
  4. The committee demanded that the report be published immediately. (passive)
  5. It's important that she be informed. (be)
  6. The law requires that every driver have insurance. (require that)
  7. We propose that the meeting be postponed. (propose that)
  8. Be that as it may, we still need a decision. (fixed phrase)
  9. We'll finish on time, come what may. (fixed phrase)
  10. We can work at the weekend if need be. (fixed phrase)

Common mistakes

❌ It is essential that he is being on time.

✅ It is essential that he be on time. (Subjunctive: be for every person.)

❌ They recommended that he doesn't accept.

✅ They recommended that he not accept. (Subjunctive negative: not + base form.)

❌ I suggest her to see a doctor.

✅ I suggest that she see a doctor. (Suggest + that clause (or suggest seeing).)

❌ She insisted that he to come.

✅ She insisted that he come. (Base form, no to.)

❌ Be it as it may, we must go.

✅ Be that as it may, we must go. (Fixed phrase: Be that as it may.)
