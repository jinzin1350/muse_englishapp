وجه التزامی (I insist that he be)The Subjunctive (I Insist That He Be…)

The subjunctive is a special verb form. The most common type is the base form after verbs like suggest, insist, recommend and adjectives like essential: I suggest that he see a doctor. (not sees) It's vital that she be on time. (not is)

The subjunctive is formal and more common in American English. British English often uses should instead: I suggest that he should see a doctor. For suggest + -ing, see Reporting Verbs.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Suggest that he see, insist that she be B2

After suggest, recommend, insist, demand, request, propose, ask + that, use the base form for every person: They insisted that he leave. I recommend that she be careful. British English also uses should + base form.

PatternExample
verb + that + subject + base formThe doctor suggested that he rest. (not rests)
with beThey demanded that the manager be fired. (not is)
BrE alternative: should + base formThe doctor suggested that he should rest.
past main verb: still base formShe insisted that I stay. (not stayed)

The base form doesn't change for he / she / it (no -s) and doesn't change for the past: We recommended that she take the job.

In informal British English, a normal tense is also heard (I suggest that he sees…), but in formal writing use the base form or should.

  • I suggest that you book early. (base form)
  • The teacher insisted that every student be on time. (be)
  • They recommended that he take a few days off. (past main verb, base form)
  • The judge ordered that the money be returned. (formal)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The doctor recommended that he takes a holiday. (formal) → ✅ The doctor recommended that he take a holiday. (base form: take)
  • ❌ She suggested me to see a doctor. → ✅ She suggested that I see a doctor. (suggest + that + subject)

Subjunctive: be.

C1 · پیشرفته

It's essential that he be…, it's vital that C1

After essential, vital, important, crucial, imperative, necessary, advisable + that, formal English uses the subjunctive (base form): It is essential that every child have access to education. Also after nouns like the suggestion / requirement / insistence that.

PatternExample
It is essential / vital that + base formIt is vital that he be told the truth.
It is important / crucial that + base formIt is important that she arrive early.
It is imperative that + base formIt is imperative that the work be finished today.
noun + that + base formThe requirement that all visitors sign in is strict.
BrE alternativeIt is essential that he should be told.

Compare: It's important to arrive early (general) / It's important that she arrive early (a specific person, formal).

  • It is essential that the patient rest for a week. (essential that)
  • It is crucial that nobody find out. (crucial that)
  • It's important that he not be late. (negative)
  • She ignored the suggestion that she apply for the job. (noun + that)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It is vital that he is informed immediately. (formal) → ✅ It is vital that he be informed immediately. (subjunctive: be)
  • ❌ It's essential that she arrives on time. (formal written) → ✅ It's essential that she arrive on time. (base form: arrive)

That he not be told, that it be done C1

The negative subjunctive is not + base form (no do): I suggest that he not go. The passive subjunctive is be + past participle: They demanded that the law be changed.

FormExample
negative: not + base formWe asked that she not tell anyone. (not doesn't tell)
negative be: not beI insist that he not be disturbed.
passive: be + past participleThey recommended that the road be closed.
negative passive: not be + past participleShe requested that her name not be published.

Don't use don't / doesn't in the subjunctive: …that he not leave (not that he doesn't leave).

  • The doctor advised that he not drive for a week. (negative)
  • The report recommends that the factory be closed. (passive)
  • She asked that her address not be given to anyone. (negative passive)
  • We insist that the problem be solved immediately. (passive)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I suggest that he doesn't go alone. (formal) → ✅ I suggest that he not go alone. (negative: not + base form)
  • ❌ They demanded that the rules are changed. (formal) → ✅ They demanded that the rules be changed. (passive: be changed)

If I were you, I wish it were, as if he were C1

The were subjunctive is used for unreal situations with all persons, especially after if, I wish, if only, as if / as though, suppose: If I were you… I wish he were here. He acts as if he were the boss. In informal English, was is also common.

PatternExample
If I / he / she / it were…If I were you, I'd accept.
I wish / If only … wereI wish it were Friday.
as if / as though … wereShe talks as if she were an expert.
Were + subject (inversion, formal)Were it not for your help, we'd have failed.
was (informal)If I was rich, I'd buy a house.

If I were you is a fixed expression for advice: use were, even in informal English.

For more on these patterns, see Second Conditional and Unreal Past.

  • If I were you, I'd talk to her. (advice)
  • I wish my brother were here. (wish)
  • He behaves as if he were the manager. (as if)
  • Were it not for the rain, we'd go out. (formal inversion)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ If I would be you, I'd go. → ✅ If I were you, I'd go. (if I were you)
  • ❌ I wish it is summer. → ✅ I wish it were summer. (unreal: were)

C2 · تسلط کامل

If need be, as it were, heaven forbid, long live C2

Some fixed expressions keep old subjunctive forms: if need be (= if necessary), as it were (= so to speak), heaven forbid (= I hope it never happens), long live…, God bless, woe betide, the powers that be.

ExpressionExampleMeaning
if need beI can work late, if need be.if necessary
as it wereShe's the boss of the family, as it were.so to speak
heaven / God forbidHeaven forbid that anything should happen to him.I hope it never happens
long live …Long live the King!may … live long
God bless (you)God bless you for your kindness.may God bless
woe betide …Woe betide anyone who's late!they'll be in trouble
the powers that beThe powers that be have decided to close the school.people in authority

These are fixed: don't change the verb form (if need be, not if need is).

  • We'll take a taxi, if need be. (if necessary)
  • He was, as it were, a second father to me. (so to speak)
  • Heaven forbid we should ever need it. (hoping not)
  • Woe betide anyone who forgets her birthday! (warning)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'll stay longer, if need is. → ✅ I'll stay longer, if need be. (fixed: if need be)
  • ❌ Long lives the Queen! → ✅ Long live the Queen! (fixed: long live)
