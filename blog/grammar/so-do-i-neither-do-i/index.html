Short Answers, So Do I, Neither Do I, I Think So

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

In conversation, we often answer with just the auxiliary verb instead of repeating the whole sentence: Do you like jazz? ~ Yes, I do. I'm tired. ~ So am I.

This makes English sound natural and less repetitive. The auxiliary must match the verb in the first sentence: be → am / is / are, present simple → do / does, past simple → did, and so on.

A1 · Beginner

Short answers: Yes, I do. No, she isn't. A1

QuestionYesNo
Are you tired?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Is she a teacher?Yes, she is.No, she isn't.
Do you like tea?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Did he call?Yes, he did.No, he didn't.
Can you swim?Yes, I can.No, I can't.
Have you finished?Yes, I have.No, I haven't.

  • Are you from Iran? ~ Yes, I am. (be)
  • Does your brother work? ~ No, he doesn't. (does)
  • Did you enjoy the film? ~ Yes, we did. (did)
  • Will you be home tonight? ~ No, I won't. (will)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Are you hungry? ~ Yes, I'm. → ✅ Are you hungry? ~ Yes, I am. (no contraction in positive short answers)
  • ❌ Do you like it? ~ Yes, I like. → ✅ Do you like it? ~ Yes, I do. (use the auxiliary)
  • ❌ Is it cold? ~ Yes, it's. → ✅ Is it cold? ~ Yes, it is. (no contraction)

Practice: A1

1. Do you speak English? ~ Yes, I ___.
Present simple question → do.
2. Is your sister married? ~ No, she ___.
Is → isn't.
3. Can you drive? ~ Yes, I ___. (one word)
Can → can.

A2 · Elementary

So do I, Neither can I, Me too, Me neither A2

StatementAgree (formal / normal)Agree (informal)
I'm tired.So am I. / I am too.Me too.
I love pizza.So do I. / I do too.Me too.
I can't dance.Neither can I. / I can't either.Me neither.
I didn't go.Neither did I. / I didn't either.Me neither.

  • I'm from Shiraz. ~ So am I! (be)
  • We went to the concert. ~ So did we. (past simple → did)
  • I haven't seen it. ~ Neither have I. (present perfect)
  • I can't wait for the holidays. ~ Me neither! (informal)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I like tea. ~ So I do. → ✅ I like tea. ~ So do I. (so + auxiliary + subject)
  • ❌ I don't eat meat. ~ So don't I. → ✅ I don't eat meat. ~ Neither do I. (negative: neither)
  • ❌ I can't swim. ~ Me too. → ✅ I can't swim. ~ Me neither. (negative: me neither)

Reply questions: Have you? Really? Is she? A2

  • I'm learning Japanese. ~ Are you? That's great! (interest)
  • She passed her driving test. ~ Did she? Wow! (surprise)
  • I can't swim. ~ Can't you? I'll teach you! (negative → negative)
  • We've bought a house. ~ Have you? Where? (interest)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I've been to Peru. ~ Did you? → ✅ I've been to Peru. ~ Have you? (same auxiliary: have)
  • ❌ She lives in Rome. ~ Is she? → ✅ She lives in Rome. ~ Does she? (present simple → does)

Practice: A2

4. I'm really hungry. ~ ___. Let's eat.
Positive with be: So am I.
5. I didn't like the film. ~ ___. It was boring.
Negative past: Neither did I.
6. I've never been to London. ~ Neither ___ I. (one word)
Present perfect: Neither have I.
7. I've just won $500! ~ ___? That's amazing!
I've → Have you?
8. My dad works in a hospital. ~ ___? What does he do?
Present simple → Does he?
9. I didn't sleep last night. ~ ___ you? Why not? (one word)
Negative past → Didn't you?

B1 · Intermediate

I think so, I hope so, I'm afraid not, I don't think so B1

PositiveNegative
I think so.I don't think so.
I hope so.I hope not.
I expect so.I don't expect so.
I guess so. (informal)I guess not.
I'm afraid so. (bad news)I'm afraid not.

  • Will it be sunny tomorrow? ~ I hope so. (positive hope)
  • Are we late? ~ I don't think so. (negative opinion)
  • Did you lose the match? ~ I'm afraid so. (bad news, yes)
  • Can you come on Friday? ~ I'm afraid not. (polite no)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Will it rain? ~ I don't hope so. → ✅ Will it rain? ~ I hope not. (hope → I hope not)
  • ❌ Is it true? ~ I think it. → ✅ Is it true? ~ I think so. (think so)

Practice: B1

10. Is the exam difficult? ~ I don't ___. It looks easy.
Negative opinion: I don't think so.
11. Will it rain on our wedding day? ~ I ___!
Hope → I hope not.
12. Did we miss the bus? ~ I'm afraid ___. It left five minutes ago. (one word)
Bad news, yes: I'm afraid so.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Using auxiliaries to avoid repetition: She can, but I can't B2

  • I can't swim, but my sister can. (can)
  • Tom didn't come, but Lisa did. (did)
  • I've finished, and so has Mina. (so + auxiliary)
  • We don't eat meat, and neither do our kids. (neither + auxiliary)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I don't smoke, but my dad smokes does. → ✅ I don't smoke, but my dad does. (auxiliary alone)
  • ❌ She's tired and so I am. → ✅ She's tired and so am I. (so + auxiliary + subject)

Practice: B2

13. I don't like horror films, but my friends ___.
Do replaces like horror films.
14. Mina has visited Paris, and so ___ her brother.
Has visited → so has.
15. I wasn't invited, but my sister ___. (one word)
Was replaces was invited.

C1 · Advanced

Nor, so it is, and other auxiliary patterns C1

  • I didn't see the accident, nor did anyone else. (formal nor)
  • You've got paint on your shirt. ~ Oh, so I have! (confirming with surprise)
  • I do think you should apologise. (emphasis)
  • He doesn't like sport, nor does he watch it. (formal)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He didn't call, nor he wrote. → ✅ He didn't call, nor did he write. (nor + auxiliary + subject)
  • ❌ Look, the sun is out! ~ So is it! → ✅ Look, the sun is out! ~ So it is! (confirming: so + subject + auxiliary)

Practice: C1

16. The company didn't apologise, ___ it offer any compensation.
Formal: nor did it…
17. It's 10 o'clock! ~ Oh, ___! I'm late!
Confirming with surprise: So it is!
18. I know you don't believe me, but I ___ lock the door this morning. I'm sure! (one word, emphasis)
Emphatic did.
