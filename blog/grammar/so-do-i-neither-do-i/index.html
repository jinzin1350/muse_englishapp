خانه / گرامر / جواب کوتاه، So do I و Neither do I

جواب کوتاه، So do I و Neither do IShort Answers, So Do I, Neither Do I, I Think So

در مکالمه، به جای تکرار کل جمله، اغلب فقط با فعل کمکی جواب می‌دهیم: Do you like jazz? ~ Yes, I do. I'm tired. ~ So am I.

این کار انگلیسی شما را طبیعی‌تر و کم‌تکرارتر می‌کند. فعل کمکی باید با فعل جملهٔ اول جور باشد: be ← am / is / are، حال ساده ← do / does، گذشتهٔ ساده ← did و غیره.

A1 · مبتدی

جواب کوتاه: Yes, I do. No, she isn't. A1

به سؤال‌های بله/نه با Yes / No + ضمیر + فعل کمکی جواب بدهید: Are you hungry? ~ Yes, I am. Do they live here? ~ No, they don't.

سؤالبلهنه
Are you tired?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Is she a teacher?Yes, she is.No, she isn't.
Do you like tea?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Did he call?Yes, he did.No, he didn't.
Can you swim?Yes, I can.No, I can't.
Have you finished?Yes, I have.No, I haven't.

همان فعل کمکیِ سؤال را بیاورید. جواب کوتاهِ مثبت را مخفف نکنید: Yes, I am. نه Yes, I'm.

جوابِ تک‌کلمه‌ای (Yes.) ممکن است خشک به نظر برسد. Yes, I do مؤدبانه‌تر و طبیعی‌تر است.

  • Are you from Iran? ~ Yes, I am. be
  • Does your brother work? ~ No, he doesn't. does
  • Did you enjoy the film? ~ Yes, we did. did
  • Will you be home tonight? ~ No, I won't. will

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Are you hungry? ~ Yes, I'm. → ✅ Are you hungry? ~ Yes, I am. جواب کوتاهِ مثبت مخفف نمی‌شود.
  • ❌ Do you like it? ~ Yes, I like. → ✅ Do you like it? ~ Yes, I do. فعل کمکی بیاورید.
  • ❌ Is it cold? ~ Yes, it's. → ✅ Is it cold? ~ Yes, it is. مخفف نکنید.

تمرین سطح A1

1. Do you speak English? ~ Yes, I ___.
سؤال در حال ساده ← do.
2. Is your sister married? ~ No, she ___.
Is ← isn't.
3. Can you drive? ~ Yes, I ___. (one word)
Can ← can.

A2 · مقدماتی

So do I, Neither can I, Me too, Me neither (من هم همین‌طور) A2

برای موافقت: So + فعل کمکی + I (مثبت): I'm hungry. ~ So am I. Neither + فعل کمکی + I (منفی): I can't swim. ~ Neither can I.

جملهموافقت (رسمی / معمولی)موافقت (خودمانی)
I'm tired.So am I. / I am too.Me too.
I love pizza.So do I. / I do too.Me too.
I can't dance.Neither can I. / I can't either.Me neither.
I didn't go.Neither did I. / I didn't either.Me neither.

ترتیب کلمه‌ها: So / Neither + فعل کمکی + فاعل: So do I. Neither have we. نه So I do.

nor هم مثل neither به کار می‌رود، به‌خصوص در انگلیسی بریتانیایی: I don't like it. ~ Nor do I.

برای مخالفت فعل کمکیِ برعکس را بیاورید: I love horror films. ~ Really? I don't. / I don't like coffee. ~ Oh, I do.

  • I'm from Shiraz. ~ So am I! be
  • We went to the concert. ~ So did we. گذشتهٔ ساده ← did
  • I haven't seen it. ~ Neither have I. حال کامل
  • I can't wait for the holidays. ~ Me neither! خودمانی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I like tea. ~ So I do. → ✅ I like tea. ~ So do I. so + فعل کمکی + فاعل.
  • ❌ I don't eat meat. ~ So don't I. → ✅ I don't eat meat. ~ Neither do I. منفی: neither.
  • ❌ I can't swim. ~ Me too. → ✅ I can't swim. ~ Me neither. منفی: me neither. (فارسی در هر دو حالت می‌گوید «من هم»، اما در منفی انگلیسی Me too غلط است.)

سؤال‌های واکنشی: Have you? Really? Is she? A2

سؤال‌های واکنشیِ کوتاه (Have you? Did she? Is it?) علاقه یا تعجب را نشان می‌دهند، مثل «جدی؟» در فارسی: I've just got married! ~ Have you? Congratulations!

برای اینکه نشان دهید گوش می‌دهید و علاقه دارید، با فعل کمکی + ضمیر به شکل سؤال جواب بدهید: I live in Calgary. ~ Do you? I've been there!

فعل کمکی با جمله جور است: مثبت ← مثبت، منفی ← منفی: I don't eat meat. ~ Don't you? I didn't know that.

لحن صدا احساس شما را نشان می‌دهد: لحنِ رو به بالا برای علاقه یا تعجب، لحنِ رو به پایین برای چیزی که از قبل حدس می‌زدید.

  • I'm learning Japanese. ~ Are you? That's great! علاقه
  • She passed her driving test. ~ Did she? Wow! تعجب
  • I can't swim. ~ Can't you? I'll teach you! منفی ← منفی
  • We've bought a house. ~ Have you? Where? علاقه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I've been to Peru. ~ Did you? → ✅ I've been to Peru. ~ Have you? همان فعل کمکی: have.
  • ❌ She lives in Rome. ~ Is she? → ✅ She lives in Rome. ~ Does she? حال ساده ← does.

تمرین سطح A2

منفی در گذشته: Neither did I.

I've ← Have you?

حال ساده ← Does he?

منفی در گذشته ← Didn't you?

B1 · متوسط

I think so, I hope so, I'm afraid not, I don't think so B1

به جای تکرار یک جمله‌واره so / not بیاورید: Is it going to rain? ~ I hope not. Will she come? ~ I think so. (فکر کنم / امیدوارم)

مثبتمنفی
I think so.I don't think so.
I hope so.I hope not.
I expect so.I don't expect so.
I guess so. (خودمانی)I guess not.
I'm afraid so. (خبر بد)I'm afraid not.

so جای یک جمله‌وارهٔ مثبت را می‌گیرد: Is the shop open? ~ I think so. (یعنی فکر کنم باز است.)

با think, expect, believe, suppose شکل منفی معمولاً I don't think so است. با hope و be afraid از not استفاده کنید: I hope not. I'm afraid not. (نه I don't hope so)

  • Will it be sunny tomorrow? ~ I hope so. امید مثبت
  • Are we late? ~ I don't think so. نظر منفی
  • Did you lose the match? ~ I'm afraid so. خبر بد، بله
  • Can you come on Friday? ~ I'm afraid not. «نه» مؤدبانه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Will it rain? ~ I don't hope so. → ✅ Will it rain? ~ I hope not. hope ← I hope not.
  • ❌ Is it true? ~ I think it. → ✅ Is it true? ~ I think so. think so.

تمرین سطح B1

10. Is the exam difficult? ~ I don't ___. It looks easy.
نظر منفی: I don't think so.
11. Will it rain on our wedding day? ~ I ___!
Hope ← I hope not.
12. Did we miss the bus? ~ I'm afraid ___. It left five minutes ago. (one word)
خبر بد، بله: I'm afraid so.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Using auxiliaries to avoid repetition: She can, but I can't B2

Use an auxiliary alone to avoid repeating a verb phrase: I haven't seen it, but my brother has. She speaks French and so does her son.

After the first clause, the auxiliary can stand for the whole verb phrase: I wasn't tired, but everyone else was. (= was tired)

Use do / does / did for simple tenses: I don't like coffee, but my wife does. He said he'd call, and he did.

This is common in contrasts with but, additions with and so / and neither, and replies: Who can help? ~ I can.

  • I can't swim, but my sister can. (can)
  • Tom didn't come, but Lisa did. (did)
  • I've finished, and so has Mina. (so + auxiliary)
  • We don't eat meat, and neither do our kids. (neither + auxiliary)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I don't smoke, but my dad smokes does. → ✅ I don't smoke, but my dad does. (auxiliary alone)
  • ❌ She's tired and so I am. → ✅ She's tired and so am I. (so + auxiliary + subject)

تمرین سطح B2

13. I don't like horror films, but my friends ___.
Do replaces like horror films.
14. Mina has visited Paris, and so ___ her brother.
Has visited → so has.
15. I wasn't invited, but my sister ___. (one word)
Was replaces was invited.

C1 · پیشرفته

Nor, so it is, and other auxiliary patterns C1

Nor + inversion adds a negative point formally (He didn't apologise, nor did he explain). So it is / So I did confirms with surprise. Stressed do adds emphasis (I do like it!).

Nor / neither + auxiliary + subject can join two negative ideas in formal speech and writing: The government didn't reduce taxes, nor did it increase spending.

So + subject + auxiliary (no inversion) confirms something with surprise: Look, it's snowing! ~ So it is! (= You're right, it is!) Compare So is it (= it is too).

A stressed do / does / did in a positive sentence adds emphasis or contrast: I do like your new haircut! She did call you, I promise.

  • I didn't see the accident, nor did anyone else. (formal nor)
  • You've got paint on your shirt. ~ Oh, so I have! (confirming with surprise)
  • I do think you should apologise. (emphasis)
  • He doesn't like sport, nor does he watch it. (formal)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He didn't call, nor he wrote. → ✅ He didn't call, nor did he write. (nor + auxiliary + subject)
  • ❌ Look, the sun is out! ~ So is it! → ✅ Look, the sun is out! ~ So it is! (confirming: so + subject + auxiliary)

تمرین سطح C1

16. The company didn't apologise, ___ it offer any compensation.
Formal: nor did it…
17. It's 10 o'clock! ~ Oh, ___! I'm late!
Confirming with surprise: So it is!
18. I know you don't believe me, but I ___ lock the door this morning. I'm sure! (one word, emphasis)
Emphatic did.
