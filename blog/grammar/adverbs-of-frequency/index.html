قیدهای تکرارAdverbs of Frequency

A1 · A2 · B2

Adverbs of frequency say how often something happens: always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never.

The key thing is word order: they go before most verbs (I often cook) but after be (I'm often tired).

A1 · مبتدی

Always, usually, often, sometimes, never A1

AdverbHow oftenExample
always100%I always have breakfast.
usuallyabout 80%She usually walks to work.
oftenabout 60%We often eat out.
sometimesabout 40%I sometimes watch TV in the evening.
rarely / hardly everabout 10%He rarely calls me.
never0%I never drink coffee.

  • I always brush my teeth before bed. (always)
  • My dad often cooks dinner. (often)
  • We sometimes go to the beach. (sometimes)
  • She never eats meat. (never)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I don't never watch TV. → ✅ I never watch TV. (never + positive verb)
  • ❌ I go always to school by bus. → ✅ I always go to school by bus. (before the main verb)

Word order: I always go, I'm always tired A1

PositionExample
before the main verbShe usually takes the bus.
after beHe's always happy. They are never late.
after an auxiliary or modalI have never seen it. You can always call me.
questionsDo you often go out? Are you always this busy?
negativesI don't usually eat meat. She isn't often late.

  • I usually go to bed at 11. (before the main verb)
  • She's always cheerful. (after be)
  • Have you ever been to Japan? (after the auxiliary)
  • I don't often see him. (negative)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Always I am tired. → ✅ I am always tired. (after be)
  • ❌ We usually are at home on Sundays. → ✅ We are usually at home on Sundays. (after be)
  • ❌ I never have been to Paris. → ✅ I have never been to Paris. (after the auxiliary)

تمرین سطح A1

1. I ___ get up at 7 o'clock, every single day.
Every day = 100%: always.
2. He doesn't like fish. He ___ eats it.
0%: never.
3. I ___ go to the cinema. Maybe once or twice a year. (one word, meaning not often)
Not often: rarely.
4. My brother ___ late for school.
After be: is often.
5. ___ go to the gym after work?
Question: Do you usually…?
6. I have ___ been to London. Is it nice? (one word, = not ever)
After the auxiliary: have never.

A2 · مقدماتی

How often? Once a week, every day, twice a month A2

ExpressionExample
every + timeI go running every morning.
once / twice / three times + a + timeWe eat out once a week. She calls twice a day.
every other + timeI wash my hair every other day. (= every second day)
on + daysI play football on Saturdays.
from time to time / now and thenWe meet from time to time. (sometimes)

  • How often do you go to the dentist? ~ Twice a year. (twice a year)
  • I see my grandparents every weekend. (every)
  • The bus comes every ten minutes. (every + number)
  • I go swimming once a week. (once a week)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I go to the gym three times in a week. → ✅ I go to the gym three times a week. (times + a)
  • ❌ How many often do you travel? → ✅ How often do you travel? (how often)
  • ❌ Every days I walk to school. → ✅ Every day I walk to school. (every + singular)

تمرین سطح A2

7. ___ do you visit your parents? ~ Once a month.
How often…?
8. I go to the gym three times ___ week.
Three times a week.
9. I play tennis ___ a week, on Mondays and Thursdays. (one word)
Two times: twice.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Seldom, rarely, frequently, invariably; Rarely have I… B2

Formal adverbMeaningExample
invariablyalwaysHe invariably arrives late.
frequentlyoftenThese problems frequently occur.
occasionallysometimesWe occasionally meet for lunch.
seldom / rarelynot oftenShe seldom complains.
Rarely / Seldom / Never + inversionemphasis (formal)Rarely have I seen such a beautiful view.

  • The trains are frequently late. (often)
  • I occasionally work from home. (sometimes)
  • Seldom do we get snow here. (inversion)
  • She invariably orders the same dish. (always)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Rarely I have seen such a mess. → ✅ Rarely have I seen such a mess. (inversion)
  • ❌ I hardly never go out. → ✅ I hardly ever go out. (hardly ever)

تمرین سطح B2

10. ___ seen such a beautiful sunset.
At the start: inversion.
11. He ___ arrives late. In ten years, he has never been on time.
Always, without exception: invariably.
12. I hardly ___ eat red meat. Maybe once a year. (one word)
Hardly ever = rarely.
