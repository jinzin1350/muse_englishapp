قیدهای تکرارAdverbs of Frequency

قیدهای تکرار می‌گویند کاری چند وقت یک بار انجام می‌شود: always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never.

نکتهٔ اصلی ترتیب کلمه‌هاست: این قیدها قبل از بیشتر فعل‌ها می‌آیند (I often cook)، اما بعد از be (I'm often tired).

A1 · A2 · B2

A1 · مبتدی

always, usually, often, sometimes, never A1

از بیشترین به کمترین: always (۱۰۰٪) > usually > often > sometimes > rarely / hardly ever > never (۰٪).

قیدچقدرمثال
always (همیشه)۱۰۰٪I always have breakfast.
usually (معمولاً)حدود ۸۰٪She usually walks to work.
often (اغلب، زیاد)حدود ۶۰٪We often eat out.
sometimes (گاهی)حدود ۴۰٪I sometimes watch TV in the evening.
rarely / hardly ever (به‌ندرت)حدود ۱۰٪He rarely calls me.
never (هرگز)۰٪I never drink coffee.

never خودش منفی است، پس فعل مثبت بیاورید: I never eat meat. (نه I don't never eat meat). در فارسی می‌گوییم «هرگز گوشت نمی‌خورم»، اما در انگلیسی فعل منفی نمی‌شود.

برای عادت‌ها این قیدها را با حال ساده بیاورید: I usually get up at 7.

  • I always brush my teeth before bed. always (همیشه)
  • My dad often cooks dinner. often (اغلب)
  • We sometimes go to the beach. sometimes (گاهی)
  • She never eats meat. never (هرگز)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I don't never watch TV. → ✅ I never watch TV. never + فعل مثبت.
  • ❌ I go always to school by bus. → ✅ I always go to school by bus. قید قبل از فعل اصلی می‌آید.

ترتیب کلمه‌ها: I always go, I'm always tired A1

قیدهای تکرار قبل از فعل اصلی می‌آیند (I often go)، اما بعد از be (I'm often late) و بعد از اولین فعل کمکی (I have never been, I can't always come).

جای قیدمثال
قبل از فعل اصلیShe usually takes the bus.
بعد از beHe's always happy. They are never late.
بعد از فعل کمکی یا فعل وجهیI have never seen it. You can always call me.
سؤالیDo you often go out? Are you always this busy?
منفیI don't usually eat meat. She isn't often late.

sometimes، usually و often می‌توانند اول یا آخر جمله هم بیایند: Sometimes I walk to work. I walk to work sometimes.

always و never معمولاً اول جمله نمی‌آیند (جز Never… برای تأکید در انگلیسی رسمی).

  • I usually go to bed at 11. قبل از فعل اصلی
  • She's always cheerful. بعد از be
  • Have you ever been to Japan? بعد از فعل کمکی
  • I don't often see him. منفی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Always I am tired. → ✅ I am always tired. بعد از be.
  • ❌ We usually are at home on Sundays. → ✅ We are usually at home on Sundays. بعد از be.
  • ❌ I never have been to Paris. → ✅ I have never been to Paris. بعد از فعل کمکی.

تمرین سطح A1

1. I ___ get up at 7 o'clock, every single day.
هر روز یعنی ۱۰۰٪: always.
2. He doesn't like fish. He ___ eats it.
۰٪: never.
3. I ___ go to the cinema. Maybe once or twice a year. (one word, meaning not often)
نه زیاد: rarely.
4. My brother ___ late for school.
بعد از be: is often.
5. ___ go to the gym after work?
سؤالی: Do you usually…?
6. I have ___ been to London. Is it nice? (one word, = not ever)
بعد از فعل کمکی: have never.

A2 · مقدماتی

How often? یعنی چند وقت یک بار؟ once a week, every day, twice a month A2

با How often…? بپرسید و با عبارت‌های تکرار جواب بدهید: every day, once a week, twice a month, three times a year, every other day. این عبارت‌ها معمولاً آخر جمله می‌آیند.

عبارتمثال
every + زمانI go running every morning.
once / twice / three times + a + زمانWe eat out once a week. She calls twice a day.
every other + زمانI wash my hair every other day. (یعنی یک روز در میان)
on + روزهاI play football on Saturdays.
from time to time / now and thenWe meet from time to time. (یعنی گاهی)

بگویید once (یک بار) و twice (دو بار)، بعد three times, four times…: I've been there twice. (two times در صحبت رایج است، اما twice بهتر است.)

  • How often do you go to the dentist? ~ Twice a year. سالی دو بار
  • I see my grandparents every weekend. every (هر)
  • The bus comes every ten minutes. every + عدد
  • I go swimming once a week. هفته‌ای یک بار

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I go to the gym three times in a week. → ✅ I go to the gym three times a week. times + a. (فارسی می‌گوید «سه بار در هفته»، ولی انگلیسی three times a week، بدون in.)
  • ❌ How many often do you travel? → ✅ How often do you travel? how often.
  • ❌ Every days I walk to school. → ✅ Every day I walk to school. every + اسم مفرد.

تمرین سطح A2

7. ___ do you visit your parents? ~ Once a month.
How often…?
8. I go to the gym three times ___ week.
Three times a week.
9. I play tennis ___ a week, on Mondays and Thursdays. (one word)
دو بار: twice.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Seldom, rarely, frequently, invariably; Rarely have I… B2

More formal adverbs: frequently (often), occasionally (sometimes), seldom / rarely (not often), invariably (always, without exception). Rarely, seldom and never at the start of a sentence need inversion: Rarely do we see such talent.

Formal adverbMeaningExample
invariablyalwaysHe invariably arrives late.
frequentlyoftenThese problems frequently occur.
occasionallysometimesWe occasionally meet for lunch.
seldom / rarelynot oftenShe seldom complains.
Rarely / Seldom / Never + inversionemphasis (formal)Rarely have I seen such a beautiful view.

For inversion, use auxiliary + subject: Seldom does she complain. Never have I felt so happy. See Inversion After Negative Adverbials.

Hardly ever = rarely (informal): I hardly ever watch TV.

  • The trains are frequently late. (often)
  • I occasionally work from home. (sometimes)
  • Seldom do we get snow here. (inversion)
  • She invariably orders the same dish. (always)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Rarely I have seen such a mess. → ✅ Rarely have I seen such a mess. (inversion)
  • ❌ I hardly never go out. → ✅ I hardly ever go out. (hardly ever)

تمرین سطح B2

10. ___ seen such a beautiful sunset.
At the start: inversion.
11. He ___ arrives late. In ten years, he has never been on time.
Always, without exception: invariably.
12. I hardly ___ eat red meat. Maybe once a year. (one word)
Hardly ever = rarely.
