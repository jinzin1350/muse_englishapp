Adverbs of Frequency (always, usually, never)

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

Adverbs of frequency say how often something happens: I always have breakfast. She sometimes works at home. We never watch TV.

From always to never

AdverbHow oftenExample
always100%I always brush my teeth before bed.
usuallyabout 90%I usually get up at 7.
oftenabout 70%We often eat out on Fridays.
sometimesabout 50%She sometimes walks to work.
rarely / hardly everabout 5–10%He hardly ever calls me.
never0%I never drink cola.

Where does the adverb go?

  • Before the main verb: I usually walk to school. They never eat meat.
  • After be (am, is, are, was, were): She's always late. We are often tired.
  • After don't / doesn't: I don't often go out.
  • In questions, after the subject: Do you often go swimming?

Sometimes, usually and often can also go at the start or at the end of a sentence: Sometimes I cook. I cook sometimes. Always and never don't usually go at the start.

Never is already negative: I never eat meat. (not I don't never eat meat)

How often…? and frequency expressions

Ask with How often…?: How often do you go to the gym? Answer with an adverb or an expression: every day, once a week, twice a month, three times a year. Frequency expressions go at the end: I go to the gym twice a week.

Examples

  1. I always have coffee in the morning. (before the main verb)
  2. My brother is never late. (after be)
  3. We usually go to the beach in summer. (before the main verb)
  4. She often reads before bed. (before the main verb)
  5. They sometimes play tennis on Sundays. (about 50%)
  6. I hardly ever eat fast food. (almost never)
  7. Are you always this busy? (question with be)
  8. He doesn't usually work on Saturdays. (after doesn't)
  9. How often do you see your grandparents? – Once a month. (frequency expression)
  10. I check my email three times a day. (expression at the end)

Common mistakes

❌ I go always to school by bus.

✅ I always go to school by bus. (The adverb goes before the main verb.)

❌ She always is happy.

✅ She is always happy. (The adverb goes after be.)

❌ I don't never eat meat.

✅ I never eat meat. (Never is already negative.)

❌ Always I get up early.

✅ I always get up early. (Always doesn't usually go at the start.)

❌ I go to the cinema one time a month.

✅ I go to the cinema once a month. (Say once a month and twice a month.)

❌ I twice a week go swimming.

✅ I go swimming twice a week. (Frequency expressions go at the end.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. Choose the correct sentence.
The adverb goes before the main verb: I always get up…
2. I ___ coffee. I don't like it.
I don't like it = 0%: never.
3. She ___ late for class.
With be, the adverb goes after it: She is never late.
4. We ___ to the cinema on Fridays.
Before the main verb, and we takes go: often go.
5. How ___ do you go swimming?
To ask about frequency: How often…?
6. My parents ___ busy at the weekend.
After be: are usually. Parents takes are.
7. He ___ calls me. Maybe once a year.
Once a year = almost never: hardly ever.
8. I go to the gym ___ a week.
Two times = twice: twice a week.
9. She doesn't ___ work on Sundays.
After doesn't, use a positive adverb: often. Never and hardly ever are already negative.
10. Do you ___ eat breakfast?
The adverb is usually.
11. I see my cousins ___ a month.
One time = once: once a month.
12. Choose the correct sentence.
Never goes before the main verb, and we don't add don't.
13. We ___ have pizza on Fridays. It's our tradition!
A tradition = every time: always.
14. Which adverb means 0%?
Never = 0%.
15. They are ___ happy to help.
After are: always.
16. Choose the correct question.
In questions, the adverb goes after the subject: Do you often go out?
