ساختار سببی با have و getCausative Have and Get (I had my hair cut)

وقتی ترتیبی می‌دهیم که شخص دیگری کاری را برایمان انجام دهد، معمولاً یک خدمت، have something done می‌آوریم: I had my car repaired. (مکانیک تعمیرش کرد؛ خودم تعمیرش نکردم.) فارسی هم می‌گوید «ماشینم را تعمیر کردم» در حالی که مکانیک تعمیرش کرده؛ انگلیسی این فرق را نشان می‌دهد.

مقایسه کنید: I repaired my car (خودم انجامش دادم) / I had my car repaired (کس دیگری برایم انجامش داد). برای مجهول به طور کلی صفحهٔ مجهول را ببینید.

B1 · B2 · C1

B1 · متوسط

have something done: خدمات B1

have + مفعول + اسم مفعول یعنی ترتیب دادن که کسی کاری را انجام دهد: I have my hair cut every month. We had the house painted.

ساختارمثال
have + مفعول + اسم مفعولI have my hair cut every six weeks.
گذشتهWe had our windows cleaned yesterday.
آیندهI'm going to have my eyes tested.
سؤالیWhere do you have your car serviced?

ترتیب کلمه‌ها مهم است: have + چیز + اسم مفعول. I had my car washed (کسی آن را شست). I had washed my car (گذشتهٔ کامل: خودم شستمش).

have اینجا یک فعل معمولی است، پس سؤال و منفی با do / does / did ساخته می‌شوند: Did you have the TV repaired?

  • I had my hair cut yesterday. آرایشگر کوتاهش کرد
  • We're having a new kitchen fitted next month. ترتیبش داده شده
  • She has her nails done every Friday. خدمت همیشگی
  • You should have your eyes tested. توصیه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I had cut my hair at the hairdresser's. → ✅ I had my hair cut at the hairdresser's. have + مفعول + اسم مفعول.
  • ❌ I cut my hair yesterday. (the hairdresser did it) → ✅ I had my hair cut yesterday. کس دیگری انجامش داد (آرایشگر).
  • ❌ Did you had your car washed? → ✅ Did you have your car washed? did + have.

get something done B1

get + مفعول + اسم مفعول همان معنی have something done را دارد، اما خودمانی‌تر است: I need to get my phone fixed. معنی «تمام کردن» هم می‌دهد: get the work done.

در صحبت روزمره get به جای have خیلی رایج است: I got my hair cut. I must get my passport renewed.

get something done می‌تواند یعنی تمامش کردن (خودتان انجامش دهید یا مطمئن شوید انجام می‌شود): I need to get this report done by Friday.

با get اغلب حس زحمت یا کاری که باید از سر بگذرانیم هست: I finally got the washing machine fixed.

  • I'm going to get my teeth checked next week. خودمانی
  • We got the heating repaired before winter. ترتیبش داده شد
  • Let's get this job done and go home. تمام کردن
  • Where can I get my shoes repaired? سؤالی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I need to get fix my laptop. → ✅ I need to get my laptop fixed. get + مفعول + اسم مفعول.
  • ❌ I got my car to wash. → ✅ I got my car washed. اسم مفعول: washed.

تمرین سطح B1

1. I don't cut my own hair. I ___ every month.
Have + مفعول + اسم مفعول.
2. Where ___ your car repaired?
سؤالی: did you have…?
3. We ___ (have / the house / paint) last summer.
Have + مفعول + اسم مفعول.
4. My phone screen is broken. I need to ___.
Get + مفعول + اسم مفعول.
5. Let's get the cleaning done یعنی چه؟
Get something done می‌تواند یعنی «تمامش کنیم».
6. I ___ (get / my hair / cut) yesterday. Do you like it?
Get + مفعول + اسم مفعول.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Had my phone stolen: things that happen to you B2

have / get + object + past participle also describes bad things that happen to you, not things you arrange: I had my bag stolen. He got his nose broken.

The same structure can describe an unpleasant experience caused by someone else: We had our car broken into last night. (We didn't arrange it!)

Compare: My bag was stolen (passive) and I had my bag stolen (focus on me as the victim). Both are correct.

Get is also used, especially for injuries and accidents: He got his arm broken playing rugby.

  • She had her passport stolen in Rome. (bad experience)
  • We had our flight cancelled. (happened to us)
  • He got his fingers trapped in the door. (accident)
  • They had their house broken into while they were on holiday. (bad experience)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I stole my wallet on the bus. (someone stole it) → ✅ I had my wallet stolen on the bus. (had + object + past participle)
  • ❌ She had stolen her phone. (it happened to her) → ✅ She had her phone stolen. (word order: had + object + participle)

Have someone do, get someone to do, make and let B2

have + person + base verb and get + person + to + verb = arrange for someone to do something: I'll have the porter carry your bags. I got my brother to help me.

StructureMeaningExample
have + person + base verbarrange (often as a boss / client)I'll have my assistant call you.
get + person + to + verbpersuade or arrangeI got my sister to lend me her car.
make + person + base verbforceMy mum made me tidy my room.
let + person + base verballowThey let us leave early.

Note the difference: have / make / let + base verb (no to); get + person + to + verb.

In the passive, make takes to: I was made to wait for an hour. Let has no passive: use be allowed to.

  • The manager had the staff work late. (arranged (as a boss))
  • I got a friend to fix my computer. (persuaded)
  • The teacher made us rewrite the essay. (forced)
  • My parents let me stay up late. (allowed)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I got my brother help me. → ✅ I got my brother to help me. (get + person + to + verb)
  • ❌ She made me to apologise. → ✅ She made me apologise. (make + person + base verb)
  • ❌ They let us to go. → ✅ They let us go. (let + person + base verb)

تمرین سطح B2

7. Poor Ahmad! He ___ on the train yesterday.
A bad experience: had his laptop stolen.
8. What does We had our car damaged mean?
A bad experience caused by someone else.
9. She ___ (have / her handbag / snatch) in the market.
A bad experience: had her handbag snatched.
10. I couldn't open the jar, so I ___ my husband to do it.
Get + person + to + verb.
11. The doctor ___ me wait for two hours!
Make + person + base verb = force.
12. I'll have my secretary ___ (send) you the contract.
Have + person + base verb.

C1 · پیشرفته

Have someone doing, I won't have it, get things going C1

have + person + -ing = cause an ongoing result (He had us laughing). won't have + person + -ing = won't allow (I won't have you talking like that). get + object + -ing = start something (get the car going).

Have + person + -ing describes an effect someone causes: The comedian had everyone laughing. In ten minutes she had the kids dancing.

I won't have + person + -ing (or + noun) = I won't accept or allow it: I won't have you speaking to your mother like that! I won't have any arguments in this house.

Get + object + -ing = make something start working or moving: It took an hour to get the engine running. Let's get things moving.

Have something done can also mean finish by a time: I'll have the report finished by Monday. (I'll make sure it's finished.)

  • Her stories had us laughing all evening. (effect)
  • I won't have people smoking in my house. (won't allow)
  • We finally got the old car going. (make it start)
  • I'll have it done by five o'clock. (finish by a time)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He had us laugh all night. (an ongoing effect) → ✅ He had us laughing all night. (ongoing effect: -ing)
  • ❌ I won't have you to shout at me. → ✅ I won't have you shouting at me. (won't have + person + -ing)

تمرین سطح C1

13. The film was so sad that it had the whole audience ___.
Have + person + -ing = an effect.
14. What does I won't have you talking to me like that mean?
Won't have + -ing = won't allow.
15. It took us ages to get the fire ___ (burn).
Get + object + -ing = make it start.
