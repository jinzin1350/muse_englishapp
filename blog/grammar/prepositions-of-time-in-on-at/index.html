حرف اضافهٔ زمان: in، on و atPrepositions of Time: In, On and At

at را برای ساعت دقیق، on را برای روز و تاریخ، و in را برای دوره‌های طولانی‌تر مثل ماه، سال و فصل بیاورید: at 7 pm, on Friday, in May.

این صفحه for, during, by, until و within را هم توضیح می‌دهد. برای for و since صفحهٔ for، since و ago را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

at 6 o'clock, on Monday, in July A1

at + ساعت (at 6 o'clock, at night)، on + روز و تاریخ (on Monday, on 5 May)، in + ماه، سال، فصل و بخش‌های روز (in July, in 2025, in the morning).

atonin
ساعت: at 6 o'clock, at 7.30, at noon, at midnightروزها: on Monday, on Fridaysماه‌ها: in July
at night, at the weekend (انگلیسی بریتانیایی)تاریخ: on 5 May, on my birthdayسال‌ها: in 2025
وقت غذا: at lunchtimeon Monday morning, on New Year's Dayفصل‌ها: in (the) summer
تعطیلات: at Christmas, at Easterروزهای خاص: on Christmas Day, on my wedding dayبخش‌های روز: in the morning / afternoon / evening
at the moment, at presenton weekdays, on weekends (انگلیسی آمریکایی)دهه‌ها و قرن‌ها: in the 1990s

انگلیسی آمریکایی معمولاً می‌گوید on the weekend و انگلیسی بریتانیایی at the weekend. هر دو درست‌اند.

قبل از this, next, last, every, today, tomorrow, yesterday حرف اضافه نیاورید: I'll see you next Friday. (نه on next Friday)

  • The film starts at 8 pm. at + ساعت
  • My birthday is on 12 March. on + تاریخ
  • We're going to Spain in August. in + ماه
  • I usually read in the evening. in + بخشی از روز

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I was born on 1995. → ✅ I was born in 1995. سال‌ها: in.
  • ❌ See you in Monday. → ✅ See you on Monday. روزها: on.
  • ❌ The meeting is at next Tuesday. → ✅ The meeting is next Tuesday. با next حرف اضافه نمی‌آید.
  • ❌ I go to bed in midnight. → ✅ I go to bed at midnight. at midnight.

تمرین سطح A1

1. The class starts ___ 9 o'clock.
ساعت دقیق: at.
2. I was born ___ 2001.
سال‌ها: in.
3. We have English classes ___ Mondays and Wednesdays. (one word)
روزها: on.

A2 · مقدماتی

on Monday morning, at night, in ten minutes, on time A2

می‌گوییم in the morning، اما با یک روز مشخص on: on Monday morning. at night یعنی شب‌ها به طور کلی، اما in the night یعنی در طول یک شب مشخص. in + مدت یعنی بعد از آن مدت از الان: in ten minutes. on time یعنی سر وقت، دیر نه؛ in time یعنی به‌موقع، زودتر از اینکه دیر شود.

عبارتمثال
in the morningI study in the morning.
on + روز + morningI have a meeting on Friday morning.
at nightOwls hunt at night.
in + مدت (از الان)The train leaves in 10 minutes. (یعنی ۱۰ دقیقهٔ دیگر)
on time / in timeThe bus was on time. (سر ساعتِ برنامه) / We arrived in time for dinner. (به‌موقع، قبل از شام)

on time یعنی سر ساعتِ درست، دیر نه. in time یعنی زودتر از اینکه برای کاری دیر شود.

  • She called me on Sunday evening. on + روز + evening
  • I can't sleep at night. at night
  • I'll be ready in five minutes. in + مدت
  • The plane landed on time. on time (سر وقت)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I have an exam in Monday morning. → ✅ I have an exam on Monday morning. on + روز + morning.
  • ❌ I'll call you after ten minutes. (from now) → ✅ I'll call you in ten minutes. از الان: in. (فارسی‌زبان‌ها «ده دقیقهٔ دیگر» را اشتباهی after ten minutes ترجمه می‌کنند.)
  • ❌ We got there on time to see the start. → ✅ We got there in time to see the start. به‌موقع، زودتر از شروع: in time.

تمرین سطح A2

4. I have a dentist appointment ___ Thursday afternoon.
On + روز + afternoon.
5. Wait here. I'll be back ___ ten minutes.
از الان: in ten minutes.
6. The trains in Japan are always ___ time. (one word)
دیر نه: on time.

B1 · متوسط

for، during یا while؟ B1

for + مدت می‌گوید چه مدت (for two hours). during + اسم می‌گوید کِی، یعنی در طول یک دوره (during the film). while + جمله‌واره (فاعل + فعل): while I was watching the film.

کلمه+مثال
forمدت (چه مدت)I slept for eight hours.
duringاسم (کِی)I fell asleep during the film.
whileفاعل + فعلI fell asleep while I was watching the film.

برای گفتن مدت during نیاورید: I lived there for three years (نه during three years). فارسی «در طول سه سال» را اشتباهی during ترجمه نکنید.

  • We stayed in Paris for a week. چه مدت
  • Please don't talk during the exam. کِی
  • My phone rang while I was driving. while + جمله‌واره
  • It rained during the night. during + اسم

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I studied English during five years. → ✅ I studied English for five years. چه مدت: for.
  • ❌ She called while the meeting. → ✅ She called during the meeting. با اسم: during.
  • ❌ During I was cooking, the phone rang. → ✅ While I was cooking, the phone rang. با جمله‌واره: while.

تمرین سطح B1

7. I've worked here ___ six months.
چه مدت: for.
8. Someone's phone rang ___ the concert.
با اسم (کِی): during.
9. I met my wife ___ I was studying in Montreal. (one word)
با جمله‌واره: while.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

By or until? From… to, within B2

Until / till = up to a time (an action continues): I'll wait until 6. By = not later than (a deadline): Please finish it by Friday. Within = before the end of a period: within 24 hours.

WordMeaningExample
until / tillcontinues up to that timeThe shop is open until 9 pm.
bynot later than (a deadline)Send the report by Monday.
from… to / untilstart and endI work from 9 to 5.
withinbefore the end of a periodWe'll reply within two days.
not… untilit doesn't happen before thenI didn't get home until midnight.

A quick test: by answers “what's the deadline?”; until answers “how long does it continue?”.

By the time + clause: By the time we arrived, the film had started.

  • I'll be at the office until six. (continues)
  • The essay must be submitted by Friday. (deadline)
  • You'll receive an answer within 48 hours. (within)
  • She didn't wake up until 11. (not… until)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Please pay until the end of the month. → ✅ Please pay by the end of the month. (deadline: by)
  • ❌ I'll wait for you by 8 o'clock. → ✅ I'll wait for you until 8 o'clock. (continues: until)
  • ❌ We work from 9 until to 5. → ✅ We work from 9 to 5. (from… to)

تمرین سطح B2

10. You must return the book ___ Monday.
Deadline: by.
11. The museum is open ___ 6 pm.
Continues up to: until.
12. I didn't go to bed ___ 2 am. I was studying. (one word)
Not… until.

C1 · پیشرفته

As of, over the last decade, on arrival C1

Formal and advanced expressions: as of / as from (starting from), over + period (during the whole period), in the run-up to (in the period before), in the course of (during), on + noun / -ing (as soon as), at the turn of the century.

ExpressionExampleMeaning
as of / as fromAs of 1 January, prices will rise.starting from
over + periodPrices rose sharply over the last decade.during the whole period
in the run-up toIn the run-up to the election, …in the period before
in the course ofIn the course of the meeting, three issues came up.during
on + noun / -ingOn arrival, please go to reception. On hearing the news, she cried.when, as soon as
at the turn of the centuryThe house was built at the turn of the century.around 1900 or 2000

On + -ing is formal and means as soon as: On receiving your payment, we will send the goods. (Upon is even more formal.)

  • As of today, the office is closed on Fridays. (starting from)
  • Unemployment has fallen over the past two years. (over a period)
  • On arrival, guests will receive a welcome drink. (on + noun)
  • Prices rose in the run-up to Christmas. (the period before)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ In arrival, please show your ticket. → ✅ On arrival, please show your ticket. (on arrival)
  • ❌ As of from Monday, the new rules apply. → ✅ As of Monday, the new rules apply. (as of or as from)

تمرین سطح C1

13. ___ 1 March, all staff must wear ID badges.
Starting from: as of.
14. ___ arrival at the hotel, please show your passport.
Formal: on arrival.
15. House prices have doubled ___ the last ten years. (one word)
The whole period: over.
