حرف اضافهٔ زمان: in، on و atPrepositions of Time: In, On and At

Use at for exact times, on for days and dates, and in for longer periods like months, years and seasons: at 7 pm, on Friday, in May.

This page also covers for, during, by, until and within. For for and since, see For, Since and Ago.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

At 6 o'clock, on Monday, in July A1

At + times (at 6 o'clock, at night), on + days and dates (on Monday, on 5 May), in + months, years, seasons and parts of the day (in July, in 2025, in the morning).

atonin
times: at 6 o'clock, at 7.30, at noon, at midnightdays: on Monday, on Fridaysmonths: in July
at night, at the weekend (BrE)dates: on 5 May, on my birthdayyears: in 2025
meal times: at lunchtimeon Monday morning, on New Year's Dayseasons: in (the) summer
holidays: at Christmas, at Easterspecial days: on Christmas Day, on my wedding dayparts of the day: in the morning / afternoon / evening
at the moment, at presenton weekdays, on weekends (AmE)decades and centuries: in the 1990s

American English often says on the weekend; British English says at the weekend. Both are correct.

Use no preposition before this, next, last, every, today, tomorrow, yesterday: I'll see you next Friday. (not on next Friday)

  • The film starts at 8 pm. (at + time)
  • My birthday is on 12 March. (on + date)
  • We're going to Spain in August. (in + month)
  • I usually read in the evening. (in + part of the day)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I was born on 1995. → ✅ I was born in 1995. (years: in)
  • ❌ See you in Monday. → ✅ See you on Monday. (days: on)
  • ❌ The meeting is at next Tuesday. → ✅ The meeting is next Tuesday. (no preposition with next)
  • ❌ I go to bed in midnight. → ✅ I go to bed at midnight. (at midnight)

تمرین سطح A1

1. The class starts ___ 9 o'clock.
Exact time: at.
2. I was born ___ 2001.
Years: in.
3. We have English classes ___ Mondays and Wednesdays. (one word)
Days: on.

A2 · مقدماتی

On Monday morning, at night, in ten minutes, on time A2

In the morning, but on + a specific day: on Monday morning. At night (in general), but in the night (during one night). In + period = after that time from now: in ten minutes. On time = not late; in time = early enough.

ExpressionExample
in the morningI study in the morning.
on + day + morningI have a meeting on Friday morning.
at nightOwls hunt at night.
in + period (from now)The train leaves in 10 minutes. (= 10 minutes from now)
on time / in timeThe bus was on time. (at the planned time) / We arrived in time for dinner. (early enough)

On time = at the correct time, not late. In time = early enough for something.

  • She called me on Sunday evening. (on + day + evening)
  • I can't sleep at night. (at night)
  • I'll be ready in five minutes. (in + period)
  • The plane landed on time. (on time)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I have an exam in Monday morning. → ✅ I have an exam on Monday morning. (on + day + morning)
  • ❌ I'll call you after ten minutes. (from now) → ✅ I'll call you in ten minutes. (from now: in)
  • ❌ We got there on time to see the start. → ✅ We got there in time to see the start. (early enough: in time)

تمرین سطح A2

4. I have a dentist appointment ___ Thursday afternoon.
On + day + afternoon.
5. Wait here. I'll be back ___ ten minutes.
From now: in ten minutes.
6. The trains in Japan are always ___ time. (one word)
Not late: on time.

B1 · متوسط

For, during or while? B1

For + a period says how long (for two hours). During + a noun says when, inside a period (during the film). While + a clause (subject + verb): while I was watching the film.

Word+Example
fora period (how long)I slept for eight hours.
duringa noun (when)I fell asleep during the film.
whilesubject + verbI fell asleep while I was watching the film.

Don't use during to say how long: I lived there for three years (not during three years).

  • We stayed in Paris for a week. (how long)
  • Please don't talk during the exam. (when)
  • My phone rang while I was driving. (while + clause)
  • It rained during the night. (during + noun)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I studied English during five years. → ✅ I studied English for five years. (how long: for)
  • ❌ She called while the meeting. → ✅ She called during the meeting. (+ noun: during)
  • ❌ During I was cooking, the phone rang. → ✅ While I was cooking, the phone rang. (+ clause: while)

تمرین سطح B1

7. I've worked here ___ six months.
How long: for.
8. Someone's phone rang ___ the concert.
+ noun (when): during.
9. I met my wife ___ I was studying in Montreal. (one word)
+ clause: while.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

By or until? From… to, within B2

Until / till = up to a time (an action continues): I'll wait until 6. By = not later than (a deadline): Please finish it by Friday. Within = before the end of a period: within 24 hours.

WordMeaningExample
until / tillcontinues up to that timeThe shop is open until 9 pm.
bynot later than (a deadline)Send the report by Monday.
from… to / untilstart and endI work from 9 to 5.
withinbefore the end of a periodWe'll reply within two days.
not… untilit doesn't happen before thenI didn't get home until midnight.

A quick test: by answers “what's the deadline?”; until answers “how long does it continue?”.

By the time + clause: By the time we arrived, the film had started.

  • I'll be at the office until six. (continues)
  • The essay must be submitted by Friday. (deadline)
  • You'll receive an answer within 48 hours. (within)
  • She didn't wake up until 11. (not… until)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Please pay until the end of the month. → ✅ Please pay by the end of the month. (deadline: by)
  • ❌ I'll wait for you by 8 o'clock. → ✅ I'll wait for you until 8 o'clock. (continues: until)
  • ❌ We work from 9 until to 5. → ✅ We work from 9 to 5. (from… to)

تمرین سطح B2

10. You must return the book ___ Monday.
Deadline: by.
11. The museum is open ___ 6 pm.
Continues up to: until.
12. I didn't go to bed ___ 2 am. I was studying. (one word)
Not… until.

C1 · پیشرفته

As of, over the last decade, on arrival C1

Formal and advanced expressions: as of / as from (starting from), over + period (during the whole period), in the run-up to (in the period before), in the course of (during), on + noun / -ing (as soon as), at the turn of the century.

ExpressionExampleMeaning
as of / as fromAs of 1 January, prices will rise.starting from
over + periodPrices rose sharply over the last decade.during the whole period
in the run-up toIn the run-up to the election, …in the period before
in the course ofIn the course of the meeting, three issues came up.during
on + noun / -ingOn arrival, please go to reception. On hearing the news, she cried.when, as soon as
at the turn of the centuryThe house was built at the turn of the century.around 1900 or 2000

On + -ing is formal and means as soon as: On receiving your payment, we will send the goods. (Upon is even more formal.)

  • As of today, the office is closed on Fridays. (starting from)
  • Unemployment has fallen over the past two years. (over a period)
  • On arrival, guests will receive a welcome drink. (on + noun)
  • Prices rose in the run-up to Christmas. (the period before)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ In arrival, please show your ticket. → ✅ On arrival, please show your ticket. (on arrival)
  • ❌ As of from Monday, the new rules apply. → ✅ As of Monday, the new rules apply. (as of or as from)

تمرین سطح C1

13. ___ 1 March, all staff must wear ID badges.
Starting from: as of.
14. ___ arrival at the hotel, please show your passport.
Formal: on arrival.
15. House prices have doubled ___ the last ten years. (one word)
The whole period: over.
