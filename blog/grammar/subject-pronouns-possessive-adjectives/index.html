Subject Pronouns and Possessive Adjectives

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

Subject pronouns (I, you, he, she, it, we, they) replace the subject of a sentence: She is a teacher.

Possessive adjectives (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) go before a noun to show who something belongs to: Her name is Maria.

The full list

Subject pronounPossessive adjectiveExample
ImyI'm Tom. My sister is Kate.
youyourYou're late. Your bus left.
hehisHe's a chef. His food is great.
sheherShe's Spanish. Her family lives in Madrid.
ititsIt's a nice hotel. Its rooms are big.
weourWe live here. Our house is blue.
theytheirThey're students. Their school is near here.

Possessive adjectives don't change for plural nouns: my brother, my brothers.

His = it belongs to a man or boy; her = it belongs to a woman or girl. The word depends on the owner, not the thing: Tom and his mother. Anna and her father.

Its or it's? Their, there or they're?

  • its = belonging to it: The company changed its name.
  • it's = it is / it has: It's cold today.
  • their = belonging to them; there = a place, or there is; they're = they are

Always use a subject

English sentences need a subject, even for weather and time: It's raining. It's 5 o'clock. (not Is raining)

You is the same for one person and for many people. They / their can also refer to one person when we don't know or don't say if they're a man or a woman: Someone left their phone here.

Examples

  1. I'm from Brazil. My name is Sara. (I / my)
  2. He works in a bank. His office is in the city centre. (he / his)
  3. She has a cat. Her cat is black. (she / her)
  4. We love our new flat. (we / our)
  5. They are my neighbours. Their children are very friendly. (they / their)
  6. It's a small town, but its beaches are beautiful. (it / its)
  7. Is this your bag? (your)
  8. My brother and his wife live in Vancouver. (his: the owner is a man)
  9. My sister and her husband are doctors. (her: the owner is a woman)
  10. It's very hot today. (it for the weather)

Common mistakes

❌ Me am a student.

✅ I am a student. (The subject pronoun is I.)

❌ This is Anna and his brother.

✅ This is Anna and her brother. (The owner (Anna) is a woman: her.)

❌ Tom is walking her dog. (It's Tom's dog.)

✅ Tom is walking his dog. (The owner (Tom) is a man: his.)

❌ The company changed it's name.

✅ The company changed its name. (Its = belonging to it. It's = it is.)

❌ Is raining.

✅ It's raining. (English sentences need a subject.)

❌ They're house is big.

✅ Their house is big. (Their = belonging to them. They're = they are.)

❌ I love mys parents.

✅ I love my parents. (Possessive adjectives don't change for plurals.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. ___ am a teacher.
Subject pronoun: I.
2. We love ___ new car.
Before a noun, belonging to us: our.
3. This is my brother. ___ name is Ali.
The owner is a man: His.
4. Anna and ___ husband live in Rome.
The owner (Anna) is a woman: her.
5. The dog is eating ___ food.
Belonging to it: its (no apostrophe).
6. My friends are from Chile. ___ live in Toronto now.
Subject pronoun for my friends: They.
7. Is this ___ phone, Maria?
Before a noun, belonging to you: your.
8. ___ raining. Take an umbrella.
It is raining: It's.
9. Tom is washing ___ car. (It's Tom's car.)
The owner (Tom) is a man: his.
10. My parents sold ___ house last year.
Belonging to them: their.
11. ___ is my sister. She's 20.
Subject pronoun for a woman: She.
12. Choose the correct sentence.
We need a subject: It's (it is) very cold.
13. Mr and Mrs Lee have three children. ___ children go to my school.
Belonging to them: Their.
14. I like Japan. ___ food is delicious.
Belonging to it (Japan): Its.
15. My sister and I share a room. ___ room is small.
Before a noun, belonging to us: Our.
16. You and Sam are late. ___ teacher is waiting.
Before a noun, belonging to you: Your.
