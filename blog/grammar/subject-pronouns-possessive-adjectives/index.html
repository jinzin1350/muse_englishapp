ضمیرهای فاعلی و صفت‌های ملکیSubject Pronouns and Possessive Adjectives

ضمیرهای فاعلی (I, you, he, she, it, we, they) قبل از فعل می‌آیند: She works in a bank. صفت‌های ملکی (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) قبل از اسم می‌آیند: Her bank is near my house.

برای me, him, her, us, them صفحهٔ ضمیرهای مفعولی و برای mine, yours, hers صفحهٔ ضمیرهای ملکی را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2

A1 · مبتدی

ضمیرهای فاعلی: I, you, he, she, it, we, they A1

ضمیر فاعلی را قبل از فعل بیاورید: I, you, he, she, it, we, they. جملهٔ انگلیسی حتماً فاعل لازم دارد: It's raining. (نه Is raining)

مفردجمع
Iwe
youyou
he (مرد / پسر)، she (زن / دختر)، it (شیء / حیوان)they (آدم‌ها یا چیزها)

انگلیسی همیشه فاعل لازم دارد، حتی وقتی در زبان‌های دیگر فعل نشان می‌دهد کیست (مثل فارسی: «خسته‌ام»): I am tired. (نه Am tired). It is cold today.

فاعل را با ضمیر تکرار نکنید: My brother lives in Ottawa. (نه My brother he lives…)

I همیشه با حرف بزرگ نوشته می‌شود. (در فارسی برای مرد و زن هر دو «او» می‌گوییم، اما در انگلیسی he برای مرد و she برای زن است؛ این را قاطی نکنید.)

  • I'm a student. (I)
  • She is my sister. (she)
  • They live in Toronto. (they)
  • It's very hot today. it (برای هوا)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ My father he is a doctor. → ✅ My father is a doctor. فاعل را تکرار نکنید
  • ❌ Is very expensive. → ✅ It is very expensive. انگلیسی فاعل لازم دارد
  • ❌ i am from Iran. → ✅ I am from Iran. I با حرف بزرگ

صفت‌های ملکی: my, your, his, her, its, our, their A1

صفت ملکی را قبل از اسم بیاورید تا نشان بدهید چیزی مالِ کیست: my bag, your phone, his car, her house, its tail, our school, their children.

ضمیر فاعلیصفت ملکی
Imy
youyour
hehis
sheher
itits
weour
theytheir

صفت ملکی با صاحب چیز هماهنگ است، نه با خودِ آن چیز: Ali loves his mother. (علی مرد است ← his). Sara loves her father. (سارا زن است ← her).

صفت ملکی برای اسم جمع تغییر نمی‌کند: my book, my books.

  • This is my brother. (I → my)
  • Sara is in her room. (she → her)
  • Reza lives with his parents. (he → his)
  • The dog is eating its food. (it → its)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Maryam is visiting his grandmother. → ✅ Maryam is visiting her grandmother. صاحبش زن است: her
  • ❌ They love they children. → ✅ They love their children. they ← their
  • ❌ Is this you bag? → ✅ Is this your bag? you ← your

تمرین سطح A1

1. This is my mother. ___ is a teacher.
یک زن: she.
2. Ali and I are friends. ___ go to the same school.
Ali and I یعنی we.
3. Where's my phone? ___ was here a minute ago. (one word)
یک شیء: it.
4. Ali has a question about his homework, so he's talking to ___ teacher.
علی مرد است ← his.
5. Mina and Leila are with ___ mother. They're sisters.
دو نفر ← their.
6. I love ___ new phone. I bought it yesterday, and it's great! (one word: a possessive)
I ← my.

A2 · مقدماتی

its یا it's؟ their، there یا they're؟ your یا you're؟ A2

صفت ملکی را با شکل کوتاه قاطی نکنید: its (ملکی) / it's (یعنی it is / it has)؛ their (ملکی) / there (مکان) / they're (یعنی they are)؛ your / you're (یعنی you are).

ملکیهم‌صدا با آنمعنی
itsit'sit is / it has
theirthere, they'reمکان / they are
youryou'reyou are
whosewho'swho is / who has

صفت ملکی هرگز آپاستروف ندارد: its, your, their, whose.

یک آزمون سریع: آیا می‌توانید به جایش it is, they are, you are, who is بگویید؟ پس شکلِ آپاستروف‌دار را بنویسید.

  • The tree lost its leaves. ملکی
  • It's a beautiful day. it is
  • They're at their friend's house over there. they are / ملکی / مکان
  • You're going to love your new room. you are / ملکی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The dog hurt it's paw. → ✅ The dog hurt its paw. ملکی: its
  • ❌ Their going to be late. → ✅ They're going to be late. they are: they're
  • ❌ Is this you're coat? → ✅ Is this your coat? ملکی: your

تمرین سطح A2

7. ___ very cold today. Wear a coat.
It is ← it's.
8. My neighbours are on holiday. ___ house is empty.
ملکی + اسم: their.
9. ___ (you / are) late again! (one word)
You are ← you're.

B1 · متوسط

it برای هوا، زمان و فاصله؛ it به عنوان فاعلِ خالی B1

it را به عنوان فاعلِ خالی برای هوا، ساعت، تاریخ و فاصله (It's raining. It's 5 o'clock. It's 10 km to the city.)، قبل از صفت + to / that (It's important to sleep well) و با it takes + زمان بیاورید.

کاربردمثال
هواIt's cold. It's snowing.
ساعت و تاریخIt's half past six. It's Monday.
فاصلهIt's about 5 km from here to the beach.
it + صفت + to / thatIt's easy to learn. It's strange that he didn't call.
it takes + زمانIt takes an hour to get there.

انگلیسی فاعل لازم دارد، حتی وقتی «انجام‌دهندهٔ» واقعی‌ای در کار نیست: It is raining (نه Is raining و نه Rains).

فاعلِ طولانیِ مصدری را با it به آخر جمله ببرید: To learn a language is hard ← It's hard to learn a language. (طبیعی‌تر)

  • It's windy today. هوا
  • It's ten past nine. ساعت
  • How far is it to the airport? فاصله
  • It's nice to meet you. it + صفت + to

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Is very hot today. → ✅ It's very hot today. هوا: it
  • ❌ This is important to drink water. → ✅ It's important to drink water. it + صفت + to
  • ❌ It needs two hours to get there. → ✅ It takes two hours to get there. it takes + زمان

تمرین سطح B1

10. ___ snowing! Let's go outside.
هوا: it.
11. ___ about 20 minutes to walk to the station.
زمانِ لازم: it takes.
12. What day is ___ today? ~ It's Friday. (one word)
تاریخ و روز: it.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Singular they, generic you and one B2

Use they / their for one person when you don't know, or don't want to say, if the person is male or female: Someone left their phone. Use you (informal) or one (very formal) for people in general: You never know.

UseExample
unknown personSomeone called, but they didn't leave a message.
after everyone, anyone, eachEveryone should bring their own lunch.
a person who uses theyAlex is my colleague. They're from Ottawa.
generic youYou can't smoke here. You never know what will happen.
generic one (formal)One should always be polite.

Singular they uses a plural verb: Someone left their bag. They are probably looking for it.

He or she is also possible in formal writing, but singular they is now standard in most style guides.

  • If a student is late, they must sign in at reception. (unknown person)
  • Everybody did their best. (after everybody)
  • You have to be 18 to vote here. (generic you)
  • One must respect the law. (generic one (formal))

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ If a customer complains, they is always right. → ✅ If a customer complains, they are always right. (singular they + plural verb)
  • ❌ Somebody left there bag. → ✅ Somebody left their bag. (possessive: their)

تمرین سطح B2

13. Someone left ___ umbrella on the bus.
Unknown person: their.
14. If anyone calls, tell ___ I'll be back at five.
Unknown person, object: them.
15. A good teacher always listens to ___ students. (one word)
Singular they: their (or his / her).
