Hedging and Distancing (it would appear that...)

Level: C2 · Free grammar guide

Hedging means making a statement less direct or less certain, so you don't claim more than you can prove: Social media causes anxiety. → The data suggest that social media may contribute to anxiety.

Distancing means showing that a claim comes from someone else, not you: The CEO has allegedly resigned. Both are essential in academic, professional and diplomatic English.

Hedging tools

ToolExamples
seem / appear (+ to)There seems to be a problem. The results appear to show a link.
it would seem / appear thatIt would appear that the problem has been solved.
modalsThis may / might / could be due to a lack of funding.
tend toPeople tend to spend more in December.
cautious verbsThe data suggest / indicate that…
adverbsarguably, possibly, probably, apparently, relatively, somewhat
phrasesto some extent, in general, there is some evidence that, it is possible that
it could be argued thatIt could be argued that the plan is too ambitious.

Would makes a hedge even softer: This would seem to be the best option. I would suggest that…

Distancing tools

  • apparently (= I heard / read this): Apparently, the shop is closing.
  • allegedly / reportedly (news): He allegedly stole the money.
  • according to: According to the report, sales fell.
  • passive reporting: It is believed that… He is said to be… See Passive Reporting Structures.

Examples

  1. It would appear that the problem has been solved. (very cautious)
  2. The results appear to suggest a link between diet and mood. (appear to)
  3. There seems to be a misunderstanding. (seem to)
  4. People tend to underestimate how long tasks take. (tend to)
  5. This may be due to a lack of funding. (modal)
  6. Arguably, the new policy has been a success. (arguably)
  7. To some extent, the critics are right. (limiting phrase)
  8. There is some evidence that the drug may help. (some evidence)
  9. The CEO has allegedly resigned. (distancing)
  10. Apparently, the meeting has been moved to Friday. (distancing)

Common mistakes

❌ It would appears that he left.

✅ It would appear that he left. (After would, use the base form.)

❌ The results are appearing to show a link.

✅ The results appear to show a link. (Appear and seem are not usually continuous.)

❌ People are tending to buy more online.

✅ People tend to buy more online. (Tend to is normally used in the simple form.)

❌ There seem to be a problem.

✅ There seems to be a problem. (Singular noun: seems.)

❌ To some extend, I agree.

✅ To some extent, I agree. (The noun is extent.)

❌ This proves that the drug works. (one small study)

✅ This suggests that the drug may work. (Don't claim more than the evidence shows.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. It would ___ that the problem has been solved.
would + base form: It would appear that…
2. The results ___ to suggest a link between diet and mood.
Plural subject + appear to.
3. People ___ to spend more money in December.
General tendency: people tend to.
4. ___, the new policy has been a success.
Hedging adverb: Arguably.
5. There is ___ evidence that the drug may help.
Cautious claim: there is some evidence.
6. The CEO has ___ resigned, although the company hasn't confirmed it.
Distancing adverb: allegedly.
7. It could ___ that the plan is too ambitious.
It could be argued that…
8. This may ___ due to a lack of funding.
may + base form: may be.
9. The new design is ___ more efficient than the old one.
Softening adverb before a comparative: somewhat more efficient.
10. To some ___, the critics are right.
Fixed phrase: to some extent.
11. Choose the most cautious (hedged) statement.
suggest + may contribute: The data suggest that social media may contribute to anxiety.
12. ___, the shop is closing next month. I heard it from a friend.
Distancing (I heard it): Apparently.
13. There ___ to be a misunderstanding.
Singular (a misunderstanding): there seems to be.
14. It is ___ that prices will fall next year, but not certain.
It is possible that…
15. The study's findings ___ be treated with caution.
should + base form (ought needs to).
16. This would ___ to be the best option.
would + base form: would seem to be.
