محتاط‌گویی و فاصله گرفتن از ادعاHedging and Distancing

B2 · C1 · C2

Hedging means making a statement less certain or less direct, so you don't claim more than you can prove: Coffee causes heart disease. → Coffee may contribute to heart disease. Distancing shows the idea comes from someone else: The minister is said to be resigning.

Hedging is essential in academic writing, reports and polite business English. For It is said that… and is thought to…, see also Passive Reporting Structures.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

May, might, could, tend to, seem to B2

DirectHedged
This is the reason.This may / might / could be the reason.
Young people spend more online.Young people tend to spend more online.
The plan works.The plan seems / appears to work.
Prices will rise.Prices are likely to rise.

  • Stress may cause sleep problems. (may)
  • Older drivers tend to drive more slowly. (tend to)
  • The new medicine appears to be effective. (appear to)
  • Costs are likely to increase next year. (likely to)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Young people tend spend more time online. → ✅ Young people tend to spend more time online. (tend to + verb)
  • ❌ The results seem showing an improvement. → ✅ The results seem to show an improvement. (seem to + verb)

Probably, possibly, largely, to some extent B2

Word / phraseExample
probably / possibly / perhapsThis is probably the best solution.
apparentlyApparently, the meeting has been cancelled. (I heard this)
arguablyShe is arguably the best player in the world. (you could say this)
generally / usuallyThe method is generally reliable.
largely / mainlyThe increase is largely due to tourism.
relatively / fairlyThe test is relatively easy.
to some extent / in some casesTo some extent, the plan was successful.

  • The decline is largely due to lower prices. (largely)
  • This is arguably the most important discovery of the century. (arguably)
  • To some extent, the results support the theory. (to some extent)
  • Apparently, she's moving to Spain. (apparently)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The results are probable correct. → ✅ The results are probably correct. (adverb, not adjective: probably)
  • ❌ In some extent, I agree. → ✅ To some extent, I agree. (to some extent)

تمرین سطح B2

1. (Cautious claim) Lack of sleep ___ affect your memory, but more research is needed.
Hedged: may.
2. People in cities ___ to have smaller families.
Tend to + verb.
3. The new policy ___ (appear) to be working.
Appear to + verb.
4. The increase in sales is ___ due to the new advertising campaign.
Largely = mainly.
5. She is ___ the greatest writer of her generation. Many critics think so.
Arguably = it could be argued.
6. ___ some extent, I agree with you, but not completely. (one word)
To some extent.

C1 · پیشرفته

It would appear that, it could be argued that C1

PatternExample
It would appear / seem thatIt would appear that the data is incomplete.
It seems likely / unlikely thatIt seems likely that prices will rise.
It could be argued thatIt could be argued that the law is unfair.
There is some / little evidence thatThere is some evidence that diet affects mood.
This suggests / indicates thatThis suggests that the method works.
It is possible / probable thatIt is possible that the results are wrong.

  • It would appear that there has been a mistake. (cautious)
  • It could be argued that social media does more harm than good. (presenting an argument)
  • There is little evidence that the treatment works. (little evidence)
  • These findings suggest that more research is needed. (suggest)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ It would appears that the plan failed. → ✅ It would appear that the plan failed. (would + base verb)
  • ❌ It could argue that the plan is too expensive. → ✅ It could be argued that the plan is too expensive. (passive: could be argued)

تمرین سطح C1

7. It ___ that the experiment was not carried out correctly.
It would appear that…
8. It could be ___ that the government acted too slowly.
It could be argued that…
9. There is some ___ that exercise improves memory. (one word)
There is some evidence that…

C2 · تسلط کامل

Is said to have, would seem to suggest, reportedly C2

PatternExample
is said / believed / thought to + verbThe company is thought to be in trouble.
is said to have + past participleHe is said to have left the country.
reportedly / allegedly / supposedlyThe minister allegedly lied.
according to + sourceAccording to the report, crime has fallen.
combined hedgesThe data would seem to suggest a link.

  • The painting is believed to be worth millions. (is believed to be)
  • She is said to have written the letter herself. (is said to have)
  • The president reportedly refused to comment. (reportedly)
  • The findings would seem to suggest that diet plays a role. (combined hedges)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ According to me, the plan is bad. → ✅ In my opinion, the plan is bad. (according to + source, not me)
  • ❌ He is said to leave the country last week. → ✅ He is said to have left the country last week. (past: to have + past participle)

تمرین سطح C2

10. The thief is believed ___ the country last night.
Past: to have left.
11. The CEO ___ resigned, although the company hasn't confirmed it.
Reportedly = according to reports.
12. ___ to the latest figures, unemployment has fallen. (one word)
According to + source.
