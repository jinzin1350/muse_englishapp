اسم و حرف اضافهNouns and Prepositions

بعد از خیلی از اسم‌ها یک حرف اضافهٔ ثابت می‌آید: the reason for the delay, the cause of the fire, a solution to the problem. اسم و حرف اضافه‌اش را با هم یاد بگیرید.

عبارت‌های ثابتِ حرف اضافه + اسم هم داریم: on purpose, by mistake, in advance. برای حرف اضافه بعد از فعل و صفت صفحه‌های فعل‌ها و حرف اضافه و صفت‌ها و حرف اضافه را ببینید.

B1 · B2 · C1

B1 · متوسط

the reason for, a solution to, a problem with B1

جفت‌های رایج: reason for، cause of، solution / answer / key to، invitation to، increase / rise in، demand / need for، problem with، relationship with، difference between، interest in، damage to، effect on.

حرف اضافهاسم‌هامثال
forreason, demand, need, request, chequeWhat's the reason for the delay?
ofcause, example, advantage, pictureThe cause of the fire is unknown.
tosolution, answer, key, invitation, damage, replyIs there a solution to this problem?
inincrease, rise, fall, decrease, interestThere's been a rise in prices.
withproblem, relationship, contactI have a problem with my laptop.
betweendifference, relationship (بین دو چیز)What's the difference between them?
oneffect, tax, informationStress has an effect on your health.

حرف اضافهٔ اسم اغلب با فعلش فرق دارد: They solved the problem (بدون حرف اضافه) / a solution to the problem.

  • Nobody knows the reason for his decision. reason for (دلیلِ)
  • Hard work is the key to success. key to (کلیدِ)
  • There's a big demand for nurses. demand for (تقاضا برای)
  • What's the difference between a crocodile and an alligator? difference between (فرقِ … با …)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ What's the reason of the delay? → ✅ What's the reason for the delay? reason for. (فارسی می‌گوید «دلیلِ تأخیر»، ولی انگلیسی for، نه of.)
  • ❌ Hard work is the key of success. → ✅ Hard work is the key to success. key to.
  • ❌ I have a problem in my car. → ✅ I have a problem with my car. problem with.

on purpose, by mistake, in advance, on holiday B1

عبارت‌های ثابتِ حرف اضافه + اسم: on purpose (عمداً)، by mistake / accident / chance (اشتباهی / تصادفی)، in advance (از قبل)، in a hurry (با عجله)، on holiday / business (در تعطیلات / سفر کاری)، for sale، on sale، in danger، at risk، out of order (خراب)، out of date (تاریخ‌گذشته)، in charge of (مسئولِ).

حرف اضافهعبارت‌هامثال
onon purpose, on holiday, on business, on sale, on fire, on footHe broke it on purpose.
byby mistake, by accident, by chance, by car, by handI took your bag by mistake.
inin advance, in a hurry, in danger, in love, in charge of, in tearsBook your tickets in advance.
atat risk, at first, at least, at the momentAt first, I didn't like it.
out ofout of order, out of date, out of work, out of stockThe lift is out of order.
forfor sale, for rent, for a changeIs this house for sale?

for sale یعنی کسی می‌خواهد آن را بفروشد. on sale یعنی در مغازه‌ها موجود است، یا (به‌خصوص در انگلیسی آمریکایی) با تخفیف.

  • Sorry, I deleted your email by mistake. by mistake (اشتباهی)
  • We met by chance at the airport. by chance (تصادفی)
  • Please pay in advance. in advance (از قبل)
  • The milk is out of date. out of date (تاریخ‌گذشته)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She did it by purpose. → ✅ She did it on purpose. on purpose.
  • ❌ I'm in holiday next week. → ✅ I'm on holiday next week. on holiday.
  • ❌ He's on a hurry. → ✅ He's in a hurry. in a hurry.

تمرین سطح B1

1. What was the reason ___ your decision?
Reason for.
2. Did you get an invitation ___ the wedding?
Invitation to.
3. Smoking is the main cause ___ lung cancer. (one word)
Cause of.
4. I'm sorry, I didn't do it ___ purpose.
On purpose یعنی عمداً.
5. I took your umbrella ___ mistake. I thought it was mine.
By mistake.
6. Sorry, I can't talk now. I'm ___ a hurry. (one word)
In a hurry.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

An increase in or of? Attitude to, advantage over B2

Increase / rise / fall in + the thing that changes (a rise in prices), but of + the amount (a rise of 5%). Other pairs: attitude to / towards, advantage of / over, respect for, connection between / with, in contrast to / with.

PatternExample
increase / rise / fall / drop in + thingThere was a sharp fall in sales.
increase / rise / fall / drop of + amountThere was a fall of 20%.
attitude to / towardsHer attitude to work has improved.
the advantage of something / an advantage over someonethe advantages of online learning; an advantage over other players
respect forHe has a lot of respect for his teachers.
connection between two things / with somethingthe connection between diet and health
in contrast to / withIn contrast to last year, sales are up.

You can use both in one phrase: an increase of 10% in the price of fuel.

  • There's been a big rise in house prices. (rise in + thing)
  • Prices went up by 3%: a rise of 3%. (rise of + amount)
  • Speaking two languages gives you an advantage over other candidates. (advantage over)
  • Children learn respect for others at home. (respect for)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ There was an increase of unemployment. → ✅ There was an increase in unemployment. (in + the thing)
  • ❌ Salaries rose. There was a rise in 4%. → ✅ Salaries rose. There was a rise of 4%. (of + the amount)

تمرین سطح B2

7. Last year there was a 10% fall ___ the number of car accidents.
The thing that changes: fall in.
8. The company reported an increase ___ 15% in profits.
The amount: increase of.
9. Young people often have a different attitude ___ money. (one word)
Attitude to / towards.

C1 · پیشرفته

Impact on, insight into, emphasis on: formal pairs C1

Formal and academic pairs: impact / emphasis / influence on, insight into, approach / contribution / commitment / access / threat / alternative / exposure to, implications for, preference for; and phrases like in the light of, with regard to, on behalf of, in terms of.

PrepositionNounsExample
onimpact, emphasis, influence, restriction, banThe course puts emphasis on speaking.
intoinsight, research, inquiryThe book gives an insight into village life.
toapproach, contribution, commitment, access, threat, alternative, exposureIs there an alternative to plastic?
forimplications, preference, responsibility, scopeWhat are the implications for students?
ofawareness, lack, knowledgeThere is a lack of evidence.

Useful formal phrases: in the light of (because of), with regard to (about), on behalf of (representing), in terms of (when we talk about), by means of (using).

  • The internet has had a huge impact on education. (impact on)
  • Her research gives new insight into the disease. (insight into)
  • We need a fresh approach to the problem. (approach to)
  • I'm writing on behalf of my company. (on behalf of)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The new law will have a big impact to small businesses. → ✅ The new law will have a big impact on small businesses. (impact on)
  • ❌ Everyone should have access for clean water. → ✅ Everyone should have access to clean water. (access to)

تمرین سطح C1

10. Climate change is a serious threat ___ wildlife.
Threat to.
11. The film gives us a fascinating insight ___ the life of a refugee.
Insight into.
12. Social media has a big influence ___ young people's opinions. (one word)
Influence on.
