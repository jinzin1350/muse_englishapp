Will and Shall

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

Will + base verb is one of the main ways to talk about the future in English: I'll call you tomorrow. It will be sunny at the weekend.

But will isn't the only future form, and it isn't always the right one. For plans, see Be Going To, and for choosing, see Will vs Going To.

A2 · Elementary

Will and won't: form and future facts A2

FormExample
Positivesubject + will ('ll) + base verbI'll be home at six.
Negativesubject + won't (will not) + base verbIt won't rain tomorrow.
QuestionWill + subject + base verb?Will you be at the party?
Short answersYes, … will. / No, … won't.Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

  • I'll be 25 next month. (future fact)
  • The shop won't be open on Sunday. (negative)
  • Will you be at home this evening? ~ Yes, I will. (question + short answer)
  • My parents will arrive at ten. (future event)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ She will comes tomorrow. → ✅ She will come tomorrow. (will + base verb)
  • ❌ I will to help you. → ✅ I will help you. (no to after will)
  • ❌ Will you to be there? → ✅ Will you be there? (will + base verb)

Instant decisions, offers, promises and requests A2

UseExample
a decision nowI'm cold. I'll close the window.
an offerYour bags look heavy. I'll carry one.
a promiseI won't tell anyone. I promise.
a requestWill you help me with this?
a refusalI won't do it!

  • I'll have the chicken, please. (deciding now (in a restaurant))
  • Don't worry, I'll pick you up from the station. (offer)
  • I'll always love you. (promise)
  • Will you pass me the salt? (request)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The phone's ringing. ~ I answer it. → ✅ The phone's ringing. ~ I'll answer it. (deciding now: will)
  • ❌ I promise I call you tomorrow. → ✅ I promise I'll call you tomorrow. (promise: will)

Practice: A2

1. Next year my daughter ___ ten.
Will + base verb.
2. It ___ rain tomorrow. The forecast says it'll be sunny.
Negative: won't.
3. ___ (will / you / be) at work on Monday?
Question: Will you be…?
4. Someone's at the door. ~ ___ open it.
A decision at the moment of speaking: I'll.
5. I'm thirsty. ~ ___ get you some water.
An offer: I'll.
6. I promise I ___ (will / not / be) late again.
A promise: won't be.

B1 · Intermediate

Predictions: I think it will…, probably B1

  • I think you'll pass the exam. (opinion)
  • I'm sure they'll enjoy the trip. (confident)
  • He'll probably be late again. (probably after will)
  • I don't think she'll like it. (negative prediction)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I think it won't snow. → ✅ I don't think it will snow. (I don't think … will)
  • ❌ She will be probably late. → ✅ She'll probably be late. (probably after will)
  • ❌ He won't probably come. → ✅ He probably won't come. (probably before won't)

Shall: offers, suggestions and formal rules B1

  • Shall I turn the heating on? (offer)
  • Shall we meet at the café at six? (suggestion)
  • What shall we do this weekend? (asking for ideas)
  • Payment shall be made within 30 days. (formal rule)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Shall I to help you? → ✅ Shall I help you? (shall + base verb)
  • ❌ Shall you come with us? (a suggestion) → ✅ Will you come with us? / Shall we go together? (shall is used with I / we)

Practice: B1

7. I don't think the bus ___ on time.
I don't think … will.
8. Choose the correct word order.
Will + probably + verb.
9. Don't worry about the interview. I'm sure you ___ (will / do) really well.
A confident prediction: will do.
10. It's dark. ___ I turn on the light?
An offer: Shall I…?
11. ___ we order a pizza tonight?
A suggestion: Shall we…?
12. In the contract: The landlord ___ (shall / repair) any damage within 14 days.
Formal obligation: shall repair.

B2 · Upper-intermediate

Won't for refusals: The door won't open B2

  • My phone won't charge. (it doesn't work)
  • The baby won't stop crying. (unwilling / impossible to stop)
  • They won't let me in without a ticket. (refuse)
  • The car wouldn't start yesterday. (past refusal)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ The window doesn't will open. → ✅ The window won't open. (won't = doesn't work)
  • ❌ Yesterday the car won't start. → ✅ Yesterday the car wouldn't start. (past: wouldn't)

Practice: B2

13. I've tried everything, but this jar ___ open.
Won't = doesn't work (even when I try).
14. Yesterday my son ___ do his homework. We argued for an hour.
Past refusal: wouldn't.
15. My computer ___ (will / not / turn) on. Can you help?
Won't = doesn't work.

C1 · Advanced

Will for habits and characteristic behaviour C1

  • My grandmother will spend hours in the garden. (typical behaviour)
  • She will keep interrupting me! (annoying (stressed will))
  • Children will always copy their parents. (general truth)
  • Water will freeze at zero degrees. (typical, general)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ He'll always leaves the door open. → ✅ He'll always leave the door open. (will + base verb)

Practice: C1

16. My dog ___ sit by the door for hours waiting for me.
Typical behaviour: will.
17. What does He WILL leave his dirty dishes everywhere! express?
Stressed will = annoying habit.
18. A good doctor ___ (will / always / listen) to the patient.
General characteristic: will always listen.
