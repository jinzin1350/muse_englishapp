خانه / گرامر / will و shall

will و shallWill and Shall

will + شکل سادهٔ فعل یکی از راه‌های اصلی صحبت دربارهٔ آینده در انگلیسی است: I'll call you tomorrow. It will be sunny at the weekend.

اما will تنها شکل آینده نیست و همیشه هم درست‌ترین انتخاب نیست. برای برنامه‌ها صفحهٔ be going to و برای انتخاب بین این دو صفحهٔ will یا going to را ببینید.

A2 · مقدماتی

will و won't: ساختار و واقعیت‌های آینده A2

will / won't + شکل سادهٔ فعل، برای همهٔ اشخاص یکسان: I'll be 30 next year. She won't be here tomorrow.

ساختارمثال
مثبتفاعل + will ('ll) + شکل سادهٔ فعلI'll be home at six.
منفیفاعل + won't (will not) + شکل سادهٔ فعلIt won't rain tomorrow.
سؤالیWill + فاعل + شکل سادهٔ فعل؟Will you be at the party?
جواب کوتاهYes, … will. / No, … won't.Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

will برای همهٔ اشخاص یکی است و بعدش شکل سادهٔ فعل بدون to می‌آید: She will come. نه She wills come و نه will to come.

آن را برای واقعیت‌های آینده و چیزهایی که از آن‌ها مطمئنیم به کار می‌بریم: Tomorrow will be Monday. The sun will set at 6.45.

  • I'll be 25 next month. واقعیتِ آینده
  • The shop won't be open on Sunday. منفی
  • Will you be at home this evening? ~ Yes, I will. سؤال + جواب کوتاه
  • My parents will arrive at ten. اتفاق آینده

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She will comes tomorrow. → ✅ She will come tomorrow. will + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ I will to help you. → ✅ I will help you. بعد از will حرف to نمی‌آید
  • ❌ Will you to be there? → ✅ Will you be there? will + شکل سادهٔ فعل

تصمیم لحظه‌ای، پیشنهاد کمک، قول و درخواست A2

will را وقتی بیاورید که همان لحظهٔ صحبت تصمیم می‌گیرید (I'll have the soup)، و برای پیشنهاد کمک (I'll help)، قول (I'll call you) و درخواست (Will you…?).

کاربردمثال
تصمیمِ همین لحظهI'm cold. I'll close the window.
پیشنهاد کمکYour bags look heavy. I'll carry one.
قولI won't tell anyone. I promise.
درخواستWill you help me with this?
امتناعI won't do it!

وقتی همان لحظهٔ صحبت تصمیمی می‌گیریم، will می‌آوریم، نه حال ساده: The phone's ringing. ~ I'll get it. نه I get it.

اگر تصمیم از قبل گرفته شده، be going to بیاورید: I'm going to paint the kitchen this weekend. (هفتهٔ پیش تصمیمش را گرفتم.) صفحهٔ will یا going to را ببینید.

  • I'll have the chicken, please. تصمیمِ همین لحظه (در رستوران)
  • Don't worry, I'll pick you up from the station. پیشنهاد کمک
  • I'll always love you. قول
  • Will you pass me the salt? درخواست

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The phone's ringing. ~ I answer it. → ✅ The phone's ringing. ~ I'll answer it. تصمیمِ همین لحظه: will
  • ❌ I promise I call you tomorrow. → ✅ I promise I'll call you tomorrow. قول: will

تمرین سطح A2

1. Next year my daughter ___ ten.
will + شکل سادهٔ فعل.
2. It ___ rain tomorrow. The forecast says it'll be sunny.
منفی: won't.
3. ___ (will / you / be) at work on Monday?
سؤالی: Will you be…?
4. Someone's at the door. ~ ___ open it.
تصمیمِ همان لحظه: I'll.
5. I'm thirsty. ~ ___ get you some water.
پیشنهاد کمک: I'll.
6. I promise I ___ (will / not / be) late again.
قول: won't be.

B1 · متوسط

پیش‌بینی: I think it will…، probably B1

will را برای پیش‌بینی بر اساس نظر یا تجربه بیاورید، اغلب با I think, I'm sure, probably, I expect: I think it'll be a great party.

اغلب با will می‌گوییم دربارهٔ آینده چه فکر یا باوری داریم: I think Brazil will win the World Cup. You'll love this film.

عبارت‌های رایج: I think / I don't think, I'm sure, I expect, I hope, probably, definitely, perhaps.

ترتیب کلمه‌ها: probably / definitely بعد از will اما قبل از won't می‌آیند: She'll probably be late. She probably won't come.

برای پیش‌بینی منفی بگویید I don't think … will، نه I think … won't: I don't think it will rain. (طبیعی‌تر از I think it won't rain)

  • I think you'll pass the exam. نظر
  • I'm sure they'll enjoy the trip. با اطمینان
  • He'll probably be late again. probably بعد از will
  • I don't think she'll like it. پیش‌بینی منفی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I think it won't snow. → ✅ I don't think it will snow. I don't think … will
  • ❌ She will be probably late. → ✅ She'll probably be late. probably بعد از will
  • ❌ He won't probably come. → ✅ He probably won't come. probably قبل از won't

shall: پیشنهاد کمک، پیشنهاد کار و قانون رسمی B1

Shall I / shall we + شکل سادهٔ فعل برای پیشنهاد کمک و پیشنهاد کاری (بیشتر در انگلیسی بریتانیایی). در متن‌های رسمی shall یعنی «باید»: The tenant shall pay the rent monthly.

Shall I…? پیشنهاد کمک است و Shall we…? پیشنهاد انجام کاری با هم: Shall I carry your bag? Shall we go for a walk? برای نظر خواستن هم به کار می‌رود: What shall I do?

shall با I / we برای آینده (I shall be there) در انگلیسی بریتانیایی رسمی و قدیمی است. بیشتر مردم I'll یا I will می‌گویند.

در متن‌های حقوقی و رسمی، shall اجبار را نشان می‌دهد: The company shall provide training to all staff.

در انگلیسی آمریکایی shall در گفت‌وگو کم به کار می‌رود. آمریکایی‌ها برای پیشنهاد کمک معمولاً Should I…? یا Do you want me to…? و برای پیشنهاد کار Should we…? یا Let's… می‌گویند.

  • Shall I turn the heating on? پیشنهاد کمک
  • Shall we meet at the café at six? پیشنهاد کار
  • What shall we do this weekend? نظر خواستن
  • Payment shall be made within 30 days. قانون رسمی

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Shall I to help you? → ✅ Shall I help you? shall + شکل سادهٔ فعل
  • ❌ Shall you come with us? (a suggestion) → ✅ Will you come with us? / Shall we go together? shall با I / we می‌آید

تمرین سطح B1

7. I don't think the bus ___ on time.
I don't think … will.
8. ترتیب درست کلمه‌ها را انتخاب کنید.
will + probably + فعل.
9. Don't worry about the interview. I'm sure you ___ (will / do) really well.
پیش‌بینی با اطمینان: will do.
10. It's dark. ___ I turn on the light?
پیشنهاد کمک: Shall I…?
11. ___ we order a pizza tonight?
پیشنهاد کار: Shall we…?
12. In the contract: The landlord ___ (shall / repair) any damage within 14 days.
اجبار رسمی: shall repair.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Won't for refusals: The door won't open B2

won't = refuses to (people) or doesn't work (things): He won't listen to me. The car won't start.

Won't can mean refuse to or be unwilling to: My son won't eat vegetables. She won't tell me what happened.

With things, won't means doesn't work even when we try: The door won't open. My laptop won't turn on.

For the past, use wouldn't: He wouldn't listen. The car wouldn't start this morning.

  • My phone won't charge. (it doesn't work)
  • The baby won't stop crying. (unwilling / impossible to stop)
  • They won't let me in without a ticket. (refuse)
  • The car wouldn't start yesterday. (past refusal)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ The window doesn't will open. → ✅ The window won't open. (won't = doesn't work)
  • ❌ Yesterday the car won't start. → ✅ Yesterday the car wouldn't start. (past: wouldn't)

تمرین سطح B2

13. I've tried everything, but this jar ___ open.
Won't = doesn't work (even when I try).
14. Yesterday my son ___ do his homework. We argued for an hour.
Past refusal: wouldn't.
15. My computer ___ (will / not / turn) on. Can you help?
Won't = doesn't work.

C1 · پیشرفته

Will for habits and characteristic behaviour C1

will can describe typical behaviour or habits (She'll sit there for hours) and, when stressed, annoying insistence (He will leave his socks on the floor!).

Will + base verb can describe what someone or something typically does: My cat will sleep anywhere. Boys will be boys. (= that's how they are)

When will is stressed in speech (/wɪl/, not 'll), it shows that the habit is annoying: He WILL play his music so loud!

In the past, use would for the same meanings: My grandfather would tell us stories every night. See Used To.

The habit use is also common in general statements: Oil will float on water. A good teacher will listen to students.

  • My grandmother will spend hours in the garden. (typical behaviour)
  • She will keep interrupting me! (annoying (stressed will))
  • Children will always copy their parents. (general truth)
  • Water will freeze at zero degrees. (typical, general)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He'll always leaves the door open. → ✅ He'll always leave the door open. (will + base verb)

تمرین سطح C1

16. My dog ___ sit by the door for hours waiting for me.
Typical behaviour: will.
17. What does He WILL leave his dirty dishes everywhere! express?
Stressed will = annoying habit.
18. A good doctor ___ (will / always / listen) to the patient.
General characteristic: will always listen.
