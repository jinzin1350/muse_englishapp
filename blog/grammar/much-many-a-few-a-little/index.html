Much, Many, A Lot Of, A Few, A Little

Level: A2 · Free grammar guide

We use these words to talk about quantity. The choice depends on the noun: countable nouns (apples, friends) or uncountable nouns (milk, time, money).

How many apples? How much milk? I've got a few friends and a little time.

Countable or uncountable?

Countable (plural)Uncountable
a large amountmany, a lot of / lots ofmuch, a lot of / lots of
a small amount (some)a fewa little
a small amount (not enough)fewlittle
questionsHow many …?How much …?

In everyday English, much and many are most common in negatives and questions: I don't have much time. Are there many people? In positive sentences, a lot of sounds more natural: I have a lot of time.

A few or few? A little or little?

A few / a little = some, a small amount (positive): I have a few friends here, so I'm happy.

Few / little = not many, not much, almost none (negative): Very few people came, so we cancelled the meeting. We have little time. Hurry!

Examples

  1. How many brothers and sisters do you have? (countable question)
  2. How much does this cost? (price)
  3. There isn't much milk left. (uncountable negative)
  4. There were a lot of people at the concert. (positive)
  5. I've got a few ideas for the party. (some (countable))
  6. Could I have a little sugar, please? (some (uncountable))
  7. Very few students failed the test. (almost none)
  8. He has little patience with children. (not much)
  9. I drank too much coffee today. (more than I want)
  10. There are too many cars in the city centre. (more than we want)

Common mistakes

❌ How many money do you have?

✅ How much money do you have? (Money is uncountable: how much.)

❌ There isn't many milk.

✅ There isn't much milk. (Milk is uncountable: much.)

❌ I have a little friends.

✅ I have a few friends. (Friends is countable: a few.)

❌ There are too much people here.

✅ There are too many people here. (People is countable: too many.)

❌ We have a little time, so hurry!

✅ We have little time, so hurry! (Not enough time: little (without a).)

❌ There's lot of food.

✅ There's a lot of food. / There's lots of food. (A lot of or lots of.)
