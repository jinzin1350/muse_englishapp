خانه / گرامر / much، many، a lot of، a few و a little

much، many، a lot of، a few و a littleMuch, Many, A Lot Of, A Few, A Little

many و a few را با اسم‌های جمعِ قابل‌شمارش بیاورید (many books, a few friends)، و much و a little را با اسم‌های غیرقابل‌شمارش (much time, a little milk). A lot of با هر دو می‌آید.

صفحه‌های اسم‌های قابل‌شمارش و غیرقابل‌شمارش و some و any را هم ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A1 · مبتدی

much, many, a lot of؛ how much, how many A1

many را با اسم جمع و much را با اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش بیاورید، بیشتر در سؤال و جملهٔ منفی. a lot of / lots of با هر دو می‌آید، به‌خصوص در جملهٔ مثبت. برای پرسیدن تعداد How many…? و برای مقدار How much…? بیاورید.

قابل‌شمارش (جمع)غیرقابل‌شمارش
سؤالیHow many apples? Are there many people?How much water? Is there much traffic?
منفیThere aren't many cafés.I don't have much time.
مثبتThere are a lot of cafés.I have a lot of time.

در جملهٔ مثبت، a lot of از much طبیعی‌تر است: I drink a lot of coffee. (نه I drink much coffee). many در جملهٔ مثبت درست است، اما در صحبت روزمره a lot of رایج‌تر است.

How much برای پرسیدن قیمت هم به کار می‌رود: How much is this jacket?

  • How many brothers do you have? قابل‌شمارش
  • I don't have much money. غیرقابل‌شمارش، منفی
  • There are a lot of people here. مثبت
  • How much is a coffee? قیمت

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ How much people came? → ✅ How many people came? قابل‌شمارش: many.
  • ❌ I don't have many time. → ✅ I don't have much time. غیرقابل‌شمارش: much.
  • ❌ There is a lot of cars. → ✅ There are a lot of cars. اسم جمع: are.

تمرین سطح A1

1. How ___ sisters do you have?
قابل‌شمارش: How many?
2. We don't have ___ milk. Can you buy some?
غیرقابل‌شمارش، منفی: much.
3. How ___ money do you need? (one word)
غیرقابل‌شمارش: How much?

A2 · مقدماتی

a few و a little: کمی، نه زیاد A2

a few با اسم جمع و a little با اسم غیرقابل‌شمارش یعنی مقداری، کمی، چند تا: I have a few friends here. Can I have a little sugar?

قابل‌شمارش (جمع)غیرقابل‌شمارش
a few eggs, a few days, a few peoplea little milk, a little time, a little money

a few و a little معنی مثبت دارند: یعنی «چند تا / کمی»، و اغلب یعنی «به اندازهٔ کافی»: I have a few friends in Toronto, so I'm not lonely.

a little قبل از صفت هم می‌آید و یعنی «یک کم»: I'm a little tired.

  • I'll be ready in a few minutes. قابل‌شمارش
  • Add a little salt. غیرقابل‌شمارش
  • We stayed in Paris for a few days. قابل‌شمارش
  • She speaks a little French. غیرقابل‌شمارش

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I have a little friends here. → ✅ I have a few friends here. قابل‌شمارش: a few.
  • ❌ Can I have a few milk? → ✅ Can I have a little milk? غیرقابل‌شمارش: a little.

تمرین سطح A2

4. There are ___ apples left. Take one if you want.
قابل‌شمارش: a few.
5. Could I have ___ more coffee, please?
غیرقابل‌شمارش: a little.
6. I'll call you back in a ___ minutes. (one word)
قابل‌شمارش: a few minutes.

B1 · متوسط

few یا a few؟ little یا a little؟ B1

a few / a little یعنی چند تا، کمی (مثبت). few / little (بدون a) یعنی زیاد نه، خیلی کم (منفی): I have a few friends (اوضاعم خوب است). I have few friends (تنها هستم).

مثبت (کمی، کافی)منفی (ناکافی، تقریباً هیچ)
I have a few friends here.Very few people came.
There's a little time left. Let's have a coffee.There's little time left. Hurry!

few و little (بدون a) نسبتاً رسمی‌اند. در صحبت روزمره معمولاً می‌گویند not many / not much: Not many people came.

very few / very little رایج است: There's very little money left.

quite a few یعنی نسبتاً زیاد: Quite a few people came.

  • Few tourists visit this area in winter. یعنی not many
  • He has little patience with lazy students. یعنی not much
  • A few of my friends came to help. چند تا (مثبت)
  • Quite a few people complained. نسبتاً زیاد

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Unfortunately, a few people came, so the party was boring. → ✅ Unfortunately, few people came, so the party was boring. معنی منفی: few.
  • ❌ Don't hurry. There's little time. → ✅ Don't hurry. There's a little time. معنی مثبت: a little.

تمرین سطح B1

7. The town is very quiet. ___ people live there now.
معنی منفی (زیاد نه): few.
8. Don't worry, we have ___ time. Let's have a coffee.
معنی مثبت (کمی): a little.
9. Very ___ students failed the exam. It was quite easy. (one word)
Very few یعنی تقریباً هیچ.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

Too much, too many, plenty of, a large number of B2

Too much / too many = more than you want. Plenty of = more than enough. In formal English: a large number of + plural nouns, a great deal of / a large amount of + uncountable nouns.

Countable (plural)Uncountable
too many peopletoo much noise
plenty of chairsplenty of time
a large number of studentsa great deal of money, a large amount of data
so many questionsso much work

Plenty of is positive: Don't worry, we have plenty of time. (= more than enough)

In formal writing, use a large number of with countable nouns and a great deal of / a large amount of with uncountable nouns. Don't use a great deal of with plural nouns.

  • There are too many cars in the city centre. (too many)
  • I spend too much time on my phone. (too much)
  • Help yourself. There's plenty of food. (plenty of)
  • The project requires a great deal of effort. (formal, uncountable)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ There's too many noise. → ✅ There's too much noise. (uncountable: too much)
  • ❌ A large number of money was stolen. → ✅ A large amount of money was stolen. (uncountable: a large amount of)
  • ❌ A great deal of people agree. → ✅ A large number of people agree. (plural: a large number of)

تمرین سطح B2

10. I can't sleep. There's ___ noise outside.
Uncountable: too much.
11. Don't rush. We have ___ time.
More than enough: plenty of.
12. There were too ___ people on the bus, so I walked. (one word)
Countable: too many.

C1 · پیشرفته

Much in formal writing, many a, a great many C1

In formal English, much and many are common in positive sentences: Much has been written about this. Many a + singular noun = many (literary). A good / great many = a very large number.

PatternExample
much in formal positive sentencesMuch research has been done on this topic. There is much debate about…
much / many as pronounsMuch of the city was destroyed. Many believe that…
many a + singular noun + singular verbMany a student has failed this exam. (literary)
a good / great many + pluralI've visited a great many countries.
as much / as many as + numberIt can cost as much as $5,000.

In everyday speech, a lot of is more natural in positive sentences. In academic and formal writing, much and many are common: There has been much discussion of…

As much as / as many as + number emphasises that the number is large: As many as 10,000 people were affected. Use as much as for money and uncountable amounts.

  • Much has changed since the 1990s. (much as a pronoun)
  • Many a traveller has got lost in these mountains. (many a)
  • She has written a great many articles. (a great many)
  • As many as 500 jobs could be lost. (as many as)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Many a students have tried. → ✅ Many a student has tried. (many a + singular noun + singular verb)
  • ❌ A great many people was there. → ✅ A great many people were there. (plural verb)
  • ❌ It can cost as many as $1,000. → ✅ It can cost as much as $1,000. (money: as much as)

تمرین سطح C1

13. ___ has been written about climate change.
Formal pronoun with a singular verb: much.
14. ___ student has struggled with this exam.
Many a + singular noun.
15. The storm affected as ___ as 20,000 homes. (one word)
Countable: as many as.
