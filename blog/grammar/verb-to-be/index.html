فعل to be (am، is، are)The Verb To Be (Am, Is, Are)

be پرکاربردترین فعل انگلیسی است. در زمان حال سه شکل دارد: am, is, are: I am a student. She is from Iran. We are happy. و در گذشته: was, were.

این صفحه شکل‌های حالِ be و کاربردهایش را توضیح می‌دهد. برای گذشته صفحهٔ was و were را ببینید.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2

A1 · مبتدی

I am, you are, he is: شکل‌ها و شکل کوتاه A1

I am (I'm)، you / we / they are ('re)، he / she / it is ('s). در صحبت کردن معمولاً شکل کوتاه را به کار می‌بریم.

شکل کاملشکل کوتاه
I amI'm
you areyou're
he / she / it ishe's / she's / it's
we arewe're
they arethey're

وقتی فاعل اسم است، برای مفرد is و برای جمع are بیاورید: My brother is a doctor. My parents are teachers.

it's (یعنی it is) را با its (مالکیت: «ـَش») قاطی نکنید: It's a nice car. Its colour is red.

  • I'm a student. (I am)
  • She's from Canada. (she is)
  • We're in the kitchen. (we are)
  • My friends are very kind. اسم جمع

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I is a teacher. → ✅ I am a teacher. (I am)
  • ❌ They is my friends. → ✅ They are my friends. (they are)

I'm not, isn't, aren't و Are you…? A1

منفی: I'm not, he isn't (is not), they aren't (are not). سؤالی: be را اول جمله بیاورید: Are you OK? Is she here? جواب کوتاه: Yes, I am. No, she isn't.

منفیسؤالیجواب کوتاه
I'm notAm I late?Yes, you are. / No, you aren't.
you aren'tAre you tired?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
he / she / it isn'tIs he at home?Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
we / they aren'tAre they ready?Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

بگویید I'm not (نه I amn't). هم he isn't درست است و هم he's not.

در جواب کوتاهِ مثبت شکل کوتاه نیاورید: Yes, I am. (نه Yes, I'm.)

  • I'm not hungry. منفی
  • Is this your bag? سؤالی
  • They aren't at school today. منفیِ جمع
  • Are you from Spain? ~ No, I'm not. سؤال + جواب کوتاه

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I amn't tired. → ✅ I'm not tired. (I'm not)
  • ❌ You are ready? → ✅ Are you ready? در سؤال be اول می‌آید
  • ❌ Yes, I'm. → ✅ Yes, I am. آخر جمله شکل کوتاه نمی‌آید

تمرین سطح A1

1. My sister ___ a nurse.
اسم مفرد: is.
2. We ___ from Tehran.
We are.
3. I ___ 30 years old. (one word)
I am.
4. ___ you a student?
سؤالی: Are you…?
5. I ___ hungry. I had a big lunch.
I'm not.
6. Is your brother married? ~ No, he ___. (one word)
جواب کوتاه: No, he isn't.

A2 · مقدماتی

I'm 25, I'm hungry, I'm cold: با be، نه have A2

انگلیسی برای سن (I'm 25)، احساس و حال (I'm hungry, I'm cold, I'm afraid)، شغل (She's a doctor)، ملیت و به دنیا آمدن (I was born in 1990) از be استفاده می‌کند، نه have. (در فارسی می‌گوییم «۲۵ سال دارم»، اما در انگلیسی have نمی‌آید.)

انگلیسیِ درست (با be)نه
I'm 25 (years old).I have 25 years.
I'm hungry / thirsty / cold / hot.I have hunger / cold.
I'm afraid of spiders.I have fear of spiders.
She's right. / He's wrong.She has right.
I was born in Shiraz.I born in Shiraz. / I am born in Shiraz.
She's a doctor.She's doctor. (a لازم دارد)

be born برای به دنیا آمدن همیشه در گذشته است: I was born in 1995.

  • My son is seven. سن
  • Are you cold? I can close the window. احساس
  • You're right. I'm sorry. حق داشتن
  • My father was born in Isfahan. (be born)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I have 30 years. → ✅ I'm 30 (years old). سن: با be
  • ❌ I have cold. Close the window. → ✅ I'm cold. Close the window. احساس: با be
  • ❌ I born in 1998. → ✅ I was born in 1998. (was born)

تمرین سطح A2

7. How old ___ your daughter? ~ She's eight.
سن: با be.
8. Can I have some water? I ___ very thirsty.
احساس: I'm thirsty.
9. I ___ born in 1992 in Mashhad. (one word)
I was born.

B1 · متوسط

I agree، نه I'm agree: be یا do؟ B1

در حال ساده be را همراه فعل دیگر نیاورید: I agree (نه I'm agree)، I don't understand (نه I'm not understand)، Do you like…? (نه Are you like…?). be را با صفت، اسم و فعل ing‌دار به کار ببرید.

درستنه
I agree with you.I'm agree with you.
I don't understand.I'm not understand.
Do you like coffee?Are you like coffee?
She works here.She is work here.
Are you ready? (صفت)Do you ready?
I'm working. (be + فعل ing‌دار)I working.

از خودتان بپرسید: آیا فعل دیگری در جمله هست؟ اگر هست، برای سؤال و منفی do / does بیاورید: Do you live here? اگر فعل دیگری نیست (فقط صفت یا اسم)، be بیاورید: Are you tired?

  • I agree with you completely. agree (بدون be)
  • Do you know him? do + فعل
  • Are you happy here? be + صفت
  • He doesn't eat meat. منفی با do

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm agree with you. → ✅ I agree with you. بدون be
  • ❌ Are you like football? → ✅ Do you like football? do + فعل
  • ❌ She is live in Toronto. → ✅ She lives in Toronto. حال ساده

تمرین سطح B1

10. I ___ with you. It's a great idea.
agree فعل است: I agree.
11. ___ you like Persian food?
فعل دیگری هست (like): Do you…?
12. I'm sorry, I ___ understand. Can you repeat that? (one word)
منفی با do: I don't understand.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

He's being silly: be in the continuous B2

Be can be continuous (am / is / are being) to talk about temporary behaviour: You're being very rude today. (= you're acting rudely now). It's also used in the passive continuous: The road is being repaired.

PatternExampleMeaning
is + adjectiveHe's rude.his character, always
is being + adjectiveHe's being rude.his behaviour now
is being + past participleThe house is being painted.passive, in progress
being + adjective (-ing form)Being late is not acceptable.as a noun

Use being + adjective for actions and behaviour: silly, rude, kind, careful, lazy, stupid, difficult, nice. Not for states like tall or tired: He's tired (not He's being tired).

  • Stop it! You're being silly. (behaviour now)
  • She's usually shy, but today she's being very friendly. (temporary)
  • My car is being fixed this week. (passive continuous)
  • Being a parent is hard work. (being as a noun)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He's being tall. → ✅ He's tall. (states: no being)
  • ❌ The bridge is be repaired. → ✅ The bridge is being repaired. (passive continuous: being)

تمرین سطح B2

13. Why ___ so difficult today? You're not usually like this.
Temporary behaviour: are you being.
14. The museum ___ renovated, so it's closed until June.
Passive continuous: is being.
15. Be careful! You ___ (be) very careless with that knife.
Behaviour now: are being.
