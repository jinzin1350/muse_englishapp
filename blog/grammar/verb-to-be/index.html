The Verb To Be (am, is, are)

Level: A1 · Free grammar guide

Be is the most important verb in English. In the present it has three forms: am, is, are. I am a student. She is from Brazil. They are hungry.

We use be to talk about names, ages, jobs, nationalities, feelings, places and descriptions.

Am, is, are

SubjectFull formShort form
II amI'm
youyou areyou're
he / she / ithe is / she is / it ishe's / she's / it's
wewe arewe're
theythey arethey're

Use is with one person or thing (My brother is tall) and are with more than one (My brothers are tall).

Negatives

Full formShort forms
I am notI'm not
you are notyou're not / you aren't
he is nothe's not / he isn't
we are notwe're not / we aren't
they are notthey're not / they aren't

There is no short form amn't in standard English. Use I'm not.

Questions and short answers

Put am / is / are before the subject: Are you tired? Is she your sister? Where are they?

QuestionYesNo
Are you hungry?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Is he a teacher?Yes, he is.No, he isn't. / No, he's not.
Are they at home?Yes, they are.No, they aren't. / No, they're not.

Don't use short forms in short yes answers: Yes, I am. (not Yes, I'm.)

What do we use be for?

  • Names and ages: I'm Sara. I'm 25.
  • Jobs: He's a nurse.
  • Nationalities and places: We're from Canada. The keys are on the table.
  • Feelings: I'm cold. Are you OK?
  • Descriptions: The house is big. The film is very funny.

Ages use be, not have: I'm 30. (not I have 30 years) The same with hungry, thirsty, cold and hot: I'm hungry.

Examples

  1. I'm a student. (I + am)
  2. My mother is a doctor. (one person + is)
  3. We aren't ready. (negative)
  4. Is this your bag? (question)
  5. They're from Japan. (they + are)
  6. It's cold today. (it + is)
  7. Are you OK? – Yes, I am. (short answer)
  8. My keys aren't in my bag. (plural negative)
  9. How old are you? – I'm 19. (age)
  10. Where is the station? (wh- question)

Common mistakes

❌ She are my friend.

✅ She is my friend. (He, she, it → is.)

❌ I is happy.

✅ I am happy. (I → am.)

❌ They is students.

✅ They are students. (They → are.)

❌ Where you are from?

✅ Where are you from? (In questions, put are before the subject.)

❌ I have 25 years.

✅ I'm 25. (Ages use be.)

❌ Yes, I'm.

✅ Yes, I am. (No short form in short yes answers.)

❌ He not is here.

✅ He isn't here. (Not goes after is: is not / isn't.)

Exercises (16)

Choose the correct answer.

1. I ___ a teacher.
I takes am.
2. My parents ___ at home.
My parents = they: are.
3. She ___ from Italy.
She takes is.
4. ___ you tired?
Questions with you: Are you…?
5. They ___ my friends.
Negative with they: aren't.
6. It ___ cold today. It's very hot!
Negative with it: isn't.
7. Are you hungry? – Yes, I ___.
Short yes answers don't use short forms: Yes, I am.
8. Is your brother a student? – No, he ___.
Short answer with he: No, he isn't.
9. How old ___ your sister?
Your sister = she: is. Ages use be.
10. Choose the correct sentence.
Ages use be: I am 30.
11. Where ___ you from?
You takes are: Where are you from?
12. We ___ late. The class starts in ten minutes.
Negative with we: aren't.
13. This ___ my new phone.
This = one thing: is.
14. ___ I in the right room?
Questions with I: Am I…?
15. My keys ___ on the table.
My keys is plural: are.
16. Choose the correct short form.
The negative of I am is I'm not. Amn't isn't standard English.
