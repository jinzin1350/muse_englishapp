فعل to be (am، is، are)The Verb To Be (Am, Is, Are)

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2

Be is the most common verb in English. In the present, it has three forms: am, is, are: I am a student. She is from Iran. We are happy. In the past: was, were.

This page covers the present forms and uses of be. For the past, see Was and Were.

I am, you are, he is: forms and contractions A1

Full formShort form

you areyou're
he / she / it ishe's / she's / it's
we arewe're

  • I'm a student. (I am)
  • She's from Canada. (she is)
  • We're in the kitchen. (we are)
  • My friends are very kind. (plural noun)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I is a teacher. → ✅ I am a teacher. (I am)
  • ❌ They is my friends. → ✅ They are my friends. (they are)

  • I'm not hungry. (negative)
  • Is this your bag? (question)
  • They aren't at school today. (negative plural)
  • Are you from Spain? ~ No, I'm not. (question + short answer)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I amn't tired. → ✅ I'm not tired. (I'm not)
  • ❌ You are ready? → ✅ Are you ready? (question: be first)
  • ❌ Yes, I'm. → ✅ Yes, I am. (no contraction at the end)

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

He's being silly: be in the continuous B2

PatternExampleMeaning
is + adjectiveHe's rude.his character, always
is being + adjectiveHe's being rude.his behaviour now
is being + past participleThe house is being painted.passive, in progress
being + adjective (-ing form)Being late is not acceptable.as a noun

  • Stop it! You're being silly. (behaviour now)
  • She's usually shy, but today she's being very friendly. (temporary)
  • My car is being fixed this week. (passive continuous)
  • Being a parent is hard work. (being as a noun)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He's being tall. → ✅ He's tall. (states: no being)
  • ❌ The bridge is be repaired. → ✅ The bridge is being repaired. (passive continuous: being)

تمرین سطح B2

Passive continuous: is being.
15. Be careful! You ___ (be) very careless with that knife.
Behaviour now: are being.
