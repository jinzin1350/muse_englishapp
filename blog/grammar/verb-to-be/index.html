فعل to be (am، is، are)The Verb To Be (Am, Is, Are)

Be is the most common verb in English. In the present, it has three forms: am, is, are: I am a student. She is from Iran. We are happy. In the past: was, were.

This page covers the present forms and uses of be. For the past, see Was and Were.

A1 · مبتدی

I am, you are, he is: forms and contractions A1

I am (I'm), you / we / they are ('re), he / she / it is ('s). In speaking, we usually use the short forms.

Full formShort form
I amI'm
you areyou're
he / she / it ishe's / she's / it's
we arewe're
they arethey're

With a noun subject, use is (singular) or are (plural): My brother is a doctor. My parents are teachers.

Don't confuse it's (= it is) and its (possessive): It's a nice car. Its colour is red.

  • I'm a student. (I am)
  • She's from Canada. (she is)
  • We're in the kitchen. (we are)
  • My friends are very kind. (plural noun)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I is a teacher. → ✅ I am a teacher. (I am)
  • ❌ They is my friends. → ✅ They are my friends. (they are)

I'm not, isn't, aren't; Are you…? A1

Negative: I'm not, he isn't (is not), they aren't (are not). Questions: put be first: Are you OK? Is she here? Short answers: Yes, I am. No, she isn't.

NegativeQuestionShort answer
I'm notAm I late?Yes, you are. / No, you aren't.
you aren'tAre you tired?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
he / she / it isn'tIs he at home?Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
we / they aren'tAre they ready?Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

Say I'm not (not I amn't). Both he isn't and he's not are correct.

In positive short answers, don't use contractions: Yes, I am. (not Yes, I'm.)

  • I'm not hungry. (negative)
  • Is this your bag? (question)
  • They aren't at school today. (negative plural)
  • Are you from Spain? ~ No, I'm not. (question + short answer)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I amn't tired. → ✅ I'm not tired. (I'm not)
  • ❌ You are ready? → ✅ Are you ready? (question: be first)
  • ❌ Yes, I'm. → ✅ Yes, I am. (no contraction at the end)

تمرین سطح A1

1. My sister ___ a nurse.
Singular noun: is.
2. We ___ from Tehran.
We are.
3. I ___ 30 years old. (one word)
I am.
4. ___ you a student?
Question: Are you…?
5. I ___ hungry. I had a big lunch.
I'm not.
6. Is your brother married? ~ No, he ___. (one word)
Short answer: No, he isn't.

A2 · مقدماتی

I'm 25, I'm hungry, I'm cold: be, not have A2

English uses be (not have) for age (I'm 25), feelings (I'm hungry, I'm cold, I'm afraid), jobs (She's a doctor), nationality, and be born (I was born in 1990).

English (be)Not
I'm 25 (years old).I have 25 years.
I'm hungry / thirsty / cold / hot.I have hunger / cold.
I'm afraid of spiders.I have fear of spiders.
She's right. / He's wrong.She has right.
I was born in Shiraz.I born in Shiraz. / I am born in Shiraz.
She's a doctor.She's doctor. (needs a)

Be born is in the past for when you came into the world: I was born in 1995.

  • My son is seven. (age)
  • Are you cold? I can close the window. (feeling)
  • You're right. I'm sorry. (right)
  • My father was born in Isfahan. (be born)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I have 30 years. → ✅ I'm 30 (years old). (age: be)
  • ❌ I have cold. Close the window. → ✅ I'm cold. Close the window. (feeling: be)
  • ❌ I born in 1998. → ✅ I was born in 1998. (was born)

تمرین سطح A2

7. How old ___ your daughter? ~ She's eight.
Age: be.
8. Can I have some water? I ___ very thirsty.
Feeling: I'm thirsty.
9. I ___ born in 1992 in Mashhad. (one word)
I was born.

B1 · متوسط

I agree, not I'm agree: be or do? B1

Don't use be with other verbs in the present simple: I agree (not I'm agree), I don't understand (not I'm not understand), Do you like…? (not Are you like…?). Use be with adjectives, nouns and -ing.

CorrectNot
I agree with you.I'm agree with you.
I don't understand.I'm not understand.
Do you like coffee?Are you like coffee?
She works here.She is work here.
Are you ready? (adjective)Do you ready?
I'm working. (be + -ing)I working.

Ask yourself: is there another verb? Then use do / does for questions and negatives: Do you live here? Is there no other verb (just an adjective or noun)? Then use be: Are you tired?

  • I agree with you completely. (agree (no be))
  • Do you know him? (do + verb)
  • Are you happy here? (be + adjective)
  • He doesn't eat meat. (negative with do)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I'm agree with you. → ✅ I agree with you. (no be)
  • ❌ Are you like football? → ✅ Do you like football? (do + verb)
  • ❌ She is live in Toronto. → ✅ She lives in Toronto. (present simple)

تمرین سطح B1

10. I ___ with you. It's a great idea.
Agree is a verb: I agree.
11. ___ you like Persian food?
Another verb (like): Do you…?
12. I'm sorry, I ___ understand. Can you repeat that? (one word)
Negative with do: I don't understand.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

He's being silly: be in the continuous B2

Be can be continuous (am / is / are being) to talk about temporary behaviour: You're being very rude today. (= you're acting rudely now). It's also used in the passive continuous: The road is being repaired.

PatternExampleMeaning
is + adjectiveHe's rude.his character, always
is being + adjectiveHe's being rude.his behaviour now
is being + past participleThe house is being painted.passive, in progress
being + adjective (-ing form)Being late is not acceptable.as a noun

Use being + adjective for actions and behaviour: silly, rude, kind, careful, lazy, stupid, difficult, nice. Not for states like tall or tired: He's tired (not He's being tired).

  • Stop it! You're being silly. (behaviour now)
  • She's usually shy, but today she's being very friendly. (temporary)
  • My car is being fixed this week. (passive continuous)
  • Being a parent is hard work. (being as a noun)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ He's being tall. → ✅ He's tall. (states: no being)
  • ❌ The bridge is be repaired. → ✅ The bridge is being repaired. (passive continuous: being)

تمرین سطح B2

13. Why ___ so difficult today? You're not usually like this.
Temporary behaviour: are you being.
14. The museum ___ renovated, so it's closed until June.
Passive continuous: is being.
15. Be careful! You ___ (be) very careless with that knife.
Behaviour now: are being.
