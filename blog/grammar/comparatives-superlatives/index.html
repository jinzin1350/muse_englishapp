صفت تفضیلی و عالیComparatives and Superlatives

صفت تفضیلی (مثل «بزرگ‌تر») را برای مقایسهٔ دو چیز به کار ببرید (Toronto is bigger than Ottawa) و صفت عالی (مثل «بزرگ‌ترین») را برای اینکه بگویید کدام در یک گروه از همه بیشتر است (It's the biggest city in Canada).

این صفحه as… as را هم توضیح می‌دهد و اینکه چطور مقایسه را قوی‌تر یا ضعیف‌تر کنید (much bigger, a bit cheaper, by far the best).

A2 · B1 · B2 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

bigger, more expensive, the biggest, the most expensive A2

صفت‌های کوتاه ‑er / the ‑est می‌گیرند (cheap → cheaper → the cheapest). صفت‌های بلند more / the most می‌گیرند (expensive → more expensive → the most expensive). بعد از صفت تفضیلی than بیاورید.

صفتتفضیلیعالی
یک‌هجایی: cheap, tallcheaper, tallerthe cheapest, the tallest
ختم به ‑e: nice, largenicer, largerthe nicest, the largest
یک حرف صدادار + یک حرف بی‌صدا: big, hotbigger, hotterthe biggest, the hottest
دوهجایی ختم به ‑y: happy, easyhappier, easierthe happiest, the easiest
دوهجایی یا بلندتر: modern, expensivemore modern, more expensivethe most modern, the most expensive

بعد از صفت تفضیلی than بیاورید (یعنی «از»): My brother is taller than me. قبل از صفت عالی the بیاورید، اغلب با in یا of: the tallest in the class, the best day of my life.

بعضی صفت‌های دوهجایی هر دو شکل را می‌گیرند: quieter / more quiet, simpler / more simple, cleverer / more clever.

  • My flat is smaller than yours. ‑er + than
  • This is the most beautiful beach in Iran. the most
  • Today is hotter than yesterday. حرف بی‌صدا دوتا می‌شود
  • Maths is easier than physics for me. y ← ier

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She is more tall than her sister. → ✅ She is taller than her sister. صفت کوتاه: ‑er.
  • ❌ This is the most cheap hotel. → ✅ This is the cheapest hotel. صفت کوتاه: the ‑est.
  • ❌ He's older that me. → ✅ He's older than me. than.
  • ❌ It's the biggest city of the world. → ✅ It's the biggest city in the world. صفت عالی + in (برای مکان).

good, better, best؛ bad, worse, worst؛ far, further A2

شکل‌های بی‌قاعده: good → better → the best، bad → worse → the worst، far → farther / further → the farthest / the furthest. قیدهای well و badly هم می‌شوند better و worse.

صفت / قیدتفضیلیعالی
good / wellbetterthe best
bad / badlyworsethe worst
farfarther / furtherthe farthest / the furthest
much / manymorethe most
little (برای مقدار)lessthe least

further معنی بیشتر، اضافه هم دارد: For further information, call us. (اینجا farther نمی‌آید)

  • Your English is better than mine. good ← better
  • That was the worst meal I've ever had. bad ← the worst
  • The station is further than I thought. far
  • She earns more money than me. much ← more

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ This film is more good than the book. → ✅ This film is better than the book. good ← better.
  • ❌ It was the baddest day of my life. → ✅ It was the worst day of my life. bad ← the worst.
  • ❌ I feel more bad today. → ✅ I feel worse today. bad ← worse.

تمرین سطح A2

1. An elephant is ___ than a horse.
یک حرف صدادار + یک حرف بی‌صدا: bigger.
2. This is ___ restaurant in the city.
صفت بلند: the most expensive.
3. Canada is ___ (cold) than Mexico.
صفت کوتاه: colder.
4. My cold is ___ today. I feel terrible.
Bad ← worse.
5. She's ___ student in our class.
Good ← the best.
6. You speak French ___ (well) than me.
Well ← better.

B1 · متوسط

as… as, not as… as, the same as B1

با as + صفت + as بگویید دو چیز برابرند (یعنی «به اندازهٔ»): She's as tall as her mother. not as / not so… as یعنی کمتر: Ottawa isn't as big as Toronto. برای چیزهای یکسان the same as بیاورید.

الگومثال
as… as (برابر)My car is as fast as yours.
not as / not so… as (کمتر)This hotel isn't as expensive as the other one.
the same (+ noun) asYour phone is the same as mine. She's the same age as me.
as much / as many… asI don't have as much time as you.
twice / three times as… asTheir house is twice as big as ours.

بعد از as… as، انگلیسی روزمره ضمیر مفعولی می‌آورد: as tall as me. انگلیسی دقیق‌تر: as tall as I am.

  • The new film is as good as the first one. برابر
  • Ottawa isn't as big as Toronto. کمتر
  • My bag is the same as yours. the same as
  • This laptop is twice as expensive as that one. twice as… as (دو برابر)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ She's as taller as me. → ✅ She's as tall as me. as + صفت ساده + as.
  • ❌ My phone is the same like yours. → ✅ My phone is the same as yours. the same as. (نه the same like)
  • ❌ It isn't as cold than yesterday. → ✅ It isn't as cold as yesterday. as… as.

much bigger, a bit cheaper, by far the best B1

صفت تفضیلی را با much, a lot, far قوی‌تر کنید (much bigger، یعنی خیلی بزرگ‌تر) و با a bit, a little, slightly ضعیف‌تر (a bit cheaper، یعنی کمی ارزان‌تر). صفت عالی را با by far قوی‌تر کنید (by far the best، یعنی بی‌شک بهترین).

شدتتفضیلیعالی
تفاوت زیادmuch / a lot / far cheaperby far the cheapest
تفاوت کمa bit / a little / slightly cheaper
بدون تفاوتno cheaper (رسمی)، not any cheaper
evenIt's even colder today. (و از قبل هم سرد بود)

با صفت تفضیلی very نیاورید: much better (نه very better). فارسی «خیلی بهتر» را very better ترجمه نکنید.

even + صفت تفضیلی نشان می‌دهد چیز اول هم از قبل زیاد بود (یعنی «حتی»): Yesterday was hot, but today is even hotter.

  • The train is much faster than the bus. much
  • Could you speak a bit more slowly? a bit
  • This is by far the best café in town. by far
  • Today is even colder than yesterday. even

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ My new flat is very bigger. → ✅ My new flat is much bigger. very با صفت تفضیلی نمی‌آید.
  • ❌ This is far the best hotel. → ✅ This is by far the best hotel. by far + صفت عالی.

تمرین سطح B1

7. This shirt is ___ that one. They cost the same.
برابر: as… as.
8. My brother isn't ___ me. I'm 1.80 m and he's 1.70 m.
کمتر: not as… as.
9. Your bag is the same ___ mine. Did you buy it in the same shop? (one word)
The same as.
10. The new phone is ___ faster than the old one. It's amazing.
تفاوت زیاد: much faster.
11. It's ___ the most popular app in the country. Everyone uses it.
By far + صفت عالی.
12. This jacket is only $5 more. It's just a ___ more expensive. (one word)
تفاوت کم: a bit more expensive.

B2 · بالاتر از متوسط

The more, the better; colder and colder B2

The + comparative…, the + comparative shows two things changing together: The more you practise, the better you get. Comparative + and + comparative shows a continuing change: It's getting colder and colder.

PatternExample
the… the…The sooner we leave, the better.
The more I read, the more I learn.
-er and -erThe days are getting longer and longer.
more and more + adjectiveLife is becoming more and more expensive.
less and lessI see him less and less.

In the… the… sentences, keep normal word order after each comparative: The older he gets, the quieter he becomes.

Long adjectives repeat more: more and more popular (not more and more popular-er).

  • The more you sleep, the better you feel. (the… the…)
  • The faster you drive, the more dangerous it is. (the… the…)
  • My son is getting taller and taller. (-er and -er)
  • Online shopping is becoming more and more popular. (more and more)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ More you practise, better you get. → ✅ The more you practise, the better you get. (the… the…)
  • ❌ It's getting more and more cold. → ✅ It's getting colder and colder. (short adjective: -er and -er)
  • ❌ Prices are getting more expensive and more expensive. → ✅ Prices are getting more and more expensive. (more and more + long adjective)

تمرین سطح B2

13. ___ you start, the sooner you'll finish.
The + comparative…, the + comparative.
14. The weather is getting ___. Winter is coming.
Continuing change: colder and colder.
15. I love history. The more I learn about it, the ___ interested I become. (one word)
The more…, the more…

C1 · پیشرفته

Superior to, nowhere near as, twice the size of, the latter C1

Formal and advanced comparison: superior / inferior / preferable to (not than), nowhere near as / not nearly as (much less), twice the size of, the former / the latter, and more + adjective + than + adjective for two qualities.

PatternExample
superior / inferior / preferable toThis model is superior to the old one. (not superior than)
nowhere near / not nearly as… asThe sequel is nowhere near as good as the original.
twice / three times the + noun + ofTheir house is twice the size of ours.
the former / the latterI visited Rome and Paris. The latter was my favourite. (= Paris)
more + adjective + than + adjectiveHe's more lucky than clever. (two qualities of one person)

When comparing two qualities of the same thing, use more, even with short adjectives: The task was more long than difficult.

Junior, senior and prior also take to: She's senior to me.

  • Walking is preferable to driving in the city centre. (preferable to)
  • The new version is nowhere near as fast as we hoped. (nowhere near as)
  • The new stadium is twice the size of the old one. (twice the size of)
  • I like tea and coffee, but the former is better for my sleep. (the former = tea)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ This method is superior than the old one. → ✅ This method is superior to the old one. (superior to)
  • ❌ The sequel is nowhere near good as the original. → ✅ The sequel is nowhere near as good as the original. (nowhere near as… as)

تمرین سطح C1

16. In my opinion, natural light is far superior ___ artificial light.
Superior to.
17. The second season was ___ as good as the first. I stopped watching.
Much less: nowhere near as… as.
18. I was offered jobs in Toronto and Calgary. I chose the ___, because it's closer to the mountains. (one word)
The second of two: the latter.
