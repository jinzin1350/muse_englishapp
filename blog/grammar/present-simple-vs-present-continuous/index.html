Present Simple vs Present Continuous

A2 · B1 · C1

The present simple is for things in general: habits, facts and permanent situations. The present continuous is for things happening now or around now, and for temporary situations.

I work from home on Fridays. I'm working from home today.

A2 · Elementary

Now or in general? A2

Present simplePresent continuous
Usehabits, facts, things in generalhappening now or around now
FormI work / she worksI'm working / she's working
Common wordsevery day, usually, often, never, on Mondaysnow, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!
ExampleIt rains a lot in April.Take an umbrella. It's raining.
ExampleShe speaks three languages.She's speaking on the phone.

  • I usually walk to work, but today I'm taking the bus. (habit vs today)
  • Water boils at 100°C. Look, the water is boiling! (fact vs now)
  • Do you play tennis? – Yes, but I'm not playing this week. (in general vs now)
  • Where's Tom? – He's having a shower. (now)
  • My dad cooks on Sundays. He's cooking right now. (routine vs now)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ Look! The bus comes. → ✅ Look! The bus is coming. (Happening now: present continuous.)
  • ❌ I'm going to the gym every day. → ✅ I go to the gym every day. (A habit: present simple.)
  • ❌ She is speaking three languages. → ✅ She speaks three languages. (A permanent ability, not now: present simple.)

Practice: A2

1. I usually <span class="gap">___</span> to work, but today I'm taking the bus.
Usually = a habit: present simple, walk.
2. Where's Tom? – He <span class="gap">___</span> a shower.
Right now: present continuous, he's having.
3. She <span class="gap">___</span> three languages.
A permanent ability: present simple, speaks.
4. Look! The bus <span class="gap">___</span>. Run!
Look! = now: is coming.

B1 · Intermediate

Temporary or permanent? B1

Permanent (present simple)Temporary (present continuous)
My parents live in Toronto.I'm living with friends until I find a flat.
Joe works hard most of the time.You're working hard today!
The river flows into the lake.The river is flowing very fast today because of the rain.

  • I work in a bank, but this month I'm working in our Paris office. (permanent vs temporary)
  • We live in Ottawa. We're staying with my aunt in Toronto this week. (live vs stay)
  • Our company isn't doing very well this year. (this year)
  • He teaches maths, but this term he's teaching physics too. (this term)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ I stay with my aunt until my flat is ready. → ✅ I'm staying with my aunt until my flat is ready. (A temporary situation: present continuous.)
  • ❌ My parents are living in the same house all their lives. → ✅ My parents have lived in the same house all their lives. (From the past until now: present perfect. See Present Perfect.)

Practice: B1

5. My parents <span class="gap">___</span> in Toronto. They moved there in 1995.
A permanent situation: present simple, live.
6. I <span class="gap">___</span> with a friend until I find my own flat.
Temporary (until…): I'm staying.
7. Joe isn't usually busy, but he <span class="gap">___</span> really hard this week.
Temporary (this week): he's working.

C1 · Advanced

Stories, jokes and live commentary C1

  • So I'm waiting for a taxi when a woman runs up to me… (background + event)
  • A man walks into a café and orders a coffee… (joke)
  • It's raining, it's getting dark, and suddenly the car stops. (background + sudden event)
  • And she serves… it's an ace! (commentary)
  • The crowd is cheering as the runners cross the line. (longer + quick actions)

Common mistakes:

  • ❌ So I'm walking home and suddenly a dog is jumping at me. → ✅ So I'm walking home and suddenly a dog jumps at me. (Main events: present simple.)
  • ❌ So I stand at the bus stop, and a man is coming up to me. → ✅ So I'm standing at the bus stop, and a man comes up to me. (Background: continuous; main event: simple.)

Practice: C1

8. In an informal story told in present tenses, which tense usually gives the main events?
Main events: present simple; background: present continuous.
9. So I'm sitting on the train, reading, and suddenly a man <span class="gap">___</span> next to me.
A main event in the story: present simple, sits down.
10. In live sports commentary, which sentence describes a quick, complete action?
Quick, complete actions: present simple, shoots.
