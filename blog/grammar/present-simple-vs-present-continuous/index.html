حال ساده یا حال استمراریPresent Simple vs Present Continuous

حال ساده برای چیزهای کلی است: عادت‌ها، واقعیت‌ها و موقعیت‌های دائمی. حال استمراری برای کارهایی است که همین حالا یا این روزها در جریان‌اند، و برای موقعیت‌های موقت.

I work from home on Fridays. I'm working from home today.

A2 · B1 · C1

A2 · مقدماتی

همین حالا یا به طور کلی؟ A2

حال استمراری برای همین حالا؛ حال ساده برای عادت‌ها، واقعیت‌ها و چیزهای کلی.

حال سادهحال استمراری
کاربردعادت‌ها، واقعیت‌ها، چیزهای کلیدر جریانِ همین حالا یا این روزها
ساختارI work / she worksI'm working / she's working
کلمه‌های رایجevery day, usually, often, never, on Mondaysnow, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!
مثالIt rains a lot in April.Take an umbrella. It's raining.
مثالShe speaks three languages.She's speaking on the phone.

بعضی فعل‌ها (know, want, like, understand…) معمولاً استمراری نمی‌شوند، حتی برای همین حالا: I know the answer (نه I'm knowing). صفحهٔ فعل‌های حالتی را ببینید.

  • I usually walk to work, but today I'm taking the bus. عادت در برابر امروز
  • Water boils at 100°C. Look, the water is boiling! واقعیت در برابر همین حالا
  • Do you play tennis? – Yes, but I'm not playing this week. به طور کلی در برابر همین حالا
  • Where's Tom? – He's having a shower. همین حالا
  • My dad cooks on Sundays. He's cooking right now. کار روزمره در برابر همین حالا

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ Look! The bus comes. → ✅ Look! The bus is coming. همین حالا در جریان است: حال استمراری.
  • ❌ I'm going to the gym every day. → ✅ I go to the gym every day. عادت: حال ساده.
  • ❌ She is speaking three languages. → ✅ She speaks three languages. توانایی دائمی، نه همین حالا: حال ساده.

تمرین سطح A2

1. I usually ___ to work, but today I'm taking the bus.
usually یعنی عادت: حال ساده، walk.
2. Where's Tom? – He ___ a shower.
همین حالا: حال استمراری، he's having.
3. She ___ three languages.
توانایی دائمی: حال ساده، speaks.
4. Look! The bus ___. Run!
Look! یعنی همین حالا: is coming.

B1 · متوسط

موقت یا دائمی؟ B1

حال استمراری برای موقعیت‌های موقت؛ حال ساده برای موقعیت‌های دائمی.

دائمی (حال ساده)موقت (حال استمراری)
My parents live in Toronto.I'm living with friends until I find a flat.
Joe works hard most of the time.You're working hard today!
The river flows into the lake.The river is flowing very fast today because of the rain.

کلمه‌هایی مثل today, this week, this year, at the moment, until… اغلب نشان می‌دهند موقعیت موقت است.

  • I work in a bank, but this month I'm working in our Paris office. دائمی در برابر موقت
  • We live in Ottawa. We're staying with my aunt in Toronto this week. live در برابر stay
  • Our company isn't doing very well this year. امسال
  • He teaches maths, but this term he's teaching physics too. این ترم

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ I stay with my aunt until my flat is ready. → ✅ I'm staying with my aunt until my flat is ready. موقعیت موقت: حال استمراری.
  • ❌ My parents are living in the same house all their lives. → ✅ My parents have lived in the same house all their lives. از گذشته تا حالا: حال کامل. صفحهٔ حال کامل را ببینید.

تمرین سطح B1

5. My parents ___ in Toronto. They moved there in 1995.
موقعیت دائمی: حال ساده، live.
6. I ___ with a friend until I find my own flat.
موقت (until…): I'm staying.
7. Joe isn't usually busy, but he ___ really hard this week.
موقت (this week): he's working.

C1 · پیشرفته

Stories, jokes and live commentary C1

In informal stories and live commentary, the present continuous gives the background and the present simple gives the main events.

In informal stories and jokes, speakers often use present tenses to make the story more vivid. The present continuous gives the background; the present simple gives the main events, in order: So I'm standing at the bus stop, and this man comes up to me and asks for my phone.

After past tenses, a present simple verb can highlight a sudden event, often with suddenly or all of a sudden: We were having dinner when all of a sudden the lights go out.

Live commentary (sport, demonstrations) works the same way: present simple for quick, complete actions, present continuous for longer ones: Silva passes to Jones… Jones shoots! The fans are going wild.

  • So I'm waiting for a taxi when a woman runs up to me… (background + event)
  • A man walks into a café and orders a coffee… (joke)
  • It's raining, it's getting dark, and suddenly the car stops. (background + sudden event)
  • And she serves… it's an ace! (commentary)
  • The crowd is cheering as the runners cross the line. (longer + quick actions)

اشتباه‌های رایج:

  • ❌ So I'm walking home and suddenly a dog is jumping at me. → ✅ So I'm walking home and suddenly a dog jumps at me. (Main events: present simple.)
  • ❌ So I stand at the bus stop, and a man is coming up to me. → ✅ So I'm standing at the bus stop, and a man comes up to me. (Background: continuous; main event: simple.)

تمرین سطح C1

8. In an informal story told in present tenses, which tense usually gives the main events?
Main events: present simple; background: present continuous.
9. So I'm sitting on the train, reading, and suddenly a man ___ next to me.
A main event in the story: present simple, sits down.
10. In live sports commentary, which sentence describes a quick, complete action?
Quick, complete actions: present simple, shoots.

همچنین ببینید:

  • فعل‌هایی که استمراری نمی‌شوند
  • فعل‌های دومعنایی: think, have, see, taste
  • always + استمراری: He's always complaining
