📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
make yourself at home /make yur-SELF at HOME/
اصطلاح · idiom
To relax and feel comfortable in someone else's house.
راحت باش، خودت را در خانه خودت بدان
""Come in, make yourself at home," she said."
matter of life and death /MAT-er uv LIFE and DEATH/
اصطلاح · idiom
A situation that is extremely important and serious.
مسئله مرگ و زندگی
"For a surgeon, every decision can be a matter of life and death."
nothing to write home about /NUTH-ing tuh RITE HOME uh-BOWT/
اصطلاح · idiom
Not special or exciting; unremarkable.
چیز خاصی نیست، معمولی
"The hotel was clean, but nothing to write home about."
on the house /on thuh HOWSS/
اصطلاح · idiom
Provided free by the establishment.
مهمان خانه، رایگان
"The drinks were on the house after our long wait."
penthouse /PENT-howss/
اسم · noun
A luxurious apartment on the top floor of a building.
پنت‌هاوس
"They bought a stunning penthouse with city views."
social housing /SOH-shul HOW-zing/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Affordable housing provided by government or non-profits.
مسکن اجتماعی، مسکن حمایتی
"Many cities struggle to provide adequate social housing."
squat /SKWAHT/
فعل · verb
To illegally occupy an empty building or land.
تصرف غیرقانونی (ملک)
"The activists decided to squat in the abandoned factory."

پادکست: شهر و زندگی روزمره

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today Lena and I are talking about city life, homes, and the strange English expressions people use when they talk about where they live.

Lena

Hello everyone. I love this topic, because last weekend I finally visited my cousin in her new flat, and she greeted me at the door with the warmest words. She said, please come in and make yourself at home.

Maya

That is such a kind thing to hear. When someone says make yourself at home, they are telling you to relax, take off your coat, sit where you like, and stop behaving like a formal guest.

Lena

Exactly. She told me to make yourself at home, so I kicked off my shoes, curled up on her sofa, and helped myself to tea, and honestly I felt like family within five minutes.

Maya

I always make yourself at home the first rule in my own flat too. Wait, let me say that properly. I always tell my guests to make yourself at home, because a relaxed guest is a happy guest.

Lena

And your flat is comfortable, Maya, but it is nothing like a penthouse. Last month I interviewed a property developer who showed me a penthouse on the fortieth floor, with glass walls and a terrace above the whole city.

Maya

A penthouse sounds glamorous, but was it really that special inside, or was it honestly nothing to write home about?

Lena

Oh, the view was spectacular, but the kitchen was tiny and the rooms felt cold, so overall the penthouse was nothing to write home about. Expensive does not always mean impressive.

Maya

That phrase is so useful. When a film, a meal, or a hotel is nothing to write home about, it is ordinary, acceptable, but not worth celebrating.

Lena

My hotel in Chicago last spring was exactly that. Clean bed, quiet street, but nothing to write home about, and I told my sister the breakfast was nothing to write home about either.

Maya

Speaking of hotels, did they ever give you anything on the house? A free coffee, a free dessert, anything like that?

Lena

Once, yes. Our flight was delayed, we arrived exhausted at midnight, and the manager said the soup and drinks were on the house. On the house means the business pays, not you.

Maya

I had a coffee on the house at my local cafe yesterday because the machine broke and I waited twenty minutes. Small kindness, but I remembered it all day.

Lena

Restaurants often put one small item on the house to keep customers loyal. My brother runs a bistro, and he says a dessert on the house costs little but earns regulars for years.

Maya

That is smart business. Now, let us move from treats to serious things, because city housing can become a matter of life and death in winter.

Lena

That is true. When temperatures fall below zero, a safe warm home is a matter of life and death for elderly people living alone. It is not a luxury question at all.

Maya

Doctors say the same during heat waves. For a patient with a weak heart, air conditioning and shade can be a matter of life and death in August.

Lena

And for a surgeon, of course, every decision in the operating room can be a matter of life and death, which is where many learners first meet this expression.

Maya

Right, a matter of life and death always signals extreme seriousness. Nobody uses a matter of life and death for a late bus or a lost umbrella.

Lena

Which brings us to social housing, because safe homes for everyone is exactly why social housing exists. Social housing means affordable homes provided by the government or by non profit organisations.

Maya

My grandmother lived in social housing for thirty years, and she loved her neighbours. Her social housing flat was small, but it was warm, safe, and close to the market.

Lena

Many cities struggle to provide adequate social housing today. Waiting lists grow every year, and families wait far too long for social housing that truly fits their needs.

Maya

That is why the opposite story, the penthouse story, feels so extreme. One family waits years for social housing while a penthouse above them sits empty for eleven months.

Lena

Before we continue, Maya, I want to remind our listeners about something that can genuinely change their English. Have you told them about the Muse English app yet?

Maya

Not yet, so here is my invitation. In the Muse English app, you learn fresh words every day with clear photos and natural pronunciation, and you can hear this podcast inside the app whenever you like. There are shadowing stories that train your speaking, quick quizzes that check your memory, full grammar lessons, and a leaderboard with points that keeps you motivated. It is completely free to install, so join us today and make these expressions part of your daily life.

Lena

I use the shadowing stories every morning myself. Now, back to housing, because there is a harder word today. What happens when people squat in an empty building?

Maya

To squat means to occupy an empty building or land illegally, without permission and without paying rent. In the nineteen seventies, many young artists would squat in abandoned factories in Berlin.

Lena

I read about a group who decided to squat in an old factory here last winter. They said they chose to squat because rents had doubled, although the owners called the police within a week.

Maya

Some people squat out of protest, and some squat simply because they have nowhere else to sleep. The law is strict, but the human stories behind it are complicated.

Lena

A friend of mine who studies law says a family should never squat in a dangerous building, because a collapsing roof turns shelter into risk very quickly.

Maya

Let us bring all of this back to everyday conversation. Imagine you visit me this weekend. What is the first thing I say at the door?

Lena

You smile, you open the door wide, and you say, come in, make yourself at home. And I do, because you truly mean make yourself at home every single time.

Maya

And if my cooking disappoints you, you will politely say the soup was nothing to write home about.

Lena

I would never say that about your soup, but I might say the salad was nothing to write home about if you served me plain lettuce again.

Maya

Fair enough. And if we go to your brother's bistro afterwards, perhaps he will give us a coffee on the house.

Lena

He probably will. He loves putting coffee on the house for family, and sometimes a whole dessert is on the house when the kitchen is closing.

Maya

Let us test our listeners with a few situations. Situation one. Your friend looks tense in your living room and keeps her coat on. What do you tell her?

Lena

I tell her to relax and make yourself at home, of course. That little sentence, make yourself at home, changes the whole feeling of a visit.

Maya

Situation two. A reviewer visits a famous restaurant and finds ordinary food and slow service. What does the reviewer write?

Lena

The reviewer writes that the famous restaurant was nothing to write home about, and that the expensive tasting menu was nothing to write home about too.

Maya

Situation three. A cafe owner apologises for a long wait and refuses your money for the cake. What just happened?

Lena

The cake was on the house. When anything is on the house, the customer pays nothing, and the business covers the cost as a gift or an apology.

Lena

Which is why social housing matters so much in the same conversation. Social housing protects the people at the other end of the market, and good social housing keeps whole neighbourhoods mixed and alive.

Maya

Lena, this feels like the perfect moment for our second reminder, because learning words like these takes daily practice, not one lucky lesson.

Lena

I agree completely. Friends, the Muse English app gives you new words every single day, each with a photo, a meaning, and clear pronunciation you can copy. You can listen to this podcast in the app, practise with shadowing stories that build real fluency, test yourself with friendly quizzes, study grammar lessons step by step, and earn points on the leaderboard as you grow. Install Muse English free today, open one short lesson, and let your English improve a little every day.

Maya

Beautifully said. Now let us talk about the history of squatting a little more, because the verb squat carries strong feelings.

Lena

Agreed. Understanding the word squat helps you read the news, because headlines about housing protests use squat again and again every winter.

Maya

Let us do a final round of quick examples, the way we do in the app quizzes. I will start. Please, sit down, take some tea, and make yourself at home.

Lena

My turn. The new shopping centre looks impressive from outside, but inside it is nothing to write home about.

Maya

The waiter smiled and said the drinks were on the house, so our celebration cost less than we expected.

Lena

For the rescue team on the mountain, reaching the injured climber before dark was a matter of life and death.

Maya

She dreams of a penthouse with a roof garden, while her brother is simply grateful for his clean and friendly social housing.

Lena

And the students decided to squat in the empty library building for one night to protest against its closure.

Maya

Nothing to write home about.

Lena

On the house.

Maya

A matter of life and death.

Lena

A penthouse, social housing, and to squat.

Maya

Excellent. Listeners, choose one expression today and use it in a real sentence about your own city. Tell a friend to make yourself at home, describe a cafe that was nothing to write home about, or explain why social housing matters in your town.

Lena

And remember, none of this learning is a matter of life and death, so relax, enjoy it, and keep going. Small daily practice beats one giant study session every time.

Maya

That is all for today on Muse English. Thank you for listening, keep practising with the app, and we will talk again tomorrow with fresh words and fresh stories. Goodbye for now.

Lena

Goodbye everyone, and wherever you are listening tonight, make yourself at home with English.
