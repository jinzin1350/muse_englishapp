itinerary /ih-TIN-uh-reh-ree/
noun
a detailed plan of a journey
"Their ambitious itinerary covered four countries in just one week."
sojourn /SOH-jurn/
noun
a temporary stay in a place
"His brief sojourn in Lisbon inspired a whole collection of poems."
detour /DEE-toor/
noun
a longer way around to avoid something
"A road closure forced us to take a lengthy detour through the countryside."
expedition /eks-puh-DIH-shun/
noun
an organized journey with a purpose
"The expedition set out to document wildlife in the remote rainforest."
caravan /KA-ruh-van/
noun

"Centuries ago, a caravan of merchants carried silk across the desert."

noun

📝 Grammar: 8A: Choosing between gerunds and infinitives

A gerund is a verb ending in **-ing** that acts like a noun. An infinitive is **to** plus the base verb, as in **to disembark** or **to traverse**. Use the **gerund after prepositions** and after verbs like **enjoy, avoid, suggest, finish**. She suggested **extending** her sojourn; they finished **planning** the expedition. Use the **infinitive after adjectives** and to express **purpose**. It is also required after verbs like **want, decide, hope, learn**. He decided **to traverse** the valley on foot. Some verbs change meaning with each form. **Remember to disembark** means do not forget;
