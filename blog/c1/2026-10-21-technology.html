📚 Vocabulary

7 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
AI /ay-EYE/
noun
Artificial intelligence; computer systems performing human-like tasks.
"AI is transforming many industries rapidly."
analogue /AN-uh-log/
adjective
Relating to a signal or data that is continuous.
"Many older devices still use analogue technology."
biotechnology /BY-oh-tek-NOL-uh-jee/
noun
Using living organisms to develop products or processes.
"Biotechnology offers solutions for medicine and agriculture."
Bluetooth /BLOO-tooth/
noun
Wireless technology for short-range device communication.
"My headphones connect to my phone via Bluetooth."
cloning /KLOH-ning/
noun
The process of creating an exact genetic copy of an organism.
"Ethical debates surround the potential of human cloning."
designer baby /dih-ZY-nur BAY-bee/
noun phrase
A baby whose genetic makeup is chosen by parents.
"The ethics of creating a designer baby are widely debated."
desktop /DESK-top/
noun
A computer designed for use on a desk.
"My new desktop has a very powerful processor."

📝Grammar: Causative: Have/Get Something Done

We use **have something done** when we arrange for another person to do something for us, usually a service. *I **had my hair cut** yesterday* — a hairdresser did the cutting, not me. **Get something done** means the same and is more informal. *I need to **get my car fixed**.* If your Bluetooth speaker dies, you might **get your Bluetooth** speaker repaired instead of fixing it yourself. Word order matters: the object comes **before** the past participle. *I had **my hair** cut* means someone cut it for me. *I had cut my hair* is past perfect — it means I did it myself.
