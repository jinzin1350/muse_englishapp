📝Grammar: The Passive: All Tenses and Passive Infinitive

The passive **moves the receiver of the action to the front of the sentence**. Form it with **be + past participle**: *The deposit was placed in escrow.* Use the passive **when the doer is unknown, obvious, or unimportant**. *Ticket prices are kept high by the oligopoly* puts the focus on the prices, not on who keeps them high. The passive **works in every tense**. *The old network was controlled by a monopoly* is past simple, and *the valley has been restored* is present perfect. *That wistful smile will never be forgotten* is the future passive. **Only the verb be changes**; the past part
