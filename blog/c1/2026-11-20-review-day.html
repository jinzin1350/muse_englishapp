📖 Muse English چیست؟

Muse English یک اپلیکیشن رایگان آموزش زبان انگلیسی است، با درس روزانه برای هر شش سطح، از A1 (مبتدی) تا C2 (تسلط کامل). هر روز لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، یک پادکست واقعی، تمرین شدوئینگ، گرامر و آزمون دارید. دربارهٔ اپ

📚 لغت‌ها

۱۰ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
anaesthetist /uh-NEES-thuh-tist/
اسم · noun
A medical doctor specializing in administering anaesthesia.
متخصص بیهوشی
"The anaesthetist carefully monitored the patient's vital signs."
adverse /ad-VERS/
صفت · adjective
Unfavorable or harmful.
نامطلوب، مضر
"The drug had several adverse side effects."
squat /SKWAHT/
فعل · verb
To illegally occupy an empty building or land.
تصرف غیرقانونی (ملک)
"The activists decided to squat in the abandoned factory."
child labour /CHYLD LAY-bur/
اسم · noun
The employment of children in industry or business.
کار کودک
"Many organizations fight against child labour worldwide."
collaboration /kuh-lab-uh-RAY-shun/
اسم · noun
The act of working together to create something.
همکاری، مشارکت
"The project was a successful collaboration between two teams."
aches and pains /AYKS and PAYNZ/
اصطلاح · idiom
Minor physical discomforts or soreness.
درد و کوفتگی، دردهای جزئی
"After the long hike, I had a lot of aches and pains."
matter of life and death /MAT-er uv LIFE and DEATH/
اصطلاح · idiom
A situation that is extremely important and serious.
مسئله مرگ و زندگی
"For a surgeon, every decision can be a matter of life and death."
alternative medicine /awl-TUR-nuh-tiv MED-uh-sin/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Medical treatments not part of conventional Western medicine.
طب جایگزین
"She explored alternative medicine for her chronic pain."
career /kuh-REER/
اسم · noun
A profession or occupation pursued for a lifetime.
شغل، حرفه
"She decided to pursue a career in medicine."
acute /uh-KYOOT/
صفت · adjective
(Of a condition) severe and sudden in onset.
حاد، شدید
"He suffered an acute appendicitis attack."

پادکست: روز مرور

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today is a special review day, so we are going to revisit words from the last three days and really lock them in.

Lena

And I am Lena. I love review days, because repetition in real stories makes words stick.

Maya

Exactly. Today we will talk about work, health, and homes, and every story will bring back familiar words. Let us start with Sara, a doctor who chose a career in medicine because she wanted work that mattered.

Lena

Sara told me that her career was never just a job. She said, if you choose a career in a hospital, some decisions become a matter of life and death, and you feel that responsibility every single morning.

Maya

A matter of life and death is not a small problem. For Sara, choosing treatment for a patient in acute pain was a matter of life and death.

Lena

Her first patient last week was a builder with terrible aches and pains after a fall. At first everyone thought his aches and pains were ordinary sore muscles, nothing serious.

Maya

But then the pain became acute. Acute means severe and sudden, and this was acute pain that arrived like a wave. Sara said, when pain turns acute, you stop guessing and you act quickly and carefully.

Lena

She called the anaesthetist immediately. The anaesthetist is the doctor who specialises in anaesthesia, and a good anaesthetist watches every vital sign while the patient sleeps safely through an operation.

Maya

This anaesthetist was brilliant. The anaesthetist explained the anaesthesia clearly, checked for any adverse reaction, and stayed calm when the room became tense.

Lena

Adverse is our word for unfavourable or harmful. A medicine can have an adverse effect, weather can be adverse, and a report can be adverse if it brings bad news.

Maya

Before we go further, let me share something that helps learners like Sara. The Muse English app gives you fresh words every day with clear photos, pronunciation you can hear, and short quizzes that check what you really remember. You can shadow stories, follow the grammar path, earn points, and see your progress. It is free to install, and a few minutes each morning can turn new words into lasting confidence.

Lena

That daily habit matters. Now, back to Sara. Her hospital could not carry out the operation alone, so nurses, surgeons, and the anaesthetist had to carry out a careful plan together, step by step.

Maya

That is true collaboration. Collaboration means working together to create something, and this collaboration saved time because everyone trusted each other.

Lena

Sara keeps saying that collaboration is the heart of her career. She has seen collaboration between hospitals, charities, and her own team, and every collaboration taught her something new.

Maya

Her work is challenging, of course. A challenging night can leave you with aches and pains of your own, tired feet, and a sore back.

Lena

After the operation, the builder asked about alternative medicine. He wondered if alternative medicine could help his recovery, perhaps gentle exercises or herbal teas alongside regular treatment.

Maya

Sara was respectful. She said alternative medicine can comfort some people, but alternative medicine should never replace urgent care when a problem is acute or when it is truly a matter of life and death.

Lena

She suggested he talk to an acupuncturist too, because an acupuncturist in her clinic helped some patients relax. She warned him to watch for any adverse effect and tell his doctor at once.

Maya

Good advice. An adverse reaction can start small, but an adverse reaction to a new drug is never something to ignore, especially after surgery, and especially for a patient who is already weak.

Lena

The builder recovered well, by the way. His aches and pains faded each day, those ordinary aches and pains became weaker, and soon he was walking the corridor and smiling at everyone he met.

Maya

Let us change the scene now, from the hospital to the city, because Sara's brother Omar works for a housing charity, and his career could not be more different from hers.

Lena

Omar cares about social housing. He believes safe social housing changes lives, because a family cannot study, work, or rest properly when they have no stable home to return to at night.

Maya

Omar told us about a family who felt forced to squat in an empty building last winter. To squat means to occupy a building illegally, and people rarely choose to squat unless every other door has closed.

Lena

He said, nobody should have to squat in a cold factory, and nobody plans to squat for months. Yet when rents rise and waiting lists grow, some desperate people still squat and hope nobody notices.

Maya

At the other end of the city, a developer was selling a penthouse with a roof garden and glass walls. That penthouse cost more than forty homes, and Omar said the contrast between a penthouse and a squat tells the whole story of his city.

Lena

It really does. Omar's job involves a lot of cold calling, calling landlords he has never met to ask for fair rents. Cold calling is hard, he hates cold calling, but cold calling sometimes finds a kind owner.

Maya

And his career prospects are growing because of that work. His manager says his career prospects are excellent, since charities need people who can speak to families, landlords, and councils with equal patience and honesty.

Lena

When Omar finally found a flat for the family, the landlady welcomed them warmly and said, please, make yourself at home. She told them to make yourself at home, put pictures on the walls, and rest properly at last.

Maya

The flat itself was nothing to write home about, just two small rooms and an old kitchen. Omar laughed and said, it is nothing to write home about, but it is warm, safe, and theirs, and that is everything.

Lena

The landlady even said their first month of heating was on the house, completely free as a welcome gift. A free coffee in a cafe can be on the house, and here kindness put real warmth on the house too.

Maya

Can I pause for our second reminder, friends? If you want these review words to stay with you, open the Muse English app today and try the shadowing story. You listen to each sentence, speak along with the voice, and repeat until the words feel natural in your mouth. The app also brings back your saved words at the right time for review, gives you a daily podcast like this one, and celebrates your streak when you finish. Install it free, choose your level, and let a small daily routine build a big change over time.

Lena

Beautifully said. Now, Omar's work also connects to a darker topic we studied this week, child labour. His charity met a teenager who had missed school for years because of child labour in a distant supply chain.

Maya

Child labour means children working in industry or business instead of learning and playing. Fighting child labour is not only a legal duty, Maya believes, it is a moral one, and child labour steals a childhood that never comes back.

Lena

Omar said his organisation works in collaboration with schools to stop child labour. That collaboration gives families support, so children can leave child labour behind and return to classrooms where they belong.

Maya

Sara sees the health side of the same problem. Children hurt by child labour often arrive with aches and pains that have been ignored for years, and sometimes with an acute injury that needs urgent care.

Lena

And in those moments, care becomes a matter of life and death again. Sara said a delayed visit can turn a treatable problem into a matter of life and death, which is why early help matters so deeply.

Maya

Let us recap our key words slowly, in context, before we finish. A career is the profession you pursue over a lifetime, and Sara's career, Omar's career, and your own future career all grow through daily choices and honest effort.

Lena

An anaesthetist gives anaesthesia and guards a patient during surgery. Remember, the anaesthetist stays awake so the patient can sleep safely, and a skilled anaesthetist notices small changes before they become serious problems.

Maya

Acute describes something severe and sudden, like acute pain or an acute illness. The opposite feeling is mild and slow, so when a doctor says acute, listen carefully and act without delay.

Lena

Adverse means unfavourable or harmful, as in adverse effects, adverse weather, or an adverse decision. If a treatment has an adverse effect, report it, because early warnings protect everyone.

Maya

Alternative medicine includes treatments outside conventional Western medicine. Some people find alternative medicine helpful for comfort and calm, but alternative medicine is a companion to urgent care, never a substitute in an emergency.

Lena

Aches and pains are those minor discomforts we all know, the aches and pains after exercise, after a long journey, or after a challenging day at work when your body asks for rest.

Maya

To squat, in our housing story, means to occupy an empty building illegally. People who squat often have no safe choice, and a city that builds enough homes gives fewer families a reason to squat.

Lena

Collaboration means working together to create something. Good collaboration needs listening, patience, and trust, and the best collaboration makes a hard task feel possible for a whole team.

Maya

And a matter of life and death is a situation that is extremely important and serious. Choosing a hospital quickly can be a matter of life and death, and so can speaking up when you see a child in danger.

Lena

Child labour, finally, is work that steals education and childhood from children. Every community that reports child labour, supports families, and funds schools helps end child labour for good.

Maya

That is our review for today. You heard career and collaboration in the world of work, acute, adverse, anaesthetist, aches and pains, and alternative medicine in the world of health, and squat, child labour, and matter of life and death across both stories.

Lena

Keep practising in the app, shadow today's story out loud, and take the quiz while these words are fresh. Small steps every day will carry your English further than one long study session once a month.

Maya

Thank you for listening to Muse English. We are proud of you for reviewing, because review is where learning becomes yours. See you tomorrow for fresh words and new stories.
