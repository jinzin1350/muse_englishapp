📚 Vocabulary

7 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
4x4 /FOR-by-FOR/
noun
A vehicle with four-wheel drive, suitable for rough terrain.
"We rented a 4x4 for our adventurous mountain trip."
all-inclusive holiday /awl-in-KLOO-siv HOL-uh-day/
noun phrase
A vacation where all costs are included in the price.
"We booked an all-inclusive holiday to Mexico."
awe-inspiring /AW-in-SPY-uh-ring/
adjective
Causing a feeling of great wonder and respect.
"The Grand Canyon is an truly awe-inspiring natural wonder."
beep your horn /BEEP yor HORN/
phrase
To make a short, loud sound with a car horn.
"Please don't beep your horn unnecessarily in residential areas."
berth /BURTH/
noun
A sleeping place on a ship, train, or plane.
"I booked a comfortable berth for the overnight train journey."
breathalyser /BRETH-uh-ly-zer/
noun
A device used to measure alcohol in breath.
"Police used a breathalyser to check for drunk driving."
budget airline /BUJ-it AIR-lyne/
noun phrase
An airline offering cheap flights with fewer services.
"We flew with a budget airline to save money."

📝 Podcast Transcript

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! Today, Lena and I are planning the perfect trip, and we are going to talk about travel in a way that will really stretch your English.

Lena

I love this topic, Maya, because travel is where advanced English comes alive. We are not just booking tickets. We are choosing how we travel, where we sleep, and how we stay safe on the road.

Maya

Exactly. Let us start with flights, because that is usually the first decision. Lena, are you a budget airline person, or do you prefer a full-service airline?

Lena

Honestly, I love a budget airline when the trip is short. A budget airline keeps the ticket cheap, and I would rather spend my money at the destination than on the plane.

Maya

That makes sense. A budget airline usually charges extra for bags and food, but if you travel light, a budget airline is a smart choice. Last year I flew with a budget airline to the coast, and the flight was perfectly fine.

Lena

The trick with a budget airline is to read the rules carefully. A budget airline can be a bargain, but only if you know exactly what is included.

Lena

Before we go further into mountain adventures, let me tell you about something that makes learning English easier every single day. If you are enjoying this conversation, you will love the Muse English app. It gives you fresh words every day with clear photos and pronunciation, plus this podcast, shadowing stories, quick quizzes, grammar lessons, and a leaderboard where you can earn points as you learn. It is free to install, and it turns small daily moments into real progress. So grab the app today and keep your English moving forward with us.

Maya

And speaking of what is included, let us talk about the other end of the scale. Have you ever booked an all-inclusive holiday?

Lena

Once, and I loved it. An all-inclusive holiday means your hotel, your meals, and often your drinks are all in one price. An all-inclusive holiday takes away all the little decisions, so you can just relax.

Maya

That is the beauty of an all-inclusive holiday. When you choose an all-inclusive holiday, you pay once and then you stop worrying about the bill. For a family trip, an all-inclusive holiday can actually save money, because nobody is arguing about where to eat every night.

Lena

True, but I also love adventure, and an all-inclusive holiday is not always adventurous. Sometimes I want mountains, dirt roads, and a real sense of freedom. That is when I dream about a four by four.

Maya

Oh, a 4x4! A 4x4 is a vehicle with four-wheel drive, built for rough terrain. If you rent a 4x4, you can leave the smooth highway behind and climb into the hills.

Lena

I rented a 4x4 once in the mountains, and it changed the whole trip. A 4x4 can handle gravel, mud, and steep tracks that a normal car cannot. Driving a 4x4 slowly up a mountain road, with the windows down, felt like real freedom.

Maya

I can picture it. And at the top, the view must have been incredible.

Lena

It was awe-inspiring. Truly awe-inspiring. The valley below was golden in the evening light, and the whole scene was awe-inspiring in a way that photos never capture.

Maya

That word fits perfectly. An awe-inspiring view is one that fills you with wonder and respect. I felt the same standing at the edge of a huge canyon. It was awe-inspiring, almost silent, and I just stood there thinking how small we are. An awe-inspiring place stays with you long after you leave.

Maya

Now, back to the journey. Not every adventure needs a car. Sometimes the best travel happens on a train, especially an overnight train.

Lena

Yes! And on an overnight train, your berth is everything. A berth is your sleeping place on a train, a ship, or a plane, usually a small bed tucked into the wall.

Maya

I booked a berth on a night train once, and it was wonderful. My berth was narrow but comfortable, and I fell asleep to the gentle rocking of the train. When you book a berth, you wake up in a new city, which feels like magic.

Lena

I agree. A comfortable berth turns a long journey into part of the holiday. I always check the berth before I book, because a good berth means a good night of sleep, and a good night of sleep means a happy traveller.

Maya

That brings us to a quick pause, because I want to remind you about the Muse English app. Every day it brings you new words with photos and clear pronunciation, this podcast to train your ear, shadowing stories to train your mouth, quizzes to test yourself, grammar lessons to build accuracy, and points and a leaderboard to keep you motivated. It is free, it is fun, and it is made for learners exactly like you. Download it today and make English part of your daily routine.

Maya

Now let us talk about driving, because driving in a new country has its own rules. Lena, what is the one thing drivers always get wrong abroad?

Lena

The horn! In some countries, drivers beep your horn at everything. To beep your horn means to make a short, loud sound with the car horn, and it can mean very different things in different places.

Maya

Exactly. In one city, people beep your horn to say hello. In another, they beep your horn when they are impatient. My advice is simple. Do not beep your horn unnecessarily, especially in quiet residential areas. If you beep your horn all the time, you will annoy everyone around you.

Lena

And there is a safety point here too. If you are driving, never drink and drive. The police can stop you and ask you to use a breathalyser.

Maya

A breathalyser is a small device that measures alcohol in your breath. If the police use a breathalyser, they can tell quickly whether a driver is over the limit.

Lena

I think a breathalyser is a good idea. A breathalyser keeps the roads safer for everyone, and knowing that a breathalyser check can happen at any time makes people think twice. Nobody wants to fail a breathalyser test and lose their licence.

Maya

Safety first, always. Now, let us put all of this together into a dream trip. Imagine this. You fly with a budget airline to save money, then you rent a 4x4 for the mountain part of the journey.

Lena

I love it. You drive your 4x4 up into the hills, you reach an awe-inspiring viewpoint, and you watch the sunset. Then you take an overnight train, sleep in your berth, and wake up by the sea.

Maya

And at the sea, you check into an all-inclusive holiday resort for three days of rest. An all-inclusive holiday after a mountain adventure is the perfect balance. You have earned the rest.

Lena

Perfect balance indeed. And on the way home, you drive carefully, you never beep your horn without a reason, and you respect the breathalyser rules. That is how a thoughtful traveller moves through the world.

Lena

Back to travel. Maya, if you had to choose one kind of trip for next year, what would it be?

Maya

I would choose the mountains again. A 4x4, a winding road, and an awe-inspiring sunrise are hard to beat. What about you?

Lena

I would choose the train. Give me a comfortable berth, a good book, and a slow journey through beautiful countryside. A berth on a night train feels like a small hotel on wheels.

Maya

Both sound wonderful. And for listeners planning their own trips, remember the vocabulary we used today. A budget airline saves money. An all-inclusive holiday saves decisions. A 4x4 opens up rough roads. An awe-inspiring view opens up your heart.

Lena

A berth gives you sleep on the move. To beep your horn is something to do sparingly and politely. And a breathalyser is the device that keeps drunk drivers off the road. Seven useful words, all from real travel.

Maya

Let us practise them one more time in a little story. Sara flew with a budget airline to the mountains. She rented a 4x4 and drove to an awe-inspiring canyon. That night she slept in a berth on a night train, and the next morning she arrived at her all-inclusive holiday resort, rested and happy. On the drive home she did not beep your horn once, and she was glad the breathalyser rules keep everyone safe.

Maya

One more tip for listeners. When you plan a trip, write down your transport words and say them out loud. Say budget airline when you compare flights, say all-inclusive holiday when you compare hotels, and say 4x4 when you look at mountain roads. Saying an awe-inspiring view out loud helps you remember it, and saying berth helps you book that night train with confidence.

Lena

And say beep your horn only when you really need to, and remember that a breathalyser check is there to protect everyone. Real travel language is not about sounding fancy. It is about being clear, polite, and safe wherever you go.

Lena

That is a perfect summary. Travel gives us stories, and stories give us language. Keep listening, keep repeating, and keep travelling, even if today you are only travelling through English.

Maya

Thanks for listening to Muse English. We will see you tomorrow with new words, a new story, and a new adventure. Goodbye for now.

Lena

Goodbye, and happy travels.

📝Grammar: The Passive: All Tenses and Passive Infinitive

The passive is **be + past participle**. To make any tense passive, put **be** in that tense. For example, **is being painted** is present continuous passive, **has been finished** is present perfect passive, and **will be announced** is future passive. Modal verbs also form passives with **modal + be + past participle**, as in **must be signed** and **can be done**. For the past, use **modal + have been + past participle**, as in **should have been sent**.
