📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
career /kuh-REER/
اسم · noun
A profession or occupation pursued for a lifetime.
شغل، حرفه
"She decided to pursue a career in medicine."
career prospects /kuh-REER PROS-pekts/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Opportunities for future advancement in a profession.
چشم‌انداز شغلی
"The company offers excellent career prospects for new graduates."
carry out /KA-ree OUT/
فعل عبارتی · phrasal verb
To perform or complete a task or activity.
انجام دادن، اجرا کردن
"The team will carry out the necessary repairs tomorrow."
challenging /CHAL-un-jing/
صفت · adjective
Difficult in an interesting or stimulating way.
چالش‌برانگیز
"The new project is challenging but very rewarding."
child labour /CHYLD LAY-bur/
اسم · noun
The employment of children in industry or business.
کار کودک
"Many organizations fight against child labour worldwide."
cold calling /KOHLD KAW-ling/
اسم · noun
Unsolicited phone calls to potential customers.
بازاریابی تلفنی (بدون سابقه)
"Many sales jobs involve a lot of cold calling."
collaboration /kuh-lab-uh-RAY-shun/
اسم · noun
The act of working together to create something.
همکاری، مشارکت
"The project was a successful collaboration between two teams."

📝گرامر: Probability: Bound To, May Well, Likely To

Beyond will, might and probably, English has many precise ways to say how likely something is.

For something almost certain, use be bound to, be sure to or be certain to plus a verb. After a challenging year of cold calling, Nadia was bound to look for a new career.

For something probable, use be likely to, or may well, might well and could well plus a verb. A worker with strong collaboration skills may well get promoted, and her career prospects are likely to improve.

You can also use there's a good chance that, in all likelihood,
