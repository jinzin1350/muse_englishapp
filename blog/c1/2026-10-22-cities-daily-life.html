📚 Vocabulary

7 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
have the time of your life /hav thuh TYM uv yawr LYF/
idiom
To enjoy oneself immensely.
"We had the time of our lives at the party."
high-rise (flat) /HY-ryz (flat)/
noun phrase
A tall building with many floors.
"They live in a modern high-rise flat downtown."
hit home /HIT HOHM/
idiom
To be fully understood and have a strong effect.
"His words about poverty really hit home."
home truth /HOHM TROOTH/
noun phrase
An unpleasant fact about oneself that is true.
"My friend told me some home truths about my spending habits."
household word /HOWS-hohld WURD/
noun phrase
A person or thing widely known by everyone.
"Coca-Cola has become a household word globally."
hovel /HUV-uhl/
noun
A small, dirty, and miserable dwelling.
"They lived in a tiny, dilapidated hovel."
lead a sheltered life /LEED uh SHEL-turd LYF/
phrase
To live protected from difficult or unpleasant experiences.
"She led a sheltered life, unaware of real-world challenges."

📝Grammar: Concession: Albeit, Much As, Notwithstanding

Concession means accepting one fact while adding something that contrasts with it. At B1 you used *although*, *despite* and *in spite of*; at C1 you can be more precise and formal. **Albeit** joins an adjective, adverb or short phrase — never a full clause. *He bought the high-rise flat, **albeit** on a tight budget.* **Much as** opens a clause and means 'although … very much'. ***Much as** she loved her tiny hovel, she dreamed of a bigger home.* **Notwithstanding** is formal and sits before or after a noun. ***Notwithstanding** the high rent, they had the time of their lives downtown.* Ot
