📚 لغت‌ها

۱۰ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
MP3 player /em-pee-THREE PLAY-er/
اسم · noun
A portable electronic device for playing digital audio.
پخش کننده MP3
"I used my MP3 player to listen to music on the bus."
population explosion /pop-yuh-LAY-shun ek-SPLOH-zhun/
اسم · noun
A sudden, rapid increase in population size.
انفجار جمعیت
"The population explosion puts strain on global resources."
bureaucratic /byoor-uh-KRAT-ik/
صفت · adjective
Relating to a system of administration with many rules.
بوروکراتیک، دیوان‌سالارانه
"Dealing with the bureaucratic paperwork was frustrating."
nuclear family /NOO-klee-er FAM-uh-lee/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
A family unit of parents and their children.
خانواده هسته‌ای (والدین و فرزندان)
"The nuclear family is still common in many cultures."
bursary /BUR-suh-ree/
اسم · noun
A grant, usually for students, to help with costs.
کمک هزینه تحصیلی
"She received a bursary to cover her university tuition fees."
deep poverty /DEEP PUV-er-tee/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Extreme lack of money and basic necessities.
فقر شدید
"Millions of people worldwide live in deep poverty."
debtor country /DET-er KUN-tree/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
A country that owes money to other countries.
کشور بدهکار
"The debtor country sought an extension on its loan repayments."
bury yourself in your books /BER-ee yor-SELF in yor BOOKS/
اصطلاح · idiom
To study very hard and intensely, ignoring everything else.
غرق مطالعه شدن
"He had to bury himself in his books before the final exams."
debit card /DEB-it KARD/
اسم · noun
A card used to pay directly from a bank account.
کارت بانکی، کارت بدهی
"I paid for groceries with my debit card."
debt relief /DET ri-LEEF/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
The partial or total cancellation of debts.
بخشودگی بدهی
"The organization advocates for debt relief for poor countries."

📝گرامر: The Unreal Past (It's time we left, I'd rather you didn't)

After certain expressions, we use a past tense to talk about the present or future. The past form shows the situation is unreal, imagined or wanted, as when a student in a nuclear family says it is time she received her bursary.

Use It's time, It's high time and It's about time + subject + past when something should happen now. It's about time adds criticism, for example when a bureaucratic office still has not offered debt relief to a debtor country.

With would rather, use the base form for the same subject: I'd rather stay. Wi
