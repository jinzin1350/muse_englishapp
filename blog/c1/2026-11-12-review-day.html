📚 لغت‌ها

۱۰ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
chatty /CHAT-ee/
صفت · adjective
Fond of talking; talkative.
پرحرف، وراج
"She's a very chatty person and loves meeting new people."
brutal suppression /BROO-tul suh-PRESH-un/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Harsh and violent control or crushing of dissent.
سرکوب وحشیانه
"The government's brutal suppression of protests drew international condemnation."
approachable /uh-PROH-chuh-bul/
صفت · adjective
Friendly and easy to talk to.
صمیمی، قابل دسترس
"Her approachable demeanor made students feel comfortable asking questions."
blowy /BLOH-ee/
صفت · adjective
Windy; characterized by strong breezes.
بادی، پرباد
"It was a blowy day, perfect for flying kites."
hands-free /HANDZ-FREE/
صفت · adjective
Operating without needing to be held.
بدون نیاز به دست، هندزفری
"Her new car has a hands-free phone system."
breezy /BREE-zee/
صفت · adjective
Pleasantly windy; light and fresh.
نسیم‌دار، خنک
"It was a breezy afternoon, perfect for a walk."
blissfully unaware /BLISS-fuh-lee un-uh-WAIR/
گروه وصفی · adjective phrase
Happily not knowing about something negative.
بی‌خبر و بی‌خیال، غافل
"She was blissfully unaware of the gossip circulating about her."
bulk up /BULK UP/
فعل عبارتی · phrasal verb
To gain muscle mass and become bigger.
عضله آوردن، حجم گرفتن
"He's trying to bulk up for the bodybuilding competition."
mega-city /MEG-uh SIT-ee/
اسم · noun
A very large city, typically with over ten million people.
کلان‌شهر، ابرشهر
"Tokyo is a prime example of a bustling mega-city."
biomass /BY-oh-mass/
اسم · noun
Organic matter used as fuel or for energy production.
زیست‌توده
"Burning biomass can be a renewable energy source."

Hello, I am Lena, and I love review days. We talked about technology in a mega-city, about emotions and character, and about nature and the environment. A mega-city can feel huge and noisy, but today we will travel through all those ideas slowly, one story at a time.

Maya

Let us start in a mega-city on a blowy morning. The wind was strong, the streets were busy, and my neighbour, a very chatty taxi driver, started talking before I had even closed the door. He is chatty in the morning, chatty at lunch, and honestly, chatty at midnight too.

Lena

I know that type, and I like it when a chatty person is also approachable. An approachable driver makes passengers relax. You can ask an approachable person a question without fear, and an approachable teacher, for example, helps students speak more. Was your driver approachable as well?

Maya

Completely approachable. He smiled, he listened, and he used a hands-free phone system so both hands stayed on the wheel. Hands-free technology matters in a busy city, because a hands-free call is safer than holding a phone. My car has a hands-free system too, although I still prefer silence when traffic is heavy.

Lena

Speaking of traffic, did you meet gridlock on the way? Last week the whole centre was in gridlock for two hours, and nobody moved at all. Gridlock teaches you patience, or at least it tests your patience in a very honest way.

Maya

We avoided gridlock by leaving early, and the morning turned breezy and bright. Breezy weather in a big city feels like a gift. It was breezy on the bridge, breezy in the park, and later it became blowy again near the river, so my hair went in every direction at once.

Lena

A blowy day can be funny and annoying at the same time. It was blowy when we visited a bird sanctuary last spring, and the birds seemed to enjoy the wind more than we did. A bird sanctuary is a protected place, and inside a bird sanctuary, quiet visitors can watch rare birds without disturbing them.

Maya

That trip sounds peaceful, very different from city life. And yet, technology connects those worlds. Today we live in a global village, where a message from a bird sanctuary can reach a classroom on another continent in seconds. The idea of a global village explains why English matters so much for travellers and scientists.

Lena

It also explains why a student with a laptop computer can learn anywhere. I take my laptop computer to the library, to trains, and even to the park on a breezy afternoon. A laptop computer is small, but it can hold a whole library of lessons, podcasts, and quizzes.

Maya

Exactly, and science gives us even bigger dreams. Scientists who study the human genome ask questions about health and family history. The human genome project changed medicine, because understanding the human genome helps doctors understand some diseases much earlier.

Lena

And beyond medicine, people dream about interplanetary travel. Interplanetary travel sounds like a film, but engineers discuss interplanetary travel seriously now. A journey to Mars would need new fuel, new food systems, and very brave travellers.

Maya

Fuel brings us neatly to biomass. Biomass is organic matter used for energy, such as wood, plants, and agricultural waste. A village can heat homes with biomass, a farm can produce power from biomass, and researchers are finding cleaner ways to burn biomass every year.

Before we continue, here is a word about learning with Muse English. That is a lovely reminder, Maya. Now, back to people and feelings. Yesterday we described a young athlete who wants to bulk up for the new season. To bulk up safely, he eats more protein, trains with heavier weights, and sleeps well. His coach says nobody should bulk up too quickly, because health always comes first.

Lena

Families also have that one person who is blissfully unaware of drama. My uncle was blissfully unaware of the surprise party we planned for him, and he walked in blissfully unaware of the cameras, the cake, and the twenty guests hiding behind the sofa. Everyone laughed, because being blissfully unaware made the surprise perfect.

Maya

Blissfully unaware is such a useful phrase. A child can be blissfully unaware of a storm outside while reading a story, and a traveller can be blissfully unaware of a delayed train until the announcement finally wakes him. Sometimes blissfully unaware is peaceful, and sometimes it creates a problem later.

Lena

That connects to its cousins. Someone who is blissfully ignorant does not know about something bad, and stays happy because of that. He remained blissfully ignorant of the office problems all weekend. A couple who look blissfully happy, on the other hand, are simply extremely happy, and the newlyweds looked blissfully happy in every photo. And the adverb blissfully on its own means in perfect happiness, as in, she smiled blissfully in the morning sun.

Lena

Now for a heavier part of our review. History lessons sometimes describe brutal suppression, meaning harsh and violent control of people who protest. A government that uses brutal suppression loses trust, and journalists around the world report brutal suppression because the world should know what happened.

Maya

It is a serious phrase, and learners should use brutal suppression only for serious situations. In the same history book, we read about families living on the breadline during hard economic years. The breadline means extreme poverty, and a family on the breadline must count every coin for food and rent.

Lena

And another difficult term was blood sport. A blood sport is a sport where animals are killed or injured, and many activists want every blood sport banned. Discussing a blood sport in class helped us practise giving opinions politely and clearly in English.

Maya

True, and a mega-city gives us endless examples. A mega-city has millions of people, huge stations, and neighbourhoods that feel like small towns. Living in a mega-city can be exciting, but a mega-city also needs clean energy, and that is where biomass returns to our story. A modern mega-city might burn biomass for district heating, grow food on roofs, and still dream about interplanetary travel at its space museum.

Lena

You are connecting everything beautifully. Let me test our listeners with a few quick scenes. Scene one, a chatty, approachable guide welcomes visitors to a bird sanctuary. Scene two, an athlete decides to bulk up before winter training. Scene three, a grandmother is blissfully unaware of the rain because she is reading happily indoors. Can you picture each scene and say the key words aloud?

Maya

Scene four, the wind turns blowy, then breezy, then blowy again, and a hands-free weather app on my phone warns me to go home. A blowy hilltop is no place for a paper map. A hands-free call lets a driver keep both hands on the wheel. A breezy garden party feels light, fresh, and relaxed. A cat sleeping through a thunderstorm is blissfully unaware of the noise. A rugby player may bulk up with careful training and good food. A mega-city never really sleeps, because its trains, markets, and hospitals work all night. And biomass can turn farm waste into useful heat and power.

Lena

That recap was clear, and I will add one more round, quickly and naturally. Our chatty guide was approachable, so everyone asked questions. The day was breezy at noon and blowy by four o clock. I made a hands-free call, heard that an athlete friend wants to bulk up, and smiled at a dog that was blissfully unaware of the busy street. Perfect. And do not forget the other friends from this week. Blissfully ignorant, blissfully happy, and the simple word blissfully itself. 📝گرامر: Ellipsis and Substitution

Natural English avoids repeating words. We either replace them with a short word, which is substitution, or leave them out, which is ellipsis.

Use one for a singular countable noun and ones for a plural noun. A chatty guide in a mega-city might say, this tour is full, but the breezy evening one still has places.

Use do, does or did to replace a verb phrase. She speaks French better than I do, and an approachable teacher answers more questions than most do.

Use so to replace a positive clause and not to replace a negative clause after v
