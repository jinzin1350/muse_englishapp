📚 Vocabulary

10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
upskill /up-SKIL/
verb
to learn new skills for your current job or a future one
"She took an evening course to upskill before the promotion interview."
networking /NET-wur-king/
noun
meeting people and building professional relationships
"She found her new job through networking at an industry conference."
schadenfreude /SHAA-dun-froy-duh/
noun
pleasure taken from someone else's misfortune
"He felt a flash of schadenfreude when his arrogant rival lost the competition."
scholastic /skuh-LAS-tik/
adjective
relating to schools or studies
"She won a scholarship for scholastic achievement."
philomath /FIL-uh-math/
noun
a person who loves learning
"He is a true philomath who reads two books a week."
exegesis /ek-suh-JEE-sis/
noun
a careful, critical interpretation of a text
"The professor gave an exegesis of the poem's final stanza."
vindictive /vin-DIK-tiv/
adjective
seeking revenge; wanting to hurt someone who hurt you
"She sent a vindictive email after being passed over for the promotion."
imperturbable /im-pur-TUR-buh-bul/
adjective
so calm that nothing can disturb or upset you
"Her imperturbable manner made her an excellent negotiator under pressure."
unflappable /un-FLAP-uh-bul/
adjective
staying calm and steady in a crisis or under pressure
"The unflappable nurse handled the emergency with steady hands."
quixotic /kwik-SOT-ik/
adjective
extremely idealistic and unrealistic; pursuing noble but impractical goals
"His quixotic plan to build a floating city attracted only dreamers."

📝Grammar: Wish and If Only

We use **wish** and **if only** to describe realities we find unsatisfactory. After **wish**, the verb shifts one step back in time: a present wish takes the past simple (**I wish I were more imperturbable under pressure**), while a past regret takes the past perfect (**I wish I had networked more strategically in my twenties**). **If only** follows the same grammar as **wish** but carries greater emotional force. **If only** every young philomath enjoyed such patient mentorship!
