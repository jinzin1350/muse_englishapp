گروه اسمی · noun phrase
The non-wage compensation offered to employees.

A person who offers a price for something.

body language /BOD-ee LANG-gwij/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Non-verbal communication through gestures and posture.

"Her confident body language impressed the interviewers."
bonus /BOH-nus/
اسم · noun
An extra payment given for good performance.
پاداش، مزایای اضافی
"Employees received a generous bonus at the end of the year."
boost your confidence /BOOST yor KON-fuh-dense/
گروه فعلی · verb phrase
To increase your self-assurance.

پادکست: کار و حرفه

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and I am here with Lena. Today we are talking about work and careers, and I have a story for you about a friend of mine who got a job offer that changed his life.

Lena

I love a good career story, Maya. And today we have seven useful words for you, so listen for them as we talk. The words are benefits package, bidder, body language, bonus, boost your confidence, brand loyalty, and capital asset.

Maya

Let us start at the beginning. My friend Daniel is a marketing manager. He works for a small company that sells coffee, and he loves his job, but last month he got a phone call from a much bigger company. They wanted him to lead their whole marketing team.

Lena

That is exciting. A bigger company, a bigger team, and probably a better salary. What did Daniel do first?

Maya

He did something very smart. He did not say yes right away. He asked about the benefits package. A benefits package is the extra things a company gives you besides your salary, like health insurance, paid holidays, and a pension. Daniel knew the salary was only part of the story.

Lena

That is such good advice. A strong benefits package can be worth a lot of money. Did the new company offer a good benefits package?

Maya

They did. The benefits package included private health insurance, twenty-five days of paid holiday, and a gym membership. Daniel was happy, but he also asked about the bonus. A bonus is extra money you get when you do your job well, and Daniel wanted to know how the bonus worked.

Lena

Before we continue the story, a quick word about the Muse English app. Every day you get new words with photos and clear pronunciation, this podcast, shadowing stories to practise speaking, quizzes, grammar lessons, and points on the leaderboard. It is free, so download it today and make English part of your daily routine, even for just a few minutes. You can learn anywhere, on the bus, at lunch, or before bed.

Lena

A bonus can really change your income. Did they offer him a bonus?

Maya

Yes, they offered a yearly bonus based on the results of his team. If his team reached its goals, Daniel would get a bonus of ten percent of his salary. That is a serious bonus, and it made the offer even more attractive.

Lena

So the benefits package was good, and the bonus was good. What happened next? Did he take the job?

Maya

Not yet. First he had to pass the final interview, and he was nervous. He told me his hands were shaking before the interview, and his voice was shaking too. I told him to take a public speaking course. A good course can boost your confidence, and confidence changes everything in an interview.

Lena

You are right. When you want to boost your confidence, practice helps more than anything. Did Daniel take a course?

Maya

He did, and the course really did boost his confidence. He told me that a good course is the best way to boost your confidence before a big interview. He practised speaking slowly, standing tall, and looking people in the eye. The course taught him that body language matters as much as words. Body language is how you communicate without speaking, through your posture, your gestures, and your face.

Lena

Body language is so important. If your body language says you are nervous, people believe your body, not your words. Did Daniel work on his body language?

Maya

He did. He practised in front of a mirror. He learned to keep his shoulders relaxed and his hands still. His body language became calm and confident, and on the day of the interview, his body language told the managers that he was ready for the job.

Maya

So Daniel went to the interview, and he was wonderful. The managers asked him about brand loyalty. Brand loyalty is when customers keep buying the same brand again and again, because they trust it. Daniel explained how he had built brand loyalty for his coffee company, and how strong brand loyalty can protect a company when prices go up.

Lena

Brand loyalty is a big idea in marketing. If customers have brand loyalty, they do not switch to a cheaper product. How did Daniel build that brand loyalty?

Maya

He told them a story. His coffee company had started a small loyalty programme, and customers who bought coffee every week got a free bag once a month. Slowly, brand loyalty grew, and customers started to recommend the coffee to their friends. That is real brand loyalty, when customers become fans, not just buyers.

Maya

And here is a quick reminder about the Muse English app, because your career in English starts with daily practice. Every day you get new words with photos and clear pronunciation, this podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar lessons, and points on the leaderboard. It is free, so download it today and start building your English, one day at a time.

Lena

That is a great answer in an interview. Did the managers like it?

Maya

They loved it. Then they asked him a harder question, about money. They asked him what a capital asset was. A capital asset is something valuable that a company owns for a long time, like a building, a factory, or expensive equipment. Daniel said the coffee company had just bought a new roasting machine, and that machine was a capital asset, because it would help the company for many years.

Lena

A capital asset is a useful word for business English. Did he give another example of a capital asset?

Maya

Yes, he said the new factory building was also a capital asset. The company had bought the building last year, and it was the biggest capital asset they owned. The managers were impressed, because Daniel understood the business, not just the marketing.

Lena

Daniel sounds like a strong candidate. What happened after the interview?

Maya

He waited for three long days. Then the phone rang, and they offered him the job. The salary was good, the benefits package was generous, and the bonus was fair. Daniel said yes, and he started his new job two weeks later.

Maya

So Daniel started his new job two weeks later, and his first week was busy. On Monday, his new team showed him the office, and on Tuesday, he met the managers again, this time as a colleague, not as a candidate.

Lena

How did he feel in his first week? Was he nervous again?

Maya

A little, but his body language was calm, and he remembered everything from his course. He told me that a good first impression can boost your confidence for the whole month. By Friday, he felt at home.

Lena

Did he learn anything new in his first week?

Maya

Yes. He learned that the company is planning to buy a new delivery van, another capital asset, and he also learned that his team will get a bonus if they finish the new campaign on time. He said the bonus gives everyone a reason to work together.

Lena

That sounds like a company that understands people. A fair bonus, a good benefits package, and a team that trusts each other.

Lena

That is a happy ending. But Maya, you also promised me a story about a bidder. Where does the bidder fit in?

Maya

Ah, I forgot the best part. Before Daniel left his old company, they sold their old coffee roasting machine at an auction. A bidder is a person who offers a price for something at an auction. The highest bidder wins. There were five bidders, and the highest bidder paid more than anyone expected. Daniel laughed and said the bidder who won got a real bargain.

Lena

That is a nice detail. So one bidder won the machine, and the company used the money to buy new equipment. A good ending for everyone.

Maya

Exactly. And Daniel told me the whole experience taught him something important. He said the public speaking course was the best way to boost your confidence, and his body language in the interview made a big difference. He also said that when you understand words like benefits package, bonus, brand loyalty, and capital asset, you can talk about business with real confidence.

Lena

That is true. These words are not just vocabulary, they are tools for your career. When you can talk about a benefits package and a bonus, you can negotiate a better job. When you understand brand loyalty and a capital asset, you can understand how a business works.

Maya

And when you watch your body language and find ways to boost your confidence, you can walk into any interview and feel ready. Daniel is proof of that. He got the job, he loves his new team, and last week he told me his first bonus is already on the way.

Lena

Before we finish, let us remember our seven words. A benefits package is the extra things a company gives you besides salary. A bidder offers a price at an auction. Body language is how you communicate without words. A bonus is extra money for good work. To boost your confidence means to feel more sure of yourself. Brand loyalty is when customers keep buying the same brand. And a capital asset is a valuable thing a company owns for a long time.

Lena

Thank you for listening, everyone. Practise these seven words this week, and try to use them in a real conversation. If you talk about work, you will find a benefits package, a bonus, and a capital asset waiting for you in every discussion. Goodbye from both of us, and we will see you tomorrow on Muse English.

Maya

Goodbye, everyone. Keep learning, keep speaking, and remember, every new word is a step forward in your career.

📝گرامر: Noun Modifiers and Compound Nouns

In English, a noun can describe another noun. The first noun works like an adjective, and the second noun is the main one.

A *shoe shop* is a kind of shop, and a benefits package is a kind of package. The head noun tells you what the thing really is.

Some combinations are one word (*toothbrush*), some are two words (*bus stop*), and some use a hyphen (*a ten-minute walk*).

The first noun is usually singular: a shoe shop, not a shoes shop. A few are plural by tradition, like a sports car or a savings account.
