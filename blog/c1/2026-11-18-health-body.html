📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
aches and pains /AYKS and PAYNZ/
اصطلاح · idiom
Minor physical discomforts or soreness.
درد و کوفتگی، دردهای جزئی
"After the long hike, I had a lot of aches and pains."
acupuncturist /AK-yoo-PUNK-chur-ist/
اسم · noun
A person who practices acupuncture.
متخصص طب سوزنی
"The acupuncturist helped relieve her chronic pain."
acute /uh-KYOOT/
صفت · adjective
(Of a condition) severe and sudden in onset.
حاد، شدید
"He suffered an acute appendicitis attack."
adverse /ad-VERS/
صفت · adjective
Unfavorable or harmful.
نامطلوب، مضر
"The drug had several adverse side effects."
alternative medicine /awl-TUR-nuh-tiv MED-uh-sin/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
Medical treatments not part of conventional Western medicine.
طب جایگزین
"She explored alternative medicine for her chronic pain."
anaesthesia /an-ess-THEE-zee-uh/
اسم · noun
A state of controlled temporary loss of sensation or awareness.
بیهوشی، بی‌حسی
"The patient was given general anaesthesia before surgery."
anaesthetist /uh-NEES-thuh-tist/
اسم · noun
A medical doctor specializing in administering anaesthesia.
متخصص بیهوشی
"The anaesthetist carefully monitored the patient's vital signs."

پادکست: سلامت و بدن

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today Lena and I are talking about health, pain, and the choices people make when their body needs help.

Lena

Hello everyone. I am Lena, and this topic is close to my life right now because after training for a charity run, I woke up with aches and pains in my knees, my back, and even my shoulders.

Maya

Oh, aches and pains after a long run are so common, but they can still worry us. Were your aches and pains just tired muscles, or did you feel an acute pain somewhere?

Lena

At first it was general aches and pains, nothing sharp. Then on Sunday I felt an acute pain in my left knee when I climbed the stairs, and acute pain always gets my attention because it comes suddenly and feels severe.

Maya

That word acute is important. An acute problem starts suddenly and is strong, while a problem that lasts for months is different. Did that acute pain last long?

Lena

Thankfully the acute pain faded after I rested and used ice, but I still called my doctor because I did not want to guess. She asked about adverse effects from a cream I had been using, because even a simple cream can cause adverse reactions on sensitive skin.

Maya

That is a good point about adverse effects. People often think adverse only describes medicine, but we can talk about adverse weather, adverse results, or an adverse reaction to a treatment. Did you have any adverse reaction?

Lena

My skin became red, which was a mild adverse reaction, so I stopped using the cream. My doctor said an adverse effect should never be ignored, even when it seems small at first.

Maya

I am glad you listened to her. A friend of mine had a different path. She had chronic aches and pains in her neck, and after months of poor sleep she visited an acupuncturist for the first time.

Lena

I have always been curious about that. What did the acupuncturist actually do, and did your friend trust the acupuncturist from the start?

Maya

She was nervous, but the acupuncturist explained everything slowly. The acupuncturist asked about her sleep, her work, and exactly where she felt aches and pains, and that careful listening helped her relax.

Lena

So the acupuncturist did more than place needles. The acupuncturist looked at her whole routine. That sounds like alternative medicine at its best, because alternative medicine often focuses on the whole person.

Maya

Exactly. Alternative medicine is a broad term for treatments that are not part of conventional Western medicine. For some people alternative medicine means acupuncture, for others it means herbal medicine or massage, and many people use alternative medicine alongside care from their doctor.

Lena

I like that balance. Alternative medicine should not mean rejecting doctors. It should mean asking questions and choosing safely. When my cousin explored alternative medicine for her aches and pains, her doctor actually supported her because the plan was sensible.

Maya

That balance matters, especially when surgery is possible. Surgery brings two words people often confuse, anaesthesia and anaesthetist. Anaesthesia is the controlled loss of sensation or awareness that lets an operation happen without pain, and an anaesthetist is the specialist doctor who gives it and watches the patient.

Lena

I remember when my father had an operation last year. The anaesthetist visited him the night before and explained how anaesthesia would work. That anaesthetist was so calm that my father finally slept well.

Maya

A good anaesthetist does much more than give anaesthesia. The anaesthetist checks breathing, heart rate, and blood pressure during the whole operation, and adjusts the anaesthesia moment by moment.

Lena

And after the operation, the anaesthetist still watches the patient as the anaesthesia wears off. I did not know that before my father's surgery, but now I have huge respect for every anaesthetist.

Maya

Install Muse English free and make these health words part of your daily routine.

Lena

That daily routine really helps, because hearing a word once is never enough. Speaking of routines, Maya, when should someone with aches and pains see a doctor quickly instead of waiting?

Maya

If the pain is acute, if it follows an accident, if there is fever, or if aches and pains keep getting worse, please do not wait. An acute symptom can be the body's alarm bell, and early help can prevent an adverse outcome later.

Lena

An adverse outcome is a phrase doctors use when treatment or delay leads to harm. I also learned that a medicine can help one person and cause adverse effects in another, so we should always read the leaflet and ask questions.

Maya

Yes, and that is why my friend asked her acupuncturist many questions before treatment. She asked whether acupuncture could have adverse effects, and the acupuncturist honestly said bruising can happen but serious adverse effects are uncommon with a trained practitioner.

Lena

Honesty builds trust. Did the sessions with the acupuncturist reduce her aches and pains in the end?

Maya

After six weeks her aches and pains were weaker, she slept better, and she moved more easily. She still does exercises from her doctor, so for her alternative medicine and conventional care worked together rather than against each other.

Lena

That story shows why alternative medicine interests so many people. People want time, listening, and gentle options, especially for long term aches and pains that affect daily life.

Maya

I agree, but we should stay critical. If someone promises that alternative medicine will cure an acute emergency, that is dangerous. An acute heart problem or acute appendicitis needs a hospital immediately, not a herbal tea.

Lena

Absolutely. My doctor used that exact example. She said acute appendicitis can become life threatening quickly, and delay can have adverse consequences for the whole body.

Maya

Your knee story had a happy ending, but imagine it had needed surgery. Then anaesthesia would have been part of the plan, and you would have met an anaesthetist before the operation.

Lena

I would probably ask the anaesthetist a hundred questions. Which type of anaesthesia is safest for me, how long will the anaesthesia last, and will the anaesthetist stay with me the whole time?

Maya

Those are exactly the right questions, and a good anaesthetist welcomes them. Some anaesthesia numbs only one area, while general anaesthesia makes the patient completely unaware during surgery.

Lena

Here is another short break with an idea for your learning. If these words feel difficult today, the Muse English app can help you review them little by little. Maya

Thank you, Lena. Now, back to our health choices. How do you personally decide between conventional medicine and alternative medicine?

Lena

I start with evidence and safety. For an acute problem I go straight to my doctor. For ongoing aches and pains I am open to alternative medicine, but I check training, I ask about adverse effects, and I tell my doctor what I am trying.

Maya

That is a mature approach. I do something similar. When I visited an acupuncturist for shoulder tension last spring, I chose a qualified acupuncturist, I kept my doctor informed, and I stopped when I noticed an adverse change in my sleep.

Lena

Wait, you saw an acupuncturist too? You never told me that story in full.

Maya

I did, and the acupuncturist helped me notice how stress affected my body. The treatment itself was gentle, and my aches and pains eased, but the bigger lesson was rest. Alternative medicine reminded me that recovery is not only about medicine.

Lena

I love that reflection. Still, I want listeners to remember the specialist words clearly. Anaesthesia is the state or the medicine that prevents pain during an operation, and an anaesthetist is the doctor who manages it. If you remember that anaesthesia is the thing and an anaesthetist is the person, you will not confuse them.

Maya

Perfect summary. And let us review the other words in natural sentences before we finish. After gardening all day, my grandfather has aches and pains in his hands. A sudden acute pain needs attention. A drug can have an adverse effect. An acupuncturist practises acupuncture. And alternative medicine describes treatments outside conventional Western medicine.

Lena

Hearing them again in different sentences really helps them stick. My own plan now is simple. I will rest my knee, watch for any acute pain returning, note any adverse reaction to new creams, and maybe book an acupuncturist for my general aches and pains if my doctor agrees.

Maya

And if surgery ever enters the picture, you will know to ask about anaesthesia and to thank the anaesthetist who keeps you safe. That knowledge makes a stressful day feel less frightening.

Lena

Exactly. Health language gives us confidence because we can describe aches and pains clearly, ask about adverse effects honestly, and understand what an anaesthetist does.

Maya

Thank you for listening to Muse English today. Keep practising these words, try the shadowing story, take the quiz in the app, and join us tomorrow for more daily English.

Lena

Goodbye for now, take care of your body, and remember that small daily practice, like good health habits, brings real results over time.
