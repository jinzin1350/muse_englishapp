📝گرامر: Must, Have To and Should

We use must and have to for obligation, when something is necessary. An active student, for example, has to bring a book to class.

We use should for advice, when something is a good idea. You should write accurately and check your work.

Must and should never take to or -s. She must go, and she should go.

Have to changes like a normal verb. She has to go, yesterday she had to go, and tomorrow she will have to go.

Mustn't means it is not allowed, do not do it. You mustn't use your phone during the exam.

Don't have to me
