📚 Vocabulary

15 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
train /TRAYN/
noun
a long vehicle that carries people on rails
"We took the train to the city."
station /STAY-shun/
noun
the building where trains and buses stop
"The station is full of people."
ticket /TIK-it/
noun
a small paper you buy to travel
"I bought a ticket for the train."
luggage /LUG-ij/
noun
the bags you carry when you travel
"My luggage is very heavy."
map /MAP/
noun
a picture that shows roads and places
"Look at the map and find the hotel."
journey /JUR-nee/
noun
a long trip from one place to another
"The journey took three hours."
hotel /ho-TEL/
noun
a building where travellers sleep and pay for rooms
"Our hotel is near the station."
guest /GEST/
noun
a person staying at a hotel or visiting your home
"She is a guest at our hotel."
passport /PAS-port/
noun
the small book that lets you travel to other countries
"You need your passport to fly."
taxi /TAK-see/
noun
a car with a driver that you pay to take you somewhere
"We took a taxi to the hotel."
arrival /uh-RY-vul/
noun
the time or moment you come to a place
"Our arrival time is ten in the morning."
departure /dee-PAR-chur/
noun
the time or moment you leave a place
"The departure is at nine o'clock."
platform /PLAT-form/
noun
the raised area where you wait for a train
"The train leaves from platform two."
bus /BUS/
noun
a big vehicle that carries many people on the road
"The bus stops near my house."
bridge /BRIJ/
noun
a road over a river or valley
"The bus went over the old bridge."

📝 Podcast Transcript

Maya

Hello everyone, and welcome back to Word Kitchen! I am Maya.

Lena

And I am Lena! Today we are talking about travel and transport. We have fifteen new words for you, and we will use them in a fun travel story.

Maya

That sounds great, Lena. So, where are we going today?

Lena

We are going on a journey! A journey is a long trip from one place to another. Our journey starts at the train station.

Maya

A station is the building where trains stop. Our first word, train, is a long vehicle that carries people on rails. So we are at the station, and we are taking the train.

Lena

Right. Before we get on the train, we need a ticket. A ticket is a small paper you buy to travel. Maya, do you have your ticket?

Maya

I do! And I have my luggage too. Luggage is the bags you carry when you travel. My luggage is very heavy today. What about you, Lena?

Lena

My luggage is light. I packed only one small bag. Oh no, look at the big clock in the station. What time is our departure?

Maya

Departure means the time you leave a place. Our departure is at ten o'clock. We still have twenty minutes.

Lena

Good. And what time is our arrival? Arrival is the time you come to a place.

Maya

Our arrival is at one in the afternoon. We will be on the train for three hours.

Lena

Three hours is a long time. Let me look at the map. A map is a picture that shows roads and places. The map says we will cross a big river.

Maya

Oh, and we will go over a bridge! A bridge is a road over a river. I love looking out the window when the train goes over a bridge.

Lena

Me too. Wait, what platform are we on? A platform is the raised area where you wait for a train.

Maya

Let me check the ticket. It says platform two. We are on platform two, but the train is not here yet.

Lena

No problem. I can see the station is very busy. Many people are waiting with their luggage.

Maya

Do you have your passport, Lena? A passport is the small book that lets you travel to other countries.

Lena

For a train in this country, I do not need my passport. But I always carry it when I travel. It is good to have it.

Maya

Smart. Oh look, the train is coming! Let's go to the platform door. Do not forget your luggage!

Lena

I have it. Wow, the train is so long. Let's find our seats. My ticket says seat number twelve.

Maya

My ticket says seat number thirteen. Perfect, we are sitting together. Here is the journey part, Lena. Three hours on the train.

Lena

I like the start of a journey. Everything is new and exciting. Where will we sleep tonight, Maya?

Maya

At a hotel. A hotel is a building where travellers sleep and pay for rooms. Our hotel is near the station in the new city.

Lena

Will we be a guest there? A guest is a person staying at a hotel.

Maya

Yes, we will be guests at the hotel for two nights. Then we take the bus to the mountains.

Lena

A bus is a big vehicle that carries many people on the road. Is the bus station far from our hotel?

Maya

No, it is very close. We can take a taxi. A taxi is a car with a driver that you pay to take you somewhere.

Lena

Good idea. A taxi is fast, and our luggage is heavy. Maya, look out the window! We are on the bridge!

Maya

Wow, the river is so beautiful. The train is moving slowly over the bridge. I can see boats on the water.

Lena

This journey is already wonderful. Let's use our words one more time, Maya. A journey is a long trip.

Maya

A train carries people on rails. A station is where trains stop. A ticket is the paper you buy to travel.

Lena

Luggage is your travel bags. A map shows roads and places. A hotel is where travellers sleep.

Maya

A guest stays at a hotel. A passport lets you travel to other countries. A taxi is a car with a driver you pay.

Lena

Arrival is when you come to a place. Departure is when you leave. A platform is where you wait for the train.

Maya

A bus carries many people on the road. And a bridge is a road over a river.

Lena

That is all fifteen words! Great job, everyone. Practice them on your next trip.

Maya

Wait, Lena, one more question. What is the most important thing for a good journey?

Lena

Hmm. I think it is a good map, a light bag of luggage, and a comfortable hotel at the end of the day.

Maya

I agree. And do not forget your ticket before the departure, or the train will leave without you!

Lena

That is true. I once missed my bus because I forgot the time of departure. Now I always check my ticket two times.

Maya

Good habit. When you travel by taxi, the driver can take you straight to the hotel door. No heavy luggage to carry.

Lena

And at the hotel, the guest room is waiting. You give your name, you get a key, and your journey for the day is done.

Maya

Then tomorrow, a new bus, a new bridge, and a new adventure. Travel is the best way to learn new words.

Lena

It really is. Okay, now we are really finished. Practice these words with a friend and plan your own journey.

Maya

Thanks for listening to Word Kitchen. See you next time!

Lena

Bye!

📝Grammar: Obligation and prohibition: have to, must, should

We use **have to**, **must**, and **should** to talk about rules and advice. Use **have to** for a rule or a need. You **have to** show your passport at the station. Use **must** for a very strong rule. You **must** stop at the red light. Use **don't have to** when something is not needed. You **don't have to** take a taxi to the hotel. Use **mustn't** when something is not allowed. You **mustn't** smoke on the train. Use **should** for good advice. You **should** check your ticket before departure.
