📝Grammar: Adverbs of Manner (slowly, well, hard)

**Adverbs of manner** tell us **how** someone does something. She speaks **quietly**, and he drives **carefully**. An **adjective** describes a noun, like a **careful** driver. An **adverb** describes a verb, like he drives **carefully**. Make most adverbs with **adjective + -ly**. **Quick** becomes **quickly**, and **careful** becomes **carefully**. Some adverbs are irregular. **Good** becomes **well**, **fast** stays **fast**, and **hard** stays **hard**. Put the adverb **after the verb**, or **after the object**. She speaks English **slowly**, not slowly English. After **be**, **look**
