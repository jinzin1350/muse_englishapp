📝Grammar: Past Simple and Irregular Verbs

We use the **past simple** to talk about finished actions in the past. The time is finished, and the action is finished too. Regular verbs add **-ed**. For example, **walk** becomes **walked**. Irregular verbs change in a different way. For example, **buy** becomes **bought**, and **wear** becomes **wore**. In negative sentences, we use **didn't** + the base verb. We say **I didn't buy a belt**, not **I didn't bought a belt**. In questions, we use **Did** + subject + base verb. For example, **Did you wear a scarf yesterday?** Many everyday verbs are irregular: **go → went**, **find → foun
