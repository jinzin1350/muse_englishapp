wallet /WAH-let/
noun

"I lost my wallet in the shop yesterday."
receipt /ruh-SEET/
noun
a small paper that shows you paid for something
"She kept the receipt to return the shoes."
price /PRYS/
noun

"The price of the bread was one dollar."
change /CHAYNJ/
noun
money you get back after you pay
"The shop gave me fifty cents in change."

noun
money in coins and paper notes, not a card

📝 Grammar: Past and present habits (used to)

We use **used to** for past habits and old states. These things were true for a long time in the past, but they are not true now. The form is **used to** plus the verb in the simple form. The verb never has s or ed. **used to** does not exist in the present. For the negative, we use **didn't use to**. Note there is no d in **use**. For questions, we use **Did you use to**. For present habits, we use the present simple with words like **usually**. This shows what you do now, not in the past.
