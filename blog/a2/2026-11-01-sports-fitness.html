📚 لغت‌ها

۵ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
against [compete] /uh-GENST/
حرف اضافه · preposition
competing with someone
در مقابل، با
"Our team played against a strong opponent."
athlete /ATH-leet/
اسم · noun
A person who competes in sports.
ورزشکار
"She is a great athlete and runs fast."
athletics /ath-LET-iks/
اسم · noun
Sports like running, jumping, and throwing.
دو و میدانی
"She loves watching athletics on TV."
beat /BEET/
فعل · verb
Win against someone in a game or fight.
شکست دادن، بردن
"Our team can beat them easily."
boxing /BOK-sing/
اسم · noun
A sport where two people fight with gloves.
بوکس
"Boxing is a popular sport in many countries."

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and this is Lena. Today we talk about sports and fitness.

Lena

Yes! Sports are a big part of my life. I love to watch athletics on TV, and I love to run.

Maya

Me too. Do you know a good athlete? My friend Sara is a great athlete. She runs fast, and she trains every day.

Lena

Wow! An athlete trains hard. Does Sara play against other runners?

Maya

Yes, she runs against the best runners in her city. Last week, she ran against a strong team, and she won the race.

Lena

That is great! To beat a strong team is not easy. Can our team beat them too?

Maya

Maybe! If we train every day, we can beat them. But first, we must learn: how do you beat a good team?

Lena

Good question. You beat a team with hard work and good plans. And you never give up, even when the game is hard.

Maya

True. And what about boxing? Boxing is a popular sport in many countries. Do you like boxing?

Lena

I like to watch boxing, but I do not box. Boxing is hard! Two people fight with gloves, and they must be fast and strong.

Maya

Yes, boxing is a tough sport. But athletics is my favorite. Athletics has running, jumping, and throwing. Which part of athletics do you like best?

Lena

I like running best. Running is simple, and you can run in a park. Do you run in the morning?

Maya

I run three times a week. An athlete like Sara runs every day, but I am not an athlete. I just run for fun and fitness.

Maya

Maya

Good! And here is a fun question for our listeners: can you beat your best time? If you run today, try to beat your time from last week. That is a great goal.

Maya

Yes. Now let us talk about watching sports. When I watch athletics on TV, I cheer for every athlete. I love to see an athlete win against a strong opponent.

📝گرامر: The or No Article?

We use no article for things in general. Athletics is fun.

We use the for a specific thing that we both know. The athlete in my street runs fast.

Use no article for sports and games. We watch athletics, not the athletics.

Use no article for meals and for by + transport. We have lunch at one o'clock, and we go by bus.

Use the for unique things. The sun, the moon, and the world take the.

Use the with rivers and mountain ranges. We say the Nile and the Himalayas, but Lake Ontario and Mount Everest take no article.
