📝Grammar: Past simple: regular verbs

We use the past simple for finished actions in the past. Words like yesterday, last night, and this morning often come with it. For regular verbs, add -ed to the verb: work -> worked, clean -> cleaned, cook -> cooked. Watch the spelling. Verbs ending in -e add only -d: like -> liked. Verbs ending in consonant + y change y to i: hurry -> hurried, study -> studied. Short verbs double the last letter: stop -> stopped, plan -> planned. For negatives, use did not (didn't) + the verb: I did not sleep well. For questions, put Did at the start: Did you cook dinner?
