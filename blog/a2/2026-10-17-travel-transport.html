📝Grammar: Present Perfect for Experience (ever, never)

We use the **present perfect** to talk about experiences in our life, when we don't say exactly when it happened. Form: **have** / **has** + past participle. Use **ever** in questions about your whole life: ***Have** you **ever** waited at the **gate**?* Use **never** with a positive verb to say it did not happen in your life: *I**'ve never** missed a train.* When you say **when** it happened, use the past simple: *I **went** there in 2022.* A common mistake is *Have you ever went?* — always use the past participle: **been**, **seen**, **eaten**.
