📚 لغت‌ها

۵ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
awful /AW-ful/
صفت · adjective
Very bad or unpleasant.
وحشتناک، افتضاح
"The weather was awful yesterday."
bad /BAD/
صفت · adjective
Not good; unpleasant or harmful.
بد، ناپسند
"The weather was really bad yesterday."
bright /BRITE/
صفت · adjective
Full of light; strong in color.
روشن، درخشان
"She wore a bright yellow shirt."
confused /kun-FYOOZD/
صفت · adjective
Unable to understand or think clearly.
گیج، سردرگم
"I was confused by the difficult question."
depressed /dih-PREST/
صفت · adjective
Feeling very sad and without hope.
افسرده
"She felt depressed after losing her job."

📝گرامر: Could for Past Ability

Could is the past of *can*. We use it for abilities we had in the past. When I was five, I could swim.

The negative is couldn't. I couldn't sleep last night because of the awful noise. She felt depressed, so she couldn't go out.

For questions, start with could: Could you ride a bike when you were five? Answer with Yes, I could or No, I couldn't.
