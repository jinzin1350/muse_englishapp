air conditioning /air kun-DISH-ning/
noun
a system that cools the air
"The car has good air conditioning."
balcony /BAL-kuh-nee/
noun
A platform outside a building, above the ground.
"We sat on the balcony and enjoyed the view."
bin /BIN/
noun
A container for rubbish.
"Put the paper in the bin."
blanket /BLANG-kit/
noun
A warm cover for a bed or for keeping warm.
"I pulled the blanket over me."
blinds /BLYNDZ/
noun
Covers for windows made of strips of material.
"Please close the blinds, it's too sunny."

📝Grammar: Must, Have To and Should

We use **must** and **have to** for obligation. Obligation means something is necessary, not a choice. We use **should** for advice. Advice means something is a good idea, like when you help a friend. **Mustn't** and **don't have to** look similar, but they are very different. **Mustn't** means it is not allowed. **Don't have to** means it is not necessary. You **must** empty the bin when it is full. You **have to** open the blinds every morning. You **should** wash your blanket every two weeks.
