archer /AR-cher/
noun

"The archer aimed carefully at the target."

noun

📝 Podcast Transcript

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! It is review day, and today we are looking back at everything we learned over the last three days.

Lena

That is right. No new words today. We are going to use the words you already know, in real conversations, so they stay in your memory.

Lena

Let us start with sport. Maya, did you go to your sports club at the weekend?

Maya

I did. I went to the club on Saturday morning, and the atmosphere there was wonderful. Everybody was friendly, music was playing, and the whole atmosphere made me want to stay all day.

Lena

That sounds lovely. What did you do at the club?

Maya

First, I watched an archer practice. The archer stood very still, took a deep breath, and aimed at the target. A good archer needs patience, strong arms, and a calm mind.

Lena

I have never tried it. Is a bow heavy to hold?

Maya

A bow is lighter than it looks, but holding a bow steady is hard. The archer showed me how to hold the bow, pull the string back slowly, and let the arrow fly. My first arrow missed the target completely, but the archer laughed and said that every archer starts that way.

Lena

So archery is the sport, and an archer is the person. I remember now. Archery looks elegant, almost like dancing with a bow in your hands.

Maya

Exactly. Archery teaches focus. And after archery, I met a canoeist who trains on the river near the club. The canoeist told me that a canoeist must read the water, not fight it.

Lena

A canoeist paddles a small, light boat, right? That sounds peaceful and exciting at the same time.

Maya

It is. The canoeist said that on a calm morning, the river is like glass, but after rain the river gets fast and wild. A good canoeist stays calm and keeps paddling, stroke by stroke.

Lena

I love that image. And what about athletics? Did you see any athletics at the club?

Maya

Yes, there was an athletics session for young runners. Athletics includes running, jumping, and throwing, and the coaches were kind and encouraging. One coach said athletics builds character, because you learn to keep going even when you are tired.

Lena

And did anyone play billiards?

Maya

Oh yes, in the club room afterwards. Billiards is a quiet game, very different from athletics. In billiards, you think carefully before every shot, and one small mistake can change the whole game.

Lena

So the club really has something for everyone. An archer, a canoeist, runners in athletics, and players of billiards, all in one club.

Maya

That is why I love the club. The atmosphere is warm, people help each other, and nobody laughs at beginners.

Lena

Speaking of beginners, Maya, I have a question for you. Have you ever played baseball? You know, with a bat?

Maya

With a bat? I tried once, years ago. A bat is a wooden stick used for hitting a ball, and I swung the bat and missed the ball three times in a row.

Lena

Poor Maya. But a bat is not only for baseball. Did you know a bat is also an animal that flies at night? That is a different kind of bat, of course, but it is fun that one word has two meanings.

Maya

That is true, and it reminds me of nature. Last week we talked about the natural world, and today I want to talk about air pollution, because it affects every athlete.

Lena

Air pollution is a big problem in cities, and it is getting worse in many places. When air pollution is high, runners cough, and even a strong canoeist finds it hard to breathe.

Maya

Yes, and air pollution is connected to acid rain. Acid rain falls when polluted air mixes with rain, and acid rain can damage forests, lakes, and old buildings.

Lena

That is sad. Acid rain hurts trees, and when trees suffer, birds lose their homes. I think of a bird with a small beak, trying to find food in a damaged forest. The beak of a bird is perfectly shaped for its food, but if the forest is damaged, even a strong beak cannot help.

Maya

That is a beautiful and sad picture. And it is why the atmosphere of a place matters so much. The atmosphere of a clean forest feels fresh and alive, while the atmosphere of a busy road feels heavy and grey.

Lena

The word atmosphere can mean the air around us, but also the feeling or mood in a place. The atmosphere in our club was joyful, and the atmosphere in a quiet library is calm and serious.

Maya

Exactly. Two meanings again, just like bat. English loves words with double lives.

Lena

Now, before we move to the internet, let me tell you about my dog, because he gives us another word. My dog loves to bark. He will bark at the postman, bark at cats, and bark at his own reflection.

Maya

Dogs bark to talk to us. When a dog starts to bark loudly, it is saying something, even if we do not understand it. My neighbour's dog will bark every morning when the children walk past the house.

Lena

And when a dog barks all night, nobody sleeps. So a bark is a loud sound, and to bark is what a dog does. A dog can bark with joy, or bark with fear.

Maya

That is a perfect example. Now, listeners, if you are enjoying this review, here is a quick word about the app that makes all of this possible.

Lena

The Muse English app gives you new words every day, with photos and pronunciation, so you can hear every word clearly and remember it easily.

Maya

You also get this podcast, plus shadowing stories where you speak along, fun quizzes to test yourself, and grammar lessons that build step by step.

Lena

And there is a leaderboard and points, so you can watch your progress grow and stay motivated day after day. It is free to install, so download the Muse English app today and keep learning with us.

Maya

Welcome back. We were talking about sport and nature, and now let us talk about the internet, because our third lesson was all about technology.

Lena

Good idea. Maya, which internet browser do you use?

Maya

I use a fast browser on my laptop, and I love my browser because it remembers my favourite pages. A browser is software to view websites, and a good browser makes life easy.

Lena

I agree. When I browse the web, I like to browse slowly in the morning with coffee. To browse means to look around casually, and I browse news sites, cooking sites, and travel sites.

Maya

And when you browse and find something good, you bookmark it. I bookmark recipes, I bookmark train times, and I bookmark articles I want to read later. A bookmark saves the page, so you do not lose it.

Lena

That is smart. I also write a blog. My blog is small, but I love my blog because it lets me share stories about sport and nature. A blog is a personal website, updated regularly, and readers can leave comments.

Maya

I read your blog every week. Last week your blog post about the canoeist was wonderful. Your blog really brings the river to life.

Lena

Thank you. And you know, writing a blog taught me about attachments too. An attachment is a file you send with an email. Once I forgot the attachment, and my friend wrote back saying, there is no attachment.

Maya

We have all done that. An attachment can be a photo, a document, or a song. Always check the attachment before you press send.

Lena

And when you send an email, you need broadband. Broadband is a fast internet connection, and without broadband, videos stop and pages load slowly. Good broadband makes a browser feel fast.

Maya

True. And one more word from that lesson: associated. When two things are associated, they are connected in your mind. For me, the word archer is associated with patience, and the word canoeist is associated with calm rivers.

Lena

For me, the word club is associated with friendship, and the word atmosphere is associated with warm evenings. Words are associated with memories, and that is how we remember them.

Maya

That is the secret of review day. When a word is associated with a story, a place, or a person, it stays with you.

Maya

Now, one serious word from our nature lesson deserves a quiet moment. AIDS is a serious disease that attacks the body's immune system, and research continues to find a cure for AIDS.

Lena

Yes, and research matters. Scientists work for years, and every step brings hope. When we learn a word like AIDS, we also learn respect for the people who live with it and the doctors who fight it.

Maya

Well said. And now, listeners, a second quick reminder about the Muse English app, because review days are even better with the right tools.

Lena

With the Muse English app, you get daily words with clear photos and pronunciation, this podcast to listen to on the go, and shadowing stories that train your speaking.

Maya

You also get quizzes that make review fun, grammar lessons that explain the rules simply, and a leaderboard with points that keeps you coming back every single day.

Lena

It is free, it is friendly, and it is made for learners like you. Install the Muse English app today, and join thousands of learners who are building their English one day at a time.

Maya

Back to our review. Lena, if you could choose one sport from our lessons, would you choose archery, athletics, or billiards?

Lena

I would choose archery, because an archer looks so calm. But I would also love to meet a canoeist and learn to paddle. A canoeist sees the world from the water, and that is special.

Maya

I would choose the club life itself. A club gives you friends, a good atmosphere, and a reason to leave the house. The atmosphere of a good club can change your whole week.

Maya

And my dog will still bark, of course. He will bark at the postman tomorrow, just as he barked today. Some things never change, and that is fine.

Lena

And on the internet, I will keep my blog, bookmark good pages, browse with my fast browser, and check every attachment before sending. With good broadband, it all feels easy.

Maya

And everything we learned stays associated in our minds: the archer with the bow, the canoeist with the river, the bat with the ball game, the bark with the dog, the club with friends, the atmosphere with feeling, and the browser with the web.

Lena

That is review day done right. Thank you for learning with us, and remember, the best way to keep words is to use them, again and again, in real life.

Maya

See you tomorrow on Muse English, with fresh words, a new story, and more conversation. Keep practising, keep smiling, and goodbye.

Lena

Goodbye, everyone.

📝Grammar: Mixed Conditionals

**Mixed conditionals** mix two times. The condition is in one time, and the result is in another. For a past condition with a present result, use **if + past perfect, would + base verb**. Add **now** to show the present result. For a present condition with a past result, use **if + past simple, would have + past participle**. The condition is usually a general fact. Do not use **would** in the **if** clause. Use **would**, **could**, or **might** only in the result. The word **now**, or a past time word like **yesterday**, tells you which mixed pattern to use.
