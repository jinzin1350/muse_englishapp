📚 لغت‌ها

۷ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
attract /uh-TRAKT/
فعل · verb
To make someone interested in something.
جذب کردن، جلب کردن
"The new store hopes to attract many customers."
auburn /AW-burn/
صفت · adjective
Reddish-brown color, usually for hair.
خرمایی مایل به قرمز
"She has beautiful auburn hair."
awkward /AWK-werd/
صفت · adjective
Difficult, embarrassing, or not graceful.
ناجور، دست و پا چلفتی
"I felt awkward when I dropped my food."
affectionate /uh-FEK-shuh-nut/
صفت · adjective
Showing love or liking.
مهربان، با محبت
"He is a very affectionate child."
bald /BAWLD/
صفت · adjective
Having little or no hair on the head.
کچل، طاس
"My uncle started to go bald in his thirties."
beard /BEERD/
اسم · noun
Hair growing on a man's chin and cheeks.
ریش
"My father has a long beard."
beautiful /BYOO-tuh-ful/
صفت · adjective
Very pleasing to the senses; lovely.
زیبا، قشنگ
"She is a beautiful woman."

پادکست: احساسات و شخصیت

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today Lena and I are talking about faces, feelings, and first impressions.

Lena

Hello everyone, I am Lena. I love this topic, because the first thing we notice about a person is often how they look, but the second thing we notice is how they make us feel.

Maya

That is so true. Our first word today is attract. When something can attract you, it makes you interested in it. A warm smile can attract people, a beautiful voice can attract listeners, and bright colours can attract our eyes.

Lena

I agree. A kind person does not try hard to attract attention, but people still want to be near them. My grandmother always said that good manners attract good friends.

Maya

What a lovely idea. Think about a shop window. A beautiful picture in the window can attract customers, and friendly workers inside can attract them back a second time. So attract is about pulling interest towards you, like a magnet.

Lena

Speaking of first impressions, let me describe my cousin. She has long auburn hair, and everyone remembers it. Auburn is a reddish brown colour, usually for hair. Her auburn hair shines in the sun, almost like copper.

Maya

Auburn hair is beautiful. My sister tried to colour her hair auburn last year, but it came out too red, and she felt a little awkward about it.

Lena

Oh, awkward is another great word for today. Awkward means difficult, embarrassing, or not graceful. We all know that awkward moment when you wave at someone, and they were not waving at you.

Maya

That happened to me last week. I felt so awkward that I wanted to disappear. But you know what? An awkward moment is never as bad as we think. Most people forget it in five minutes.

Lena

Exactly. And here is something that always helps in an awkward situation. Be affectionate and kind. Affectionate means showing love or liking. An affectionate word or a warm hug can melt any awkward feeling away.

Maya

Before we go further, let me tell you about something that can help you learn words like these every single day. In the Muse English app, you get fresh daily words with clear photos and real pronunciation, so words like attract and auburn stay in your memory. You can listen to this podcast in the app, practise with shadowing stories, and test yourself with fun quizzes. There are grammar lessons too, plus a leaderboard and points that keep you motivated. The app is free to install, so come and join us today.

Lena

I use the app every morning, and the shadowing stories are my favourite part. Now, where were we? We were talking about being affectionate. My dog is the most affectionate animal I know. He waits at the door for me every evening.

Maya

My cat is affectionate too, but only when she wants food. Still, an affectionate pet can make a whole house feel warm. People who are affectionate usually attract strong friendships, because everyone loves feeling loved.

Lena

That is true. My uncle is very affectionate, and he is also famous in our family for another reason. He is completely bald. Bald means having little or no hair on your head. He started to go bald when he was thirty.

Maya

And does he feel awkward about being bald?

Lena

Not at all. He says being bald is beautiful in its own way, and honestly, he is right. He has a big grey beard, and the beard with the bald head looks strong and kind at the same time. Children are not scared of him, because he is so affectionate and funny.

Maya

I love that story. A beard is the hair that grows on a man's chin and cheeks. Some men grow a beard because it looks warm, and some grow one because a beard can attract a lot of compliments.

Lena

My brother grew a beard last winter. At first it looked a little awkward, because it grew in patches. But after two months, his beard looked full and beautiful, and now he will not cut it.

Maya

Ha, that sounds familiar. My father had a beard when I was small. His beard was auburn when he was young, the same auburn colour as your cousin's hair, but now it is white as snow.

Lena

It is funny how hair tells a story. Auburn hair, a grey beard, a bald head. These are just the outside. What is beautiful on the inside matters much more.

Maya

Beautiful is our next word, and it is a word with a big heart. Beautiful means very pleasing, lovely. A person can be beautiful, a song can be beautiful, and a kind action can be the most beautiful thing of all.

Lena

I think an affectionate heart is beautiful. When someone helps a stranger, that moment is beautiful, and it can attract kindness from everyone around.

Maya

Let me share a small story about an awkward day that turned beautiful. Last spring, I went to a new class. I did not know anyone, I dropped my books in front of everyone, and I felt completely awkward.

Lena

Oh no, what happened next?

Maya

A girl with auburn hair helped me pick up my books. She was so affectionate and warm that my awkward feeling disappeared in one minute. We are best friends now. Her kindness was beautiful, and it taught me that warm people attract warm people.

Lena

That is a beautiful story, Maya. And it shows something important. We cannot choose our hair or our face, but we can choose to be affectionate, and we can choose how we act when we feel awkward.

Maya

Very well said. And now, a quick word for everyone listening who wants to remember all of this. In the Muse English app, today's seven words are waiting for you with photos, pronunciation, and a word quiz. You can shadow today's story sentence by sentence, collect points, climb the leaderboard, and follow the grammar path step by step. Everything is free, and a little practice every day really works. Open the Muse English app after this episode and keep your streak alive.

Lena

Yes, do it right after we finish. Now let us look at our words one more time with fresh examples. A bright window display can attract shoppers. A friendly smile can attract new friends. Confidence can attract success.

Maya

And auburn is not just for hair in stories. Autumn leaves can be auburn, and an auburn sunset can paint the whole sky. When I see that colour, I always think of warm and beautiful evenings.

Lena

For awkward, remember this. Everyone feels awkward sometimes. A new job can feel awkward. A first date can feel very awkward. Even famous actors feel awkward on stage. It is a normal human feeling.

Maya

And the cure is often simple. Be affectionate. Send an affectionate message. Give an affectionate smile. Small affectionate actions make big awkward moments small.

Lena

Let us not forget our two gentlemen's words. Bald is a normal, natural word. Many strong and beautiful actors are bald. And a beard can be short, long, black, grey, or even auburn. A beard is just hair, but for many men it is part of who they are.

Maya

My grandfather was bald, and he never grew a beard, but his affectionate laugh was the most beautiful sound in our house. When he laughed, his whole face would attract everyone to the kitchen table.

Lena

What a picture. You see, listeners, these seven words are not just words. Attract, auburn, awkward, affectionate, bald, beard, and beautiful. They help you describe real people and real feelings in English.

Maya

Let us do a tiny practice together. Think of someone you love. Is that person affectionate? Does their smile attract everyone? Is their hair dark, or is it auburn? Do they have a beard?

Maya

Let me paint one more picture for you, listeners. Imagine a small Saturday market. The smell of fresh bread can attract hungry visitors from the whole street. An old man with a long white beard is selling flowers, and his affectionate dog sits beside him, happy to meet everyone.

Lena

I can see it. And at the next table, a young woman with auburn hair is selling paintings. Her work is beautiful, and the colours attract a small crowd. Nobody feels awkward there, because the market feels like one big family.

Maya

And maybe a bald baker is giving free cakes to children, with a big affectionate smile. That market has everything. A beard here, auburn hair there, a bald head shining in the sun, and beautiful kindness everywhere.

Lena

What I love about that picture is that every person in it is different, and every person in it is beautiful in a different way. English gives you the words to describe them all with love and respect.

Maya

And think of a beautiful moment from this week. Maybe it was awkward at first, but it ended well. Try to tell that story in English tonight, using at least three of today's words.

Maya

That is your friendly homework. Thank you for listening to Muse English today. Keep practising, keep smiling, and remember, a kind and affectionate heart is always beautiful.

Lena

Thank you everyone. Do not feel awkward about making mistakes in English. Mistakes help you grow. We will see you tomorrow with seven fresh new words. Goodbye for now!

Maya

Goodbye everyone, and happy learning!
