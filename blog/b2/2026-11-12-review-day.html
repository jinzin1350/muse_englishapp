📚 لغت‌ها

۱۰ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
blood pressure /BLUD PRESH-er/
اسم · noun
The force of blood moving through your body.
فشار خون
"The doctor checked my blood pressure."
CEO /SEE-ee-oh/
اسم · noun
Chief Executive Officer; the highest manager in a company.
مدیر عامل
"The CEO made an important decision for the company."
carpenter /KAR-pin-ter/
اسم · noun
A person who builds and repairs things made of wood.
نجار
"My father is a carpenter and makes furniture."
brick /BRIK/
اسم · noun
A hard block used for building walls.
آجر
"The house is made of red bricks."
bandage /BAN-dij/
اسم · noun
A strip of cloth to cover an injury.
باند، نوار زخم‌بندی
"She put a bandage on her cut finger."
bowl /BOHL/
اسم · noun
A round, deep dish for food.
کاسه
"I ate a bowl of soup for lunch."
bathroom /BAH-throom/
اسم · noun
A room with a toilet and a bath or shower.
حمام، دستشویی
"The bathroom is upstairs, next to my room."
break down /BRAYK DOWN/
فعل عبارتی · phrasal verb
To stop working (for a machine or vehicle).
خراب شدن، از کار افتادن
"My car broke down on the way to work."
be promoted /bee pruh-MOH-ted/
گروه فعلی · verb phrase
Get a higher position in your job.
ترفیع گرفتن، ارتقا یافتن
"She was promoted to manager last month."
business plan /BIZ-nis PLAN/
اسم · noun
A detailed plan for a new business.
طرح کسب و کار
"We need a solid business plan to get funding."

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! Hello everyone, I am Maya, and today is a very special review day.

Lena

Hi, I am Lena, and I love review days because we do not learn new words today, we practise the words from the last three days until they feel easy.

Maya

Exactly. This week we talked about work, health, and the home, and today we will tell one big story that uses all of those words again and again.

Lena

I like that idea. Where does our story begin?

Maya

It begins with a carpenter named Daniel. Daniel is a carpenter who builds beautiful wooden doors, and he works for a small company that wants to build houses.

Lena

A carpenter who can build well is very valuable. Did Daniel want to build his own company one day?

Maya

He did. Daniel wrote a business plan last year, a clear business plan with prices, costs, and ideas for new houses. He showed his business plan to a bank, and the bank liked his business plan very much.

Lena

A good business plan can change a life. What happened next?

Maya

The CEO of a bigger company read the plan. The CEO invited Daniel to a meeting, and the CEO said Daniel could be promoted if his team finished a new house on time.

Lena

To be promoted is a big dream for many workers. Daniel must have felt happy when the CEO said he could be promoted.

Maya

He was happy, but he was also nervous. To be promoted, he had to prove he could lead a team, not just work as a carpenter.

Lena

That makes sense. A CEO does not promote people for no reason. The CEO wants leaders who stay calm when problems happen.

Maya

And problems did happen. On the first morning, Daniel set his alarm clock for five o'clock, because he did not want to be late.

Lena

I never bother with an alarm clock on Sundays, but on work days an alarm clock saves me. Did Daniel bother to eat breakfast?

Maya

He did not bother at first, but his wife put a bowl of hot soup on the table and said, eat from this bowl before you go. So Daniel ate from the bowl, thanked her, and left.

Maya

At the building site, Daniel the carpenter looked at the plans. The new house needed a strong brick wall, a new bathroom, and a warm basement for storage.

Maya

Many times. Daniel chose every brick himself. He said, a good brick lasts, a bad brick breaks, so he checked each brick with his hands.

Lena

That is a careful carpenter. What about the bathroom in the new house?

Maya

The bathroom was small but bright. Daniel built a wooden shelf for the bathroom, fitted a mirror in the bathroom, and painted the bathroom a soft white colour.

Maya

I agree. Now, friends, before we continue our story, let me tell you about something that can help your English every single day, wherever you are.

Lena

Yes, this is a good moment. If you are enjoying this review, the Muse English app can help you practise these same words again later today, with clear photos, simple meanings, and pronunciation you can listen to and repeat.

Maya

In the app you get a fresh lesson every day for your own level, with a podcast like this one, shadowing stories where you speak along with the voice, short quizzes, and grammar lessons that use the words you already know.

Lena

It is completely free to install, and you earn points and climb the leaderboard as you learn, so open the app after this episode and review todays words in your own lesson.

Maya

Thank you, Lena. Now back to Daniel. On the second day, something went wrong at the site. The old truck did break down on the road to the house.

Lena

Oh no, when a truck does break down, the whole day can be lost. Did it break down near the site or far away?

Maya

It did break down only ten minutes from the site, but the team lost two hours. Daniel stayed calm even though machines break down at the worst times.

Lena

A good leader stays calm when things break down. What did the CEO say when she heard the news?

Maya

The CEO called Daniel and asked about the delay. Daniel explained that a vehicle can break down, but his team would work later to finish the brick wall.

Maya

Exactly. That afternoon, Daniel cut his finger while he was working as a carpenter on a wooden frame. It was a small cut, but it bled.

Lena

Did he have a bandage with him? Every worker should carry a bandage for small cuts.

Maya

He did. He washed his finger in the bathroom, put a clean bandage on the cut, and changed the bandage again after lunch.

Lena

A simple bandage can prevent a bigger problem. I always keep a bandage in my bag, and a second bandage in my car.

Maya

That is wise. The next morning, Daniel felt dizzy at work. His friend at the adult education centre, where Daniel teaches a class on Saturdays, told him to check his health.

Lena

Feeling dizzy can be a sign of high blood pressure. Did Daniel check his blood pressure that day?

Maya

He did. A nurse checked his blood pressure at a small clinic, and his blood pressure was a little high, so she told him to rest and drink water.

Lena

Blood pressure is so important. My father checks his blood pressure every week, because high blood pressure can be dangerous if you ignore it.

Maya

Daniel learned that lesson. He rested in the basement of the new house, where it was cool and quiet, and he drank water and ate fruit from a bowl.

Lena

The basement is a good place to rest on a hot day. Did anyone bother him in the basement, or could he rest in peace?

Maya

Nobody wanted to bother him. His team knew rest would boost his energy, and a short rest can boost your work for the whole afternoon.

Maya

After his rest, Daniel felt better. He went back upstairs, picked up another brick, and helped his team finish the wall, brick by brick.

Maya

That evening Daniel visited an art gallery with his wife to relax. The art gallery had paintings of old houses, and one painting showed a carpenter at work.

Lena

An art gallery is a lovely way to relax after hard work. Did the painting make Daniel blush or smile?

Maya

He smiled, and his wife blushed a little when the guide said the carpenter in the painting looked like Daniel. She did blush, and then she laughed.

Maya

On the last day of the project, the team worked on small details. They cleaned the bathroom again, polished the wooden doors that the carpenter had made, and put a bowl of flowers on the kitchen table.

Lena

A bowl of flowers sounds beautiful. I often put a small bowl of fruit in my kitchen, and guests always notice that bowl.

Maya

The CEO arrived at noon to see the finished house. The CEO walked through the bathroom, looked at the brick wall, tested the doors, and smiled.

Lena

What did the CEO say about the business plan Daniel had written?

Maya

The CEO said the business plan had worked perfectly. Because the house was finished on time, Daniel would be promoted next month, and the whole team celebrated.

Lena

So Daniel would be promoted at last, and that wonderful news gave the whole team a real boost.

Maya

Yes, and there is a funny ending too. On his way home, Daniels car did not break down, his blood pressure felt fine, and he did not need a bandage that day.

Lena

A perfect day at last. No need for a bandage, no truck to break down, and good news from the CEO about being promoted.

Maya

Before we finish, let me share one more helpful idea for your English, because review only works if you keep practising after today.

Lena

That is so true. In the Muse English app, you can listen to this podcast again, read the transcript, do the shadowing story sentence by sentence, and take the quiz to check which words you truly remember.

Maya

The app gives you daily words with photos and pronunciation, grammar lessons, points for every correct answer, and a leaderboard that makes learning feel like a friendly game with learners around the world.

Lena

Install Muse English for free today, choose your level, and spend ten minutes a day with us. Small daily practice in the app will make words like these feel natural and easy.

Maya

Now let us remember the small body words from the week too.

Lena

Of course. Daniel did not bite his nails when he was nervous, but many people bite their nails under stress. One worker joked that if you bite a hard sweet, you might hurt a tooth.

Maya

And at football last weekend, Daniels brother got a black eye in a game. A black eye looks painful, but his black eye healed in a week.

Lena

When the nurse checked Daniels eyes, she asked him to blink. We blink many times a day, and you blink faster when light is bright.

Maya

Those little words matter every day, just like blood pressure and bandage.

Lena

Let us say our review words one last time in short sentences, so listeners can hear them clearly in context.

Maya

Good idea. The carpenter showed his business plan to the CEO, and the CEO told the carpenter he would be promoted.

Lena

The carpenter put a bandage on his finger, washed his hands in the bathroom, and ate soup from a bowl at lunch.

Maya

He laid one brick, then another brick, checked his blood pressure at the clinic, and hoped his car would not break down on the way home.

Lena

And if your car does break down, or you need a bandage, or a doctor checks your blood pressure, now you have the English words for those real moments.

Lena

Thank you for listening today, everyone. Keep practising, be kind to yourself, and come back tomorrow for fresh new words.

Maya

Goodbye for now, and happy learning from all of us at Muse English.

📝گرامر: Future Continuous and Future Perfect

The future continuous (will be + -ing) describes an action in progress at a time in the future. This time tomorrow, the carpenter will be laying a brick, and the CEO will be checking the new bathroom.

The future perfect (will have + past participle) describes an action completed before a time in the future. By Friday, Daniel will have finished his business plan, and by June he will have been promoted.
