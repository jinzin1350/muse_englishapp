📚 Vocabulary

10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
algorithm /AL-guh-ri-thum/
noun
a set of rules a computer follows to solve a problem
"The new algorithm recommends videos based on what you watch."
interface /IN-ter-face/
noun
the screen or control system you use to operate a device or app
"This app has a clean interface that is easy to navigate."
encryption /en-KRIP-shun/
noun
the process of coding information so only allowed people can read it
"The bank uses strong encryption to protect your data."
bandwidth /BAND-width/
noun
the amount of data an internet connection can carry
"Video calls need a lot of bandwidth to run smoothly."
virtual /VUR-choo-uhl/
adjective
not physically real but appearing real on a screen
"We had a virtual meeting with our team in London."
sensor /SEN-sur/
noun
a small device that detects changes like heat, light, or movement
"The sensor turns the lights on when you enter the room."
backup /BAK-up/
noun
a saved copy of data you can use if the original is lost
"Always keep a backup of your important files."
upgrade /up-GRADE/
verb
to make a device or system newer or better
"I want to upgrade my phone to a faster model."
privacy /PRY-vuh-see/
noun
the right to keep your personal information safe
"You should check your privacy settings on social media."
robot /ROH-bot/
noun
a machine that can do tasks automatically
"A robot delivered the package to our office."

Maya

Hello and welcome back to Word Kitchen, the show where we take tricky English words and cook them down into something easy to understand. I am Maya.

Lena

And I am Lena. Today we have a delicious theme for you, technology. Not the complicated textbook kind, but the technology you touch every single day.

Maya

Exactly. Your phone, your laptop, your smart speaker, they are all full of interesting technology. And today we will learn ten useful words that describe it. By the end of the show, you will be able to talk about your gadgets like a native speaker.

Lena

Our first word is algorithm. An algorithm is a set of rules a computer follows to solve a problem. When your music app recommends a new song, an algorithm chose it for you.

Maya

That is right. Every time you open a social media app, an algorithm decides which posts you see first. So the algorithm is basically the brain behind your feed.

Lena

It is everywhere. Search engines use an algorithm to rank websites, and banks use an algorithm to spot unusual payments. Once you know the word algorithm, you start noticing it in your life every day.

Maya

Great tip. Now word number two, interface. The interface is the screen or control system that lets you use a device or an app. All the buttons, menus, and icons you touch are part of the interface.

Lena

I love this word because it is so practical. You can say, this app has a clean interface, which means it is simple and easy to use. Or you can complain that a website has a confusing interface.

Maya

And when designers talk about their job, they often say they design the interface, the look and the feel of the app. So the interface is really the face of any software.

Lena

Word three is encryption. Encryption is the process of coding information so that only allowed people can read it. When you send a secret message, encryption scrambles it into unreadable code.

Maya

Your bank uses strong encryption to protect your money and your data. Good encryption means hackers cannot read your private information even if they steal it.

Lena

So next time someone asks why your messages are safe, you can say, because of end to end encryption. It is a powerful word, encryption, and it keeps your digital life private.

Maya

Which brings us perfectly to word four, bandwidth. Bandwidth is the amount of data your internet connection can carry at one time. Think of it like a road. A wide road carries more cars, and high bandwidth carries more data.

Lena

That is why video calls need so much bandwidth. If your bandwidth is low, the video freezes and the sound breaks up. Nobody likes a call with bad bandwidth.

Maya

And when everyone in the house is streaming movies at once, they are all sharing the same bandwidth. More bandwidth means everyone gets a smooth connection.

Lena

Word five is virtual. Virtual means something is not physically real, but it appears real on a screen. A virtual meeting happens online, not in a real office.

Maya

During the last few years, virtual events became huge. People attended virtual concerts, virtual classes, and even virtual weddings from their living rooms.

Lena

My grandmother took a virtual cooking class last month. She learned to bake bread through her tablet. A virtual classroom can feel surprisingly real when the teacher is good.

Maya

Word six is sensor. A sensor is a small device that detects changes like heat, light, or movement. The sensor in your phone turns the screen off when you hold it to your ear.

Lena

Modern cars are full of sensors. A parking sensor beeps when you get too close to another car. A temperature sensor tells your smart home to turn on the heating.

Maya

Even your fitness watch has a sensor that counts your heartbeat. The tiny sensor works all day without you noticing it. Technology quietly collects information through every sensor.

Lena

Word seven is backup. A backup is a saved copy of your data that you can use if the original is lost. If your laptop breaks, your backup saves your photos and documents.

Maya

I learned this the hard way. I lost a whole project once because I had no backup. Now I make a backup of my important files every single week.

Lena

Smart. Many people keep a backup in the cloud, which means a copy stored on the internet. Whether it is a hard drive or the cloud, a backup gives you peace of mind.

Maya

Word eight is upgrade. To upgrade means to make a device or a system newer or better. When your phone offers a software update, you upgrade to get new features.

Lena

I decided to upgrade my old laptop last year. The upgrade made it twice as fast, and I could finally edit videos without waiting forever.

Maya

Businesses also upgrade their systems to stay safe. An upgrade often fixes security problems too, so upgrading is not just about speed, it is about safety.

Lena

Word nine is privacy. Privacy is your right to keep your personal information safe and under your control. When you share less online, you protect your privacy.

Maya

This word matters more every year. Companies collect your data, so you should check your privacy settings regularly. Your privacy settings decide who can see your photos and posts.

Lena

And remember our word encryption from earlier. Strong encryption is one of the best ways to defend your privacy. Privacy and security always go together.

Maya

Our final word is robot. A robot is a machine that can do tasks automatically. A robot vacuum cleans your floors while you relax on the sofa.

Lena

In many factories, a robot builds cars and packs boxes faster than any human. The robot never gets tired and never makes a careless mistake.

Maya

Some restaurants even use a robot to deliver food to your table. Watching a little robot roll toward you with your dinner is honestly a lot of fun.

Lena

Okay, time for a quick recap, Maya style. Let us cook these ten words once more so they really stick in your memory.

Maya

An algorithm is the smart set of rules behind your apps. An algorithm picks your songs, your posts, and your search results.

Lena

The interface is the screen you touch, the face of the app. A good interface feels simple, a bad interface feels like a puzzle.

Maya

Encryption scrambles your data so strangers cannot read it. Banks and messaging apps rely on encryption every day.

Lena

Bandwidth is your internet road. Wide bandwidth gives you smooth video calls, narrow bandwidth gives you frozen faces.

Maya

Virtual means online but real feeling, like a virtual class or a virtual concert. A virtual meeting still needs a real smile.

Lena

A sensor detects the world for you, light, heat, movement. Your phone, your car, and your watch each carry a clever sensor.

Maya

A backup is your safety copy. Keep a backup of your files, and you will never cry over a broken laptop again.

Lena

To upgrade is to make things newer and better. Upgrade your phone, upgrade your software, upgrade your life.

Maya

Privacy is your right to control your own information. Guard your privacy settings like you guard your front door.

Lena

And a robot is your automatic helper, from the robot vacuum to the robot chef. The future is full of friendly robot assistants.

Maya

Listeners, before we close, let us answer a question from one of our listeners. Sam from Berlin wrote to us. He says, I bought a new smart home system, and the instructions are full of difficult words. Can you help me understand them.

Lena

Oh, perfect timing, Sam. Let us use your smart home as an example for all ten of our words today.

Maya

Great idea. So Sam, when you set up the system, you first meet the interface. The interface is the app on your phone where you control everything. If the interface is friendly, setup takes five minutes.

Lena

Then each room has a sensor. The motion sensor notices when you walk in and turns on the lights. The temperature sensor keeps your home warm or cool exactly the way you like it.

Maya

Your doorbell camera uses strong encryption, so the video of your front door stays private. Nobody outside your family can watch it, because encryption locks the video with a secret code.

Lena

But all of this needs enough bandwidth. If your bandwidth is small, the cameras freeze and the lights respond slowly. Sam, a faster internet plan gives your smart home the bandwidth it needs to work smoothly.

Maya

Some systems even learn from you. The learning algorithm studies your habits and guesses when you come home. A smart algorithm can turn on the heating ten minutes before you arrive.

Lena

You can also talk to the whole house through a virtual assistant. A virtual assistant is not a real person, but it listens and answers like one. Just say good morning, and your virtual helper reads the news and starts the coffee machine.

Maya

And if something breaks, do not panic. Your settings have a backup in the cloud, so you can restore everything in one tap. A daily automatic backup protects you from losing your setup.

The company will also send you a free upgrade once a year. Each upgrade adds new features and fixes old bugs, so always install the upgrade when it arrives.

📝Grammar: Present perfect and past simple

The **present perfect** is formed with **have/has + past participle**. Use it for past experiences when you do not say exactly when they happened. I have tested a cleaning robot at home, and I have never used an interface this simple before. Use the **present perfect** for recent actions that have a result now. It goes with **yet**, **already**, and **just**. She has just made a backup of her files. Have you upgraded your phone yet?
