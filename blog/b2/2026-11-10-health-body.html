bandage /BAN-dij/
اسم · noun
A strip of cloth to cover an injury.
باند، نوار زخم‌بندی
"She put a bandage on her cut finger."
bite /BYTE/
فعل · verb
To cut or hold something with your teeth.
گاز گرفتن
"The dog tried to bite my hand."
black eye /BLAK EYE/
اسم · noun
A dark mark around your eye from a hit.
کبودی دور چشم
"He got a black eye playing football."
blink /BLINK/
فعل · verb
To quickly close and open your eyes.
پلک زدن
"She blinked several times in the bright light."
blood pressure /BLUD PRESH-er/
اسم · noun
The force of blood moving through your body.
فشار خون
"The doctor checked my blood pressure."
blush /BLUSH/
فعل · verb
Your face turns red because you are embarrassed.
سرخ شدن (از خجالت)
"She blushed when he complimented her dress."
boost /BOOST/
فعل · verb
Help something to increase or improve.
تقویت کردن، افزایش دادن
"Exercise can boost your energy levels."

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today Lena and I are talking about health, small injuries, and the little things our bodies do without asking us first.

Lena

Hello, everyone. I am Lena. This morning started in a very silly way. I cut my finger while I was opening a packet in the kitchen, and before I had even finished my tea, I needed a bandage.

Maya

Oh no. Was it a bad cut?

Lena

No, it was small, but it kept bleeding a little, so I washed it, dried it, and put a clean bandage on it. My neighbour saw the bandage and asked if I had been in a fight.

Maya

A bandage can make a tiny cut look very dramatic. People see a bandage and they imagine a big accident.

Lena

Speaking of football, did your brother also get that famous black eye?

Maya

He did. A ball hit him near the eye, and by the evening he had a real black eye. It was dark blue and purple, and he looked very proud of his black eye, like it was a medal.

Lena

A black eye can look funny in photos, but a black eye can also be serious. If someone with a black eye cannot see clearly, or the black eye gets worse, they should see a doctor.

Lena

Did he blink a lot? When my eye hurts, I blink and blink, and I cannot stop.

Maya

He did blink a lot at first. The bright light in the bathroom made him blink, and then he would blink again because he was thinking about it. You know how that works. Once you notice that you blink, you cannot stop noticing.

Maya

Doctors say we blink to keep our eyes clean and wet. Every time you blink, your eyes get a tiny rest. So blink is a small word, but blink describes something your body does all day without your help.

Lena

And here is another body word we cannot control. When I am embarrassed, I blush. My face goes red, everyone sees it, and then I blush even more because everyone saw the first blush.

Maya

I blush too. Last week I called my teacher by the wrong name, and I felt my face get hot. I knew I was starting to blush, and of course Lena, you had to point at me and laugh.

Maya

Bite is a great word because it works in so many situations. A dog can bite, a mosquito can bite, and you bite into an apple. Yesterday a mosquito gave me a bite on my arm, and that bite itched all night.

Lena

That is a lovely example. English loves to reuse bite in new ways. Now, what about blood pressure? That sounds more serious than a mosquito bite.

Maya

Blood pressure is the force of blood moving through your body. At my last check-up, the nurse put a wide band around my arm, the machine squeezed my arm, and she wrote down my blood pressure.

Lena

I remember my first blood pressure test. I was nervous, so my blood pressure was a little high. The doctor smiled and said, relax, breathe slowly, and we will check your blood pressure again in ten minutes.

Maya

That is important. Stress can push blood pressure up, and rest can bring blood pressure down. Exercise, good sleep, and less salt can all help your blood pressure stay in a healthy range.

Lena

So blood pressure is silent, but it matters. That is very different from a black eye, which everyone can see from across the street.

Maya

True. Nobody can hide a black eye, and nobody needs a machine to find a blush. But blood pressure needs a test, and that is why regular check-ups matter, even when you feel healthy.

Lena

Let us talk about our last word, boost. I love boost because it sounds positive. To boost something means to help it increase or improve.

Maya

Exercise can boost your energy. A short walk can boost your mood. Good food can boost your health, and a kind word can boost a friend's confidence before an exam.

Maya

Learning English works the same way. Small daily practice can boost your vocabulary much more than one long, painful study day. Ten minutes every day will boost your listening, boost your speaking, and boost your confidence step by step.

Maya

That is exactly what happened to my brother after the black eye. The doctor told him to rest, walk gently, and eat well. Two weeks later he said the rest had helped boost his fitness in a strange way, because he finally slept enough.

Lena

Wait, rest helped boost his fitness? That sounds backwards.

Maya

It does, but his body needed time. He stopped biting his nails, he changed his bandage on time, and he stopped checking the black eye every five minutes.

Lena

And did he still blush when people asked about the black eye?

Maya

Every single time. Someone would say, nice black eye, and he would blush, blink quickly, and change the subject. Once, he put on sunglasses at dinner to hide the black eye, and everyone at the table started laughing.

Lena

Sunglasses at dinner. That is a strong look. If a mosquito does not bite him at dinner too, he is lucky.

Maya

Let me tell you about my clinic visit last spring, because it used almost all of today's words in one hour. I went for a simple check-up. First, the nurse measured my blood pressure.

Lena

And was your blood pressure normal?

Maya

It was, but while she was working, she noticed an old bite on my leg and asked if a dog had tried to bite me. I said no, it was an insect bite from a garden party.

Lena

Garden parties are dangerous places. Insects, sunshine, and cake.

Maya

Then the doctor looked at a small cut on my hand from cooking and said I should cover it with a bandage at work. She put a fresh bandage on it herself, much better than my bandage at home.

Maya

While she was talking, the light above me was so bright that I started to blink again and again. She laughed and said, do not worry, everyone wants to blink under that light.

Lena

And let me guess. You began to blush.

Maya

I did blush, because I felt silly. But she was kind, and she told me that a blush is normal, a blink is normal, and even a little fear at the clinic is normal.

Lena

A bandage. Easy. My turn. Your face turns red because the whole class is looking at you.

Maya

I blush. And I probably blush even more when you announce it.

Correct. Next one. A ball hits you, and the next morning the skin around your eye is dark.

Maya

A black eye. And if I get a black eye, please do not take a photo.

Lena

I promise nothing. Next. You close and open your eyes very quickly, many times a day.

Maya

Blink. We blink thousands of times a day, and we almost never notice.

Lena

Maya

Blood pressure. And a calm, quiet room helps you get a true blood pressure reading.

Lena

Next. A mosquito lands on your arm at night and leaves a red, itchy mark.

Maya

A bite. One small bite, and I scratch it all evening, even though I know I should not.

Lena

Last one. A good night of sleep helps increase your energy the next day. It helps to do what to your energy?

Maya

Boost. Sleep can boost energy, a walk can boost mood, and practice can boost English. Boost is our happy word today.

📝گرامر: Should Have, Could Have, Needn't Have

We use modal + have + past participle to talk about the past. Should have shows a good idea that did not happen, could have shows a possibility that did not happen, and needn't have shows an action that happened but was not necessary.

Should have expresses regret: you did not do the right thing. Shouldn't have expresses criticism: you did something, but it was a mistake. Ought to have means the same as should have and is a little more formal.

Could have shows a missed possibility in the past. Couldn't have shows that something was impossible.

**Needn'
