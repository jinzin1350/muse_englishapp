📚 لغت‌ها

۱۰ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
corporation /kor-puh-RAY-shun/
اسم · noun
A large company or business.
شرکت بزرگ، ابرشرکت
"She works for a multinational corporation."
academic /ak-uh-DEM-ik/
صفت · adjective
related to education, studying, and thinking

"She has a strong academic background in science."
childminder /CHYLD-myn-der/
اسم · noun
A person who looks after children in their own home.
پرستار کودک (در خانه خود)
"The childminder picks up the kids from school."
custom-built /KUS-tum-BILT/
صفت · adjective
Made specially for a particular person or purpose.
سفارشی‌ساز، دست‌ساز
"They live in a beautiful custom-built house."
button /BUT-un/
اسم · noun
A small, round piece used to fasten clothes.
دکمه
"My shirt has a missing button."
camcorder /KAM-kor-der/
اسم · noun
A portable video camera.
دوربین فیلمبرداری
"He used a camcorder to record the wedding."
civil servant /SIV-ul SER-vunt/
اسم · noun
Someone who works for the government.
کارمند دولت
"My aunt is a civil servant."
back up /BAK UP/
فعل عبارتی · phrasal verb
To support someone or something.
حمایت کردن، پشتیبانی کردن
"Can you back up my story?"
cable /KAY-bul/
اسم · noun
A thick wire carrying electricity or signals.
کابل، سیم
"We need a new cable to connect the TV."
active /AK-tiv/
صفت · adjective
always doing things, not sitting still
فعال، پرتحرک
"My dog is very active and loves to run."

پادکست: روز مرور

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! I am Maya, and today is a special review day.

Lena

And I am Lena. Hello, everyone. Today we are not learning new words. Instead, we are looking back at the words from the last three days, and we are going to use them again and again in real conversation.

Maya

That is right. Review is powerful. When you meet a word again in a new story, you remember it for much longer. So today we have a film story, a work story, and a technology story, and all of them use only the words we have already learned together.

Lena

I love that plan. Where do we start?

Maya

We start with Lena the filmmaker. In our story, a large corporation asked her to make an academic film about working life. A corporation is a large company or business, and academic means related to education, studying, and thinking.

Lena

Yes, and that academic film had to be absorbing, so interesting that people could not stop watching it. The corporation wanted teachers to accept the film for their classes, and the teachers said they would accept it if the story felt real.

Maya

So you visited real workers. Tell us about the first one.

Lena

The first worker was a childminder. A childminder is a person who looks after children in their own home. This childminder was wonderful. She was active all morning, playing, reading, and cooking with the children, and the children were active too.

Maya

And you filmed her with a camcorder, right?

Lena

I did. I used a small camcorder, a portable video camera. I pressed the red button on the camcorder, checked the cable that carried the picture to my screen, and started filming. The childminder wore a blue coat with a silver button, and at one point that button fell off, and we all laughed.

Maya

That sounds like a perfect real moment for an academic film. Did the corporation like it?

Lena

The corporation loved it. They said the childminder showed real customer care, even though her customers are families, not shoppers. Customer care means the service you give to people before and after they choose you, and good customer care builds trust.

Maya

Before we meet the next worker, let us pause for a moment and talk about how you can practise words like corporation, academic, and childminder every day.

Lena

Yes. In the Muse English app, review is easy and friendly. Every day you get new words with photos and pronunciation, you hear this podcast, you practise shadowing stories sentence by sentence, and you finish with quizzes and grammar lessons. Your points and streak grow as you learn. The app is completely free to install, so open Muse English today and do today's review lesson.

Maya

Thank you, Lena. That daily habit really matters. Now, who was the second worker in your film?

Lena

The second worker was a civil servant. A civil servant is someone who works for the government. This civil servant worked in a small office that gave admission to a local museum. Admission means the right to enter a place or join a group, and on Saturdays admission was free for families.

Maya

Did he give you any advice?

Lena

He gave me excellent advice. Advice means suggestions about what someone should do. He said, read your questions aloud before you film, because when you read aloud, speaking so others can hear, you notice mistakes. So I read my questions aloud in his office, and he was right, I changed two of them.

Lena

The civil servant offered to back up my plan. To back up someone means to support someone or something. He said, I will back up your film at the city meeting, and his support helped a lot, because the corporation also promised to back up the project with more time and a better room.

Maya

That room was special, was it not?

Lena

It was. The corporation gave us a custom-built studio. Custom-built means made specially for a particular person or purpose. The studio had custom-built shelves for the camcorder, a custom-built desk for my computer, and soft lights that made everyone look calm.

Maya

And in that studio you met a third group of people.

Lena

Yes, a team from a technology competition. A competition is an event where people try to win. This competition asked young makers to build useful tools. One team showed a phone with a missing button, a broken cable, and an old camcorder, and they repaired all three in one afternoon.

Maya

Tell us about the repair, because that is where our technology words come alive.

Lena

First, they opened the phone and found a tiny cell inside the battery pack. In our word list, cell means a small room in a prison, but in technology a cell can also be a small unit that holds power, and the team used that second sense carefully in their notes. Then they checked a chemical, a substance used in chemistry or produced by it, that had damaged the cable. The chemical had made the cable weak, so they replaced the cable with a stronger one.

Maya

And what was the small label on the cable?

Lena

The label had the letters A R D on it. In our list, ard is a technical abbreviation or specific term, a short technical word that engineers use on labels. The team leader said, when you see a technical term like ard on a cable, stay in contact with the engineer and ask what it means. To stay in contact means to keep communicating with someone regularly, and good contact saved them time.

Lena

And the team won a small prize in the competition, not because their tools were new, but because they explained everything clearly. Their academic explanation was simple, active, and absorbing, and the judges said it was the most absorbing presentation of the day.

Maya

Let us take our second short break here and remind everyone listening how to keep this progress going after today.

Lena

Good idea. Friends, imagine doing this review every day in the Muse English app. Each morning you get words for your own level, with clear meanings, photos, and pronunciation. You listen, you shadow, and you take a short quiz. It takes only a few minutes, it is free to install, and it really works. Install Muse English today and make tomorrow's lesson your next small win.

Maya

Thank you, Lena. Now let us go back to the film. How did it end?

Lena

On the last day, the childminder invited us to her home again. Her active children showed us their drawings, the civil servant brought a letter from the city, and the corporation sent a photographer. The letter said the city would accept the film for all its schools, and teachers could use it in academic classes from next month.

Maya

Did you accept their final request? They wanted a shorter version, I think.

Lena

I did accept it. To accept means to agree to take something or agree with an idea. I accepted the request because the shorter film would reach more classes. The childminder helped me choose the best moments, the civil servant checked the facts, and a kind editor promised to back up every file, so we would never lose the film.

Lena

Exactly. Good friends back up your work and back up you. On that last day, my camcorder battery was almost empty, the cable was hot, and one button felt loose, but everyone stayed calm. The custom-built studio was quiet, the academic teacher read the final words aloud, and the children sang a short song.

Maya

And the corporation?

Lena

The corporation paid for a small celebration. Their customer care team wrote personal thank you messages to every family, because customer care is not only for shops, it is how any corporation should treat people. They also stayed in contact with the childminder and the civil servant, and that contact turned into a new project about active play for young children.

Maya

So the story ends with everyone still connected.

Lena

It does. And if I tell the story again in simple words, it goes like this. A corporation asked for an academic film. A childminder opened her home. A civil servant opened his office. A custom-built studio gave us space. A button, a cable, and a camcorder gave us pictures. Active children gave us energy, and friends who back up each other gave us courage to finish.

Maya

That is a beautiful summary, and it is also a perfect review. Listeners, can you retell that summary in your own words? Try to use corporation, academic, and childminder in your first sentences. Then use custom-built, button, and camcorder. Then finish with civil servant, back up, cable, and active.

Maya

Strange words need extra meetings. That is why review day exists. You do not need to be perfect today. You need to meet the words again, hear them, say them aloud, and use them once in a sentence that is true for you.

Lena

For me, a true sentence is this. I am an active learner, I work with a corporation that cares about education, and I love making academic films that families enjoy.

Maya

And my true sentence is this. A good childminder, a fair civil servant, and a teacher who gives honest advice can change a whole street, especially when a community will back up their work.

Maya

We will finish with a quick recap. Corporation, a large company. Academic, related to education and studying. Childminder, a person who looks after children at home. Custom-built, made specially for a person or purpose. Button, the small round piece that fastens clothes, and also the control you press on a camcorder. Camcorder, a portable video camera. Civil servant, a person who works for the government. Back up, to support someone. Cable, a thick wire that carries electricity or signals. Active, always doing things, not sitting still.

Maya

Thank you for spending this review day with us. Keep your streak alive in the app, do the quiz, and come back tomorrow for fresh new words at your level.

Lena

Goodbye for now, keep speaking, keep shadowing, and remember, small daily steps build strong English.

📝گرامر: Phrasal Verbs: Word Order (turn it off, look after them)

A phrasal verb is a verb plus a particle like up, off, out or after: *turn off, look after, give up*. Where the object goes depends on the type of verb.

With separable verbs, a noun can go before or after the particle: *Turn the TV off* or *Turn off the TV*. But a pronoun always goes in the middle: *Turn it off*, never *turn off it*.

A custom-built desk in a corporation office was *put together* in the middle position, and an active civil servant can *pick you up* at seven — the pronoun sits between the verb and the particle.
