روز مرور — درس انگلیسی B2

📅 2026-11-20📊 سطح B2 (بالاتر از متوسط)📚 ۱۰ لغت تازه🎧 پادکست روزانه

📖 Muse English چیست؟

Muse English یک اپلیکیشن رایگان آموزش زبان انگلیسی است، با درس روزانه برای هر شش سطح، از A1 (مبتدی) تا C2 (تسلط کامل). هر روز لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، یک پادکست واقعی، تمرین شدوئینگ، گرامر و آزمون دارید. دربارهٔ اپ

📚 لغت‌ها

۱۰ لغت تازه لغت‌های تازه با معنی فارسی، مثال واقعی و تلفظ.
auburn /AW-burn/
صفت · adjective
Reddish-brown color, usually for hair.
خرمایی مایل به قرمز
"She has beautiful auburn hair."
awkward /AWK-werd/
صفت · adjective
Difficult, embarrassing, or not graceful.
ناجور، دست و پا چلفتی
"I felt awkward when I dropped my food."
attract /uh-TRAKT/
فعل · verb
To make someone interested in something.
جذب کردن، جلب کردن
"The new store hopes to attract many customers."
current account /KUR-unt uh-KOUNT/
اسم · noun
A bank account for daily money transactions.
حساب جاری
"I need to check my current account balance."
department store /dih-PART-munt STOR/
اسم · noun
A large shop selling many types of goods.
فروشگاه بزرگ
"We bought new clothes at the department store."
beard /BEERD/
اسم · noun
Hair growing on a man's chin and cheeks.
ریش
"My father has a long beard."
cholera /KOL-uh-ruh/
اسم · noun
A serious disease causing severe diarrhoea.
وبا
"Cholera can spread quickly in poor areas."
colour /KUL-ur/
اسم · noun
The appearance of something from light.
رنگ
"Red is my favourite colour."
beautiful /BYOO-tuh-ful/
صفت · adjective
Very pleasing to the senses; lovely.
زیبا، قشنگ
"She is a beautiful woman."
civil war /SIV-ul WOR/
گروه اسمی · noun phrase
A war between groups in the same country.
جنگ داخلی
"The country suffered a long civil war."

پادکست: روز مرور

گفت‌وگوی امروز را گوش کنید.

📝 متن پادکست

Maya

Welcome to Muse English! Hello everyone, and welcome back to our special review day. Today we are not learning new words. Instead, we are looking back at the last three days and locking in twenty one wonderful words together.

Lena

Hello Maya, and hello listeners. I love review days, because this is where the words really start to feel natural. We talked about nature and the environment, about money and shopping, and about how people look and feel when they meet for the first time.

Maya

Exactly. So let us start with a little story. Last weekend I visited my cousin in a small town, and the first person I noticed was a woman with long auburn hair. Her auburn hair was shining in the sun, and honestly, that rich auburn colour was so beautiful that everyone turned to look.

Lena

Auburn is such a lovely word for that reddish brown colour, is it not? And you said beautiful, which is another one of our review words. The town itself was beautiful in the morning light, and my cousin said the old market square looks beautiful in every season.

Maya

It really does. But I have to confess, I felt a little awkward when I arrived. I walked into the wrong cafe, I dropped my bag, and for a moment everything felt awkward and embarrassing. Have you ever had an awkward first moment like that?

Lena

Oh, many times. Last month I felt awkward at a new department store when I could not find the door. And my brother says he always feels awkward when he meets new people, but an awkward start does not mean a bad day. Sometimes an awkward moment becomes a funny story later.

Maya

That is so true. Speaking of that department store, it was huge. We spent two hours inside the department store looking at coats and shoes. A good department store can attract a lot of visitors, especially on a rainy Saturday.

Lena

Yes, and shops work hard to attract customers. Bright windows attract families, music can attract younger shoppers, and friendly staff attract people who want help. When a shop knows how to attract people, the whole street feels alive.

Maya

Inside, I picked up a woven basket near the fruit stand. I put apples and bread in my basket, and I noticed how the colour of each apple was different. One was deep red, one was golden, and the colour of the third was a soft green.

Lena

Colour is everywhere when you shop. The colour of a coat can change how you feel, and I always check the colour in natural light before I buy. My sister chose a scarf because its colour matched her eyes, and she still loves it.

Maya

At the till, I wanted to pay with my credit card, but I stopped and thought about my credit limit first. My credit card is useful, but my credit limit reminds me to be careful. I also checked my current account on my phone before I paid.

Lena

That is smart. Your current account shows your daily money, so checking your current account before a big shop is a good habit. I opened my current account when I got my first job, and I still use that same current account today.

Maya

And your credit rating matters too, does it not? A good credit rating helps you borrow money later, and paying on time protects your credit rating. My cousin told me she checks her credit rating once a year, just to be safe.

Lena

She is wise. Now, before we go further, let me share something that helps me review words like these every single day, because I use it on the bus and at home.

Maya

Please do, because I think our listeners will love it.

Lena

I open the Muse English app every morning, and it gives me fresh daily words with clear photos and real pronunciation, so the words stay in my mind. I listen to this podcast, I practise with shadowing stories, and I test myself with quick quizzes. The grammar path builds step by step, and I earn points on the leaderboard, which keeps me motivated. It is free to install, so try it today.

Maya

I agree with every word. The app makes a review day feel easy. Now, let us move from shopping to people. In the cafe, we met an old fisherman with a long grey beard.

Lena

I remember you telling me about him. His beard was long and white, and he was very affectionate with his little granddaughter. He hugged her warmly, and you could see he is an affectionate grandfather who loves telling stories.

Maya

Yes, and he told us his own father had gone bald quite young. He laughed and said being bald never stopped his father from being happy. His father was bald by thirty, but he was confident, kind, and full of energy.

Lena

I love that. Appearance words like beard, bald, and auburn describe what we see, but words like affectionate describe what we feel from a person. The fisherman had a beard you notice at once, and his beard moved when he laughed.

Maya

He also told a serious story about the sea. Years ago, after a storm, there was a casualty on one of the boats. That one casualty changed the whole village, and people still remember the sailor with respect.

Lena

That is sad, but it is an important word. A casualty is a person injured or killed in an accident or war, and hearing about a casualty reminds us to stay safe at sea. The village built a small memorial for that casualty and for others who were hurt.

Maya

The fisherman also talked about the past, when a civil war divided families in his grandfather's time. He said the civil war was the hardest period the country ever lived through, and that no one wants another civil war ever again.

Lena

History teaches us a lot through words like civil war. When people understand what a civil war costs, they work harder for peace. My history teacher always said that remembering a civil war helps the next generation choose dialogue instead.

Maya

He also spoke about health in the old days. He said cholera once spread through a port town when the water was dirty. Cholera is a serious disease, and cholera can spread quickly where clean water is hard to find.

Lena

That is why clean water and good hospitals matter so much. Doctors today work hard so that cholera does not return, and when cholera appears, health teams move fast to help families and protect children.

Maya

And nature was part of his story too. On the rocks we saw a crab lift one sharp claw to defend itself. That little claw looked strong, and the crab waved its claw at a hungry bird until the bird flew away.

Lena

A claw is small, but it tells a big story about survival. Every animal has something like a claw, a beak, or strong legs to stay safe. I will never forget watching that crab use its claw so bravely.

Maya

The fisherman cares deeply about conservation as well. He joins conservation clean ups on the beach, he teaches children about conservation, and he believes conservation protects the sea for the future.

Lena

Conservation connects to climate change too. He told us climate change is changing the fishing seasons, and many families worry about climate change and hotter summers. When we talk about climate change honestly, we can plan better together.

Maya

And each of us has a carbon footprint. Our carbon footprint grows when we drive and fly a lot, and we can shrink our carbon footprint by walking, cycling, and wasting less food. Even a small change to your carbon footprint helps.

Lena

Before we continue with more stories, I want to pause and remind everyone listening how powerful a few minutes a day can be, especially on a review day like this one.

Maya

Go ahead, this is the perfect moment for it.

Lena

If you have not installed the Muse English app yet, today is a great day to start, because it turns review into a simple daily habit. You get new words with photos and pronunciation every day, podcasts like this one, and shadowing stories at your own speed. Quizzes show you what you remember, grammar lessons guide you step by step, and points on the leaderboard show your progress. Install it free today.

Maya

Beautifully said. Now imagine we go back to that department store together. The department store is having a sale, and the sale will attract hundreds of people.

Lena

I can picture it. The windows are full of colour, and every colour is bright and cheerful. A woman with auburn hair is choosing a dress, and her auburn scarf matches her hair perfectly.

Maya

Near the door, a young man feels awkward because he forgot his wallet. It is an awkward moment, but a kind worker helps him, and he pays from his current account using his phone instead.

Lena

At the next counter, an older man with a neat beard is buying gloves. His beard is dark with a little grey, and he says his beard keeps him warm in winter. Beside him, a mother calls the scene beautiful, and the evening sky outside was beautiful too.

Maya

Let us do a quick final check together, listeners. Auburn, for that warm reddish brown hair. Awkward, for a moment that feels difficult or embarrassing. Attract, when something makes people interested and draws them in.

Lena

Current account, for your everyday bank account. Department store, for a large shop with many kinds of goods. Beard, for the hair on a man's chin and cheeks. Cholera, for that serious disease linked to dirty water. Colour, for what light gives to everything we see. Beautiful, for something very pleasing and lovely. And civil war, for a war between groups inside the same country.

Maya

And do not forget the other friends from this week. Basket, credit card, credit limit, and credit rating for your money life. Carbon footprint, climate change, conservation, claw, and casualty for nature and history. And affectionate and bald for describing people with warmth and honesty.

Lena

If you can use those words in your own sentences tonight, this review day has done its job. Thank you for reviewing with us, and join us tomorrow for brand new words.

Maya

Goodbye for now, and happy learning. Keep practising, keep smiling through the awkward moments, and we will see you tomorrow on Muse English.
