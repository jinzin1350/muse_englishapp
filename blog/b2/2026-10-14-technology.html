📚 Vocabulary

10 new words Learn new words with real examples and audio.
influencer /IN-floo-uhn-ser/
noun
a person with a large online audience who can change what people buy or think
"The influencer recommended a new skincare brand to her two million followers."
viral /VY-ruhl/
adjective
spreading very quickly online to huge numbers of people
"Her cooking video went viral and got ten million views in two days."
screenshot /SKREEN-shot/
noun
a picture of exactly what is on your phone or computer screen
"He sent me a screenshot of the error message so I could help him fix it."
webinar /WEB-uh-nar/
noun
an online class or meeting that people join over the internet
"Our company holds a monthly webinar to train new employees."
podcast /POD-kast/
noun
a series of spoken audio episodes you can listen to online
"I listen to a daily English podcast on my way to work."
mute /MYOOT/
verb
to silence a microphone, speaker, or chat notifications
"Please mute your microphone when you are not speaking in the meeting."
spam /SPAM/
noun
unwanted messages or emails sent to many people at once
"I get so much spam in my inbox that I miss important emails."
firewall /FYR-wal/
noun
software that protects a computer network from hackers and attacks
"The office firewall blocks dangerous websites automatically."
reboot /ree-BOOT/
verb
to restart a computer or phone completely
"If the app freezes, just reboot your phone and try again."
hashtag /HASH-tag/
noun
the hash symbol used to group social media posts by topic
"When you post the photo, use the hashtag Toronto Eats to reach more food lovers."

📝Grammar: Causative: Have/Get Something Done

Sometimes another person does the work for you. You arrange it and you pay for it, but you do not do it yourself. English has a special causative structure for exactly this situation, from phone repairs to podcast editing. The core pattern is **have + object + past participle**. This pattern tells the listener that someone else performed the action as a service for you. The focus is on the result you received. You can also use **get + object + past participle**.
