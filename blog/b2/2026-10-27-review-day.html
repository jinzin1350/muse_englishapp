Oh yes. A backache is a pain in your back. If you sit at a desk all day, you can get a bad backache. Maya, have you ever had a backache?

Maya

Sadly, yes. Last month I worked at my computer for ten hours, and by the evening I had a terrible backache. My back was aching, and I could not sit comfortably. So I stood up, I stretched, and I walked around the room.

Lena

Before we go on, here is something for our new listeners. It is free, so download it today and keep learning with us.

Lena

That is good advice. When your back aches, movement usually helps. And what about a simple ache? An ache is a dull, continuous pain. A backache is one kind of ache, but your head can ache too, and your muscles can ache after exercise.

Maya

Exactly. And here is another health word: ankle. Your ankle is the joint that connects your foot and your leg. If you twist your ankle while playing football, you have to rest it.

Lena

And as we get older, we talk about ageing. Ageing skin needs special care, and ageing bodies need gentle exercise. Medical science continues to advance, and doctors can now help people in amazing ways. For example, some people receive an artificial hip, a man-made replacement for a damaged hip joint, and after the operation they can walk again.

Maya

That is wonderful. An artificial hip can change a person's life. And modern hospitals work almost automatically now. Doors open automatically when you get close, and lights switch on automatically too. Automatically means without human control or thought.

Maya

Great tip, Lena. Now let us talk about money. Yesterday's words were all about money and shopping. Lena, what is an amount?

Lena

An amount is simply a quantity of something. Please pay the full amount, the shopkeeper says. If you buy three books, the total amount is the price of all three together.

Maya

And where do you keep your money? In a bank account, of course. I opened a new bank account last week, and now I can save a small amount every month. But sometimes I need cash quickly, so I look for a cash machine. A cash machine is a machine where you can get money.

Lena

And be careful: the bank charges interest on loans. To charge interest means to ask for money as a fee. The government also collects corporation tax, the tax that companies pay on their profits. Some people say the government plans to reduce corporation tax, and others say it should stay the same.

Maya

Money can make people competitive. A competitive person wants to win or be more successful than others. My brother is very competitive in sports, and he is competitive at work too. But smart people consolidate. To consolidate means to combine things into one stronger whole. We need to consolidate our debts, my friend said, and pay one amount each month instead of five.

Lena

That is a good plan. Consolidate, simplify, and breathe. Now, Maya, let us move to technology, our third day of words.

Maya

Yes! Technology words are everywhere. First, an app. An app is a small computer program for a phone. I downloaded a new app for learning English, and I use that app every morning.

Lena

And an application is a formal request, often for a course or a job. Her application for university was successful. So an app on your phone is short and friendly, but an application is formal. Do not mix them up!

Maya

Good point. Now, the Web. The Web is the internet, a system of linked computer documents. I found the information I needed on the Web. When you use the Web, you often need an account. An account is a record of your money or your online profile. I need to create an account to use this app.

Lena

And when you create an account, you give your address. Your address is the number and street where you live. What is your home address? the form asks. Sometimes you need to alter your address. Ready! Go!

Maya

You sit at your desk all day, and in the evening your back hurts. What is the word?

Corporation tax. The government plans to reduce corporation tax this year.

Brilliant. Now: a small program on your phone that helps you learn English.

Lena

An app! I downloaded a new app for learning English, and I love that app.

Maya

Lena

An application. Her application for university was successful.

Wonderful. Now: you look at numbers very carefully to understand a problem.

Analyse. We need to analyse the data to find a solution, and we analyse our mistakes to learn faster.

Exactly. Now: a door that opens by itself, with no person touching it.

Automatically! The door opens automatically when you get close.

And the place where you find information, a system of linked computer documents?

📝Grammar: Reporting Verbs (suggest, advise, deny, refuse)

Instead of **say** and **tell**, we often use a **reporting verb** that shows the speaker's purpose. Each reporting verb has its own **pattern**: a to-infinitive, object + to-infinitive, -ing, preposition + -ing, or a **that** clause. Use verb + **to**-infinitive after **agree**, **offer**, **promise**, **refuse**, **threaten**, **decide** and **claim**. Use verb + object + **to**-infinitive after **advise**, **ask**, **tell**, **warn**, **remind**, **encourage**, **persuade**, **invite** and **order**. Use verb + **-ing** after **deny**, **admit**, **suggest** and **recommend**. **Sugges
