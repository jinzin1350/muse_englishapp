📝 Grammar: 8B: Reported speech — sentences and questions

We use reported speech to tell someone what another person **said**. It is how we share news, stories, and messages in everyday conversation. When the reporting verb (**said**, **told**) is in the past, tenses usually shift one step back. Present simple becomes **past simple**, **will** becomes **would**, present perfect becomes **past perfect**, **can** becomes **could**, and **must** becomes **had to**.
