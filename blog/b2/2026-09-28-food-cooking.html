📝Grammar: Possibility and probability: must / may / might / could

When you talk about how sure you are, English uses different modal verbs. Use must when you are almost 100 percent sure: it is a logical conclusion from what you see or know. Use may or might when it is 50-50: possible, but you are not sure. Use could for a weak possibility. For a negative conclusion use can't. Note: mustn't means 'not allowed', which is different. For the past, use must have plus the past participle.
