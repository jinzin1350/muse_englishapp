📝Grammar: Choosing between comparatives and superlatives

Use **comparative adjectives + than** to compare two people, places, or things. You can compare two tutors, or decide which textbook is better for you. Short adjectives take **-er**: tall → **taller**, big → **bigger**. Longer adjectives use **more**: modern → **more modern**, and one-syllable adjectives ending in -ed use **more** too: tired → **more tired**. Three adjectives are irregular: good → **better**, bad → **worse**, far → **further** (or **farther**). A good assessment score always feels better than a bad one.
