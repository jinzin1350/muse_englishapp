📝 Grammar: 8A: Choosing between gerunds and infinitives

A gerund is a verb with -ing that works like a noun. We use **verb + -ing** after prepositions and phrasal verbs, such as *good at*, *give up*, or *feel like*. We also use **verb + -ing** as the subject of a sentence, and after verbs like **enjoy**, **don't mind**, **miss**, and **keep**. We use **to + verb** after adjectives, and to show reason or purpose, the reason why we do something. We also use **to + verb** after verbs like **want**, **need**, **learn**, **decide**, **agree**, and **hope**. Some verbs take **verb + person + to + verb**: **ask**, **tell**, **want**, and **would like*
