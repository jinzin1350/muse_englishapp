Beautiful. Browser, bookmark, blog, broadband, browse, attachment, associated. Seven useful technology words for your everyday life.

Then send yourself an email with an attachment, maybe a photo you like, just to practise the word attachment.

And when you browse tonight, notice how many times you use these words in your head. That is how vocabulary becomes yours.

Lovely idea. Real conversations are the best way to remember new words. Use them, and they will stay with you.

Before we say goodbye, let's clear up two small confusions. First, browse and browser. They look similar, but they are different.

📝Grammar: Non-Defining Relative Clauses

A **non-defining relative clause** adds extra information about someone or something we have already identified. We put it between **commas**, and the sentence still makes sense without it. Use **who** for people, **which** for things, **whose** for possession, **where** for places, and **when** for times. For example, my favourite blog, **which** I read every week, is about travel. Never use **that** in a non-defining clause. Never leave out the pronoun either. The pronoun is always there, between the commas. **Which** can also refer to a whole idea.
