📝 Grammar: 6A: Passive (all tenses)

We use the passive (**be** + past participle) when we do not say, know, or need to say who does the action. If your wallet disappears, you say 'My wallet **has been stolen**' — the thief is unknown, and that is the point. The object of the active verb becomes the new subject. 'They built the bridge' becomes 'The bridge **was built**.' Use **by** + agent only when the doer matters. 'Gladiator **was directed** by Ridley Scott' — here we want to name the director.
