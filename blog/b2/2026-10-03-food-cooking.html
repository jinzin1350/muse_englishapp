📝 Grammar: 5A: Past tenses - simple, continuous, perfect

**Past simple** (worked, went, devoured) is for finished actions in the past. We use it when we say, ask, or know when the action happened. We also use **past simple** for repeated past actions, like the big family **feast** you enjoyed every winter. **Past continuous** (was/were + verb-ing) shows an action in progress at a specific past time. It often pairs with a short **past simple** action that interrupts it. We also use **past continuous** with while for two actions happening together. Never use it with non-action verbs like want, need, or know. **Past perfect** (had + past participle
