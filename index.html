Questions

Is Muse English free?

Yes: lessons, podcast, vocabulary, shadowing practice and quizzes are free.

Do I need an account?

Not to read lessons: every lesson on this site is free to read without an account. A free account saves your progress and points in the app.

Which level should I choose?

A1: first words and simple sentences. A2: everyday conversations and essential grammar. B1: real topics, longer conversations. B2: work, culture and opinions. C1: nuance, idioms and complex ideas. C2: near-native fluency and precision. Not sure? Open today’s A2 lesson. If most of the words are new, try A1; if they feel easy, try B1.
