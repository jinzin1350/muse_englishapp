Maya: Hello and welcome to Word Kitchen! I am Maya.
Lena: And I am Lena. Today we talk about travel and transport. Ten easy words!
Maya: Word one: car. A car is a small road vehicle with four wheels.
Lena: A car takes you from home to work, or to the shops. I have a car. My car is red. Do you have a car?
Maya: Yes, my car is blue. I drive my car to work every day. A car is very useful.
Lena: Word two: bus. A bus is a big vehicle. Many people ride in a bus.
Maya: A bus is bigger than a car. I take the bus to the city. The bus is cheap. Do you like the bus?
Lena: Yes, the bus is good. I read a book on the bus. A bus stop is near my house.
Maya: Word three: train. A train is long. It runs on rails.
Lena: A train is fast. I love the train. Last week I took the train to my mother's house. The train was clean and quiet.
Maya: Trains are great for long trips. A train can be faster than a car.
Lena: Word four: taxi. A taxi is a car you pay to take you somewhere.
Maya: When I am late, I take a taxi. A taxi is fast, but a taxi is not cheap. The taxi driver was very kind.
Lena: Word five: plane. A plane flies in the sky.
Maya: A plane is the fastest way to travel. My first plane trip was to London. A plane has many seats. I like to sit by the window in a plane.
Lena: Word six: bike. A bike has two wheels. You ride a bike with your legs.
Maya: A bike is good for short trips. My son has a new bike. On Sundays we ride our bikes in the park.
Lena: Word seven: ticket. A ticket is a small paper. It lets you travel.
Maya: You need a ticket for the train. You need a ticket for the plane. I bought my ticket yesterday. A ticket can be cheap or dear.
Lena: Word eight: road. A road is a long hard path. Cars and buses use the road.
Maya: The road to the airport is long. Be careful on the road. A wide road is good for a bike.
Lena: Word nine: station. A station is the place where trains and buses stop.
Maya: I waited at the station for my friend. The station was big. A station has many people in the morning.
Lena: Word ten: airport. An airport is the big place where planes take off and land.
Maya: The airport is very big. I was at the airport at six in the morning. My plane left the airport at eight.
Lena: Now a quick game! I say a word, and you think of the meaning. Car!
Maya: A small vehicle with four wheels. Bus!
Lena: A big vehicle for many people. Train!
Maya: A long vehicle on rails. Taxi!
Lena: A car you pay for. Plane!
Maya: It flies in the sky. Bike!
Lena: Two wheels and your legs. Ticket!
Maya: A small paper for travel. Road!
Lena: A long path for cars. Station!
Maya: The place for trains and buses. Airport!
Lena: The big place for planes. Great! You know all ten words.
Maya: Let's say them one last time: car, bus, train, taxi, plane, bike, ticket, road, station, airport.
Lena: A car on the road. A bus to the city. A train from the station.
Maya: A taxi to the airport. A plane in the sky. A bike in the park. A ticket for the train.
Lena: Thank you for listening to Word Kitchen! Practice these words, and see you tomorrow!
