My Day at Work

I have a new job.
My job is at an office.
The office is big and clean.
There is a desk in my office.
My desk is new.
I put my book on the desk.
The teacher works at a school.
The teacher is very kind.
The teacher helps the children learn.
My teacher has a big desk.
The doctor helps sick people.
The doctor is at the office.
The doctor sits at her desk.
My job is very good.
I like my job.
I work in the office every day.
My desk is in the office.
The doctor and the teacher are friends.
