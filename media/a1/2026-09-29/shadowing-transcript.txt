My Day in the City
My street is quiet in the morning.
There is a small shop on my street.
I buy fresh milk at the shop every day.
The shop is next to the market on my street.
I go to the market on Saturdays, before the traffic starts.
The market has fresh fruit and vegetables.
After the market, I walk to the park.
Children play in the park, far from the traffic.
I sit on a bench in the park and rest.
The bench under the big tree is my favorite bench.
From the bench we can see the bridge over the river.
The bridge is old and beautiful, and a small shop stands near the bridge.
We walk over the bridge to the square.
The square is big and open.
Many people meet at the square, or rest in the park near the square.
Near the square there is a bakery.
The bakery has warm bread and sweet cakes, and the bakery opens very early.
Next to the bakery there is a cinema.
We go to the cinema on Friday evening.
After the cinema, the traffic is heavy. The traffic moves slowly, so we walk home to our quiet street and talk about the cinema.
