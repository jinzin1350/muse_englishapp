Maya: Hi everyone! Welcome to Word Kitchen. I am Maya, and I am in my kitchen today. I am cooking dinner. And my friend Lena is here with me.
Lena: Hi, Maya! Hi, everyone! Your kitchen smells so good, Maya. What are you making?
Maya: I am making vegetable soup. I have a big pot on the stove. The pot is full of hot water. A big pot is best for soup.
Lena: A big pot! I can see the pot. It is very big. Is the soup for many people?
Maya: Just for us two. But I love a big pot. In a big pot, I can put a lot of vegetables. More vegetables, more taste.
Lena: What vegetables do you put in the pot?
Maya: First, carrots. I love carrots. I cut three carrots and put them in the pot. Carrots are sweet, and carrots make the soup sweet.
Lena: Carrots are my favorite too. The carrots look so fresh. What else goes in the pot?
Maya: Potatoes. I cut two potatoes into small pieces. The potatoes go into the pot with the carrots. Potatoes are soft and good in soup. I really love potatoes.
Lena: I love potatoes too. Fried potatoes are the best, but potatoes in soup are wonderful.
Maya: Yes! And now a tomato. I cut one tomato into the pot. A tomato makes the soup red and fresh. One tomato is enough for this pot.
Lena: Tomatoes are great. I eat tomatoes every day. Do you put salt in the soup, Maya?
Maya: Yes, a little salt. Salt makes food tasty. The soup needs a little salt. But not too much salt. Too much salt is not good.
Lena: Right. A little salt is enough. Your soup will be perfect. Do you use a pan too? I see a pan on the stove.
Maya: Yes, this pan is for the onions. The pan is hot now. I put a little oil in the pan. Just a little oil.
Lena: Oil in the pan. I can see the oil. The oil is hot.
Maya: Now the onions go into the pan. They fry in the hot pan. Listen! The pan is singing. A hot pan and a little oil. That is all you need.
Lena: The pot has the soup, and the pan has the onions. The pot and the pan are both busy today!
Maya: Ha! Yes, the pot and the pan are my best friends in the kitchen. The pot is for soup, and the pan is for frying. Now, let me pour the soup into a bowl.
Lena: A bowl? Which bowl do you use?
Maya: This big bowl. A bowl is perfect for hot soup. I like to eat soup from a bowl. Here, Lena, this bowl is for you. And this bowl is for me.
Lena: Thank you! The soup in this bowl smells amazing. The bowl is hot, so I will be careful.
Maya: Wait! I have something sweet for after dinner. A cake!
Lena: A cake? Oh, I love cake. Do you make the cake with sugar?
Maya: Yes, with a lot of sugar. Sugar makes the cake sweet. I put two cups of sugar in the cake. No sugar, no cake! Sugar is the secret.
Lena: Sugar is the best secret. And butter? Does the cake have butter?
Maya: Yes, butter too. I melt the butter in a pan. Look, the butter is soft now. Soft butter is best for cake. Then I mix the sugar and the butter together.
Lena: Sugar and butter! The sugar and the butter make the cake sweet and soft. The butter smells so good.
Maya: Exactly. Now we eat. First, a hot bowl of soup. Then cake with sugar and butter. A perfect dinner.
Lena: What a kitchen! A pot, a pan, a bowl, salt, sugar, butter, oil, carrots, potatoes, and a tomato. Ten wonderful words, and one wonderful dinner.
Maya: And one wonderful friend. Come to my kitchen any time, Lena.
Lena: I will! Thank you for cooking with us today. Goodbye, everyone!
