In the Kitchen

I cook soup in a big pot with carrots.
The pot is hot, and the carrots are sweet.
I fry eggs in a big pan with oil.
The pan is hot, and the oil is ready.
I eat soup from a big bowl near the pot.
I fill the big bowl with soup and carrots.
Add a little salt to the pot of soup.
Salt makes the soup and carrots tasty.
I put sugar in my tea and butter on my bread.
The sugar is sweet, and the butter is soft.
I cut a red tomato for the salad.
I put tomatoes in a bowl; they are red.
I peel potatoes for the big pot.
I put potatoes in a bowl; the carrots are orange.
I put potatoes and tomatoes in the pan.
The pan is hot with oil and potatoes.
I pour oil on the salad with tomatoes.
Butter melts in the hot pan with salt.
I mix butter and sugar for the cake.
The cake has sugar, and the soup has salt.
