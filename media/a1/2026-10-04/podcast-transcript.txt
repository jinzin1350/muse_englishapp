Maya: Hello and welcome to Word Kitchen. I am Maya.
Lena: And I am Lena. Today we talk about school things.
Maya: Yes, five small words: book, pen, pencil, notebook, and bag. These words live in every classroom.
Lena: Let us start with book. A book has pages. You read a book. I read a book every morning with tea.
Maya: Nice. I read a book before bed. A good book is my friend. Yesterday I bought a new book at the shop. It is a small book, only one hundred pages.
Lena: One hundred pages. That is a real book.
Maya: Now pen. A pen is for writing. I write with a blue pen. Lena, what color is your pen?
Lena: My pen is black. I always have a pen in my pocket. At work, I use a pen all day. Yesterday my pen ran out of ink, so I bought a new pen.
Maya: That happens to me too. Now pencil. A pencil is also for writing, but with a pencil you can erase. I draw with a pencil. A short pencil is in my bag right now.
Lena: You carry a pencil in your bag?
Maya: Yes. My bag is full of school things. My bag has a book, a pen, and a pencil. Look, this is my bag. It is a big red bag.
Lena: My bag is small. My bag is blue. In my bag I have one notebook. A notebook has clean white pages. You write in a notebook. I write my ideas in a notebook.
Maya: Same here. I have a new notebook. I open my notebook and write a new word in it. This notebook is green. My old notebook is full.
Lena: Let us review the five words. Book. Pen. Pencil. Notebook. Bag.
Maya: Good idea. Repeat after us. Book. I love my book. Pen. I need a pen. Pencil. A yellow pencil. Notebook. My notebook. Bag. My bag.
Lena: Now a small story. Every morning I take my bag. I put my book in the bag. I put my pen in the bag. I put my pencil in the bag. And my notebook goes in the bag too.
Maya: Your bag sounds heavy.
Lena: A little. But I am ready for school. At school I open my book. I read one page. Then I take my pen. I write in my notebook. The teacher speaks, and I write fast.
Maya: And your pencil?
Lena: With my pencil I draw a small house. The house has a door and a window. The teacher smiles.
Maya: That is lovely. So today: a book for reading, a pen for writing, a pencil for drawing, a notebook for your ideas, and a bag to carry them all.
Lena: Here is your homework. Take your bag. Put a book in it. Put a pen in it. Put a pencil in it. Put your notebook in it. Then say the words out loud: book, pen, pencil, notebook, bag.
Maya: One more tip. Write the words in your notebook. Book. Pen. Pencil. Notebook. Bag. Your notebook is your best teacher.
Lena: And put that notebook in your bag. Then your bag has everything you need.
Maya: Say them three times. Book, pen, pencil, notebook, bag. Book, pen, pencil, notebook, bag.
Lena: You did great. Next time we learn five new words.
Maya: See you in the kitchen. Bye.
