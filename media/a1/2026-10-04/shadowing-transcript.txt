My School Bag

This is my school bag.
My bag is red.
I put a book in my bag.
The book is big.
I love my new book.
I read my book at home.
I put a pen in my bag.
The pen is blue.
I write with my pen.
My pen is in my hand.
I put a pencil in my bag.
The pencil is yellow.
The pencil is short.
I draw with my pencil.
I put a notebook in my bag.
My notebook is small.
I open my notebook.
I write in my notebook every day.
My bag is ready for school.
I take my bag and go.
