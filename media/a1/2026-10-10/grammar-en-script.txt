Tutor: I love becomes she loved.
Tutor: Mom says, The cookie is sweet.
Tutor: We tell a friend: Mom said the cookie was sweet.
Tutor: Is becomes was.
Tutor: A common mistake: do not say Anna said I love cake.
Tutor: Say Anna said she loved cake.
Tutor: So remember: I love becomes she loved, and is becomes was.
