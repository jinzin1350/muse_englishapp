Tutor: Hello! Today we learn prepositions of place.
Tutor: Prepositions of place tell us where something is.
Tutor: We say in for inside something.
Tutor: The milk is in the fridge.
Tutor: We say on for a surface.
Tutor: Your phone is on the table.
Tutor: We say under for below something.
Tutor: The dog is sleeping under the table.
Tutor: We say next to for at the side.
Tutor: Sit next to me.
Tutor: We say between for two things.
Tutor: The bank is between the cafe and the hotel.
Tutor: Be careful! We say next to, not next.
Tutor: The book is next to the lamp.
