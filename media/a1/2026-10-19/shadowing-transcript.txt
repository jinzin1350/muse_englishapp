A Warm Kitchen

Maya is in the kitchen.
She makes hot soup.
The soup is hot and delicious.
She puts chicken in the soup.
She puts fish and chicken in the soup too.
Fish and chicken make the soup delicious.
She likes fish and chicken.
She drinks hot tea.
She puts lemon in the tea.
Lemon makes the tea delicious.
The lemon is in the hot tea.
She feels warm and happy.
Good food makes her feel good.
It gets dark early in winter.
In the dark, she drinks her tea.
The dark kitchen feels warm.
Her shoulder is tired.
She puts her bag on her shoulder.
Her shoulder and her knee hurt.
She sits down and touches her knee. Her knee feels good now.
