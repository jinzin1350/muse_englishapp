Lena: Birds! A bird is an animal with wings. A bird can fly. I saw a small bird in the park. The bird was singing in the tree. I love that little bird!

Lena: The river was so calm. A river can be long and wide. People swim in the river in summer. I want to visit that river again!
Maya: The sea is big water, bigger than a river. The sea is blue and deep. I love the sea in the morning. We walked by the sea and heard the waves.
Lena: The sea! Many boats sail on the sea. Fish and big animals live in the sea. I want to swim in the sea this weekend.
Maya: A flower is the pretty part of a plant. Flowers have bright colors. I picked a red flower for you. The flower smells so nice! One flower can make your day happy.
Lena: Thank you! I love that flower. A flower needs sun and rain. In spring the garden is full of flowers. That yellow flower is my favorite.

Lena: I love the rain! After the rain, the air is fresh. The rain fills the river. Plants drink the rain. Yes, I really love the rain!

Lena: That cloud looks like a bird! Clouds change every minute. White clouds mean a sunny day. I love watching a cloud drift by.
