A Walk in Nature
The sun is in the sky.
The tree is big and green.
A bird sits in the tree.
I see a white cloud.
The flower is red and pretty.
Rain falls from the cloud.
The grass is wet from the rain.
The river runs to the sea.
The mountain is very high.
The warm sun shines on the grass.
A bird flies over the river.
The tree grows near the mountain.
I pick a yellow flower.
The sea is blue and deep.
The cloud brings more rain.
Grass and a flower grow by the river.
The bird sings in the morning sun by the sea.
The mountain is behind the cloud.
The bird rests in the tree by the river near the sea.
The sun, the rain, the grass, and the flower make the mountain beautiful.
