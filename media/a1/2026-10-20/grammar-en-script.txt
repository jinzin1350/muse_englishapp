Tutor: Today we learn there is and there are.
Tutor: We use there is and there are to say something is in a place.
Tutor: Use there is with one thing. There is a basin in the bathroom.
Tutor: Use there are with many things. There are two bedrooms in my house.
Tutor: For no, say there isn't. There isn't a basement in my flat.
Tutor: For many things, say there aren't. There aren't any boxes in the bedroom.
Tutor: For a question, say is there. Is there an armchair in your room?
Tutor: For many things, say are there. Are there any towels in the bathroom?
Tutor: A common mistake. Do not say there are a book. Say there is a book.
Tutor: Remember. One thing, there is. Many things, there are.
