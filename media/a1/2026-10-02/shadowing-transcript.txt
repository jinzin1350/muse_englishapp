Shadowing: Money and Shopping

1. I have some money in my wallet.
2. My wallet is small and red.
3. I count my money.
4. I see a small coin on the table.
5. The coin is round and shiny.
6. I put the coin in my wallet with my money.
7. I go to the shop to buy bread.
8. I look at the price of the bread.
9. The price is low, so the bread is cheap.
10. I buy the cheap bread with cash.
11. Cash is paper money, and a coin is metal money.
12. I pay for the bread with cash.
13. I take the cash from my wallet and pay the shop.
14. Shops sell milk and eggs and cheese, and I pay for the milk.
15. They sell expensive apples and cheap oranges.
16. The apples are expensive today, much too expensive.
17. The price of the apples is very high.
18. They are too expensive, so I do not buy them.
19. I buy cheap bananas because shops sell cheap bananas every day.
20. The shops sell fruit, and I pay a low price for the bananas.
