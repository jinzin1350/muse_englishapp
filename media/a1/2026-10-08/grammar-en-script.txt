Tutor: Hello, learners. Welcome to grammar time.
Tutor: Today we learn the second conditional.
Tutor: It is for dreams and wishes. Not real things.
Tutor: Look at this sentence. If I were a nurse, I would help many people.
Tutor: Am I a nurse now? No. It is a dream.
Tutor: The first part uses if and a past verb. If I were a nurse.
Tutor: The second part uses would. I would help many people.
Tutor: Here is one more. If I had a fever, I would go to the hospital.
Tutor: Do I have a fever now? No. It is a wish.
Tutor: Common mistake. Do not use would in the if part.
Tutor: Say: If I had a fever. Not: If I would have a fever.
Tutor: Remember: if plus past, then would plus verb.
