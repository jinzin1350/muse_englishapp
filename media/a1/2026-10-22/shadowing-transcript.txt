A Fun Week

1. What is your age? Tell me your age.
2. My age is ten.
3. Age is just a number.
4. I am happy at my age.
5. Do you like to fly?
6. I want to fly one day!
7. We will fly to Paris next week.
8. To fly is very fun. Fly with me!
9. Tuesday is the second day of the week.
10. He finished second in the race.
11. The second of May is my birthday.
12. I was second in the game. Second is good!
13. Let us talk about Friday.
14. What about Saturday? Tell me about your day.
15. We talk about films. About is a small word.
16. I love action films!
17. An action film is very exciting.
18. We watch an action film on Sunday.
19. Do you like action films? I love action films!
