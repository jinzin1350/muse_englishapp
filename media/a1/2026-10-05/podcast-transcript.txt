Maya: Hi everyone, and welcome to Word Kitchen. I am Maya.
Lena: And I am Lena. Today we talk about feelings.
Maya: Yes. We learn five new words today. All five are about how we feel.
Lena: The first word is happy. When you smile, you are happy.
Maya: I am happy today. The sun is out, and I am happy.
Lena: My friend is happy too. She is happy because it is her birthday.
Maya: A happy day is a good day. Do you feel happy now? I hope you are happy.
Lena: Happy people laugh a lot. I love a happy face.
Maya: The next word is sad. Sad is not happy. When you cry, you are sad.
Lena: Yesterday I was sad. My favorite cup broke, and I was sad.
Maya: It is okay to be sad sometimes. A sad movie can make us sad.
Lena: But sad days pass. Tomorrow is a new day, and the sad feeling goes away.
Maya: A sad friend needs a hug. Call your friend when he is sad.
Lena: The third word is tired. After a long day, you are tired.
Maya: I am tired tonight. I worked all day, and now I am tired.
Lena: My brother is tired too. He ran a lot, and he is tired.
Maya: When you are tired, you need rest. A tired body needs sleep.
Lena: Are you tired now? Sit down, rest, and the tired feeling will go.
Maya: A tired student can sleep early. Go to bed when you are tired.
Lena: The fourth word is hungry. When you need food, you are hungry.
Maya: The baby is hungry. She is hungry and wants milk.
Lena: I am hungry at noon. Lunch time makes me hungry.
Maya: A hungry stomach says, eat now. Do not wait when you are hungry.
Lena: Eat an apple, and you are not hungry anymore. Good food ends a hungry feeling.
Maya: A hungry dog eats fast. Feed the dog when he is hungry.
Lena: The last word is angry. Angry means you are mad.
Maya: He is angry with me. I was late, and he is angry.
Lena: Do not be angry. Take a breath when you feel angry.
Maya: An angry voice is loud. Talk soft, and the angry feeling goes away.
Lena: I was angry yesterday, but now I am not angry. Angry days are short.
Maya: An angry heart is heavy. Let the angry feeling go, and smile.
Lena: Let me tell you about my day yesterday. I will use our five words.
Maya: Yes, please. Tell us.
Lena: In the morning, I was happy. The sun was out, and I was happy.
Maya: A happy morning is the best start.
Lena: At noon, I was hungry. I ate rice and bread, and then I was not hungry.
Maya: Good. A hungry person must eat.
Lena: In the afternoon, I was tired. My work was hard, and I was tired.
Maya: A tired person must rest. Did you rest?
Lena: Yes. I sat down, and the tired feeling went away.
Maya: In the evening, my brother was angry. He lost his ball, and he was angry.
Lena: Oh no. Was he angry for a long time?
Maya: No. We talked, and he was not angry anymore. He was happy again.
Lena: And no one was sad yesterday. That is good. A sad day is a hard day.
Maya: I love your day. Happy, hungry, tired, angry, and no sad.
Lena: So today we learned five feeling words. Let us say them one more time.
Maya: Happy, sad, tired, hungry, and angry.
Lena: How do you feel today? Are you happy, sad, tired, hungry, or angry?
Maya: Tell a friend how you feel. Use the new words every day.
Lena: Thank you for listening to Word Kitchen. See you tomorrow.
