A Busy Day

1. I am happy today.
2. My sister is happy too.
3. We are happy in the park.
4. A happy smile is nice.
5. The dog is sad in the rain.
6. She is sad at night.
7. Do not be sad, my friend.
8. A sad song is on the radio.
9. I am tired now.
10. My dad is tired after work.
11. We are tired, but we are happy.
12. Rest is good for tired people.
13. I am hungry at noon.
14. My mom is hungry too.
15. We eat bread when we are hungry.
16. A hungry cat wants milk.
17. He is angry today.
18. My brother is angry too, but I am not angry.
19. Do not be angry, smile.
