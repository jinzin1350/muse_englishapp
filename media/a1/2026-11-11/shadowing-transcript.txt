A Hot Lunch at Home

I put garlic in the hot soup.
The garlic soup is hot and good.
Mom adds garlic to the rice.
I like garlic on my bread.
The garlic bread is hot.
Mom cooks a hamburger for me.
My hamburger is hot and big.
I eat a hamburger with cheese.
Dad wants a hamburger too.
The hamburger box is heavy.
This bag is heavy for me.
The heavy box has ten hamburgers.
I cannot lift the heavy bag.
Please help me with the heavy box.
I buy green grapes at the market.
The grapes are sweet and cold.
I eat hot soup and grapes after lunch.
I wash the grapes and share the grapes with Mom.
