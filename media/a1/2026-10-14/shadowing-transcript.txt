My Family Counts
I have one cat.
The cat drinks one cup of milk.
One cat, one cup. Good!
My sister has two dogs.
The dogs eat two bones.
Two dogs, two bones. Fun!
My brother has three pens.
He writes with three pens.
Three pens, three colors. Nice!
I see five birds in the sky.
The birds sing five songs.
Five birds, five songs. Happy!
My grandmother has six eggs.
She cooks six eggs for us.
Six eggs, six smiles. Yum!
One, two, three, five, six. My family counts!
