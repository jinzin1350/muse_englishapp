Maya: Hi, Lena! Welcome to Word Kitchen! Today we talk about colors and numbers. It is easy and fun.
Lena: Hi, Maya! Yes! Colors and numbers are everywhere. Let us start with the color red.
Maya: Red is a bright color. Look, I see a red apple on the table.
Lena: And I see a red car in the street. Red cars are fast and beautiful.
Maya: Red is also the color of fire. A big red fire is very hot.
Lena: I have a red shirt. My red shirt is new, and I love it.
Maya: So red is everywhere. Now let us talk about blue.
Lena: Blue is the color of the sky. The sky is blue today, Maya.
Maya: Yes, blue is calm and quiet. I see a blue door on my street.
Lena: My cup is blue. A blue cup of hot tea is very nice in the morning.
Maya: Blue is great. Now let us count together. One, two, three, four.
Lena: Four! Four is a number. I have four pens in my bag.
Maya: Four pens? I have four pens too. And look, four of my pens are red!
Lena: Ha! Four red pens and four green pens. Green is the color of grass.
Maya: The grass in the park is green. Green is a fresh color.
Lena: Green apples are sour. I eat a green apple every morning.
Maya: Now yellow. Yellow is the color of the sun. The sun is yellow in the blue sky.
Lena: I see a yellow bird in the tree. The yellow bird sings in the morning.
Maya: Look, four birds! One, two, three, four. Four yellow birds on the green tree.
Lena: Beautiful! Now the color black. Black is the darkest color. The night is black.
Maya: A black cat walks at night. The black cat is quiet and soft.
Lena: My shoes are black. Black shoes are good for school and for work.
Maya: White is next. White is the color of milk. The milk in my glass is white.
Lena: Snow is white. In winter, the snow is white and cold and clean.
Maya: Now a big number. I have ten fingers. Let us count them. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!
Lena: Ten! Ten is a big number. Look, ten white flowers in the garden.
Maya: The white flowers are pretty. And look, the pink flowers are pretty too.
Lena: Pink is a soft color. Pink is like a soft, light red.
Maya: I see a pink balloon at the party. Look, four pink balloons!
Lena: Four pink balloons and ten pink flowers. So much pink!
Maya: Brown is next. Brown is the color of wood. Our table is brown.
Lena: Chocolate is brown. Brown chocolate is sweet, and I love it.
Maya: Look, ten pieces of brown chocolate on the table. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!
Lena: Wow! Can we eat them? Ten pieces is a lot!
Maya: Maybe after the show! Now let us say all our words one more time.
Lena: Red is bright. Blue is calm. Green is fresh.
Maya: Yellow is warm. Black is dark. White is clean.
Lena: Pink is soft. Brown is warm. Four is a number. Ten is a number.
Maya: Great work today, friends! Remember these ten words, and we will see you tomorrow in the Word Kitchen!
Lena: Bye bye!
