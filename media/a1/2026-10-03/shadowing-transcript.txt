Today's Shadowing Story: The Party

1. Today we have a party at home. The tree in the garden is green.
2. I see four red balloons and one red flag on the wall.
3. My sister wears a red dress and red shoes.
4. Four friends come to our home.
5. They sit on four blue chairs.
6. The blue chairs are new, one friend wears a blue hat, and the blue door is open.
7. I eat a green apple, and my sister eats a green pear.
8. Ten green apples are in the brown bag.
9. The brown bag is big, and the bag is brown.
10. My mother takes the brown bag with ten apples home.
11. My brother sees a yellow bird in the tree.
12. The yellow bird sings in the morning sun.
13. Four yellow flowers and one yellow cup stand on the table.
14. Ten friends drink white milk from ten white cups.
15. The white milk is cold, and white clouds are in the sky.
16. I wear a black shirt and black shoes.
17. At night, the sky is black.
18. The black cat is quiet and soft.
19. I see a pink flower and a pink balloon in the park.
20. The pink flower is small, and four pink balloons are in the sky.
