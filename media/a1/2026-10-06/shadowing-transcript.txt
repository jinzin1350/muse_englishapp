My Computer and Phone

1. I have a small black phone.
2. My phone is on the table.
3. Where is my phone charger?
4. The charger is white.
5. Plug the charger in the wall.
6. Now my phone has power.
7. My computer is on my desk.
8. Look at the big screen.
9. The screen shows pictures.
10. I move the mouse with my hand.
11. Click with the mouse.
12. I play games on my computer.
13. My father works on his computer.
14. The computer screen is bright.
15. Touch the phone screen.
16. The mouse is gray.
17. I use my charger every day.
18. The mouse helps me click.
