I go for a walk in nature.
The sky is big and blue.
I look at the sky.
The sun is in the sky.
The moon comes up at night.
I see the moon in the dark sky.
The moon is round and bright.
Next to the moon there is a star.
One star is small but bright.
I see another star in the sky.
The star twinkles in the dark sky.
I love the moon and the star.
A tree has a green leaf.
I pick up a leaf from the ground.
The leaf is small and green.
I put the leaf in my bag.
A gray stone is on the road.
The stone is smooth and hard.
I take the stone home with me.
I love my stone and my leaf.
