Tutor: Hello! Today we learn can and can't.
Tutor: Can talks about what you can do.
Tutor: I can swim.
Tutor: She can speak three languages.
Tutor: Use can, then the base verb.
Tutor: He can play. Not he cans play.
Tutor: Can't means cannot.
Tutor: I can't drive.
Tutor: Can you cook? Yes, I can.
Tutor: Can I use your phone? Yes, you can.
Tutor: A common mistake is can to play. Say can play.
Tutor: Remember, can is the same for I, you, he, she, we, and they.
