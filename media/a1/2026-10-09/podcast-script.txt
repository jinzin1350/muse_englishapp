Maya: I love your city too. But I grew up in a village. A village is a very small town. Few people live in a village. My village is quiet. There are no big shops in my village. There are trees in my village. I love my village.
Lena: Your village sounds nice! In my city, I go to the library. A library is a place with many books. I read at the library. I love the library. The library is quiet too. The library in my city is very big. It has many, many books.
Maya: My village is too small for a library. But my school has books! A school is a place where children learn. I went to the village school. The school was small, but the teachers were great. I loved my school.
Lena: In the city, my school is big! My school has one thousand children. One thousand children learn at my school. That is a very big school! After school, I walk to the library. I read at the library every day.
Maya: Wow! That is a big school. When my family visits the city, we sleep in a hotel. A hotel is a place where you sleep on a trip. Our hotel is small. The hotel has a nice bed. We love the hotel.
Lena: I know that hotel! When your family comes to my city, they sleep in the hotel near my home. The hotel is close to the library. It is close to my school too.
Maya: Yes! And from the hotel, we walk in the city. The city is big, but the hotel is quiet. The hotel is nice.
Lena: My school is close to the library. I go to the library after school. The library is my favorite place in the city. I read at the library with my friends.
Maya: I want to see your big school! And I want to see your library. My village has no library, so I will take books from your library to my village school.
