Sara lives in a small village.
Her village is quiet and green.
Today Sara goes to the city.
The city is big and loud.
Sara visits a school in the city.
Many children learn at the school.
The school is big and new.
Sara likes the school.
The school is next to a library.
Sara loves the library.
She reads books at the library.
The library has many books.
In the evening, Sara goes to a hotel.
The hotel is small and clean.
Sara sleeps in the hotel.
She likes the hotel bed.
The next day, Sara goes back to her village.
The village is quiet again.
The city is fun, but the village is home.
Sara loves the city and the village.
