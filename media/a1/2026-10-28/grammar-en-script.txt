Tutor: Today we learn three small words: in, on, and at.
Tutor: These words tell us about time.
Tutor: Use at for a clock time.
Tutor: We say at seven o'clock.
Tutor: We say at night.
Tutor: Use on for a day.
Tutor: We say on Monday.
Tutor: We say on my birthday.
Tutor: Use in for a month or a year.
Tutor: I was born in July.
Tutor: My aunt was born in April.
Tutor: A common mistake is to say on seven o'clock.
Tutor: No, we say at seven o'clock.
Tutor: Remember: at for a time, on for a day, in for a month.
