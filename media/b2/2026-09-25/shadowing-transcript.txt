Ali loved to travel more than anything.
Last spring, he planned a trip to Istanbul.
Istanbul was his dream destination for many years.
He wrote a short itinerary with three days of fun.
On the first day, he wanted to see the old city.
He booked a cheap accommodation near the sea.
The bus fare to the airport was only $2.
He woke up early because he wanted to be punctual.
At the station he waited on Platform 3.
His train was full, but the seats were spacious.
He watched the scenic fields through the window.
At the airport there was a short delay.
The workers said the flight would leave one hour later.
Ali did not want to postpone his trip.
He drank tea and waited with the other people.
After the delay they could embark on the plane.
He had a long layover in another city.
He used the time to eat and rest.
When he landed he went through customs quickly.
The officer checked his baggage and smiled.
A taxi took him through the congested streets.
He used his phone to navigate to the hotel.
The room was clean, spacious, and quiet.
The next morning he visited a famous landmark.
The old building was tall and very beautiful.
From the top the view of the city was breathtaking.
He took many photos of the sea in the boats.
In the evening he walked by the water.
He thought about his daily commute back home.
On the last day he bought small gifts for his family.
His voyage home was calm and easy.
When he returned he told his friends everything.
He said it was the best trip of his life.
