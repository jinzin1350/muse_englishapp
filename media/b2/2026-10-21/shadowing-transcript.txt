Alex had one clear aim: to find work that truly mattered.
His aim was not money, but a career with real purpose.
With that aim in mind, he began to apply for jobs every morning.
He decided to apply for a position at a respected architecture studio.
To apply for the role, he wrote a careful letter about his dreams.
A famous architect led the studio, and Alex admired her buildings.
The architect invited Alex for an interview on a rainy Tuesday.
During the interview, the architect asked about his proudest achievement.
Alex described his greatest achievement: a school garden he had designed.
The friendly administrator welcomed him and showed him around the office.
The administrator explained the daily schedule and introduced the team.
On his first day, Alex received access to the studio's project files.
This access meant he could study every plan the team had created.
With full access to the library, his confidence grew quickly.
Even the administrator could not believe how fast he used that access.
His aim now was to learn something new every single week.
He asked the architect to be his mentor, and she agreed.
The architect taught him to sketch faster and think deeper.
Every month brought a new achievement, small but meaningful.
His biggest achievement that year was a park design for the city.
The administrator helped him organize the files for that project.
Thanks to the administrator, nothing important was ever lost.
Alex wanted to apply for a bigger role, so he prepared for months.
He chose to apply for the senior designer position in the spring.
When he got the promotion, his aim shifted to helping newcomers.
The studio chose Alex to be an ambassador for young architects.
As an ambassador, he visited schools and shared his story.
Being an ambassador taught him that teaching is also learning.
The ambassador program soon welcomed five new young designers.
The studio gave every intern full access to the same tools Alex had.
Each intern's first achievement was celebrated by the whole team.
Alex knew his aim had been reached, and a new aim was already forming.
He was proud to be an ambassador for the studio he loved.
