The carpenter arrived early with his business plan under his arm
The CEO read the business plan and smiled at the carpenter
The carpenter said he hoped to be promoted this year
He laid the first brick beside the new bathroom door
A sharp tool cut his finger, so he put a bandage on it
He washed the cut in the bathroom and changed the bandage
At lunch he ate hot soup from a large bowl
The nurse checked his blood pressure after lunch
His blood pressure was normal, so he returned to work
His old truck began to break down on the road home
He called the CEO when the truck started to break down
The CEO sent help because good workers should be promoted
The next day the carpenter laid another brick with care
He placed one brick, then another brick, in a straight line
In the bathroom he fitted a mirror and left a bowl of flowers on the shelf
He put a clean bowl on the table in the new kitchen
A second bowl held fruit for the workers at break time
He hurt his knee, so he tied a bandage around it
A fresh bandage kept his knee clean while he worked
The doctor checked his blood pressure again on Friday
High blood pressure runs in his family, so he walks daily
His car did not break down on Friday, and he was glad
A good business plan helped the carpenter win the job
The CEO praised the business plan in front of the team
At last the carpenter knew he would be promoted in June
His wife celebrated because he would finally be promoted
He cleaned the bathroom and polished every brick in the hall
The finished house had a bright bathroom and a strong brick wall
He ate a last bowl of soup and thanked the kind CEO
The happy carpenter locked the door and went home to rest
