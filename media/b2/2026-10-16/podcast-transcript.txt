Lena: And now, back to money talk. So far we've covered overdraft, cashback, and markup. Next comes the biggest money word in adult life — mortgage. A mortgage is a large loan from a bank that lets you buy a house, which you then pay back over many years, usually twenty or thirty.

Maya: Also learn the two main types. A fixed-rate mortgage keeps the same interest rate for the whole term — predictable and calm. A variable-rate mortgage can go up or down, which is cheaper at first but riskier later. Most first-time buyers sleep better with a fixed mortgage.
