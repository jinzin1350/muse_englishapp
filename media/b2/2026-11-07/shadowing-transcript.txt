Sara loves to travel, and last summer she planned a trip along the coast.
She wanted to visit an old castle, and she wanted to stay in a small family business.
The hostel was a small business, and her room had a bunk bed near the window.
Sara slept in the bunk bed on the top, and she could see the coast from the bunk bed.
Every morning she walked along the coast to the old castle on the hill.
The castle was strong and beautiful, and the castle looked over the whole coast.
Inside the castle, Sara learned the story of the castle and the kings who lived there.
After the castle, she found a small cafe, and the cafe was a family business too.
The owner of the business told her about his business and his dream to grow the business.
Sara loved the coffee, and she sat in the cafe and watched the waves on the coast.
On the third night, the room next to hers was noisy, and Sara could not sleep in her bunk bed.
In the morning, she went to the desk to complain about the noise.
She did not want to complain in an angry way, because she liked the family business.
The family listened, and they moved her to a quiet room, so she did not need to complain again.
A good business listens when a guest needs to complain, and this business listened.
Sara spent two more days on the coast, and she visited a second castle by the sea.
The second castle stood on a rock, and the wind from the coast was strong.
Sara took many photos of the castle, and she bought a book about the castle.
On the last day, she packed her bag, and she said goodbye to the family business.
At the airport, the crew on the plane smiled, and the crew helped her with her bag.
The crew were kind, and the whole crew worked hard to help every passenger.
Sara thanked the crew, and she told the crew that the flight was wonderful.
At home, Sara opened her suitcase, and she found damage on one wheel.
The damage was not terrible, but the damage made her sad, because the suitcase was new.
She wrote to the airline to complain about the damage, and she sent photos of the damage.
The airline said sorry for the damage, and they paid to repair the damage.
Sara was happy, because it is good to complain politely when there is damage.
Now Sara is planning a new trip along a different coast, to a new castle.
She will stay in a small business again, and she will sleep in a bunk bed again.
She will thank the crew, and she will enjoy the coast, the castle, and the sea.
