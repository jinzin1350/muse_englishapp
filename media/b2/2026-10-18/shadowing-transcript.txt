The Adventurous Road Trip
Last summer, my friend Sara planned an adventurous road trip.
She wanted to be adventurous and explore the mountains by car.
On the first day, she had a small accident on a narrow road.
The accident was not serious, and nobody was hurt in the accident.
After the accident, she drove more carefully for the rest of the trip.
That evening, she stayed in a small apartment by a quiet lake.
The apartment had a kitchen, so she cooked dinner in the apartment.
She loved the apartment because it felt like a real home.
The next morning, she left the apartment and continued her journey.
As she started to approach the coast, she could see the sea.
The road was beautiful as she continued to approach the ocean.
Dark clouds began to approach from the west, and it started to rain.
At the cafe, she met a couple who told her about a lovely B&B nearby.
She drove to the B&B and the family welcomed her warmly.
The B&B had a beautiful garden with colorful flowers.
Breakfast at the B&B was delicious, with fresh bread and honey.
She thanked the owners of the B&B and promised to return one day.
Before the road trip, she had flown to the city and felt airsick on the plane.
The flight was long and bumpy, and she felt airsick for two hours.
A flight attendant gave her medicine for airsickness.
The medicine helped her airsickness, and she felt better soon.
Now she always prepares for airsickness before she flies, so she never feels airsick again.
On the last day, she felt truly adventurous and happy.
She was proud of her adventurous spirit and her adventurous choices.
She told her friends that the small accident taught her to slow down.
Every accident, she said, can become a funny story later.
She missed her little apartment by the lake and the kind family at the B&B.
As she started to approach her home city, she smiled.
The lights of the city seemed to approach slowly through the evening rain.
It was the most adventurous trip of her life, and she will never forget it.
She hates feeling airsick, so now she always chooses a seat near the wing to avoid airsickness.
