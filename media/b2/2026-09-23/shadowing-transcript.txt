Maya's Big Week

Maria woke up early on Monday with one clear goal in her mind.
She wanted a new job, so she opened her laptop and updated her resume.
Her old resume was messy, and she knew a clean resume makes a strong first impression.
She rewrote every line of her resume until her resume looked perfect.
On Tuesday, a big company called and invited her to a job interview.
Maria felt nervous before the interview, but she prepared well for the interview.
During the interview, she answered every question with a calm smile.
The interview ended with good news, and that successful interview changed her life.
She got the job, and two years later she earned a well-deserved promotion.
The promotion surprised her, because she had not expected such a fast promotion.
After the promotion, she moved into a bigger office with a window.
With the new position came a much better salary.
Her old salary had been low, but her new salary finally matched her skills.
She celebrated her higher salary with dinner and her family.
Her new colleague Daniel showed her around on the first day.
Daniel was a friendly colleague, and soon every colleague in the office knew her name.
She often had lunch with her favorite colleague and talked about their projects.
One Friday, her manager gave the team a very tight deadline.
The deadline was Monday morning, and nobody liked such a short deadline.
Maria stayed late to meet the deadline, and she finished just before the deadline.
To finish on time, she had to work overtime on Saturday.
She did not mind the overtime, because the project was important to her.
Daniel also worked overtime, and they ordered pizza during their long overtime evening.
A month later, Maria decided to negotiate a higher salary.
She was a little afraid to negotiate, but she practiced how to negotiate with a friend.
When she finally sat down to negotiate, she spoke clearly and with confidence.
Her manager listened, and because she dared to negotiate, she won a better deal.
Years passed quickly, and Maria became a respected leader in her company.
One day she started to think about the right age to retire.
She wanted to retire at sixty and spend more time with her grandchildren.
She saved money every month so that when she decides to retire, she can travel the world.
After she retires, she does not want to sit at home all day.
She will retire from meetings and deadlines, but not from interesting work.
She plans to take small freelance projects just for fun.
Freelance work will keep her busy, and she already has two freelance clients waiting.
She smiles when she thinks about her freelance future after a long career.
Maria learned that a good resume, a strong interview, and an honest colleague can open many doors.
