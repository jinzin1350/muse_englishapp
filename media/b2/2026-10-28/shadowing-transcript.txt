Nina is an ambitious young manager at a busy shop.
She is able to speak three languages, and she is able to help every customer with a smile.
At her age, many people think she is too young for such a big job, but her age does not stop her.
One Monday morning, a supplier gave her an abrupt phone call about a late delivery.
The abrupt news made her feel a sudden flash of anger.
She felt anger rising in her chest, but she refused to answer in an aggressive way.
An aggressive reply would only make the problem worse, she thought.
She took a slow breath and said, I am able to solve this calmly.
Nina is ambitious, but she knows that ambition needs patience too.
She called the supplier back and spoke in a warm and steady voice.
She said, I understand the problem, and I am absolutely sure we can find a solution.
Absolutely, the supplier said, let us fix this together.
The supplier apologized for the abrupt call and promised a new delivery time.
Nina smiled and thanked him, and her anger melted away completely.
Later that day, an ambitious student visited the shop and asked Nina for advice.
The student said, I am ambitious, but I worry that my age will hold me back.
Nina laughed kindly and said, Your age is not a wall, it is only a number.
She told him, If you are able to work hard and stay patient, you are able to reach your dreams.
The student asked, What if I feel anger when things go wrong?
Nina answered, Anger is normal, but an aggressive answer never helps anyone.
She added, An abrupt email or an abrupt word can break trust in one second.
The student nodded and said, Absolutely, I will remember that.
Nina felt proud, because she was able to help someone younger than herself.
That evening, her team faced another abrupt change in the work plan.
Some workers felt anger, and one worker spoke in an aggressive tone.
Nina stayed calm and said, I understand your anger, and I hear you absolutely.
She reminded them, We are ambitious people, and we are able to finish this together.
Her calm words worked, and the team returned to work with fresh energy.
At the end of the day, Nina wrote in her notebook, Age teaches patience, and patience beats anger.
She closed the notebook and thought, Absolutely, tomorrow will be a better day.
She never let an aggressive moment define her day.
