Nina packed her old camcorder for a film about the town.
She pressed the button, but the camcorder stayed silent.
Her brother fixed the button with a tiny screwdriver.
Then he connected a long cable to the television.
The cable carried the picture from the camcorder to the screen.
At the museum, Nina filmed a dark prison cell.
The cell had a heavy door and one small window.
A guide explained that one prisoner slept in each cell.
Nina promised that the guide would back up her facts.
Her friends also agreed to back up her story about the cell.
In the science room, a teacher showed a safe chemical.
The chemical turned the clear water bright green.
She warned the children never to touch a strong chemical at home.
Every chemical bottle in the room had a clear label.
On the desk, a small sign showed the letters ard.
The teacher said an ard is a short technical term in the system.
If the ard fails, the machine adds too much chemical.
Nina filmed the ard sign with her camcorder.
Later, the camcorder battery died because the charger cable was lost.
A kind guard lent Nina a cable from his office.
While they waited, he told them stories about the old cell.
Nina sewed a loose button back onto her coat.
The button on the camcorder bag was missing too.
Her brother promised to back up her film project at school.
His teacher said his class would back up the project as well.
At home, Nina plugged in the cable and pressed the button to play the film.
The whole family watched the cell, the chemical, and the ard on screen.
Nina smiled, because good friends always back up each other.
