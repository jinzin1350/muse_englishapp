Nora walked into the department store with auburn hair shining in the light.
The department store was beautiful in the morning sun.
She felt awkward when she dropped her bag near the door.
A kind worker smiled and the awkward moment passed quickly.
Bright windows in the department store attract many visitors every day.
Good music and friendly staff attract families to the shop.
Nora loved the colour of a warm red dress.
The colour of her scarf matched her auburn hair perfectly.
She checked her current account before she decided to buy anything.
Her current account showed she could afford one beautiful dress.
The beautiful dress made her smile in the mirror.
Outside the shop, the evening sky looked beautiful too.
An old man with a long beard waited near the counter.
His beard was grey, but his eyes were kind and bright.
He said his beard kept him warm in winter.
Nora still felt a little awkward when she paid.
She used money from her current account and felt calm again.
On the wall, a photo showed the town during a civil war.
The teacher explained how the civil war changed many families.
No one in the town wanted another civil war ever again.
A nurse talked about how cholera once spread through dirty water.
Doctors worked hard so cholera would not return to the town.
Clean water stopped cholera and kept children safe.
The shop windows attract children with toys and colour.
Every colour in the window was bright and cheerful.
Nora touched her auburn hair and laughed at her awkward start.
The man with the beard waved goodbye with a warm smile.
She left the department store carrying one beautiful bag.
Her awkward morning had become a happy and beautiful day.
She promised to check her current account and visit again soon.
