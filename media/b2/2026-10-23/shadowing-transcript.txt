The Bungalow by the Sea

Last spring, Daniel decided to apply for a job in a small coastal town.
He wanted to apply for the position because the company offered a house with it.
His friends said it was a brave choice to apply for work so far from home.
The company administrator called him the next morning with good news.
The administrator explained that the job came with a lovely bungalow near the beach.
Daniel had never lived in a bungalow before, but the idea felt exciting.
The bungalow stood on a quiet street, only five minutes from the beach.
On his first day, the administrator showed him around the office.
The friendly administrator gave him the keys and a warm welcome.
Daniel's first choice was to unpack the kitchen before anything else.
He made a careful choice about where to put every box.
It was a difficult choice, but he finished the kitchen by noon.
In the kitchen drawer he found an old chopping board.
He cleaned the wooden chopping board and placed it by the stove.
Every good cook needs a strong chopping board, he thought with a smile.
In the bedroom closet, there was not a single coat hanger.
He bought ten new coat hangers from the little shop downtown.
He hung his jackets carefully, one on each coat hanger.
The last coat hanger held his favourite blue jacket.
That weekend, Daniel planned a small adventure holiday along the coast.
An adventure holiday was exactly what he needed after the long move.
He invited two friends to join his adventure holiday by the sea.
They drove down a bumpy road toward a hidden beach.
The bumpy road made them laugh all the way there.
After the bumpy ride, the view was worth every kilometre.
They spread their towels on the warm sand of the quiet beach.
The beach was empty except for a few seagulls.
Daniel had dreamed of this beach for months.
Swimming in the clear water felt exhilarating.
The whole day was exhilarating from morning to night.
He knew he would never forget that exhilarating weekend.
