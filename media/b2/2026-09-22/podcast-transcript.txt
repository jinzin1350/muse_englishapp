Lena: Word seven is surcharge. A surcharge is an extra charge added to the normal price.
Maya: Like at the airport, where the airline adds a fuel surcharge to every ticket.
Lena: Exactly. And some restaurants add a surcharge on holidays, so the menu price is not the final price.
Maya: That is annoying. A surcharge should be clear before you pay, not a surprise at the end.
Lena: I agree. I always ask if there is a surcharge before I order anything extra.
Maya: Once a taxi driver tried to add a night surcharge at noon, and I said no way.

Maya: Word eight: outlet. An outlet is a shop that sells famous brands at lower prices.

Maya: Word nine: retail. Retail is the sale of goods directly to customers in shops, the normal shop price.
Lena: So the retail price is what you see on the tag in the store.
Maya: Exactly. That jacket has a retail price of two hundred dollars, but I got it online for much less.
Lena: Retail shops have higher prices because they pay rent and staff. Online stores often skip those costs.
Maya: Still, I like to try clothes on in a retail store before I buy them.
Lena: Me too. The retail experience lets you touch the fabric and check the size.
Maya: And the last word for today is cashier. A cashier is the person who takes your money at the shop.
