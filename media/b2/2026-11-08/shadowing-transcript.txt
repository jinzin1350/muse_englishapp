Sara woke up early on a hot morning by the coast.
The sun was already boiling hot, and the sea shone bright blue along the coast.
Sara did not want to complain about the heat, so she smiled and packed her bag.
She walked to the old park, where a great tree stood beside the coast road.
A thick bough hung low over the path, and Sara sat in its shade.
On the bough, she saw a small green bud, and then another bud beside it.
Every bud on the tree promised a flower, and Sara felt happy.
The park crew was working near the tree, watering the flowers.
The crew worked hard in the boiling sun, but no one in the crew stopped to complain.
Sara watched the crew with a good attitude, and she thanked them for their work.
A good attitude makes hard work easier, and the crew smiled back at Sara.
Suddenly, a fire broke out in a dry bin near the gate.
A fire can break out so fast, and Sara felt afraid for a moment.
The crew ran to the bin, and they stopped the fire before it could break out again.
A fire can break out anywhere, so the crew checks every bin in the park each morning.
Sara was glad the fire did not appal the children in the park.
Cruel news can appal anyone, but kind help can calm every fear.
It would appal Sara to see the old tree hurt, so she was glad the tree was safe.
Sara walked back to the coast, and the wind felt cool on her face.
At the hotel, a guest began to complain loudly about his cold food.
The guest did not complain politely; he shouted, and his attitude was rude.
The manager stayed calm, and with an assertive voice she answered him.
To be assertive is to speak clearly and kindly, and the manager was assertive and fair.
An assertive attitude can solve a problem, but an angry attitude only makes it worse.
The guest listened, and slowly his attitude changed.
He stopped trying to complain, and he thanked the crew for their help.
Sara ate her lunch under the big bough, and she watched a bud open in the sun.
The bud opened slowly, and the flower inside was soft and pink.
The day was still boiling, but Sara did not complain once.
She walked home along the coast, and she thought about the brave crew.
A good attitude, an assertive voice, and a kind heart can change any boiling day into a good day.
