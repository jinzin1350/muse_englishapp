A New Beginning at Work

1. Sara finished university and wanted to start her career.
2. She applied for an internship at a busy marketing company.
3. The internship lasted three months, and she learned a lot.
4. Her manager was kind, and the internship taught her real skills.
5. She was grateful that the internship opened the door to a real job.
6. Every new worker started with a six-month probation.
7. During her probation, Sara arrived early every day.
8. Her boss watched her work closely during the probation period.
9. She passed her probation because she never stopped learning.
10. At the end of the year, she received a small bonus.
11. She saved her bonus for a future trip abroad.
12. She knew that a bonus was nice, but growth was better.
13. Some workers spend their bonus fast, but Sara was careful with money.
14. Her career was moving in the right direction.
15. Sara started networking at evening events in the city.
16. Through networking, she met people from many different companies.
17. One night, networking helped her meet an entrepreneur named Alex.
18. She learned that networking was about giving, not just taking.
19. The entrepreneur was building a small food delivery app.
20. Alex, the entrepreneur, needed help with marketing.
21. She liked the entrepreneur's energy and honest way of working.
22. The hiring process at the new startup was very fast.
23. Hiring at a startup is different from hiring at a big company, and Alex said hiring the right people was his biggest challenge.
24. This new job was the biggest step in her career so far.
25. As an applicant, she prepared carefully for every interview.
26. She was the best applicant among fifty people.
27. Every applicant took a short test, but Sara scored highest.
28. An applicant usually waits weeks, but she heard back in two days.
29. Her friends were surprised when she decided to resign.
30. To resign politely, she wrote a kind letter to her old boss.
31. Her boss was sad when she chose to resign, but he understood.
32. She gave two weeks of notice when she chose to resign, which is the polite custom.
33. A year later, her old company announced a big layoff.
34. The layoff was not her fault, and she sent kind messages to friends hurt by the layoff.
35. Today, Sara loves her career and her new team, and she is grateful she left before the layoff.
