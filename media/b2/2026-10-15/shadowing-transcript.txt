The Busy Intern

Last summer, my friend started an internship at a busy company.
The internship taught her how a real office works.
Her manager said the internship would last three months.
Every Friday, she joined the team for networking.
Networking helped her meet people from every department.
She loved networking because it made the big company feel small.
During probation, she worked very hard every day.
The probation period ended with a great review.
After probation, her boss praised her work.
The company gave every worker a small bonus.
Her bonus was a thank-you for her hard work.
She saved the bonus for a new phone.
One worker decided to resign last month.
To resign, he wrote a polite letter to his boss.
He chose to resign and start his own business.
In meetings, she learned to mute her microphone.
Please mute the chat when you are not speaking.
She forgot to mute once, and everyone laughed.
Her inbox filled with spam every morning.
Spam messages waste time and hide real emails.
She deleted the spam and kept the important mail.
An influencer visited the office for a product launch.
The influencer took photos with the whole team.
A smart influencer asks questions and listens well.
For the launch party, she wore a silver accessory.
The accessory was a gift from her sister.
One small accessory can change a whole outfit.
The party served cookies that were soft and chewy.
The chewy cookies were the best part of the evening.
She ate two chewy cookies and smiled all night.
