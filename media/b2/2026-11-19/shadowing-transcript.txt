Sara had beautiful auburn hair that shone in the morning sun.
Her warm smile could attract people from across the room.
She was affectionate with her little brother and hugged him every day.
Her grandfather was bald, but he had a long grey beard.
Sara felt awkward on her first day at the new school.
A kind teacher with a short beard made her feel welcome.
The beautiful garden behind the school began to attract butterflies.
Sara's auburn hair moved softly in the wind as she walked.
She was not awkward for long because the other children were friendly.
An affectionate cat followed Sara home from school.
The cat had beautiful eyes and soft auburn fur.
Sara's bald grandfather smiled when the cat rubbed against his beard.
At first the cat felt awkward in the new house.
But Sara was so affectionate that the cat soon felt safe.
Beautiful music from the radio began to attract the whole family.
They sat together and listened, and nobody felt awkward anymore.
Grandfather said his beard was auburn when he was young.
He was not bald then, and he had thick, beautiful hair.
Sara loved his stories and gave him an affectionate hug.
The beautiful painting in the window could attract many visitors.
Sara stood in the crowd and did not feel awkward at all.
A man with a black beard took a photo of the beautiful painting.
Sara noticed his auburn coat and his kind, affectionate smile.
On the way home, Sara saw a bald man walking an affectionate dog.
The dog was beautiful, with soft auburn spots on its white fur.
Sara waved, and the bald man smiled through his grey beard.
That evening, Sara wrote in her diary that kind people attract kind people.
She felt beautiful inside, calm and happy, and not awkward at all.
