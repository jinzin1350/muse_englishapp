Mute is a small button with a very big job.
She quickly pressed mute and felt embarrassed.
From now on, she will always check the mute button first.

Her friend added a funny hashtag to the post.
The hashtag Morning Laughs fit the video perfectly.
A good hashtag can help a post go viral and travel far.
She always adds a hashtag to her own posts now.

This podcast teaches one English word every day.
She loves that podcast because the host speaks slowly and clearly.

One quick reboot fixed everything.
Reboot is the oldest trick in tech support.

A strong firewall keeps hackers away from your files.
Without a firewall, her photos and passwords would be unsafe.
She was glad the firewall was working silently in the background.
