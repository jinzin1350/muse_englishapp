# Muse English 2026-10-12 (C1) — Topics Summary
"Nature and Environment: The Hidden Systems of the Natural World" — Maya and Lena explore ten advanced C1 nature-and-environment words through a flowing conversation.

## Subjects covered
- Coral reefs as a fragile ecosystem: how ecosystems connect living things and their environment
- Polar bear habitat loss from melting sea ice; protecting habitats
- Carbon emissions, record global emissions, net-zero emissions goals, cutting emissions through transport
- Conservation projects in Kenya: elephants, modern scientific conservation, conservation success stories
- Soil and coastal erosion: cliffs, farmland topsoil, wildfire aftermath, fighting erosion
- A weekend field-trip idea: visiting a wetland reserve to see the vocabulary in real life
- Permafrost thaw in the far north, methane release, the vicious circle of warming
- Desertification of farmland, green belts of trees reversing it
- Carbon sequestration: forests as natural sequestration machines, engineered sequestration projects
- Anthropogenic climate change: the human fingerprint on warming
- Regenerative farming and ocean projects: healing nature while growing
- Two app promo segments (fresh copy): daily words with photos/pronunciation, podcast, shadowing stories, quizzes, grammar, leaderboard points

## Vocabulary taught (each used 8+ times in context)
ecosystem, habitat, emission, conservation, erosion, permafrost, desertification, sequestration, anthropogenic, regenerative
