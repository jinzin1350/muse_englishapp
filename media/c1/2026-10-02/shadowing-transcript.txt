Shadowing: Sara's Team

1. When Sara became team leader, the office grew tense.
2. Her manager was ambivalent about her style, praising her results yet questioning her methods.
3. At first the team seemed ambivalent too, caught between respect and doubt.
4. Old habits made her mercurial in meetings, warm one minute and sharp the next.
5. Her mercurial moods confused the new interns, who never knew what to expect.
6. Yet the clients loved her effusive greetings at every event.
7. She offered effusive praise to the designers after their presentation succeeded.
8. Unlike her, the taciturn accountant seldom spoke unless asked.
9. People learned to read the taciturn accountant's quiet nods and small smiles.
10. After a harsh email, Sara sent a contrite message to the whole team.
11. Her contrite tone surprised everyone and softened the tension in the room.
12. The dispute with finance had turned acrimonious over the budget cuts.
13. She refused to let the acrimonious emails destroy years of good work.
14. By spring the sales team looked ebullient about the new quarter.
15. Their ebullient mood spread through the office like morning light.
16. The director remained aloof during the celebrations, watching from his office.
17. Some said his aloof manner hid a fear of looking foolish in public.
18. Then Sara made a magnanimous offer to share credit with finance.
19. Her magnanimous gesture ended the argument within a single afternoon.
20. Not everyone was pleased; one critic kept a supercilious attitude toward her plan.
21. She ignored his supercilious comments and invited him to join the project.
22. Slowly the team's feelings shifted, and the ambivalent manager became a true supporter.
23. Her once mercurial temper steadied into calm, reliable leadership.
24. Visitors often remarked on her effusive welcome at the front desk.
25. Even the taciturn accountant began sharing ideas at Friday meetings.
26. When she missed a deadline, her contrite apology won the client back.
27. The once acrimonious rivalry with finance became a friendly partnership.
28. On the project's final day, the ebullient team raised their glasses together.
29. The formerly aloof director finally walked down to shake her hand.
30. In her speech she thanked everyone with a magnanimous heart and no hint of a supercilious word.
31. Her mentor praised the once ambivalent team for trusting her, and the mercurial storms were long forgotten.
32. That night she sent effusive thanks to the taciturn accountant, whose silent work had saved the quarter.
33. Even the rival who once wrote acrimonious notes sent a contrite congratulations.
34. The ebullient party continued, and nobody seemed aloof any more.
35. It ended as a magnanimous celebration, free of any supercilious tone.
