Maya woke up early in the busy mega-city.
She opened her laptop computer and checked the news.
The news said the global village was awake and talking.
Outside her window, gridlock filled every street.
She put on her hands-free headset and called her friend.
Her friend worked on the human genome project.
They talked about interplanetary travel and future missions.
Maya said the mega-city felt small in the global village.
She worked on her laptop computer while the gridlock grew worse.
Her hands-free system let her type and talk at once.
The human genome data arrived on her laptop computer.
She dreamed that interplanetary travel would one day be normal.
In a mega-city, a single accident can cause total gridlock.
The global village shared the traffic news in seconds.
Maya used a hands-free call to warn her team about gridlock.
Scientists who study the human genome help doctors predict disease.
A new rocket made interplanetary travel feel closer than ever.
Maya carried her laptop computer to a quiet cafe.
From the cafe, the mega-city looked calm and bright.
She joined a video call with the whole global village team.
Her hands-free headset kept her hands on her laptop computer.
The team discussed the human genome and interplanetary travel.
Someone joked that gridlock on Earth was worse than travel in space.
Maya laughed and said interplanetary travel had no gridlock at all.
In the evening, the mega-city lights came on one by one.
Maya closed her laptop computer and walked home slowly.
She loved how the global village connected her mega-city to the world.
At home, she read about the human genome before dinner.
She made one last hands-free call to say good night.
Tomorrow, the mega-city, the global village, and her dreams of interplanetary travel would meet again.
