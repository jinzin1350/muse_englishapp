Sara started her first real job in the spring.
She wanted autonomy, but she knew she was still learning.
On her first day, every stakeholder in the project was introduced to her.
Her manager asked each stakeholder to share one piece of advice.
Sara felt lucky, because the company valued mentorship.
Her mentor, Daniel, offered her weekly mentorship through short coffee chats.
Good mentorship, he said, means asking questions, not just giving answers.
Sara asked how she could earn more autonomy in her role.
Daniel smiled and said autonomy grows when trust grows.
To build trust, she decided to upskill in the evenings.
She chose an online course to upskill, and her weekly upskilling routine began.
Colleagues noticed her effort to upskill, and her work improved fast.
Soon her manager gave her a small task for delegation practice.
He said delegation is how leaders multiply their impact.
Sara learned that delegation also builds the whole team.
She practiced delegation by sharing small research tasks with interns.
Her careful delegation earned her the respect of her team.
At lunch, Daniel explained why networking matters so much.
He told her that networking is simply helping people before you need help.
Sara began networking at Friday team events.
One honest conversation through networking brought her a new project.
Her natural networking impressed even her manager.
After six months, her manager listed every stakeholder she had impressed.
Each stakeholder praised her growth, and every stakeholder noticed her calm attitude.
Daniel suggested she join the company's formal mentorship program.
With formal mentorship, Sara could guide new hires herself one day.
Her new autonomy let her plan her own projects.
She protected that autonomy by always meeting her deadlines.
The company even paid for a course so she could upskill further.
Her improved skills made delegation smoother and faster.
When a senior colleague took a sabbatical, Sara covered her work.
A sabbatical, Daniel explained, is a long break to rest and recharge.
The colleague returned from her sabbatical full of fresh ideas.
Sara dreamed of earning her own sabbatical one day.
She never forgot the mentorship that started it all, and she saved her travel plans for a future sabbatical.
