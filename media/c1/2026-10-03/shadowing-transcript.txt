A good regimen mixes brutal hard days with gentle recovery days.

Endurance fades quickly when fortitude runs out.

Chase the daily work, not only the exhilaration, and your body will recuperate properly.

Skip real rest, and you never fully recuperate.
Even the underdog of the tournament must recuperate between brutal matches.

Talent opens the door, but grit walks through it.
