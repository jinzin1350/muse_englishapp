In the small town of Riverbend, the shops were quiet and the streets felt empty.
Reporters said Riverbend had an ailing economy, and many families, some already in abject poverty, were worried about jobs.
For years, some families in Riverbend had lived in abject poverty, with very little money for food or heating.
The new mayor, Ana, promised to boost the economy and bring hope back to the town.
To boost the economy, Ana first listened to shop owners, teachers, and young parents.
Ana visited families living in abject poverty and promised them real help, not empty words.
Her plan was to achieve economic growth that would help every family, not only the rich.
If the town could achieve economic growth, new jobs would open in shops, workshops, and schools.
First, the council decided to allocate funds for a new market hall in the centre of town.
To allocate funds wisely, Ana asked local people to vote on the most urgent projects.
The council chose to allocate funds for road repairs, a library, and training courses for young workers.
Another part of the plan was to alleviate debt for small businesses that were struggling to survive.
A small bakery could alleviate debt with a lower loan payment, and then hire one more baker.
When a town can alleviate debt for its shops, those shops can stay open and keep their workers.
Ana also warned everyone to check the APR before signing any loan agreement.
A high APR can turn a small loan into a heavy burden, so always ask about the APR first.
The APR tells you the true yearly cost of borrowing, and a fair APR protects families from surprise charges.
Week by week, the market hall filled with stalls, and the ailing economy began to recover from the ground up.
New stalls meant new jobs, and new jobs helped the town achieve economic growth step by step.
As jobs returned, fewer families lived in abject poverty, and children had warmer homes and fuller lunchboxes.
The ailing economy was slowly getting stronger, and people started to smile again in the streets.
But Ana knew that an ailing economy needs steady care, not just one good month, if the town wants to boost the economy for good.
So the council continued to allocate funds for training, so young people could learn new skills.
With fresh skills, young workers found better jobs, and that helped the town boost the economy and achieve economic growth even more.
Local banks also agreed to alleviate debt for families who had fallen behind on their payments.
To alleviate debt in a fair way, the banks lowered some interest rates and stretched payments over more years.
Ana reminded the town council that the APR on every new community loan must stay low and clear.
When people understand the APR, they borrow with confidence instead of fear.
By the end of the year, Riverbend could finally say it had begun to achieve economic growth, thanks to careful plans to allocate funds.
The town still had problems, but no family should live in abject poverty while neighbours have more than enough.
Riverbend showed that a caring plan can rescue an ailing economy, boost the economy, and give every family a fair chance.
