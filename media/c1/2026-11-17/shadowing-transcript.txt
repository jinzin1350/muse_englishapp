Nadia wanted a career that helped people and paid a fair wage.
Her career prospects looked weak in her small home town.
Every morning, she had to carry out a long list of dull office tasks.
The work was challenging, but Nadia refused to give up on her career.
At a community centre, she learned how child labour destroys childhoods.
She decided that fighting child labour would give her career real purpose.
A local charity offered her a job built on collaboration with schools.
Through collaboration with teachers, she taught children about child labour.
The charity asked her to carry out a survey of workshops and factories.
It was challenging to carry out the survey without any funding.
Her career prospects improved when a newspaper praised the survey.
Years earlier, a sales job had taught her cold calling in a noisy office.
She hated cold calling, but cold calling had made her brave on the phone.
Now she used cold calling to ask factory owners about child labour.
Some owners found her questions challenging and refused to answer.
In collaboration with a lawyer, she wrote a report on child labour.
The team had to carry out interviews in five different towns.
That challenging journey in winter weather tested the whole team.
Good collaboration helped them carry out every interview on time.
A big company read the report and offered Nadia a better career.
The new job promised excellent career prospects and a higher salary.
Her first task was to carry out an audit of the company factories.
She checked that no factory used child labour in its supply chain.
She also ended cold calling in the sales team and trained staff in collaboration.
The director called the audit challenging, yet Nadia stayed calm and polite.
Within a year, her career prospects were stronger than ever before.
Young workers now ask Nadia how to build an honest career with strong career prospects.
She tells them that collaboration, courage, and challenging work shape a career.
