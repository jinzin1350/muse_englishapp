The Calm Office
Elena was the most phlegmatic person in the whole company.
When the servers crashed on Monday morning, her phlegmatic calm amazed everyone.
She gave phlegmatic instructions while others shouted.
Even her phlegmatic boss looked nervous that day.
His phlegmatic silence confused the angry clients.
Across the hall, the new intern was imperturbable in a different way.
Nothing could rattle her imperturbable focus.
The imperturbable receptionist handled three crises before lunch.
He maintained an imperturbable expression during the argument.
But the intern's real gift was being unflappable.
An unflappable leader, she guided the team through the outage.
Staying unflappable takes practice, she always said.
Her unflappable voice on the phone reassured the customers.
To be unflappable in chaos is a rare skill.
Not everyone in the office shared her grace.
Their rival, Marcus, was known to be vindictive.
He sent a vindictive message to the team after losing the account.
A vindictive attitude poisons every meeting it touches.
She refused to answer his vindictive email.
The most vindictive people are often the most unhappy.
Marcus felt a secret schadenfreude when Elena's project slipped.
His schadenfreude was obvious to everyone in the room.
Schadenfreude is a poor substitute for real success.
She caught his schadenfreude and simply smiled.
Even a flash of schadenfreude can damage a friendship.
In the corner office sat Daniel, the office dreamer.
His quixotic plan to redesign the entire product thrilled the interns.
It was a quixotic dream, but a beautiful one.
Investors called his ideas quixotic and walked away.
Yet every breakthrough begins as a quixotic hope.
Without support, Daniel's project began to languish.
Talented people languish when nobody believes in them.
She refused to let her own career languish.
A neglected garden will languish; so will a neglected dream.
Do not let your ambitions languish in silence.
