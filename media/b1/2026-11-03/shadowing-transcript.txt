Sara loves shopping, but she always shops in a smart way.
Last Saturday, a large number of shops in her town had a big sale.
A large amount of people went to the sale early in the morning.
Sara wanted a new coat, so she read a review of the coat online first.
The review said the coat was warm, and another review said the size was true to the chart.
Sara checked the size chart twice because she wanted the right size.
She did not want the wrong size, so she measured her old coat at home.
At the shop, she had to push the heavy door to get inside.
Inside, she had to push her cart through a large number of busy aisles.
She had to push the cart slowly because a large amount of shoppers were in the way.
A shop assistant offered to push the cart for her, but Sara liked to push it herself.
A bright sale sign tried to make her buy three coats, but she said no.
Loud music tried to make her spend more money, but she remembered her plan.
Sara is saving up for a trip, so she saves up a little money every week.
She wants to save up enough money before the summer, so she does not waste money on sales.
In the shop, she found the coat in the right size, and she was very happy.
The size was perfect, and the price was fair, so she bought only the coat.
At home, Sara looked at her closet and saw a large number of old clothes.
A large amount of those clothes were old, torn, or too small.
She decided to throw away the clothes she never wears.
She did not throw away everything, but she threw away the broken shirts and old shoes.
When you throw away old things, your home feels clean and calm.
Sara gave the good clothes to a charity shop and threw away only the broken ones.
Now her closet is tidy, and she can see every size and every color clearly.
Sara wrote a review of her new coat to help other shoppers.
In her review, she wrote about the warm fabric and the true size.
A good review can make a shopper choose well, and it can make a shop keep a customer.
Sara's friends read her review, and the review made them visit the same shop.
Now Sara saves up for her trip, shops with a list, and never buys a large number of things she does not need.
She knows that a sale can make you buy too much, but a smart shopper checks the size, reads a review, saves up for what really matters, and does not let sales push her to buy things she does not need.
