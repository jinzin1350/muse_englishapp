Tom and the Flu
Tom woke up ill with the flu.
The flu made his whole body feel painful.
His illness started with a sore and painful throat.
He drank a large quantity of warm water.
The doctor said rest would reduce his fever.
Tom felt too ill to go to work.
A bad illness can make simple tasks painful.
He took medicine to reduce the pain in his head.
His mother brought him a hot quantity of soup.
The flu kept Tom in bed for three days.
She told him to reduce his work and rest.
Tom was worried that obesity ran in his family.
His painful knees made walking difficult when he was ill.
The doctor said daily walks could reduce the risk of obesity.
He decided to reduce the quantity of sugar in his tea.
A healthy quantity of vegetables filled his plate every evening.
His illness taught him to listen to his body.
When the flu finally left, Tom felt strong again.
He promised to reduce fried food to once a week.
Obesity is an illness that grows slowly over many years.
Tom now checks the quantity of salt in his food.
A painful back showed him that rest matters.
He was ill less often after he changed his habits.
Good sleep can reduce stress and reduce tiredness.
Tom tells friends that the flu is not a small illness.
He says a painful illness changes how you see health.
Every day he eats a smaller quantity of sweet food.
His new habits help him fight obesity step by step.
Tom no longer feels ill every winter.
And he knows that small changes reduce the risk of obesity and illness.
