Who Are You? A Shadowing Story About Character

Hello! Here is today's shadowing story. It uses all 10 words from today's lesson. I will read each sentence two times. First, just listen. Then, speak along with me.
A generous friend is a treasure you keep for life.
My uncle is generous with his time and his money.
Being generous makes other people feel warm inside.
A generous and honest friend is hard to find.
Sometimes I feel jealous when my friends travel without me.
He was jealous of his brother's new car for weeks.
Don't be jealous of others; focus on your own path.
My roommate feels jealous and moody on bad days.
My little brother is stubborn about eating vegetables.
She is too stubborn to ask for help when she needs it.
Even a stubborn person can learn to listen.
Even a stubborn boy can feel anxious inside.
The weather here is moody, changing every hour.
My sister gets moody when she misses her sleep.
Teenagers can be moody, but their hearts are good.
I was anxious before my first day at the new school.
The dog looked anxious during the loud storm.
She felt anxious waiting for the test results.
The brave firefighter saved the cat from the tree.
It was brave of him to tell the truth at last.
Brave people feel fear but act anyway.
A brave and curious traveler explores the whole world.
A curious child asks questions all day long.
Stay curious, and the world will surprise you.
His curious mind loves books and museums.
An honest worker admits his mistakes quickly.
Just be honest with me; I can handle the truth.
Honest answers build trust between friends.
A patient teacher never shouts at students.
Be patient with yourself while you are learning.
It takes a patient heart to wait for good news.
A patient and optimistic nurse cares for every child.
She stays optimistic even when things go wrong.
An optimistic view of life brings more friends.
Try to stay optimistic when the road gets hard.
Great job! Practice this story until you can say every sentence without thinking. See you tomorrow!
