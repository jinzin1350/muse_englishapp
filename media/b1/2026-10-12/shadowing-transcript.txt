Last spring, my friend Sara and I planned an exciting road trip to the mountains.
We packed our bags and drove early in the morning to start our road trip adventure.
A road trip is always more fun with good music and good company.
On the second day, we stopped at a beautiful campsite near a lake.
At the campsite, we met a family who had traveled there by train.
They told us their campsite was the best place they had ever stayed.
From the mountains, we drove to the coast to embark on a small cruise ship.
We had to embark before noon, so we woke up very early that day.
It was my first time to embark on a ship, and I felt so excited.
During the cruise, we joined a morning excursion to an old fishing village.
The excursion guide showed us colorful houses and a busy market.
Our excursion ended with a delicious lunch by the sea.
The next excursion took us to a lighthouse on a high cliff.
When the ship returned, we had to disembark quickly because of the rain.
We waited on the deck until it was our turn to disembark.
After we disembark, we always count our bags to make sure nothing is missing.
It is never easy to disembark when you are having so much fun.
At the hotel, a friendly porter carried our heavy bags to our room.
The porter knew all the best restaurants in the town.
We asked the porter about a good excursion for the next day.
The porter smiled and gave us a useful map of the city.
On our flight home, we had a long stopover in a big city.
During the stopover, we drank coffee and watched the planes take off.
A stopover is a nice chance to stretch your legs and relax.
Our stopover felt short because we talked about our round-trip plans and laughed the whole time.
The flight was smooth, but there was a little turbulence before landing.
The pilot warned us about the turbulence, so we fastened our seat belts.
A little turbulence is normal, and there is no reason to be afraid.
When we finally landed, we felt terrible jet lag.
The jet lag was worse than the turbulence, to be honest.
We slept for ten hours, and the jet lag slowly went away.
I booked a round-trip ticket, so coming home was easy and cheap.
A round-trip ticket saves money when you travel far from home.
Next year, I want another road trip, another round-trip ticket, and another campsite under the stars.
Even with turbulence and jet lag, I will always love to disembark in a new place and embark on a new adventure.
