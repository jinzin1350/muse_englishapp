Sara and the Helpful Blogger
Sara loves to browse online shops in her browser every evening.
Her browser is fast, so she can browse for an hour without problems.
When she finds a gift she likes, she presses add to basket at once.
She checks the add to basket button before she adds anything else.
Her brother, a blogger, told her to add to basket only what she needs.
That blogger writes a blog about smart shopping every week.
His blog helps readers browse better and spend less.
Every blogger in his group shares one new blog post on Mondays.
Sara reads his blog in her browser before breakfast.
She opens a new browser tab when a blog post has many photos.
Before she pays, she likes to call her mother for advice.
Her mother always says to call her before a big purchase.
Sara will call the shop if the colour of a dress looks different online.
She had to call her bank last month because a payment failed.
When friends call Sara, she tells them about her favourite blog.
The blogger decided to call the shop and ask about delivery times.
After shopping, Sara remembers to back up her phone photos.
She knows she must back up every receipt from online shops.
Her computer asks her to back up her files every Friday.
If you back up your photos, you never lose your memories.
The blogger also writes about why we should back up our computers.
On Sundays, Sara and the blogger browse a travel blog together.
They browse slowly and add to basket a guidebook for their trip.
Their favourite blogger uses a simple browser that blocks noisy adverts.
Sara opened her browser, read a blog about cameras, and smiled.
She pressed add to basket for a small camera bag she loved.
Then she decided to call the blogger and thank him for his advice.
That night, she remembered to back up her new photos and close her browser.
