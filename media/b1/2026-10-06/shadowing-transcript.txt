A Week of Getting Well

1. Last Monday I woke up with a strange symptom in my throat.
2. The symptom worried me because I had an important meeting that day.
3. By evening, another symptom appeared, and I decided to rest.
4. A small symptom can become a big problem if you ignore it.
5. On Tuesday I visited my doctor, described each symptom, and waited for her diagnosis.
6. Her diagnosis was a light throat infection.
7. She explained that an infection means germs are fighting your body.
8. To beat the infection, the doctor can prescribe some medicine.
9. Doctors prescribe medicine to help you heal faster.
10. She would also prescribe rest and a warm home remedy for my throat.
11. A clear diagnosis makes the recovery faster and calmer.
12. My recovery began on Wednesday, and the worst symptom was already gone.
13. During recovery I ate nutritious soup and drank warm tea.
14. Rest is the best remedy when your body is tired.
15. A slow recovery is normal, so I stayed patient all week.
16. On Thursday I remembered my old allergy to spring pollen.
17. My allergy usually brings sneezing and red, itchy eyes.
18. Luckily my allergy was quiet this year because I stayed indoors.
19. A simple allergy pill helps, but my doctor's diagnosis for my throat was the real priority.
20. She decided to prescribe an allergy pill too, and hot lemon tea as a home remedy.
21. An allergy is annoying, yet mine is easy to control.
22. On Friday I started a new habit: a vitamin and a nutritious breakfast.
23. One vitamin a day is enough if you eat real food.
24. An orange is full of natural vitamin C, and a nutritious lunch keeps me strong.
25. My grandmother's best remedy was chicken soup, plus a daily vitamin in winter.
26. Now my goal is strong immunity for the cold months.
27. Good sleep and nutritious food build your immunity.
28. A morning walk and a vitamin also help your immunity.
29. Strong immunity means fewer sick days.
30. My immunity is my shield, and I take care of it every day.
31. I learned that one small infection can teach you a lot about health.
