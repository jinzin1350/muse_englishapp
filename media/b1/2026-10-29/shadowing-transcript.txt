Sara wants a new job, so she asks her friend Tom to advise her.
Tom is happy to advise Sara, because he knows a lot about jobs.
Tom says, I advise you to write a short CV first.
Sara writes her CV, and she puts her best skills on her CV.
Then Sara writes an application for a job in a big office.
She checks her application twice, because a good application is very important.
Sara sends her application on Monday, and she waits for good news.
On Tuesday, the office calls Sara, and they like her application a lot.
Sara is happy, but she is also a little nervous about her first day.
On her first day, Sara cannot find the bin in the new office.
She asks a kind woman, where is the bin for old paper?
The woman shows Sara the bin, and Sara says thank you with a big smile.
Sara works with a calculator all morning, and her calculator helps her a lot.
Her new calculator is fast, and Sara loves her new calculator.
At lunch, Sara meets a man who served in the army for many years.
The man says the army taught him to be calm, tidy, and on time.
He says the army also taught him to help new people, and Sara likes that idea.
Sara thinks the army sounds hard, but she likes the man's calm way of working.
After lunch, Sara's computer decides to break down, and Sara feels worried.
When a computer breaks down, work stops, and everyone waits.
But Tom calls Sara and tries to advise her again.
Tom says, I advise you to stay calm when things break down.
Sara stays calm, and soon the computer works again.
Sara learns that machines break down sometimes, and that is normal.
Sara tells Tom, my computer tried to break down today, but I stayed calm.
At the end of the day, Sara cleans her desk and empties the bin.
She puts her calculator in her bag, and she smiles at her new friends.
Sara also puts a small bin next to her desk, so her desk stays clean.
Sara goes home and tells Tom about her wonderful first day.
Tom says, I am happy that I could advise you, Sara.
Sara says, thank you, Tom, and I will advise other people one day too.
Sara updates her CV that evening, because her new job belongs on her CV.
She also keeps a copy of her application, because a good application is worth keeping.
Sara sleeps well, and she dreams about her new office, her new calculator, and her new life.
