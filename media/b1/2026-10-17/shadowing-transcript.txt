Daniel's New Job
Daniel started a new job at a big company last month.
On his first day, the training was long but useful.
The training taught him how to use the computers.
Every new employee gets one week of training.
Daniel is a hard-working employee.
His manager is happy with the new employee.
After the first month, Daniel wrote his first report.
The report was about the new training program.
His manager read the report and smiled.
A clear report is important in every job.
Daniel received his first paycheck last Friday.
His paycheck was bigger than he expected.
He saved half of his paycheck in the bank.
A paycheck feels like a reward for hard work.
One benefit of his job is free lunch.
Another benefit is three weeks of holiday.
Every employee loves the health benefit.
A good benefit can be better than more money.
Daniel dreams of a long career in this company.
He wants his career to grow step by step.
A strong career starts with small steps.
His sister works freelance from home.
She does freelance design for many clients.
Freelance work gives her a lot of freedom.
Sometimes freelance months are quiet, but she enjoys the freelance life.
Daniel asked his manager for more training.
The new training will help every employee.
His next report will be about the training results.
He wants his paycheck to grow with his career.
Daniel is happy with his benefit package and his career path.
