Omar's New Job

1. When Omar started his new job, his commute was very long.
2. His commute took almost an hour by bus every morning.
3. He talked to his colleague about the long commute.
4. His colleague told him the salary was worth the commute.
5. The salary was good, and there was a bonus at the end of the year.
6. A bonus made the long commute feel easier.
7. Omar worked the night shift for the first month.
8. The night shift was quiet, and his shift ended at six in the morning.
9. His colleague on the same shift helped him a lot.
10. Sometimes there was overtime before a big deadline.
11. Omar did not mind the overtime because the deadline was clear.
12. When the team met the deadline, everyone received a bonus.
13. A bonus was a nice surprise after a hard month of overtime.
14. After one year, Omar earned a promotion.
15. The promotion meant a higher salary and a better shift.
16. His colleague congratulated him on the promotion.
17. A promotion was exactly what Omar had wanted for years.
18. In his new job, Omar gave interviews to new workers.
19. He asked each person in the interview about their salary hopes.
20. An interview is also a chance to ask about the shift and the commute.
21. Omar always told people in the interview about possible overtime.
22. He explained every deadline clearly in the interview.
23. His new salary was higher, but the commute was still long.
24. Omar thought about a shorter commute every day.
25. Then his best colleague decided to resign.
26. To resign was a hard choice for his colleague.
27. His colleague wanted to resign because there was too much overtime.
28. Before he could resign, the company offered him a promotion.
29. The promotion came with a higher salary and less overtime.
30. He stayed, and Omar was happy his colleague did not resign.
31. A second bonus came in December, and Omar saved it.
