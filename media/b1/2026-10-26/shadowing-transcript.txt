Sara loves Saturday shopping.
On Saturday morning, Sara takes her big bag and goes to the market.
She puts her phone and her wallet in the bag.
At the market, Sara buys a new bag for her mother.
The new bag is red, strong, and very cheap.
Sara stops at a cashpoint near the market.
There is always a long line at the cashpoint on Saturday.
At the cashpoint, Sara takes out some cash for the small shops.
Sara is happy that the cashpoint is open on Saturdays.
Sara pays for most things with her credit card.
Her credit card is in her bag, next to her phone.
Sara is careful with her credit card and never spends too much.
Sara always checks her credit card bill at the end of the month.
Today Sara wants new furniture for her room.
She looks at chairs, tables, and other furniture in a big shop.
The furniture in the shop is beautiful but expensive.
Sara chooses a small table, and the table is the first item she buys.
Then she buys a lamp, and the lamp is the second item on her list.
The shop worker puts every item in a big bag for Sara.
Sara carries the bag to the door, but the bag is very heavy.
She walks too fast, trips, and falls over in the street.
When Sara falls over, her bag opens and every item falls out.
A kind man helps Sara when she falls over.
He says, "Be careful, or you will fall over again."
Sara does not want to fall over again, so she walks slowly.
Sara laughs and says, "Thank you. I will walk slowly with my bag."
Sara's brother calls her and talks about income tax.
He says income tax is money we pay to the government.
Sara thinks income tax is boring, but her brother says income tax is important.
Her brother says, "We pay income tax from our salary, and income tax pays for schools and roads."
Sara goes home with her bag, her table, and her lamp.
At home, she puts the new furniture in her room.
She puts every item in its place and smiles.
Sara loves her new furniture, her new bag, and her Saturday shopping.
