Shadowing: Mina's Big Week

Mina is a hardworking student at a busy high school.
She has an important exam on Monday morning.
The exam will cover the whole semester.
Mina wants a good grade on this exam.
A good grade will help her win a scholarship.
With a scholarship, she can study at a big university.
At the university, she will earn a degree in science.
Her dream is to graduate with top marks one day.
She must memorize many facts before the exam.
To memorize well, she reads her notes out loud.
Saying the words again and again helps her memorize the difficult terms.
Flashcards help her memorize the key ideas and earn a good grade.
Her teacher gave the class a long assignment.
The assignment is due on Friday afternoon.
Mina starts every assignment early and never late.
She asks her tutor for help with the assignment.
Her patient tutor explains everything slowly.
The tutor also helps her prepare for the exam.
On Tuesday, Mina attends a long lecture.
The lecture is about the history of science.
She takes careful notes during the whole lecture.
She enjoys the lecture because the teacher tells stories.
After the lecture, she reviews her notes twice.
To concentrate, she studies in a quiet library.
She turns off her phone so she can concentrate.
It is hard to concentrate in a noisy room.
They quiz each other before the exam so they can both graduate on time.
Short breaks help her concentrate for many hours.
Her friend Ali also wants a good grade.
Ali hopes to win a scholarship too.
He dreams of a degree from a famous school and hopes to graduate with honors.
One day they will both graduate together and celebrate.
Mina's tutor believes in her future degree.
A rested mind gets a better grade on any exam.
Next year she will graduate, earn her degree, thank her tutor, and use her scholarship with pride.
