Sara's Day of Feelings

Sara is a cheerful girl with a big smile.
Every morning she wakes up calm and ready for the day.
She is confident in her English class and answers every question.
Her little brother is shy and hides when guests come.
Sara speaks in a confident voice, and that helps her shy brother relax.
Her mother is proud of both children every single day, and Sara is proud of her mother too.
Sara feels grateful for her warm home and kind family.
At school, her best friend is sensitive and cries easily.
Sara stays calm with her sensitive friend and listens carefully.
She is never impatient with her little brother.
When she makes a mistake, she does not feel embarrassed for long.
Her teacher is cheerful and always tells funny stories.
The cheerful teacher makes every student feel confident.
One day, a new student came and sat alone in the corner.
He looked lonely, far from his old friends.
Sara walked to him with a calm and friendly smile.
She was not shy about saying hello first.
The boy was shy too, but he smiled back at her.
She told him she felt lonely on her first day as well, and she knew the lonely boy needed a friend.
Soon the lonely feeling was gone, and they laughed together.
She felt proud when the teacher asked her to help the new student.
At lunch, she spilled juice on her shirt and felt embarrassed.
Her new friend said, do not be embarrassed, it happens to everyone.
She stayed calm and smiled again, and soon the embarrassed feeling was gone.
A confident student never lets a small mistake stop them.
In the afternoon, the bus was late and the children got impatient.
Sara told them to stay calm because the bus was coming.
She was cheerful even while she waited in the hot sun.
He told the children that a calm mind is stronger than an impatient heart.
Sara felt proud of her new friend for speaking English well.
She knew sensitive hearts feel things deeply, so she spoke softly and kindly.
She never felt impatient, even in the long line at the shop.
She spoke gently to her sensitive friend every day.
She was grateful for her teacher's kind words.
A grateful heart is a happy heart.
That night she felt grateful for all the people in her life.
She fell asleep calm, cheerful, and ready for a new day.
