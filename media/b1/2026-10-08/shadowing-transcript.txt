The City I Love

Every morning I walk through my neighborhood and say hello to everyone I meet.
My neighborhood has small shops, quiet streets, and friendly people.
The oldest resident of our street tells wonderful stories about the old days.
As a new resident, I joined the local football team last spring.
Near the station, a tall new skyscraper shines in the morning sun.
From the top of that skyscraper, you can see the river and the hills.
I walk to work on the wide sidewalk along the main road.
The sidewalk near my home has trees that give shade in summer.
My aunt lives in a green suburb outside the city center, and every suburb near us has a train station.
In her suburb the houses have gardens and the streets are quiet.
The fountain in the central square is my favorite place to sit and read.
At noon the water of the fountain sparkles like glass in the sun.
The old bridge over the river is more than a hundred years old.
From the middle of the bridge you can watch the boats pass below.
Always stop at the crosswalk and look both ways before you cross.
The crosswalk near the school has bright yellow lines for safety.
On Saturdays we drink tea on the rooftop of our building and watch the sunset.
From our rooftop the whole city looks like a sea of lights.
I was late today because of a terrible traffic jam on the highway.
A traffic jam can turn a short drive into a very long one.
Our neighborhood festival is in June, and every family brings food.
Every resident of the building received a letter about the new park project.
A second skyscraper with shops and offices will open next year.
On a warm evening the sidewalk fills with people and music.
My cousin moved from a suburb to the city for his new job.
Children throw coins into the fountain and make wishes.
A new bridge for walkers and cyclists will open in the spring.
My little brother learned to use the crosswalk safely last week.
My dream is to build a small house with a rooftop for summer evenings.
To avoid the traffic jam, I now leave home thirty minutes earlier.
The whole neighborhood gathered around the fountain for the summer concert.
One friendly resident painted the bench near the old skyscraper.
I love riding my bike on the wide sidewalk across the new bridge.
We watched fireworks from the rooftop after the traffic jam finally cleared.
The suburb built a new crosswalk near the train station last month.
