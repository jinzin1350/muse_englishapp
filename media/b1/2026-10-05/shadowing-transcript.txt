A Day of Travel

Ali is a commuter who loves the city transit.
Every morning, this commuter checks the train timetable.
The timetable says the train leaves at eight o'clock.
Ali pays the bus fare with his travel card.
The fare is cheap, so the commuter smiles.
The friendly conductor greets every commuter on the train.
The conductor checks tickets while the transit moves through the city.
At the big station, Ali takes the escalator down to the metro, far from the noisy highway.
The escalator is fast, and the commuter stands on the right.
A good transit system helps every commuter save time.
Today there is a detour because of road work near the broken escalator.
The bus takes a detour around the closed intersection.
The intersection is busy, so the detour is slow, and the conductor looks worried.
Ali studies the timetable during the long detour.
The conductor tells the commuters about the detour.
Back on the highway, the bus moves fast again, just like the timetable promised.
The highway connects the city to the small towns.
From the highway, Ali sees a wide intersection below.
Every commuter relaxes when the highway is clear.
At the station, the escalator carries people up to the street.
Ali walks past the busy intersection near his office.
He pays no fare for the short walk past the quiet intersection, and he likes that.
The conductor waves goodbye to the morning commuters.
Ali checks the timetable for his trip home, and puts his passport back in his bag.
His passport is in his bag because he flies tomorrow.
He needs his passport for the international flight.
The transit to the airport is quick and the fare is fair.
At the airport, the escalator takes him to departures.
He shows his passport at the control desk, and there is no extra fare for the airport bus.
Tomorrow this happy commuter will fly, and his passport is ready for the trip.
