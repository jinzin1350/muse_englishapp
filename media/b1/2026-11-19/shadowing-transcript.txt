Last summer we travelled to a small seaside resort for our holiday.
My friend Anna told me, I recommend this seaside resort for a quiet holiday.
We took a reliable train to the town, and it arrived on time.
Our hotel was just round the corner from the station.
In the morning, the sea was a little rough, so we walked on the warm sand near the water.
The children built a big castle with the wet sand.
At lunch, a waiter said, I recommend the fish soup today.
After lunch, dark clouds came and the sea became rough.
The rough waves pushed our small boat up and down.
Our captain was calm and reliable, so nobody felt afraid.
In the evening, a band played loud rock music on the beach.
I love rock music, so I danced on the sand with Anna.
The hotel café was round the corner, so we walked there for tea.
The owner played gentle rock music and said, I recommend the cake with honey and nuts.
The next day, the sea was rough again, and swimming was not safe.
We played games on the sand and listened to rock songs instead.
Anna bought a reliable map because our phones had no signal.
With the map, we found a quiet beach just round the corner.
On our last day, we took a reliable little bus to the seaside resort market near the harbour.
Now I recommend this seaside resort to everyone, and our next holiday there is just round the corner.
