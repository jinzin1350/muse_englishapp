Mia loves her dog Max and her cat Luna more than anything in the world.
Every morning Mia puts the leash on Max and takes him for a long walk in the park.
The red leash is Max's favorite, and he jumps with joy when he sees the leash.
Mia holds the leash firmly near the road, but she lets the leash go loose in the open field.
In the park Max loves to play fetch with his bright yellow ball.
Mia throws the ball far, and Max runs fast to fetch it back to her.
They play fetch for twenty minutes, and Max never gets tired of fetch.
After fetch they sit on a bench, and Mia gives Max some water from her bag.
At night Max sleeps in his wooden kennel in the corner of the garden.
The kennel is warm and dry, with a soft blanket inside the kennel.
Max walks into his kennel every evening without being asked, because he loves his kennel.
Luna the cat prefers to stay inside the house all day.
When Luna feels happy and safe, she begins to purr on Mia's lap.
Mia can hear the gentle purr of Luna even from the kitchen.
The soft purr tells Mia that Luna feels calm and loved.
Mia likes to groom Luna slowly, and she sings a soft song while she grooms her.
Luna has long silky hair, and Mia likes to groom her carefully to keep the hair smooth.
The short grooming session takes only ten minutes, but Luna enjoys every second of it.
Mia also likes to groom Max after rainy walks, when his fur gets wet and dirty.
Luna loves her tall scratching post next to the window.
Mia bought the scratching post last month to protect her new sofa.
Now Luna uses the scratching post every day, and the sofa stays safe.
The scratching post is made of strong rope, perfect for Luna's sharp claws.
Last winter Mia found a small stray cat behind the garage.
The poor stray was cold, hungry, and afraid of people.
Mia gave the stray some warm milk and a soft blanket to sleep on.
Within a week the little stray trusted Mia and stopped hiding.
Today the former stray lives with Mia, Max, and Luna as one happy family.
Mia is proud that she gave the leash-walk habit, the cozy kennel, and lots of love to all her pets.
Mia learned to groom both pets herself, and she can groom them better than the vet can.
She plays fetch with Max every afternoon, and she listens to Luna's sweet purr every single day.
