Shadowing: A Healthy Week

Last Monday I woke up with a terrible headache, and the headache stayed all morning.
Water and rest usually help a headache, because a headache often comes from stress.
By noon my headache was finally gone, which was a relief after such a painful headache.
On Tuesday my little brother had a fever, and the fever worried our mother.
A fever means your body is fighting an illness, so a fever is often a good sign.
His fever was high, so we called the clinic, and the nurse said the fever should fall by night.
On Wednesday I had a sore throat, and the sore throat made it hard to swallow.
Warm tea with honey is good for a sore throat, and my sore throat felt better after two days.
Do not shout when you have a sore throat, because shouting only makes a sore throat worse.
The doctor gave me some medicine for my throat, and I took the medicine with water.
Always read the instructions on your medicine, because too much medicine can be dangerous.
Keep your medicine away from small children, and take each medicine exactly as the doctor says.
I bought the medicine at the pharmacy near my house, and the pharmacy is open until late.
The staff at the pharmacy answered all my questions, and a good pharmacy helps the whole neighborhood.
You can buy vitamins at any pharmacy, but for strong medicine you need a pharmacy with a prescription service.
On Thursday I made an appointment with my doctor, and the appointment was easy to book by phone.
My appointment is at ten in the morning, so I will arrive early for the appointment.
Please call if you must cancel an appointment, because a missed appointment wastes the doctor's time.
The doctor was kind during my appointment, and after the appointment I felt much calmer.
On Friday my friend had a small injury while playing football, and the injury was not serious.
An injury can happen in any sport, so ice the injury right away.
Rest is the best cure for a small injury, and his injury healed in one week.
I cleaned the cut and put on a bandage, because a bandage keeps dirt out of the wound.
Change the bandage every morning, and a fresh bandage helps the cut heal faster.
She kept the bandage on for three days, then took the bandage off at last.
On Saturday I felt an ache in my back, and the ache came from sitting too long.
A warm bath helps any ache in the body, so I took a bath to ease the ache.
Stretch every day to avoid an ache, because a small ache can become a big problem.
On Sunday I started a better diet, and my new diet includes more fruit and vegetables.
A healthy diet keeps your body strong, and a good diet gives you more energy every day.
