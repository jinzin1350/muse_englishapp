The Green Valley

Last spring, I walked through a beautiful green landscape.
The mild climate kept the landscape green, with hills, trees, and a small river.
Many kinds of wildlife lived in that green valley.
I saw birds, rabbits, and deer, and the wildlife was not afraid of me.
The climate in the valley was warm and pleasant.
People said the climate was perfect for farming.
In autumn, the farmers worked hard to bring in the harvest.
The harvest was rich because the rain came at the right time.
But one year, the drought came and there was no rain for three months.
The long drought made the fields dry and brown.
During the drought, the farmers saved every drop of water.
The changing climate brought drought, which was bad for the harvest and for the wildlife.
The warm climate made the river very small, and the fish had little water.
Then, in winter, heavy rain fell for many days, and the village feared a flood.
The river grew bigger, and a flood covered the low fields.
The flood damaged some houses near the river.
After the flood, the river slowly returned to its normal size.
The village also had a problem with pollution.
Smoke from the old factory made pollution in the air.
The pollution was bad for the wildlife and for the children.
So the people decided to recycle more and waste less.
They learned to recycle paper, plastic, and glass.
When everyone recycles, there is less pollution in the air and water.
Children learn to recycle at school too.
The village built solar panels on the school roof.
Solar energy is clean and does not cause pollution.
With solar power, the air became cleaner every year.
Many houses now use solar panels for hot water.
A rare bird lived in the valley, and it was endangered.
The bird was endangered because its home was disappearing.
The villagers protected the bird, and now it is not endangered anymore.
Protecting an endangered animal takes time and love.
From the top of the hill, the whole landscape looked like a painting.
I took many photos of the beautiful landscape and the golden harvest that day.
