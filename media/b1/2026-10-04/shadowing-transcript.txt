Anna's Kitchen Week

1. Anna loves cooking dinner for her family.
2. Every Sunday she tries a new recipe.
3. She finds each recipe in an old cookbook.
4. A good recipe lists every ingredient clearly.
5. She checks that she has each ingredient at home.
6. The first ingredient on the list is always olive oil.
7. Without the right ingredient, the flavor changes.
8. She cares most about the final flavor.
9. A rich flavor makes a simple dish special.
10. Salt and lemon can improve the flavor a lot.
11. While she cooks, she enjoys a small snack.
12. Her favorite snack is a piece of cheese.
13. A light snack keeps her energy up.
14. She never eats a big snack before dinner.
15. Her brother likes spicy food.
16. He adds spicy sauce to almost everything.
17. Too much spicy heat can hide the other tastes.
18. She prefers food that is only a little spicy.
19. First, she will boil some water for the pasta.
20. She boils the pasta for ten minutes.
21. When you boil vegetables, do not boil them too long.
22. Next, she will fry some onions in a pan.
23. She fries the onions until they turn golden.
24. You can also fry an egg for a quick lunch.
25. On weekends, she likes to bake bread.
26. When she bakes, the kitchen smells wonderful.
27. She learned to bake from her grandmother.
28. Baking cookies is easier than baking bread.
29. After dinner, there is always some leftover food.
30. She saves the leftover pasta for Monday.
31. Leftover rice is perfect for fried rice.
32. Nothing goes to waste when you plan your leftovers.
33. On Saturday morning, she does the grocery shopping and plans her grocery budget.
34. Her grocery list is based on the week's recipes.
35. At the store, she buys fresh groceries for the week.
