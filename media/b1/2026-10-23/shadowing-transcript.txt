Nina woke up early on Saturday morning.
First, she opened the fridge and took out some milk.
The fridge was almost empty, so she made a shopping list.
She poured the milk into a bowl of cereal.
After breakfast, she decided to do housework.
I do housework every Saturday, she said to herself.
She cleaned the kitchen and washed every bowl.
Then she opened her chest of drawers and folded her clothes.
Every shirt went back into the chest of drawers.
She found a bunch of old magazines under the bed.
She threw the bunch of magazines into the recycling bag.
Her brother Tom called and said, Are you kidding me?
I'm not kidding, he said. Our flight is at risk of being late!
Nina said, The flight? Are you kidding me? We leave tomorrow!
Tom said, Stop kidding around and help me pack.
Nina packed a bag and put a bunch of bananas inside.
She also packed a big bowl for snacks on the plane.
The airport is far, said Tom, and we are at risk of heavy traffic.
If we are late, we are at risk of missing the flight.
At the airport, they bought coffee and waited.
The airport was busy, but their flight was on time.
On the plane, Nina ate a delicious meal.
The food was so delicious that she asked for more, and every bite had a wonderful taste.
She could taste the salt and the lemon in the fish.
Taste this, she said to Tom. It is delicious!
Tom said, You're kidding! It looks terrible.
But he tried it and said, Wow, it really is delicious.
After landing, they took a taxi to their hotel.
The hotel room had a small fridge and a chest of drawers.
Nina put her clothes in the chest of drawers and smiled.
She took a bunch of photos from the window.
What a day, she said. I could not do housework tomorrow, I will be on the beach!
