The New Team
Sara is a manager in the administration of a large university.
Her job is to assemble a team for a new student center.
To assemble a team, she first looks for people who are an ideal fit for the work.
She knows that finding an ideal fit for each role takes time, but it is worth the wait.
She believes that an ideal fit for a job means skill, kindness, and patience.
One morning, she interviews a young man with a strong affinity for languages.
His affinity for French and Spanish makes him an ideal fit for the international office.
Sara also meets a woman who once worked in the administration of a hospital.
The woman knows how the administration of a big organization works, day after day.
Sara asks about the adoption leave policy, because families matter to her team.
She explains that adoption leave gives new parents time to bond with their child.
A good adoption leave policy, she says, makes workers loyal and happy.
Then she meets a quiet engineer who seems anti-social at first.
His anti-social manner worries some people, but Sara is patient with him.
She learns that his anti-social behavior is only shyness, not coldness.
Soon, the quiet engineer helps her assemble the project plan.
Together, they assemble a schedule, assemble a budget, and assemble a list of materials.
Sara tells the team about the annual bonus for a successful year.
She says the annual bonus rewards the whole team, not just one star.
Everyone smiles when they hear about the annual bonus.
Sara's affinity for languages helps her welcome students from many countries.
Her affinity for their cultures makes every student feel at home.
One colleague takes adoption leave to care for his new daughter.
The team sends him gifts, and his adoption leave becomes a happy memory for all.
The quiet engineer, once called anti-social, now eats lunch with the team every day.
No one calls him anti-social anymore, because they know his kind heart.
At the end of the year, Sara must assemble a report on the team's work.
She wants to assemble the report with care, so every person's effort shines.
The university praises her administration of the project.
Her administration is fair, clear, and kind, and the team trusts her completely.
The team receives a generous annual bonus, and they celebrate together.
Sara smiles, because she built more than a team: she built a family at work.
