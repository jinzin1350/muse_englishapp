The Perfect Mountain Journey
For the long flight to the mountains, Sara booked business class.
In business class, she could sleep flat and arrive full of energy.
In business class, the cabin crew welcomed her with a warm smile.
Her corner of the cabin was quiet, bright, and calm.
She put her small bag under the seat in front of her in the cabin.
Outside the cabin window, the clouds looked like a white sea.
When the plane landed, she left her business class seat and walked out of the cabin feeling fresh.
At the station, she found her carriage near the front of the train.
The carriage was almost empty, so she chose a seat by the window.
An old man in her carriage was reading a thick newspaper.
The carriage moved slowly through green hills and small villages.
By the evening, the carriage was warm and full of soft light.
High in the mountains, a wooden chalet was waiting for her.
The chalet had a steep roof, small windows, and a wooden balcony.
Inside the chalet, a fire was burning in the corner.
Sara made hot tea in the tiny kitchen of the chalet.
From the chalet, she could see the whole valley below.
Her friends were coming later on a charter flight from the capital.
Their charter flight was cheaper because the whole group travelled together.
Nobody worried about a cancellation fee, because the plan was certain.
Last year, Sara had paid a heavy cancellation fee for a hotel room.
She had learned to always check the cancellation fee before booking.
This time, there was no cancellation fee to fear at all.
Down in the village, a man stared at the yellow clamp on his car wheel.
The traffic officers had put a clamp on the wheel of his car.
They clamp any car that blocks the narrow mountain road.
Sara promised herself she would never let anyone clamp her car.
A clamp can turn a perfect holiday into a stressful day.
Her friends said the charter flight had landed exactly on time.
For a big group, a charter flight is often the smartest choice.
On the way home, she flew business class again and smiled all the way.
She came home with no cancellation fee, no delays on her charter flight, and a heart full of mountain air.
