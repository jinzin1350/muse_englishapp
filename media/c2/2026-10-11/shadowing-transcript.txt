The Flâneur's City

1. On the first morning of autumn, I walked into the cosmopolitan heart of the city.
2. Street signs in four languages told me I had entered a truly cosmopolitan neighborhood.
3. A cosmopolitan city never sleeps, and every cosmopolitan district hides a story beneath its noise.
4. My guide called this whole region a conurbation, where twelve towns had melted into one.
5. From the hilltop, the conurbation stretched to the horizon with no gap of green between districts.
6. Nobody could say where the old town ended, because the conurbation had erased every border that the old conurbation maps still showed.
7. But gentrification had transformed the old quarter, and the baker who sold cheap bread was gone.
8. The gallery owners praised gentrification, while the street musicians cursed its name.
9. Gentrification painted the walls in bright colors yet pushed the rents beyond all reason.
10. She had watched gentrification advance street by street, and each street grew a little colder.
11. Beyond the center, the sprawl of suburbs rolled onward like an endless gray ocean.
12. The planners blamed sprawl for the traffic, and the commuters blamed the planners.
13. Sprawl swallowed a farm a week, and nobody noticed except the old farmers themselves.
14. If we cannot tame sprawl, our grandchildren will inherit a city with no countryside at all.
15. Yet the old town was a palimpsest, with Roman stones sleeping beneath the modern pavement.
16. She read the street like a palimpsest and found a medieval arch inside a glass tower.
17. Every palimpsest city keeps its ghosts in plain sight, and to understand the present you must read the palimpsest of the past.
18. A flâneur drifted past me slowly, a man with no watch and no destination.
19. The flâneur smiled at the pigeons, for he was the true owner of the boulevard.
20. To be a flâneur, you must abandon your schedule and follow your curiosity like a true flâneur with nowhere to be.
21. Near the river, a brownfield waited, the rusted skeleton of an abandoned factory.
22. The council promised the brownfield would become a park, with trees where machines once roared.
23. Children already played along the edge of the brownfield, inventing games among the ruins.
24. A brownfield is a promise the city has not yet honored, and I hoped it would honor this one.
25. One abandoned hotel was a hideous eyesore, with shattered windows and peeling paint.
26. The mayor declared it an eyesore and ordered it demolished before the autumn festival.
27. An eyesore can poison a whole street, yet every eyesore eventually meets the wrecking ball.
28. In the empty station at midnight, the city felt liminal, caught between yesterday and tomorrow.
29. The long platform was a liminal place, neither departure nor arrival, only waiting.
30. Every traveler knows that liminal hour when one journey ends and another has not begun.
31. A megalopolis contains a thousand liminal spaces, stations and corridors where nobody truly belongs.
32. The guide described our destination as a megalopolis, a chain of cities with no beginning or end.
33. To cross a megalopolis by train is to watch one civilization dissolve into another.
34. By morning I had walked through the entire megalopolis and felt like a citizen of the whole planet.
