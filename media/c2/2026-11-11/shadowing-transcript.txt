Nina and the Bursary

Nina applied for a bursary at your earliest convenience because the office would close soon.
The bursary would make Nina better off than a loan ever could.
Her first form disappeared inside a bureaucratic office with endless windows.
A kind clerk said the bureaucratic delay was normal but deeply unfair.
Nina decided to bury yourself in your books while she waited for news.
Her teacher said, bury yourself in your books and trust the quiet work.
Every morning, you bury yourself in your books before the city wakes.
When doubt arrived, you bury yourself in your books and begin again.
Before the final exam, you bury yourself in your books one last time.
At school, Nina had survived bullying that followed her from class to class.
The bullying stopped only when teachers finally named the bullying openly.
She now helps younger students report bullying without fear or shame.
A school with no bullying gives every mind room to grow.
The university wrote, please confirm your bursary at your earliest convenience.
Nina replied at your earliest convenience and attached every document twice.
She knew a bureaucratic mistake could cancel a bursary in one day.
Still, the bursary arrived, and her family celebrated with simple food.
The dean promised to bestow the bursary certificate at a spring ceremony.
He would bestow honours on students who had overcome the hardest years.
To bestow an award is to honour effort in public and with dignity.
Nina felt better off in knowledge, in confidence, and in hope.
Her brother asked whether he would be better off leaving college early.
She answered that he was better off finishing what he had started.
A student with support is better off than a student alone with debt.
Anyone is better off when help arrives early and without humiliation.
Nina sent her final forms at your earliest convenience, exactly as requested.
The phrase at your earliest convenience sounded polite but felt urgent.
Behind every bureaucratic form, Nina imagined a real student waiting.
She promised to fight bullying and simplify every bureaucratic rule she could.
One day, Nina would bestow a bursary herself and bestow hope with it.
