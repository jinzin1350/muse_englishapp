Maya: Exactly. Even a complicated trip can become an odyssey. Missed flights, lost luggage, a bus that breaks down in the desert. At the time it is stressful. Later, it is your favorite story.
Lena: So an odyssey is a journey with a plot. If your travel story has twists and turns, call it an odyssey and people will listen twice as carefully.
Maya: Wonderful. Now let us lighten up with jaunt. A jaunt is a short, enjoyable trip, usually for pleasure. The perfect weekend escape.
Lena: A jaunt! That word sounds like fun. A Saturday jaunt to the countryside. A quick jaunt to the beach with friends.
Maya: Yes. A jaunt is small and happy. You do not need a visa or a big plan for a jaunt. My favorite jaunt this year was a Sunday drive to a vineyard an hour away.
Lena: That is the charm of a jaunt. It is low effort and high reward. Honestly, a little jaunt every month keeps me sane. Work hard, then jaunt a little.
Maya: Agreed. Now, even a jaunt needs a plan, and that brings us to itinerary. An itinerary is a detailed plan of a journey: the route, the dates, the stops.
Lena: An itinerary. The document that turns chaos into a trip. A good itinerary lists your flights, your hotels, your sightseeing days.

Maya: Traverse. Strong word. Explorers traverse deserts. Sailors traverse oceans. To traverse is to cross with effort and purpose.
Lena: I like how active it sounds. You do not just visit a mountain range, you traverse it. Hikers traverse ridgelines. Trains traverse continents.
