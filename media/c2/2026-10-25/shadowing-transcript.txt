In a small town near a great forest, there was an animal rescue shelter.
The animal rescue shelter cared for dogs, cats, and wild birds.
A kind animal rights activist named Sara worked at the animal rescue shelter.
Sara the animal rights activist believed every animal deserved safety.
One winter, heavy rain began to besiege the town.
The rising river threatened to besiege the shelter itself.
Water can besiege a building quietly, rising inch by inch through the night.
The animal rights activist called her friends for help before the flood could besiege the road.
Affluent families from the city heard the news and sent money.
An affluent businessman donated a truck, and an affluent doctor donated medicine.
But the richest gift came from people who knew absolute poverty.
Absolute poverty means having no food, no clean water, and no safe home.
Families facing absolute poverty still shared their bread with the shelter dogs.
Absolute poverty had taught them kindness, not selfishness.
At night, poachers tried to ambush a deer near the forest.
An ambush is a surprise attack from a hidden place.
The animal rights activist warned the rangers, and the ambush failed.
No one could ambush the deer again, because the rangers watched the forest.
Back at the shelter, an old appliance broke down.
An appliance is a machine made for one job, like a heater or a fridge.
The broken appliance was the heater, and the animals were cold.
An affluent engineer fixed the appliance with spare parts.
Soon the appliance hummed again, and the shelter was warm.
The animal rights activist thanked the affluent donors and the poor families alike.
She said absolute poverty and wealth both exist in one town.
She said an animal rescue shelter belongs to everyone.
The town learned that kindness can defeat an ambush of fear.
It learned that no storm can besiege a community that stands together.
And the animal rescue shelter still stands by the river today.
