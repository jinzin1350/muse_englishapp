My Day in Nature

On Saturday morning, I woke up early to visit the forest.
The forest was green and quiet, and I felt very happy.
In the forest, I saw tall trees and heard birds sing.
I walked deep into the forest and found a small river.
The river was clear, and I could see fish in the river.
I sat by the river and listened to the water in the river.
Then I saw a big mountain far behind the river.
The mountain looked beautiful under the morning sun.
I wanted to climb that mountain one day, but the mountain was too far.
The ocean was blue and big. The ocean smelled fresh, and I wanted to hear the ocean all day. The ocean is my favorite place.
I took off my shoes and felt the warm sand under my feet.
The sand was soft, and I played with the sand like a child.
I loved the warm sand, so I sat down on the sand.
I drew a big flower in the sand with a stick.
Next to my flower, I drew a small leaf.
A real leaf fell from a tree and landed on my flower drawing.
The leaf was green, and I put the leaf in my pocket.
Suddenly, a dark cloud covered the sun.
That cloud was big and gray, and the cloud brought rain.
I watched the cloud move slowly across the sky.
The rain started, and I ran under a tree because of the rain.
The rain stopped after ten minutes, and the sky was clear again.
Then the wind came, and the wind dried my clothes.
The wind moved the leaves, and I loved the soft wind.
I picked a flower for my mother and walked home.
Every flower in my garden made me smile, and each flower had a different color.
What a perfect day in nature!
