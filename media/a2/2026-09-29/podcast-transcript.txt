Word Kitchen: A Walk Through Nature

Maya: Hello and welcome back to Word Kitchen, everyone! I am Maya.
Lena: And I am Lena. Today we are cooking something different. No food, no kitchen today.
Maya: That is right, Lena. Today our ten new words are all about nature and the environment.
Lena: I love this topic. When I was a child, my grandmother had a house near a river. In the summer, we sat by the river every day.
Maya: A river is a long line of water that moves to the sea. My father taught me to swim in a river when I was eight years old.
Lena: That sounds wonderful. I also remember a small bridge over that river. We crossed the river on the bridge, and the water was so clear.
Maya: Clear water is the best. And next to that river, was there a forest?
Lena: Yes! A big forest was just behind the house. A forest is a big place with many trees. We walked into the forest every morning.
Maya: I love walking in a forest. The air in a forest is fresh and clean. In the forest, the trees are so tall that you cannot see the sky.
Lena: And in the morning, the forest is quiet. Only birds sing. But be careful in a forest at night. It can be dark and a little scary.
Maya: True. Now let us talk about high places. Our third word is mountain. A mountain is a very high hill of rock.
Lena: Oh, I climbed a small mountain last year! The mountain was not very high, but the view from the top was amazing.
Maya: I have never climbed a mountain. Was it hard?
Lena: A little hard, but beautiful. On the mountain, we saw snow. And far away, we could see the ocean.
Maya: The ocean! Our fourth word. An ocean is the big sea around the land. I love the ocean because it never ends.
Lena: Me too. When you stand by the ocean, the ocean looks bigger than everything. The sound of the ocean is so relaxing.
Maya: Yes! Last summer I sat on the beach for three hours and just listened to the ocean. The ocean makes me feel small, but in a good way.
Lena: That is a beautiful feeling. Now, let us move from big places to small things. Our fifth word is flower. A flower is the colored part of a plant.
Maya: Flowers make everything nicer. My sister grows flowers on her balcony. Red flowers, yellow flowers, pink flowers.
Lena: I bought a flower for my mother yesterday. One small flower, but she smiled for a long time.
Maya: A flower is a small gift with a big meaning. And next to every flower, you find our sixth word, leaf. A leaf is a flat green part of a plant or a tree.
Lena: In the autumn, the leaves change color. A green leaf becomes yellow or red. I love picking up a beautiful leaf when I walk.
Maya: In the park near my house, every leaf on the ground is like a small carpet. And look, a new leaf is growing on this tree now. Spring is here.
Lena: When a leaf falls from a tree, it is dry. Kids love to play with a dry leaf. A leaf is flat and light.
Maya: Now the weather words! Our seventh word is rain. Rain is water that falls from the sky.
Lena: I like the rain when I am at home. The rain on the window makes a nice sound. But I do not like rain when I forget my umbrella.
Maya: Haha, same! Yesterday the rain started suddenly. The rain was so strong that the street became a small river.
Lena: Oh no! So the rain made the street look like a river. Nature is funny like that.
Maya: Exactly. And our eighth word is wind. Wind is air that moves. When the wind is strong, the trees dance.
Lena: The wind can be cold or warm. Yesterday the wind was warm, and it felt like summer. Today the wind is cold, so I need a jacket.
Maya: In my city, the wind is always strong in March. The wind moves the clouds, and the wind makes the flags fly.
Lena: Speaking of clouds, let us do our ninth word first. Sand. Sand is very small pieces of rock on the beach.
Maya: Sand between your toes! I love walking on warm sand. When I was a child, I built castles with sand every summer.
Lena: Me too! And warm sand is the best place to sit and watch the ocean. The sand was hot, the ocean was cool. Perfect day.
Maya: Do you know what? The sand on some beaches is white, and on other beaches the sand is black. Nature has so many colors.
Lena: And now our last word, cloud. A cloud is soft white water floating in the sky.
Maya: Look at that big cloud! Every cloud has a shape. That cloud looks like a rabbit. That small cloud looks like a boat.
Lena: I love watching the clouds move. When a cloud covers the sun, it gets cooler. When the cloud goes away, the sun is warm again.
Maya: Let us review our ten words quickly. River, forest, mountain, ocean, flower, leaf, rain, wind, sand, cloud.
Lena: Say them with us! River, forest, mountain, ocean, flower, leaf, rain, wind, sand, cloud.
Maya: Great job, everyone! Go outside this weekend and find these things in nature.
Lena: And use your new words when you talk about them. See you next time on Word Kitchen!
