Tutor: Hello! Today we learn comparatives and superlatives.
Tutor: We use a comparative to compare two things.
Tutor: We say, my bicycle is faster than your bicycle.
Tutor: We use a superlative for one thing in a group.
Tutor: We say, Sara had the best beach holiday of her life.
Tutor: Short words add er and est.
Tutor: Cheap, cheaper, the cheapest.
Tutor: Long words use more and the most.
Tutor: Expensive, more expensive, the most expensive.
Tutor: Good, better, the best. Bad, worse, the worst.
Tutor: Remember, we use than after a comparative.
Tutor: A train is faster than a bicycle.
Tutor: A common mistake is to say more better. Just say better.
