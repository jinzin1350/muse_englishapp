Tutor: Hello! Today we learn must, have to, and should.
Tutor: We use must and have to for obligation.
Tutor: Obligation means something is necessary.
Tutor: For example: you must empty the bin.
Tutor: She has to get up at 5 for work.
Tutor: We use should for advice.
Tutor: Advice means something is a good idea.
Tutor: For example: you should wash your blanket every week.
Tutor: Mustn't means not allowed.
Tutor: For example: you mustn't use your phone during the exam.
Tutor: Don't have to means not necessary.
Tutor: For example: we don't have to wear a uniform at school.
Tutor: Common mistake: never say must to.
Tutor: Say you must go, not you must to go.
Tutor: Remember: must and have to for rules, should for advice.
