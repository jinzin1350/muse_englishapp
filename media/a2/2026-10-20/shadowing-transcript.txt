Maya's New Home

Maya moved into a new house last week.
The house is small, but it is very bright.
Her favorite room is the living room.
The living room has big windows with new blinds.
In the morning, she opens the blinds to let the sun in.
At night, she closes the blinds for privacy.
There is a soft blanket on the sofa.
When it is cold, she pulls the blanket over her legs.
She also keeps a warm blanket in the bedroom.
The kitchen has a bin under the sink.
She throws vegetable peels in the bin every day.
There is also a small bin in the bathroom.
The house has good air conditioning.
In summer, the air conditioning keeps every room cool.
She turns the air conditioning on before dinner.
Her favorite place is the small balcony.
She drinks coffee on the balcony every morning.
In the evening, she reads a book on the balcony.
The balcony faces a small park.
Yesterday, she cleaned the blinds and emptied the bin.
Then she washed the blanket and turned on the air conditioning.
She sits on the balcony with her warm blanket and enjoys the cool air conditioning.
Now the house is clean, and Maya loves her new home.
