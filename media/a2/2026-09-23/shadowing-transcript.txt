The Daily Trip

I love my morning trip to work.
Every trip starts at the small airport near my house.
At the airport, I check my flight on the big screen.
My flight is at nine, and I know the flight number well.
I like to reserve my seat before a long trip, and I always reserve early.
You can reserve a flight seat on the phone, and it is easy to reserve.
My friend wants to reserve a ferry trip for the summer.
A friendly driver takes me to the airport every day.
The driver says hello to every passenger in the car.
The driver knows the road to the airport very well, and the driver is never late.
A passenger must show a ticket before a long flight.
On the ferry, every passenger has a good seat.
A ferry is a big boat for people and cars.
The ferry trip across the river takes twenty minutes.
Many people on the ferry eat lunch on the trip.
I take the subway when I travel in the city.
The subway is fast, and the subway trip is cheap.
Many people on the subway look at the train schedule.
The schedule tells every passenger when the subway arrives.
I always check the schedule before I leave home, because the schedule can change.
Sometimes there is a delay, and the delay is annoying.
A delay of ten minutes is not a big problem, but a long delay is bad.
When there is a delay, I wait at the airport with a coffee.
After the trip, every passenger thanks the good driver.
The airport has a big schedule on the wall for every flight.
