A Big Family Day

1. I have a big family.
2. My aunt came to visit us yesterday with my uncle.
3. My aunt brought a big cake, and my uncle brought a guitar.
4. My cousin came with my aunt and uncle.
5. My cousin is the same age as me, and my cousin is my best friend.
6. My nephew is a little boy, and my nephew loves dinosaurs.
7. My nephew played with my cousin in the garden.
8. My niece is a little girl, and my niece loves to draw.
9. My niece played with my nephew in the kitchen, and my niece made him laugh.
10. My grandmother has a new grandchild.
11. The grandchild is the son of my aunt, and my grandmother loves her grandchild.
12. The baby laughed at the dog, and the baby smiled at me.
13. My brother is married, and his baby is one year old.
14. My sister is single, and she is happy to be single.
15. My sister is a teenager, and every teenager needs good friends.
16. My uncle told a funny story at dinner, and my uncle sang a song.
17. My parents are married, and married people help each other.
18. My aunt and uncle are a married couple.
19. My cousin is single, and my cousin likes to travel, and he is single and happy.
20. The grandchild slept in my grandmother's room.
21. The baby slept after lunch, and my mother kissed the baby.
22. My niece is seven years old, and my nephew is five years old.
23. A teenager goes to school every day, and the teenager helped my mother.
24. I love my big family!
