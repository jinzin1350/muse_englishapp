Sara's Boat Trip

Sara wants to take a boat trip on Saturday.
She will book online on Friday evening.
It is easy to book online at home.
Her brother will book online too.
You can book online in two minutes.
They must book a seat for the morning boat.
Please book a seat for me too.
Dad will book a seat near the window.
We always book a seat before a long trip.
Sara will bring a book for the boat.
Her favourite book is about the sea.
She reads her book on every trip.
A good book makes a long trip short.
The small boat is waiting at the water.
Many people board the boat in the morning.
Please board slowly and sit down.
Children board with their parents.
We board the boat and find our seats.
Do not run when you board.
The boat moves slowly on the quiet lake.
Sara opens her book on the boat.
After the trip, she will book online again.
Next week, they will book a seat and board a bigger boat.
