A Saturday in the City

On Saturday morning, I walk to the library.
The library is quiet and full of books.
I borrow two books from the library for my sister.
After the library, I meet my friend at the museum.
We buy tickets at the door of the museum.
Inside the museum, we see old cars and old boats.
The museum is big, so we stay for two hours.
After the museum, we are hungry.
We eat lunch at a small restaurant near the park.
The restaurant is not expensive, and the food is fresh.
The cook at the restaurant smiles and brings us hot soup.
We pay at the restaurant and walk to a cafe.
The cafe is small but very warm.
At the cafe, I drink coffee and read my new library book.
My friend drinks tea at the cafe too.
The cafe has soft music and kind people.
In the evening, I walk home and stop at the pharmacy.
I buy medicine for my mother at the pharmacy.
The pharmacy is open late on Saturdays.
The woman at the pharmacy helps me find the right box.
At home, I put the library books on my desk.
It was a good day in the city.
