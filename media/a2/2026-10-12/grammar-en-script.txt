Tutor: Hello! Let us talk about quantifiers.
Tutor: Quantifiers are words like a lot of, many, much, a few, and enough.
Tutor: They tell us how much or how many of something we have.
Tutor: We use many with countable nouns, like many chargers.
Tutor: We use much with uncountable nouns, like much time.
Tutor: In positive sentences, we often use a lot of, like a lot of photos on my laptop.
Tutor: A few is for small numbers, like a few emails.
Tutor: A little is for small amounts, like a little time.
Tutor: Remember, enough goes after the adjective. My wifi is fast enough.
Tutor: One common mistake. Do not say many water. Say much water.
