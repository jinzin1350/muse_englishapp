Tutor: Hello. Today we learn reported speech.
Tutor: Reported speech tells what someone said.
Tutor: Use told with a person. She told me her hand hurt.
Tutor: Use said with no person. She said her hand hurt.
Tutor: The tense goes one step back.
Tutor: Present becomes past. I am tired becomes she said she was tired.
Tutor: That is optional. He said that his foot hurt.
Tutor: For questions, use if. Are you tired becomes he asked me if I was tired.
Tutor: Do not use do or did. She asked me where I lived.
Tutor: A common mistake is said me. Say told me, not said me.
Tutor: She told me her eyes were tired. He said his nose was cold.
Tutor: She asked me if my mouth hurt.
