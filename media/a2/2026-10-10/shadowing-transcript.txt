A Busy Day for My Body

1. This morning I washed my hands with warm water.
2. I cleaned my hands because breakfast was ready.
3. I held my spoon in my right hand.
4. My hand was a little cold, so I rubbed my hands together.
5. My brother waved his hand and said hello.
6. After breakfast I walked to school on my feet.
7. My feet moved fast because I was late.
8. On the way, my foot hit a small stone.
9. My foot hurt for one minute, then it was fine.
10. I stopped to rest my feet under a big tree.
11. Then I opened my eyes wide and looked around.
12. My eyes saw a red bird in the tree.
13. The bird sang, and my eyes watched it fly away.
14. In class, I read the board with my eyes.
15. My eyes were tired at noon, so I closed them for a minute.
16. At lunch I smelled hot soup with my nose.
17. My nose loved the smell of the soup.
18. I had a small cold, and my nose was a little blocked.
19. I used a tissue to clean my nose.
20. Then I ate the soup with my mouth.
21. My mouth was happy because the soup was warm and good.
22. I talked to my friend with my mouth after lunch.
23. He opened his mouth and laughed at my joke.
24. In the evening, my hand, my foot, my eyes, my nose, and my mouth all rested at home.
