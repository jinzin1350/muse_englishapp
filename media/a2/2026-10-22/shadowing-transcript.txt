My Week and My Month

I always wake up early on Monday morning.
My brother always drinks coffee in the afternoon.
We met one year ago, in the spring.
My birthday is in April, the fourth month.
I visit my grandparents four times a month.
On Friday afternoon, I go to the park.
She started her new job two months ago.
He always helps his mother on Sundays.
April is my favorite month of the year.
We eat dinner together four times a month.
I saw that movie one week ago.
The children play outside every afternoon.
My father always reads in the afternoon.
Our wedding was ten years ago, in April.
They go to the beach four times a month.
I always call my sister on Saturday.
He bought his car three days ago.
April brings flowers and warm weather.
In the afternoon, we drink tea together.
My friends and I meet four times a month.
We moved to this house five years ago.
She always smiles on Monday morning.
We clean the house four times a month.
It rained a lot last April.
