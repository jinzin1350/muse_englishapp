Sara loves to shop, but she always plans her money first.
Every month, she checks the amount of money in her bank account.
Her bank account shows her the amount she can spend.
Last week, Sara wanted a new jacket, and the amount was 90 dollars.
She asked herself, can I afford this jacket?
She looked at her bank account again and said, yes, I can afford it.
Sara did not want a bank loan for a jacket.
A bank loan is for big things, not for small shopping.
Her brother took out a bank loan to buy a car, and he pays a fixed amount every month.
Sara thinks a bank loan is a big choice, so she saves money instead.
On Saturday, Sara stayed at home and cooked a nice dinner.
At home, she can relax, and she does not spend money.
She likes to stay at home with her family on cold days.
Her friend Mina called and asked, can you afford a new phone?
Sara said, I cannot afford a new phone right now, because the amount is too big.
Mina said, you can save a small amount every week, and soon you can afford it.
Sara liked that idea, and she opened a new bank account for her savings.
Now she puts a small amount in her bank account every week.
When she stays at home, she saves money, and her bank account grows.
Sara is happy at home, and she is proud that she can afford what she needs.
She does not need a bank loan, because she plans well.
Sara smiles and says, I know the amount I have, I check my bank account, and I buy only what I can afford.
