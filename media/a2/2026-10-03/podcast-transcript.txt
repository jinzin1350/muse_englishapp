Maya: Hi, Lena! Welcome back to Word Kitchen!
Lena: Hi, Maya! I am so hungry today. Are we cooking?
Maya: Yes! Today we talk about food and cooking. It will be delicious.
Lena: I hope so. I love food that is delicious.
Maya: Me too. Let us start in the kitchen. My kitchen is small, but I love my kitchen.
Lena: My kitchen is small too. What is our first word today?
Maya: Our first word is sweet. Sweet! I love sweet food.
Lena: I love sweet food too. Chocolate cake is sweet. And this tea? Is it sweet?
Maya: No, this tea is not sweet. It is a little sour. Do you want something sweet?
Lena: Yes! Maybe a sweet apple? Apples can be sweet.
Maya: Good idea. Here is a sweet red apple. Now, our next word is snack. A snack is a small food between meals.
Lena: Oh, I eat a snack every afternoon. My favorite snack is fruit.
Maya: Fruit is a good snack. Yesterday I had a snack at four o'clock in the afternoon: a small piece of cheese.
Lena: A snack is perfect when you are hungry but dinner is far away. So, what do we cook now?
Maya: We make soup! Soup is easy and warm. The word soup is perfect for today.
Lena: I love soup. My mom makes delicious soup. Her soup is always delicious.
Maya: My soup is delicious too! At least I think so. A delicious soup needs good vegetables.
Lena: And what makes it delicious? Good salt, and cooking it slowly.
Maya: Yes. A delicious meal is simple. Delicious food does not need to be expensive.
Lena: True. Simple food can be delicious. Now tell me, do you like spicy food?
Maya: Oh, the word spicy! I like spicy food very much. A little spicy soup is wonderful.
Lena: I cannot eat very spicy food. Spicy food makes my mouth burn!
Maya: Really? I love spicy curry. Spicy noodles are my favorite. But not too spicy, okay?
Lena: Okay, we will make the soup a little spicy, not very spicy. Deal.
Maya: Deal. Now, the next word is fresh. We need fresh vegetables for our soup.
Lena: Fresh vegetables are important. Old vegetables are not good. Fresh food tastes better.
Maya: Yes, I buy fresh bread every morning, and fresh fruit every week. Fresh is always better.
Lena: Let us cut the vegetables. Where is the knife? In the kitchen, right?
Maya: In my kitchen, everything is close. My kitchen has a big table and a small oven.
Lena: Oh, the oven! The word oven. Do we need the oven for soup?
Maya: Not for soup, but I want to make bread in the oven. The oven is hot now.
Lena: Be careful with the oven. The oven is very hot. Put the bread in the oven and wait.
Maya: The bread is in the oven now. In twenty minutes it will be ready. The oven smells wonderful.
Lena: While we wait, tell me about the word grill. Do you grill food?
Maya: Yes! In summer I grill fish and vegetables. Food from the grill is delicious.
Lena: I grill chicken sometimes. To grill is to cook over fire. Grill is a fun word.
Maya: My father loves to grill. He says food from the grill tastes special. Do you want to grill tomorrow?
Lena: Maybe. Today the soup is enough. Now, what about dessert? The word dessert!
Maya: Dessert! I love dessert. After a meal, I always want a small dessert.
Lena: What is your favorite dessert? My favorite dessert is ice cream.
Maya: My favorite dessert is chocolate cake. A sweet dessert makes me happy.
Lena: Today our dessert is simple: fresh fruit. A fresh dessert is healthy.
Maya: Healthy and sweet! Fruit is a sweet dessert. Perfect.
Lena: Let us look at the soup. Is it ready? It smells delicious!
Maya: Yes! The soup is ready. Let us eat. This soup is delicious.
Lena: It is delicious! A little spicy, very warm, and delicious. You are a great cook!
Maya: Thank you! And the bread from the oven is ready too. Fresh bread from the oven!
Lena: This is a perfect meal. A delicious soup, fresh bread, and a sweet dessert.
Maya: Let us remember our ten words. Say them with me: delicious, spicy, oven, snack, sweet, kitchen, dessert, fresh, grill, soup.
Lena: delicious, spicy, oven, snack, sweet, kitchen, dessert, fresh, grill, soup. I know them all now!
Maya: Great! Practice these words when you cook in your kitchen. See you next time on Word Kitchen!
