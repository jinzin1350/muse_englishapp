A Delicious Day in the Kitchen

Every Saturday, Sara cooks a big meal in her small kitchen.
Her kitchen is old, but she loves her kitchen.
First, she buys fresh vegetables at the morning market.
She always buys fresh bread and fresh fruit too.
For lunch, she makes a hot bowl of soup.
Her soup is delicious because she cooks it slowly.
She says, "A delicious soup needs love and time."
Everyone says her soup is delicious.
Her brother likes spicy food, so she makes the soup a little spicy.
But her mother does not like spicy food, so she makes a second pot that is not spicy.
"Not too spicy!" her mother always says, and they laugh.
Between lunch and dinner, Sara eats a small snack.
Her favorite snack is a sweet apple.
Sometimes her snack is a little piece of cheese, and sometimes it is a small dessert.
After the snack, she puts bread in the oven.
The oven is very hot, so she is careful with the oven.
Twenty minutes later, she takes warm, fresh bread from the oven.
The bread from the oven smells wonderful.
In summer, Sara loves to grill fish in the garden.
"Food from the grill tastes special," she says.
She wants to grill chicken next weekend, and her friends will help with the grill.
For dessert, she makes a sweet cake.
Her sweet cake is delicious, and everyone asks for her dessert recipe.
"A sweet dessert is the best end to a meal," she says.
At the end of the day, her kitchen is quiet, and Sara is happy.
