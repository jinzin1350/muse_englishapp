The Doctor Says Rest

1. Last Monday I woke up with a bad cough.
2. My throat was sore, and my head hurt a little.
3. I took my temperature and saw a small fever.
4. "You need rest," my mother said.
5. I stayed in bed and tried to rest all morning.
6. Every cough made my throat feel more sore.
7. I drank warm tea with honey for the sore throat.
8. At noon the fever was still there.
9. My sister brought soup and told me to rest.
10. In the evening my cough got worse.
11. The fever went up, and a headache started.
12. My mother called the doctor about the fever and the cough.
13. The doctor said, "Drink water and get more rest."
14. "A headache and a fever need quiet and rest," she said.
15. I closed my eyes, and the headache slowly went away.
16. On Tuesday my throat was still sore.
17. My sore throat and my headache were both small now.
18. But the cough was smaller and the fever was gone.
19. I ate breakfast and took more rest in the afternoon.
20. By Wednesday the sore throat was almost gone.
21. The cough came only two times that day.
22. I had no fever and no headache at all.
23. My mother smiled and said, "See? Rest is the best medicine."
24. Now I always rest when I feel sick, and I get better fast.
