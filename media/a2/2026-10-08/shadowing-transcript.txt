A Day of Sports

I like to run in the morning.
The park is quiet and green.
My dog likes to run with me.
We run to the big tree.
Then we run back home.
After the run, I drink water.
My friend Ali likes to play football.
We play in the park on Sundays.
Ali brings the ball.
The ball is red and round.
I kick the ball to Ali.
Ali kicks the ball back to me.
We play a fun game.
The game starts at ten.
Many children come to play the game.
Everyone wants to win.
I run fast to win.
Ali runs fast too.
At the end, our team wins the game.
We are happy. We win.
We play again next week.
Sports make us strong and happy.
