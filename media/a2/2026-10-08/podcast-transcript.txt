Maya: That sounds like fun. Is your brother good at the game?
Lena: He is very good. The game is easy for him. The game is hard for me.

Lena: Yes, he jumps and gets the ball. He wants to win every game.
Maya: I understand. I also want to win when I play a game. Do you win often?
Lena: Not often. My brother can run faster than me, so he can win the race to the ball.
Maya: But you are learning. Every time you play, you get better. One day you will win.

Lena: Maybe on Sunday. But I will not run fast. We can run slow and talk.
Maya: That is perfect. Slow is fine. The important thing is to play and have fun.
Lena: You are right. A game does not always need a winner. But a small win feels good.
Maya: Yes, it does. When you win, you smile all day. When I finish my run, I smile too.

Maya: And did your team win the game?

Maya: That is so true. Sport is about friends, not just the win.
Lena: Exactly. After the game, we sat on the grass. We were tired. My legs hurt from the long run.
Maya: Haha, running is hard work. But you did it. Next time, maybe you will win.
Lena: Maybe. If I run every morning like you, I will be faster and I can win.
Maya: That is the spirit. Keep running, keep playing, and enjoy every game you play.
