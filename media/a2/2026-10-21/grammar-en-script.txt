Tutor: Hello! Let's learn about much, many, a few, and a little.
Tutor: We use these words to talk about quantity. How much do we have?
Tutor: First, countable nouns. These are things we can count, like beans and apples.
Tutor: We say: How many beans do you want?
Tutor: Next, uncountable nouns. These are things we cannot count, like beef and sugar.
Tutor: We say: How much beef do you need?
Tutor: In positive sentences, use a lot of. I have a lot of time.
Tutor: For a small amount, say a few with countable nouns. I have a few friends.
Tutor: Say a little with uncountable nouns. Could I have a little sugar, please?
Tutor: Remember: many with countable nouns, much with uncountable nouns.
Tutor: One common mistake: never say how many money. Always say how much money.
Tutor: Money is uncountable, so we use much.
