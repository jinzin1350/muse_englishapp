Lena is planning a big barbecue for her friends.
She wants to add many good dishes to the menu.
First, she buys fresh beef at the market.
The beef is red and looks very fresh.
She will grill the beef on Saturday afternoon.
She asks her brother to help with the barbecue.
Her brother loves to cook at a barbecue.
They decide to add beef kebabs with peppers.
Next, Lena buys a bag of beans.
She will cook the beans slowly in a big pot.
The beans will be soft and full of flavor.
Her friends always ask for more beans.
Lena also buys a fresh aubergine.
The aubergine is dark purple and shiny.
She slices the aubergine and adds a little salt.
Grilled aubergine is her favorite barbecue food.
At two o'clock, Lena starts the barbecue fire.
She puts the beef on the hot grill.
Then she adds the aubergine slices next to the beef.
The beans are cooking in the kitchen.
She adds brown sugar to the beans.
The barbecue smells wonderful, and everyone is happy.
