A Day in the City

I walk in the park every morning.
The park near my apartment is very beautiful.
My neighbor walks in the park with her dog.
My neighbor is kind and friendly.
Our neighbor works at the hospital.
The hospital is on the next street.
Our street is quiet and clean.
My apartment is on a busy street.
A big crowd walks down the street every morning.
The crowd was waiting for the bus near the hospital.
I visit my friend at the hospital on Fridays.
The hospital is not far from the cinema.
We watched a good film at the cinema.
The cinema near the park is quiet and comfortable.
A big crowd waited outside the cinema last night.
On Saturday I go to the market on our street.
The market is full of fresh food and friendly people.
I buy fruit at the market every week.
There is always a big crowd at the market on Sundays.
After the market, I visit my neighbor for tea.
I visit my mother every weekend.
We visit the park together in the quiet morning.
My apartment is small but quiet.
I like my apartment because it is near the park.
Say hello to your neighbor and enjoy your quiet street.
