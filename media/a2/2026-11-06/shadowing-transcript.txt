Sara is a chef in a small restaurant.
The chef cooks soup every day.
Today the chef wants to make cabbage soup.
The chef buys a big cabbage at the market.
The cabbage is green and round.
The chef also buys a carrot.
A carrot is long and orange.
The chef puts the cabbage and the carrot in a pot.
The chef adds water and salt to the pot.
While the soup cooks, the chef looks out of the window.
The chef sees a car park next to the restaurant.
Many cars are in the car park.
The car park is big and clean.
A man leaves his car in the car park and walks to the restaurant.
The man asks the chef for soup.
The chef gives him a bowl of cabbage soup.
The man eats the carrot soup and smiles.
After lunch, the chef walks to a farm.
On the farm, the chef sees a calf.
The calf is small and drinks milk from its mother.
The calf runs in the field and the chef laughs.
The calf is happy on the farm.
On his way back, the chef walks past the car park.
The chef loves his job, his cabbage and carrot soup, and the little calf on the farm.
