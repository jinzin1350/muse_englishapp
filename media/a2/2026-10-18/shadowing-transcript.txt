Maya's Bright Day

Yesterday was an awful day for Maya.
The weather was awful and cold.
She missed the bus in the bad rain.
Her phone had a bad battery.
She was confused at the bus station.
She was confused because all the buses were late.
She felt sad, but she was not depressed.
She thought, "A bad day is not a bad life."
Today is a bright new day.
The sun is bright in the sky.
Maya wears a bright yellow shirt.
Her bright shirt makes her happy.
She is not confused today.
The map is clear, and she is not confused.
She meets her friend at the park.
Her friend says, "No more awful days!"
They laugh, and the awful morning is gone.
They drink hot tea. Nobody feels depressed today.
Maya says, "I was sad, but I was never depressed."
A friend can help when you feel depressed.
The evening is bright and warm.
Maya goes home with a bright smile.
