Sara loves to travel to new cities.
Last summer, she saw a big aircraft in the sky.
The aircraft flew over her house early in the morning.
She waved at the aircraft and smiled.
She hoped to fly in an aircraft one day.
At the train station, she heard an announcement.
The announcement said the train to the city was ready.
Sara listened to the announcement again and found her platform.
The announcement helped her a lot, and she felt calm.
Slowly, the train started to approach the station.
The train continued to approach, and Sara picked up her bag.
When the train stopped, a kind woman began to approach Sara.
The woman smiled and said, "Do you need help?"
Sara wanted to find the old castle, so she decided to ask somebody the way.
She was shy, but she knew she had to ask somebody the way.
The woman showed her the way and told her about the castle.
The castle was the most famous attraction in the city.
Every year, many people visit that attraction.
Sara walked to the attraction and took many photos.
Near the attraction, she met a boy who also wanted to ask somebody the way.
They decided to ask somebody the way together.
A shopkeeper told them the way to the park, another lovely attraction.
Sara loved the park attraction and the castle attraction.
In the park, a friendly dog began to approach Sara, and she laughed.
On the way home, she saw an aircraft in the evening sky and thought about her wonderful day.
