A Day of Numbers

Tom wakes up at seven o'clock every morning.
He drinks coffee and eats bread at seven.
At eight, he leaves the house for work.
He drives for eight minutes to the bus stop.
His bus leaves at eight o'clock.
The bus is number nine.
Nine people are already on the bus.
Tom reads the news on his phone for seven minutes.
At nine o'clock, Tom starts his first class of twelve students.
He is a football coach, and his team has eleven players.
Eleven players run on the field together.
The captain is player number eleven.
At noon, Tom eats lunch with eleven friends.
After lunch, he rests for eight minutes and feels fresh again.
The afternoon class has nine students.
Tom teaches them seven new drills.
Practice ends at seven o'clock in the evening.
Tom buys twelve eggs and seven apples on the way home.
He puts the twelve eggs in the fridge.
At home, his daughter shows him her drawing of twelve stars.
They eat dinner together at eight o'clock.
Tom reads a story with nine pages to his daughter.
Then she sleeps, and Tom plans the next twelve months.
Seven days a week, Tom is proud of his eleven players.
