Tutor: Hello! Today we learn past continuous and past simple.
Tutor: Past continuous is was or were plus verb-ing.
Tutor: I was sleeping. They were running.
Tutor: We use it for a long action in the past.
Tutor: Past simple is for a short finished action.
Tutor: I called. She arrived. He broke his pen.
Tutor: Often we use them together in one sentence.
Tutor: I was sleeping at nine o'clock when you called.
Tutor: She was cooking dinner when I arrived.
Tutor: The eleven players were running on the field at seven.
Tutor: A common mistake: do not say I was sleep. Say I was sleeping.
Tutor: Remember: was and were plus verb-ing for the long action.
Tutor: Past simple for the short action.
