My Saturday Shopping

I went to the big market on Saturday morning.
I took one coin from my pocket.
A coin is small, but it is real money.
I bought a sweet apple for one coin.
The old man smiled and took my coin.
I put another coin in my bag for the bus.
Then I wanted to buy some bread.
I had to pay for the bread at the counter.
To pay means to give money for something.
I always pay with a smile.
After the market, I met my friend Ali at a small cafe.
We drank tea and talked about football.
The waiter brought the bill to our table.
A bill shows how much money you must pay.
The bill was small, so we were happy.
Ali wanted to pay, but I wanted to pay too.
We looked at the bill again and split the bill.
Ali paid with his card.
A card is easy, and you do not need cash.
I pay with my card when I buy big things.
I never forget my card at home.
After the cafe, we saw a beautiful watch in a shop window.
The watch was expensive, very expensive.
Some things are expensive, and some things are cheap.
I do not buy expensive things on a normal day.
