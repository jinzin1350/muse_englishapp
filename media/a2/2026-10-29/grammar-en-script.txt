Tutor: Hello! Today we learn possessive pronouns.
Tutor: A possessive pronoun shows who owns something.
Tutor: The words are mine, yours, his, hers, ours, and theirs.
Tutor: We use them so we do not repeat the noun.
Tutor: This is my bag. This bag is mine.
Tutor: Your car is red, and mine is blue.
Tutor: Sam forgot his book, so I gave him mine.
Tutor: Lisa says the blue bike is hers.
Tutor: Their garden is big, but ours is bigger.
Tutor: We also say a friend of mine.
Tutor: She is a friend of mine, not a friend of me.
Tutor: Be careful. Possessive pronouns have no apostrophe.
Tutor: Never write yours with an apostrophe.
