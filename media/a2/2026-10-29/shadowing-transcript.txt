Tom and the Blue Bandage

Tom loves to play football with his friends every Saturday.
Last Saturday, Tom ran fast and hurt his ankle.
His ankle was sore, and he could not walk well.
An ankle injury is no fun, but Tom stayed calm.
He sat on the grass and looked at his ankle.
My ankle hurts, Tom said to his friend Sam.
Sam helped Tom walk home slowly, because a hurt ankle needs rest.
At home, Tom felt an ache in his leg.
The ache was dull and slow, and it stayed all evening.
An ache like that tells you to rest and drink water.
An ache can stay for hours, but rest helps an ache go away.
Tom also felt pain in his back after the long walk home.
His back was tired, and his back hurt when he stood up.
A sore back needs a soft chair and a short rest.
The next morning, Tom had a bad backache from sitting all day, and the backache made him walk slowly.
His backache was worse in the morning, but a short walk helped his backache.
Tom learned that a backache goes away when you move a little and sit less.
While he walked, Tom cut his finger on a small stone.
His mother put a bandage on his finger.
A bandage keeps a small cut clean and safe.
Tom liked his blue bandage, and he showed the bandage to Sam.
Soon his ankle felt better, his ache went away, and his back felt strong again.
Now Tom plays football again, but he rests when his ankle hurts, and he always keeps a bandage in his bag.
