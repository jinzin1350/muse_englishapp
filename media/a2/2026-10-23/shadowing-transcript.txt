My Busy Kitchen Week

I had a busy week in my kitchen.
Two days ago, I bought beef and an aubergine.
I cooked the beef with onions two days ago.
The beef was very tasty, so I ate beef again yesterday.
I also bought an aubergine two days ago.
I like to add salt to the aubergine before cooking.
Add some oil, and the aubergine will be perfect.
I always add garlic to my food. Always!
In the afternoon, I sat on the balcony with my tea.
My balcony is small, but the balcony has a nice view.
In the afternoon, the balcony is my favorite place.
The air conditioning was on because the afternoon was hot.
I always check the air conditioning on a hot afternoon.
I turned the air conditioning off in the evening.
Four times a month, I clean the whole kitchen.
I visit my family four times a month too.
I cleaned it four times a month all last year as well.
Last Saturday, we had a barbecue in the garden.
A barbecue with friends is always fun.
At the barbecue, my uncle made a joke: "I saw a ghost two days ago!"
We laughed, and then we ate beef at the barbecue.
After the barbecue, I took a blanket to the sofa.
The blanket was warm, and I fell asleep with the blanket.
I always sleep well with a warm blanket.
It was a busy week, and I enjoyed it from start to finish.
