Sara rides her bike to the bus stop every morning.
Her bike is red, and the brake on her bike works well.
She checks the brake again before she leaves the bus stop.
Her friend Tom waits at the bus stop and goes by bus.
Tom says going by bus is easy, but Sara loves her bike.
Today they travel together, first by bus and then by plane.
At the airport, Sara shows her boarding card at the gate.
Tom cannot find his boarding card in his bag.
Sara helps Tom look for the boarding card in his coat.
He finds the boarding card under his book and smiles.
On the plane, Sara thinks about her bike and its new brake at home.
She misses her bike, the quiet bus stop, and the morning ride.
Tom likes going by bus, but today the plane feels exciting.
After the trip, Sara rides her bike home from the bus stop.
She presses the brake, stops her bike, and waves to Tom, who goes home by bus.
