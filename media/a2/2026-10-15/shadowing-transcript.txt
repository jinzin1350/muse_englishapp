I pay the bill at the shop.
I pay by card, but the machine is broken.
I have a coin in my pocket, a lucky coin.
A coin is small and round.
I count my coins: seven coins.
Seven coins pay for a warm scarf.
The scarf is long and warm.
I put the scarf around my neck.
I need five more coins for a belt.
A belt goes around my waist.
The belt is not expensive.
I pay the full bill with twelve coins.
I give the bill to the man.
I see gloves on the table.
The gloves are warm and soft.
I put the gloves on my hands.
I have seven keys on my keyboard.
The keyboard is on my laptop.
I press the keyboard and write an email.
An email is a message on the internet.
I send the email to my friend.
I count: seven, eight, nine, eleven, twelve.
Twelve is my lucky number.
