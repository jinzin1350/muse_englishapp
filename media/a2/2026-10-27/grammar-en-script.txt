Tutor: Hello! Welcome to your grammar minute.
Tutor: Today we learn prepositions of place.
Tutor: Prepositions of place tell us where something is.
Tutor: Use in for inside something.
Tutor: My books are in my bag.
Tutor: Use on for a surface.
Tutor: There is a clock on the wall.
Tutor: Use under for below something.
Tutor: Your shoes are under the bed.
Tutor: Use next to for at the side.
Tutor: Our house is next to a park.
Tutor: Be careful. Say next to, not next.
Tutor: Say in front of, not in front.
Tutor: Remember in, on, under, and next to.
