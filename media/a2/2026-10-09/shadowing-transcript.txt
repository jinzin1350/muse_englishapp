A Busy School Day

1. Mina is a happy student.
2. She likes her teacher.
3. Her teacher likes Mina too.
4. Every morning they have a new lesson.
5. The lesson starts at eight o'clock.
6. The teacher reads a story in the lesson.
7. Each student listens very well.
8. After the lesson, the students play outside.
9. Then they come back for the next lesson.
10. The teacher writes new words on the board.
11. Every student writes the words in a notebook.
12. At noon the lesson ends.
13. The students eat lunch together.
14. In the afternoon, the teacher gives homework.
15. Mina does her homework at home.
16. The homework is easy today.
17. Her mother helps her with the homework.
18. She finishes her homework, and then she plays.
19. On Friday there is an exam.
20. The exam is about the lessons of the week.
21. Mina studies hard for the exam.
22. The teacher says the exam will be short.
23. Mina is not afraid of the exam.
24. She is a good student.
25. She loves her teacher and her school.
