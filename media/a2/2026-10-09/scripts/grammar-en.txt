Tutor: Hello! Let's talk about grammar.
Tutor: Today we learn about gerunds and infinitives.
Tutor: A gerund is verb plus i n g.
Tutor: For example: reading, learning, doing.
Tutor: We use the gerund after some verbs.
Tutor: Like this: I enjoy reading.
Tutor: We also use it after at, of, and for.
Tutor: Like this: She is good at teaching.
Tutor: An infinitive is to plus verb.
Tutor: For example: to read, to learn, to do.
Tutor: We use it after want, need, and decide.
Tutor: Like this: I want to pass the exam.
Tutor: A common mistake: do not say I enjoy to read.
Tutor: Say I enjoy reading.
Tutor: Remember: enjoy plus i n g, want plus to.
