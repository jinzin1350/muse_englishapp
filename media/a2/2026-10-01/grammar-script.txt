Tutor: Today, let's learn about have to, must, and should. Use have to when something is a rule or a need. You have to show your passport at the airport. Use must for a very strong rule. You must stop at the red light. Use don't have to when something is not needed. You don't have to take a taxi. The hotel is near the station. Use must not when something is not allowed. You must not smoke on the train. And use should for good advice. You should check your ticket before the departure time. Now you try!
