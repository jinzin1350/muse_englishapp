Lena: We are going on a journey! A journey is a long trip from one place to another. Our journey starts at the train station.
Maya: A station is the building where trains stop. Our first word, train, is a long vehicle that carries people on rails. So we are at the station, and we are taking the train.
Lena: Right. Before we get on the train, we need a ticket. A ticket is a small paper you buy to travel. Maya, do you have your ticket?
Maya: I do! And I have my luggage too. Luggage is the bags you carry when you travel. My luggage is very heavy today. What about you, Lena?
Lena: My luggage is light. I packed only one small bag. Oh no, look at the big clock in the station. What time is our departure?
Maya: Departure means the time you leave a place. Our departure is at ten o'clock. We still have twenty minutes.
Lena: Good. And what time is our arrival? Arrival is the time you come to a place.
Maya: Our arrival is at one in the afternoon. We will be on the train for three hours.
Lena: Three hours is a long time. Let me look at the map. A map is a picture that shows roads and places. The map says we will cross a big river.
Maya: Oh, and we will go over a bridge! A bridge is a road over a river. I love looking out the window when the train goes over a bridge.
Lena: Me too. Wait, what platform are we on? A platform is the raised area where you wait for a train.
Maya: Let me check the ticket. It says platform two. We are on platform two, but the train is not here yet.
Lena: No problem. I can see the station is very busy. Many people are waiting with their luggage.
Maya: Do you have your passport, Lena? A passport is the small book that lets you travel to other countries.
Lena: For a train in this country, I do not need my passport. But I always carry it when I travel. It is good to have it.
Maya: Smart. Oh look, the train is coming! Let's go to the platform door. Do not forget your luggage!
Lena: I have it. Wow, the train is so long. Let's find our seats. My ticket says seat number twelve.
Maya: My ticket says seat number thirteen. Perfect, we are sitting together. Here is the journey part, Lena. Three hours on the train.
Lena: I like the start of a journey. Everything is new and exciting. Where will we sleep tonight, Maya?
Maya: At a hotel. A hotel is a building where travellers sleep and pay for rooms. Our hotel is near the station in the new city.
Lena: Will we be a guest there? A guest is a person staying at a hotel.
Maya: Yes, we will be guests at the hotel for two nights. Then we take the bus to the mountains.
Lena: A bus is a big vehicle that carries many people on the road. Is the bus station far from our hotel?
Maya: No, it is very close. We can take a taxi. A taxi is a car with a driver that you pay to take you somewhere.
Lena: Good idea. A taxi is fast, and our luggage is heavy. Maya, look out the window! We are on the bridge!
Maya: Wow, the river is so beautiful. The train is moving slowly over the bridge. I can see boats on the water.
Lena: This journey is already wonderful. Let's use our words one more time, Maya. A journey is a long trip.
Maya: A train carries people on rails. A station is where trains stop. A ticket is the paper you buy to travel.
Lena: Luggage is your travel bags. A map shows roads and places. A hotel is where travellers sleep.
Maya: A guest stays at a hotel. A passport lets you travel to other countries. A taxi is a car with a driver you pay.
Lena: Arrival is when you come to a place. Departure is when you leave. A platform is where you wait for the train.
Maya: A bus carries many people on the road. And a bridge is a road over a river.
