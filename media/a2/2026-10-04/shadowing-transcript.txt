A Day at the Market

1. Yesterday morning I went to the market with my wallet and some cash.
2. I wanted to buy fruit, bread, and some cheese.
3. At the first shop, the price of apples was very good.
4. I put ten apples in my bag.
5. I paid with cash because the shop was small.
6. The woman gave me some change back.
7. I counted the change and said thank you.
8. She gave me the receipt too.
9. I put the receipt in my wallet.
10. At the next shop, I looked at the price of cheese.
11. The price was too high, so I did not buy it.
12. I asked the man about the price of bread.
13. He smiled and said the price was one dollar.
14. I opened my wallet and took out some cash.
15. I gave him the cash and waited for my change.
16. He gave me the receipt and wished me a good day.
17. I lost my receipt on the way home!
18. But my wallet was safe in my pocket.
19. At home I checked my wallet again.
20. I had a little cash left, two receipts, and that was all.
21. My bag was full, and I got fair change at every shop.
22. Shopping is easy when you count your change and keep your wallet safe.
