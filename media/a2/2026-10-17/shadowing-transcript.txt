Sara wanted to visit her friend in the city.
She took a small bag and carried it to the station.
Her train would depart at nine in the morning.
Sara arrived at the station early and found her gate.
Wait, at the station there is no gate! She laughed.
At the airport, you wait at the gate for your plane.
She wished the train station had a gate too.
Sara's train did not depart on time.
She sat and watched the big board until it was time to depart.
The train finally departed, and Sara was happy.
When the train arrived, she felt excited.
It is always fun to arrive in a new city.
She carried a map, but she did not need it.
Sara carried her bag to the hotel.
The hotel was on the corner of a quiet street.
She turned left at the corner and saw a small cafe.
On the corner, a man sold fresh fruit.
Sara bought an apple and carried it to her room.
The next day, her friend arrived at the hotel.
They walked to the corner to catch a bus.
At the corner, they saw a beautiful old church.
Sara carried her camera and took many photos.
They wanted to depart for the park at two o'clock.
When they arrived at the park, the sun was shining.
