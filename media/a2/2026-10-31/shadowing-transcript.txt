Sara went to the market and bought a bunch of bananas.
She also bought a bunch of broccoli because she loves fresh broccoli.
The broccoli was green and fresh, and the bunch was big.
After lunch, her coffee was bitter, and she did not like the bitter taste.
She said, "This coffee is bitter, so I will drink water."
At the train station, Sara heard an announcement about the train.
The announcement was clear, and a second announcement said the train was late.
When the train started to approach the station, Sara stood up to watch it approach.
As the train began to approach the city, Sara looked out of the window.
In the city, Sara wanted to visit a famous attraction.
She did not know the way, so she asked somebody the way to the attraction.
A kind man showed her the way, and she thanked him after she asked somebody the way.
The attraction was a tall old tower, and many people visit that attraction every day.
At the attraction, Sara hurt her ankle on the steps.
Her ankle was sore, and she said, "My ankle aches."
The ache in her ankle was strong, and the ache made walking hard.
A friend put a bandage on her ankle, and the bandage helped at once.
The bandage stayed on her ankle, and she kept a second bandage in her bag for the trip home.
Sara rested, and the ache in her ankle slowly went away.
Before she left, she asked somebody the way to the train station.
She heard one more announcement, and the announcement said the train was ready.
Sara smiled because her ankle felt better, the bitter coffee was forgotten, and the trip was a happy day.
