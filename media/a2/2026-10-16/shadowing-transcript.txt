Sara woke up and felt nervous about her new job.
She was nervous because she did not know her coworkers.
At breakfast, she felt less nervous and smiled.
On the bus, a friend called and she stopped feeling nervous.
At work, everyone said hello, so she was no longer nervous.
In the afternoon, her boss surprised her with a small gift.
Sara was surprised because she did not expect a gift.
The gift was a book, and she felt surprised and happy.
Her friend was surprised too when Sara showed her the book.
Being surprised by kindness is a wonderful feeling.
In the evening, Sara felt worried about a phone call from her mother.
She was worried because her mother sounded tired.
Do not be worried, her mother said with a laugh.
Sara felt less worried after the kind words.
Being worried is normal when you love someone.
Later, Sara sat alone and felt a little lonely.
The house was quiet, and lonely feelings came to her.
She called her best friend and stopped feeling lonely.
Her friend said, you are never lonely when I am here.
No one should feel lonely for a long time.
Before bed, Sara thought about her day and felt grateful.
She was grateful for her new job and her kind boss.
She felt grateful for her mother's love and her friend's call.
Being grateful made her smile before she slept.
Every day has feelings, and Sara was grateful for all of them.
